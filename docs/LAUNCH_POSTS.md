## Facebook (longer, warmer — the story behind it)

**I built a library, and I'm giving it away.**

Today I'm opening Arjuna Badger Press to the world. It's a publishing house with one author and one rule: the books are free. Free to read in your browser, free to download, free forever — because this is a gift, not a product, and the best things ever done for me were given the same way.

What's on the shelf: ancient-mystery thrillers that race across Africa and the ancient world, a true-to-life survival novel set in the Namib, dignity-first travel stories, and the quiet literary ones in between. Two dozen finished books, and more coming.

There's a conviction under all of it. The adventure genre has long carried a quiet poison — the idea that someone *else* built the wonders of the deep past. This house was built to invert that. Brilliance is human. It is African. It is *ours* — shown, not told.

And here's the part I'm proudest of today: my first novel, *Resonance* — set in present-day South Africa, in the heart of KwaZulu-Natal — now reads in **five languages: English, Afrikaans, isiZulu, Spanish and French.** A South African story, kept rooted in its own ground, opened to readers anywhere on earth. The others will follow, each into the languages of the cultures it touches.

The whole library is free, and stays free. If a book moves you, you can support the press — only if you want to. And there's an open invitation on the site for readers to write the foreword to a book they love; the one we choose gets published, with a printed copy sent to the writer.

The archer's eye. The badger's nerve. *Sawubona.*

📚 Start reading, free: **arjunabadger.press**

### Notes for posting
- **Visuals:** lead with the Resonance cover, or a 5-language shelf graphic. For IG, a carousel (cover → "5 languages" tile → a free-to-read tile) outperforms a single image.
- **Pin** the X thread; cross-post 1/ as the standalone if you only want one tweet.
- **`Sawubona`** (isiZulu, "I see you") is the thread that runs through the books' dedications — it's authentic to the house, not decoration. Keep it.
- **Don't** claim "AI-written" or "AI-translated" in the launch copy unless you want that to be the story — it's true and disclosed on the site, but the launch is about the *gift*, not the method. (Relevant because the same AI-authorship gates the competition entries — see LITERARY_COMPETITIONS.md.)
