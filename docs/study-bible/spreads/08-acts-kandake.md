## Passage (context)

On the desert road, Philip meets an *Aithiops*, a eunuch or official of great authority under **Candace** (Kandake), queen of the Ethiopians. He reads Isaiah, invites interpretation, believes, and is baptised. He goes on his way rejoicing.

---

## Notes

### Africa in the Text

“Ethiopia” here points principally to **Meroitic Kush**, centred in modern Sudan — Kandake being a Meroitic royal title. This is African participation in apostolic-era expansion, centuries before modern European missions.

### Words

Do not auto-map to the modern Ethiopian state or assume he founded the Aksumite church. Religious status (Jew, proselyte, God-fearer, gentile) is left ambiguous — preserve the ambiguity.

### Tradition

Later traditions connecting him to Ethiopian church origins are TRAD/reception. Acts never says he founded a church. Royal Christianity at Aksum is fourth century (HIST).

### Africana readings

Celebrate agency: he owns the scroll, asks the question, requests baptism. Avoid turning him into a mascot or a genetic ancestor for all Black Christians.
