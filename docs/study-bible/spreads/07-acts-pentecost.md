## Passage (context)

Devout Jews from every nation under heaven hear the apostles in their own languages. The list includes **Egypt** and **the parts of Libya belonging to Cyrene**, among many other regions.

---

## Notes

### Africa in the Text

Luke’s Pentecost map already includes African Jewish diaspora. Christianity’s public birth scene is not Europe-first. Cyrenaica and Egypt are named as places from which pilgrims and residents come.

### Jewish story

These are Jewish hearers. African geography here is diaspora Judaism, not a claim that Pentecost invented a separate non-Jewish African church on the spot.

### Words

“Libya belonging to Cyrene” ties the broader Libyan region to the Cyrenaican centre — see [Atlas](../atlas.md).

### Africana readings

Use this list to correct “Christianity arrived with European ships.” Also resist overclaim: a pilgrim list is not a complete demography of Africa.
