## Passage (context)

At Antioch, prophets and teachers include **Lucius of Cyrene**, alongside Barnabas, Simeon called Niger, Manaen, and Saul. The church fasts, the Spirit sets apart Barnabas and Saul, and they are sent.

---

## Notes

### Africa in the Text

A Cyrenian is among the leaders who commission the Pauline mission. North Africa is not waiting at the edge of the story; it is inside the sending church. Pair with Simon of Cyrene at the cross and Cyrenians at Pentecost.

### Words

“Niger” is a Latin nickname meaning “dark/black.” It notes appearance without modern racial science. Do not overbuild a biography from a name alone.

### Ancient worlds

Cyrene’s Jewish diaspora linked Libya to Jerusalem and to Syrian Antioch’s multicultural church.

### Africana readings

Teaching point: African presence in leadership, not only in conversion anecdotes. Still: one nicknamed leader is not a demography of all Antioch.
