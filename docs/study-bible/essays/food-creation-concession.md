# Food in Scripture: creation and concession

### Essay

**Read with:** [Genesis 1](../spreads/01-genesis-1.md) · [Genesis 9](../spreads/02-genesis-9.md) · [Claims audit §6](../claims-audit.md)

---

Genesis opens with a plant-fed world. Genesis 9 permits animal food with a blood limit. Torah then regulates meat, sacrifice, and festivals. Prophets dream of peace that ends predation.

### A defensible sequence

1. Creation ideal — shared plant provision (Gen 1:29–30).
2. Violence enters the human story.
3. Animal consumption permitted and bounded (Gen 9:3–4).
4. Israel’s law makes eating a covenant practice, not a free-for-all.
5. Peace visions remember a world without harm.

### What not to claim

- That “vegan” is a biblical word.
- That Genesis 9 never happened.
- That Ice Age Europe explains Genesis.
- That carnivory is racially destined.

### Africana and ethical readings

Land, fasting, hospitality, and justice matter in African Christian practice. Readers may choose plant-forward discipleship as Constructive horizon without rewriting HIST.

### Labels

Gen 1 plant gift — TXT Established. Gen 9 permission — TXT Established. Permanent universal vegan command — Unsupported. “Ideal then concession” theology — Probable interpretive frame.
