# Egypt in Scripture

### Essay

**Read with:** [Exodus spread](../spreads/04-exodus-egypt.md) · [Matthew 2](../spreads/06-matthew-egypt.md) · [Atlas](../atlas.md)

---

Egypt (*Miṣrayim*) is the Bible’s most frequent African stage. It is granary and prison, wisdom and whip, refuge and empire.

### Memory layers

- **Patriarchal sojourns** — famine drives Jacob’s family to Joseph’s Egypt.
- **Exodus** — forced labour and departure become Israel’s core freedom memory.
- **Monarchy and prophets** — alliances, horses, and oracles; “woe to those who go down to Egypt for help.”
- **Infancy gospel** — Matthew’s Holy Family flees Herod into Egypt.
- **Diaspora and teaching** — Apollos of Alexandria; Greek Jewish Scripture traditions associated with Egyptian Jewish life.

### African, not racial cartoon

Egypt is African. Its people were diverse across three thousand years of pharaonic and later history. This edition refuses Eurocentric removal of Egypt from Africa and refuses a single modern racial stamp for all Egyptians.

### Both sides

Exodus critique of oppression stands. So does later Egyptian Christian brilliance — Alexandrian theology, Coptic liturgy, desert monasticism. Indict empire; do not erase a people.
