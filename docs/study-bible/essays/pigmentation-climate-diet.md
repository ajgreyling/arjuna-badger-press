# Pigmentation, climate, and diet

### Essay

**Read with:** [Claims audit §7](../claims-audit.md) · [How to read](../how-to-read.md)

---

Human skin colour varies with ancestry, ultraviolet environment, selection, migration, and chance. It is not a moral ranking and not a simple thermometer.

### What science supports

- Dark pigmentation is ancient in *Homo sapiens* and protective under intense UV.
- Lighter pigmentation evolved multiple times in Eurasia, interacting with UVB and vitamin-D biology.
- Diet (including marine vitamin D) and farming transitions complicate simple stories.
- Cold alone does not “cause” white skin.
- Meat eating does not cause depigmentation; vitamin-D-rich animal diets can reduce pressure to lighten.
- Humans remained omnivores with substantial plant use even at high latitudes.

### What racial mythology claims

“People went north, ate only meat, turned white, and became civilised.” That package is unsupported. Some ancient Europeans retained darker pigmentation for millennia. Some Arctic peoples retained darker skin with marine diets and metabolic adaptations.
