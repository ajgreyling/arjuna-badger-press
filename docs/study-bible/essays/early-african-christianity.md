# Early African Christianity

### Essay

**Read with:** [Acts spreads](../spreads/07-acts-pentecost.md) · [Atlas](../atlas.md)

---

African Christianity is not a colonial afterthought. By the time modern European missions arrived, African churches already had centuries of worship, argument, martyrs, and manuscripts.

### Four centres

1. **Egypt / Alexandria** — Greek Jewish Scripture; Clement, Origen, Athanasius, Cyril; Coptic traditions; desert monasticism. Mark as founder = TRAD.
2. **Roman North Africa** — Carthage, Numidia, Hippo; Perpetua and Felicity; Tertullian, Cyprian, Augustine; Latin theology; Donatist controversies.
3. **Aksum** — fourth-century royal adoption; Frumentius; Red Sea and Alexandrian links.
4. **Christian Nubia** — Nobatia, Makuria, Alodia; sixth century onward; long-lived Christian kingdoms.

### New Testament roots

Pentecost lists Egypt and Cyrene. Simon of Cyrene carries the cross. The Kandake’s official is baptised. Lucius of Cyrene teaches at Antioch. Apollos is Alexandrian. These are seeds, not the whole tree.

### Guardrails

Call these figures and institutions African geographically. Do not invent modern racial IDs for every father. Do not detach Latin North Africa from Africa because the literature is Latin.

### Evidence

Broad outline — HIST/MAT Established. First-century Alexandrian church details — sparse; handle as Probable/TRAD where appropriate.
