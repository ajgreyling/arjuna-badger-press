# Cush, Nubia, and biblical Ethiopia

### Essay

**Read with:** [Ebed-melech](../spreads/05-jeremiah-ebed-melech.md) · [Acts 8](../spreads/08-acts-kandake.md) · [Atlas](../atlas.md)

---

**Cush** in most political and military texts points to the African kingdom south of Egypt — Kush/Nubia in present-day Sudan and southern Egypt. English Bibles often said “Ethiopia,” which confuses modern readers.

### Power, not footnote

Prophets know Cush as wealth, chariots, and alliance. Chronicles remembers Zerah the Cushite’s campaign. Jeremiah’s Ebed-melech is a Cushite who trusts God. Acts’ Kandake official serves a Meroitic court.

### Name discipline

| Term | Use |
|---|---|
| Cush / Kush | Preferred for most OT political references |
| Nubia | Modern scholarly umbrella — useful, not identical to every “Cush” |
| Ethiopia / *Aithiopia* | Greek; often south-of-Egypt; not auto-modern Ethiopia |
| Aksum | Later Horn kingdom — separate timeline |

### Skin without race science

Jeremiah 13:23 notices Cushite skin. Ancient writers could see phenotype. They did not run Atlantic-world racial law. Do not read modern racism backward — or deny African presence to avoid the topic.

### Evidence

Middle Nile for most Cushite power texts — Probable/Established. Automatic modern Ethiopia — Unsupported. See [Met Nubia](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/nubia).
