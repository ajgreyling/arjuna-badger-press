## What we pay for

Three kinds of find, weighted by how much they matter to the promise.

| Find | What it means | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Factual error** | A claim about the real world — history, science, geography, a real person — that is simply **wrong**. (Or a *contested* claim we presented as settled fact, with only one side.) | **R750** |
| **Cultural / sensitivity issue** | A people, custom, language, or belief **misrepresented** — handled without the dignity it deserves, or just gotten wrong. | **R400** |
| **Continuity fault** | An **internal contradiction** — a timeline that doesn't add up, a prop that changes, a name that slips, an eye colour that moves. | **R150** |

> Why these weights? A wrong fact can launder a falsehood into a reader's head — that's the worst
> thing a "grounded" book can do, so it pays the most. A cultural misstep wounds the people whose
> stories we're trying to honour — mission-critical, so it pays well. A continuity slip is a craft
> bruise — real, worth fixing, but it doesn't break trust the way the other two do.

## The rules (short, and in your favour)

1. **First valid report of an issue wins it.** Reports are timestamped. If two people find the same
   thing, the earlier one is paid.
2. **Tell us *where* and *why*.** Point to the book and the place (chapter, line, a quote). For a
   factual or cultural find, say **why** it's wrong — a source, a correction, the real story. "I
   didn't like it" is not a finding; "this ceremony is shown wrong, here's how it actually works"
   is.
3. **Contested ≠ wrong — and it still pays.** If something is genuinely *disputed* and we told only
   one side as if it were fact, that's a **both-sides failure** and it pays at the **factual** tier.
   We would rather know.
4. **We adjudicate, in the open.** Every accepted find is fixed and logged on the
   [Fixes &amp; Finders](FINDERS.md) page with your name or handle. The credit is half the reward.
5. **One reward per distinct issue.** A list of ten typos is kind, but it's one continuity-tier
   reward, not ten. Genuinely separate findings each pay.
6. **Be in South Africa** (for now). We pay by instant EFT / PayShap, so we need an SA payout.
   We'll open it wider later.
7. **Good faith.** We read every report from a person who wants the book to be *right*. We ignore
   bad-faith nitpicking, AI-generated spam, and "gotchas" that aren't.

**Who decides?** We do — the press makes the call, and shows its working on the Fixes page so you
can see we're fair. Disagree with a call? Tell us; we've reversed ourselves before.

## Why we're doing this

Because the honey badger doesn't let go until it's right. Because we built tooling that *measures*
the work — a continuity gate, a fact-and-balance check, a machine-tell scanner — and the honest
next step is to let the sharpest readers in the country test it against the one thing tooling can't
fully see: lived truth, and local knowledge. You know KwaZulu-Natal, the Free State veld, isiZulu,
the real history, better than any model ever will.

So: read the books free. If you catch us out, we pay you, we fix it, and we thank you by name. That
is the whole deal.
