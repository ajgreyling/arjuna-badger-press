## ⚠️ Is this a scam? No — and here's exactly how to know

Times are hard, WhatsApp scams are everywhere, and "get paid from your phone" is the oldest hook
there is. You are right to be careful. So here is how a real offer is different from a scam — check
every one of these against us:

- **We will NEVER ask you for money.** No "registration fee", no "activation", no "unlock your
  reward". **Money only ever flows ONE way — from us to you.** If anyone asks *you* to pay, send
  money, or buy a voucher, it is **not us** — it's a scam using our name. Walk away.
- **We will NEVER ask for your bank login, PIN, OTP, or card number.** To pay you, we need only the
  details *you choose to give* for receiving money (e.g. your PayShap / cellphone number or account
  number). We never need your password or a one-time PIN. **Anyone asking for your OTP is a
  criminal.**
- **We will NEVER message you privately first.** We do not slide into your DMs. We **broadcast** on
  our official channel and on this website — *you* come to *us*. **If someone WhatsApps you
  privately claiming to be Arjuna Badger Press, offering you money or "a position", it is FAKE.**
  Real us never starts a private chat.
- **There are no "agents", no "managers", no fees, no upfront anything.** You read a free book, you
  find a real mistake, you submit it on the public form, we judge it in the open, and if it's good
  we pay you. That's the whole thing.
- **Everything is public and checkable.** Real books with our real author's name, a real website you
  can inspect, and a public [Fixes &amp; Finders](FINDERS.md) ledger of who we've actually paid. A
  scam has none of that.

> **The one test that never fails:** a scam wants to *take* something from you (money, a fee, your
> bank OTP) or get you into a *private* chat. We only ever *give* (a cash prize for a real find), and
> we only ever speak in *public*. **If a message about this bounty asks you for anything, or comes as
> a private DM, it is not us.** Verify everything here: **[arjunabadger.press/bounty](https://arjunabadger.press/bounty.html)**.

## The prize pool and what you win

**Total prize: R5,000** split between winners by category:

- **Factual errors: R4,000** — A claim about the real world — history, science, geography, a real
  person — that is simply **wrong**. (Or a *contested* claim we presented as settled fact, with only
  one side. That still counts here.)
- **Continuity errors: R1,000** — An **internal contradiction** — a timeline that doesn't add up, a
  prop that changes, a name that slips, an eye colour that moves.

**If your find is accepted, you get:**

1. **Cash prize** — Your share of the category pool (split equally among winners in that category)
2. **Your fact in the final edition** — Your correction is woven into the published book and credited
   in the sources by your name or handle
3. **A signed hard copy** — A finished book, signed by the author, with your name listed in the
   sources

## The rules (short, and in your favour)

1. **First valid report of an issue wins it.** Submissions are timestamped and public. If two people
   find the same thing, the earlier one gets the prize; the later one is logged as a duplicate.
2. **Tell us *where* and *why*.** Point to the book and the place (chapter, line, a quote). For a
   factual find, say **why** it's wrong — a source, a correction, the real story. "I didn't like it"
   is not a finding; "this fact is incorrect, here's the right one" is.
3. **Contested ≠ wrong — and it still pays.** If something is genuinely *disputed* and we told only
   one side as if it were fact, that's a **both-sides failure** and it qualifies. We would rather
   know.
4. **Your submission grants us the right to use your fact.** By submitting, you agree that your
   finding can be incorporated into the final edition with or without attribution at the press's
   discretion. You will be credited in the sources.
5. **One reward per distinct issue.** A list of ten typos is kind, but it's one continuity-tier
   reward, not ten. Genuinely separate findings each pay.
6. **We adjudicate, in the open.** Every find is judged on the public board with our reasoning. The
   credit — your name or handle in the sources — is part of the award.
7. **Good faith.** We read every report from a person who wants the book to be *right*. We ignore
   bad-faith nitpicking, AI-generated spam, and "gotchas" that aren't.

**Who decides?** We do — the press makes the call, and shows its working on the public board.
Disagree with a call? Say so on the record; we've reversed ourselves before.

We can only run a prize we can actually **pay**. So:

  the country you'd be paid in and your payout method.

  payment providers won't settle. Finds from there are still welcome and credited by name — we just
  can't send a cash prize.
- You must be old enough to be paid (18+, or have a guardian receive on your behalf).
- One person, one identity. No sockpuppets to farm the early-tier rates.

If you're unsure whether we can pay you, ask in your submission — we'll tell you before you spend
hours hunting.
