## About

I am building Arjuna Badger Press into a real publishing platform for authors, readers, narrators,
and printers.

The public site is already live at `https://arjunabadger.press`: free books, a reader/PWA shell,
AI-assisted authoring surfaces, direct distribution, narrator intake, and a marketplace thesis for
ACX-style audiobook production outside the usual rails plus small-batch printing matched to idle
printing press capacity.

I am also available as a consultant for teams that need practical technical help:

- turning AI interest into production workflows developers will actually use;
- SaaS architecture and technical rescue work;
- APIs, automation, integrations, and internal tooling;
- delivery-system diagnosis without process theatre;
- AI-assisted developer workflows that keep human judgement in the loop;
- founder/product support where the outcome has to be working software, not a slide deck.

My credibility is banked, not theoretical: more than two decades in enterprise software across
banking, SaaS products, GIS-enabled systems, product development management, technical leadership,
architecture, automation, and hands-on development.

I have led teams, built high-volume banking systems, worked across C#, Java, SQL Server, T-SQL,
PL/SQL, VB.NET, VBA, Delphi, APIs, data analytics, DSL tooling, AI-enabled workflows, and production
SaaS systems. Recently I have been working with MCP server patterns, local LLMs, AI coding agents,
and long-context agent memory.

The thing I care about most now is building useful systems that widen access: books without old
publishing gates, audiobooks for countries excluded by the usual platforms, small print runs that do
not require warehouse economics, and AI tools that help real people finish real work.

## What not to lead with

Do not lead with generic corporate phrasing such as:

- "passionate about innovation";
- "driving digital transformation";
- "responsible AI implementation";
- "stakeholder alignment";
- "measurable value beyond hype".

Those are not false, but they sound like everyone else. Lead with the actual thing being built:
Arjuna Badger Press, the reader/PWA, the authoring workflow, ACX-style audio outside the gates,
dead-press-time printing, and the practical consulting offer.
