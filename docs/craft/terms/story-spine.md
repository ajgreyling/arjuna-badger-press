# Story spine

> [← Craft Glossary](../CRAFT_GLOSSARY.md) · *Structure & Story*

**Plain English:** The **spine** is the load-bearing narrative thread: protagonist +
want + escalating opposition + climax. Not theme (the meaning underneath) and not outline notes (the
plan) — the *felt* through-line a reader can summarize after reading. In a relay adventure, the spine
is the **key-chain** (each node hands the next a key). In a proof-chain thriller, it is the chain of
evidence. In a competence loop, it is the crisis that will not stay solved.

**Rule:** State the spine in one sentence with a verb: "*An engineer must outrun the people who built
the myth by reading machines everyone else calls magic.*" If you cannot, the draft is probably
episodic.

**Fail:** **Spineless middle** — set-pieces or chapters that entertain but do not advance the through-
line. **Two spines** — a B-plot that overgrows and snaps the main thread. **Decorative spine** — a
premise stated in chapter one and then forgotten until the finale.
