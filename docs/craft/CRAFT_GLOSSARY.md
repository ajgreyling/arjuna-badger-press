# Craft Glossary — Dictionary Index

The working vocabulary of Arjuna Badger Press — craft an MFA teaches, distilled into

## The Craft of Making a Novel *(the process / the steps)*

- **[Premise / hook / "what if"](terms/premise-hook-what-if.md)** — The compressed engine of the whole book — the single dramatic question or "what if" that a reader could repeat in one sentence and want the answer to.

- **[Theme / controlling idea](terms/theme-controlling-idea.md)** — What the book argues about life, underneath the plot.

- **[Outlining vs. discovery ("plotters vs. pantsers")](terms/outlining-vs-discovery.md)** — Two legitimate routes to a draft — plan the structure first (outline), or write to find out what happens (discovery / "pantsing").

- **[Drafting / the rough first draft](terms/drafting-the-rough-first-draft.md)** — Getting the whole shape down, badly, all the way to the end.

- **[Rest / the drawer / fresh eyes](terms/rest-the-drawer-fresh-eyes.md)** — Time away from the manuscript before revising, so you read it as a stranger would.


## Structure & Story

- **[Three-act structure](terms/three-act-structure.md)** — The oldest macro-shape.

- **[The Hero's Journey *(monomyth, Campbell/Vogler)*](terms/the-hero-s-journey.md)** — A mythic overlay on the three acts: Ordinary World → Call to Adventure → Refusal → Mentor → Crossing the Threshold → Tests/Allies/Enemies → Ordeal → Reward →…

- **[Save the Cat beat sheet *(Blake Snyder)*](terms/save-the-cat-beat-sheet.md)** — A 15-beat commercial template (Opening Image, Theme Stated, Setup, Catalyst, Debate, Break into Two, B-Story, Fun and Games, Midpoint, Bad Guys Close In, All…

- **[Scene & sequel *(Dwight Swain, mid-level structure)*](terms/scene-and-sequel.md)** — The engine *below* the act, *above* the line.

- **[Stakes](terms/stakes.md)** — What the POV character stands to lose.

- **[Inciting incident / point of no return](terms/inciting-incident-point-of-no-return.md)** — The event that knocks the protagonist out of equilibrium and *starts the actual story* (inciting incident), and the slightly later moment they can no longer…

- **[Opening / hook](terms/opening-hook.md)** — The first line, paragraph, page, and chapter — the audition.

- **[Causality / the "therefore, but" test](terms/causality-the-therefore-but-test.md)** — Scenes should connect by *consequence*, not mere sequence.

- **[Architectural blueprint *(or just "blueprint")*](terms/architectural-blueprint.md)** — The locked, top-level decision about *how a book is shaped* — its skeleton — chosen before a word is drafted.

- **[Quest-relay / relay / key-chain](terms/quest-relay.md)** — A story shape where each scene is a baton-pass.

- **[Beat / beats](terms/beat.md)** — The smallest unit of *story movement* — one thing happening that changes the situation.

- **[Set-piece](terms/set-piece.md)** — A big, memorable, self-contained dramatic sequence — the chase, the collapsing tunnel, the flooded chamber.

- **[Plant & payoff *(setup / payoff)*](terms/plant-payoff.md)** — A **plant** is a detail dropped early and quietly.

- **[Reverse-payoff / crumb](terms/reverse-payoff-crumb.md)** — A payoff that points *backward* into earlier books.

- **[MacGuffin *(McGuffin)*](terms/macguffin.md)** — An object everyone in the story chases that's really just an excuse to drive the plot — its *specific nature* doesn't matter (the briefcase, the microfilm).

- **[Outline](terms/outline.md)** — The structured plan of the whole book — scene by scene, with beats, setting, and POV — written before drafting.

- **[Coda](terms/coda.md)** — A short closing passage *after* the climax — the final chord, the "and afterward…" that gives a sense of rest.

- **[Thesis / theme-said-aloud](terms/thesis-theme-said-aloud.md)** — Your **theme** is what the book is *about* underneath (here: the cost of knowing, consciousness-based reality).

- **[Dramatic embodiment / "show, don't tell"](terms/dramatic-embodiment-show-don-t-tell.md)** — Let the reader *experience* an emotion or fact through action and concrete detail, rather than being *told* it.

- **[Velocity / propulsive](terms/velocity-propulsive.md)** — Forward momentum — the sense that the story is *pulling* you.

- **[POV (point of view)](terms/pov.md)** — Whose eyes and head we're inside for a given scene.

- **[Canon](terms/canon.md)** — The "official truth" of the story world — names, facts, history, rules.

- **[Mythos](terms/mythos.md)** — The invented belief-system / cosmology of the world (here: Adam's Calendar, ancient machines, resonance).

- **[Two-layer timeline / braid](terms/two-layer-timeline-braid.md)** — Two chronologies running at once — the present-day quest and the ancient past — that **braid** together (converge) by the end.

- **[Act](terms/act.md)** — A major movement of the plot — setup, confrontation, or resolution — each with its own arc and turn.

- **[Scene](terms/scene.md)** — The smallest complete unit of on-stage conflict — goal, opposition, and a change that propels what comes next.

- **[Story spine](terms/story-spine.md)** — The through-line of cause and consequence that holds a book upright — what the story is *about* at the plot level, beat to beat.

## The Machinery (this repo's quality tools)

- **[Rubric](terms/rubric.md)** — A scoring grid — the explicit criteria something is judged against, with weights.

- **[Gate / gatekeeper](terms/gate-gatekeeper.md)** — A **gate** is an automatic checkpoint that *blocks* progress if rules are broken (continuity, key-chain, timeline, mythos).

- **[Continuity](terms/continuity.md)** — Internal consistency over time — the same object can't be in two places at once, eye colour can't change, yesterday's event must stay happened.

- **[Cold read](terms/cold-read.md)** — Reading a manuscript with *fresh eyes and no context* — no notes, no intentions — to judge how it actually lands for a stranger.

- **[Craft audit](terms/craft-audit.md)** — A structural critique one level *above* the sentence — voice, modulation, reveal order, abstract drift.


## Book Furniture (the parts that aren't the story)

- **[Front matter](terms/front-matter.md)** — Everything before chapter one — title page, copyright, dedication, epigraph.

- **[Epigraph](terms/epigraph.md)** — A short quotation at the start of a book or chapter that sets a mood or theme.

- **[Colophon](terms/colophon.md)** — A small publisher's/printer's mark or note (often a logo or a line about how the book was made), traditionally at the very end or on the title page.
