# BookLife Prize — eligibility email (draft)

*Send before entering. Goal: get a written ruling on whether the human-in-the-loop workflow counts
as "AI-assisted" (eligible) or "AI-generated" (ineligible) under BookLife's policy. Their answer is
the single most useful data point for the whole competition strategy. Be honest and specific — a
vague ask gets a vague (or unfavourable) answer.*

**To:** the BookLife / Publishers Weekly contact (use the address on
https://booklife.com/about-us/the-booklife-prize-faqs.html or booklife@booklife.com — verify current)
**Subject:** BookLife Prize eligibility — disclosed human-directed, AI-assisted fiction

---

Hello,

I'd like to confirm eligibility before entering the BookLife Prize, because my work sits on the line your AI policy draws and I'd rather ask plainly than guess.

I write under the imprint Arjuna Badger Press (arjunabadger.press). My novels are produced with a human-in-the-loop process that I disclose openly: I conceive the premise, characters, plot, themes and structure; I direct the drafting of prose with the help of a large language model; and I revise, cut, fact-check and shape every page. The creative direction, the decisions, and the final responsibility are mine throughout — the model is a drafting tool under my hand, not an unattended author. The books are also free to read and self-published on my site.

Your published policy says AI-*assisted* work is eligible and that AI-*generated* text can appear in fiction where it serves the narrative and the reader understands what the author wrote versus what AI produced — but that a fully AI-generated work is not eligible. My question is simply: **does a human-directed, human-revised, openly-disclosed process like the one above read as "AI-assisted" (eligible) or as "AI-generated" (ineligible) for the Prize?**

If it's eligible, I intend to enter a completed novel in the Mystery/Thriller category (a contemporary thriller set in South Africa), and possibly a second in SF/Fantasy/Horror. If there's a disclosure statement or a specific way you'd like the AI involvement described on entry, I'm glad to provide it — transparency is a principle of the press, not a problem to manage.

Thank you for the steer.

Best regards,
Andries J. Greyling
Arjuna Badger Press · arjunabadger.press
info@arjunabadger.press
