# Literary Competitions for Arjuna Badger Press — Actionable Report

*Prepared 2026-06-19. Author: Andries J. Greyling. Catalogue: self-published, free-to-read,
openly AI-authored/AI-assisted fiction (ancient-mystery thrillers, literary survival,
travel-thrillers, grimdark mil-SF, Sherlock retelling, secondary-world fantasy).*

> **TL;DR.** Two facts gut the field, and the AI one is fatal more often than the self-pub one.
> Disclosed AI-authorship eliminates virtually every prestige and genre award; across 2024–25 the
> gatekeepers hardened from silence to explicit bans, several prohibiting AI *at any point in the
> writing process*. Honest disclosure — central to this project — is exactly what gets screened out.
> Self-publication independently knocks out the SA prestige tier (Sunday Times CNA, Caine, Island
> Prize) which want a print ISBN and a third-party publisher. **The realistic target set is small:
> pay-to-enter indie/self-pub programs that openly permit (or are lenient on) disclosed AI, plus the
> BookLife Prize, plus — uniquely — the Next Generation Indie "AI-Generated Books" category.**

**Two strongest leads:**
- **BookLife** — single best prestige-bearing fit (PW imprimatur, free, self-pub explicit, thriller+SFF+general). Risk: their line is "*if any content originated with AI, it is AI-generated*," and fully-AI work is ineligible; the fiction carve-out was likely written for *in-world AI text*, not whole-novel drafting. **Email them describing the human-in-the-loop workflow and ask which side of the line you're on before entering.**
- **Next Gen Indie AI-Generated Books category** — ironically one of the few venues where the disclosure is an *asset*. Honest, on-label, no clawback risk.

## Next steps

1. **Lead with BookLife Prize** (fiction, **31 Aug 2026**). First email them the exact workflow (human conception/outline/direction + LLM drafting + human revision + disclosure) and ask: AI-assisted (eligible) or AI-generated (ineligible)? That answer is the single most useful data point. If green-lit: strongest thriller (African Gold / History Before Time) → Mystery/Thriller; fantasy or mil-SF → SF/Fantasy/Horror. Free.
2. **Enter Next Gen Indie's AI-Generated Books category** — disclosure is an asset there.
3. **Treat "silent" indie awards (Page Turner, Hoffer, Chanticleer, Readers' Favorite) as a paid tier — verify AI policy in writing before paying.** "Silent today" can become "banned next cycle"; avoid a public clawback. Chanticleer's genre divisions are the best genre match if they clear you.
4. **SA prestige tier is closed as configured.** Only structural workaround: **Dinaane Debut Fiction Award** (Jacana) — uniquely allows online-only self-pub, *but* needs a previously-unpublished debut by an SADC citizen, ≥40k words, 3 physical copies, AI policy unstated. Viable only for an unreleased manuscript held back from the free site, and only after confirming AI-assistance is acceptable. Otherwise redirect SA-rootedness to eBook/Africa categories within IPPY/Chanticleer.
5. **Decouple the 40K title** from award strategy entirely (IP-encumbered).
6. **Reframe: pursue reviews + visibility, not laurels.** BookLife Critic's Report / PW review pathway + indie-award finalist seals deliver more practical value to a free-to-read indie press than juried prizes that screen out disclosed AI on principle.

**Verify before spending money / holding back a manuscript:** AI stance of Page Turner, Eric Hoffer,
Chanticleer, Readers' Favorite, SALA, and Dinaane — confirm each by email.
