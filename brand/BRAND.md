# Arjuna Badger Press — Brand Guidelines

> **Arjuna Badger Press** — a publishing & editorial studio that helps authors get their
> story *told*: professionally edited, published across every EPUB platform, narrated for Audible,
> and **printed through the cheapest idle capacity on earth so the artist keeps most of the money.**
> The brand is the consumer face of the autonomous manuscript-craft tooling in this repository.

**Status:** Arjuna Badger Press (see

## 1. The idea behind the name

Two emblems that already live at the heart of this body of work, fused into one house:

**Arjuna** — the archer-hero of the *Mahabharata* and the listener of the *Bhagavad Gita*. He is the
disciplined **eye that sees only the target**, and the man taught the deepest craft-ethic in the
canon: **right action without attachment to the reward** (*nishkama karma*). That is the studio's
spine — it is *literally* the question the *History Before Time* India books are built on ("the self
that acts is not the self"). Arjuna is **focus, mastery, and doing the work for the work's sake** —
not for the gatekeepers' approval, not for the racket's permission.

**The honey badger** — *Stoffel*, the fearless animal that runs through *The African Gold Trilogy*
and the *History Before Time* spin-offs as Jakobus Swart's binding emblem (the RESONANCE dedication:
*"be fearless like the African honey badger"*). Fearless, an escape artist, nearly unkillable — and,
in the deepest beat of the trilogy, the one creature strong enough to **let itself be carried.**

**The archer's eye. The badger's nerve.** Discipline and courage; the steady aim and the animal that
breaks into the sealed room. Every documented trait is the studio's promise:

- **The eye that doesn't waver** (Arjuna) — we finish the book. Studio-standard craft, all the way
  through; we don't stop at "draft done," which is the whole market gap.
- **Action without attachment** (the Gita) — we do the craft for the craft, and route the money to
  the artist. Unattached to the 90% racket, the standing-press waste, the approval of the machine.
- **Fearless** (the badger) — it takes on animals ten times its size. The indie author vs. the
  legacy machine; the little house that takes on the racket and doesn't care who's watching.
- **The escape artist** (the badger) — the real Stoffel broke out of *every* enclosure built to hold
  him. We get a book *out* of every box the industry builds to keep it in.
- **Nearly unkillable** (the badger) — your book stays in print forever, on demand; the author who
  won't quit.
- **And it lets itself be carried** — the tender note under the ferocity: *the strongest thing a
  strong creature does is let itself be helped.* Craft in service of the locked-out voice. This is
  the whole point of the African-voices mission: this is for the authors nobody else would carry.

**One-line:** *The archer's eye. The badger's nerve.*

## 2. Logo (v3 — the house crest · LOCKED 2026-06-14)

A **heraldic shield crest, gold on warm-black**: a dignified **honey-badger bust** in profile at the
centre; above it, **Arjuna's ornate recurve bow** (the Gandiva) arched across the shield; behind, an
**African scene** — a lone acacia, a low gold sun, distant mountains, veld; below, the wordmark
**ARJUNA BADGER PRESS** over a laurel. It fuses the two emblems of §1 — the archer's weapon and the
badger's nerve — in African gold light. Dark is home: the master sits on Badger Black.

> **Note on the earlier concept.** A previous draft of this brief specified a *charging badger whose

> was **superseded** by the locked crest above (AJ, 2026-06-14). The dorsal-stripe / arrow idea lives

### Construction & clear space
- **Clear space:** keep padding equal to the wordmark's cap-height on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** the `mark-only` medallion no smaller than **24 px**; the full crest no smaller
  than **160 px** wide (the three-word wordmark must stay legible).
- **Prefer dark.** The crest is engraved gold lit from within — it reads best on Badger Black; use the
  cream `logo-on-light` only on warm white/Cloud, never on a mid-tone.

### Don'ts
- ✗ Don't make the badger cute/cartoonish — the bust should read dignified and formidable.
- ✗ Don't flatten the gold to a single dull tone — keep the engraved gold-on-dark depth (the foil look).
- ✗ Don't recolour the gold to another hue, or place the dark-on-cream version on a busy/mid-tone ground.
- ✗ Don't stretch, skew, or rotate the crest. Don't outline or re-typeset the wordmark.
- ✗ Don't use the full crest below ~160px wide — switch to `mark-only` (the medallion) for small sizes.
- ✗ Don't typeset "Arjuna Badger Press" in a different font next to the mark; use the lockup.

## 3. Colour (the badger system — warm earth, honey, bone, with a charge of red)

Warm African earth, honey, and bone, with a charge of red. Reads ground-level, brave, sun-baked, and
disciplined. Strong on dark (Badger Black) surfaces.

### Core palette

| Token | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Badger Black** | `#161513` | 22 · 21 · 19 | Primary dark surface (warm near-black — the body, the "ink"). |
| **Iron Earth** | `#3A332B` | 58 · 51 · 43 | Raised dark surface / cards on black. |
| **Bone Stripe** | `#EDE9E0` | 237 · 233 · 224 | Primary light text on dark; the signature underline / arrow-line. (Not pure white.) |
| **Veld Ochre** | `#B07A3C` | 176 · 122 · 60 | Primary accent — honey/dug-earth. CTAs, links, "the spark." |
| **Veld Dust** | `#C8A86B` | 200 · 168 · 107 | Lighter ochre — highlights, hover, warm fills. |
| **Sting Red** | `#C2401E` | 194 · 64 · 30 | The bee-sting / the loosed-arrow charge. Danger, the one hot CTA. Sparingly. |

### Supporting ramp

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Dry Grass | `#7E7A5A` | Muted secondary text on dark; the veld mid-tone. |
| Earth 700 | `#2A241D` | Deep shadow, borders on dark. |
| Earth 500 | `#5A5043` | Mid-shadow, muted dividers. |
| Bone Dim | `#BDB6A6` | Muted body text on dark (more legible than Dry Grass for paragraphs). |
| Cloud | `#F6F4EF` | Warm off-white page background for light mode. |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | Reserved — print, max-contrast headlines. |

### Usage rules
- **Ochre is a spark, not a wall.** Use Veld Ochre for the one thing you want clicked or read first.
  Rule of thumb: **accent on ≤ 10% of any view.** Sting Red is rarer still — one charge per view.
- **Dark is home.** Default surface is `Badger Black`; light mode (`Cloud`/Paper) is the alternate,
  for long-form reading and print/PDF.
- **Text contrast (WCAG — verify in build):** Bone Stripe on Badger Black ≈ 15:1 (AAA). Veld Ochre
  `#B07A3C` on Badger Black ≈ 5:1 — fine for ≥16px/links/large text, **not** small body; use Bone Dim
  or Bone for body. Sting Red on Badger Black ≈ 5.5:1 — large text/icons only. Never ochre or red
  text on Cloud/white for body — use Badger Black for text and reserve the warm colours for fills.
- **Focus state:** 2px `Veld Ochre` ring, 2px offset. Always visible.

## 4. Typography

| Tier | Typeface | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / wordmark** | A bold, gritty grotesk or characterful slab (e.g. **Archivo Black**, **Saira Condensed**, or a slab like **Bitter** at heavy weight) — it should look like it would win a fight, and aim straight. | Logo, big numerals, section eyebrows. ALL CAPS, letter-spacing `+0.04em`. |
| **Headings** | **Space Grotesk** (Google Fonts) | H1–H4. Tight, modern, confident. |
| **Body** | **Inter** (Google Fonts) | Paragraphs, UI, forms. 16px min on web. |
| **Long-form / book pages** | **Atkinson Hyperlegible** (same as [the420code.org](https://the420code.org/)) | Novel body text — EPUB, PDF, read-online. Modern sans, maximum legibility. Non-Latin scripts keep their own faces. |
| **Mono (optional)** | **JetBrains Mono** | Code, the "tooling" story. |

**Type rules**
- Headlines on dark in **Bone Stripe**, never pure white unless you want maximum punch.
- One display-cap line per view, max. The display face is seasoning.
- Don't letter-space body text. Do letter-space the all-caps eyebrows/labels.
- **The dorsal-stripe / arrow-line underline** (a short bone bar) is the house emphasis device under
  headlines.

## 5. Voice & tone

**Who we sound like:** focused, fearless, competent, and secretly tender — the archer's calm over the
badger's nerve. We mock the *industry* (the 90% racket, the standing-press waste, the AI-slop mill)
and we are reverent about the *author's book.* We use machine craft and say so plainly; the author
and the story are the hero, never the tool.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Focused, plain, a little funny | Breathless corporate "revolutionary / game-changing" |
| Irreverent about the **industry** | Disrespectful of the **author** or their work |
| Confident (the maths is on our side; the aim is true) | Reckless / noise for its own sake |
| Tender underneath, once per long piece | All swagger, no heart |
| Honest about automated vs human | Pretending a human did the machine parts (or vice-versa) |

> **Note — the wit always punches *up* at the industry, never *down* at the author**; and every long
> piece earns the one tender beat (the carried badger). The archer half of the brand keeps the swagger
> disciplined: aim before you loose.

**Voice test:** if a sentence could be said by someone with a steady eye and an animal's nerve who
just walked through a fence to get your book to a reader and kept most of the money for you — it's
on-brand.

**Tagline (primary):** **Your story, told true.**
**Taglines (alt):** *The archer's eye. The badger's nerve.* · *Aim true. Dig in.* · *The gatekeepers
kept 90%. We didn't care.* · *We Uber'd the press. You keep most of the money.* · *Craft, with claws.*
· *Action, without attachment.* (the Gita bridge) · *Be fearless like the African honey badger.* (the
canon bridge) · *Breaks into the room they locked you out of.* (mission)

**Boilerplate (≤ 50 words):**
> Arjuna Badger Press is a publishing studio for authors. We finish your book to a studio standard —
> edited, designed, an ebook everywhere, a narrated edition — and route the printing to the cheapest
> idle press on earth, so you keep most of the money and all of the rights. The archer's eye, the
> badger's nerve.

**Words we like:** focus, aim, true, craft, claws, told, kept, yours, the maths, idle press, carried,
voice, finished, fearless, the gatekeepers.
**Words we avoid:** AI-powered (as a boast), unleash, supercharge, magic, effortless (the work is the
point), ninja/rockstar, "synergy." *(We DO allow "disrupt" now — it's literally the thesis — but
sparingly and never as empty boast.)*

## 6. Imagery & motifs

- **Texture:** the bone dorsal-stripe / arrow-line (underline / divider / page-edge / line of flight),
  dug earth & claw-furrows, a subtle honeycomb (honey badger → honey — warm hex texture, *not*
  circuitry), tracks in dust.
- **Photography:** if any — dusk veld, golden hour, ground level, alive. Warm low light. Hands,
  paper, a fence with a hole in it, a press running at night. Never stock "businesspeople at a laptop."
- **Dividers/mark:** a short bone-coloured bar (the dorsal stripe / arrow-line) is the house divider.
  One per section.
- **Conscience note:** the honey badger is "least concern," so the conservation tie-in shifts to the
  animal it shares the veld with — **the pangolin (the most-trafficked mammal) and broader African
  wildlife** (Stoffel's real home, Moholoholo as a rehab centre). A line of revenue/messaging
  supporting that conservation is on-brand: *the fearless thing fighting for the gentle thing.* Never
  a gimmick. (The pangolin remains a *cause the house fights for* — a knowing nod to where the brand
  began.)

## 7. Implementation

Design tokens ship machine-readable so the website consumes them directly:

- `brand/tokens.json` — all colours + type scale as JSON (for JS/build tools).
- `brand/tokens.css` — the same as CSS custom properties (now `--abp-*`).

Import `tokens.css` once and use the variables; do not hard-code hexes in components. When in doubt,
this document (and the binding [`BRAND_BRIEF_ARJUNA_BADGER.md`](BRAND_BRIEF_ARJUNA_BADGER.md)) wins
over any single mock-up.
