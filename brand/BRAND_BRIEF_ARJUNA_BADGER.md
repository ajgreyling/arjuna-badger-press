## 0. The name

**Arjuna Badger Press.** Short forms: **Arjuna Badger**, **ABP**, **the Press**.

- **"Badger"** is the load-bearing animal (AJ's requirement) and is **already binding series canon** —
  Stoffel the honey badger is Jakobus Swart's emblem across the whole body of work (the Court is built
  of honey-badger faculties; *"be fearless like the African honey badger"* is the RESONANCE
  dedication). The company name and the author's life's work rhyme.
- **"Arjuna"** carries the **craft-discipline** half: the archer of the *Mahabharata*, the listener of
  the *Bhagavad Gita* — the unwavering eye, mastery, and above all **right action without attachment
  to the reward** (*nishkama karma*). This is *literally* the spine of the *History Before Time* India
  books ("the self that acts is not the self"), so the name binds the press to the canon at the level
  of theme, not just mascot. Arjuna is *why we do the craft for the craft and route the money to the
  artist.*
- **"Press"** makes it unmistakably a publisher and reads like a real, slightly roguish indie house.

## 1. The two emblems — why the archer *and* the badger

| Source | What it brings to the brand |
|---|---|
| **Arjuna — the steady eye** | Focus. The archer who sees only the target. We finish the book; the aim doesn't waver. |
| **Arjuna — action without attachment (the Gita)** | We do the craft for the craft and let the artist keep the money. Unattached to the racket's approval. The ethic, not just the swagger. |
| **The badger — fears nothing; attacks 10× its size** | The indie author vs. the legacy machine. The little house that takes on Penguin's economics. |
| **The badger — breaks into anything sealed** (Stoffel escaped every enclosure) | Routing a book through the cracks in a broken machine. You can't keep the author locked out. |
| **The badger — nearly unkillable** | Your book stays in print forever, on demand. The author who won't quit. |
| **AJ's canon — the badger that lets itself be carried** | The one tender note under the ferocity — *the strongest thing a strong creature does is let itself be carried.* Fearless, and gentle underneath. |

**The fusion in one line: *the archer's eye, the badger's nerve.*** Discipline + courage; aim before
you loose, then dig in and don't let go.

**Conscience carry-over:** the honey badger is "least concern," so the conservation tie-in stays on
**the pangolin (the most-trafficked mammal) and broader African wildlife** — a cause the house fights
for, and a knowing nod to where the brand began (*Piezo Pangolin*). The pangolin doesn't vanish; it
becomes the gentle thing the fearless thing protects.

## 2. Personality & voice — focused, fearless, secretly tender

**The archer's calm over the badger's nerve.** It doesn't posture — it takes aim, then *does the
thing,* and doesn't care who's watching or what the rules said.

- **Focused, not frantic.** Aim before you loose. Confidence backed by the trilogy (proof of craft)
  and the routing economics (proof of margin).
- **Irreverent about the *industry*, reverent about the *author's book.*** We mock the 90% racket,
  the standing-press waste, the "AI slop mill" — never the author or their work. The book is sacred;
  the machine is fair game.
- **Plain, punchy, a little funny.** "We Uber'd the press." "The gatekeepers kept 90%. We didn't
  care." Short sentences. Earned swagger. No corporate mush.
- **Tender underneath, once.** Somewhere in every long piece, the carried-badger note: this is also
  for the author nobody would carry — the locked-out voice. Ferocity in service of gentleness.

**Voice test:** if a sentence could be said by someone with a steady eye and an animal's nerve who
just walked through a fence to get your book to a reader and kept most of the money for you — it's
on-brand.

## 3. Visual direction (the archer-and-badger system)

The badger palette stays (the animal stays). The archer adds **one motif**: the bone dorsal stripe
now also reads as **the line of a loosed arrow.**

- **Mascot logo:** a honey badger — low, wide stance, head down and forward (charging/digging),
  black body with the bold **bone-white dorsal stripe** from nose to tail, where the stripe resolves
  at one end into a slim **arrowhead** (the archer's mark). The stripe-arrow is the signature device:
  underline under the wordmark, page-edge motif, "line of flight." Keep one tender variant (the badger
  curled/carried) for the conscience/mission contexts.
- **Palette (unchanged from the badger system):**
  - **Badger Black** `#161513` (the body; the "ink"/background).
  - **Bone Stripe** `#EDE9E0` (the warm off-white dorsal stripe / arrow-line — primary light text /
    the signature underline). *Not* pure white.
  - **Veld Dust** `#C8A86B` / **Ochre** `#B07A3C` (African earth, honey, dug ground — the warm accent,
    "the spark you click").
  - **Dry Grass** `#7E7A5A` and **Iron Earth** `#3A332B` for the ramp.
  - One hot accent: **Sting Red** `#C2401E` (the bee-sting / the loosed-arrow charge).
- **Texture motifs:** the dorsal stripe / arrow-line, dug earth / claw-furrows, honeycomb (subtle hex,
  *not* circuitry), tracks in dust.
- **Type:** a bold grotesk or characterful slab for the wordmark; a clean readable serif/sans for body.
  **ARJUNA BADGER PRESS** wants weight and a little grit — it should look like it would win a fight and
  aim straight.
- **Tone of imagery:** dusk veld, golden hour, ground level, alive. Never sterile.

## 5. What carries over vs. what changes (the rename contract)

**Carries over unchanged:**
- The offer: done-with-you **finishing & distribution** studio (edit → format → ebook → audio →
  print), "the author writes, the tooling only measures & alarms — it never writes the prose."
- The wedges: anti-AI-slop ("craft, with claws"), the **African-voices / ACX-wall bridge**, and the
  **"Uber the press / artist keeps most"** print thesis (`PRINT_DISRUPTION.md`).
- The whole SaaS / productisation stack and the print-broker architecture — unaffected; it's the
  engine, not the skin.
- The **badger** mascot and the **Badger Black / Bone Stripe / Veld Ochre / Sting Red** palette.

**Changes (this brief defines the new):**
- Name: → **Arjuna Badger Press** (was *Fearless Badger Press*, was *Piezo Pangolin*).
- Added meaning: **the archer** — focus, mastery, *action without attachment* (the Gita/India-books
  bridge). The first word now carries the craft-discipline; the badger carries the nerve.
- One new motif: the dorsal stripe doubles as **the line of a loosed arrow.**
