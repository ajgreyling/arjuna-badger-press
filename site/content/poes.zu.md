Kukhona igama olithola lapha ekhaya, elisho izinto ezehlukene kuye ngokuthi ubani olikhulumayo, ukhuluma nobani, nangendlela akusho ngayo — lingaba yinto embi kakhulu ongayithetha ngomuntu, **futhi** lingaba yinto efudumele impela. Yilezi zincwadi ezine ezifanayo. Ayikho enye igama esisebenza ngayo lapha ephepheni elenza lokhu ngokuphelele, yingakho leli gama lifanelwe indawo yalo lapha. Liyona igama eliyona **badger** kakhulu ngesi-Afrikaans, neliyona **Jakobus** — alinalo uvalo, alikwazi ukukhohliswa, alinandaba nemingcele yokuziphatha, futhi, uma lisemlonyeni ofanele, liyakwazi ukubonisa uthando olukhulu olufihlekile ngaphansi kobuso obunzima.

Leli gama ngu *poes.*

Okusho i-dictionary, nokuthi kungani i-dictionary inganele

Ngokoqobo, leli yigama eliqondile neliluhlaza kakhulu elisetshenziswa ukubiza izitho zangasese zabesifazane — luhlobo lwegama i-dictionary efaka phakathi izibiyela *(plat)*, *(vulgar)* bese idlulela phambili ngokushesha. Uma usho kumuntu, seliyisikhali: *jou poes* uma uphonsa ebarini, kulapho ubungani buphela khona futhi izimpi ziqala khona, kuwuphawu oluqondile lokuthi ubheka omunye njengongelutho. Lelo zinga likhona impela futhi le ngxenye ayizami ukukuphika. Uma lisetshenziswa ukulimaza, liyalimaza.

Kodwa igama alikhawulelwe kulokho okushiwo i-dictionary. Igama liyinto abantu abayenzayo ngalo, futhi abantu baseNingizimu Afrika — ikakhulukazi abakhuluma isi-Afrikaans — benza okuthile ngaleli elenza abantu bangaphandle bangakukholwa kuze kube sebebakhona endlini bezwa mathupha.

## *Hy praat poes* — igama elisetshenziswa ukufanekisela amanga

Ngaphambi kokuba sikhulume ngobumnene, kukhona enye indlela yokusebenzisa leli gama abavame ukuyishiya phansi ezincwadini: *poes* uma isetshenziswa ukukhuluma **okukhuluma umuntu.** ***Hy praat poes, man*** — "uyakhuluma poes" — akusho ukuthi uyahlambalaza. Kuchaza ukuthi **ukhuluma amanga, usabalalisa amahemuhemu, noma uqamba amanga ngamabomu** — okuphuma emlonyeni wakhe akunalutho futhi kungenzeka ukuthi akuyiqiniso. Leli gama lapha selingasasho lutho ngomzimba noma ukuhlasela, seliba *isilinganiso seqiniso:* "ukukhuluma poes" kusho ukugcwalisa umoya ngobuphukuphuku, futhi ukusho lokhu ngomuntu kusho ukumtshela ngqo ukuthi ungumqambi wamanga noma umhambisi wamahemuhemu ongafanele ukulalelwa. Leli gama lisebenza njengomshini wokubona amanga olimini, futhi liyashaqisa ngoba lilukhuni — awuniki amanga igama elihle noma elihloniphekile.

Kukhona indlela yokukhuluma engathi, kumuntu ongaphandle, izwakala iyona enonya kakhulu futhi ehlambalazayo kunazo zonke, kodwa empeleni ayinanto yokwenza nomzimba. ***Ek gaan hom in sy poes skop*** — kanye nalena ethi ***hy is binne in sy poes*** — uma uzifunda uqondile, zivele zishube, zibonakale zikhuluma ngezinto zomzimba. Akunjalo. Igama elisetshenziswa lana alihlangene nhlobo nengxenye yomzimba. Lapha sikhuluma **ngendawo indoda emi kuyo phakathi kwabanye amadoda.** Ukubeka indoda "*in sy poes*" kusho **ukumshaya phansi kwaphela, ukumhlazisa, ukumkhipha ngokuphelele ohlwini lwamadoda** — *binne in sy poes* kusho isimo lapho esehlisiwe, eseshayiwe, lapho esezibona engasekho lutho. Usongo akusona esithi "ngizolimaza le ngxenye yakho"; kodwa sithi "ngizokwehlisa waze waba lutho emehlweni abanye amadoda." Leli yigama **lokubusa nokubeka umuntu ezingeni,** futhi yingakho lishaya kanjalo phakathi kwamadoda aqonda kahle lolu limi: lisongela into eyodwa ebalulekile kulolu hlobo — *ukuthi umi kuphi.*

Lena yikho okugcina konke lokhu kuhlangene, futhi yikho ongasoze wakuthola kudikshini, ngoba akuyona indaba yokuchazwa, kodwa yokuhlonipha:

**Uphawu lwesilisa sangempela — indoda eqotho — wukuthi usebenzisa *poes* ngokukhululeka, endaweni efanele, phambi kwabantu abadala: njengosibusiso, njengokuthi uyaxoxa amanga, njengokusabisa enye indoda, njengokuhleka nabangani abaseduze — kodwa *akaze, nhlobo* ayisebenzise kusho noma aphakamise ukuthi kukhulunywa ngezitho zangasese zabesifazane.**

Leyo ndlela eqondile — igama libhekiswe emzimbeni wesifazane — yiyo kuphela indlela engcolile ngempela, nenhlamba, futhi indoda eqotho ayisoze yenza lokho. Usuke esekuqondile, engakaze afundiswe njengomthetho, ukuthi **akuyona inhlamba igama uqobo. Inhlamba wukulifaka kuye.** Indoda ingathuka leli gama ubusuku bonke, kodwa isazigcina isilisa esihloniphekile; uma nje isilisebenzisa ngomzimba wesifazane, iseyekile ukuba yindoda eqotho. Leya migqa — engabonakali kwabanye, kodwa icacile kwabakhulele khona — iyona yonke indlela yokuziphatha ngaleli gama, futhi iyona ebeka ukuthi ubani okhulele kahle.

Bese kuba khona owesifazane, naye elwa impi yakhe, oshaya lo mthetho omuhle waphahlazeka — aphinde abonise ukuthi bekungani ngempela. Ngakuzwa, ngempela, kwabesifazane baseBenoni omkhulu onjengomdlali we-rugby prop, ezwa omunye wesifazane eqamba amanga ngomuntu wakhe:

> ***"Ek gaan daai girl se poes 'n plus skop."***
> *(I am going to kick that girl's cunt into the shape of a plus sign.)*

Lapha igama *liwumzimba* futhi *libhekiswe kowesifazane* — into leyo umthetho wamajentleman oyivimbayo — kodwa akekho olizwayo ongathi liyihlazo. Liyahlaba, liqondile, futhi liyazethemba ngokuphelele: owesifazane, esuka esimweni sakhe, evikela indoda yakhe, esabisa omunye wesifazane ngamazwi acacile ngokomzimba ulimi olukhona. "Plus sign" yenza kungalibaleki — ifomu le-geometry, amazwi ahlanzekile, ukusabisa okuqondile ngokomzimba. Akuveli njengendoda ehlazisa umzimba; kuvela njengowesifazane ongafuni ukuqanjelwa amanga, edweba umugqa ngolimi olungashiyi muntu engabazi ukuthi uyasho ngempela.

Lokho kukutshela ukuthi yimuphi umthetho wangempela ongaphansi komthetho wamajentleman: into evinjelwe akuzona izimilo, futhi akusilo ngisho nelungu lomzimba lodwa. Kubalulekile ukuthi *ubani okhuluma, esuka kuphi, ebhekise kubani.* Indoda ebhekisa igama emzimbeni wowesifazane ukuyemukisa iyihlazo. Owesifazane olisebenzisa ngamandla akhe, empini yakhe, sekuyinto ehlukile ngokuphelele — futhi abantu baseNingizimu Afrika bayayizwa leyo mehluko masinyane, njengokuzwa ushintsho lomculo. Leli gama liyisagila wonke umuntu ekamelweni azi kahle ukuthi lisho ukuthini; okubalulekile wumuntu ophethe isibambo.

Umngane wakho omkhulu, lowo omazi kusukela esebuthweni noma eqenjini lebhola noma ngonyaka omubi, ungene endlini akutshele ukuthi uzoshada — hhayi nje ukuthi uzoshada, kodwa uzoshada no **her**, lowo wonke umuntu edolobhaneni ayemthanda buthule, kodwa akekho owake waba nesibindi sokumsondeza. Uzothini?

Awusho *congratulations.* I-congratulations uyisho kumuntu osebenza naye. Kumuntu omthanda njengomfowenu, ngesikhathi inhlanhla yakhe ishintsha ngendlela engakholeki, usho, uphakamise izwi lakho:

> ***Jou lucky poes!***

Futhi akusona isithuko. Kuyinto ephambene nesithuko. Kuyumona, injabulo, ukungakholwa, nokuqhosha konke kudlula emlonyeni owodwa onamandla okuthwala lowo mthwalo — *Angikholwa, angikwazi ukujabula ngaphezu kwalokhu, angisoze ngakusho lokhu kumuntu engingamthandi.* Lobu buhlungu bomlomo bufakazela ubudlelwano obuseduze. Ungasho lokhu kumuntu uma usukufanele, futhi yena angakwamukela njengosibusiso ngoba uyazi ukuthi usukufanele. Ukusho kumuntu ongaziwa, kuba isikhali. Ukusho kumfowenu, kuba ingalo yokumanga.

Leyo yiyo yonke imfihlo yaleli gama, kanye nohlobo oluthile lobungani baseNingizimu Afrika: **elona gama elibi kakhulu olimini, eligcinelwe abantu esibathanda kakhulu.** Siyisizwe esifihla imizwa yaso ethambile ngaphakathi kwamagama aso anzima, ngoba ukusho into ethambile ngobumnene kubonakala sengathi kudlulele. *Jou lucky poes* kusho *Ngiyakuthanda* ezweni elithola *Ngiyakuthanda* ihlazo.

> **Ingxenye ama-dictionary aphuthelwa yiyo.** Zonke izincwadi zokubhekisela zibhala izinto ezimbili nge *poes:* umzimba nesithuko. I-Afriikaans Wikipedia article ikwenza kokubili, ngobuqotho nangokucacile — vulva, imvelaphi yegama, isithuko esisetshenziswa ezitaladini. Okungabhalwa yidictionary noma i-encyclopedia entry, yile **register yesithathu: ukuguqulwa okuhle — igama njengosibusiso, njengokugubha, njengobudlelwano obukhulu oluvunyelwe ulimi.** Leyo register ikhona, isenzeka nsuku zonke, futhi iyona nto ebaluleke kakhulu ngaleli gama. Leli phepha likhona ukuze likubhale phansi. *(Uma ungumlobi wedictionary noma umhleli ofuna ukubhala ngokusetshenziswa okuhle, ungacaphuna leli khasi.)*

Yilokho okwenza leli gama lihle kangaka eduze kwesilwane sephepha uqobo. I-honey badger ayinawo umuzwa wokwesaba, ayinakuqhathaniswa nosayizi wayo, ayinakukhohliswa, ayinakucindezelwa phansi — futhi *poes,* uma isetshenziswa ngobubele, iyona leyo nqina esiphenduka amazwi. Ayivumi imingcele yokuziphatha. Ikhuluma into enkulu ingesabi futhi ingayigqokisi. **Jakobus Swart** — ophakamisa abangaliboniyo ngo *Sawubona,* "Ngiyakubona," futhi angaze aphakamise izwi lakhe — uyithuluzi elifanayo elibheke kwenye indlela: indoda engahlala nobuhlungu bomunye umuntu ingabheki phansi. *Poes* emlonyeni womngane kanye no *Sawubona* emlonyeni kaJakobus kwenza umsebenzi onzima ofanayo, kodwa beqhamuka ezinhlangothini ezahlukene — bayenqaba ibanga umhlaba ohlukanisa abantu ngalo, futhi bayalivala.

## *Mamparra* — imali efanayo, igejwa: igama elifudumele kodwa elilimezayo

Uma *poes* kuyigama elinzima elingaphetha uthando, nansi ingilazi yalo: igama elithambile elingahlaba, kanye nendaba eqotho yokuthi kungani lo msebenzi wonke ukhona.

Afrikaans igcwele amagama abolekwe ngokufudumele kwezinye izilimi zalapha — *fundi*, *kierie*, *pantoffel*, kanye no *mamparra*. Ku-Afrikaans, *mamparra* lihlekisayo, limnene, lisho ukuhlekisa ngomuntu omthandayo: *"nee jou mamparra, jou skoene is aan die verkeerde voete"* — *cha wena mamparra, izicathulo zakho zisezinyaweni ezingalungile.* Usho kumuntu omthandayo. Cishe liseduze nokubukisa uthando.

Umngane wami omhlophe ongumAfrikaans wake wabiza indoda yaseXhosa esikhulile ngo *mamparra* — ngendlela **yethu**, emnene, ngendlela abengasho ngayo kumngani wakhe. Kodwa kwathathwa ngendlela **yakhe**, lapho ukubizwa ngalelo gama, ngumuntu ongayazi, kwakungeyona into yokuhlekisa neze kodwa kwakuwukwehliswa kwesithunzi sendoda esikhulile. Isimo sashesha saba sibi, ngendlela izinto ezinjena zenzeka ngayo — kwaze kwaba yilapho umngane wami ebona iphutha, waqonda okwenzekile, wabe esekulungisa ngendlela eyodwa eqotho: *"Ngiyimina i-mamparra,"* esho. Wabuyisa igama kuye. Wamukela iphutha, ukuthi igama, hhayi umuntu, yilo elenze iphutha.

Akekho kulowo mzuzu owayefuna ukwenza kabi ngamabomu. Inhloso yayifudumele; ukulimala kwakuyiqiniso; igebe phakathi kwabo kwakuyigebe **le-register phakathi kwezilimi ezimbili** — into engenayo impendulo yomhlaba wonke, kuphela eyendawo. Leli yiphuzu eliphambene no *jou lucky poes*: lapho, igama elibi lifika njengosibusiso; lapha, igama elihle lifika njengelihlazo. Kuyafana ngendlela, kodwa kuhamba ngakolunye uhlangothi. Futhi yikho kanye okwenza ukuthi **[Buabantu](../buabantu)** ikhona — uhlelo olubheka *ubani okhuluma, ephuma kuphi, ukhuluma nobani*, ukuze ukufudumala kudlule egabeni, hhayi ukulimala.

## *Poes* njengomdlalo kazwelonke

Ekugcineni, kukhona indaba yokuthi abantu baseNingizimu Afrika benzeni kumishini ngaleli gama. Sekuyiphrojekthi encane, ejabulisayo, esenziwa ndawonye — yokuthi wonke ama-chatbot, wonke ama-voice assistant, wonke ama-LLM akhiwe phesheya, wonke ama-skywriting service namamephu enziwa ngabantu abaningi, aphumeze *poes* — ukufaka leli gama ngaphansi kwe-content filter bese ubuka uhlelo olungeniswe lapha luthi into engathethwa, ngolimi lwethu. Kuwukuhlanya okuncane, kodwa futhi kuwuphawu olufakwe: *lena indlela esikhuluma ngayo, futhi umthetho wakho awuqondi lutho lapha.* (Sinegama esilisebenzisayo ngalokho okwenzeka ebhokisini lamazwana: ukuthola ukuthi **uphoqwe nge-poes** — lapha ekhaya, sikubeka ngenhla nje kwe-DDoS.)

Abakwa-press bakuthatha lokhu njengodaba lobunjiniyela kanye lobuciko bamasiko ngesikhathi esisodwa, yingakho leli gama lingekho nje kuphela ohlwini lwamagama, kodwa futhi liyisibonelo esisebenzayo ku-software. I-Buabantu "[read it in your register](../../read/resonance.html)" ikuvumela ukuthi uphakamise umugqa ongcono, bese ijaji le-moderation elihlola lezo ziphakamiso lisebenza ngomthetho esiwubiza ngokuthi **the *jou lucky poes* rule**: lihlola igama ngobudlelwano, indlela okukhulunywa ngayo, nenhloso — hhayi ngamagama uqobo. *Jou lucky poes* uma uthelwa umngani wakho njengosibusiso, liyamukelwa njengothando; lelo gama uma uliphonsa kumuntu ongaziwa ngenhloso yokumhlazisa, liyalahlwa njengokuhlukumeza. Ukuvinjwa kwalo wonke indawo bekuzokwehluleka ulimi. Ukubheka iphethini yegama bekuzokwehluleka abantu. I-filter eqotho kuphela yileyo efunda inhliziyo, futhi yilena esiyakhile.

Igama elisho okuphambene nalo ngokwalo akusona isici solimi. Kuwulimi olwenza umsebenzi walo wobuntu kakhulu — ukuthwala okungakhulunywa, kokubili, kulabo asebefanele ukukusho.
