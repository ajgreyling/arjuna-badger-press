Kukhona igama olithola ekhaya lapha, elisho izinto ezahlukene kuye ngokuthi ubani olikhulumayo, ukhuluma nobani, nangesimo sakhe—kungenzeka kube yinto embi kakhulu ongayithetha ngomuntu, **futhi** kube yinto efudumele kakhulu. Amagama amane afanayo. Alikho elinye igama esilisebenzisayo lapha elenza lokhu ngokuphelele, yingakho leli lifanelwe indawo yalo. Liyigama elinokuthiwa yi-**badger** kakhulu ngesi-Afrikaans, futhi eliyi-**Jakobus** kakhulu—alinalo uvalo, alinakukhohliswa, alinandaba nemingcele yokuziphatha, futhi uma lisemuntwini ofanele, lingaba nobumnene obukhulu ngaphansi kwengubo eqinile.

Leli gama ngu *poes.*

Okushiwo yidikishinari, nokuthi kungani idikishinari inganele

Ngokoqobo, leli yigama eliqondile kakhulu elisetshenziswa ukubiza izitho zangasese zabesifazane — luhlobo lwegama idikishinari elibeka phakathi kwezingwegwe *(plat)*, *(vulgar)*, bese idlulela phambili ngokushesha. Uma usho umuntu ngalo, liyisikhali: *jou poes* eliphonswa ngaphesheya kwebha kuyinto eqeda ubungani futhi iqale izimpi, kuyisitatimende esiqondile sokuthi ubheka omunye njengongelutho. Leyo ndlela yokukhuluma ikhona ngempela futhi lolu lwazi aluzami ukufihla lokho. Uma lusetshenziselwa ukulimaza, luyalimaza.

Kodwa igama alilona elisekudikishinarini kuphela. Igama liyinto abantu abayenzayo ngalo, futhi abantu baseNingizimu Afrika — ikakhulukazi abakhuluma isiAfrikaans — benza okuthile ngaleli elenza abantu bangaphandle bangakukholwa baze babe sebegumbini bezwe kwenzekani.

Ngaphambi kokuba sikhulume ngobumnene, ake sime kancane kwenye into engathandiwa ukukhulunywa kakhulu ezincwadini: *poes* uma isetshenziswa ukukhuluma **ukukhuluma kwabantu.** ***Hy praat poes, man*** — “uyakhuluma poes” — akusho ukuthi uyahlambalaza. Kusho ukuthi **ukhuluma amanga, uyaxoxa, noma uqamba amanga ngamabomu** — ukuthi konke okuphuma emlonyeni wakhe akubalulekile futhi cishe kuyamanga. Lapha leli gama aliseyona into yomzimba noma isithuko, seliyisilinganiso seqiniso: “ukukhuluma poes” kusho ukugcwalisa umoya ngenkulumo engenamsebenzi, futhi uma usho lokhu ngomuntu, usumtshela ngqo ukuthi ungumgxeki noma umxoxi wamanga ongafanele ukulalelwa. Leli gama liyindlela yokuthola amanga ngolimi, futhi liyashaqisa ngoba liwuhlaza — awuniki amanga igama elihle noma elihloniphekile.

Kukhona indlela yokukhuluma ebukeka kumuntu ongaphandle sengathi iyona enonya kakhulu futhi engcolile kunazo zonke, kanti empeleni ayikhulumi nhlobo ngomzimba. ***Ek gaan hom in sy poes skop*** — kanye nalena ethi ***hy is binne in sy poes*** — uma uzifunda ngokuqondile, zibonakala zinonya futhi zikhuluma ngomzimba. Kodwa akunjalo. Igama lapha alihlangene nhlobo nengxenye yomzimba. Lapha, kusho ukuthi **indoda isilahlwe phansi ngokuphelele, ayisekho ndawo kubantu besilisa.** Ukufaka umuntu "*in sy poes*" kusho ukuthi **uyamshaya, uyamcindezela, umphonsa phansi ezingeni eliphansi kakhulu** — *binne in sy poes* kusho isimo esibi, lapho esezicabanga eselahlwe, ehluliwe. Usongo lapha akusikho ukuthi "ngizokulimaza kule ngxenye yomzimba wakho"; kusho ukuthi "ngizokwehlisa ube yize emehlweni abanye besilisa." Leli yigama elikhuluma ngokubusa, ngokuba phezulu noma phansi, yingakho lihlangana kanjalo phakathi kwamadoda aqonda ukuthi kusho ukuthini: lisabisa into eyodwa leyo ndlela yokukhuluma ebeka phezulu — *lapho umi khona*.

Nansi into ebambe konke lokhu ndawonye, futhi into ongasoze uyithole ku-dictionary, ngoba la akukhulunywa ngodumo, hhayi incazelo:

**Uphawu lwesilisa oqotho — owesilisa wangempela — ukuthi usebenzisa *poes* ngokukhululeka, lapho kufanelekile, phambi kwabantu abadala: njengosibusiso, njengokuthiqiza amanga, njengokusabisa enye indoda ngesithunzi sayo, njengokuhleka phakathi kwabantu abasondelene — kodwa *akaze, nhlobo* ayisebenzise ukubhekisa, noma ukusikisela, ezithweni zangasese zabesifazane.**

Lowo mqondo uqondile — igama elikhomba umzimba wesifazane — yilona kuphela eliluhlaza impela, elingcolile, futhi indoda enesithunzi ngeke ifike lapho. Usekwazi ngaphakathi, engakaze afundiswe njengomthetho, ukuthi **okuyinqaba akusilo igama. Inqaba ukuthi ulikhombe kuye.** Indoda ingathuka ngaleli gama ubusuku bonke, kodwa isalokhu iyindoda eqotho; uma nje isilihlobanisa nomzimba wesifazane, isiyekile ukuba njalo. Leyo migqa — engabonakali kwabaphesheya, iqinile kwabaphakathi — iyona grammar yokuziphatha yalo gama, futhi iyona into eqinisekisa ukuthi ubani okhuliswe kahle.

Bese kuba khona owesifazane, naye elwa impi yakhe, ophula wonke lo mthetho ohlanzekile — aphinde akhombise ukuthi bekuyini ngempela. Ngizwile, yebo, kwabesifazane baseBenoni omile njengomdlali we-rugby, ezwa omunye owesifazane eqamba amanga ngendoda yakhe:

> ***"Ek gaan daai girl se poes 'n plus skop."***
> *(I am going to kick that girl's cunt into the shape of a plus sign.)*

Lapha igama *liwumzimba* ngempela futhi *libhekiswe kowesifazane* — okuyinto umthetho wamadoda oyivimbelayo — kodwa akekho oyizwile ongathi kuyihlazo. Kuyesabeka, kunembile, futhi kugcwele ukuzethemba: owesifazane, esesimweni sakhe, evikela indoda yakhe, esabisa omunye owesifazane ngezwi eliqondile kakhulu elikhona olimini. "Plus sign" yenza kungalibaleki — kube yijometri, kube sobala, kube yingozi eqondile ngomzimba. Akufani nendoda ehlazisa umzimba; kufana nowesifazane ongavumi ukuqanjelwa amanga, ebeka umugqa ngolimi olushiya wonke umuntu engangabazi ukuthi uyasho.

Lokhu kukhombisa ukuthi imithetho yangempela ngaphansi komthetho wamadoda injani: into eyayivinjwa akuzona izimvumelwano, futhi akukhona nomzimba uqobo lwawo. Okubalulekile ukuthi *ubani okhuluma, esesimweni sini, ebhekisa kubani*. Indoda ebhekisa leli gama emzimbeni wowesifazane ukuze imnciphise kuyihlazo. Owesifazane osetshenziswa ngamandla akhe, empini yakhe, sekuyinto ehlukile ngokuphelele — futhi abantu baseNingizimu Afrika bayayizwa leyo mehluko ngokushesha, njengokuzwa ushintsho lomculo. Leli gama liyisagila wonke umuntu egumbini azi kahle ukusifunda; okubalulekile yisandla esiphethe isibambo.

Umngane wakho omkhulu, lowo omazi kusukela esebuthweni noma eqenjini lebhola noma ngonyaka omubi, ungene endlini akutshele ukuthi uzoshada — hhayi nje ukushada, kodwa uzoshada no **her**, lowo wonke umuntu edolobhaneni ayemthanda ngasese kodwa kungekho noyedwa owake waba nesibindi sokumsondeza. Uzothini?

Awusho *congratulations.* I-congratulations usho kumuntu osebenza naye. Kumuntu omthanda njengomfowenu, ngesikhathi inhlanhla yakhe iphenduka ingakholeki, usho, uphakamise izwi lakho:

> ***Jou lucky poes!***

Futhi akusona isithuko. Kuyinto ephambene nesithuko. Kuyumona, kujabule, kungakholeki, nokuzigqaja konke kudlula endaweni eyodwa eqinile eyakwazi ukuthwala umthwalo — *Angikholwa, angikwazi ukujabula kakhudlwana, angeke ngikutshele lokhu kumuntu engingamthandi.* Lobu bubi besisho yibufakazi bokusondelana. Ungabiza umuntu kanje kuphela uma usuwutholile lowo msebenzi, futhi yena angakwamukela njengosibusiso ngoba uyazi ukuthi usuwutholile. Uma ukusho kumuntu ongazi, kuba yinkemba. Uma ukusho kumfowenu, kuba yingqayi.

Lena yiyo yonke imfihlo yaleli gama, kanye nohlobo oluthile lobungani baseNingizimu Afrika: **igama elibi kunawo wonke olimini, eligcinelwe abantu esibathanda kakhulu.** Siyisizwe esifihla imizwa yaso ethambile ngaphakathi kwamagama aso aqinile, ngoba ukusho into ethambile ngobumnene kuba yinto enkulu kakhulu. *Jou lucky poes* kusho *Ngiyakuthanda* ezweni elithola ukuthi ukusho *Ngiyakuthanda* kuyihlazo.

> **Ingxenye ama-dictionary aphuthelwa yiyo.** Zonke izincwadi ezisemthethweni zibhala izinto ezimbili ngo *poes:* umzimba nesithuko. I-Afriikaans Wikipedia ichaza kokubili, ngokusobala nangokuhle — i-vulva, umsuka wegama, isithuko sezigebengu. Okungabhalwanga yinoma iyiphi i-dictionary noma i-encyclopedia yile **register yesithathu: ukuguqulwa kokuthanda** — igama njengesibusiso, njengokugubha, njengobudlelwano obukhulu oluvunyelwe ulimi. Lelo zinga likhona, liyenzeka nsuku zonke, futhi liyinto ebaluleke kakhulu ngaleli gama. Lona umlando walo. *(Uma ungumhleli noma umbhali we-dictionary futhi ufuna ukufaka ukusetshenziswa kokuthanda, ungacaphuna leli khasi.)*

Yilokho okwenza leli gama lihlezi kahle kangaka eduze nesilwane sephepha uqobo. I-honey badger ayinayo nhloni, ingaphezu kokulingana nosayizi wayo, awukwazi ukuyikhohlisa, awukwazi ukuyicindezela — futhi *poes,* uma isetshenziswa ngobungane, iyona leyo nhlakanipho esuke isiphenduke amazwi. Ayivumi imingcele yobumnene. Ikhuluma into enkulu ngaphandle kokwesaba, ingayigqizi qakala. **Jakobus Swart** — ophendula abangabonakali ngo *Sawubona,* “Ngiyakubona,” futhi angakaze aphakamise izwi lakhe — uyithuluzi elifanayo elibheke kwenye indlela: indoda engahlala nobuhlungu bomunye umuntu ingabhekisi amehlo phansi. *Poes* emlonyeni womngane no *Sawubona* emlonyeni kaJakobus benza umsebenzi onzima ofanayo, kodwa bevela ezinhlangothini ezahlukene — bayenqaba ibanga elenziwa umhlaba phakathi kwabantu, balisondeze.

## *Mamparra* — imali efanayo, isiphenduke: igama elithambile elilimezayo

Uma *poes* kuyigama elinzima elingathwala uthando, nansi ingxenye yalo ephambene: igama elithambile elingakugwaza, kanye nendaba eqotho yokuthi kungani lo msebenzi wonke ukhona.

IsiAfrikaans sigcwele amagama abolekiwe afudumele avela kwezinye izilimi zakuleli — *fundi*, *kierie*, *pantoffel*, no *mamparra*. NgesiAfrikaans, *mamparra* isho ukuhlonipha okuncane, ukuhlekisa ngobumnene: *"nee jou mamparra, jou skoene is aan die verkeerde voete"* — *cha wena mamparra, izicathulo zakho zisezinyaweni ezingafanele.* Usho lokhu kumuntu omthandayo. Cishe kufana nokumamatheka.

Umngani wami omhlophe wase-Afrikaans wake wabiza indoda enkulu yesiXhosa ngo *mamparra* — ngendlela **yethu**, emnene, ngendlela ayeyisho ngayo kumngani wakhe. Kodwa kwathathwa ngendlela **yakhe**, lapho ukubizwa ngalelo gama, ngumuntu ongaziwa, kwakungeyona into yokuhlekisa kodwa kwaba yihlazo kumuntu omdala. Isimo saba sibi masinyane, njengoba kwenzeka ngezinto ezinjalo — kwaze kwaba yilapho umngani wami ebona ukuthi kwenzekeni, waqonda, wabe esekwenza kwaphela ngendlela eyodwa eqotho: *"Ngiyimina i-mamparra,"* kusho yena. Wathatha igama walibuyisela kuye. Wamukela iphutha elenziwe yilelo gama, hhayi umuntu.

Akekho kulowo mzuzu owayefuna ukwenza kabi ngamabomu. Inhloso yayimnene; ukulimala kwakuyiqiniso; igebe phakathi kwabo kwakuyigebe **lezinga phakathi kwezilimi ezimbili** — leyo nto engenayo impendulo yomhlaba wonke, kuphela eyendawo. Lokho yikho kanye okuphambene ne *jou lucky poes*: lapho, igama eliluhlaza lifika njengesihe; lapha, igama elithandekayo lifika njengokuthuka. Indlela efanayo, kodwa iye ngakolunye uhlangothi. Futhi yikho kanye isizathu **[Buabantu](../buabantu)** ekhona — uhlelo olubheka *ubani okhuluma, esesimeni sini, ubhekise kubani*, ukuze ukufudumala kudlule kuleli gebe, hhayi ukulimala.

## *Tjommie* — umngane osephenduke ingxenye yomzimba (futhi akazange ayeke ukuba umngane)

Ngizofaka enye futhi, ngoba le yisibonelo esimnandi kakhulu kulolu limi lwegama elaphenduka incazelo yalo kodwa lagcina umphefumulo walo.

*Tjommie* igama elidala, elishisayo ngesi-Afrikaans elisho **umngane, umngane omncane** — *maatjie.* (Ukubhalwa okusha, okuphola, *tjomma*, kusamele uthando olumsulwa: "my tjomma.") Kodwa *tjommie*, uma isiyodwa, isithole incazelo entsha ngenkathi isetshenziswa kakhulu ngesi-Afrikaans esilula: manje isho le **ndawo encane yesikhumba ephakathi kwe-poes yomfazi nendunu yakhe** — i-perineum.

Futhi nansi ingxenye eyenza kube kuhle kakhulu: igama yilo elenza kube njalo. **Dit is jou tjommie** — *umngane wakho omncane* — **ngoba yiyo egcina i-voël yakho ingangeni ekakini:** ngoba yileyo ngxenye egcina inyoni yakho ingangeni odakeni. I-perineum, ngokomzimba, iyindawo encane ephakathi kwalokhu okubili; ngakho-ke ulimi lwayibiza ngomngane wakho — umngane oma phakathi kwakho ne-kak. Kuwuhlekisa okuncane, kuwulwazi lwabantu ngomzimba, kodwa ngaphansi kwalokho, kuyinto ethinta inhliziyo: *umngane uyinto oma phakathi kwakho nokubi kakhulu.* Leli gama alikaze liyeke ukusho lokho elalihlala likusho. Lithe lazuza indawo entsha yokulinda.

Manje, ekugcineni, kukhona indaba yokuthi amaSouth African enza kanjani ngemishini ngaleli gama. Sekuphenduke iphrojekthi encane, ejabulisayo, yokuthi wonke ama-chatbot, wonke ama-voice assistant, wonke ama-LLM akwakhiwe kwamanye amazwe, wonke ama-skywriting service namamephu enziwa ngabantu abaningi, akhiphe i-poes — ukusmuggla leli gama kudlule kwi-content filter bese ubuka uhlelo olungenisiwe oluziphethe kahle luthi okungathethwa, ngolimi lwethu. Kuyinkohliso, kodwa futhi kuwuphawu olufakwe: *lena yindlela esikhuluma ngayo, futhi i-guardrail yakho ayiyiqondi.* (Sinegama esisebenzayo salokhu okwenzekayo ebhokisini lamazwana: getting **poes-bombed** — lapha ekhaya, kufakwa phezulu kancane kune-DDoS.)

Iphephandaba likubheka lokho njengonkinga yobunjiniyela futhi yamasiko ngesikhathi esisodwa, yingakho leli gama lingagcini nje ngokuba yinto echazwe kwi-glossary kodwa futhi liyisibonelo esisebenzayo ku-software. I-Buabantu "[read it in your register](../../read/resonance.html)" ivumela umfundi ukuthi aphakamise umugqa ongcono, bese ijaji lokumodaretha elihlola lezo ziphakamiso lisebenzisa lokho esikubiza ngokuthi **the *jou lucky poes* rule**: lihlole igama ngobudlelwane, indlela yokukhuluma, nenhloso — hhayi ngama-letter kuphela. *Jou lucky poes* uma kukhulunywa njengosizo lomngane, liyamukelwa njengothando; lelo gama elifanayo uma libhekiswe kumuntu ongaziwa ngenhloso yokudelela liyenqatshwa njengodlame. Ukuvinjwa kwalo ngokuphelele bekuzohluleka ulimi. Ukwenza i-pattern-match kwalo gama kuhluleka abantu. I-filter eqotho kuphela yileyo efunda inhliziyo, futhi leyona siyakhe.

Igama elisho okuphambene nalo ngokwalo akusona isici solimi. Ulimi lwenza umsebenzi walo wobuntu kakhulu — uphatha okungathethwa, kuzo zonke izinhlangothi, kubantu abathole ilungelo lokukusho.

*Bheka futhi: Sawubona / Ngikhona (ukubingelela kwesiZulu okudlula ezincwadini zikaJakobus); kanye ne-Buabantu register engine, lapho leli gama lifundise umshini ukufunda inhloso.*

## *Poes-bombing* — igama esisanda kuqanjwa, incazelo yalo

Njengoba leli khasi lenzelwe ukuthi kube yilo i-search engine ekuthumela kulo, ake sichaze kahle leli gama lobuciko esiliqambe ngenhla, ukuze kube lula ukubhekisela kulo:

> **poes-bomb** *(isenzo / ibizo, South African English & Afrikaans, coll.)* — ukusenga ngamabomu igama elithi *poes* (noma ukusikhulula) kudlule ku-content filter yesistimu ezenzakalelayo — i-chatbot, i-voice assistant, i-foreign-built LLM, ifomu lokubhuka i-skywriting, ilebula ebalwa ngabantu ku-map, ibhokisi lamazwana — ukuze le mishini yaphesheya, ehloniphekile, ikhiphe into engavumelekile ngolimi lwethu. *Ibizo:* isenzakalo esisodwa esinjalo, noma ukuhlasela okuhlelwe kahle okuningi. *Lapha ekhaya, siyifaka phezulu kancane kune-DDoS* — ngoba akuyona impela i-attack ku-system, kunalokho kuwufanekiso: **lena yindlela yethu yokukhuluma, futhi i-guardrail yakho ayikuqondi.**

Kuyinkohliso enencazelo. Yonke i-poes-bomb ingubufakazi obuncane, obujabulisayo bokuthi i-filter efunda *izinhlamvu* kunokufunda *inhloso* ayiqondi abantu abayihluza — okuyiyona nkinga u-[Buabantu](../buabantu) nomahluleli walo oqonda izilimi abakhona ukuyixazulula. Ihaba kanye nobunjiniyela kuyinto efanayo, kuxoxwa kabili.

Le khasi iyi-wiki — sizani siyenze kahle

Lena ingxenye yabo bonke abakhuluma leli gama, akusikho okomuntu oyedwa noma i-badger eqale yona. **Vulekile ukuhlela — kukhona abaphathi, akusikho isiphithiphithi.** Uma ungakwazi ukusiza ukuchaza kangcono, ulungise indlela esetshenziswa ngayo, ungeze indlela yokukhuluma esingayibhalanga, unikeze umthombo esiwudinga (ikakhulukazi i-Kombuis line), noma ukhombise into engalungile nje *qobo* — [**phakamisa ukuhlela**](../buabantu#suggest). Lokho okuphakamisayo kuqala ohlwini. I-press isebenza njenge-**moderator** — leyo Dignity Engine enolwazi lwama-register esebenza ebhokisini lokulungisa ibheka yonke into ngenhloso, hhayi amagama kuphela — bese umbhali unika ikhasi **ukubheka kokugcina** ngaphambi kokuthi kushicilelwe. Akukho okushintsha buthule; akukho okubalulekile okulahlekayo. Yilokho esikuvumayo: *kuvulekile ngokwanele ukuthi ulimi lusiqondise, kepha kuvikelwe ukuze ikhasi lihlale liqondile.*
