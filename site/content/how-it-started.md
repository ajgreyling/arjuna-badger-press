## How the house pays for itself (2026-06-20 pivot)

**Text is free.** Every finished book stays on [arjunabadger.press](https://arjunabadger.press) — read online, EPUB, PDF, no paywall. That is deliberate, not a placeholder.

**Why not the big ebook stores?** South Africa issues ISBNs free through the National Library — but the NLSA application site is broken, there is no working alternative, and buying commercial ISBNs for this catalogue is prohibitively expensive. Wide upload to Apple and the rest needs your own ISBNs. So the text ships here, free, out of spite if necessary.

**Revenue = real human audiobooks.** The business pivots to **Arjuna Audio**: paid audiobook editions narrated by **local voice artists** — the talent ACX and the usual royalty rails lock out. Not synthetic voice slop. Narrators get a transparent floor (**≥5% of net profit for ≥5 years** on qualifying projects) and can negotiate higher. [Become a narrator →](https://arjunabadger.press/narrators.html)

| What | Model |
|---|---|
| Ebook / PDF / read-online | Free on the press site |
| Human-narrated audiobook | Paid — artists share upside |
| Wide KDP / Apple / Kobo | On hold until ISBNs are sorted |

## What the $100 bought

A trilogy and its siblings — **twenty-eight titles on the live shelf**, **over a million words** in the curated corpus. A continuity engine that hard-gates fiction like production software. A self-editing loop that treats machine-tells as measurable defects. A publishing house with a face. A live reading site with parallel translation editions rolling out. **Text free on the site** because the ISBN gate failed; **revenue from human-narrated audiobooks** and the local voice artists the big platforms exclude. A thesis for giving most of the money to the artist and the door away free to the unheard.

The subscription was the chisel. **The discipline was mine.**
