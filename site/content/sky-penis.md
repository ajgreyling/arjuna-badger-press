# The Sky-Penis Files

*The world's shenanigans with human genitalia — the airborne branch*

There is a companion to [the *poes* entry](poes.html) that the badger could not leave alone, because
it proves the same thesis in a different sky. On the *poes* page we noted South Africa's small,
gleeful national sport of getting every imported machine to emit the unsayable — *poes-bombing.* It
turns out we are not special. Give a human being a blank canvas — a clear sky, a radar tracker, an
empty stretch of ocean — and a vehicle that can draw on it, and a certain percentage of humans will,
without fail, draw a penis.

It is one of the most reliable findings in the behavioural record. The medium changes — cave wall,
school desk, wet cement, contrail, GPS flight-tracker — and the impulse does not. This page documents
the airborne canon: the times military aviators, the most disciplined drawers of lines on Earth, used
the most expensive instruments ever built to draw the oldest graffiti there is.

A note on sourcing, because this page means to be quotable: **we separate what is documented from what
is merely reported.** The confirmed cases carry their sources. The rest are flagged honestly as
*reported, not yet verified* — a flying story is exactly the kind of thing the internet loves to
embroider, and an entry that prides itself on the truth cannot launder a good yarn into a fact.

## The confirmed canon

### The Whidbey Island Growler · 16 November 2017 *(documented)*

The founding text. A **US Navy EA-18G Growler** — an electronic-warfare jet of **Electronic Attack
Squadron 130, the "Zappers,"** out of **Naval Air Station Whidbey Island** — drew an unmistakable,
enormous phallus in clean contrail over **Okanogan County, Washington.** The crew, it later emerged,
believed the thick contrails would dissipate before anyone noticed. They did not. The shape hung
serenely in a clear blue sky for all of the county to enjoy.

The Navy's response set the template for every apology since: the act had **"no training value,"** it
was **"absolutely unacceptable,"** the crew would be **held accountable** — and Naval Air Station
Whidbey Island went so far as to send an **apology letter to a local school** whose pupils had seen
the drawing. The aviators received administrative, non-punitive discipline.
([CNN, 17 Nov 2017](https://www.cnn.com/2017/11/17/politics/navy-aircrew-obscene-skywriting) ·
[The War Zone — an oral history of the incident](https://www.twz.com/28016/an-oral-account-of-the-birth-of-a-sky-penis))

### The Finnish cadets on Flightradar24 · April 2026 *(documented)*

The most recent entry in the canon, and proof the impulse crosses every border. **Finnish Air Force
cadets**, flying a routine training exercise, coordinated their trainer aircraft so that the lines
they traced on public flight-tracking software resolved into a pair of giant penises over Finland.
Unlike a contrail, this art existed only on the radar map — a purely digital obscenity, drawn for an
audience of flight-tracker enthusiasts who, of course, screenshotted it instantly. The Finnish Air
Force confirmed the incident and disciplined the cadets involved.
([India Today, 22 Apr 2026](https://www.indiatoday.in/world/story/finnish-pilots-make-penis-shaped-flight-patterns-during-training-faces-disciplinary-action-2900068-2026-04-22) ·
[The Daily Beast](https://www.thedailybeast.com/pilots-facing-punishment-over-giant-penis-flight-patterns-in-finland/) ·
[Aerospace Global News](https://aerospaceglobalnews.com/news/finnish-trainee-pilots-phallic-flight-path/))

## Reported, not yet verified

These cases circulate widely in "sky-penis round-up" lists, but we could not independently confirm the
specific details (date, aircraft, squadron, location) against primary or mainstream reporting. We
print them as *claims*, plainly flagged, and we'd be glad of a solid source —
[send us one](feedback.html) and we'll promote the case to the confirmed canon, named and dated.

- **The Salton Sea Marine flight · October 2018** *(unverified — and doubtful).* Reported as a US
  Marine Corps **T-34C Turbo Mentor** of **VMFAT-101** out of **MCAS Miramar**, tracing a penis over
  the Salton Sea on flight-tracking software. We flag this one with extra caution: **VMFAT-101 is an
  F/A-18 Hornet training squadron, not a T-34C unit** — the aircraft-and-squadron pairing does not
  line up, which is exactly the kind of seam where a fabricated or scrambled story shows. Treat as
  unconfirmed.
- **The Tartus tanker · November 2022** *(unverified).* Reported as a US **KC-135 Stratotanker** of US
  Air Forces in Europe flying a holding pattern in the Eastern Mediterranean off **Tartus, Syria** —
  pointedly near the Russian naval base there — that on the digital tracker resembled a phallus aimed
  at the installation. The story has it that the USAF denied any intent and called the shape a "total
  accident." We could not confirm the date, aircraft, or any official statement.
- **The Coast Guard Hercules · July 2025** *(unverified).* Reported as a US Coast Guard **HC-130J
  Super Hercules** tracing two detailed phallic shapes over open ocean off the coast of Mexico, with
  leadership calling it "absolutely unacceptable" with "zero operational value." Given how heavily the
  2017 Navy incident was covered, the absence of comparable reporting for this one is itself a reason
  for caution. Unconfirmed.
- **The Luke F-35 coincidence · May 2019** *(unverified — claimed accidental).* Reported as two **F-35
  Lightning II** stealth fighters out of Luke AFB whose overlapping dogfight-training contrails *purely
  by coincidence* resembled genitalia, with the Air Force said to have reviewed the telemetry to prove
  it was an accident of standard maneuvering. Unconfirmed — and the most plausible-as-accidental of the
  lot, which is exactly why it's the easiest to invent.

## The reading

Why does a page at a South African press about a fearless badger care about American and Finnish
aviators drawing in the sky? Because it is the *poes* page's argument with the volume turned up. A
guardrail — a content filter, a flight regulation, a code of conduct, the dignity of an apology
letter to a primary school — cannot fully suppress the human urge to plant the crudest, most ancient
flag in the most pristine available space. The impulse is not malice. It is the same nerve that makes
*poes* a word of love between friends and a flag planted in a chatbot's output: **the refusal to let
a sanctioned, polite, expensive system have the last word.** It is mischief as a tiny act of freedom —
and it is, gloriously, everywhere.

We file the whole genre, as we file *poes-bombing,* somewhere just above a DDoS: not really an attack,
more a signature. *Humans were here. Humans will always have been here.*
