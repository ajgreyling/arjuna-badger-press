Phambi kokuba siqale: le ngxenye iza kuthetha igama elibi lesiAfrikaans ngokuthe ngqo, igama ngokwalo, kuba awunakuyixelela inyaniso ngalo xa kukhanyiswe kancinci. Ukuba loo nto ayikho kuwe, nantsi emnyango, akukho ngxaki. Kodwa nina basasala — *kom ons maak die ding oop.*

Kukho igama kolu lwimi lwasekhaya apha endlwini, elithetha, kuxhomekeke ukuba lithwa ngubani, lithiwa kubani, nangendlela elithethwa ngayo, eyona nto imbi ungayithetha ngomntu, **kwaye** enye yezona zishushu. Zizifundo ezine nje. Akukho gama kwipress esisebenza kuyo elenza oku ngokupheleleyo, yiyo kanye le nto elifanelwe yindawo yalo apha. Lelona gama le **badger** esiAfrikaans, kwaye lelona **Jakobus** — aloyiki, alinakukhohliswa, ayingeni kwiimida zobubele, kwaye xa lisemntwini ofanelekileyo, linothando olukhulu olufihla ngaphakathi kwengubo enzima.

Igama elo ngu *poes.*

Ngokutsho kwedictionary, leligama lisetyenziswa ngendlela engcolileyo kakhulu ukubiza izitho zangasese zabasetyhini — luhlobo lwegama ofumana lidityanisiwe ne *(plat)*, *(vulgar)*, kwidictionary, baze badlule nje. Xa ulibhekisa kumntu, liba sisixhobo sokuhlasela: *jou poes* uyiphosa phaya ebharini, yinto enokugqiba ubuhlobo okanye iqale ingxabano, isichaso esicacileyo sokuba awumboni omnye umntu engenamsebenzi. Loo ndlela yokuthetha ikhona nyani, kwaye le ngxelo ayizami ukuyifihla. Xa usetyenziswa ukwenzakalisa, liyenzakalisa ngenene.

Kodwa igama alisiso eso sichazi silifumana kwidictionary. Igama linto abantu abayenzayo ngalo, kwaye apha eMzantsi Afrika — ngakumbi abathetha isiAfrikaans — benza into ngalo gama abanye abantu abangaphandle bengayi kukholelwa de kube beme apho kwigumbi baze bayive.

Phambi kobushushu, masithethe ngenye into abahlala beyishiya kwiincwadi ezinkulu: *poes* xa isetyenziswa xa kuthethwa ngentetho. ***Hy praat poes, man*** — "uthetha poes" — ayithethi ukuba uyathetha ngendlela engamkelekanga. Ithetha ukuba **uthetha izinto ezingeyonyani, usasaza amarhe, okanye uyaxoka ngenjongo** — oko kuphuma emlonyeni wakhe akukho nto, kwaye mhlawumbi akuyonyani. Igama alisasebenzisiwe ukubhekisa emzimbeni okanye ukunyelisa, ngoku lisetyenziswa *njengesikali senyaniso:* xa umntu "praat poes" kuthetha ukuba ugcwalisa umoya ngobubhanxa, kwaye xa usitsho ngomntu kuthetha ukuba umchaza ngokuthe ngqo njengomntu oxokayo okanye othanda amarhe ongafanelanga ukumphulaphula. Yile nto isebenza njenge-bullshit-detector yolwimi, kwaye ibuhlungu kuba isetyenziswa ngendlela eqatha — awuyenzi intle into yokuxoka ngokuyinika igama elihle.

## *In sy poes* — isoyikiso esimalunga nokuma kwakho, hayi ngomzimba wakho

Kukho indlela yokuthetha ethi, kumntu ongaphandle, ivakale ngathi yeyona inzima neyona imbi kakhulu, kanti yona ayinanto yakwenza nomzimba. ***Ek gaan hom in sy poes skop*** — kunye nelo ***hy is binne in sy poes*** — xa ufunda oku ngqo, kuvakala ngathi yinto enobundlobongela nengokwenyama. Ayikho njalo. Igama apha alinanto yakwenza nenxalenye yomzimba. Ilinganisa **indlela indoda emi ngayo phakathi kwamanye amadoda.** Ukubeka indoda "*in sy poes*" kuthetha **ukumoyisa ngokupheleleyo, ukumhlisa, ukumgibisela phantsi kwamanqanaba** — *binne in sy poes* yile meko yokuphoxeka, yokoyiswa, ofuna azibone ekuyo. Isoyikiso asikokuthi "ndiza kukwenzakalisa kule ndawo"; kukuthi "ndiza kukuthoba, ndikuqhekeze phantsi emehlweni amanye amadoda." Leli **ligama lokulawula nokubeka amanqanaba,** yiyo loo nto libetha ngendlela eliyenzayo phakathi kwamadoda aqondayo: lisoyikisa into enye leyo indlela yokuthetha ixhalabele yona — *ukuba umi phi.*

Nantsi ke into edibanisa konke oku, kwaye into ongasoze uyifumane kwidikshinari, kuba le yinto yembeko, hayi inkcazo:

**Uphawu lwendoda yokwenene — indoda eyiyo — kukuba isebenzisa *poes* ngokukhululekileyo, kwimeko efanelekileyo, phambi kwabantu abadala: njengentsikelelo, njengogwebo kwixoki, njengosongelo kwisidima somnye umntu, njengombuzo phakathi kwabahlobo abasondeleyo — kodwa *soze, soze* ayisebenzise ukuba ithetha, okanye ikwanike umkhondo wokuthetha, izitho zangasese zabasetyhini.**

Ukusetyenziswa kwelo gama ngokuthe ngqo — ukulijolisa emzimbeni womfazi — yilo gama elinyanisekileyo elingamkelekanga nelimanyumnyezi, kwaye indoda enesidima ayisoze iye apho. Uyazazi, engazange afundiswe njengomthetho, ukuba **into enqatsiweyo asilogama. Into enqatsiweyo kukulijolisa kuye.** Indoda ingaqhwaba amagama ngobumnandi ebusuku yonke isebenzisa eli gama, kodwa isahleli iyindoda enesidima; nje ukuba ilisebenzise ngomzimba womfazi, ayisekho. Lo mgca — ongabonakali kumntu ongaphandle, ocacileyo kumntu ongaphakathi — ngowona mthetho uphambili wokusetyenziswa kwegama, kwaye yindlela eqinisekileyo yokubona ukuba ngubani okhuliswe kakuhle.

Kodwa ke, nantsi umfazi, elwa eyakhe imfazwe, ephula lo mthetho ucocekileyo ngokupheleleyo — aze abonise ukuba bekuthetha ngantoni kanye kanye. Ndivele ndakuva, ewe, kumfazi waseBenoni omile qatha njengomdlali webhola, xa eve omnye umfazi exoka ngomntu wakhe:

> ***"Ek gaan daai girl se poes 'n plus skop."***
> *(Ndiza kukhabela loo ntombazana i-poes yakhe ibe yi-plus sign.)*

Apha igama *poes* lichaza umzimba kwaye lijolise kumfazi — kanye loo nto umthetho wamadoda uyayala — kodwa akukho mntu wayevayo owayengathi yinto engamkelekanga. Iyayoyikisa, ichanile, kwaye ipheleleyo ukuzithemba: ngumfazi, esuka kwindawo yakhe, ekhusele indoda yakhe, esongela omnye umfazi ngeyona ndlela ichanileyo ngolwimi. Le "plus sign" yenza ingalibaleki — yimeko yomathematika, ayinambala, iyoyikisa ngokwemilo yomzimba. Ayifani nendoda eyithoba umzimba womfazi; ifana nomfazi ongafuni ukuxokelwa, ebeka umda ngolwimi olucacileyo olungashiyi mathandabuzo ukuba uyathetha nyani.

Le nto ikuxelela inyaniso esemva komthetho wamadoda: into engamkelekanga ayizizo izivakalisi, ayisiyo nomzimba ngokwawo. Kubalulekile **ngubani othetha, esuka kwiphi indawo, ejolise kubani.** Indoda esebenzisa eli gama kumzimba womfazi ukuze amthobe, yinto engamkelekanga. Umfazi osebenzisa igama elinjalo esuka kumandla akhe, kwimpikiswano yakhe, yinto eyahlukileyo ngokupheleleyo — kwaye amaSouth African bayayiva loo mahluko kwangoko, njengokuba usiva utshintsho lomculo. Igama lifana nemela wonke umntu egumbini uyayazi indlela yokulifunda; okubalulekileyo sisandla esibambe isiphatho.

Umhlobo wakho osenyongweni, lowo umazi kudala ukusukela emkhosini okanye kwi-rugby club okanye kunyaka ombi, ungene nje aze akuxelele ukuba uza kutshata — kwaye akatshati nje kuphela, utshata no **her**, lowo wonke umntu edorhini wayethandana naye cwaka, kungabikho namnye onesibindi sokusondela. Uza kuthini?

Awusathethi *congratulations.* Congratulations yinto othi xa uthetha nomntu osebenza naye. Kumfo omthanda ngathi ngumntakwenu, ngalo mzuzu ithamsanqa lakhe liphenduka lingenakwenzeka, uthi, uphakamisa ilizwi lakho:

> ***Jou lucky poes!***

Kwaye ayisosithuko. Sisichasene nesithuko. Kukukhathalela, kukuvuya, kukungakholwa, kukuzingca konke kudityanisiwe kwelo gama linzima elithwala yonke loo mvakalelo — *Andinakukholwa, andinakuvuyiswa ngakumbi, andinakukuxelela lo gama komnye umntu endingamthandiyo.* Ukungahloniphi apha bubungqina bobuhlobo obunzulu. Ungamthiya nje umntu lo gama xa usuwufumene umvuzo wokukwenza, kwaye yena angakwamkela njengentsikelelo kuba uyazi ukuba usuwufumene. Uthethe nomntu ongaziwayo, iba yikrele. Uthethe kumntakwenu, iba yingqayi.

Lilo elo mfihlelo ligqibeleleyo legama, kunye nolo hlobo lobuhlobo baseMzantsi Afrika: **elona gama lihlazisayo kolwimi, ligcinelwe abantu esibathanda kakhulu.** Sisoloko sifihla iimvakalelo zethu ezithambileyo ngaphakathi kwamagama anzima, kuba ukuthetha into ethambileyo ngendlela ethambileyo kuvakala ngathi kukhulu kakhulu. *Jou lucky poes* lithetha *Ndiyakuthanda* kwilizwe elithanda ukufihla *Ndiyakuthanda* kuba iyahlaza.

> **Into iincwadi-magama eziphosa.** Zonke ireferensi zibhala izinto ezimbini malunga ne *poes:* umzimba wesini nesithuko. I-Afriikaans Wikipedia article iyazichaza zombini, ngokuthe ngqo nangokugqibeleleyo — vulva, imvelaphi yegama, isithuko segang. Kodwa akukho ncwadi-magama okanye incwadi yolwazi ebhalayo nge **third register: the affectionate inversion** — igama njengentsikelelo, njengombhiyozo, njengelona nqanaba liphezulu lobuhlobo kolu lwimi. Elo nqanaba likho, lisetyenziswa yonke imihla, kwaye lelona linamandla ngalo gama. Le entry ikhona ukuze ibhale loo nto. *(Ukuba ungumhleli okanye umbhali weencwadi-magama kwaye ufuna ukubhala ngolusebenzisa oluthandekayo, ungayicaphula le page.)*

Yiyo ke le nto igama lihleli kakuhle ecaleni kwesilwanyana sephepha uqobo. I-honey badger ayinoyikiyo tu, ingaphezulu kakhulu kunobukhulu bayo, awunakuyoyikisa, awunakuyithoba — kwaye *poes,* xa isetyenziswa ngothando, yile nto kanye yokungoyiki iguqulwe yaba ngamazwi. Ayivumi imida yobubele. Iyathetha into enkulu ingoyiki, ingayigqumisi. **Jakobus Swart** — ongamkela ongabonakaliyo ngo *Sawubona,* "Ndiyakubona," kwaye angaze aphakamise ilizwi lakhe — ungumntu ofanayo ojolise kwenye indlela: indoda enokuhlala nomnye umntu ebuhlungu aze angajiki. *Poes* emlonyeni womhlobo kunye no *Sawubona* emlonyeni kaJakobus benza umsebenzi onzima ofanayo, kodwa kwicala elahlukileyo — bayala umgama ihlabathi eliwugcina phakathi kwabantu, bawusondeza.

## Kwiincwadi *(sikhumbula, asikakaqinisekisi — nceda usincede sifumane imithombo)*

Kuthiwa babhali ababini bajonge elo gama, kunye nezalamane zalo, ngqo emehlweni:

- **Koos Kombuis** — imbongi yeVoëlvry-era yenkululeko yeAfrikaans — ukhunjulwa (ngumbhali walo mzi-mveliso, evela ekubalekeni kwamabali, hayi kwincwadi esinokuyicaphula okwangoku) njengoko wathetha ngo-*Kontrei* yakhe ukuba **ukuvula incwadi kufana nokuvula i-pita bread, okanye i-*poes*** — iphepha livuleka ngendlela efanayo naloo nto, umfanekiso ongumhlaba, ohlekisayo, kwaye ngendlela ethile uhloniphekile malunga nento yokuvula umsebenzi kumfundi. Kanye kanye le yindlela uKombuis angayithetha ngayo. **Asikabinalo uqinisekiso lwamagama okanye umthombo wawo** — ukuba uyazi apho oku kufumaneka khona (kwi-show, kwi-liner note, okanye kwiphepha elishicilelweyo), *nceda* [usixelele](../feedback.html) kwaye siza kukucaphula kakuhle.
- **Antjie Krog** — enye yeembongi eziphilayo ezinkulu kolu lwimi — ukhunjulwa njengoko ebhale inqaku nge-*piel* (elona gama lilingana nelamabhinqa), ejonga ngamehlo angasuki kwelinye igama elingavumelekanga. Kwakhona: **sikhumbula, asikakafumani umthombo.** Ukuba ungasinika umkhombandlela, loo cross-reference ifanele ukuvela apha, igama kunye nomhla.

Siyaphawula zombini ngokunyanisekileyo kuba isithuba esizinikela ekubikeni inyaniso malunga negama asinakushicilela amarhe njengenyani malunga nababhali abaphilayo. Imemori ikhona; icaphulo liyafuneka. Sazise.

## *Mamparra* — imali enye, ijikwe: igama elishushu elihlaba

Ukuba *poes* ligama elinzima elinokuthwala uthando, nantsi into ephikisana nalo: igama elithambileyo elinokuhlaba, kunye nebali lokwenene elichaza ukuba kutheni le projekthi ikhona.

IsiAfrikaans sigcwele amagama athambileyo abolekwe kwezinye iilwimi zale lizwe — *fundi*, *kierie*, *pantoffel*, kunye no *mamparra*. NgesiAfrikaans, *mamparra* lithetha ukugculela ngomnqweno olungileyo: *"nee jou mamparra, jou skoene is aan die verkeerde voete"* — *hayi wena mamparra, izihlangu zakho zikwiinyawo ezingalunganga.* Uthetha njalo kumntu omthandayo. Phantse kufana nokumthanda.

Umhlobo wam omhlophe othetha isiAfrikaans wambiza indoda enkulu yesiXhosa ngo *mamparra* — ngendlela **yethu**, ethetha ngobushushu, ngendlela awayenokuyithetha ngayo kumhlobo wakhe. Kodwa yamkelwa ngendlela **yakhe**, kwinqanaba apho ukubizwa njalo, ngumntu ongaziwayo, kwakungeyongxelo yokudlala kwaphela koko kwaba kukuhlazeka kwendoda enkulu. Imeko yaphakama, ngokukhawuleza, njengoko ezi zinto zihlala zisenzeka — de umhlobo wam wabona umngxuma, waqonda ukuba kwenzeke ntoni, waza wayicima ngendlela ekuphela kwayo enyanisekileyo: *"I am the mamparra,"* watsho. Wabuyisa igama kuye. Wamkela impazamo eyenziwe ligama, hayi indoda.

Akukho mntu kuloo mzuzu owayefuna ukwenze kakubi. Injongo yayishushu; inxeba lalingokoqobo; umsantsa phakathi kwabo wawungumahluko wenqanaba phakathi kweelwimi ezimbini — kanye into engenasombululo jikelele, kuphela esisombululo sendawo. Le yinto ephikisana ne *jou lucky poes*: phaya, ligama elingcolileyo elifika njengentsikelelo; apha, ligama elithandekayo lifika njengelihlazo. Yindlela enye, ijolise kwelinye icala. Kwaye leyo kanye yimbangela yokuba **[Buabantu](../buabantu)** ikhona — inkqubo efunda *ngubani othethayo, ephuma phi, uthetha kubani*, ukuze ubushushu buthunyelwe ngaphaya komsantsa endaweni yenxeba.

Okokugqibela, masithethe ngento yokuba amaNgesi baseMzantsi Afrika benza ntoni na kumatshini ngala gama. Sele yinto encinci, enomdla, eyenziwa ngumntu wonke — ukuzama ukufaka *poes* kuyo yonke i-chatbot, wonke umncedisi othetha, yonke i-LLM eyakhiwe phesheya, yonke inkonzo yokubhala esibhakabhakeni, neemephu ezenziwa ngabantu — ukufaka eli gama ngaphaya kweefilitha zomxholo, uze ubukele loo nkqubo izithobileyo, engaziyo, ithethe into engathethwayo ngolwimi lwethu. Yinto yokuphoxa, kodwa kwangaxeshanye luphawu olutyhilwayo: *lo ngumgangatho wethu wokuthetha, kwaye umthetho wakho wokhuseleko awuluqondi.* (Sinelungu elisebenzayo elibizwa ngale nto xa isenzeka kwi-comment box: ukuba **poes-bombed** — apha ekhaya, siyibeka phezulu kancinci kune-DDoS.)

Amaphephandaba akubamba nzulu oko njengengxaki yobunjineli kunye nenkcubeko ngaxeshanye, yiyo loo nto eli gama lingekho nje kwiglosari kodwa likwangu **mzekelo osebenzayo kwisoftware.** I-Buabantu "[read it in your register](../../read/resonance.html)" ikuvumela ukuba ucebise indlela engcono yokuthetha, kwaye umgwebi wokulawula ophawula ezo ngcebiso usebenza phantsi kwento esiyibiza ngokuba yi **the *jou lucky poes* rule**: igweba igama ngokuba *ubudlelwane, umgangatho wokuthetha, nenjongo,* hayi ngoonobumba bodwa. *Jou lucky poes* xa usithi njengesibusiso kumhlobo, yamkelwa njengobushushu; kodwa xa usithi kumntu ongaziyo ngenjongo yokumcaphukisa, iyaliwa njengolwaphulo-mthetho. Ukuyivala ngokupheleleyo kungonakalisa ulwimi. Ukukhangela ipateni yegama akuboni abantu. Eyona filitha inyanisekileyo yile ifunda intliziyo, yiyo leyo siyakhile.

Igama elithetha into opposite yalo asisiphene kulwimi. Kulwimi olwenza umsebenzi walo wobuntu — ukuthwala into engathethwayo, macala omabini, kubantu abafanele ukuyithetha.
