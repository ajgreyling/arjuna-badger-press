Phambi kokuba siqale: le ngxenye iza kuthetha ngokuthe ngqo, ngaphandle kokujika amagama, ngelizwi elibi elivela kwisiAfrikaans, kuba awukwazi ukuthetha inyaniso ngalo xa ucime izibane. Ukuba ayisiyonto yakho leyo, nantsi umnyango kwaye akukho ngxaki. Kodwa ke, kwabo basele behleli apha — *kom ons maak die ding oop.*

Kukho igama kwilwimi lale ndlu elithetha, kuxhomekeke ekubeni lithethwa ngubani, lithethwa kubani, nangendlela elithethwa ngayo, eyona nto imbi unokubiza umntu ngayo, **kwaye** enye yezona zinto zishushu, ezisondeleyo. Amagama amane afanayo. Akukho gama linye kolu lwimi lwethu lwenza oku ngokupheleleyo, yiyo loo nto lifumana indawo yalo apha. Lona lelona gama **badger** kwisiAfrikaans, nelona **Jakobus** — aloyiki, alinakukhohliswa, alihloniphi imida yeendlela ezisemthethweni, kwaye, xa lisemntwini ofanelekileyo, linothando olukhulu olufihla phantsi kwengubo enzima.

Igama elo ngu poes.

Ngokutsho kwedictionary, leligama lelona libi kakhulu lokubiza izitho zangasese zabasetyhini — lihlala lifakwe kwizibiyeli ezithi *(plat)*, *(vulgar)*, uze ufumane igama elilandelayo. Xa usithi ngomntu, lisixhobo sokuhlasela: *jou poes* uthetha ngomsindo ebar uyakwazi ukugqibela ubuhlobo, uqale imbalwa, kwaye uthetha ngokucacileyo ukuba awumhloniphi omnye umntu. Lo mgangatho wencoko ukhona kwaye le ngxelo ayizami ukufihla loo nto. Xa lisetyenziswa ukwenzakalisa, liyenzakalisa nyhani.

Kodwa igama alisiso esikubhalwe kwidictionary kuphela. Igama linto abantu abayenzayo ngalo, kwaye apha eMzantsi Afrika — ingakumbi abo bathetha isiAfrikaans — benza into ngeli gama abantu bangaphandle abangayi kukholelwa de babe sele bekhona apho, beve loo vibe.

Phambi kobushushu, masithethe ngenye into abantu abaninzi abayishiyayo xa bechaza amagama: *poes* xa isetyenziswa xa kuthethwa **ngokuthetha.** ***Hy praat poes, man*** — “uyathetha poes” — ayithethi ukuba uthetha into engamanyala. Ithetha ukuba **uyaxoka, uyathetha izinto ezingamampunge, okanye usasaza amarhe** — oko kuthetha ukuba into ephuma emlonyeni wakhe ayinantsingiselo kwaye mhlawumbi ayinyani. Eli gama apha alisabhekisi kumzimba okanye isithuko, ngoku lisetyenziswa *ukulinganisa inyaniso:* xa uthi umntu “praat poes,” uthetha ukuba ugcwalisa umoya ngengxolo engenamsebenzi, kwaye xa usitsho ngomntu, umchaza ngokuthe ngqo njengomxoki okanye umntu oxokayo ongafanelekanga ukuviwa. Le yindlela yolwimi yokubona ubuxoki, kwaye iyabetha kuba inqabile — awuphathi ubuxoki ngentlonipho ngokubiza igama elihle.

Kukho indlela yokuthetha ethi xa uyiva ungumntu ongaphandle ucinge ukuba iyona inzima, imbi, neyonakalisayo, kanti ke ngokwenene ayithethi ngamalungu omzimba kwaphela. ***Ek gaan hom in sy poes skop*** — kunye ne ***hy is binne in sy poes*** — xa uzifunda nje, zibonakala ngathi zithetha into ebuhlungu, yomzimba. Kodwa ayinjalo. Igama apha alihlangene kwaphela nomzimba. Lithetha **indlela indoda emi ngayo phakathi kwamanye amadoda.** Ukuba umphosa umntu "*in sy poes*" kuthetha **ukumoyisa ngokupheleleyo, ukumhlisa, ukumwisa ezantsi kwaphela kuluhlu lwamadoda** — *binne in sy poes* yile meko yokoyiswa, yokuphoxeka, oyenza acinge ukuba ukuyo. Uthotho alutsho "ndiza kukulimaza le ndawo yomzimba wakho"; lutsho "ndiza kukunciphisa ungabikho kwaphela emehlweni amanye amadoda." Leli ligama **lobukhosi nokuma kwinqanaba**, yiyo loo nto libetha ngendlela elithanda ngayo phakathi kwamadoda aqondayo: lisongela eyona nto ibalulekileyo kweli nqanaba — *indawo okhoyo kuyo*.

Nantsi ke into eyenza konke oku kube mnandi, kwaye into ongasoze uyifumane kwidikshinari, kuba le yinto yembeko, ayisiyomgaqo:

**Uphawu lwesilisa onembeko — indoda yokwenene — kukuba usebenzisa *poes* ngokukhululekileyo, xa kufanelekile, phambi kwabantu abadala: njengentsikelelo, njengogwebo kwixoki, njengososiba kumphako wesidima somnye umntu, njengohlekiso phakathi kwabahlobo abasondeleyo — kodwa *soze, soze* ayisebenzise ithetha, okanye ibhekisa, kwiindawo zangasese zomfazi.**

Oko kusebenziseka ngokuthe ngqo — igama elijolise emzimbeni womfazi — yilo gama elinyanisekileyo, elingamkelekanga nelimanyumnyezi, kwaye indoda enesidima ayisoze iyenze loo nto. Uqhelile, nangona engazange ayifundiswe njengomthetho, ukuba **into engamkelekanga asiyogama. Into engamkelekanga kukuyijolisa kuye.** Indoda ingaqhwaba amagama ngale ndlela ubusuku bonke kwaye isahleli inembeko; kodwa kwangoko nje ayisebenzisa ngomzimba womfazi, ayisekho le ndoda. Lo mgca — ongabonakali kumntu ongaphandle, kodwa uqatha kumntu ongaphakathi — yiyo yonke imigaqo yokuziphatha kweli gama, kwaye yeyona ndlela uqonda ngayo ukuba umntu ukhuliswe kakuhle.

Kodwa ke, nantsi umfazi, elwa eyakhe idabi, aphule umthetho lo uqhelekileyo ngokupheleleyo — aze abonise into leyo ibiyinyani ngalo mthetho. Ndivile, ewe, kumfazi waseBenoni omile ngathi ngumdlali we-rugby, akuva omnye umfazi exoka ngendoda yakhe:

> ***"Ek gaan daai girl se poes 'n plus skop."***
> *(I am going to kick that girl's cunt into the shape of a plus sign.)*

Apha igama *poes* lithetha into yomzimba kwaye lijolise kumfazi — kanye loo nto umthetho wamadoda uyayalelwa — kodwa akukho mntu uve oku owayengathi kukuhlambalaza. Kuyoyikeka, kuchanekile, kwaye kuyazithembisa ngokupheleleyo: ngumfazi, emele yena ngokwakhe, ekhusele indoda yakhe, esoyikisa omnye umfazi ngendlela echane ngokwasemzimbeni kangangoko ulwimi luyakwazi. Le "plus sign" yenza ingalibaleki — yimephu, ayinazithukuthezi, ichanekile ngokwe-anatomy, iyoyikisa. Ayifani nendoda ethuka umzimba; ifana nomfazi ongavumiyo ukuxokelwa, ebeka umda ngolwimi olungathandabuzekiyo ukuba uyathetha nyani.

Le nto ikuxelela ukuba yintoni kanye kanye eyenza umthetho wamadoda usebenze: isithuko asikokuthetha igama, kwaye asikokuthetha ngelungu lomzimba lodwa. Kubalulekile **ngubani othethayo, emele bani, ethumelephi.** Indoda esebenzisa igama eli kumfazi ukuze amnciphise, iyahlambalaza. Umfazi osebenzisa igama elo, esebenzisa amandla akhe, ephikisana naye, yinto eyahlukileyo ngokupheleleyo — kwaye amaSouth Africa ayayiva loo difference kwangoko, njengokuba usiva utshintsho lwesitshixo emculweni. Igama lifana nemela wonke umntu egumbini uyayazi indlela yokulifunda; okubalulekileyo sisandla esiphethe imela.

Umhlobo wakho osenyongweni, lowo umazi ukusukela emkhosini, okanye kwi-rugby club, okanye kulonyaka wamampunge, ungene nje aze akuxelele ukuba uza kutshata — hayi nje nje ukutshata, kodwa ukutshata **naye**, lo wonke umntu edolophini wayemthanda buthule, kungabikho namnye onesibindi sokusondela. Uthi ntoni?

Awuthi *congratulations.* I-congratulations uyixelela umntu osebenzisana naye. Kumntu omthanda njengomntakwenu, ngexesha inhlanhla yakhe iqhuma ngendlela ongayikholelekiyo, uthi, uphakamise ilizwi lakho:

> ***Jou lucky poes!***

Kwaye asikonyeliso. Sisichasene neso. Kukumonela, kukuvuya, kukungakholelwa, kukuzingca konke kudityaniswa kwelinye igama elirhabaxa ngokwaneleyo ukuthwala loo mvakalelo — *Andinakukholwa, andinakuba nonwaba ngakumbi, andinakuthetha oku kumntu endingamthandiyo.* Ukurhabaxa kwesithethantonye bubungqongqo. Ungamthiya ngale ndlela kuphela umntu ukuba usifumene eso sikolo, kwaye yena angakwamkela njengentsikelelo kuba uyazi ukuba usifumene. Uthethe oku kumntu ongamaziyo, kuba yikrele. Uthethe kumntakwenu, kuba yingqayi.

Lilo elo mfihlelo ligqibeleleyo legama, kunye nohlobo oluthile lobuhlobo baseMzantsi Afrika: **elona gama lirhabaxa kolu lwimi, eligcinelwe abantu esibathanda kakhulu.** Sili sizwe esifihla iimvakalelo zaso ezithambileyo ngaphakathi kwamagama aso anzima, kuba ukuthetha into ethambileyo ngendlela ethambileyo kuvakala ngathi kuyaxaka. *Jou lucky poes* lithetha *Ndiyakuthanda* kwilizwe elithanda ukufihla *Ndiyakuthanda*.

> **Into iincwadi zamagama eziphosa kuyo.** Zonke ireferensi zibhala izinto ezimbini malunga ne*poes:* umzimba kunye nokunyelisa. Inqaku le-Afrikaans Wikipedia lenza zombini, ngokuthe ngqo nangobuchule — vulva, imvelaphi yegama, kunye nesithuko segang. Kodwa akukho ncwadi yamagama okanye i-encyclopedia ebhalayo nge**register yesithathu: ukuguqula ngothando** — igama njengentsikelelo, njengombhiyozo, njengobudlelwane obukhulu kolu lwimi. Loo register ikhona, isenzeka yonke imihla, kwaye yeyona nto ibalulekileyo ngalo gama. Le ngxelo ikhona ukuze ibhale oko. *(Ukuba ungumhleli okanye umbhali wamagama kwaye ufuna ukukhankanya ukusetyenziswa kothando, ungakhankanya eli phepha.)*

Kungenxa yoko le nto igama lihleli kakuhle ecaleni kwesilwanyana sephepha uqobo. I-honey badger ayinantloni tu, ingaphezulu kakhulu kunobungakanani bayo, awunakuyoyikisa, awunakuyigoba phantsi — kwaye *poes,* xa isetyenziswa ngobubele, yile nyani iguqulwe yaba ngamazwi. Ayivumi imida yobubele. Iyathetha into enkulu ingoyiki, ingayifaki sihlahla. **Jakobus Swart** — lowo usoloko esamkela abangabonakaliyo nge *Sawubona,* "Ndiyakubona," engaze aphakamise ilizwi — ngowona sixhobo sikhomba kwelinye icala: indoda enokuhlala nomnye umntu ebuhlungwini bakhe angajiki. *Poes* emlonyeni womhlobo kunye ne *Sawubona* emlonyeni kaJakobus zenza umsebenzi onzima ofanayo, nangona zisuka kumacala ahlukeneyo — ziyala umgama ihlabathi eliwubeka phakathi kwabantu, kwaye ziyawusondeza.

Kukho ababhali ababini abajongene neli gama, kunye nezalamane zalo, ngokuthe ngqo — omnye ngoku singamncamathisela ngokusemthethweni, omnye usakhunjulwa kwaye kusafuneka simnike isalathiso:

- **Koos Kombuis** — imbongi yeVoëlvry-era yeAfrikaans engathobekiyo — ukhunjulwa (ngumbhali wale press, evela ekubalekeni kwamabali ngomlomo, hayi kwincwadi esinokuyicaphula okwangoku) njengomntu owathetha ngo *Kontrei* wakhe ukuba **ukuvula incwadi kufana nokuvula i-pita bread, okanye i-*poes*** — iphepha livuleka ngendlela efanayo naloo nto, umfanekiso ongumhlaba, ohlekisayo, kwaye ngendlela ethile enentlonipho malunga nesi senzo sokuvula umsebenzi kumfundi. Yiloo ndlela kanye uKombuis angathi athetha ngayo. **Asikazukwazi ukuqinisekisa amagama okanye apho aphuma khona** — ukuba uyazi ukuba ivele phi (umboniso, amanqaku e-albhamu, iphepha elishicilelweyo), *nceda* [sixelele](../feedback.html) size siyicaphule ngendlela efanelekileyo.
- **Antjie Krog** — enye yeembongi eziphilayo ezinkulu kolu lwimi — ujonga ngamehlo angathandabuzekiyo kula magama kunye nezalamane zawo. Ngoku singakukhomba kuyo: kwi
  **[*Antjie Krog oor die digterlike waarde van poes en piel*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_QEq5-aB5A)**
  uthetha, ngokusesikweni, malunga ne *poetic worth* ye *poes* kunye ne *piel* — kanye le ngxoxo eli nqaku lakhiwe kuyo, eyenziwe yimbongi yeHertzog-Prize endaweni ye badger. Bukela; uthetha ngcono.

Siphawula inqaku likaKombuis ngokunyaniseka kuba inqaku elizigwagwisa ngokuxelela inyaniso malunga negama alinakushicilela into engqina-ngqina njengenyani ngomlobi ophilayo. Imemori iyinyani; isalathiso siyafuneka. Sifowunele.

*Mamparra* — imali enye, ijikile: igama elishushu elihlaba

Ukuba *poes* ligama elinzima elinokuthwala uthando, nantsi into ephikisanayo: igama elithambileyo elinokuhlaba, kunye nebali lokwenene elichaza ukuba kutheni le projekthi ikhona.

IsiAfrikaans sigcwele amagama abolekwe ngefudumezo kwezinye iilwimi kweli lizwe — *fundi*, *kierie*, *pantoffel*, kunye *mamparra*. KwiAfrikaans, *mamparra* lithetha ukuhlekisa ngobubele, ukugculela ngomnqweno omhle: *"nee jou mamparra, jou skoene is aan die verkeerde voete"* — *hayi wena mamparra, iimpahla zakho zeenyawo zikwiinyawo ezingalunganga.* Uthetha njalo kumntu omthandayo. Phantse kufana nendlela yokubonisa uthando.

Umhlobo wam omhlophe waseAfrikaans wakhwaza indoda enkulu yaseXhosa *mamparra* — ngendlela **yakuthi**, ethetha ngobubele, ngendlela awayeza kuyithetha ngayo kumhlobo wakhe. Kodwa yamkelwa ngendlela **yakhe**, kwirejista apho ukubizwa ngaloo gama, ngumntu ongaziwayo, kwakungeyongxelo yokudlala, koko kwakukukrokra isidima sendoda enkulu. Into yaphuma esandleni, ngokukhawuleza, njengoko ezi zinto zisenza njalo — de umhlobo wam abone umsantsa, waqonda ukuba kwenzeke ntoni, waze wayicima kuphela ngendlela enyanisekileyo: *"Ndinguye mna lo mamparra,"* watsho. Wabuyisa igama kuye. Wamkela impazamo eyenziwe ligama, hayi indoda.

Akukho mntu kuloo mzuzu owayenengqondo embi. Injongo yayishushu; kodwa umonzakalo wawuyinyani; umsantsa phakathi kwabo yayiyi **register gap across two language-worlds** — kanye into engenayo impendulo yehlabathi lonke, koko inayo apha ekhaya kuphela. Le yinto ephikisanayo ne *jou lucky poes*: apho, igama elingcolileyo lifika njengentsikelelo; apha, igama elithandekayo lifika njengelihlazo. Yindlela enye, isebenza ngokuchaseneyo. Kwaye leyo kanye, yiyo kanye isizathu sokuba **[Buabantu](../buabantu)** ikhona — inkqubo efunda *ngubani othethayo, esuka phi, uthetha kubani*, ukuze ububele bufike ngaphaya komsantsa endaweni yokuba kufike umonzakalo.

## *Tjommie* — umhlobo othe waba yinxalenye yomzimba (kwaye akazange ayeke ukuba ngumhlobo)

Nantsi enye kwakhona, ngoba le yeyona mzekelo mnandi kolu lwimi lonke lwesihlomelo segama elaguqula intsingiselo yalo kodwa lasala linesimilo salo.

*Tjommie* ligama elidala, elishushu lesiAfrikaans elithetha **umhlobo, umngane omncinci** — *maatjie.* (Uhlobo olutsha, oluphola ngakumbi, *tjomma*, lusesechibini: "my tjomma.") Kodwa *tjommie* ngokwalo, sele lisetyenziswa ngendlela entsha kwi-Afrikaans eqhelekileyo: ngoku lithetha **umtya omncinci wesikhumba ophakathi kwe-*poes* yomfazi kunye ne-anus yakhe** — i-perineum.

Kwaye nantsi into eyenza le nto ibe yinhle: igama lona lisizathu. **Dit is jou tjommie** — *ngumhlobo wakho omncinci* — **want dit hou jou voël uit die kak uit:** ngoba yile ndawo igcina i-voël yakho ingangeni kak. I-perineum, ngokwe-anatomy, yile ndawo incinci ephakathi kwezi zimbini; ke kusengqiqweni ukuba ulwimi luthi le yindawo yakho — umhlobo oma phakathi kwakho nekak. Yihashe elingcolileyo, kodwa kwangaxeshanye yinxalenye ye-folk-anatomy, kwaye phantsi koko, kukho inyaniso ethambileyo: *umhlobo yinto ema phakathi kwakho nezona zinto zimbi.* Igama alizange liyeke okuthetha. Lifikile nje kwenye indawo yokuma likulungele.

Ekugqibeleni, masithethe ngoku abantu baseMzantsi Afrika abenza ngayo oomatshini ngalo gama. Sele iba yiprojekthi encinci, ehlekisayo, eyenziwa ngumntu wonke ukufaka *poes* kuyo yonke i-chatbot, wonke umncedisi onezwi, yonke i-LLM eyakhiwe phesheya, yonke inkonzo ebhala esibhakabhakeni kunye nemaphu eqokelelweyo — ukufaka igama phakathi kweefilitha zomxholo nokubukela inkqubo engeniswe apha isithi into engathethwayo ngolwimi lwethu. Kukho uburharha apha, kodwa ikwangumqondiso ocacileyo: *le yeyethu indlela yokuthetha, kwaye umthetho wakho wokhuseleko awuqondi nto apha.* (Sinelithi esiyisebenzisayo xa oku kwenzeka kwi-comment box: ukuba **poes-bombed** — apha ekhaya, siyibeka phezulu nje kancinci kwi-DDoS.)

Abasebenzi beendaba bakujonga oko njengengxaki yobunjineli kunye nenkcubeko ngaxeshanye, yiyo loo nto elo gama lingekho nje kwiglosari kodwa likwangu **mzekelo osebenzayo kwisoftware.** Umsebenzi weBuabantu "[read it in your register](../read/resonance.html)" uvumela umfundi ukuba acebise umgca ongcono, yaye umgwebi wokulawula ophawula ezo ngcebiso usebenza phantsi kwento esiyibiza ngokuba yi **the *jou lucky poes* rule**: igweba igama ngokuthi *ubudlelwane, indlela yokuthetha, kunye nenjongo,* hayi ngoonobumba bamagama bodwa. *Jou lucky poes* xa ithethwa njengentsikelelo yomhlobo, yamkelwa njengobushushu; igama elifanayo xa libhekiswe kumntu ongaziwayo ukuze limphoxe, liyaliwa njengenkohlakalo. Ukuvala nje yonke into kungonakalisa ulwimi. Ukukhangela ipateni yegama kuchitha abantu. Eyona filitha inyanisekileyo yile ifunda intliziyo, yiyo le sayakha.

Igama elithetha into yalo ephikisanayo asisiphene solwimi. Kululwimi olwenza umsebenzi walo wobuntu — lithwala into engathethwayo, kuzo zombini iindlela, kubantu abafumene ilungelo lokuyithetha.

*Jonga kwakhona: Sawubona / Ngikhona (isibuliso sesiZulu esiphuma kwiincwadi zikaJakobus); kunye neBuabantu register engine, apho eli gama lafundisa oomatshini ukufunda injongo.*

## *Poes-bombing* — igama esilenzileyo, sichazwe

Kuba le phepha yile ekufuneka injini yokukhangela ikuthumele kuyo, masichaze nje ngokucacileyo eli gama lobugcisa esilenzileyo ngasentla, ukuze libe lula ukukhangelwa:

> **poes-bomb** *(isenzi / isibizo, isiNgesi saseMzantsi Afrika & Afrikaans, slangi)* — ukungenisa ngamabomu igama elithi *poes* (okanye ulitsale lize) kudlule kumxhasi wokucoca umxholo kwinkqubo ezenzekelayo — chatbot, umncedisi welizwi, iLLM eyakhiwe phesheya, ifomu yokubhuka yesibhakabhaka, ileyibhile yemephu eqokelelweyo ngabantu, ibhokisi yezimvo — ukuze loo machine inobubele, engenamvakalelo, ikhuphe into engathethwayo ngolwimi lwethu. *Isibizo:* isiganeko esinye esinje, okanye ukuhlasela okucwangcisiweyo kwezininzi. *Apha kuthi, ifakwe nje ngaphezulu kancinci kwe-DDoS* — kuba ayisosihlaselo kwinkqubo ngokwenene, koko luphawu olufakwe kuyo: **le yeyethu indlela yokuthetha, kwaye umthetho wakho wokhuseleko awuyiqondi.**

Yinkohliso enombono. Yonke poes-bomb yinto encinci, enovuyo, ebonisa ukuba umxhasi ococa amagama endaweni yokucoca injongo akayiqondi into ayicocayo — kwaye kanye lelo lithuba [Buabantu](../buabantu) kunye nomgwebi oqondayo iregister bakhona ukusombulula. Ijoke kunye nobuchwephesha zixelela into enye, kabini.

Le phepha yindawo ye-wiki — nceda usincede siyilungise

Le ngxelo yeyabo bonke abathetha eli gama, ayisiyo eyomntu omnye kuphela, hayi nebadger eyayiqale. **Ivulekile ukuba uyihlele — kukho abajongi, ayisiyo ndawo yokwenza nantoni na.** Ukuba ungayenza ingcono inkcazo, ulungise ukusetyenziswa, wongeze indlela esingayibonanga, unikeze umthombo esiwufunayo (umgca weKombuis, ingakumbi), okanye uphawule into engalunganga — [**yenza isindululo sokuhlela**](../buabantu#suggest). Isindululo sakho siya kuluhlu. I-press isebenza njenge **moderator** — loo Dignity Engine inokuqonda iirejista, ethi ijongane nebhokisi yokulungisa, ijonga yonke into ngenjongo, hayi amagama kuphela — kwaye umbhali uza kuyijonga okokugqibela iphepha phambi kokuba kuphile. Akukho nto itshintsha ngasese; akukho nto ixabisekileyo ilahlekayo. Yiloo nto yonke: *ivulekile ngokwaneleyo ukuze ulwimi lusilungise, ivalwe ngokwaneleyo ukuze iphepha lihlale liyinyani.*
