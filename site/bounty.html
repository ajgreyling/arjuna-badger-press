The rules

  1. Tell us where and why. Point to the book and the place (chapter, line, quote). Say why it's wrong — a source, a correction, the real story.
  2. First valid report wins it. If two people find the same thing, the earlier one gets the prize. Duplicates are logged.
  3. Your submission grants us the right to use your fact. Your finding can be incorporated into the final edition. You'll be credited in the sources.
  4. One reward per distinct issue. A list of ten typos is one continuity-tier reward, not ten. Genuinely separate findings each pay.
  5. We adjudicate in the open. Every find is judged publicly with our reasoning. Your name or handle goes in the sources.
  6. Good faith only. We ignore spam, AI-generated noise, and "gotchas" that aren't real.
