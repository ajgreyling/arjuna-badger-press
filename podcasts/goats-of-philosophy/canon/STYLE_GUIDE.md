# The GOATs of Philosophy — Style Guide

## What this actually is
Not a transcript of a real episode. A **fictional parody script** in the shape of a Joe Rogan
Experience episode, written for someone who wants the *idea* of that episode without a co-host
committing 30+ hours to actually writing 25,000-40,000 words of dialogue. This project holds:
1. A full beat-sheet/outline for the entire imagined episode (`OUTLINE.md`).
2. One fully-scripted excerpt (~2,500 words) from partway through the long guest conversation, so
   the voice and rhythm actually exist on the page, not just as a summary.

## The real-person disclaimer (hard gate, same family as the living-author rule elsewhere in this
catalog)
**Alex O'Connor is a real, living philosopher and podcaster** (host of *Within Reason*, formerly
*The Cosmic Skeptic Podcast*; ~750k+ YouTube subscribers as CosmicSkeptic). This script puts
invented dialogue in his mouth. That is only defensible if the views voiced are **consistent with
his actual, well-documented public positions**, not invented shocking claims used for cheap
entertainment. Verified public positions used as the floor for his dialogue (sourced 2026-07-30):
- **Agnostic atheist**, self-described, but publicly on record as "increasingly drawn to the figure
  of Jesus" and suspicious that truth can be "exhausted by argument alone" — not a stock New Atheist.
- **Free will skeptic / hard incompatibilist**: argues that authorship over one's own actions is
  impossible whether the universe is deterministic or random; other senses of "freedom" (freedom
  from external constraint) may exist but don't grant authorship.
- **Emotivist about morality** (self-described), while remaining a serious, rigorous ethical debater.
- **Moved away from strict materialism** toward taking consciousness/idealism seriously as an open
  problem, without fully adopting idealism — publicly describes himself as "freaked out" by the hard
  problem of consciousness.
- **Former vegan**; no longer vegan for personal/practical/health reasons, but remains critical of
  factory farming and now favours legislative/systemic reform over individual-consumer boycotts.
- **British republican** (anti-monarchy), publicly on record including in conversation with Piers
  Morgan.
- Hosts long-form conversations with guests including Richard Dawkins, David Deutsch, Sam Harris,
  Jordan Peterson, Carlo Rovelli, and Slavoj Žižek — i.e. someone already fluent in exactly the
  register a JRE-style long-form conversation requires.
No line in this script is a real quote. Every position voiced should trace back to one of the above.
If a future pass wants him to take a stance not listed here, verify it first or flag it clearly as
invented rather than let it read as documented fact.

## Joe Rogan (voice)
Genuinely curious, not performing stupidity — asks the naive-sounding question that's actually the
load-bearing one. Physical, sensory register ("that's wild," "100%," "have you ever done DMT
though"). Loves an idea that reframes something he thought was settled. Brings in tangents (MMA,
hunting, comedians, elk meat) not as deflection but because that's genuinely how his mind associates.
Never mean, never trying to win — trying to *get* it.

## Jamie Vernon (voice)
Mostly silent, occasional dry one-word confirmations ("There it is." / "Yep." / "That's the one.").
The producer pulling things up on screen when named. Never a philosophical position of his own in
this script — he's texture, not a debater.

## Structure discipline
- The eight historical GOATs are **outline only** in this project — a beat-sheet of what a real
  10-minute Joe-and-Jamie riff on each would hit, not scripted dialogue. Fully scripting all eight
  would roughly double the project's scope for content that's inherently more repetitive than the
  main event.
- The long guest segment is **outline plus one fully written excerpt**, picked from deep enough into
  the imagined conversation (post free-will, pre-DMT) that it reads like a real mid-conversation
  groove rather than a cold open.
