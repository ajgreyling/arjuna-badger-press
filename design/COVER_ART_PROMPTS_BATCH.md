# Cover art prompts — the books still needing house-style covers

> **House style = the cinematic, painted/photoreal scene** of the History Before Time covers (e.g.
> *The Calendar of Stone*): a rich, atmospheric landscape with a focal subject, golden-hour or moody
> light, depth, and a small human/figure for scale — then the gold serif title typeset on top. These
> are generated as ART (no text) in ChatGPT / Midjourney / DALL·E, then I typeset + install them.
>
> **How to hand them to me:** generate each at **2:3 portrait**, **no text baked in**, save the file,
> and tell me the path (e.g. `~/Downloads/whatever.png`). I crop to 1800×2700, typeset the title in
> the house lockup, install in every cover slot, and **commit** (covers must be committed or they
> deploy as a black placeholder).
>
> Leave a darker band at the **top** (for the series eyebrow) and the **bottom** (for the title).
> Shared palette: deep teal-to-black sky, warm African/earth gold, bronze/ochre. End MJ prompts with
> `--ar 2:3 --style raw --v 6`.
