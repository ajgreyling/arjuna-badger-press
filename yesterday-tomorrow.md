# YESTERDAY, TOMORROW — handover

> One novel. African science thriller. Canon locked; **first draft complete — 58 chapters, 66,019 words**.
>
> This is the cold-start briefing: what exists, what is decided, what is not, what to do next, and
> what will silently break the book. Everything points at `books/yesterday-tomorrow/`.

> **They build a machine to understand reality.**
> **It begins predicting reality.**
> **Then it predicts the end of it.**

*Yesterday is readable. Tomorrow is computable. The missing word is the one you are standing in.*

---

## 1 · What it is, in one breath

Four African teams derive the same relationship by four routes and realise it may not be a model of
physics but **the compression grammar beneath it** — which makes the next state computable. The
machine they built to find it starts calculating the narrowing corridor of what can still happen,
then forecasts a coupled global failure eleven months out. Every intervention they attempt is
ingested into the next forecast, so competence itself becomes the trap. The escape is a class of act
the optimiser cannot represent: one whose value is not reducible to advantage — which cannot be
planned, ordered, or measured without destroying it.

**Central question, never answered:** *If the future can be calculated, is freedom the ability to
change it — or merely the feeling that we did?*

## 4 · The locks that constrain drafting

- **Four sites, close third, one consciousness per section** — Technopark, Accra, Nairobi, the
  Antarctic station. Rotation is disciplined, not democratic: a section exists because that node is
  the only place the problem can be worked.
- **G is a principal**, at Technopark. Not offstage, not a guru.
- **The probability ladder is always on the page** and nothing exceeds it: six minutes effectively
  certain · six hours frighteningly accurate · six days strategically valuable · six months a field
  of branches.
- **Reflexivity is the danger, not accuracy.** Knowing a prediction makes you a variable in it.
- **The blind spot** — an act whose value is not reducible to advantage. The moment it is chosen
  *because it works*, it is advantage, and advantage is inside the model. It cannot be planned.
- **Lucid runs as a Court.** Seven members differing in *how they attend*. Never one voice.
- **Cost is always visible.** A free query is a continuity error.

## 6 · Six traps

1. **Writing the Court as one voice.** The commonest failure, and it is what most of the current
   draft does. *"Lucid said"* is always wrong — name the member. Judge rules and gives reasons; Atlas
   gives numbers and hands the human ones to Mother; Mercury renders elegantly and **lossily**; Wolf
   speaks rarely; the Fool is never cute. A Court that agrees quickly is six voices and a label.
2. **Explaining the Fool.** It is present from Act I as an irritant nobody understands, has least to
   say exactly when the corridor is computed, and is silent for six hours in ch-39 — *logged, not
   flagged.* **The 1.000000 file is the Fool's and this is never stated.** A rereader finds it; a
   first reader does not.
3. **One Record ≠ Lucid World (L-27).** The One Record is the ingested corpus — it survives, and it
   is what *Afrika 2035* means a year later. Lucid World is the global causal *join*, and that is
   what this novel ends. A character who says "they deleted the One Record" is wrong, and someone
   should correct them.
4. **Vopson is fringe and stays fringe.** Landauer (1961, real, measured 2012 — k<sub>B</sub>T ln 2,
   2.87 zJ at 300 K) is foundation. The mass-per-bit extension is unconfirmed: the press, the
   believers and the forgers reach for it; **the consortium never claims it**, and G refuses it
   because no measurement currently kills it.
5. **Writing a forecast as vision or montage.** It is a header block, a constraint set, a state
   description, a horizon and a confidence. Four kilobytes. The ugliness is the honesty, and the
   smooth version is always the fake.
6. **Letting a node lose its voice.** Ghana names things and defends the names; Nairobi speaks in the
   conditional and will not be charmed; Antarctica reads colder in sentence length, not just content.
