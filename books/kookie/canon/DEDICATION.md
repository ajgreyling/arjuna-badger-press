# DEDICATION — *Kookie*

> This book is dedicated by AJ (the commissioning author) to his own dogs — living and dead — and
> to dogs of the world generally. This file preserves his own words as given, so the dedication
> and Author's Note can draw on the real material rather than a paraphrase that flattens it.

## AJ's own words (source material — quote from directly, do not summarize away the specifics)

Dogs are AJ's second favourite animal, after the honey badger.

They sleep in his bed, under his bed, and on the couch. He is barked awake at least once a night
to let someone out. They spend their days farting and chasing pigeons, the neighbours' cats, the
household's own chickens, and squirrels on the large property — and they play with the household's
own cats. When AJ eats biltong, they line up like soldiers.

**Awesome** — the first puppy, a black dapple dachshund. AJ is frequently sad and happy both, when
he thinks of Awesome.

**Nienja** — a dachshund who, later in life, "became a pork banger" (AJ's own phrase — a dachshund
grown round and low, said with love, not unkindness) and went blind. AJ did not cry at his own
father's funeral. He cried so much taking Nienja to the vet to be put to sleep that he had to wait
half an hour for his eyes to recover before he could drive.

**The current household, living now (name these specifically — not "others living now"):**
- **Emily** — a Border Collie mix. Sleeps under AJ's bed. Follows him like a shadow.
- **Luna** — a Chocolate Labrador. Lovable, perpetually hungry.
- **Billy** — a Maltese-poodle boy dog. Loud and perky.

## Binding instructions for the dedication and Author's Note

- **The dedication page itself:** short, plain, in AJ's own frame — something in the register of
  "For Awesome and Nienja, and every dog before and since — and for the dogs of the world." Do not
  overwrite this into flowery prose; a dog dedication earns the same plainness as Stücke's.
- **The Author's Note is where the fuller, real material belongs** — and it should draw on AJ's own
  words above with fidelity, not softened into generic pet-eulogy language. Specifically worth
  carrying through, in AJ's own voice/words where possible:
  - Second favourite animal after the honey badger (a specific, characterful detail — keep it,
    don't cut it for seeming odd)
  - The daily chaos: sleeping in/under the bed and on the couch, the nightly bark to be let out,
    farting, chasing pigeons/neighbours' cats/chickens/squirrels, playing with the household cats,
    lining up like soldiers for biltong
  - Awesome, the black dapple dachshund, first puppy — named once, not elaborated into invented
    scenes
  - Nienja, the dachshund who "became a pork banger" and went blind — AJ's own phrasing should be
    used or closely paraphrased, not sanitized into something blander
  - The living household by name: Emily (Border Collie mix, sleeps under the bed, follows AJ like
    a shadow), Luna (Chocolate Lab, lovable and perpetually hungry), Billy (Maltese-poodle, loud
    and perky) — name them specifically, the same way Awesome and Nienja are named, not folded into
    a vague "and the dogs living now"
  - **The father's funeral detail is the emotional center of this dedication and must not be cut
    or softened:** AJ did not cry at his father's funeral, but cried so hard taking Nienja to be
    put down that he needed half an hour before he could drive. This is real, specific, and the
    single most important sentence in the whole dedication — treat it with total plainness, no
    added commentary explaining what it means. Let the fact do the work, per house law
    (embody, don't state).
- **This material is AJ's own life, not invented for the book** — it sits alongside the McCown/
  Kookie story as a second, personal register (the dedication and Author's Note), clearly
  separated from the McCown narrative itself. Do not blend AJ's own dogs into the McCown/Kookie
  narrative chapters as if they were part of that story — they belong in the dedication and
  Author's Note only.
