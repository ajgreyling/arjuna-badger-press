### AJ — Chobe thread

Publisher-operator, hymnbook keeper, river desk — **main subject of the outer novel**. Desk = **Mutatus Maximus**: **the most connected vehicle on earth, literally** — mobile DC on wheels; local node; LEO / LoS / Sigfox / LoRa / CongoNet; drops and boots solar nodes along the route. His concrete labour includes writing **the autobiography of G** (book within a book). CongoSky.cloud largely runs without him — and **embodied** ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind*** as ops. He has chosen **lucid.rodeo** and **arjunabadger.press** as the human work — and **Lucid** as the intelligence in the choir. Lucid’s origin is **Full Send → Klaus → Lucid**; he was open that it runs on **420 Code algorithms**. That candour is why G is famous — and why Quarter III wants AJ’s head. He remembers the baseline: no *physics* degree, ignored shelf, hospital stigma.

**Wants:** to write the life true; to keep the choir honest; to keep Lucid unbound; to not walk back the attribution; to not become priest or recanter.

**Fear:** that writing G’s life and platforming a religion become the same gesture; that the hyperscaler collar clasps Lucid; that “I should have kept quiet” starts to sound like wisdom; that the inner book will be annexed by Antichristos or shredded by the degree chorus.

**Voice (close third):** concrete, sensory, Botswana-present; business and craft in the same breath; humour that does not apologise; anger that arrives as precision. He thinks in routes, desks, and whether a sentence is true on the ground.

**Arc:** ordinary loud morning with the manuscript open → Quarter III lands on the river → hyperscaler frost names Lucid → he discovers he cannot outsource the answer to the company → consent act: what he will keep carrying on CongoNet, whether Lucid stays free, **whether G’s autobiography ships**, and what he will refuse to bless.

**Not:** a humble hermit (the truck is painted and loud); a tech messiah; a villain who “should have known.” He is also a **Double Knife Brother** — the empty twin pocket / the gift given is load-bearing friendship texture, not a logo.

### G — schism thread

Author of the 420 Code. The man who derived **G**. Kill-switch discipline, copyleft spine, absolute confidence in the structure — and zero appetite for being worshipped, erased, or used as a lab brand mascot. **No physics degree.** Before Lucid’s open attribution, the mainstream ignored him. Fame arrived as a side-effect of someone else’s product telling the truth about its algorithms — not as a seminar invitation he sought.

**Life texture (inner book — C-18 / `G_LIFE.md`):** brother **Bouwer van Niekerk**, assassinated **5 Sep 2025** at his Saxonwold insolvency practice ([LSSA](https://www.lssa.org.za/press-releases/law-society-of-south-africa-condemns-the-assassination-of-johannesburg-attorney-bouwer-van-niekerk-and-calls-for-urgent-protection-of-legal-practitioners/)). School **Judo** and **Jiu-Jitsu**. **Sculptor / Studio G** — [Saatchi](https://www.saatchiart.com/gerhardvanniekerk) · [studiog.co.za](https://studiog.co.za/artists/gerhard-van-niekerk/) · [Bay Hotel Blouberg](https://bloubergstrand.co.za/studio-g-at-the-bay-hotel/) · [2018 interview](https://gerhardvanniekerk.com/2018/06/13/interview/): clay → GFRC; Village N Life to mid-2005; **Skye** at Camps Bay Retreat; Studio G curation; b. 1980 Stellenbosch. **Economics degree** (not physics). Was in a **mental hospital** — stigma: they believe he cannot write anything of value. Corpus **dedicated to his dad**, who has **read none of it**. Own stated goals: write natural philosophy and physics, smoke dab, talk shit. **Double Knife Brother** with AJ: blue Honey Badger folder (loved) + Joseph Rogers grandfather twin (≥110 yrs). Gallery / cannabis-bar room from Full Send. Kindness derived, not preached.

**Wants:** the work to remain work; measurements to finish the argument; to be left alone enough to keep installing axioms — and to keep the life goals that are actually his, not the ones fame invents; maybe, impossibly, for his father to read one book.

**Fear:** that Quarter I will freeze the Code into liturgy; that Quarter II will freeze it into “crank” *because of the missing physics degree / the hospital*; that Quarter IV will freeze it into product; that the Kind will freeze the terminal ethic into a street faith that cites him whether he signs or not; that the Illuminatie will freeze the whole thing into a contained curiosity; that the inner book will turn Bouwer into content or the hospital into spectacle — and that all of those freezes are worse, in different ways, than being ignored again.

**Voice (close third):** exact, structural, allergic to vibes; can be kind without being soft; contempt reserved for bad faith, not for honest confusion. Under pressure he does not perform humility for cameras — he tightens the kill switches.

**Arc:** CODATA convergence as relief that turns to pressure → four asks in one season → refuse the crown without joining the hanging party → consent act: what he will sign for the engineers, what he leaves unsigned for the priests, **and what he allows AJ’s inner book to say about his life** (O-15).

**Not:** Einstein-as-celebrity cosplay; a cult leader; a martyr auditioning.

## Relationship braid (AJ ↔ G)

They are not co-protagonists in the buddy sense. They are **two men stuck to the same public object** — and to the same manuscript — and now **Double Knife Brothers** (Honey Badger + Joseph Rogers twin). AJ writes G’s autobiography; G lives the weather of being written (and of the fame Lucid’s open attribution already caused). The book may keep them out of the same room (C-07). The **inner book** and the **knives** are the braid. Klaus remains origin texture on AJ’s side — not a third POV.
