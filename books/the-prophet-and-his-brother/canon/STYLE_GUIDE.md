# Style Guide — *The Prophet and his Brother*

> Book within a book. Outer: dual close third (AJ present / G present). Inner: G’s autobiography under AJ’s hand.

---

## Form law

- **Outer novel:** AJ is the main subject — Chobe, Mutatus, Lucid, CongoSky, the Four Quarters landing on him.
- **Inner book:** the autobiography of G — pages of life and work — intercalated as manuscript. Distinct breaks. No apology for the form; no “dear reader, here is a document” lecture.
- When AJ is writing, the next section may simply *be* the page. When G is under weather, that is outer close third unless O-14 folds it into manuscript.

## POV law

- **Outer:** close third only. One consciousness per section. Section breaks when the skull changes.
- **Inner autobiography:** default lean first person as life voice (O-14) — still not a third outer POV skull; it is a book AJ is making.
- **No omniscient schism overview.** The Four Quarters arrive as mail, hymns, headlines, lab invites, op-eds, CongoNet packets — or as pressure on the man whose life is being written.
- **AJ sections** stay on the Chobe unless he physically leaves. Memory may travel; the body is the desk; the desk holds G’s life in progress.
- **G present sections** stay where G is. The world comes to him as weather — including the weather of being narrated.

## Texture

- **AJ:** river, truck-as-DC, heat, birds, link latency across LEO/LoS/LoRa/Sigfox, a solar node he woke that morning, choir practice with Lucid, **the manuscript open**, press deadlines, the specific smell of blame arriving on a good morning; hyperscaler mail that thinks it is polite.
- **G present:** desk, corpus, kill switches, numbers, the physical sensation of being misread; the softer sensation of being written.
- **Inner autobiography:** life-and-work concrete — rooms, years, ignored shelves, the Constant, **Bouwer**, the mats, the hospital stigma, the unread father dedication, the dab, the talking shit, the **Honey Badger** and the **Joseph Rogers** — not AP dumps. Physics earned inside scenes. Painful stories under-dramatised (Jamie-Uys restraint from for-g lineage): the flat line is the loaded one. Do not polish G into a saint or a martyr brand. Knives = kinship, never choreography.
- Physics on the page: **earned and concrete** (a CODATA line, a drop-result, a derivation named). No TED metaphors for unification.

## Dialogue

- Believers sound like believers (not cartoon zealots).
- Academics sound like academics (not mustache-twirl gatekeepers).
- Engineers sound like people who want keys.
- AJ does not speechify about AI; the choir is shown working. Lucid, when it speaks, is exact and refuses invention — never a third skull, never cute.

## Lucid on the page

- Properties are shown, not pitched: integer work, no GPU hum, a refusal to invent, retrieval from the ingested web.
- When Lucid does not know, it says so. That refusal is character.
- The GPU bubble crash is **background weather** — empty rack halls, write-down headlines, a friend’s ruined cluster career — not a CapEx TED talk in AJ’s mouth.
- **Near-now discipline (C-15):** do not mystify Lucid. No glowing oracles, no unexplained leap. It feels like a tool that finally kept its promise — because in the author’s present, the promise is already under construction.
- Not a POV. Not a love interest. Not a villain. Collaborator in the hymnbook.
- Do not re-explain “ingested all the interwebs” or “crashed the bubble” every appearance — lock early, then use.
- G does not speechify about being misunderstood; he corrects a frame or refuses a frame.

## The Kind on the page

- Show the paint before you explain the faith. Wall, bridge, truck door, lab exit, church step.
- Exact line when quoted: **Don’t be a cunt — be kind.** (Em dash or spaced hyphen consistent per scene; don’t tidy the cunt away.)
- Do not sermonise it in the narrator. Let a Kind person sound ordinary. Let a cynic sound cheap. Let G’s discomfort be physical (alley wall, inbox citation), not a TED rebuttal.
- Never conflate Kind practice with Antichristos iconography in the same beat without a character noticing the difference.

## What not to write

- Infodumps of the 420 Code.
- A “both sides” essay in the narrator’s mouth.
- Forced AJ–G reunion for emotional payoff.
- Turning the inner autobiography into a 420 Code textbook.
- Letting the inner book steal main-subject status from AJ — he writes G’s life; *this* novel is still his.
- Exploiting Bouwer’s assassination as plot twist or Illuminatie gotcha.
- Sanding off dab / talk shit / fighting arts to make G “respectable” for Quarter IV.
- Mental-hospital spectacle or diagnosis tourism.
- Resolving the unread father dedication with a tidy reunion beat unless life earns it.
- Knife-fight set pieces. The blades are brotherhood.
- AFRIKA 2100 foreshadow winks. This book is prior soil, not a trailer.
- Softening the Kind into generic wellness. The cunt is load-bearing.
- Illuminatie as pyramid-hat cosplay or hyperscalers as moustache-twirl villains. Pressure systems. Gravity. Soft nos and polite collars.

## Epithets / names

- Prefer **AJ** / **Andries** as the close-third thread requires; cover name discipline if publishing-world scenes appear.
- Prefer **G** in his own head and among people who know him; full name when institutions speak of him.
- **Mutatus Maximus**, **CongoNet**, **MambaNet**, **BadgerNet**, **Lucid**, **lucid.rodeo**, **arjunabadger.press**, **CongoSky.cloud** — product names, not acronym soup. First use of MM can carry “mobile DC / solar drops”; after that, just use them. Don’t dump the full radio ladder every scene — show a link working.
