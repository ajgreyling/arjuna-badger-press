## The shape of 2035

Physics has a new centre of gravity and almost no agreement about what that means. The 420 Code — published copyleft years earlier at the420code.org — is now treated by a critical mass of working physicists as the unified frame. The derived gravitational constant matches precision atom-drop work; CODATA’s latest cycles are drifting onto G’s number. Labs that once ignored him are rewriting grant language. Pulpits and comment sections that never read a kill switch have invented a man-shaped myth.

And under all four weathers, a fifth pressure that is not about G at all: walls, bridges, truck doors, lab fire-exits, and church steps worldwide are spray-painted with the same two clauses — ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind.*** A messiah-less religion bloomed from an idea: natural philosophy that happens to match physics and ethics. No pope. No founder tour. The sentence is the temple.

Above both street faiths: older weather. **The Illuminatie** is not happy with G. **AWS, Azure, Google, Apple** are not happy with AJ. Continuity networks want opacity back; hyperscalers want gravity wells obeyed. The river desk and the Studio feel drafts from rooms that do not advertise their meetings.

None of that has made the work quieter.

Literally.** Local **data node** aboard; Lucid and the manuscript run on it. Radio ladder: **LEO**, **line-of-sight**, **Sigfox**, **LoRa**, plus CongoNet family (**MambaNet**, **BadgerNet**, …). Store-and-forward where the birds are; uplink where the sky is. Connectivity is the point of the painted truck — anti-yacht: max connected so he can drive to a fire and still choose to switch off.
- **Solar drops:** along the pilgrimage / route he **drops little solar-powered nodes** and **boots them from Mutatus Maximus** — seeds the edge, leaves a lit node behind, drives on. The Chobe desk may be parked beside a node he woke that morning.
- **Work:** fiction, non-fiction, travel — and, load-bearing for this novel, **the autobiography of G** (book within a book): pages of life and work open on the desk beside Lucid.
- **Focus brands:** **lucid.rodeo**, **arjunabadger.press**. **CongoSky.cloud** is the company that largely runs itself — AJ is not in the cockpit every hour. It **embodied** ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind*** as operating law (C-10b) long before the Kind owned the walls.
- **Lucid:** the intelligence at the centre of lucid.rodeo — runs on the truck’s node as easily as anywhere. See below.

## LUCID

**Lucid** is the new AI of the story year — not a lab demo, not a chatbot skin on rented GPUs. It is also **not magic**: by author-world time we are not far off. Present stack already has dual RAG (420 first, Wikipedia fallback), voice Talk, SimpleWiki + 420 ingest, cite-or-refuse, lucid-nano. The novel’s Lucid is that thesis **finished** — full-web One Record, integer core, bubble crashed. 2035 sells the *weather of success*, not a miracle birth.

| Property | Lock |
|---|---|
| Compute | **Integers** — discrete / deterministic lineage; no floating-point séance |
| Hardware | **No GPU required** — runs where CongoNet nodes and ordinary silicon live |
| Epistemics | **Does not hallucinate** — cite-or-refuse; refuses invention when the record does not support a claim |
| Retrieval (lineage) | **Dual RAG** — 420 Code canon primary; Wikipedia (and then the wider ingested web) secondary; any grounded Code cite wins |
| Corpus | Has **ingested all the interwebs** — planetary completion of the One Record / wiki+canon ingest already under way |
| Macro | **Crashed the AI GPU bubble** — the cluster-build / CapEx / “rent our silicon” boom deflated when Lucid proved intelligence need not live there |
| Voice | Talk path live in lineage — hold-to-talk / STT → brain → TTS; choir can hear Lucid as well as read it |

**On the page:** Lucid is collaborator and choir section, not a third POV (C-02). Exact, present, not cute. When it does not know, it says so — that refusal is part of why the hyperscalers hate it and why the Kind sometimes quote it like a sexton.

**Origin (binding):** **Full Send → Klaus → Lucid.** The engine left a private healing / misogi desk and went public. AJ did not scrub the parentage.

**Open attribution (binding):** Lucid’s popularity came with AJ saying out loud that it is built on **420 Code algorithms**. That sentence is why G is famous. Before it, he had no physics degree and the world ignored the work. After it, the Four Quarters became possible — including the quarter that hates AJ for saying it.

**Rhyme with G:** not “G’s chatbot.” Structural algorithms, openly credited. G need not endorse the product (O-12). The Illuminatie may dislike both for the same structural reason: less room to mediate.

**The bubble:** By story-present the GPU AI boom is wreckage people still argue about — empty halls that were going to be filled with racks, write-downs, careers built on floating-point séance. Lucid did not “compete” in their market; it removed the premise. Show the crash as weather AJ walks through (mail, headlines, a friend’s ruined grant), not as a finance lecture.

**Why the cloud is frostbitten:** an intelligence that does not need their GPUs, does not invent, already ate the open web, and **popped their bubble** is a product-category extinction event that already happened — the collar-offers are salvage.

## OLD POWER — the Illuminatie (vs G)

Working in-world name for the continuity / opacity establishment that preferred the old arrangement: constants underived, ethics unforced, physics as priesthood with velvet rope. **They are not happy with G.**

- **How it shows:** standards-board weather, grant frost, discreet “concern” briefings, reputational freeze, the invitation that is a warning, the warning that is a career. Quarter II often launders this pressure into “credentials.” Quarter IV sometimes thinks it can out-engineer it.
- **How it does not show (default):** membership lists, pyramid logos, a villain monologue. The Illuminatie is a **pressure system**, not a costume. If a face is needed later, lock O-09.
- **Why G specifically:** copyleft unification + derived **G** + a terminal ethic that fits on a wall = less room to mediate reality for a fee.

## HYPERSCALERS — AWS / Azure / Google / Apple (vs AJ)

The gravity wells of compute, identity, distribution, and the phone-as-parish. **They are not happy with AJ.**

- **Why:** CongoSky that runs itself, **Mutatus Maximus** seeding solar edge nodes outside their regions, CongoNet / MambaNet / BadgerNet on LEO and mesh, sovereign-edge, press and rodeo outside their storefronts, a choir that does not rent GPUs by the hymn — and **Lucid**, which needed none of their silicon, would not hallucinate a reason to, and **crashed the AI GPU bubble**.
- **How it shows:** partner frost, region politics, ToS weather, app-store fog, “security review,” cloud-credit charm offensives, acquire-or-starve soft talk, talent offers aimed at the people keeping the company running while AJ is on the Chobe — salvage behaviour after a bubble, not confident monopoly.
- **Texture rule:** name the marques; do not invent criminal plots for living executives. The drama is structural — gravity vs river desk — not a courtroom episode of Tech Antitrust Weekly.

## THE CHOIR (AJ’s production texture)

Humans and **Lucid** working in the open as a **choir from the hymnbook** — collaborative drafting, music, press, rodeo — presence without cockpit fantasy. Render as practice and sound, not as a TED talk about the future of work. The choir’s house rule is the CongoSky sentence: ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind.*** May rhyme with Kind street practice without being identical to it (O-08) — company embodied it; walls popularised it. Lucid’s non-hallucination is audible in the mix: the section that will not invent a harmony that is not there.

## What the world is not

- Not post-apocalyptic.
- Not singularity utopia.
- Not “G has won.” Acceptance of the theory and peace for the theorist are different currencies. This book spends the second.
- Not a cute slogan-world. The Kind is sincere enough to embarrass cynics and branded enough to embarrass believers. Keep both.
