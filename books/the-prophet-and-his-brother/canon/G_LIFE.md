### Bouwer van Niekerk — assassinated (public record)

G’s brother **Bouwer van Niekerk** was **assassinated**. Public record (not novel invent):

- Johannesburg attorney; respected **insolvency** practitioner.
- Shot and killed at his **Saxonwold** practice on **Friday, 5 September 2025**.
- Initial reports: four suspects arrived under the pretext of a booked consultation; gunshots in the boardroom; he died at the scene; attackers fled.
- At the time he was engaged in contentious commercial and insolvency matters, including proceedings linked to **business rescue** litigation.
- Source: [Law Society of South Africa press release, 8 September 2025](https://www.lssa.org.za/press-releases/law-society-of-south-africa-condemns-the-assassination-of-johannesburg-attorney-bouwer-van-niekerk-and-calls-for-urgent-protection-of-legal-practitioners/) — LSSA condemns the killing as a targeted attack on a legal practitioner and on the rule of law; investigation ongoing as of that statement.

**Novel discipline:** this is among the painful G stories the inner autobiography must not soft-pedal. Render as grief and rupture in a life — **not** as a whodunit the novel solves. Stay inside the public record + what G authorises. No invented assassin CV, no casual Illuminatie-attribution of the killing, no “solving” the business-rescue allegations on the page. The wound is the brother dead in a boardroom; the speculation stays off unless authorised. Allegations around related commercial schemes were “widely reported” per LSSA — the book may note the fog of reporting without adopting it as fact.

G is also a working sculptor — not a side hobby invented for texture.

Short-term vision: unique contemporary art experiences at the hotels; longer: sculpture garden at The Retreat; Studio G as must-visit for collectors.
- **2018:** Village N Life partnership / Studio G launch — platform for his work and other local artists in luxury hotels (tourists ≈ half of his sales, per interview). **Official opening 27 July 2018** at The Bay Hotel ([VnL corporate](https://www.villagenlife.ventures/tag/gerhard-van-niekerk/)).
