## One breath

The year is 2035. G’s 420 Code is worldwide accepted as the unified theory of physics. He has derived the gravitational constant **G**; atom-drop experiments and the latest CODATA releases are converging on his number. AJ sits on the edge of the Chobe River in northern Botswana, in **Mutatus Maximus** — **the most connected vehicle on earth, literally**: painted mobile data centre on wheels; local node; LEO / line-of-sight / Sigfox / LoRa / CongoNet; dropping little solar nodes along the way and booting them from the truck — writing fiction and non-fiction and travel books, and the autobiography of G. CongoSky.cloud mostly runs itself — and it **embodied** ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind*** as operating law before the Kind owned the spray cans. He is leaning into **lucid.rodeo** and **arjunabadger.press** — and into **Lucid**, the new AI that runs on integers, needs no GPU, does not hallucinate, has ingested all the interwebs, and **crashed the AI GPU bubble**. Humans and Lucid dance and sing like a choir from his hymnbook. Walls everywhere are spray-painted ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind*** — a messiah-less religion that bloomed from natural philosophy matching physics and ethics, needing no founder to keep spreading. **The Illuminatie** is not happy with G. **AWS, Azure, Google, Apple** are not happy with AJ — Lucid popped their boom; the frost is salvage. And G is drowning in a planet that has divided into four equal quarters around him — belief, contempt, blame, and gospel — none of which will let him work in peace.

## High concept

> *A book within a book: AJ on the Chobe is the main subject, writing the autobiography of G — the pages of his life and work — while Lucid (Full Send → Klaus → product, openly 420 Code) has already made that degree-less ignored physicist famous, crashed the GPU boom, and split the planet into four camps; the Illuminatie freeze G, the hyperscalers salvage-freeze AJ, and neither desk can un-platform what measurements, graffiti, and a manuscript in progress have already done.*

It reads like near-future literary dual-POV with an intercalated autobiography — autofiction-adjacent on the AJ thread, life-and-work pages for G, pressure-cooker on G-present under schism. The climax is not a proof (the proof is already landing in CODATA). The climax is custody: what AJ will keep writing and platforming; what G will sign, leave unsigned, and allow said about his life.

## Why this book exists

AFRIKA 2100 without human texture was boring. This is the human decade: the loud vindication, the religion, the crank chorus, the platform-rage, the labs that quietly retool, the integer mind that finished arriving. Write the lived 2035 first — close enough to now that Lucid is extrapolation, not fantasy. Rebuild 2100 later from what this book makes true.

## The engine — the Four Quarters

The planet’s opinion of G (and, by adjacency, of AJ) has split into four roughly equal camps. The book treats them as **weather systems**, not cartoon factions — each gets its best argument on the page.

| Quarter | Name (working) | Stance | What it wants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I — Faith** | *Hail the Antichristos* | Full belief → new religion | Liturgy, pilgrimage, G as eschatological figure |
| **II — Academy** | *No Degree* | Crank — no *physics* degree (he has economics/adjacent); hospital stigma laundered as epistemology | Erasure, gatekeeping, “not physics” / “cannot write anything of value” |
| **III — Platform** | *Who Gave Him the Mic* | Angry at AJ for platforming G — especially via open Lucid→420 attribution | Retraction, apology, AJ’s head on a stick |
| **IV — Gospel** | *Rework the Stack* | Physics as operational truth | CERN / applied projects rebuilt on 420code rails |

G’s struggle is that all four are *true for someone*, and the measurements that should settle the fight only intensify it.

## Parallel weather — the Kind

Not a fifth quarter *about G*. A **messiah-less** religion that bloomed from an idea: natural philosophy that happens to match physics and ethics. Its liturgy is public and cheap — spray paint, stencils, CongoNet packs — and its whole scripture fits in two clauses: ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind.***

- **Antichristos** wants a man.
- **The Kind** wants a sentence.

The Kind can embarrass both the crank chorus (who call it graffiti theology) and Antichristos (who keep trying to put G’s face above the words and getting painted over). AJ may already be living next to it in the hymnbook choir. G may find that the one faith that does *not* need him is the hardest to refuse ownership of.

## Origin spine (how fame happened)

> **Full Send → Klaus → Lucid → open “built on 420 Code algorithms” → G famous.**

Author-present truth carried into the fiction: G has no physics degree; people ignore the work. The novel does not pretend the academy discovered him. Lucid got popular fast; AJ refused to launder the algorithms; the ignored shelf emptied into the Four Quarters. Quarter III is specifically angry at *that* candour. Quarter II still says *no degree* as if the attribution never happened.

## What we do not do

- Make G a saint or a cartoon crank. He is a working mind under weather.
- Make AJ a villain for platforming or a hero for retreating. He is a publisher-operator who chose a desk by a river.
- Resolve the Four Quarters into one correct camp. The book ends with the weather still blowing; only the two men have decided what *they* will do inside it.
- Gate scenes on AFRIKA 2100 continuity.
