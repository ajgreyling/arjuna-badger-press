## LOCKED

- **C-01 · Title & year.** Title **LOCKED:** *The Prophet and his Brother*; story year **2035**. Former working title *The Four Quarters* retained as the name of the schism engine only (C-04 — the four camps).
- **C-02 · POV.** Dual **close third** on the outer novel. Two threads only as principals: **AJ** (Andries J. Greyling) and **G** (Gerhard van Niekerk). **AJ is the main subject of the book** — the A-thread gravity. No omniscient overview of the schism; the quarters are encountered through what each man sees, hears, and refuses.
- **C-02b · Book within a book.** Formal structure, binding: the outer novel is AJ on the Chobe in 2035; inside it runs **the autobiography of G** — the manuscript AJ is writing, which is **the pages of G’s life and work**. Effectively **a book within a book**. The reader receives both: river-desk present (outer) and life/work pages (inner). The inner book is not a paste of Artist’s Proofs; it is autobiography — lived years, desks, kill switches, the ignored shelf, the Constant landing — under AJ’s hand, with Lucid in the choir. AJ does not vanish into ghostwriter anonymity: he remains the main subject of *this* novel while authoring *that* one. The braid: **AJ writes the life; G lives the weather of having a life written** (and of the fame Lucid’s open attribution already caused).
- **C-02c · How the inner book appears.** Inner-autobiography sections are typographically / structurally distinct from outer AJ present (chapter heads, manuscript cues, or clear section breaks — house craft, not gimmick). Outer AJ may be caught mid-sentence of the inner book; the next section may *be* that page. G’s present-tense schism weather may still appear as outer close third on G (co-principal under pressure) **or** bleed through what AJ is writing that day — lock mix in O-14. Default lean: **both** — outer G-present for 2035 weather; inner autobiography for life-and-work deep pages.
- **C-03 · Physics state.** By 2035 the 420 Code is **worldwide accepted** as the unified theory. G has **derived the gravitational constant G**. Atom-falling / precision drop work and **latest CODATA** are **converging** on his derivation. Vindication is measurement, not manifesto. G’s formal credential is **economics (or adjacent)**, not physics (C-18) — the “crank without degree” quarter means without a *physics* degree.
- **C-04 · The Four Quarters.** Global opinion (texture-level, not census) splits ~¼ each: (I) *Hail the Antichristos* faith; (II) crank-without-degree academy; (III) platform-rage at AJ; (IV) physics-gospel engineers reworking CERN / applied stacks on Code rails. All four are live weather; none is “defeated” by the last page.
- **C-05 · AJ’s desk.** Northern Botswana, **Chobe River**. He writes from **Mutatus Maximus** — matatu-inspired overkill overlander; anti-yacht; Beast as tender if present; and, load-bearing, a **mobile data centre on wheels**: **the most connected vehicle on earth. Literally.** Onboard: **local data node** + radio ladder (**LEO**, **line-of-sight**, **Sigfox**, **LoRa**, CongoNet / MambaNet / BadgerNet kin). Along the route he **drops little solar-powered nodes** and **boots them from MM** — seeds the edge, drives on. Focus: **lucid.rodeo** + **arjunabadger.press** + **Lucid** (C-14). **CongoSky.cloud** largely runs itself — and **embodied** ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind*** as company operating law (C-10b). Humans + Lucid as **choir from the hymnbook**. Near-now: rhymes Klaus edge vision (`In Mutatus Maximus` traveling DC + high-site solar nodes) — 2035 is the practice finished at pilgrimage scale.
- **C-06 · G’s struggle.** G is not enjoying vindication. The four weathers press him simultaneously. He does not want a religion named after him or against him; he wants the work to stay work.
- **C-07 · Braid rule.** Threads may share a week and a theme; they need not share a room. CongoNet / the Code / public weather are the allowed bridges. No forced reunion beat.
- **C-08 · Consent climax.** Each thread ends in a **consent act** (what to platform / what to sign), not a battle and not a proof. The Constant is already landing; the question is custody of meaning.
- **C-09 · Living-person discipline.** AJ and G are rendered as fiction principals drawn from life. No invented crimes. Private pain stays private unless AJ has put it on the record elsewhere. Antichristos is satire of reception, not a hit on any living faith community’s doctrine as doctrine.
- **C-10 · The Kind (messiah-less ethic-religion).** Parallel to the Four Quarters — not a fifth quarter about G, but a **messiah-less** faith that bloomed from an idea: natural philosophy that happens to match physics and ethics. Its entire public liturgy fits on a wall: ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind.*** People spray-paint it everywhere (bridges, trucks, lab doors, church steps, CongoNet stencil packs). No founder-worship required; G is optional to the practice and often unwanted by it. Antichristos wants a man. The Kind wants a sentence. Both can be true in the same city on the same morning.
- **C-10b · CongoSky.cloud embodied the ethic.** Before the Kind was street paint, **CongoSky.cloud** ran on it — company law, product posture, hire/fire weather, partner terms, the hymnbook choir’s working rule. Not a poster in reception: the operating system. When Lucid went public and the walls caught fire, the world could point at a cloud that had already been living the sentence. AJ’s river desk is downstream of a company that embodied ***Don’t be a cunt — be kind*** while it still had to answer invoices. The Kind can claim the walls; CongoSky already claimed the practice.
- **C-11 · Ethic provenance.** In-world the sentence is structurally derived in the 420 Code (terminal ethic), not a slogan invent. The religion’s claim is that the same ladder that yields the physics yields the ethic — so the spray paint is not “vibes,” it is the popular face of a coherence claim. The book may show sincere Kind practice and opportunistic Kind branding; it may not collapse the Kind into Antichristos or into Quarter II’s mockery.
- **C-12 · Old power vs G — the Illuminatie.** Capital-P establishment / continuity networks (working in-world name: **the Illuminatie**) are **not happy with G**. Not as a Saturday-morning cabal lecture — as pressure: funding weather, standards boards, discreet briefings, reputational freeze, the soft no. They preferred a universe whose constants stayed *underived* and whose ethics stayed *unforced*. G’s copyleft unification is a sovereignty problem for people who trade in opacity. Render as institutional weather G can feel; do not require a membership list or a smoke-filled room scene unless O-09 locks one.
- **C-13 · Hyperscalers vs AJ.** **AWS, Azure, Google, Apple** (and kin) are **not happy with AJ**. CongoSky / CongoNet / sovereign-edge / LEO mesh / hymnbook choir cut against their gravity wells — compute, identity, distribution, the phone as parish. Pressure arrives as partner frost, ToS weather, cloud-credit politics, app-store fog, talent poaching, “compliance” letters, and the offer that is really a collar. AJ on the Chobe can still feel a Seattle/Redmond/Cupertino draft. Named marques allowed as fiction-world institutions; no invented criminal conspiracies attributed to living executives.
- **C-14 · Lucid.** **Lucid** is the new AI of 2035: runs on **integers**, **needs no GPU**, **does not hallucinate**, and has **ingested all the interwebs**. It is the technical and cultural centre of **lucid.rodeo** — the thing AJ is leaning into while CongoSky.cloud runs itself. Deterministic / discrete compute lineage rhymes with G’s stack without making Lucid “G’s chatbot.” Choir texture: humans and Lucid singing from the hymnbook. **Lucid crashed the AI GPU bubble** — CapEx, cluster fantasy, and hyperscaler AI margins deflated when intelligence stopped needing their silicon. Hyperscaler unhappiness (C-13) is not vibes; it is a burst balance sheet.
- **C-15 · Lucid register — near-now, not magic.** We are not far off. Author-present Lucid (2026) already ships: **cite-or-refuse**; **dual RAG** (420code canon first, Simple English Wikipedia fallback — any grounded 420 cite wins); **voice** Talk path (STT → Lucid brain → TTS, G-voice twin on lucid.rodeo); **SimpleWiki** vector+graph ingest live (~158k articles); **420 corpus** offline + Neon lanes; **lucid-nano** MCU proof; potion offline semantic. On the page: never mystify the mechanism; never break the thesis (determinism, refusal, no GPU dependency for the core path). Fiction = *adoption weather and consequences*. **Domain lock:** product is **lucid.rodeo** (not lucide).
