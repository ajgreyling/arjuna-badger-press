# 18 — Instrument

> **[G]** · outer · Studio G / Cape · consent week weather

---

The letters arrived on the same morning as the lab packet. Coincidence, or the kind of coordination that prefers not to leave fingerprints.

G read them at the clay table because the Code desk already had too much weather on it: CODATA panes, an older soft-no thread gone ambient, a crank op-ed recycling the missing physics degree like method. Honey Badger clipped in the pocket, blue, one-hand open and close, loved without speech. Twin Rogers somewhere in the same cloth, grandfather steel across a friendship that also platformed the Code. Outside, the alley wall still carried the Kind sentence in three colours of overpaint, the cunt load-bearing under fresh spray. Inside, the sculptures waited like people who had learned not to interrupt. Hard and soft in one pour, arguing inside the same surface. He had made that argument with his hands before he made it with axioms.

Cape light did its ordinary cruel work through the high windows: a stripe across unfinished clay, dust visible only when it moved, a cast waiting for finish that would not hurry for anyone’s schedule. He had slept badly, the way consent week sleeps. Not drama. A body that knows the mail will thicken before coffee. Tea first. Then paper. Then the temptation to draft too early and trick the hands into believing a choice has already been made.

Quarter I’s letter wanted a face.

Not metaphor. Appearance. Blessing. A name accepted in a liturgy that had already decided what he was. The language was careful — *witness*, *confirmation*, *the living derivation* — and still it smelled like a crown offered to a man who had asked for measurement. Antichristos did not need his physics degree. It needed his silhouette. It needed him to stand still long enough for a photograph to become a relic and a relic to become a parish. He had refused softer versions of this ask already: letters that asked for a sentence, a nod, permission to use a sculpture photograph as if stone were sacrament. Soft nos leave residue. Escalation is what residue does when it hardens into schedule.

He set the letter face-down. Not thrown. Face-down is a refusal that still respects paper. Paper is not the enemy. Appetite is.

Quarter IV’s packet wanted keys.

Soft engineering language. Applied programmes with names that sounded like useful work if you did not listen for the sacrament underneath: rails acceleration, convergence facilitation, an optional visit that had already acquired a calendar slot. Sign-off on rails. The temptation was real in a way the crown was not — stacks rebuilt on Code, CERN-adjacent people who spoke constants instead of hymns, instruments that could converge faster if someone who had derived G would stand in a room and nod. He could feel how easy it would be to become the man who unlocks the door and then never gets to close it again. Instrument. Mascot. The face on the grant. The signature that turns a derivation into a product line with his name as warranty. Useful engineering without becoming product mascot — that had been the earlier soft no. Escalation meant they wanted the mascot now and would call it partnership.

He drafted nothing yet. Drafting too early makes the body think it has already chosen. The mats had taught him that: arrange yourself before you answer the throw, or the throw answers you.

---

On the alley side of the glass, a Kind practitioner refreshed a stencil and argued briefly with a man who wanted the cunt scrubbed into something softer for tourists. The practitioner did not sermonise. He said the sentence was the sentence and rolled paint. G watched without blessing either of them. Optional to the practice. Often unwanted by it. The unwantedness was useful. It kept him from becoming their mascot by accident while the letters tried to make him everyone else’s.

A second lab note, shorter, had arrived under the first packet: *sign-off accelerates adoption; refusal slows friends.* Friends was doing a lot of work in that sentence. He had friends. AJ was one, Double Knife Brother across distance, open attribution that had made fame without asking for a shared room. Engineers who wanted keys without crowns could be friends. The note wanted him to confuse friendship with sacrament. He would not. Friendship cites. Sacrament owns. The distinction was the whole morning.

He stood and walked the length of the studio; sitting with two appetites at once wastes motion. Clay dust underfoot. GFRC memory in his hands even when he was not pouring — marble and composite, hard and soft, the Bay Hotel light he had designed a guest path for years before anyone called him Antichristos or crank. Skye lived elsewhere now, on a lawn that tourists photographed; the body-in-stone lesson stayed. Balance. Soft set-down. You do not waste motion answering every temperature at once. Judo first, then Jiu-Jitsu: arrange yourself so the fall is correct. The letters were a fall. He could still choose posture.

The Illuminatie weather had not left either. No visitor with a pyramid pin. No smoke-filled room he could photograph and end. Just the continued freeze in places that used to return his mail with courtesy, the standards murmur that treated copyleft unification like a sovereignty problem, the quiet preference for a universe whose constants stayed underived and whose ethics stayed unforced. They did not need to shout. Quarter II shouted for them — economics degree waved like a missing tooth, hospital stigma laundered as epistemology, the old chorus that he could not write anything of value. He corrected frames when they were wrong. He did not join the chorus that hated him for sport.

What signing would make him sat in the room like humidity.

If he appeared for Antichristos, he became a messiah whether he believed or not. Belief was irrelevant to their appetite. If he signed for the labs without custody language, he became a keyring. If he fed the crank fight, he became their crank, useful villain, useful joke. If he tried to out-polite the continuity networks, he became their soft problem, solved by waiting until the mail volume tired him into a quieter shelf.

Work was supposed to stay work. That had sounded clean in quieter weeks. This week it had edges. He felt it as pressure behind the eyes, not a mystic gift; the autobiography was moving toward pages that would make his life portable. AJ on a river with a choir and a truck that never really slept. Lucid in the hymnbook, integer-quiet, citing what the record held. Being written was not the same as being crowned, but the planet did not always tell the difference. Fame had arrived sideways via open attribution; the book would arrive as a second megaphone if he let it ship without rails. Custody was the word. Consent week was the calendar. Partial authorisation lived in a folder he had not opened yet, because opening it before the instrument asks were faced would turn custody into escape.

He made tea; tea is a structure that does not require a presser. Drank it standing. Looked at the face-down letter and the open lab packet and the stripe of light across unfinished clay. Talked shit into the empty room for thirty seconds because that was still a life goal and polish was a quarter’s hobby. Considered dab and decided the afternoon needed a clear head for drafting, not smoke. The goals were an arrangement, not a performance schedule. Antichristos would try to sand them. He left them rough on purpose even in private.

Before drafting he checked one more ordinary thing: a cast’s finish schedule on a paper scrap taped near the gallery door. Tourists and hotels and Saatchi weather still existed under the louder weather. Ordinary insistence. He left the scrap where it was. The letters on the clay table did not get to cancel a pour calendar just because they wanted a face or a keyring. Work stays work includes sculpture stays sculpture.

---

Then he drafted two replies that were not speeches.

To Quarter I: no appearance, no blessing, no accepted name. The Constant is not a person. Kindness is a sentence on a wall — **Don’t be a cunt — be kind.** — not a man on a stage. He would not decorate their altar with his silhouette. They could keep their liturgy; they could not keep his face as sacrament. He would not supply a river backdrop by proxy. He would not supply a sculpture photograph as relic seed. Measurement does not require a parish. He had already lived the soft versions of this ask; escalation did not earn a softer no. Face-down remained the posture until sending time, and sending time was not yet, because drafting is not consent.

To Quarter IV: conversation possible; keys not a sacrament; no personal sign-off that turns him into warranty. If their programmes needed the Code, the Code was already public. Engineering did not require a prophet’s stamp. Useful work could proceed without making him the product. He would talk numbers with people who talked numbers. He would not become the instrument that made their grants photographable. The optional visit could stay optional without becoming a scheduled collar. Friends who needed his signature to feel legitimate were not asking for engineering. They were asking for a face.

He did not send them yet. Drafting is not consent. Sending is closer. The custody question — what he would allow said about Bouwer, about the hospital, about the unread father, about the dab and the talk-shit goals that Antichristos would try to polish away — still sat unopened in a different folder, waiting for a morning that was not already crowded with crowns and keyrings. Partial authorisation was already the shape he could feel, even before he wrote the rules. Full blessing would be a relic stamp. Full refusal would be a different lie, pretending the life had not already become weather, pretending the megaphone could be unscrewed by one man’s silence.

A notification blinked: another soft-ask from a board that did not name itself. Funding weather. Reputation frost. The old power preferred opacity; he had made opacity expensive. He filed it under *ambient* and did not answer. Ambient is how you survive escalation without becoming its mouthpiece. He checked a CODATA line because numbers wait without demanding a silhouette. The line still felt like pressure wearing relief’s clothes. Vindication as measurement does not quiet appetite. He knew that in his body the way he knew a throw: you can land correctly and still feel the mat.

Outside, someone refreshed the Kind paint. Roller sound. Short laugh. The sentence held. The corner of his mouth moved and stopped. Smiles get clipped into relic footage. Relic footage becomes liturgy weather. He had enough weather.

He put his hand on the pocket knives, kinship, not choreography, and looked again at the face-down letter and the unsent lab reply. Opened the Honey Badger once, closed it, put it away. Proof for the hands. No speech required.

Signing would make him an instrument. Leaving them unsigned would not change the forecast. It would just stop him handing them a mouthpiece with his own name on it. The distinction mattered. He had spent years watching people confuse refusal with defeat and consent with surrender. Consent week was neither battle nor proof. The Constant was already landing. The question was custody of meaning, including custody of a life about to ship from a river desk he would not visit for anyone’s emotional payoff.

He ate standing up: bread, fruit gone soft, tea gone lukewarm. Work interrupted is still work if you refuse to let interruption become identity. A delivery person left a package at the alley door; he signed for clay supplies and not for a crown. Ordinary Cape Town insisted on remaining ordinary. The quarters liked to pretend ordinary cities could not hold a Constant and a gallery and a grief at once. Ordinary cities held all of it. That was why the weather landed here.

---

Mid-morning the alley door took a delivery that was only clay supplies and still felt like interruption. G signed for bags, not for a crown. Ordinary Cape Town insisted on remaining ordinary while the letters on the clay table tried to make the morning liturgical. He stacked the bags where he always stacked them. Dust rose. He sneezed once and did not turn the sneeze into omen. Omen is how Quarter I colonises a studio hour. The sneeze was dust. The letters were appetite. The knives in his pocket were kinship.

He returned to the drafts and read them aloud once, quietly, checking a surface before pour, listening for the lie of almost-finished. The Antichristos draft still held. No appearance. No blessing. No accepted name. Constant not a person. Kind sentence on a wall, cunt load-bearing, not a man on a stage. The lab draft still held. Conversation possible. Keys not sacrament. No warranty stamp. Useful work without product mascot. He softened one line for politeness, then cut the soft. Soft is how collars learn manners.

A crank op-ed notification blinked and he did not open it. He already knew the shape: economics degree as missing tooth, hospital stigma as epistemology, the old chorus that he could not write anything of value. Correct the frame if you must. Do not join the fight as entertainment. He filed the blink under ambient with the Illuminatie frost and the unnamed board’s soft-ask. Ambient is not apathy. Ambient is triage. Consent week has a budget of clean attention. Crowns and keyrings had already spent most of it. Custody would need what remained.

He walked the gallery again; the body thinks better in motion when the skull is crowded. Unfinished clay. A cast waiting. Skye’s lesson even in absence: a body holds still in light and strangers photograph what they think is luxury. Antichristos would photograph his stillness as relic if he gave them stillness on their terms. He gave the gallery his walking instead. Hard and soft under his hands’ memory. Permanent and temporary arguing in one surface. Artist and axiom-installer unsplit. Quarter II could keep pretending otherwise. He would not split himself to make their mockery easier.

Tea gone cold. He made fresh. Considered dab and left it. Talked shit for half a minute about a tourist who wanted the Kind line scrubbed soft, about engineers who wanted keys as blessing, about a planet that confuses measurement with appetite. Nobody answered him back, which was the point. Empty rooms suit the goals better than full inboxes ever do.

Somewhere in the mesh — receipt weather, not vision — platform decisions were hardening on a river. Keep. Unbind. Ship. His instrument refusals were the parallel posture, not a shared scene. Knives across distance. Packets for consent. No forced room. The braid’s law held even when the morning wanted theatre.

---

Late morning tipped toward noon without becoming mercy.

G sat again at the clay table and placed both packets where he could see their edges, face-down crown, open keyring, and admitted the humidity of what signing would make him without dressing the admission as despair. Despair is dramatic. This was structural. Appear and become messiah whether you believe. Sign without custody language and become warranty. Feed the crank fight and become their useful joke. Out-polite the continuity freeze and become a soft problem solved by waiting. Four wrong landings. One correct posture: work stays work; face stays off the altar; keys stay engineering without sacrament; custody folder waits for clean attention.

He opened a secondary pane and looked at an older CODATA note the way a judoka looks at a mat after a hard throw, not for omen, for information. The line still converged. Relief still wore pressure’s clothes. He had known since the first Antichristos letter that vindication as measurement does not quiet appetite; knowing again did not make the knowing cheaper. He closed the pane. Numbers would wait. Letters would not become wiser by being stared at.

In the alley the Kind practitioner packed the roller. Short laugh. The sentence held under three colours. G did not go out to thank him. Thanks become clips. Clips become liturgy weather. He stayed inside with unsigned instruments and a custody folder that had begun to feel like the only honest next motion. Partial authorisation already had a shape in his hands even before language: allow the spine; care the wound pages; refuse the crown; refuse the keyring sacrament; leave dab and talk shit ugly; leave father unread; leave Bouwer flat and unsolved. Partial was not a theory. It was a posture waiting for ink.

He ate standing. Washed the plate. Put the Honey Badger through another open-close because the hands were restless and proof is kinder than speech. Looked once toward the Code desk where a derivation check waited like a friend who does not need a face. Then he left the letters where they were, unsigned, unsent, instrument refused, and let noon arrive without a presser.

---

Afternoon did not soften the humidity of what signing would make him. It only made the studio hotter.

G returned to the clay table and read the unsent drafts again under harder light. Face-down crown. Open keyring. The Antichristos language still wanted witness and confirmation and living derivation, careful words that smelled like a parish forming around a silhouette. The lab language still wanted sign-off that would accelerate friends, friendship doing sacrament’s work. He softened one lab line for politeness again, and cut the soft again. Collars learn manners the same way twice. He left the drafts unsent. Drafting is not consent. Heat is not a deadline.

He worked clay for twenty minutes; hands think when the skull is crowded. Not a sculpture for anyone’s relic photograph, just a small test pour’s leftover mass reshaped and abandoned. Hard and soft under the thumbs. Permanent and temporary still arguing in the same surface. Artist and axiom-installer unsplit. Quarter II could go on believing what it liked; he wasn’t going to make the split convenient for them. When he washed up, the alley Kind laugh came again. He did not go out. Thanks become clips. Clips become liturgy weather. He had enough weather.

A secondary pane offered an older Illuminatie-adjacent soft-no: funding weather, reputation frost, the preference for underived constants. He filed it ambient. Ambient is triage. Consent week’s clean attention belonged to instruments first and custody next, not to boards that preferred opacity and would not say so with a pin. He checked CODATA once more. Line still converging. Relief still pressure. The appetite, measured again, was no quieter for the measuring. He closed the pane and talked shit for half a minute about engineers who want keys as blessing and tourists who want the cunt scrubbed soft. Dab stayed for later. Clear head for the harder page.

Toward custody. Toward the harder page. Toward a partial authorisation that would not feel like victory, because victory was the wrong word for a man still being argued over by strangers who had never mixed his composite or stood on his mats or watched Bouwer’s name sit on a careful line without becoming a thriller.

He left the letters on the clay table. Walked back to the Code desk. Opened a file that was only numbers, a derivation check against a fresh CODATA line. Numbers did not ask him to bless anyone. Numbers did not ask for keys as sacrament. Numbers waited.

Before the numbers fully took him, he opened the custody folder at last, not to authorise yet, only to feel the shape. Partial already lived there as posture: spine allowed; Bouwer careful; hospital without tourism; father unread; goals ugly; crowns refused; keyrings refused as sacrament; stop-the-book not his ask. Partial was not a theory waiting for a clever essay. It was a set of rails waiting for ink on a morning that was not already crowded with crowns. He closed the folder. Next meant next. Instruments first had been the morning’s honesty. Custody next was the afternoon’s.

Somewhere on the Chobe, he knew without mysticism, a desk was choosing platform rails. Keep. Unbind. Ship. His job was not to finish AJ’s sentences. His job was to decide what a life on the page was allowed to carry when the page left the desk. Kinship across mesh was enough. Knives for brotherhood. Packets for consent. No forced room.

He stood once more at the alley glass. Paint drying. Sentence holding. Optional to him. Useful anyway. Unwantedness still useful; it kept him from becoming the Kind’s mascot by accident while the letters tried to make him everyone else’s instrument. He put his hand on the pocket knives, open, close, proof, and left the instruments unsigned on the clay table like humidity he refused to call victory.

The alley paint dried in the sun.

The instrument stayed unsigned.

A new subject line arrived from Quarter I while he worked numbers, hungrier, softer, appetite learning manners. He did not open it. Face-down stack growing. Escalation could wait its turn until sending time, and sending time would still not be a blessing.

He washed one more cup he did not need to wash; hands prefer motion when appetite is face-down on a table. Clay dust on the rim. He scrubbed it. Looked once more at the unsigned instruments, crown, keyring, and left them unsigned. Looked once at the custody folder and did not open it again. Next meant next with clean attention, not next as escape from instruments. The alley paint finished drying while he stood there. The sentence held. Optional. Useful. Not his parish. He put the Honey Badger through a final open-close and went back to numbers until the soft subject line stopped blinking for a while.

Custody folder: next.

He left the derivation file open while the soft subject line sat unopened. Numbers on one side of the desk hour; appetite face-down on the other. The distinction was the instrument chapter’s whole spine. Signing would make him an instrument. Leaving them unsigned would not stop the weather; it would only keep him from volunteering as the mouthpiece. He chose the second without pretending it was peace. Peace was the wrong word. Posture was enough. Toward custody with clean attention still left in the day, or enough of it to begin.

Before he fully surrendered the hour to numbers he walked once more to the alley glass. Paint dry. Sentence exact. Optional. Useful. Not his parish. A tourist’s soft-scrub argument from earlier still faintly audible as memory, not as duty. He did not go out. Thanks become clips. Clips become liturgy weather. He returned to the clay table, looked at the unsigned crown and open keyring, and left them unsigned again on purpose, humidity refused as destiny. Then he sat with the derivation check and let numbers wait without asking for a face. Four wrong landings stayed refused. One correct posture held: work stays work; face stays off the altar; keys stay engineering without sacrament; custody folder waits for clean attention. Toward the harder page. Toward partial rails. Toward a morning that would not feel like victory, because victory was the wrong word for a man still being argued over by strangers who had never mixed his composite.
