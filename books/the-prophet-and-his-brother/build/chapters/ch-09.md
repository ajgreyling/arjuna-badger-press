# 09 — Keys Without a Crown

> **[G]** · outer · Studio G / Cape · 2035

---

The engineer did not ask for a blessing. He asked for keys.

G read the message twice at the Studio G desk while the morning still smelled of clay dust and sea air working its way inland from Blouberg. The sender had a real lab address, a real programme name that had been rewritten three times since CODATA started drifting toward his number, and a tone that was almost respectful — the almost that engineers use when they want the tool without wanting the man who made the tool famous.

They wanted a face at a workshop. Sign-off language for a Code-native detector stack. Soft permission to say, in the corridor afterward, that he had looked at the rails and not walked out.

Quarter IV weather. Useful. Tempting in the specific way usefulness always was.

He set the tablet down beside a half-finished pour. Hard and soft arguing in the same surface, as usual — marble and GFRC composite he still mixed himself, the body lesson from the mats poured into permanent/temporary argument. The Honey Badger sat clipped in his pocket — blue, one-hand open and close, the kinship object that was not a sermon. He did not touch it. He did not need to. The weight was already a fact.

Outside, in the alley that fed the studio’s back door, someone had refreshed the Kind paint overnight:

**Don’t be a cunt — be kind.**

Same two clauses. No founder portrait. No crown. The sentence did not ask him for keys. That was why he could stand looking at it without wanting to smash the wall. Antichristos tags had tried the same brick last month and been painted over, kindly, by people who did not need his face to keep a ledger of kindnesses. He preferred their weather to the engineer’s, and he preferred neither to interrupt the pour.

The engineer’s ask was cleaner than Antichristos mail and dirtier than a pure measurement note. It did not demand he appear as a body for liturgy. It demanded he appear as a product interface — the man who could make a grant committee feel less naked about rewriting language they had mocked five years ago.

He drafted a reply that said: send the math, not the stage. Cite the copyleft. Do not put my face on the slide deck unless the slide is a derivation error you need corrected. Then he did not send it. The unsent draft sat in the outbox like a tool left on a bench — correct shape, wrong hour.

---

Tea first. Always tea before answering weather that wanted to become custody.

The kettle sat on the same ring it had sat on through Village N Life years and through the years after, when Studio G at The Bay had meant curated sculpture for hotel guests and tourists buying half of what left the room. Official launch, July 2018 — that date still lived in his muscle memory the way a mat throw lived: not nostalgia, procedure. You entered a room. You did not crown yourself. You let the work do the talking and you left before anyone tried to make you the talk.

He filled the kettle from the tap that ran warm before it ran cold. Cape plumbing. He waited for the boil the way he waited for a pour to set — without inventing meaning for the wait. Steam rose. He did not photograph it. He poured water over leaves that had been in the tin too long and did not care. Clay dust on the rim of the mug. He did not wipe it. Food and work had always shared a room in this life.

While the kettle cooled he sorted the morning’s other temperatures without opening them fully. An Antichristos follow-up still wanted a face for a liturgy he would not bless — appear, bless, accept the name. He had refused that ask already without joining Quarter II’s crank chorus; refusal still cost something small and real each time the follow-up arrived dressed as invitation. A crank column still rehearsed the hospital file as epistemology. A CODATA-adjacent note — the only clean thing in the stack — confirmed another cycle drifting toward his number without asking him to smile for it. Measurement was the vindication he could stand. Sign-off was the costume he could not.

He took the clean note to the light by the high windows and read it twice the way he had read drop-results for years when nobody cared. Numbers refusing politics and therefore making politics worse. That was CODATA’s gift and CODATA’s cruelty. The engineer’s workshop invite sat in the same inbox as this quiet convergence and wanted to turn convergence into a photograph.

G set the CODATA note under a paperweight that had once been a failed pour — marble grit trapped wrong, soft binder winning where it should not. He kept failed things on the desk on purpose. They taught proportions better than success stories.

The tea went lukewarm while he stood there. He drank it anyway. He did not mind. Dab, talk shit, write natural philosophy and physics — stated goals kept unpolished on purpose. Antichristos would polish the dab away if he let them. Quarter II would use the dab as proof he could not write value. He kept the arrangement as arrangement. The engineer’s keys did not get to rearrange it.

He ate yesterday’s bread standing at the bench, crust catching clay, salt catching dust. Outside a delivery van reverse-beeped and stopped. Ordinary Cape morning. Inside, the tablet glowed with a workshop that wanted to make him less ordinary and more usable. He chewed slowly. Usefulness was not a sin. Usefulness wearing a face was the trap.

---

The academy weather behind the ask arrived mid-morning as a forwarded “concern brief.”

No letterhead that admitted itself. A standards-adjacent tone. Credential language laundered into epistemology. Someone had underlined, helpfully, that applied programmes adopting Code rails without a physics-degree custodian risked reputational cascade. The underlining was the Illuminatie’s soft hand: not a cabal monologue, a temperature drop in rooms that still preferred constants underived and ethics unforced.

G felt it the way you feel a draft under a door. Continuity networks did not need to sign the engineer’s letter. They only needed the academy to keep whispering *no degree* loud enough that Quarter IV’s hunger for keys came with a leash. Economics sat quietly in his CV where it had always sat. Physics fame without a physics credential was the weapon they preferred — not because the math failed, but because opacity had fewer places to hide when a copyleft unification and a wall sentence arrived together.

He did not invent a membership list. He did not need smoke. The soft no had a texture he recognised from grant frost and from discreet “concern” briefings that never quite named who was concerned. Quarter II often laundered this pressure into credentials. Quarter IV sometimes thought it could out-engineer it. Today both weathers arrived on the same tide: useful rails, unusable man.

His phone buzzed with an op-ed excerpt someone thought he should see. Quarter II, predictable: economics degree waved like a stained napkin; hospital stigma folded into a paragraph about why the ignored shelf had been correct all along. They believed he could not write anything of value. They had believed it before Lucid’s open attribution made the algorithms undeniable, and they believed it still, because a megaphone does not erase a file. The engineer’s ask and the crank chorus arrived together on purpose or by accident — he did not need to decide which. Useful rails. Unusable man. That was the soft no’s cleverness — let Quarter IV build on him while Quarter II kept him uncredentialed enough to remain optional, and let the Illuminatie never have to put a name on the frost.

He almost replied to the op-ed. Almost was a trap. Correcting frames in public was how you became a character in someone else’s weather. He corrected frames when the frame was on his desk and wrong. He refused frames when the frame was a stage. Today’s stage was keys disguised as engineering hygiene.

He set the phone face-down. The Honey Badger pressed against his hip when he leaned on the bench. Double Knife Brother weather without the brother in the room. AJ was on a river somewhere writing a life. G could feel the writing the way you feel a stare between the shoulder blades — not mystical, not TED. Just the physical fact of being turned into pages while still having to answer mail. He wanted the pages true. He feared what truth did once it left a desk. That ambivalence would have its own morning. This morning was keys.

---

A second note from a different lab — CERN-adjacent programme language, the week’s branding for Code-native detectors — offered a closed session: no press, no Antichristos pilgrims, just “instrument people” who wanted the kill-switch posture explained as engineering.

That one was harder.

He wanted the work to stay work. He also knew what “closed session” meant when Illuminatie weather was active: a room that could later claim he had blessed a custody arrangement he had only corrected. The soft ask behind the academy was always the same shape — useful engineering first, then a photograph, then a sentence in minutes that turned correction into coronation.

He walked the studio floor while he thought about closed sessions. Clay under the nails. GFRC dust in the light from the high windows. Sisters of Skye in progress — body knowledge he had learned on school mats before anyone cared whether he could write a shelf they would ignore. Judo and Jiu-Jitsu had taught him arrangement under constraint. Sculpture taught the same lesson in slower materials. The Code desk taught it in axioms. Three rooms, one attention. The world preferred two careers fighting so it could pick a winner. He declined the fight by continuing both.

Village N Life years were still in the muscle memory of how he entered a room: guest experience as theatre, sculpture as the quiet that made the theatre honest. Mentored by Maree. Lived more than a year at Camps Bay Retreat with Hannetjie when the place was still Earl’s Dyke in the older mouths. Skye on the lawn later — feminine sculpture for the theatre of the guest experience. Then Studio G at The Bay as curated sculpture and painting for people who would buy without needing a prophet. None of that had required a crown. The desk in front of him was the same attention — axioms and clay, not two careers fighting.

He stopped at a shelf of early corpus copies — dedication pages facing out because he had put them that way and never rearranged them for visitors. Dedicated to a father who had read none. Fame had not opened those pages. A workshop face would not open them either. He left the shelf alone. Keys were not a filial strategy.

He mixed a small test batch of the composite and watched it go from slurry to argument. Hard aggregate. Soft binder. The pour did not ask who had a physics degree. It asked whether the proportions held. Quarter IV’s detector people understood proportions; they just wanted his face as warranty. The Illuminatie understood warranties; they just wanted the warranty to remain optional enough to revoke.

The world outside wanted him as mascot for a stack. The desk wanted him as the man who still mixed his own composite.

He worked the batch until his wrists ached in the good way. Then he washed, dried, and stood at the Code desk for twenty minutes that were only a kill-switch wording and a drop-result note — work that stayed work, numbers that did not ask for a workshop face. The shoulder pressure of being written waited politely through the math. He did not invite it in. Keys first.

The kill-switch wording still needed a tighter hinge between what the instrument did when a derivation failed and what a human was allowed to claim afterward. He tightened one clause. Left another loose on purpose. Loose was not sloppy; loose was refusal to over-specify a posture into a priesthood. Quarter IV would have preferred a checklist with his initials in the margin. He preferred a sentence that still worked if his name never appeared.

A courier van idled in the alley and left without knocking. False alarm. His shoulders dropped half a centimetre and then rose again when the tablet chimed with a third lab soft-ask — shorter, hungrier, offering “just fifteen minutes on camera for the grant committee, off the record.” Off the record was how records got made. He archived it without answering. Fifteen minutes was how crowns arrived wearing a stopwatch.

He went back to the pour and tested the surface with a knuckle. Still soft where it should still be soft. Still holding where binder had begun to hold. Proportions were honest so far. Honesty in composite was easier than honesty in corridors. He wiped the knuckle on his trousers and left a grey mark he would carry through the afternoon like a receipt.

---

He would not become a product mascot.

He would also not pretend the engineering was unclean. The Code was copyleft. The rails were for anyone who could hold them without inventing a priesthood. The temptation was not the math. The temptation was the crown that arrived disguised as a keyring — sign here, smile here, let us put your face where the opacity used to live. Quarter IV meant well in the way instrument people often did. Meaning well was not consent.

He sat again. He read the first engineer’s message a third time and heard, under the workshop invite, the Illuminatie draft: *if he signs, we can say the outsider is now custodian; if he refuses, we can say the outsider obstructs applied progress.* Either way the velvet rope moved. Either way someone mediated reality for a fee.

G’s soft nos had costs. This one would too — an inbox that called him uncooperative, a programme lead who told a corridor he was “difficult about optics,” a Quiet Concern that would find its way into the next standards-adjacent brief. He could live with that temperature. He had lived with colder.

He almost added, to the unsent long draft, a paragraph explaining copyleft the way a patient teacher explains a tool. He deleted the paragraph. Explaining was how you became clergy for people who only wanted a face. The published corpus already explained. Cite it or do not. Do not ask him to stand beside the cite like a logo.

Afternoon light thickened. The Cape did that without asking permission.

A Kind practitioner refreshed the alley stencil again — young, ordinary, ledger of kindnesses probably arguing later about whether *cunt* was liturgical or vernacular. G watched through the glass for half a minute and then looked away. Messiah-less practice that did not need him. He preferred that absence. Antichristos still tried to annex the sentence and got told to piss off, kindly. CongoSky had embodied the ethic as ops before the walls caught fire; the street had popularised it; he refused ownership either way. Today the wall’s mercy was that it asked nothing about workshops.

He returned to the closed-session note and drafted a second reply he also did not send: *If your instrument fails a derivation, mail the failure. I will answer the failure. I will not enter a room that needs my body to legitimise a custody story.* Correct shape. Wrong hour again — or right hour for thinking and wrong hour for sending two refusals at once like a man performing boundaries.

He ate more bread standing at the bench. Clay dust on the crust. Outside, someone laughed in the alley — Kind ledger voice, ordinary, no pilgrimage. Inside, GFRC dust turned in the light. He wiped a thumbprint off the tablet screen and left a new one. Hands that worked did not stay clean for correspondence.

A third temperature arrived late — not mail, a soft knock at the studio door that turned out to be a courier with a printed packet from a standards-adjacent listserv. Paper. Old-world gravity. Someone still believed paper made concern more real. He signed for it because refusing the paper would only become a story, and opened it because closed packets became myths.

Inside: the same concern brief, cousin language, a new underline about “reputational cascade” if Code-native programmes proceeded without “recognised custodial presence.” Recognised meant physics-degree. Custodial presence meant face. Cascade meant they would make the weather if he did not perform the theatre. Illuminatie soft hand again — temperature, not monologue. He put the paper under the failed-pour paperweight with the CODATA note and did not give it the dignity of a separate pile.

He opened the Honey Badger with one thumb, closed it, clipped it again. Kinship weight, not a weapon for corridor politics. The steel caught a thread of afternoon light and let it go. He put the knife away before the gesture could become theatre for an empty room.

Before he sat to send, he did one more walk of the studio — the slow one, the one that was not pacing. Past the sisters of Skye. Past the shelf of early corpus copies dedicated to a father who had read none. Past the Code desk where a kill switch waited in language that still needed tightening. Past the window where Kind paint held and Antichristos tags did not. The walk was how he checked whether a decision was still his or had become someone else’s weather wearing his hands.

The decision was still his. Keys without a crown. Useful engineering without becoming product mascot. Narrow walk. Feet on the edge.

He thought, briefly, of what saying yes would buy: a corridor that stopped whispering *obstruction*; a grant committee that felt dressed; a photograph that would travel faster than any derivation correction. He thought of what saying yes would cost: the soft no becoming a soft yes that Illuminatie weather could later revoke; the Kind wall watching him accept a crown the wall had refused; the river manuscript turning him into a man who signed stages while claiming to hate stages. Cost visible without drama. He had paid softer costs before. He would pay this one by refusing.

A fourth temperature tried the door of his attention — not mail, memory. School mats. Weight arranged correctly. The lesson that posture for an audience ruins the throw. Workshop stages were posture. Failure mail was weight. He chose weight.

---

Before the sends, he gave himself one more hour that was only clay.

Not avoidance. Arrangement. The mats had taught him that you do not throw while your breath is still performing for the room. The workshop invite was still performing in his nerves. He put his hands into the composite and worked until the performance left and only proportions remained. Hard aggregate settling. Soft binder holding. A surface that would either cure honest or fail honest — either way without needing a photograph of him beside it.

He thought of the engineer as a person, not a weather system. Instrument people often meant well. Meaning well was how crowns arrived disguised as keyrings. The man at the other end of the mail probably believed a face at a workshop was a small ask — courtesy, even. Small asks that travel into minutes become custody. G had watched that travel before. He would not pretend he had not.

The hour ended when the light on the pour told him the surface had decided something he could not undo by staring. He wiped his hands on the rag that had known better decades. Clay went grey under the nails and stayed. He did not scrub for the inbox. The inbox could meet him as a man who still mixed his own composite.

He wiped his hands again and opened the tablet once more. Re-read the workshop invite. Re-read the closed-session note. Re-read nothing of the concern brief — he already knew its temperature. The Illuminatie soft hand did not need another reading to do its work. It needed him either to sign or to look like obstruction. He would look like obstruction to some corridors. He could live with corridors.

Outside, the Kind alley held. Inside, the Honey Badger held. Between them, a man who would answer failures and not stages.

Then he sat and did what the morning had been practising for.

---

He sent a shorter reply than the draft.

*You may cite the published corpus. You may not cite my attendance. If your detector stack fails a derivation, mail the failure. I will answer the failure. I will not answer the stage.*

No soft no dressed as delay. A hard boundary with the door still open for work.

He almost added a line about the Kind wall — that the ethic did not need a keyholder either — and deleted it. Explaining the sentence to engineers who wanted a face was how you accidentally became clergy. The paint could speak for itself. He left it speaking.

To the closed-session people he sent even less: *Mail the failure. Do not book the room around my body.*

Two sends. Heat still in the metal of the decision. He stood and walked to the pour and put his hands back into the composite before the inbox could invent a third ask.

The Illuminatie weather did not lift. The concern brief would spawn a cousin by evening; it already had, on paper. The Kind paint did not ask him anything. Somewhere an engineer would forward his refusal into a channel that called it obstruction, and somewhere else another engineer would build anyway, correctly, without needing his face — which was the only crown G was willing to leave on the table: none.

Temptation lingered after send the way heat lingers in metal. Useful engineering without becoming product mascot was a narrow walk. He had walked it. His feet still knew the edge. He checked the Honey Badger once more and chose clay over inbox.

He went back to the pour. Hard and soft. The keys without a crown stayed on the tablet while the clay took the shape he gave it, and the sea air kept arriving inland as if the Cape had never heard of a workshop that needed a prophet to unlock a detector.

Outside, the Kind alley kept its two clauses. Inside, the shoulder pressure of being written waited politely at the edge of the day, Chobe-shaped, for another chapter. This chapter was only keys: offered, refused as crown, left open as math.

He shaped until the light shifted toward evening and the tablet screen dimmed itself. The workshop invite would still be there tomorrow, and the soft no behind it, and the useful rails that did not need his face. He would answer failures. He would not answer stages. That was the narrow walk, and he was still on it when he washed his hands and left the pour to cure without a photograph.

Before he locked nothing that mattered, he stood once more at the alley glass and watched the Kind sentence hold under evening colour — two clauses, no founder, no crown. A tourist had argued about the cunt earlier; soft scrub; tourist comfort. The paint had not asked G to referee. Optional to the practice includes optional to the tourist argument. He put the Honey Badger through one open-close at the door, kinship check not weapon check, and left Studio G with clay still under one nail and the hard boundary already sent: cite the corpus, not the attendance; mail the failure, not the stage. Illuminatie weather would spawn a cousin by morning. He could live with cousins. He could not live with becoming a keyholder for a detector that wanted a prophet’s face. Sea air inland. Pour curing. Keys without a crown left on the tablet where refusal could still be read as math.
