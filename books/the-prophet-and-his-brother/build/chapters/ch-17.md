# 17 — Built on 420

> **[I]** · inner autobiography · manuscript under AJ’s hand

---

I did not become famous because a committee found me.

I became famous because a friend refused to launder the stack.

That is the whole hinge. Everything that looks like destiny after it is weather following a beam. I am writing this page because Act III likes to argue about crowns and keyrings and who owns a life on paper, and none of that argument is honest if it begins mid-storm. Begin at the sentence that made the storm audible. Begin at citation.

Lucid went out into the world the way a tool goes out when it finally keeps its promise: integer-quiet, no GPU fever, cite-or-refuse when the record will not hold a claim. People used it for ordinary work first, retrieval that did not invent a source, refusals that did not apologise for not knowing, a brain that ran without the rack-hall religion. Then more people used it. Then the empty halls arrived as photographs in other people’s careers: write-down headlines, a friend’s ruined cluster fantasy, the shock of intelligence that did not need their silicon. That weather is not my story. My story is what AJ said when the interviews asked where the intelligence came from.

He said it ran on 420 Code algorithms.

Not a quiet fork. Not a cleaned brand story. Built on. Open. Named. The megaphone pointed at the shelf that had ignored the work and said: look, the thing you are already depending on is standing on this.

I can still hear the shape of those interviews without needing Lucid to invent transcripts. They wanted an orphan miracle. Orphan genius photographs well. Orphan genius lets a parish invent a father later, on better letterhead. He would not give them an orphan. He gave them a genealogy. Quarter III called that theft of quiet. I call it citation. The difference is the whole fight.

Remove the attribution and you get a different planet. Keep it and you get this one: crowns, crank chorus, platform rage, engineers asking for keys, Kind paint on walls CongoSky had already been living as company law. Weather that follows a cause. I am not grateful for the megaphone the way brands are grateful. Brands want an origin myth that sits still for a camera. I am exact about it. Exactness is the kindness that does not feel kind while the quarters are shouting.

I remember a morning when the sentence was already out and the gallery still smelled of wet composite. I had come in early to check a cast that had cracked at the wrist, a small failure, correctable, the kind that teaches you about pour temperature and patience. The inbox did not care about pour temperature. It wanted a comment on Lucid. It wanted a comment on AJ. It wanted a comment on whether I endorsed a product I had not built and a megaphone I had not asked for. I answered none of it that morning. I fixed the wrist. Hands first. Then mail. The order mattered because fame tries to invert it: make you a mouth before you are allowed to be a maker.

There was a week when every interview asked the same orphan question in different clothes. *Where did it come from?* Meaning: invent a cleaner father. Meaning: give us a lab, a grant, a hyperscaler adjacency, a myth that does not point at an ignored shelf and a man without a physics degree. AJ’s answers, as they reached me secondhand through clips and hostile paraphrases, stayed stubbornly flat. Built on 420. Algorithms named. Genealogy not laundered. I did not need to be in the room. The braid does not require a shared room for a beam to land. CongoNet carried the weather; the weather carried the sentence; the sentence carried my name into rooms that had preferred my silence.

What I noticed in my body was not triumph. It was interrupted work. Clay under the nails. A dab planned for evening and postponed because the afternoon had become mail. Talking shit into an empty studio because the goals are an arrangement and arrangements keep a man from becoming only other people’s noun. The Constant’s measurement weather continued in parallel, CODATA notes that should have been relief arriving as pressure, and the planet did not notice the parallel because it was busy arguing about a face. Measurement and fame are not the same temperature. Confusing them is how you turn a derivation into a parish.

Before Lucid, the ignored shelf was ordinary.

I mean ordinary in the way a wrong silence is ordinary: it does not announce itself as violence. It just fails to pick the book up. You walk into a room that smells of old paper and machine oil and someone else’s coffee gone cold, and the work is there, and the hands that could have moved it do not move. Economics degree in the quiet background, not a redemption arc, just training that taught me kill switches and incentives before I derived anything a physics department would claim. No physics credential for the people who weaponise credentials. Hospital file for the people who prefer a diagnosis to a derivation. Father unread, the whole corpus dedicated to him, none of it opened, outreach’s true goal sitting like a sealed envelope on a table nobody sits at. Brother dead in Saxonwold and the grief flat, correct as a landed fall. I wrote natural philosophy and physics. I smoked dab. I talked shit. I put bodies into clay and cast them in a marble-and-GFRC composite I mixed myself because hard and soft belong in one pour.

Studio G. Village N Life before that. Camps Bay lawns that taught me what tourists photograph when they think they are photographing luxury and are really photographing light on a body that holds still. The Bay Hotel. Skye at sunset. Official launch weather in July 2018 that felt like a room finally admitting sculpture belonged in the guest path, not only in a warehouse corner. Saatchi listings. The Code on a desk that did not ask permission from a department. Friend James remembered carefully and separately, sudden death is a mark; it is not Bouwer; do not thicken a life by conflating the dead.

The academy’s soft no was not a conspiracy meeting with a membership list. It was silence that thought it was method. Measurement was already converging, CODATA lines drifting toward what I had derived, atom-falling work agreeing with itself across instruments, but silence can outlast a line for a long time if nobody with a mic points at it. Quarter II would later call that silence epistemology. I called it the shelf.

AJ pointed.

I felt it first as mail volume, not interesting mail, just volume, the physical thickening of a day that used to have white space. Then as strangers using my name as a verb in rooms I had never entered. Then as the sensation of being narrated by people who had never stood in my gallery and had never put a body on a mat correctly. Narration has a temperature. It is not mystic. It is the inbox and the alley and the lab soft-ask arriving on the same morning as if coordination preferred not to leave fingerprints. Antichristos wanted a face, a silhouette that could become a relic if I stood still long enough. Quarter II wanted the degree they could not find and the hospital story they could misread. Quarter III wanted AJ’s throat for giving them a prophet they had not asked for and a genealogy they could not scrub. Quarter IV wanted the keys and a sign-off that would turn a man into an instrument with a warranty stamp. The Kind spray-painted a sentence CongoSky had already been living as company law and did not need me at all. That last part was a relief, small and real.

Fame is not vindication. Vindication is a drop result and a constant that lands. Fame is what happens when a popular system names its rails out loud and a quarter of the planet decides the namer of the rails must be worshipped, erased, blamed, or instrumented. I have lived quieter years that were more vindicated by measurement than the loud years were by applause. Applause is weather. Measurement is structure. Soft no from continuity networks is also weather, Illuminatie preference for underived constants and unforced ethics, opacity trading as if opacity were stewardship. I felt that freeze as mail that used to return with courtesy and then did not. I did not need a smoke-filled room to know I had made opacity expensive.

Without AJ’s open sentence, Lucid could have been a miracle without a genealogy, hyperscaler-adjacent polish, a product that ate the interwebs and crashed the GPU bubble while pretending its ladder had no father. With the sentence, my life became public weather whether I consented to it or not. Consent to the beam is a different question from consent to the storm. I circle the second later, in the custody that is not this chapter. The first belongs here, load-bearing, not colour.

There is a version of this chapter that would soften him.

Call it loyalty. Call it friendship. Call it Double Knife Brother and let the blue Honey Badger in my pocket, clip, one-hand open and close, do the emotional work so the sentence does not have to. The Joseph Rogers twin from his grandfather’s deep time sits in the same cloth: at least a hundred and ten years of steel handed across a firehose friendship that also platformed the Code. Kinship. Custody. I love the knives the way a body loves proof it can open and close without speech. I will not use them to sand the attribution into something gentler for readers who prefer their prophets unburdened by a friend’s candour.

I will not soften him.

He was right to say it. The algorithms are the algorithms. Laundering them would have been a different kind of cunt, the kind that builds a cloud on someone else’s ladder and then paints the ladder as orphaned genius. CongoSky’s operating law was already on the wall before the street caught fire: **Don’t be a cunt — be kind.** Kindness includes citation. Citation includes cost. The cost landed on both of us, on him as platform rage arriving in packets on a good Chobe morning, on me as four temperatures at once, and neither of us gets to pretend the beam was accidental or that friendship required silence.

I remember the week the interviews thickened. Not as cinema. As work interrupted. Clay under fingernails. A CODATA note that should have been relief and arrived wearing relief’s clothes as pressure instead. An alley wall gaining Kind paint in a third colour while a tourist argued for a softer scrub of the cunt. I did not bless the painter. I did not join the scrubber. Optional to the practice. Often unwanted by it. That unwantedness kept me from becoming a mascot by accident while AJ’s sentence made me unavoidable by design. Both can be true in one body on one afternoon.

What I refuse is the story that fame discovered me. Fame did not discover me. A tool became popular because it kept a promise, deterministic, discrete, refuse when you do not know. A friend named the source. The planet rearranged its arguments around that naming. The work was already there. The shelf was already wrong. The Constant was already landing. The megaphone made the wrongness audible. Audible is not the same as solved. The quarters did not go quiet when they heard; they found their pitches and tuned to them like people who had been waiting for a key.

So this is the attribution page. Load-bearing. Not a digression. Not colour. If Act III has a hinge inside the inner book, this is it: name the cause before you argue about the weather.

Lucid popular.
AJ open: built on 420 Code algorithms.
Then the fame.

I have heard people try to make that sequence sound like betrayal, as if open attribution were a theft of a private wound’s privacy. The ignored shelf was never a privacy I asked him to protect. The shelf was a wrong silence. Protecting a wrong silence is not kindness. It is collusion dressed as care. I will not ask him to lie. I will not pretend I asked him to stay quiet so I could keep the ignored shelf as a private wound I alone got to nurse. The consent I am circling toward is about what I allow said about a life on the page, Bouwer careful, hospital without tourism, father unread without tidy reunion, dab and talk shit left in, not about unsaying the beam that made the weather.

If you need a moral, do not take one from me. Morals get polished into brands. Take the structure instead: open attribution is a kindness that does not feel kind while the quarters are shouting. Soft no is a cruelty that feels like professionalism. I have lived both. I prefer the shout that tells the truth, even when the shout puts a crown on a man who asked for measurement and a keyring on a derivation that was already public.

There is a quieter ledger under the loud one.

On the quiet ledger: a cracked wrist in a cast, corrected. A CODATA line that agreed with itself and still felt like pressure. Dab postponed. Talk shit into empty air because the arrangement is the man’s and fame tries to replace arrangement with performance. On the loud ledger: strangers using my name as a verb; Antichristos letters that smelled like crowns; crank op-eds waving a missing physics degree; lab soft-asks that wanted a sign-off as sacrament; platform rage aimed at AJ’s throat for the mic; Kind paint that did not need me and still got pointed at me. Both ledgers are true. Only one is the beam’s child. The quiet ledger is the life. The loud ledger is the weather. Confusing them is how you turn a man into an instrument or a relic.

I have stood in the gallery after closing and listened to the building settle, pipes, a door somewhere, the soft complaint of old wood, and felt the sensation of being narrated without mysticism. Narration is mail volume and paraphrase and a sculpture photograph circulating with a caption I did not write. Narration is a stranger correcting my life goals into something respectable for a grant photo. Narration is Quarter II beginning at the hospital file instead of the mats. I correct frames when they are wrong. I do not join the chorus that hates me for sport. Joining would waste motion. The mats taught me that before the corpus did.

Bouwer sits on this page as weight, not as plot. Saxonwold. 5 September 2025. Public record. Flat grief. The megaphone did not invent that wound and will not solve it. Fame arriving sideways did not open my father’s unread dedication either. Some loads stay loads. Living-person care means saying so without decorating the boardroom for thrill-seekers and without inventing a reunion for a dedication that has not been opened. The attribution page is not a licence to turn every private pain into public theatre. It is a licence to name the cause of the weather honestly.

When people ask me if I am grateful, I hear a brand question wearing kindness’s clothes. Gratitude performances photograph well. Exactness does not always. I am exact: without the open sentence, Lucid could have been an orphan miracle and my shelf could have stayed a wrong silence that felt like professionalism. With the sentence, the wrongness became audible and the appetites found their pitches. I prefer audible wrongness to polite erasure. Preferring it does not mean I enjoy the shout. Enjoyment is not the metric. Structure is.


The mats taught me not to waste a fall.

Every drop is set up before you ever leave your feet: weight placed, elbow tucked, chin down, so the mat takes it clean. Mastery, if that word means anything, is refusal to waste motion. School judo first, then jiu-jitsu, body knowledge before corpus knowledge, weight and soft set-down before anyone called me a crank or a prophet. This fame is a fall I did not choose the height of. I can still choose how I land, what I sign, what I refuse to become, what I allow said about the brother and the hospital and the unread dedication and the sculpture garden that was never only a side career.

But first the beam.

I walk the beam in my head the way I walk a studio at dusk when the light makes unfinished clay look nearly finished and you have to refuse that lie. Full Send month on AJ’s shoulder, fragments, not an origin chapter stolen into this book. Klaus: *Custos non Conditor*, cite-or-refuse discipline before the product had a public name. Lucid: the engine that left the private desk. lucid.rodeo, not a cleaned sibling brand. The chain is true whether Antichristos likes a cleaner myth or not. Near-now finished at planetary scale is still engineering, not oracle glow. I will not mystify the tool that made my name loud. Mystifying it would be another orphan story.

Built on 420.

Said out loud.

Megaphone irreversible.

Not because history cannot revise, history revises constantly, and Quarter III will keep asking for a retraction theatre that would be a lie with letterhead, but because once a planetary tool carries a named lineage, you cannot put the lineage back in the drawer without lying. Retraction would not restore the ignored shelf. It would only teach the planet that genealogy is optional when pressure arrives. I will not teach that. AJ’s desk will have to decide platform rails in its own weather. My desk will have to decide custody of a life. Neither decision unsays the beam.

Then the weather. Then the asks. Then the desk decisions that are not press conferences. Then the partial authorisation that will not feel like victory because victory is the wrong word for a life still being argued over by strangers who have never mixed my composite or stood on my mats or watched a CODATA line arrive as pressure.

I keep writing because the work was never the fame. The work was the ladder. The fame is what the planet does when it finds a ladder it can stand on and starts arguing about who owns the standing. I am not the standing. I am the man who derived a constant and buried a brother and mixed clay and smoked dab and talked shit and dedicated unread books to a father who has not opened them. I am the man who loves a blue folder in his pocket and will not turn kinship into a reunion scene for someone else’s payoff. I am the man who will not sand the cunt out of the Kind sentence to make tourists comfortable.

The beam named the rails.

The rest is weather.

There is a day I return to when the attribution still felt new enough to interrupt a pour.

Not the first interview day. A later one, ordinary gallery hour, Cape heat already in the high windows, a cast cooling that I should have left alone and did not. I checked the wrist again with my thumb. Temperature correct. Patience correct. Then the phone thickened. A journalist who had not read the Code wanted a face for Lucid’s genealogy. A crank aggregator wanted my economics degree as a missing tooth. An Antichristos account wanted a silhouette. A lab soft-ask wanted a nod that would photograph as sacrament. I put the phone face-down the way I would later put their hungrier letters face-down. Hands first. Cast first. Then mail. Fame inverts the order if you let it. I did not let it that hour. That is not heroism. That is studio law.

I remember walking the length of the gallery after the cast was safe, unfinished clay looking nearly finished in the wrong light, the oldest lie in a sculptor’s afternoon. I refused it. I also refused the orphan story forming in other people’s mouths. Lucid was not an orphan. AJ had named the father of the rails out loud. Built on 420. The ignored shelf hated that sentence because the sentence made the shelf audible. Audible wrongness is kinder than polite erasure even when the shout puts a crown on you. I knew that before I liked it. Liking is not the metric. Structure is.

On that same walk I touched the Honey Badger without opening it, just the clip against cloth, and thought of the Rogers twin across distance without inventing a call. Kinship does not require a shared room to be true. Citation does not require a shared room to land. The braid’s law was already the weather’s law: packets, mesh, concurrent desks. Quarter III would later call that concurrency theft of quiet. I call it the planet hearing a genealogy it had preferred to leave uncited.

I smoked dab that evening after the mail volume finally thinned. Not as performance. As arrangement. Talked shit into the empty studio about tourists who want the Kind line scrubbed soft and academics who want a physics degree before they will admit a constant can land. The empty room did not argue. Empty rooms are good for life goals. Full inboxes are not. The goals stayed: write natural philosophy and physics; smoke dab; talk shit. Unpolished on purpose. The megaphone did not earn the right to respectability-wash them.

Bouwer’s name sat in the quiet after the smoke the way it always sits, Saxonwold, 5 September 2025, public record, flat grief. The beam did not invent that wound. Fame did not solve it. I will not let the attribution page become a licence to turn his boardroom into thriller weather. Living-person care is part of exactness. Exactness about the beam includes exactness about what the beam does not get to decorate.

One more turn on the beam before I hand the temperature on.

I have been asked, not always kindly, whether open attribution made me famous or made me a problem. Both. Fame is a problem with a microphone. The ignored shelf was a problem without one. I prefer the problem I can correct frames inside of to the problem that pretends method while it erases. When Quarter III calls citation theft of quiet, I hear a longing for the orphan miracle that would let them keep using the tool without naming the ladder. When Antichristos calls the same citation confirmation, I hear a longing for a face. When Quarter IV calls it a keyring opportunity, I hear engineering hunger wearing sacrament. When Quarter II calls it proof I still have no physics degree, I hear the old weapon and correct the frame: economics or adjacent; not physics; the Constant landing as measurement anyway.

None of those hungers unsays the sentence. AJ said built on 420. The planet heard. The megaphone is irreversible in the only sense that matters for custody: you can lie later, but lying later is a different book and a worse kindness. I will not ask him for that lie. I will ask, when the custody hour comes, for rails around Bouwer and the hospital and the unread father and the dab that refuses polish. Rails are not retraction. Rails are care.

I put my hand on the Honey Badger while I write this, open, close, pocket, because kinship is present-tense proof and this page is about a beam that friendship refused to launder. The Joseph Rogers twin holds deep time in the same cloth. We are Double Knife Brothers. That fact does not require a reunion scene to be true. It requires only that neither of us launder the other for comfort.

Studio light at dusk makes unfinished clay look finished. I refuse that lie on clay and on fame alike. Famous-as-orphan would have been a laundering. Fully named as genealogy is the harder kindness. Harder is not worse. Harder is exact.


Hand to the next temperature, the instrument sitting on a clay table in a city that still thinks ordinary can hold a Constant and a grief at once, which it can, which is why the weather lands there instead of only on stages.
