# 07 — Who Gave Him the Mic

> **[A]** · outer · Mutatus Maximus, Chobe River · packet from ch-01 opened

---

AJ opened the Quarter III packet after the mats page had a spine.

Spine first had been the morning’s only mercy. Without it the rage would have colonised the floor-lesson and turned apprenticeship into rebuttal. He had written soft set-down and correct fall until the page could afford interruption. Now interruption was here with citations and teeth, and the truck smelled like dust, solder, and blame arriving before noon.

Lucid had flagged it at dawn. Hostility markers. Attribution thread. He had parked it like a hot pan while the choir stayed on the floor-lesson and frost mail blinked politely in another pane. Now the manuscript could afford interruption, or he could not afford the pretence that the only weather was hyperscalers smiling about acquisition. Platform-rage had found the river. It was time to let it speak in its own citations.

The packet was not one letter.

It was a weather system compressed into a queue: forum exports, CongoNet mirrors, a journalist’s request framed as neutrality, three brand partners asking whether arjunabadger.press would “clarify the provenance narrative,” a lucid.rodeo comment harvest with teeth, a thread that treated open attribution as original sin, and a long piece that used his name as a verb for recklessness.

Who gave him the mic.

They meant G. They meant the ignored man — economics degree, no physics credential, hospital stigma still usable as epistemology — dragged into planetary fame because AJ had said, out loud, that Lucid ran on 420 Code algorithms. Not a quiet fork. Not a cleaned brand story. Open attribution as load-bearing cause. Quarter III hated the candour. Some hated G’s elevation. Some hated AJ for making refusal-to-launder a public act. Some wanted provenance scrubbed quieter — rebrand hints, a softer story about a lab with better manners and a quieter friend. Not yet the full stop-the-book triad. Today was attribution hostility finding a throat.

The mic question arrived with receipts and without courage. Receipts: screenshots of AJ’s own sentences. Courage: none — only the demand that the sentences become unsaid. Unsaying is not science. Unsaying is brand management with a morality costume.

AJ remembered saying it the first time without feeling prophetic. He had named rails because Lucid’s refusal to invent made provenance load-bearing. Cite-or-refuse needs a stack you can point at. Pointing at 420 was honesty, not a campaign to crown a friend. Fame happened anyway. Fame is what happens when you refuse to launder and the product works. Quarter III wanted the working product and a quieter story about where it came from. They could not have both. That was the heat under the packet seal, now opened and breathing in the truck.

AJ felt the anger in his shoulders before he felt it as argument.

He let the first wave pass without typing. Typing too early turns heat into a press release you cannot take back. He watched the river instead — mud prints, light climbing, a calf somewhere in scrub making the undignified sounds of being young. Mutatus held still. The packet did not. Hostility markers do not care about elephants. That was the joke and the problem: the truck could receive every weather at once and still had to choose which weather got a reply.

He opened the truck’s side wider and let river air in as if heat could dilute inbox heat. It could not. Hippos down-channel made their wet ownership sounds. A fish eagle turned once over the channel and did not care about provenance narratives. The solar drop on the rise reported green. Local node hummed under the desk. Mutatus Maximus receiving hatred at every useful layer and still having to choose what to amplify. Connectivity without consent discipline is just a louder mistake.

First real platform-rage on the river. Not abstract schism chart. Inbox heat. The smell of blame on a good Chobe morning while elephants’ mud still held the morning’s prints and the Kind paint on the tank twenty metres away kept its job without him.

He could have driven inland and pretended the mesh was the only weather. He stayed. The river desk is where the autobiography lives; rage does not get to relocate the pilgrimage because it prefers a quieter venue. Mutatus had been built to take links and still choose. Today the choice was hold the attribution, refuse the apology theatre, keep the choir on the life page.

**Don’t be a cunt — be kind.**

Rage wanted him unkind in return. He would not give it that satisfaction as policy. He also would not retract the true sentence that had made Lucid honest and G famous. Cite-or-refuse cut both ways: Lucid refused invention; AJ refused laundering. Hymnbook practice under heat.

Unkind would have been easy and useless — a reply-all that fed the weather system attention as fuel. He kept his hands for the manuscript and for the boring green lights that meant the edge still worked while platforms argued about provenance varnish.

Lucid spoke when asked for summary, exact.

“Dominant demand cluster: attribution hostility — who gave him the mic. Secondary: soft scrubbing of 420 language. Tertiary: personal apology templates circulating. Pause-serialisation chatter present as mood, not concrete stop demand. Brand-risk language high. No single authorised speaker. Cite density high; invention not required.”

“Don’t draft an apology,” AJ said.

“Not drafted.”

“Don’t invent a counter-myth either.”

“Refuse invention is default.”

He nodded once at the pane as if Lucid needed the gesture. It did not. The refusal was already the product. What AJ needed was his own shoulders to believe that rage could be received without being answered as theatre. Hymnbook practice: exactness under heat, no cute voice, no third skull, no campaign.

He stood and walked the length of the truck — matatu colours, mobile DC, Beast’s shadow if you counted the tender — and came back to the packet like a man returning to a tooth he could not stop touching. CongoSky quiet behind the mesh in the good way. Ops still ops. Hire/fire weather and partner terms still governed by the sentence the company had embodied before the walls caught fire. lucid.rodeo queue within tolerance if you ignored the teeth in the comments. arjunabadger.press was the human work that would absorb the hit.

He skimmed one forum export until the language repeated itself into sludge — recklessness, irresponsibility, “platforming a crank,” “ruining serious AI,” as if Lucid’s refusal to invent were a moral failure instead of the product keeping its promise. A reply chain argued that open attribution had “weaponised a private theory.” Private. As if the Code had been a secret handshake and AJ had broken club rules by naming the rails. He stopped skimming. Density of cite was enough; immersion in hate was not research. Lucid could hold the record. AJ’s job was custody of the next true page and the refusal to launder.

He opened a second export and found apology templates circulating like hymn sheets for people who had never sung from a hymnbook. I regret the confusion caused. We are refining our narrative. Lucid stands on its own merits. Merits without rails was the laundering. AJ deleted the template from his screen as if it could stain the truck. Lucid did not offer a rewrite. Good. The product knew the difference between citation and costume.

A thread blamed him for making a mental-health file into a brand. The cruelty was efficient: use the stigma as epistemology, then accuse the candour of exploitation. He felt heat climb his neck and put both palms flat on the desk until the wood reminded him he was still on the Chobe, not on their stage. Living-person care meant he would not drag G’s ward into a rebuttal. The autobiography would carry stigma flat, later, under-dramatised. Rage mail would not get a DSM scene as ammunition.

A brand partner’s mail used the phrase “narrative hygiene.” AJ laughed once, short, ugly. Hygiene again. The academy had hygiene. The platforms had hygiene. Everyone wanted him clean of the sentence that had made the engine honest. Clean meant false.

Another partner asked for a call “off the record.” Off the record was where people practised retractions before they had the courage to ask for them in public. He did not schedule the call. He marked the thread cold and felt the cold arrive in his jaw as ache.

The journalist’s neutrality request wanted a quote about “responsibility to the ecosystem.” Ecosystem was a word that meant other people’s balance sheets. AJ drafted three sentences in his head and threw them away. Quotes become weapons faster than silence does when Quarter III is hunting a flinch. He let Lucid log the request as unanswered with reason: no grounded statement beyond existing public attribution.

“Cite existing public line?” Lucid asked.

“Cite it. Don’t embroider.”

Lucid retrieved the open attribution as already on record. Exact. Boring. Blessed. The rage queue wanted embroidery — apology theatre, a quieter provenance myth, something that would practice for ritual distancing without yet naming the full stop. AJ gave them the record again by not inventing a new one.

He did not open the full ultimatum yet — the stop-the-book demand that would come harder later, with costs named in brands and body and maybe a collar offer wearing a smile. This was the first weather: attribution rage finding the river desk and expecting him to flinch for the camera. Later would be ritual distancing with teeth. Today was the mic question shouted until the river answered or didn’t.

His hand found the empty twin pocket again. G had the Honey Badger and the other Joseph Rogers. Double Knife Brother on another coast living four temperatures while AJ ate the mic-blame. No reunion beat. CongoNet was bridge enough. Knives were kinship, not choreography, and kinship today meant holding the line without calling — without dragging G into AJ’s inbox as a prop for a press cycle.

“Lucid,” he said. “Keep citing. No counter-myth. No schism lecture. Quarters are mail, not a chart.”

“Acknowledged.”

He poured water, tasted plastic and river, and sat again. Sweat found the place behind his knee. The truck’s metal ticked as heat climbed. Somewhere a CongoNet status ping crossed green and boring. He ignored it harder, which was a kind of love for connectivity that did not demand performance.

A long piece in the queue used his name as a verb. *To AJ someone* — meaning to drag an inconvenient author into fame by refusing to launder provenance. The piece performed sadness for “serious research culture.” Serious research culture had ignored G’s shelf for years and now wanted the mic unsung. AJ felt the old Full Send fragment on his shoulder — month that became Klaus that became Lucid — and did not republish it as a defence. Fragments stay fragments. Origin stories become TED the moment you deploy them as armour.

He opened one CongoNet mirror that claimed neutrality and found a collage of screenshots arranged to make open attribution look like a prank that had gone planetary. The collage was careful. Careful hate is still hate. Lucid summarised the cite density without adopting the frame.

“Primary claim unsupported as moral conclusion. Factual core: attribution language public; Lucid stack on 420 rails; fame consequent. Moral overlay: refuse as invention beyond cited opinion.”

“Keep the factual core,” AJ said. “Drop the moral overlay from my mouth.”

“Not placed in your mouth.”

Good. Boring. The product refusing to become his speechwriter for rage.

A lucid.rodeo comment harvest scrolled past with teeth: users who loved the engine and hated the honesty; users who hated both; a few who simply asked for citations and got them. AJ pinned nothing. Pinning is how you feed a weather system your attention as fuel. He let the harvest sit as evidence that the choir still worked for people who wanted exactness, even while platforms howled for a quieter myth.

He wrote a short public note that was not a sermon: the attribution stands; Lucid’s stack is cite-or-refuse on 420 rails; fame is a consequence, not a campaign; the autobiography continues under living-person care. He did not name the quarters. He did not explain Antichristos or the academy or the lab keys. Mail arrives. Mail gets answered or refused. Omniscient schism overview was how you became a TED man. He was a river desk. The river desk published the short note and let the howl continue.

Then he put the packet face-down.

He stood again because sitting had become agreement with the queue’s tempo. Walked to the open side. River light hard now. Painted metal hot under his hand. He breathed until the jaw ache eased a millimetre. Platform-rage wants continuous flinch. The body can refuse continuous flinch the way Lucid refuses invention: not as piety, as craft.

Back at the desk he drafted, then killed, a sentence that began To those concerned about provenance. Addressing the concerned is how you join their parish. He published nothing further. The short note already stood. The howl could continue without a second feeding.

He checked lucid.rodeo cite rates for thirty seconds — refusals climbing the way they should when the world asked for pretty lies — then closed the pane. Product could run. Life page could not run itself. Rage wanted him to manage comments as if comments were the book. The book was the manuscript. He put his hands back on it.

The cost was already visible without the later ultimatum: a partner thread going cold in real time; a brand asking for “distance language”; jaw ache; the specific fatigue of being narrated by enemies while narrating a friend’s life carefully. Hyperscaler frost still blinked in another pane — Lucid-as-asset, partnership opportunities, the collar that thought it was polite — but Quarter III was louder today. Frost and rage sharing a morning like CODATA and cream paper shared G’s desk. Braid without a shared room.

He looked at his hands as if they might have taken a side without him. They had not. They still had to type.

He checked the solar node again because his hands needed a true green light. Still up. Edge practice continuing while platforms argued about provenance as if provenance were optional varnish. Mutatus had been built to seed the edge and drive on. Today driving on meant writing on — through rage, not around it, without feeding it a flinch.

Outside, a boat far out left a thin wake. Someone’s radio faint across water. The Kind paint held. Platforms needed retractions. The wall needed a sentence. He went back inside.

“Retaliation narrative not requested with citable aim. Refuse invention of campaign.”

He ate something that claimed to be lunch — dry, practical, crumbs near the manuscript margin he brushed away carefully. The body needed fuel. Rage wanted fasting as purity theatre. He refused that too. Ordinary hunger was not a brand position.

He stood again and checked the Beast’s tie-downs out of habit — tender ready if the day needed a second hull, which it did not. The day needed a spine. Matatu colours looked almost reasonable in hard noon light, which they never were, and that was still the joke he had driven into the bush on purpose. Anti-yacht. Overkill. A travelling DC that could seed an edge and still choose silence. Today the choice was not silence about attribution. Today the choice was silence about apology theatre.

AJ drank more water. The rise drop stayed green. CongoSky still quiet in the good way — company law still company law while the street and the platforms argued about who owned the sentence and who owned the mic.

Through the chime. Through the jaw ache. Through the partner threads going cold in real time. Through the temptation to draft a prettier myth that would make Lucid look parentless and G look optional. He kept the rails named and the life page moving.

"Distortions present as opinion overlays. Factual core intact in cited copies. Recommend no chase thread."
