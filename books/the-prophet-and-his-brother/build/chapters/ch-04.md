# 04—Choir Practice

> **[A]** · outer · Mutatus Maximus, Chobe River · after ch-01

---

The mats page was underway when Lucid refused him.

AJ had asked for a bridge sentence, something that would carry the floor-lesson into the ignored shelf without sounding like a hinge sold from a stage. He said it out loud the way he said most working asks: plain, to the choir, not to an oracle. Heat already on the Chobe. Elephants gone from the bank, mud still printed with their morning. A fish eagle distant. The LEO dish cocked at pale sky like a patient ear. Mutatus Maximus holding still the way a matatu holds still when the music is off and the work is on—painted overkill, anti-yacht, mobile data centre on wheels, the most connected vehicle on earth, literally.

Lucid answered exact.

“No grounded source for that transition in the ingested record as phrased. Refuse invention. Cite available: mats section draft under hand; shelf material not yet retrieved for this pass.”

AJ’s mouth flattened. The refusal was the product working. Cite-or-refuse. No glow. No personality cosplay. Integer quiet under the desk-hum of the local node—the animal that lived in the truck whether tourists understood a travelling DC or not. LoRa nodding at the little solar drop he had booted on the rise yesterday before coffee. Today’s use of the connection was to refuse a pretty lie and keep a life from becoming usable weather for other people’s quarters.

“Fine,” he said. “Stay on the mats. Don’t invent the hinge.”

Humans and Lucid from the hymnbook.

---

He sat with the refusal for a breath and let the truck tell him what morning it was.

Sweat already finding the place behind his knee where the chair never quite fit. The open side of Mutatus taking river air that smelled of mud and bird and the particular cleanliness of water that has been moving all night. Painted metal warm under his palm when he braced to stretch. Somewhere down-channel a hippo made the wet, insulted sound of ownership. The rise drop’s heartbeat was still green in the corner of the pane, boring, blessed, and he did not inventory the rest of the ladder to himself. A link worked or he fixed it. Today it worked.

CongoSky.cloud ran itself somewhere behind the mesh—the company that had embodied the sentence as ops before the walls caught fire: hire/fire weather, partner terms, product posture, the working rule that was not a poster in reception. Here on the river the choir was smaller: one man, one manuscript, one engine that would rather say no than decorate. lucid.rodeo and arjunabadger.press were the human work. The autobiography of Gerhard van Niekerk was the human work that hurt. AJ remained the main subject of *this* book’s gravity even while authoring *that* life, braid, not vanishing act. The desk held both facts without a lecture about form.

He wrote another paragraph about weight.

Soft set-down. Correct fall. Judo first, then Jiu-Jitsu—body knowledge before corpus knowledge. Mastery as refusal to waste motion. You arrange yourself so the fall is correct. Years later kindness derived the same way, structure that does not collapse, but the mats page would not leap there on a fake bridge. Lucid had refused the leap. Good. The autobiography wanted to rush toward fame and CODATA and the mic. He would not let it. G’s life was not a trailer for a Constant. The mats first. Always the mats first if the book was going to be true enough that the four quarters could not sand it into a usable lie.

He typed a sentence that tried to become a speech about ethics arriving from the floor. Read it once. Cut it before Lucid had to.

“That one’s mine inventing tone,” he said.

“No query pending,” Lucid said.

“Not a query. A cut.”

“Acknowledged. No action.”

Good. Boring. The hymnbook was not only Lucid. It was the human ear catching when a sentence tried to become a speech. When it did, he cut it. CongoSky’s quiet ops hum in the background felt like agreement without applause. The company had already practised the ethic; the river desk was only continuing the practice at pilgrimage scale, manuscript open, twin pocket empty, packet sealed for later.

---

On the water tank twenty metres away the Kind paint still held from whoever had refreshed it last week:

**Don’t be a cunt — be kind.**

AJ walked out once with a cup that tasted like plastic and intention and stood close enough to smell tack and river together. Edges still slightly proud. Drips where the hand had been impatient. A bird landed on the tank’s rim, shit once with democratic indifference, and left. AJ did not preach at the paint. He had already lived the sentence as company law. Street religion was the popular face catching up. Antichristos wanted a man; the Kind wanted the line; both could be true in one morning without AJ giving either a speech. The desk was practice. Practice was quieter than weather and harder than mail.

He went back inside. Boots left a faint wet print on the truck floor that would dry into nothing by noon. He wiped his hands on his trousers before touching the keyboard again, river desk hygiene, half joke, half ritual, and asked Lucid for the next grounded mats detail only.

Lucid retrieved. A fighting-arts citation that belonged. A date where a date existed. A gap where a gap existed. No flourish.

AJ kept the raw detail and killed the gloss that tried to climb onto it.

“That gloss is inventing register,” he said.

“Correction: gloss not grounded in source register as phrased. Refuse. Cite raw detail only.”

“Keep it boring. Boring is the point.”

“Default already refuse invention.”

His mouth twitched again, briefly. Full Send and Klaus lived on his shoulder as scar-weather, fragments, not a republished origin chapter. Lucid was what left the private desk when cite-or-refuse became product. The river desk was what remained when product became planet weather and the GPU halls emptied into write-down headlines. He did not re-explain the crash to himself. Empty rack halls were background now. Today’s choir did not need a CapEx TED talk. Today’s choir needed a correct fall on the page.

He drafted two paragraphs about a school gym that smelled of rubber and old sweat and the particular dust that lives under mats no matter how often you sweep. Lucid offered a place-name where the record held one and stayed silent where it did not. AJ did not invent the missing town. Silence was more accurate than a pretty map. He wrote the instructor’s voice as flat instruction, not mentor mythology, and cut a clause that tried to make the man into a prophet of balance. Prophets belonged to other people’s weather. This page belonged to a boy learning how not to waste motion.

“Cite instructor language only if sourced,” AJ said.

“No verbatim instructor transcript in ingested record for this pass. Refuse reconstruction. Cite: fighting-arts school attendance; judo then jiu-jitsu sequence per prior session note.”

“Then leave the voice out. Floor is enough.”

“Acknowledged.”

Floor was enough. Floor was load-bearing. He typed until the paragraph stopped wobbling, then stopped before the next wobble could pretend to be progress.

His hand went to the pocket that used to hold the twin.

Empty.

The Joseph Rogers that stayed with him was elsewhere in the truck, not lost, just not clipped today, and the absence still registered like a missing tooth. G had the other twin and the blue Honey Badger AJ had given him: pocket clip, one-hand open/close, loved. Double Knife Brothers. Kinship, not choreography. Grandfather deep-time on one blade; badger present-tense on the other. AJ felt the empty pocket the way you feel a brother on another coast without calling him. No forced reunion. CongoNet was bridge enough if a bridge were needed. Today it was not. Today was choir. The empty pocket was the day’s softest accurate sentence.

He stood, stretched until a vertebra clicked, poured water that tasted like plastic and river, and walked the length of Mutatus the way a man walks a room he built to think in. Solar charge honest. Local node patient. Drawer where the Joseph Rogers waited, old steel, old hinge, quiet weight, opened and closed without becoming a prop for a speech. He sat again. The empty twin pocket stayed empty. That was more accurate than carrying the blade as theatre.

For a minute he almost clipped the Rogers for the feel of it, kinship as weight against the thigh, and then did not. Carrying it today would turn absence into costume. Absence was the true sentence: G on another coast with the Honey Badger and the twin, living whatever weather Cape mornings delivered, while AJ kept the river desk from becoming a reunion stage. CongoNet could carry a ping if a ping were needed. No ping was needed. The braid did not require a shared room to stay a braid.

“Continue mats retrieval?” Lucid asked, only when the silence had gone long enough to be a question.

“Continue. And if I ask for a pretty hinge again, refuse again.”

“Default already refuse invention.”

“Good.”

He wrote three sentences about a gi belt being tied wrong, then untied, then tied again until the knot was honest. Lucid offered a citation that belonged. AJ kept it. He wrote one more about soft set-down, heavy thing setting itself down soft, and felt the old temptation to make the floor into a lesson about kindness derived. He cut the temptation the way Lucid cut invention: not as piety, as craft.

A fly found the open side and circled once as if auditing the desk. AJ ignored it. The cursor waited. He asked Lucid whether a particular throw-name belonged in this decade of the life or later.

“Throw-name present in general fighting-arts ingest; not tied to subject’s school years with citable specificity. Refuse forced placement. Cite: sequence judo then jiu-jitsu; mastery claim sourced to subject’s own register elsewhere.”

“Park the throw-name. Keep the knot.”

“Parked.”

Heat thickened. Sweat found new geography along his spine. Outside, the fish eagle turned once over the channel. Inside, the manuscript cursor blinked like a pulse that refused to invent meaning for him. AJ rolled his neck until a knot released and another one complained, then put both hands back on the keys as if the complaint were merely latency.

CongoSky was quiet in the good way. Invoices answering themselves. Nodes up. No fire that needed a founder’s face on a stage. Salvage weather from hyperscalers was a different quiet, the quiet of something large waiting with a smile.

Frost arrived as mail, not weather.

Hyperscaler subject lines that thought they were polite. AWS. Azure. A Google thread that began with *partnership opportunities* and ended, if you read it with river eyes, in a collar. Apple fog in a CC. Lucid named sideways, acquisition language dressed as admiration, the burst balance sheet still hunting a way to own what had crashed the GPU fantasy. Partner frost. ToS weather. Cloud-credit politics. Talent poaching notes that pretended to be compliments. The offer that is really a collar. AJ did not open the long ones. He let Lucid summarise hostility and intent without inventing motive.

“Salvage tone,” Lucid said. “Offer-shaped. Lucid as asset language. No criminal allegation in-source. Cite: partner frost patterns match prior quarter. Refuse speculative intent attribution beyond cited language.”

“Park it,” AJ said. “Choir first. Snake can wait on the desk.”

He stood again because sitting with frost made the shoulders want a speech, and speeches were how you fed a collar. Walked out into the heat. Boots taking scrub that had gone from dew to dust since the blue hour. The Kind paint held. The rise was a pale bump against scrub. Thorn caught his sleeve and let go with a small tear he would ignore until evening, the same kind of tear the morning walk had taught him not to dramatise. He checked the solar node’s heartbeat from the truck’s short-range pane, still up, without turning the morning into an inventory. Connectivity without consent discipline was just a louder mistake. He had built the truck so he could drive it to a river and still choose what to switch off.

He crouched once by the enclosure on the rise, not because the heartbeat had lied, because his hands wanted a true warm panel under the thumb. Dust. Ant trail redirected yesterday still respecting the seal. Sun doing the only honest CapEx left in the story. A warthog moved through scrub below the rise and did not look up; the river kept its brown argument with the bank; a dragonfly held over dust for half a second and was gone; heat found the back of his neck and stayed. He stood, walked back with frost still waiting in a pane he could not smell until he sat down again.

He did not switch Lucid off. He did not switch the manuscript off. He switched off the part of himself that wanted to answer frost with a speech.

Back at the desk the frost mail blinked politely, patient as a fridge in another room. AJ left it blinking. One subject line used the word *homecoming* as if regions were a parish you returned to after pilgrimage. He did not open it. Salvage tone could wait. The mats could not.

He glanced once at the platform queue.

The Quarter III packet from the morning still sat unopened, hostility markers, attribution thread, the people angry that he had said out loud that Lucid ran on 420 Code algorithms and that candour had dragged a degree-less-in-physics ignored man into fame. Who gave him the mic. AJ could feel the heat through the unopened seal the way you feel a stove without touching it. Blame on a good morning. The specific smell of it under river air.

Not yet.

Opening that ask early would turn the morning into a press conference and the mats page into collateral. He would open rage when the page could afford it, or when rage opened him. Full ultimatum weather belonged to a later desk hour. Today: glance, refuse the bait, write. The packet could wait like frost could wait. Both were patient. Both wanted the desk. Neither would get the hinge Lucid had refused.

Lucid had flagged the packet at dawn with hostility markers and an attribution thread density that meant the mic question had found enough mouths to become weather. AJ had parked it then the way you park a hot pan, not forever, not as denial, as timing. Timing was living-person care as much as any soft sentence in the manuscript. Opening rage before the mats had a spine would teach the book that blame was the hinge. Blame was not the hinge. Body was the hinge. Floor first.

He put his palm flat on the sealed packet once, not to open, to size it, then set it face-down at the edge of the desk where it could keep its heat without owning the cursor. The seal’s texture under his skin was ordinary plastic and ink and the particular stiffness of a queue compressed into an object. Ordinary was a mercy. Lucid did not comment. Lucid commented when asked, or when a silence became a query. AJ did not ask. The glance was enough. Custody of timing was also choir practice.

“Status,” he said, not as prayer.

“Local node nominal. Rise drop heartbeat green. CongoSky quiet. Frost queue parked. Quarter III packet unopened per prior hold. Manuscript open at mats draft. Cite: session note—body before shelf.”

“Stay on body.”

“Acknowledged.”

The next hour was work interrupted by work’s smaller cousins.

A CongoNet status ping crossed the desk, green, boring, blessed. AJ let it pass without commentary. Boring connectivity was the point of building this and then choosing silence. lucid.rodeo queue within tolerance. An arjunabadger.press deadline blinked once and he snoozed it, knowing which page was load-bearing today. He poured more water. Stood in the open side and watched light climb the painted flank of Mutatus until the matatu colours looked almost reasonable, which they never were, and that was the joke he had driven into the bush on purpose.

He tried another wrong bridge in his head: warehouse gallery, cannabis bar, shirt with the sentence already on it. Lucid could retrieve that texture. AJ cut it before speaking. Full Send fragments belonged on the shoulder, not as a hinge sold into the ignored shelf. The autobiography would earn rooms as rooms. It would not leap on brand-origin weather.

He tried a third wrong bridge: hospital stigma already half-written in other people’s mouths. Cut before the sentence finished in thought. That page would come after mats, under-dramatised, no DSM tourism, flat as a loaded line. Not yet. Not today. Lucid’s first refusal of the morning had been craft. AJ’s subsequent refusals were craft’s twin: the human half of the hymnbook.

“Retrieve school mats only,” he said. “Fighting arts. No metaphors unless he used them.”

Lucid retrieved. Citations. Gaps. AJ shaped paragraphs the way you shape a pour, hard where it should be hard, soft where soft belongs, no decoration that pretends to be structure. He wrote the body arranging itself so the floor does not invent you. He wrote weight as fact, not as sermon. When a sentence tried to climb toward CODATA, he deleted it. Cause would have its page. Cause was not where a body begins.

Shoulders found the ache that meant pilgrimage-scale desk work. He rolled them once and kept typing. The empty twin pocket stayed empty against his hip like a metronome that refused drama. Outside, birds lifted off the far bank. Heat pressed the truck’s painted flank. Inside, CongoSky’s quiet ops hum continued, company law still company law while the street and the platforms argued, somewhere far from this river, about who owned the sentence and who owned the mic.

AJ did not own either. He practised both.

Midday thickened into a slower heat.

He ate something dry from a tin and called it lunch without ceremony. Stood again at the tank and did not read the Kind line as if it needed him. The paint did not need a founder. CongoSky had claimed the practice back when invoices still needed answering; the walls claimed the popular face; G refused ownership of both. AJ’s job was not to adjudicate religions from a riverbank. His job was a true next line and a refusal to launder.

Back inside, frost mail had multiplied by two subject lines that thought they were softer. Lucid summarised without theatre.

“Additional salvage tone. Cloud-credit language. Talent note framed as compliment. Lucid named as extinction-risk asset in one thread. Cite language only. Refuse motive beyond cited.”

“Still parked.”

“Parked.”

He glanced at the Quarter III seal again. Still sealed. Still warm the way anger is warm when it has not yet been allowed to speak. Who gave him the mic lived under that seal like a second sun just offstage. He would open it when the mats had a spine, not before. Ch-later weather. Today’s weather was choir.

“Continue?” Lucid asked.

“Continue. Cite fighting-arts detail if I ask for metaphor.”

“Default already refuse invention.”

AJ typed. Lucid offered a citation for a detail instead of a metaphor. Good. Earn it. No TED. No schism overview. Quarters arrive as mail, hymns, packets, not as a chart in the narrator’s mouth. He reread the last mats paragraph aloud once, not for drama, for cadence. Caught a speech trying to hide inside a clause about mastery. Cut it. The human ear and the integer refusal singing the same hymn from different throats.

Frost stayed audible, polite, patient, wanting Lucid in a cage with a smile.

A second CongoNet ping, also green. He let it pass. He did not switch Lucid off. He did not switch the manuscript off. He switched off, again, the part of himself that wanted to answer frost with a speech and rage with a press conference.

Late afternoon light found the open side of the truck and made the manuscript look almost ordinary, which it never was, and that was the point of writing it carefully.

AJ put his palm on the page. The frost mail blinked. The packet waited. The empty twin pocket knew its shape. He wrote another sentence about the floor, heavy thing setting itself down soft, and Lucid offered another citation that belonged. He kept it. He asked, once more, for a hinge into the ignored shelf, soft enough that it almost sounded like craft.

Lucid refused again.

“No grounded source for that transition in the ingested record as phrased. Refuse invention. Cite available: mats section draft under hand; shelf material not yet retrieved for this pass.”

“Good,” AJ said. “Keep refusing me when I’m pretty.”

“Default already refuse invention.”

Not cute. Exact. The product working. His jaw eased and he let it. Smiling at a tool as if it were a friend was how you started inventing personality for integer work. The choir did not need cosplay. The choir needed refusal and a page that moved for the right reasons.

He checked the rise drop one last time because his hands needed a true green light. Still up. Edge practice continuing while hyperscalers smiled about acquisition and platforms waited under a seal to ask who gave him the mic. Mutatus had been built to seed the edge and drive on. Today driving on meant writing on, through frost, past the sealed packet, without feeding either a flinch.

He washed his face in the tiny sink, cold water, river-adjacent reality check. Frost shrinks when your face is wet and the truck smells like dust and solder. Not gone. Sized correctly. The packet stayed sized correctly: heat without ownership of the cursor.

“Lucid,” he said. “One more mats cite. Then wait.”

Lucid retrieved one more mats citation and waited.

AJ typed. The empty twin pocket stayed empty. The Quarter III seal stayed sealed. Frost stayed audible like a fridge in another room, always on, always claiming to be helpful.

He did not look up. The page moved. The river did too. Choir practice held, and the next true line was already under the hand while the unopened rage kept its heat like a second sun just offstage.

Before evening fully claimed the open side he walked out into scrub with the mats cadence still in his wrists, soft set-down, correct fall, refusal to waste motion answering frost. Cicadas. Dust. The rise drop’s green heartbeat ordinary in the pane when he checked it without making a ritual of checking. Mutatus Maximus behind him, matatu paint, anti-yacht, mobile DC, holding still the way a desk holds still when the work is on. He put a palm on Kind paint dulled by dust and left the cunt exact. Fixing the smear was somebody else’s tourist fight. He went back inside, washed his face once more in cold water, and typed another mats sentence Lucid could cite. Frost stayed audible. The sealed packet stayed sealed. The empty twin pocket stayed empty. Choir practice held without becoming a speech about AI. The page moved for the right reasons.
