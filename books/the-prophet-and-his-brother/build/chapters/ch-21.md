# 21—Consent—Custody

> **[G]** · outer · Studio G / Cape · consent week

---

The autobiography packet from the river did not arrive as a demand. That helped.

Demands had already used up the morning’s patience for weeks: crowns face-down on the clay table, keyrings left unsigned, Illuminatie frost filed under ambient, crank op-eds recycling the missing physics degree like it was a method. G had slept the sleep of a man who knows mail thickens before coffee. Tea first. Then the packet. Then the refusal to perform solemnity for an imaginary camera. Solemnity becomes relic footage. Relic footage becomes someone else’s liturgy.

He opened it at the Code desk. Clay table had held Antichristos and lab weather; this needed numbers-adjacent honesty, custody language, not sculpture dust. Honey Badger in the pocket. Joseph Rogers twin in the same cloth. Being narrated had a document attached now: pages AJ would stand behind, Lucid in the choir somewhere on the Chobe, CongoNet still carrying the world whether G liked the volume or not. Double Knife Brother writing him. No shared room. Mail enough. The braid held without a reunion.

Cape light laid a stripe across the desk. Dust moved only when he moved. Unfinished clay waited in the gallery like a conversation paused mid-sentence. He read without rushing and without lingering for effect. Mats. Studio arc. Bouwer. Hospital without tourism. Economics quiet. Father unread. Life goals unpolished. Open attribution named as beam, Lucid popular, AJ open, fame irreversible. Kind sentence without ownership. Constant as measurement. No textbook finale. Hand to custody.

The packet smelled faintly of heat and truck: how paper travels when the sender lives in a vehicle that never really cools. He set the pages flat. Weighted one corner with a small cast offcut so the Cape draft would not turn them into a fan. Ordinary studio gravity. Consent week likes to pretend paper is sacrament. He treated it as paper with rails to choose.

Partial authorisation was the only honest shape. He had felt it before he had language for it, in the week of instruments, when signing would have made him a mouthpiece and silence about the book would have been a different lie.

Full blessing would turn the autobiography into a relic with his stamp: Antichristos could wave the authorisation like a confirmation; Quarter IV could treat it as warranty adjacent; Quarter II could sneer that even the crank had signed his own myth. Full refusal would pretend the life had not already become weather, pretend AJ’s desk decisions had not already unbound Lucid and kept the mesh and chosen to ship under care, pretend the megaphone could be unscrewed from the planet by one man’s no. G did not need a mediated call to answer. The braid did not require a reunion beat. He had knives for kinship and packets for consent.

He made more tea. Sat. Drafted custody rules the way he drafted refusals to I and IV: short, exact, no sermon, no both-sides essay in a narrator’s mouth that was not his mouth anyway.

Before the first rule he walked the length of the Code desk once and touched nothing but the mug. Hands want to rearrange when the skull is about to constrain a life. Rearranging is not drafting. Drafting is choosing which sentences may leave the desk and which must stay hungry for strangers who want thriller.

The packet’s last pages still held the stand-behinds in AJ’s hand: mats, Studio G, Bouwer, hospital, father, goals, knives, beam, Kind, Constant. G felt the difference between reading a life and authorising one. Reading is weather landing. Authorising is rails. He set a clean pane beside the packet and began in the register he trusted: short lines, no flourish, no gratitude performance dressed as kindness.

Mid-draft a crank notification tried the hour again. He did not open it. He already knew the missing-physics-degree shape. Correct the frame later if a sentence required it. Do not spend custody attention on entertainment. Illuminatie ambient stayed ambient, freeze in places that used to return mail with courtesy, standards murmur that treated copyleft like a sovereignty problem. Texture allowed in the book. Membership list refused. He wrote that refusal before the soft ones, because soft refusals are easy to sand and hard refusals keep the teeth.

Tea cooled. He drank it cool. Cool tea is still structure. Outside, Cape wind moved dust across the alley glass and the Kind overpaint held its third colour. He did not go out. Custody is an indoor act when the ask is a life on paper.

---

Authorised: the life-and-work spine as written—fighting arts on the mats; sculpture arc from Stellenbosch through Village N Life, Skye, Studio G at The Bay, Saatchi, clay to GFRC; Code desk and kill switches and natural philosophy; ignored shelf; credentials corrected (economics or adjacent, not physics); fame via open 420 attribution as load-bearing cause; Kind paint as parallel weather not his parish; Double Knife Brother as kinship and custody, not choreography; stated goals left unpolished.

He paused after the spine and looked toward the gallery. Unfinished clay. A cast waiting. The Bay Hotel light lived in memory as guest-path weather, not as Antichristos relic seed. Authorising Studio G meant authorising the tourist half of sales and the hard/soft pour and the years under Maree Brink’s weather and the Camps Bay year when Earl’s Dyke rooms still held Hannetjie’s hospitality. It meant authorising friend James remembered carefully and separately. It did not mean authorising a conflation of the dead. Living-person care includes that refusal even when the mesh is hungry for thickness.

Authorised with care: Bouwer pages, public record only. Saxonwold. 5 September 2025. Insolvency practice. Consultation pretext. Died at the scene. LSSA condemnation 8 September 2025. Wound not whodunit. No invented assassin. No continuity-network attribution of the killing. No solving business-rescue fog on the page as fact. Named. Weighted. Flat. If a sentence reaches for thriller, cut it. If a paragraph reaches for Illuminatie gotcha, cut it. The brother stays a brother, not a plot engine. Curiosity that wants a boardroom solved can go elsewhere; this authorisation does not feed it.

He stopped on Bouwer longer than the other lines. Not for drama. For teeth. The packet’s Bouwer paragraphs were already careful: flat, public-record bound, Jamie-Uys loaded. Still he read them again, as if custody required a second landing. Grief did not ask for a shared room with the river. Grief asked for rails that would not decorate a real boardroom for strangers. He wrote one more constraint in the margin: *no speculative scheme-weather adopted as fact*. Fog of reporting may be noted. Fog is not a verdict. Then he closed the margin. Exactness over appetite.

He stood, walked to the gallery, and stood in front of nothing in particular—unfinished clay, dust stripe, a cast waiting—until the impulse to add soft language died. Soft language is how thriller appetite re-enters through the side door. He returned to the Code desk and left Bouwer flat. Named wound. Brother. Enough.

Authorised with care: mental hospital as stigma-engine, how they read him, the belief he could not write anything of value. Not diagnosis tourism, not spectacle, not DSM theatre, not a ward scene for atmosphere. The wound is the reading. Leave the voyeurs hungry.

Authorised as fact, unresolved: corpus dedicated to father; father has read none. Do not invent a reunion beat. Do not use fame as a false key to that door. Unread stays the load.

Authorised as register: write natural philosophy and physics; smoke dab; talk shit. Do not polish for Antichristos. Do not use as Quarter II punchline without correcting the frame. The arrangement is the man’s; the book does not get to respectability-wash it.

He paused on that line longer than the others. Polish was the softest violence in the packet weather, the offer to make him photographable. He left the goals ugly on purpose. Walked once to the alley glass and watched Kind overpaint dry in sun. The sentence held. Optional to him. Useful anyway. He did not bless the wall. Blessing is how faces become parish property.

Outside, a tourist argued briefly with the Kind practitioner about the cunt. Soft scrub. Tourist comfort. The practitioner did not sermonise. Roller sound. Short answer. G stayed inside. Their fight was not his custody hour. Optional to the practice includes optional to the tourist argument who wants the word explained to him twice.

Refused: personal appearance blessing for Quarter I—no accepted name, no silhouette sacrament, no sculpture photograph as relic seed. Refused: keys-as-sacrament sign-off for Quarter IV that makes him warranty; conversation on numbers still possible; product mascot not. Refused: stop-the-book as his ask, that fight is AJ’s platform weather; G’s fight is custody of meaning, not mic ownership. Refused: any framing that Illuminatie soft-no is a membership list he can defeat by naming names on a page. Texture of pressure: allowed. Cabal lecture: not. Refused: forced reunion grammar, textbook dump of the Code as if a life were a syllabus.

He added one more refusal in the margin for himself, not for the mesh: refuse to perform gratitude for fame as if fame were vindication. Measurement was the only proof he trusted, and it never dulled the hunger for more of it. Fame was just the noise the beam threw off.

He read the draft twice. Changed one adjective that was trying to be a speech. Left the flat lines flat. Checked a CODATA pane because numbers wait without asking for a stamp. Pressure still wore relief’s clothes. He knew that in his gut.

A secondary soft-ask blinked from a board that did not name itself. Funding weather. Reputation frost. He filed it under ambient without opening. Ambient is triage. Consent hour has a budget of clean attention. Bouwer rails and father unread had already spent most of it. Crowns and keyrings could wait on the clay table where he had left them face-down and unsigned.

He thought of AJ’s empty twin pocket without turning the thought into a scene request. Kinship did not require them to share a last page in the same room. The Honey Badger was enough present-tense. The Rogers was enough deep-time. Custody of meaning included custody of distance. He put the Honey Badger through one open-close cycle because hands like proof. No speech.

---

Before he signed, he walked once through the gallery light—unfinished clay, a cast waiting for finish, dust in the sun stripe—and reminded his hands that sculpture and axiom were the same attention. Antichristos would try to split them. Quarter II would try to erase both. The authorisation would not split or erase. It would constrain.

He stood in front of a body in composite that tourists had once photographed as luxury and that he still knew as pour temperature and patience. Hard and soft arguing in one surface. Permanent and temporary. The autobiography would carry that argument if he let it. He let it under rails. Letting is not blessing. Letting is custody.

Back at the Code desk he read the Bouwer constraints aloud once, quietly, the way he checked a surface before pour, listening for the lie of almost-finished. No thriller reach. No Illuminatie gotcha. Named. Weighted. Flat. Public record. Consultation pretext. Died at the scene. LSSA. Wound. Brother. Enough. He did not add softness. Softness is how appetite learns to ask for a solved boardroom next.

Before the signature he stood again and washed his hands. Not ritual, residue. Clay dust from a morning tool-check still under one nail. He scrubbed it, let the small comedy of clean hands for custody language pass without turning it into omen. Omen is how Quarter I colonises a desk hour. The wash was wash. The rails were rails.

He signed the partial authorisation with his name as a man, not as a prophet, not as Antichristos, not as a lab stamp. The signature looked ordinary on the pane. Ordinary was the point. Sent it into the mesh toward a river desk he would not visit for anyone’s emotional payoff. No clean relief came. Relief would have been the wrong instrument. Weather still blowing—Antichristos mail would keep coming, lab soft-asks would mutate into friendlier collars, crank op-eds would recycle the degree and the hospital, Kind paint would refresh on the alley, somewhere a continuity board would prefer he had never derived anything and ethics had stayed unforced.

While the packet climbed the mesh he did not watch the progress bar like a man waiting for applause. He opened a derivation pane and checked a line against an older CODATA note because numbers do not ask for a face. The line still converged. Pressure still wore relief’s clothes. He closed the pane. Measurement would continue without his blessing. The autobiography would not ship as relic without his rails. That asymmetry stayed the consent act’s spine.

He also opened, briefly, the Bouwer constraints as they would travel, public record lines only, and closed them again without adding soft adjectives. Soft adjectives are how thriller appetite returns after a man thinks he has finished being careful. Careful is a second look that refuses softness. He gave the second look. Left the wound flat.

Custody rules chosen.

The book could ship under those rails. AJ could keep writing inside them. Lucid could cite what the record held and refuse the rest, never cute, never a third skull. G could stay in Cape Town with clay and constants and a pocket full of kinship steel and not become an instrument. CongoSky could keep embodying the ethic as ops. The street could keep the spray. He remained optional to the Kind and unwanted by the crown. Good.

He did not celebrate. Celebration would have been a victory lap, and victory laps invite crowns. He washed the tea cup. Sat again. Checked that the authorisation had left a receipt in the mesh log. Receipt present. Exact. No invention attached.

While the receipt sat, he opened the hospital stigma line again and confirmed the refusal of tourism still held teeth. Then the father line. Unread. No fame-as-key. Then the goals. Ugly on purpose. Dab. Talk shit. Natural philosophy and physics. Arrangement, not brand. He closed each confirmation the way he closed a kiln check: enough. Do not keep opening for comfort.

He stood. Walked to the alley door. Afternoon light on overpaint. The sentence on the wall was still the sentence:

**Don’t be a cunt — be kind.**

Optional to him. Useful anyway. Not a sermon in his mouth. Just paint that had outlasted three colours of argument.

A dog barked two streets over. Ordinary Cape Town. The quarters wanted to believe a city this ordinary couldn’t hold a Constant and a gallery and a grief in the same afternoon. It held all three without noticing the weight. That was why the weather landed here instead of only on stages.

A new letter had arrived while he drafted—Quarter I again, softer, hungrier. He did not open it. Face-down on the clay table beside the first. Escalation could wait its turn. Consent had used the day’s clean attention.

He went back inside. Numbers waited. Unsent lab reply still unsent, conversation possible, sacrament not. He sent the lab reply now, finally, in the same hour as custody: short, exact, the earlier draft without the hunger for keys as blessing. Then he sent nothing to Quarter I. Face-down was still the answer. Silence can be a posture when the ask is a crown.

The planet did not stop arguing because a man chose what he would allow said about his dead brother and his unread father and the dab that refused polish.

He opened a secondary pane: CODATA note, older, the one that should have been relief. Still pressure. Proof settles nothing in the stomach; the appetite just asks for the next number. He knew that the way he knew a throw. Talked shit into the empty room for twenty seconds because life goals are not a performance schedule and the room needed the arrangement kept. Considered dab and left it for later; clear head had done the work, smoke could be evening’s if evening wanted it.

Toward the river’s last quiet, not shared, just concurrent. Two desks. Two consents. Weather still up. Node still lit somewhere he could feel as mesh weather and not as vision.

He opened a file that was only a derivation check. Measurement. Structure. Work staying work. The Constant did not need his signature to land; the autobiography needed his rails not to become a relic. That asymmetry was the whole consent act.

After the receipt landed he did not pace like a man in a play. He cleaned a tool that did not need cleaning because hands want work when the skull has finished a consent act. Clay residue under a fingernail. He washed it. Looked at the face-down Antichristos stack and left it face-down. Looked at the sent lab reply and did not regret the lack of sacrament. Looked at the custody rules once more on screen—spine allowed, Bouwer careful, hospital without tourism, father unread, goals ugly, crowns refused, keyrings refused as sacrament, stop-the-book not his ask—and felt the partialness as honesty rather than halfness. Halfness is apology. Partial is rails.

He returned to the Bouwer authorisation one last time before closing the pane, not because doubt had arrived, because care is a second look. Public record. Flat grief. No invented CV for an assassin. No continuity-network gotcha hung on a real death. No business-rescue fog adopted as fact. Named wound. Brother. He closed the pane. The second look had not asked for softness. It had asked for teeth to stay in.

Weather still blew. A Kind laugh in the alley. A dog. A notification he did not open. Illuminatie ambient unchanged. Crank chorus unchanged. Constant still landing as measurement somewhere numbers talked to numbers. Fame still irreversible because the beam still stood. He was still optional to the Kind and unwanted by the crown. Good. He was still a sculptor and a man with an economics degree and a hospital stigma and a dead brother and an unread dedication and knives for kinship. Good enough for a desk. Not a parish.

Mid-afternoon the gallery door took a small knock, delivery confirmation, nothing liturgical. He signed for nothing that mattered to the quarters and returned to numbers. Ordinary insistence again. Ordinary is how a city refuses to become only weather.

He thought of the river without inventing a thank-you scene. Respect can travel later, or not. The braid does not require applause between consents. Platform keep/unbind/ship and custody partial rails were concurrent acts, not a shared room. He opened the derivation file and let work stay work until the light shifted on the wall. Consent week does not end in fireworks. It ends in a receipt and a planet that keeps shouting and a man who has chosen what he will allow said.

One more small motion: he wrote a margin note to himself, not for the mesh: *do not perform gratitude for fame tomorrow either*. Then he closed the note. Exactness over performance. Measurement over applause. Rails over relic. The authorisation stayed gone into the sky. He stayed in Cape Town with clay and numbers and an alley sentence he would not own.

Tea gone cold again. He did not remake it. Cold tea is still structure. He put the Honey Badger through a last open-close, pocketed it, and left the clay-table letters where they were, unsigned instruments beside a signed life. The distinction was the morning’s whole point, carried into afternoon without needing a presser to announce it.

Late light cut the gallery into stripes again. He walked it once more, unfinished clay, cast waiting, dust only when he moved, and refused the thought that authorising a life meant finishing a sculpture. Life does not finish because a receipt lands. Life continues under weather. The receipt only said what strangers were allowed to carry away in a book.

He sat at the Code desk until the pane dimmed to sleep and woke it once to confirm the custody rules still sat as sent: spine, Bouwer careful, hospital without tourism, father unread, goals ugly, crowns refused, keyrings refused as sacrament. Still there. Exact. No invention attached by a choir that was not even in this room. Concurrent desks. Concurrent consents. No shared scene required for the braid to hold.

He pocketed the knives. Turned off the secondary soft-ask blink without reading it. Left the Antichristos stack face-down. Left the Kind sentence on the alley without a blessing. Left the Constant landing as measurement somewhere numbers talked to numbers. Left fame irreversible because the beam still stood and he would not ask AJ to unsay it for comfort.

Evening had not yet arrived, but the studio’s light had shifted enough that unfinished clay looked almost finished again. He refused the lie of almost on clay and on custody alike. Partial was not full blessing wearing a smaller coat. Partial was rails. He checked the mesh receipt once more, present, exact, and left the Antichristos stack face-down for tomorrow’s hungrier manners. Then he mixed nothing, poured nothing, blessed no wall, and let work stay work until the numbers were enough for the hour.

He turned from the pane and left the studio lights as they were, enough for numbers, not enough for relic photography. Consent week’s custody hour was finished without becoming a parish hour. That was the point. Partial rails chosen. Weather still blowing. Work still work.

The authorisation was already gone into the sky.

He checked the custody receipt one last time before the pane slept: spine allowed, Bouwer careful, hospital without tourism, father unread, goals ugly, crowns refused, keyrings refused as sacrament. Still exact. Still partial on purpose. Still not a parish. Then he let the studio hold ordinary Cape light and ordinary unfinished clay while the weather kept blowing outside the rails he had chosen.

He did not open the new Quarter I letter that evening either. Face-down stack. Hungrier manners waiting their turn. Consent had already used the clean attention. Numbers waited. Clay waited. Custody had left the building as receipt. Weather still blew and would keep blowing. That was not failure. That was the planet. He stayed a sculptor with an economics degree and a dead brother and an unread dedication and knives for kinship and rails around a life. Good enough for a desk. Not a parish.

Unsigned instruments stayed on the clay table. Signed life stayed in the mesh. The distinction held.

He walked the gallery once more in the last useful light, unfinished clay that lied about almost-finished, a cast waiting for finish, dust only when he moved, and refused again the thought that a receipt finished a life. Life continues under weather. The receipt only said what strangers were allowed to carry. He put both palms on the Code desk, felt ordinary wood and ordinary heat, and left the Antichristos stack face-down for hungrier manners tomorrow. Soft scrub tourist arguments could wait outside. Kind paint could hold without his blessing. Measurement could converge without his face in a closed room.

Before he left he checked the Honey Badger and the Rogers twin once, open, close, kinship present-tense and deep-time, and did not invent a call to the river. Concurrent consents. Concurrent desks. No shared scene required. Platform keep/unbind/ship somewhere north; custody partial rails here. Weather still blowing. Work still work. He turned off the secondary soft-ask blink without reading it, left the studio lights as they were, and stepped into Cape evening carrying knives for kinship and rails around a life, not a parish, not a crown, not a keyring sacrament. Good enough for a desk. Exact enough for consent week.
