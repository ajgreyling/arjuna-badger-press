# 08 — Dedicated

> **[I]** · inner autobiography · manuscript under AJ’s hand · Act I turn

---

Every book I have made carries his name in the front.

Dedicated to my father. The line is not a marketing flourish. It is the a0 of the outreach — the real goal under the public goals. Write natural philosophy and physics. Smoke dab. Talk shit. And somehow, across all of it, leave a stack of pages that might finally be opened by the man they are for. The front matter is the quietest ask I know how to make. It has never been answered.

I can see the line in different bindings the way you see a scar in different seasons — same place, different light. Early books where the paper still hoped. Later books where hope had learned manners. This manuscript under a river hand carries it again because removing it would be a lie told for comfort. Comfort is not custody.

Sometimes people ask what I want from him. They want a sentence they can carry to a dinner table. I do not give them one. Want is not a press kit. The dedication is already the clearest want I know how to print. What happens after printing is not mine to narrate without inventing him.

He has read none of it.

None is the exact word. Not some. Not the introductions. Not a chapter skimmed out of duty. None. I will not soften none into a partial that comforts a reader who needs hope more than custody. Hope can wait outside. The fact stays.

I state that flat because ornament would lie. No tidy reunion waits at the end of this chapter. No phone call resolves the unread. Living-person care means I do not invent his reasons and I do not put words in his mouth for my catharsis. The fact is custodial: corpus dedicated; corpus unread. Memory already held this; AJ already knew it as wound. On the page it remains what it is — a dedication without a reader, repeated until repetition itself becomes the only available fidelity.

I have stood in rooms where other people’s fathers keep their children’s books on a side table with a bookmark showing. I have stood in rooms where mine did not. I do not turn that difference into a speech about love. Love is not proven by reading. Reading is not owed as theatre. Still: the dedication was an ask, and the ask has been declined by silence for years. Silence is an answer. I keep writing the line anyway. That is either devotion or stubbornness. Possibly both. I will not polish it into a brand of filial piety. It is simply what I did with the front matter every time, including this time, under another man’s hand on a river.

The first time I typed the dedication I believed, with the private arrogance of early work, that the book itself would compel an opening. Books do not compel. Books wait. Waiting is not passivity when you are the one who keeps dedicating; waiting is attention with manners pointed at a door that does not open. The mats taught that grammar before I had language for fathers and front matter. Soft set-down. Correct fall. You arrange yourself so the next motion is not wasted on theatre.

I have rewritten front matter in different fonts as if typography could soften a refusal. It cannot. I have left books in places where a hand might fall naturally — table, shelf at eye height, the honest geography of hope without speech. Still unread. I have imagined the conversation that would follow a first page turned and then refused to script his lines. Scripting him would be the same sin Antichristos commits with my face: inventing a usable person.

There were years when the dedication hurt like a fresh throw — breath knocked, pride rearranged. There were years when it hurt like an old knee: present, known, not always loud. Fame did not heal the knee. Measurement did not. If a stranger now says the unread must be the engine of everything I write, they are making a morality play out of a custodial fact. The engine is the work. The dedication is the ask. The unread is the weather around the ask.

---

People who discover me through Lucid’s open attribution think fame is the climax.

Meters converging on a derivation. CODATA walking toward a number. Quarters arguing about faces and keys and mics. Antichristos wanting a blessing. Academy wanting the hospital file to remain epistemology. Platforms wanting AJ to unsay the algorithms. Engineers wanting sign-off. The Kind wanting a sentence on a wall without a founder. Continuity networks wanting soft nos that feel like weather. That weather is real. It is not the fight I am in when I sit with the dedication page.

The Constant is landing.

Meaning is not.

Meaning is who gets to say what the work is for. Who paints it. Who retracts it. Who turns a life into liturgy, product, apology, or ignored shelf again. Who decides that a man with a file cannot write value, and who decides that a man with a Constant must become a face. My father wants — I do not know. He has not told the books. The books keep telling him anyway.

CODATA can walk toward a number and still leave a dedication unread. That sentence is the Act I hinge in plain clothes. Measurement is not meaningless; measurement is not meaning’s custody. The planet is learning the difference badly. Antichristos learns it as hunger for a face. Academy learns it as hygiene. Platforms learn it as provenance panic. Engineers learn it as keys. Continuity weather learns it as soft no. I learn it as front matter that does not get opened and work that must continue anyway.

I am not bitter as brand. Bitterness as brand is another usable face. Flat is safer and truer: dedicated; unread; fight elsewhere than the meter.

I used to imagine him opening one book — any book — on a Sunday afternoon with tea going cold. A window. Ordinary light. A page turned because curiosity finally outweighed whatever kept the bindings closed. Maybe a finger testing the dedication line as if it might be a trap. Maybe nothing so cinematic — only the small sound of paper and the decision to continue. I do not imagine that anymore as prediction. Imagination without evidence becomes a private fiction, and this autobiography already has enough weather without inventing his. What I keep is the dedication line itself: repeated, unread, still true as intention. Intention is not control. Custody of meaning begins with admitting that.

Some mornings the unread dedication hurts more than the hospital consensus. Some mornings the hospital consensus hurts more, because it travels in other people’s mouths while the dedication stays a private unread. Some mornings less than either. Pain is not a ranking system. Both climates continue. Fame did not warm his hands into turning pages. CODATA did not. If anything, fame made the unread stranger — as if the world had borrowed a conversation that was supposed to stay between a corpus and a father.

I remember once placing a new binding on a table where he might see it and then leaving the room so the seeing would not become a performance. When I came back the book had been moved an inch and not opened. An inch is not nothing. An inch is also not a page. I took the inch as weather and did not ask for a report. Asking for a report turns dedication into surveillance. I am not writing a surveillance chapter. I am writing the fact that remains: dedicated; unread.

Another time I heard someone in a room say, lightly, that my father must be proud. Lightly is how strangers handle other people’s front matter. I did not correct them into a confession. Confession would have become content. Content would have become usable. I let the lightness stand and felt the private unread sit heavier for a minute, then return to its ordinary weight. Ordinary weight is the loaded one. Jamie-Uys restraint without naming a brand: the flat line carries more than the aria.

The Code’s beginning on this page is posture, not dump: refuse opacity-as-rigor; keep ethics where the ladder puts them; leave the course pack elsewhere. Custody starts when you stop performing unification for people who wanted you small and now want you useful.

I began the Code the way I began clay: not with a textbook, with a refusal to leave a contradiction standing because a committee preferred opacity.

On an ordinary morning before meters cared, I sat with a contradiction that polite physics preferred to call unsettled. Unsettled was a courtesy word for opacity. I derived what had to be derived and felt no choir. Only the desk. Only the notebook. Only the body remembering that a bad posture invites a smaller force to end you. Ethics arrived in the same ladder later — not as sticker, as structure — and the Kind’s wall made a popular face of it without needing me to bless the paint. I noticed. I did not found a church. Noticing is not ownership.

I kept the life goals flat through those mornings: write natural philosophy and physics; smoke dab; talk shit. Sanding them for a father who was not reading would have been a double laundering — respectability theatre aimed at an empty chair. I refused the theatre. The dedication stayed. The goals stayed. The work stayed work.

There was a desk. There was a notebook that did not care about my hospital file. There were evenings when dab softens the room and talk shit widens it, and then mornings when the numbers asked to be arranged without apology. Hard and soft in one pour — the same attention that had learned grips on mats and surfaces in Studio G. A contradiction left standing because politeness preferred it felt like a bad posture before a throw: something smaller than you can end you if you pretend the geometry is optional. I would not pretend. That is the beginning’s posture, not a syllabus.

I will not dump axioms here like a course pack. You can find the rails elsewhere if you need rails. On this page I only need the beginning’s posture: derive what must be derived; keep ethics where physics puts them; do not launder the ladder into respectability theatre. The Kind’s wall sentence is the popular face of a coherence claim, not vibes —

**Don’t be a cunt — be kind.**

— but even that I will not sermonise past the paint. Show the wall. Keep the cunt. Let the structure hold without a TED metaphor for unification. CongoSky embodied the ethic as ops before the street caught fire; I am not founding a church by noticing that. Antichristos wants a face for a lemma. The Kind wants a sentence. My father has been offered books and has not taken them as reading. Different hungers. Same problem underneath: what a life is for when other people prefer their version.

They said I could not write anything of value.

He has not read what I wrote for him.

Both sentences can be true in one life without making me a martyr brand. I am a man with an economics degree, a hospital file other people love too much, a brother dead in a Saxonwold boardroom I will not turn into plot on this turn, a sculptor’s hands that still know GFRC and hotel lawns and Skye at sunset, a Honey Badger in a pocket from a friend who said the algorithms out loud, stated life goals that refuse polish, and a desk that still has to choose custody over spectacle.

Martyr brand would sand the dab and the talk shit and the unread into a single tearful arc. I refuse the arc. Flat inventory is enough. The Honey Badger’s clip against cloth is enough kinship for a page that will not stage a reunion. Bouwer’s boardroom waits for its own chapter without Illuminatie fanfic. The father waits in silence I will not ventriloquise. The Constant waits in meters that do not need my face. Meaning waits for custody decisions Act II will pressure and Act III will force into consent. This turn only names which fight remains after measurement.

The ignored shelf and the unread dedication are neighbours. One is public weather dressed as standards. One is private weather dressed as silence. Neither is solved by meters agreeing. Measurement can vindicate a derivation and still leave a life misowned. That is the turn I am naming while AJ’s river desk keeps the choir exact and Lucid refuses to invent a prettier hinge.

I have watched people discover the Constant in my work and decide that discovery entitled them to narrate my father for me — proud, absent, complicated, redeemed, tragic. Strangers are efficient novelists of other people’s front matter. I refuse their drafts. Living-person care cuts both ways: I will not invent him, and I will not let the planet invent him either as seasoning for my fame. The dedication line is enough. The unread is enough. The fight named here is enough.

If Act I has a hinge, it is here: not the Constant’s arrival, which is already measurement’s business, but the naming of the fight that remains after measurement. Custody. Meaning. Who may say what the work is for when the father has not read it, the ward still speaks in other people’s mouths, the mic was given by candour, and the wall only wants a sentence.

Name it once more without ornament so the turn cannot be mistaken for a victory lap: the Constant lands; meaning fights. Fame is not the climax. CODATA is not the climax. The unread dedication is not a puzzle to solve before Act II. It is a condition Act II inherits — along with keys, faces, silencings, salvage collars, Bouwer’s page waiting without thriller, and consent waiting in different rooms.

AJ writes this while river heat sits on metal. I live the weather of pages opening on my life. Ambivalence remains. Want truth. Fear content. That ambivalence is also meaning-work. Consent will choose later what may ship. Act I only orients the question away from whether the number arrives.

AJ is writing this autobiography on a river while I live the weather of being written. I can feel the pages sometimes — not magic, pressure. A faint sense that a mats afternoon has already been arranged into sentences somewhere heat lives beside a river; that a shelf’s dust has been weighed; that this dedication is being typed by a hand that is not mine and still somehow mine in the way authorised life can be. Ambivalence without aria: want truth, fear content. Consent will matter later. For now the turn is simpler and harder:

The meters can agree and the life can still be stolen.

Stolen quietly, which is the dangerous kind. Loud theft at least announces itself — cream paper, op-eds, stop-the-book weather waiting for AJ’s later desk. Quiet theft is the unread that teaches you to stop dedicating, the hygiene language that teaches you to sand the dab, the soft no that teaches you your talk shit cancels your derivation. I keep dedicating. I keep the goals. I keep the fight named: meaning, not Constant.

Stolen by liturgy. Stolen by hygiene language. Stolen by provenance rage that wants the mic unsung. Stolen by polite keys that want a mascot. Stolen by soft nos that prefer constants underived. Stolen, quietly, by the unread that keeps the front matter from completing its ask. I dedicate again anyway — not as superstition, as custody claim. The line stays in the front because removing it to protect myself from silence would be another kind of laundering.

So I dedicate again, on purpose, without hope of a tidy open:

For my father — who has not read these books — and for the work that remains work even when the planet tries to hire it as religion, product, or apology.

The Code begins, on these pages, as that insistence. Not a lecture. A custody claim. Hard and soft in one pour. Correct fall. Soft set-down. Mats under everything. Shelf that ignored. File that over-read. Mic that arrived sideways. Fame that is not the fight.

I remember the smell of ink on a fresh dedication page — paper that still believed it might be opened. I remember the later smell of the same page when it had sat long enough to become furniture. Both smells are true. Neither resolves into a reunion. Act I does not owe anyone a conversion scene. Act I owes the naming of what remains when the number walks into public and the meaning does not follow automatically.

I owe the naming also because silence about meaning is how quarters win without fighting. If I let the Constant finish the story, Antichristos will paint a face on the finish. If I let the academy finish it, the file will remain epistemology. If I let platforms finish it, the mic will be unsung and the rails laundered. If I let engineers finish it, keys will become a crown by soft degrees. If I let continuity weather finish it, soft nos will feel like climate instead of choice. The father line sits outside their finishes: unread, repeated, refusing to become their plot.

Meaning is the fight because custody is the fight. Who holds what the number is for. Who may say what the life is for. Who paints, who retracts, who refuses to kneel. The unread dedication is not a side wound. It is the private proof that even true front matter can fail to land — and still the work remains work.

There is a particular quiet after you write a dedication you know will go unread. Not tragic quiet. Working quiet. The quiet of a studio after a pour when you cannot hurry the set. The quiet of a mat after class when the cleaner is still wet and the next lesson has not arrived. Into that quiet the planet will send keys and faces and silencings. Into that quiet AJ’s desk will send the next page. Into that quiet I keep the three goals unpolished: write; dab; talk shit. The father line sits above them like a fourth goal that refuses to become a campaign.

Act I ends where the Constant stops being the question.

Ends as orientation, not as solved equation. The meters keep walking whether or not a father opens a book. The fight that remains is custody of meaning under weather that wants faces, retractions, keys, and soft nos. I name it here so Act II cannot pretend the climax was already measurement.

What remains is meaning — who holds it, who paints it, who retracts it, who refuses to kneel — and the next weather is already at the door, asking for keys, faces, silencings, salvage collars, and soft nos that cost real sales.

The mats stay under everything. The ignored shelf stays as smell. The dab stays. The talk shit stays. The economics degree stays quiet in the background. The physics stays on the desk. The Honey Badger stays in the pocket. None of that is polished for Quarter I or apologised for to Quarter II.

Hand off to Act II without a victory lap: the number walks; the meaning fights; the dedication stays unread; the mic stays given; the wall stays messiah-less; the brother waits for his page; the soft nos gather. I now turn over the next leaf — not as ending, as handoff — while the dedication stays unread and the fight stays named: meaning, not Constant; custody, not crown; work, not usable lie. The next weather can arrive now, already knocking at the door.

I can hear the knock as lab soft-asks and partner frost and cream paper and hygiene op-eds — temperatures already lived, now escalating. I do not open the door on this page. Opening is Act II’s labour. Closing Act I mid-motion is the adult work: leave the Constant to the meters; keep the meaning-fight named; leave the dedication unread without turning unread into plot twist; leave the mic given without apology theatre; leave the wall messiah-less; leave Bouwer for careful grief; leave consent for later rooms.

The leaf turns because the fight is named, not because it is won.

I am not finished with pain on these pages. Bouwer waits. Soft nos wait. Keys wait. The stop-the-book weather waits for AJ’s desk. Consent waits for both of us in different rooms — what may be said, what may be signed, what may ship — and that wait is already part of the meaning-fight, not a sequel tease. Act I only turns the question: away from whether the Constant lands — it lands — toward who may hold the meaning when it does.

Act I’s last motion is not triumph. It is orientation. The Constant can land in public and still leave a life misowned. I will not pretend otherwise to comfort a reader who wants the meters to finish the story.

If you came for a textbook, leave. If you came for a reunion, leave. If you came for a prophet’s tidy arc from ward to crown, leave. What remains is a dedication unread, a mic given by candour, a wall that wants a sentence, and a fight that meters cannot settle.

What remains is also a man who still writes, dabs, talks shit, and refuses to sand those facts for anyone’s usable photograph. Act I can end on that refusal without applause.

I close this page mid-motion: dedication written; books still unread; fight named.

Not the number.

The custody of what the number is for — hand already turning toward Act II’s mail.

Custody stays the word even when it is ugly. Custody is not ownership of a father. Custody is refusal to let quarters finish the story of a life while meters finish the story of a Constant. Different finishes. Same morning of the world.

Act II can have the keys and the soft nos and the salvage collars. Act I leaves them the orientation they keep trying to steal: the number walks; the meaning fights; the dedication stays unread; the mic stays given; the wall stays messiah-less. I do not tidy the father into a last-page open. I do not tidy the Constant into a finished story.

Meaning, not Constant. Front matter still true. Fight named. Hand off without victory lap. Dedication unread. Work remains work. The unread stays. Act I turns here. I turn the leaf.

Before the leaf fully turns there is one more desk texture I will not sand for comfort: tea gone cold beside a book my father has not opened; Kind paint smell from an alley that does not need my face; the Honey Badger’s pocket click as kinship across a distance that does not require a shared room; CODATA pressure wearing relief’s clothes somewhere on a pane I will not stamp for sacrament. Quiet facts under loud weather. I carry both without confusing them. Confusing them is how you become instrument or relic. I refuse both costumes and leave the dedication unread on purpose — not as cruelty, as fidelity to what is. The unread is the load. The fight for meaning is the handoff. Act II can have the mail. Act I keeps the orientation.
