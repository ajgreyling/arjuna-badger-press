# *The Indifferent Desert* — a standalone historical novel

An **original retelling of a true story.** In **May 1940**, two German geologists in South-West Africa —
**Henno Martin** and **Hermann Korn**, anti-Nazi émigrés who fled Hitler in 1935 — refused to be interned
as enemy aliens when the war reached the colony. Instead they **drove into the Namib with a rifle, two
cars, and Korn's dog Otto, and survived in the Kuiseb canyon for two and a half years** by their wits and
their bushcraft. Henno Martin later wrote it up as the classic memoir *The Sheltering Desert* (1956). This
is the story told again, from the documented facts, in this project's voice.

- **A pure standalone** — its own world and canon. **Not** *History Before Time*, **not** *The Unheard*,
  **not** the African Gold trilogy. **No Order, no Jakobus** (it is 1940). Shares only the project's
  grounded house voice and the Namib itself.
- **POV:** close-third on **Henno Martin** (the survivor, the disciplined one); **Korn** the volatile,
  brilliant co-lead seen from outside; **Otto** the third heart.
- **Tone:** *great real bushcraft; not always a fun read; real story; real respect.* The romance of
  "desert adventure" stripped out — endurance, hunger, the endless water problem, the diet that nearly
  broke them (lean game → "rabbit starvation"), the slow erosion of body and mind, and two scientists who
  kept *observing* the desert even as it nearly unmade them.
- **Real, and cleared:** both men are long deceased and may be depicted (fairly, with respect). The
  **book** *The Sheltering Desert* is still in copyright, so this is an **original retelling of the true
  EVENTS, not a derivative of Martin's text** — facts are free; his sentences are his.
- **The German thread:** sympathetically-drawn anti-Nazi Germans — part of the project's conviction that
  the German strand is woven into the living fabric of South(ern) Africa (the author lives in Somerset
  West, "Little Germany"), not just its colonial past. Cousin in setting to the Namibia / Rehoboth material.
