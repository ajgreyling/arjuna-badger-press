# Dedication

For **Ferdie** —

who grew up in **Okahandja** and knows the **Namib** the way some people know a prayer:
not as scenery, but as **grammar** — rock, water, distance, and the long patience of survival.

I met him in the **bush near the Chobe**, where Botswana goes quiet,
and he turned out to be **brother-tight** with the man I write as Jakobus,
and one of the most **interesting, generous minds** I have ever sat with under a tree.

This book is about two geologists who **read the desert to stay alive**.
Ferdi is what it looks like when that same country is still being read —
by a **real polymath**, with **real degrees**, doing **real work** —
a century later, and in peace.

*For him. For the Namib. For the conversation by the water.*
