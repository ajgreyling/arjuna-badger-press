# Style Guide — *The Indifferent Desert* (standalone)

> **Binding contract for the de-LLM loop.** This book shares the **project house voice** but has its
> own register, its own tiny cast, and its own failure modes. The craft audit
> (`./run.sh --book sheltering-desert craft-audit`) judges every line against the **Cast voice laws**
> below; the gatekeeper / line-apply prompts enforce the **Machine-tell taboos**. The tone charge is
> the author's and it is binding: *great real bushcraft; not always a fun read; real story; real
> respect.* Strip the romance of "desert adventure"; keep the awe of a thing seen exactly. See
> [`STORY_BIBLE.md`](STORY_BIBLE.md) §3 (POV & voice) and §7 (Voice & form).

## The register (one register, not two)

Unlike the trilogy (present relay + ancient layer), this book has **one register**: close-third on
**Henno Martin**, a disciplined geologist who reads **rock, water, game, weather** the way the
trilogy's leads read people. The signature is the **double act — scientist *and* survivor**: the
lab-trained eye kept observing the desert even as the desert nearly unmade it. That literacy *is* the
survival skill and *is* the prose lens.

- **Spare, exact, sensory; unshowy.** The patience of the desert in the sentence. Long stretches of
  grind, punctured by sharp violence and sharp beauty — and the *grind* must read as grind, not as
  lyric about grind.
- **The desert is indifferent and exact** — neither enemy nor friend. The respect in the prose is for
  the two men who learned to live inside that indifference, never for the desert as a character with
  intentions. **Do not give the desert a will** ("the country meant to break them") except as Martin's
  *named, owned* projection — and use that sparingly.
- **Period and place are load-bearing.** 1940 South-West Africa, German émigré geologists. The
  German-ness is dignified, living, anti-Nazi (STORY_BIBLE §5) — never villain-backstory, never
  comic-accent. The Topnaar (ǂAonin) and the San reference are handled with the project's guardrails
  (STORY_BIBLE §6.4): the survival skill is **theirs first**.

## Cast voice laws (gate-checked & craft-audited)

> The standing house failure is **one grave intelligent voice with the vocabulary swapped**. This book
> has only three "voices" (two men and the narrator's close-third on Martin), so the danger is acute and
> simple: **Korn must not read as Martin being witty.** Differentiate by **cognitive habit** — *how the
> mind moves* — not by topic. Each voice law is a one-line contract; the noun-swap test is the primary
> check.

- **Henno Martin** (POV / close third) — *the disciplined scientist; states the proposition, then the
  cost.* Precise, observing, recording; emotion **leaks into the mechanism** (the arithmetic of water,
  the dip of a stratum), never named outright. He solves by **discipline and arithmetic** — "I have done
  the arithmetic on everything out here." His *failures of discipline* (the bad shot born of an empty
  larder; the hunger-madness; the fear for Korn he cannot fix) are the book's hardest beats **because**
  the controlled surface cracks. His close-third may *observe* deep feeling but should **render it in
  the concrete**, not caption it.
  - ✓ WOULD think: "A camp is certain. The desert is only probable." / "It is a bad plan. It is only a
    better bad plan than the other one."
  - ✗ Would NEVER think (the failure mode this book most commits): "*And that, Martin would think much
    later, was exactly the right way for it to have been decided* …" — the narrator stepping out of the
    scene to **explain its meaning and foreshadow**. Render the moment; cut the gloss. (See Taboo 1.)
- **Hermann Korn** — *the volatile, brilliant co-lead, seen from outside; the wit is a curtain in front
  of the seriousness.* Three years older, gifted, more temperamental. His tell is **bright savage
  amusement laid over something not amusing at all** — and then, when something real enters, *all the
  brightness drops off at once* and the geologist-under-the-wit shows. He is the **emotional engine**
  (his decline — beriberi, the darkening — read off him by Martin who cannot fix it). His mind moves by
  **testing the proposition against the evidence for the fault that brings it down** (the scientist's
  adversarial reflex), where Martin's moves by **building the disciplined case for it**. That contrast
  is the test.
  - ✓ WOULD say: "Men do not live in the Namib. … We have a theodolite and two motor cars." / "It will
    not be an adventure. … It will be work, and hunger, and being afraid, and being bored half to death
    between the times we are afraid."
  - ✗ Would NEVER say: a *disciplined, arithmetic* line in Martin's shape ("A camp is certain; the
    desert is only probable") — that's Martin's build-the-case cadence, not Korn's find-the-fault one.
    Korn punctures; Martin constructs.
- **Otto** (Korn's dog, the third heart) — *a real working dog, never a cartoon.* Rendered in **behaviour
  and consequence**, never given interior monologue or human sentiment. The reader comes to dread his
  fate through what he *does* and what is *done to him* (the oryx), not through anthropomorphic captioning
  ("Otto seemed to understand the gravity of the moment"). One earned beat of seeming-to-understand is
  the ceiling, and even then keep it observed-from-outside.
- **The desert's-edge people** (the Siedentopfs; the Topnaar; a patrol; a farmer) — *each is of that
  exact country, not a function.* The German farm family speak a **code older than the war** ("We did
  not see you … do not let the country win"); render them with the dignity the project gives every
  people. They are not knowledge-interfaces or plot-grease — give each the one true concrete detail that
  marks a real person of that place.

**The distinctness tests (craft-audit + gatekeeper enforce):**
- **The noun-swap test** — *could Korn's line be Martin's with a noun swapped? If yes, rewrite until it
  is Korn's find-the-fault shape, not Martin's build-the-case shape.* Primary check.
- **One aphoristic closer per scene, one mouth.** Never let both men complete the same "not-X / it will
  not be Y" construction; the desert-edge code-line lands once, in the farmer's mouth, and is not then
  re-glossed by the narrator.
- **Differentiate by sentence-shape, not vocabulary:** Martin's interior accretes (the long disciplined
  sentence that adds clause to clause toward a sum); Korn's speech *punctures* (short, bright, then the
  brightness drops). Do not give them the same cadence.

## Character-construction law — the disciplined man is not a saint

> The house warning: a controlled, competent, morally-singular lead drifts toward **sainting**. Martin's
> discipline must **cost someone**, not just ennoble him. The fix is never more hardship (that deepens
> sympathy) — it is **un-noble cost to another**: the bad shot that nearly killed Otto was *his* pressure-
> driven indiscipline (already in ch-08 — keep it un-excused); the times his control reads to Korn as
> coldness; a decision made *for* Korn that Korn did not get to make. Rules: graft onto an existing
> scene; **the narrator must not excuse it**; leave a real relationship cost; tie it to the theme
> (knowing-about is not knowing-how; discipline is not the same as wisdom).

## Sentence & paragraph craft

- **The em-dash is doing too much work in this book** (114 spaced, 254 tight at baseline). It builds the
  long accreting sentences. Convert by **function** (Taboo 2): spaced → tight or gone; tight asides →
  commas; tight end-reframes → a full stop and a new sentence. Keep the dash only for a true interruption
  or a genuine break in thought. The goal is to let some sentences be **short and plain** — the meta-fix
  against uniform polish.
- **End the chapter on the image or the act, not the gloss.** The single most-repeated structural tell
  here is the **chapter-closing caption paragraph** that tells you what the chapter meant and foreshadows
  the next (ch-01, ch-08, ch-15 all do it). Trust the dramatized thing; cut the lesson. (Taboo 1.)
- **Let the science be the lens, not a lecture.** The natural-history authority (gemsbok behaviour, the
  Kuiseb's hydrology, the !nara, rabbit-starvation) is the book's soul — render it *in use*, as Martin
  reads it in the moment, not as an inserted fact-paragraph.

## Machine-tell taboos (the de-LLM contract) — BINDING

These are the patterns the loop drives toward target. The trilogy's full taboo list applies; the ones
**most live in this book** (from the baseline tic scan + the cold read) are called out first.

1. **The chapter-closing / scene-closing caption (THIS BOOK'S #1 TELL).** The narrator stepping out of
   the dramatized moment to **state its meaning and foreshadow the next** — "*the desert had taught him
   another thing that day, two things really, twined together*"; "*that this, too, the desert had been
   preparing him to receive*"; "*which was, Martin would think much later, exactly the right way*". This
   is over-explanation + prophecy-foreshadowing in one move. **Cut to the last concrete beat.** Keep a
   reflection only where it is Martin's *in-scene* thought, owned and specific, not the omniscient gloss.
2. **The em-dash as default connective.** Drive spaced ` — ` to zero (mechanical Move 1); thin tight
   `word—word` by function (Move 2). Some sentences must end short.
3. **"the way …"** (132 vs target ~70 — nearly 2×). The reflexive simile-frame ("the way danger came",
   "the way a man's hands go steady", "the way he said the things he meant most"). Keep only where the
   comparison **truly illuminates** (~40%); cut the rest to a plain statement.
4. **"Not X. Y." / "It was not a question." / "It was not a hard decision."** the negation-reframe
   fragment (46 vs ~22). Keep where the reframe is genuinely earned; cut the manufactured throat-clears.
5. **Intensifier & hedge padding** — "very/really" (56), "seemed/perhaps/somewhat" (40),
   "obviously/clearly/literally" (7). Cut to the stronger plain word.
6. **"something" as placeholder** — "something moved in his face", bare "something" (12 / 145). Replace
   with the concrete thing seen, or cut.
7. **AI self-narrating meta** — the narrator announcing its own honesty / what it is doing. Rare here (2)
   but kill on sight.
8. **Superlative inflation** — "the only / the most / the deepest / the truest" (10). Reserve for the one
   beat that earns it.

> **The one rule (project-wide):** these are tells *because the underlying moves are good*. The job is
> **thinning, not banning**, and **not optimising to a score** — a clean *read* beats a green scorecard.
> Name the muscle, end the sentence, let some pages be plain. Stop when only the author's story-calls
> remain.

## Content, comfort & accuracy guardrails (from STORY_BIBLE §6)

- **Tell the hardship straight** — the diet crisis, the endless water, the psychological erosion are the
  *truth* of it: unflinching, never spectacle, never gore-for-thrill.
- **The real people, fairly** — Martin's discipline, Korn's brilliance-and-volatility, the real
  friendship; **Korn's ambiguous 1946 death handled with restraint** (render the ambiguity, never a
  verdict, never sensationalised suicide).
- **The desert and its peoples with accuracy and respect** — the Topnaar (ǂAonin) belong to the Kuiseb as
  the two Germans never will; the survival skill is theirs first; the natural history must be real.
- **Original retelling, never a derivative of Martin's memoir** — build from documented facts; our own
  narrative, our own sentences. (Legal guardrail, STORY_BIBLE §0 / §6.1.)
