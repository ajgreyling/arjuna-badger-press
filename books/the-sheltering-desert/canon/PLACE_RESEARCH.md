## The two men (both confirmed geologists)
- **Henno Martin** (b. 15 Mar 1910, Freiburg; d. 7 Jan 1998, aged 87) and **Hermann Korn** (b. 1907,
  Seggerde; d. 9 Aug 1946) — German geologists, both doctoral students of **Hans Cloos**; closest friends
  from Bonn. Martin: "quietly resolute, self-disciplined"; Korn: brilliant but volatile. Troubled by Nazi
  authoritarianism, they **emigrated Aug 1935**, arriving Walvis Bay / Swakopmund Sept 1935. Pre-war they
  mapped the escarpment, worked the Naukluft, and **from 1937 located water wells for farmers** (geologists
  *and* working surveyors). [Geological Society of Namibia](https://geolsocnamibia.org/henno-martin-hermann-korn/) · [Henno Martin (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henno_Martin)

## The dog — Otto (confirmed)
- **Otto** was **Korn's dog**, a central named character and their alarm system ("alerted them in case of
  danger"). Real beat: Otto was **gored by an oryx** and saved by a sympathetic farming family (the
  Siedentopfs). [padlangsnamibia](https://padlangsnamibia.com/padlangs-namibia/henno-martins-sheltering-desert) · [bushguide101](https://bushguide101.com/the-henno-martin-shelter-at-kuiseb-canyon/)

## The decision & timeline
- **Drove into the desert 25 May 1940** — two vehicles (a bakkie + a Chevrolet coupé), basic supplies, a
  pistol + rifle/shotgun, and Otto. Trigger: Britain + South Africa at war with Germany → the SA mandatory
  administration of SWA began **interning German-descended men as enemy aliens.** Rather than surrender,
  they disappeared. **Lasted two and a half years, until Sept 1942.** [Henno Martin (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henno_Martin)

## The place — the Kuiseb canyon (confirmed)
- The **Kuiseb River canyon** in the Namib, the schist gorges of its upper reaches. First shelter: a rock
  overhang named **"Karpfenkliff" / Carp Cliff** (for a carp-filled pool in the Kuiseb below). **Three
  hideouts** over the period (the overhang, then two stone-and-wood shacks); vehicles hidden in side
  gorges; shelters gave shade, wind protection, defence against hyenas. [bushguide101](https://bushguide101.com/the-henno-martin-shelter-at-kuiseb-canyon/)

## The survival / bushcraft (confirmed — incl. the diet crisis)
- They lived "like ancient Bushmen," hunting **mountain zebra, oryx/gemsbok, ostrich, springbok,
  klipspringer, steenbok** (biggest single kill: a **feral cattle bull**), and constantly fighting for
  **water** from the Kuiseb's pools/waterholes.
- **The famous nutritional crisis is real:** in dry months the game was thin/lean → inadequate fat → a
  "longing for fat." This is **"rabbit starvation" / protein poisoning** (lean meat without fat → acute
  malnutrition), compounded by **vitamin-C lack.** It degraded them mentally — Martin: the "distinction
  between human beings and animals became blurred." They ate **organ meat (liver first, for vitamins),
  sometimes raw,** to cope. **Grim, un-romantic, well-attested — not a comfortable adventure read.**
  [academia.edu handbook](https://www.academia.edu/8394718/Henno_Martin_s_The_Sheltering_Desert_a_handbook_for_desert_archaeologists) · [Hektoen: rabbit starvation](https://hekint.org/2022/01/26/rabbit-starvation-protein-poisoning/)

## How it ended (Sept 1942) — confirmed, with a date correction
- By **Sept 1942** **Korn's beriberi** (thiamine deficiency, exactly as recalled) became severe enough that
  they **abandoned the desert for medical help in Windhoek.** On return they were **NOT interned** and were
  **taken on as surveyors.**
- ⚠ **Correction to common priors: Hermann Korn died 9 Aug 1946 — POST-war, not at war's end.** His car ran
  off a railway bridge into the dry **Gammams riverbed** near Windhoek at night; he was killed, aged ~38/39;
  **circumstances never fully settled (accident / recklessness / possibly suicide)** — render the ambiguity,
  not a verdict. [findagrave — Korn](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/199899400/hermann-korn)
- **Henno Martin** went on to a major career: Director, SWA branch of the SA Geological Survey (1947–63);
  Director, Precambrian Research Unit, UCT (1963–65); Chair of Geology / Director, Geol.-Palaeont. Institute,
  **University of Göttingen** (1965–75); **died 1998.** [Geological Society of Namibia](https://geolsocnamibia.org/henno-martin-hermann-korn/)

## Why it commands respect
- A classic of desert/survival literature for its **unsentimental realism:** two trained scientists who,
  even while slowly starving, kept **observing and recording** the desert (game aggregations after rain —
  "three to four thousand springbok together with zebra and gemsbok"; rainfall-driven grass; arid-ecosystem
  dynamics). Admired by bushcraft/desert people because the endurance is real and the romance is stripped
  out. One paper treats it as "a handbook for desert archaeologists." [researchgate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271644892_HENNO_MARTIN'S_THE_SHELTERING_DESERT_A_handbook_for_desert_archaeologists)
