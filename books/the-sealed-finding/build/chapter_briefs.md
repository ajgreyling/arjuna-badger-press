> Per-chapter continuity briefs so chapters can be drafted independently without drift. Each brief states
> what the chapter **inherits**, what it **must contain**, what it **must not do**, and what it **hands
> forward**. Read with `prompts/draft-chapter.md`, `build/story_state.md`, and the canon.
>
> **Universal rules for every chapter below:**
> - Begin with `# <Chapter Title>` then the dated line in italics, e.g. `*Day Three — Friday 6 November 2054*`.
> - Close third, procedural, dry. The book is frightening because it is **calm**.
> - **No precognition, ever.** Readers *assess*, stacks *compute*, findings are *filed*.
> - **Both truths on every page.** Never let the reader settle.
> - **Cassim is loyal.** No ambiguity, ever, on any re-read.
> - Artifact blocks are rationed and never re-narrate a scene we have watched.
> - No character states the theme. No speeches. End on motion or a turn.
> - South African, Gauteng, November 2054. Language braids unglossed and unitalicised.

  not paid since March. Her certainty is a private religion **because it cannot be checked** — show this
  as a habit, not a speech (she says a small prayer over each name and would deny it is a prayer).
- **Wynand (B), 41:** flat, over-precise, apologises before speaking. Broke in 2050 over a finding on a
  nineteen-year-old; four days. Kept on because recalibration takes four years and the vacancy freeze is
  two per cent. **His file records his falling dissent rate as "improvement"** — he has read his own file
  and knows the word is in it (PP-012).

  notebook, a disciplinary offence under her contract (PP-009). What is in it: not names — *dates and
  shapes*, her own findings, and a column she cannot fill in.

**Turn (required):** Karabo submits a written request to be told the outcome of **one** finding — any
finding, her choice, from three years ago. The refusal comes back inside the hour on a template with a
**form reference number ending in a high sequence**, which tells her exactly how many people have asked
before. She copies the reference number into the notebook.

**Must not:** mysticism, reverie, lit-from-within, visions, anything uncanny. If the scene would still

**Hands forward:** Karabo's notebook and her fury with nowhere to go (pays ch-20); Wynand's "improvement"

## ch-14 — "How to Farm a Finding" · Day Four, Saturday, after dark · ~3,600 words
**POV:** Motaung.

**Must contain — the frame, and it is technical, not theatrical (PP-003):**
The Federation did not plant evidence. They planted **inputs**, over about fourteen months, each one
lawful on its own:
1. A security-industry newsletter in **September** published a "watch list" naming Motaung personally,
   with his suburb.
2. A Federation-funded litigant served papers on him **personally** rather than on the Directorate, in
   October — a choice with no legal advantage and one behavioural effect.
3. A threat against **Miriam**, sourced through a burner and routed to his desk in October with Grobler's
   name attached to it, which the Directorate logged, as it logs everything.
4. Grobler's own diary was made public in a way that put him repeatedly within a kilometre of Motaung's
   routine movements without either man knowing.
Grievance, means, proximity, escalation. The stack did what it is built to do. **And *PRIOR CONTACT: None
of record* stayed true the whole time**, which is why it convicted him.

- Grobler admits it flatly, without pleasure, as a man describing an operation.
- **And then the blade:** Grobler says he does not know whether the finding is therefore wrong. Neither
  does Motaung. Neither does anyone. That is paragraph four, aimed at the man who wrote it.
- **Case card #2** (PP-014): half a page, a real person, never mentioned again. Suggested: a man named
  in 2049 whose file shows a 3-of-3 concurrence and an order renewed five times, and an employment note
  reading "Estate bakery. Satisfactory."

**Turn:** he was farmed, and the finding may still be right, and there is no procedure on earth that can
separate those two sentences.
