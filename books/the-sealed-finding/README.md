# *The Sealed Finding* *(working title)*

An **unauthorised homage** to Philip K. Dick's novella *"The Minority Report"* (1956) — a craft exercise
under the Arjuna Badger Press house tooling, on the **Faithful Modern** shelf. **Not licensed. Not for
commercial release.** The book keeps the *arcs and the engine* — a bureau that punishes before the act; the
founder named by his own instrument; the successor he wrongly fears; the faction that wants the bureau dead
and is right; the dissent filed where he may not read it; the recursion; the deliberate murder that makes
the machine true — and every name, world, institution, device and sentence is **original**.

Provenance is disclosed, never hidden — same standing as the press's *Warhammer 40,000* fan novel
([`../no-fear-cycle/`](../no-fear-cycle/)) and its other PKD homages ([`../the-scramble/`](../the-scramble/),
[`../the-dreaming/`](../the-dreaming/)). See [`canon/ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md`](canon/ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md)
for the line we hold: **take the engine, never the text.**

> **The 2002 film is not a source and not a model.** No sighted oracle, no eye transplants, no
> gesture-glass, no chase spectacle, no exculpatory ending. The novella is colder and braver.

## The three craft mandates (why this book exists)

1. **Inference, not prophecy.** Dick needed mutants in a tank; we do not. Everything the Directorate does
   is buildable today plus ten years. **No precognition, ever.**
2. **The named are people.** The source's precriminals are a statistic. Here they have names and Tuesdays.
3. **Both truths, always.** The Directorate is a horror **and** it works — lowest homicide since 1908. The
   book refuses to arbitrate.

## Substrate & structure

Near-now **South Africa**, November 2054, after the Brownout years. Real streets, real bureaucracy, no
spectacle. **Three parts, each opening with the verbatim text of one finding** — and the reader holds all
three while no character holds more than one until the end. A **six-day clock**, dated on every chapter.

| Part | Opens with | Engine |
|---|---|---|
| **I — THE WARRANT** | Finding A | the Directorate at work; his own name; the wrong suspicions; the flight through a net he specified |
| **II — THE DISSENT** | Finding B *(sealed)* | the courteous abduction; the Federation's correct case; the ledger; **the recursion** |
| **III — THE PROOF** | Finding C | the vise; the assembly; the shot, chosen; the Estate; *it will be you* |

**The twist, modernised:** the three findings were never a vote. The stacks ingest the Directorate's own
operational record, a distributed finding becomes an input, and a two-line change to the refresh schedule
in 2049 means the three Readers have been answering about three differently-informed men ever since. There
is no majority. All three findings are correct. **Every 2–1 warrant since 2049 is void** — which is
eleven thousand people, at the Estate, right now.
