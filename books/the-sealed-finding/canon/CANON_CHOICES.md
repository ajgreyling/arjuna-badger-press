# CANON_CHOICES — *The Sealed Finding*

> anything **OPEN** must be resolved (and moved up to LOCKED) before the relevant part drafts.

## LOCKED (2026-07-25, scaffold)

- **Lineage & standing.** Faithful Modern homage to Philip K. Dick's *The Minority Report* (1956); arcs
  and engine kept per the **arc ledger** (SOURCE_NOTES), all text original; unauthorised, **not for
  commercial release** (ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE).
- **The 2002 film is excluded as a model.** Novella only. No sighted oracle, no eye transplants, no
  gesture-glass, no exculpatory ending. (The house precedent for an excluded register reference is
  *Ball Lightning* on [`../the-long-silence/`](../the-long-silence/canon/CANON_CHOICES.md).)
- **NO PRECOGNITION.** The hard line of the book. The Directorate infers; it does not see. Nothing in the
  manuscript may be ambiguous on this — not a dream, not a hunch, not a Reader who "just knows"
  (MYTHOS).
- **Three craft mandates.** (1) Inference, not prophecy. (2) The named are people. (3) Both truths,
  always — the machine is a horror *and* it works. These are the reason the book exists (STYLE_GUIDE).
- **Structure.** Three parts — **THE WARRANT · THE DISSENT · THE PROOF** — each opening with the verbatim
  artifact of one finding. The reader holds all three; no character holds more than one until the end.
- **The clock.** Six days. **Day One: Wednesday 4 November 2054. Day Six: Monday 9 November 2054**, window
  expiring at 24:00. Every chapter carries the clock.
- **The ending is the source's ending.** He kills Grobler deliberately, in public, with full knowledge, to
  keep the Directorate standing. No reprieve, no last-minute exoneration, no "he chose otherwise." The
  book's mercy is that we understand him, not that we spare him.
- **Title.** Working title ***The Sealed Finding*** — the instrument, the drawer, and the thesis. The
  phrase is spoken once, mundanely, in Part One (PP-010) and recoded by Part Three.

## LOCKED — direction set (2026-07-25, drafting pass)

### The world & texture
- **Setting: real near-now South Africa, Gauteng.** Pretoria (the Directorate's head office, Struben
  Street), Johannesburg and the East Rand for operations, and the **Doringkop Preventive Estate** on the
  Highveld out past Delmas. Present-tense of the book: **November 2054**. Real streets, real weather, real
  bureaucracy. No flying cars. There is a photocopier that jams.
- **Why South Africa (binding, and not decoration):** it is the country with the strongest possible case
  *for* a machine like this — a generation of violent crime the old law could not touch — and the
  strongest possible case *against* it, in living memory: detention without trial, ninety-day, the pass
  book, a state that once decided in advance which people were the dangerous kind. The book is set here
  because the argument is unanswerable here in both directions (mandate #3).
- **Founding history.** The **Brownout years** (2027–2031): grid failure, municipal collapse, the murder
  rate vertical, private security filling the vacuum and billing for it. The Directorate was constituted
  in 2035 under the Preventive Enforcement Act, with a sunset clause that has been renewed four times.
  Murder in Gauteng is now the lowest recorded since 1908. **This is true and the book never disputes it.**

### The institution (LOCKED — NOT Precrime with a new sign)
- **The Directorate of Preventive Enforcement** ("the Directorate"). A directorate, not a mythic bureau:
  it has an audit committee, a communications office, a staff canteen, and a two per cent vacancy freeze.
- **The anticipatory warrant** (s.14 of the Act). A person against whom two of three Readers concur is
  **named**. "He's been named" is the ordinary phrase; nobody says "precriminal."
- **Preventive custody.** The named are held at the **Doringkop Preventive Estate** — "the Estate" — under
  a renewable order. Conditions are decent. Visits are permitted. Nobody is beaten. **Nobody leaves.**
  The decency is load-bearing: it is what makes the country able to live with it.
- **The sealed finding** (s.14(6)). Where one Reader dissents, the dissenting finding is sealed and
  retained but **not disclosed to the named person or their representative**, because disclosure would
  supply a reasonable-doubt argument in every warrant ever issued. The provision was written to be
  temporary. It is nineteen years old.

### The Readers (LOCKED — NOT the precogs)
Three people, three air-gapped stacks, three sealed suites in the Struben Street basement. The statute
requires a human signature on a deprivation of liberty, so a person must hold the intent finding; the
stacks compute the behaviour, the Reader carries the intent. They never meet each other, never learn each
other's findings, and — the cruellest clause — **are never told outcomes**, because knowing would
contaminate them. A Reader can work twenty years and not once find out whether they were right.

- **Reader A — Grace Sithole**, 52. Eleven thousand files. Steady the way a bridge is steady, and about as
  well maintained. Filed **Finding A**.
- **Reader B — Wynand Loots**, 41. Broke four years ago and was kept on, because calibrating a replacement
  takes four years and the vacancy freeze is at two per cent. Filed **Finding B** — the sealed one.
- **Reader C — Karabo Ntuli**, 26. Newest, best, and keeping a private tally in a hardcover notebook,
  which is a disciplinary offence. Filed **Finding C**, and appended a note to it that no Reader has ever
  appended to a finding.

Recruitment is from people with no other options — debt, a dead parent's estate, a rural post with no
post. Their consent is documented, witnessed, and a fiction. This is the modern equivalent of Dick's
exploited precogs and it is not softened.

### Cast (LOCKED names — native, specific, zero Dick echoes)
- **Josiah Motaung**, 61 — Director; founder. Built it after **Beauty Ngobese** (2027), whose murder he
  predicted to three magistrates in writing and was not permitted to prevent. Keeps her docket in his
  drawer. *Not* John Anderton: not American, not a Commissioner, not the same marriage, not the same man.
- **Ayesha Cassim**, 34 — Deputy Director, seconded by the review committee eleven weeks ago. Brilliant,
  courteous, and completely loyal. The red herring. *Not* Witwer.
- **Miriam Motaung**, 57 — Registrar of Findings; the keeper of the sealed drawer. Has been copying every
  sealed finding since 2043. She is the only person in the building who knows the true dissent rate.
  *Not* Lisa: she is not rescued, she is not a device, and she is right first.
- **Cornelius "Neels" Grobler**, 68 — retired general, chairman of the **Federation of Security Providers**.
  The man Motaung is named to kill. His case against the Directorate is **correct**, which is the point;
  his interest in making it is **financial**, which is also the point. *Not* Kaplan.
- **W/O Dries Lombard**, 49 — Directorate internal security; twenty-two years at Motaung's shoulder; on the
  Federation's payroll since 2051. *Not* Fleming.
- **Beauty Ngobese** (1998–2027) — the reason the Directorate exists. Appears only in a docket and in
  Motaung's memory, and must never be sentimentalised.

### The mechanism of the recursion (LOCKED — this is the twist, and it is plumbing)
The three stacks ingest the Directorate's own operational record as a feature set. **They have to** — the
Directorate's actions change the world it is predicting, and a stack blind to its own institution's
behaviour was found in 2049 to be worse than useless. So the operational record goes in.

A finding *enters* the operational record when it is distributed.

Therefore the three findings on Josiah Motaung were **not concurrent**, and have not been concurrent for
anyone since the 2049 refresh-schedule change, which was a two-line entry in a change-control log signed
by a systems manager who has since emigrated:

- **Stack A** computed at 04:00 on Day One. Subject: a man who does not know. → **Finding A: he kills.**
- **Finding A distributed 06:00.**
- **Stack B** refreshed 08:00. Subject: a man who has been named and will read it. → **Finding B: he does
  not kill** — because a warned man avoids the stranger he is warned about. *This is the sealed finding.*
- **Finding B sealed and entered 12:00.**
- **Stack C** refreshed 12:30. Subject: a man who has read both. → **Finding C: he kills anyway** —
  deliberately, instrumentally, because the dissent in his hand is the thing that will end his life's work.

There is no majority. There are three different men, and each finding is correct about the one it was
computed for. The statute's concurrence test assumes a simultaneity the plumbing stopped delivering five
years ago, and **every 2–1 warrant since 2049 is therefore void** — which is thousands of people, at the
Estate, right now. Grobler is going to say so on Monday, and he will be right.

### The set-piece (LOCKED — Part Three, the only one at full calibre)
**The assembly.** The Federation's annual congress, Monday 9 November, a convention floor in Sandton, four
hundred delegates, three broadcast crews, and a retired general with the sealed finding in his hand and
eleven minutes of the country's attention. Motaung is there because there is nowhere else the six days
could have gone. The horror is not the shot. **The horror is the reader understanding, a half-beat before
the room does, that the shot is the only remaining argument** — and that it works.

- **DO NOT:** stage a chase, a standoff negotiation, a last-second reprieve, or a speech from Motaung. He
  does not explain himself in the room. He explains himself, partially and badly, to one person, later.
- Trust the image; never caption the awe (STYLE_GUIDE).

### The ending (LOCKED)
- No trial. A Director who has fulfilled a finding is the Directorate's best evidence, and the machine is
  not going to waste it. He is **processed onto the Estate under his own instrument**, indefinite order,
  reviewed annually, never released.
- **Miriam takes voluntary spousal residence** — a small, awful mercy that exists in the regulations
  because someone kind once inserted it.
- **Cassim takes the chair** and the Directorate does not miss him for a single day. That is the cruellest
  fact in the book and it is delivered flatly, in one line.
- The last scene is the warning (A-18): *the seat draws the finding; it will be you.* The **Ngobese
  docket** changes hands (PP-008). No paragraph explains what it means.
