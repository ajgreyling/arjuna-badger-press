# SOURCE_NOTES — *The Sealed Finding*

> **SSOT for fidelity.** What the source *is*, what we keep, what we retire, and — because the author's
> brief was explicit — **the arc ledger**: every craft element and arc of the original, listed, with the
> transposition that carries it. If a beat is not in the source's *engine*, it is ours to invent; if it is
> in the engine, our version must **rhyme**, never transcribe.

## The source

Philip K. Dick, ***"The Minority Report"*** — novella, first published in *Fantastic Universe*, January
1956. Collected many times since; the definitive text is in the Dick short-fiction collections.

**We do not own this and are not licensed.** See [`ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md`](ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md).

**The 2002 film is a different work and is not our source.** Its inventions — the sighted third oracle
with a name and a dead mother, the eye transplants, the gesture-glass, the "you can choose" ending — are
the studio's, and they solve the story's problem by *softening* it. We are adapting the 1956 text, whose
ending is colder and whose idea is larger.

## What we retire (and why)

1. **Precognition.** Dick needed an oracle; 1956 offered no other mechanism. We have one, and it indicts
   the reader's actual world rather than a fictional one. **The Directorate never sees the future. It
   computes a person.** (Mandate #1 — MYTHOS hard line, no exceptions, not even ambiguously.)
2. **The mutants as spectacle.** The precogs' deformity is a 1950s shorthand for otherness. Ours are
   ordinary people recruited out of no-choice circumstances, which is both more modern and more damning.
3. **The colony-world escape hatch.** Off-world exile lets the book end somewhere else. Ours ends *inside
   the machine*, which is where the argument is.

## Where the source is thin (this book's repair brief — binding)

Named so we do not repeat them. The novella is a masterpiece of idea; these are the costs of its
compression.

1. **The precriminals are a statistic.** We are told the murder rate fell; we never meet one of the
   thousands in detention. **Repair:** the named are on the page with names and Tuesdays (mandate #2).
2. **The wife is a device.** Lisa exists to be suspected and then to rescue. **Repair:** the wife has her
   own eleven-year campaign, her own moral position, and she is right before anyone else is (CHARACTERS).
3. **Novella compression.** The reveals arrive stacked, delivered largely in speeches. **Repair:** the
   recursion is *structural* (three parts, three findings) so it can be discovered under pressure and paid
   for in scene, not explained across a desk (STYLE_GUIDE, PLOT velocity gate).

## What we keep at full calibre

Dick's best single move is that **the twist makes the reader's own certainty collapse** — you spend the
story arguing about whether the machine is right, and the ending informs you that "right" was never a
coherent thing to ask of it. That is the bar. Our recursion must land as *that*: not a clever plot turn
but the floor going out from under a word.
