## One sentence

The man who built a South African state directorate that arrests people for murders they have not yet
committed is named by his own machine — and discovers that the report which would clear him, sealed by
law in a drawer his wife has been quietly copying for eleven years, is not a second opinion but a second
*future*, computed for the man he would have been if he had never read it; so that when he stands in a
convention hall on the sixth day and shoots a stranger in front of four hundred people, he does it
knowingly, to make the machine true, because the only alternative is to prove it void and empty the Estate
of eleven thousand people who were going to kill somebody.

## Why this book exists

Dick wrote the definitive story about punishment before the act, and he had to invent mutants to do it. We
don't. The machine he needed a miracle for is now a procurement decision, and the moral catastrophe he
imagined is a live policy debate in every country with cameras. **This retelling exists to run his engine
on real hardware** — and to make the reader hold, without relief, the two facts a real version produces:
that it imprisons the innocent, and that it works.

We keep the **arcs** (all twenty-two of them, ledgered in [`SOURCE_NOTES.md`](SOURCE_NOTES.md#the-arc-ledger-binding)),
the **engine**, and Dick's best craft move — the twist that collapses a concept rather than solving a
mystery. We invent every scene, name, institution, device, and sentence. Same standing as
[`../the-scramble/`](../the-scramble/) and [`../the-dreaming/`](../the-dreaming/).

**North star:** the engine in `SOURCE_NOTES.md`. If a beat is not in Dick's engine, it is ours; if it is
in the engine, it must **rhyme**, never transcribe. **The 2002 film is not a source and not a model.**

**The three findings are three futures for three differently-informed men, and all three are correct.**

---

## The structural signature

**The recursion made shape.** The reader is handed all three findings, at the head of each part, before the
characters have them. No character holds more than one until the end. The book's dramatic irony is not
"who did it" — it is watching a man walk out of the first finding and into the third, and being unable to
name the moment he crossed.
