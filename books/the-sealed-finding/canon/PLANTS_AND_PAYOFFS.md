## Misfire guard (Chekhov in reverse)

- **No deus ex machina.** Nothing introduced late may save him or the eleven thousand. No hack, no leak, no
  sympathetic judge, no technical exploit. **The machine is not defeatable and must never look defeatable**
  (MYTHOS).
- **The withheld gun.** The book never answers whether Finding A would have been *right* about the man who
  had not read it. It cannot be answered — that is paragraph four — and any draft that implies an answer
  has broken the book's central law.
- **Cassim's loyalty never fires as betrayal.** It is a gun the reader keeps expecting; it is deliberately
  never loaded, and the reader's disappointment is the indictment of the reader's own suspicion.
