## Structure → structure

| Dick structural move | Function | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Novella compression; reveals stacked and spoken | velocity; ideas over furniture | **three parts headed by the three findings** — the recursion becomes shape, discovered in scene, not delivered across a desk |
| The reader learns with Anderton | identification with a wrong man | **inverted**: the reader is given all three findings up front; no character holds more than one until the end. The tension moves from *what is true* to *when does he cross* |
| Flat 1950s procedural texture | authority; the ordinariness of atrocity | the **artifact register** (findings, custody logs, case cards) held apart from the prose by a razor |

| Lisa as device | a hinge, not a person | **repaired**: Miriam has an eleven-year campaign and the only honest number in the building |
