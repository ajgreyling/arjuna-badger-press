## PROLOGUE — *Tuesday 3 November 2054*

An uptake, start to finish, on a stranger: two officers, a printed card read aloud, twenty minutes to
pack, a call permitted, a decent breakfast on the road. Nobody raises their voice. The man has done
nothing. He will never be released, and no one — including the reader, including the author — will ever be
able to say whether he would have done it. **PP-001, PP-002, PP-014.**

*Function:* the machine, demonstrated, before we meet anyone who built it. The reader must be unsettled
and unable to say by what.

## The reader-contract payoff (negative space)

Across all three parts the reader assembles the three clauses of the law (MYTHOS) from fragments no
character holds together: *the intervention destroys the evidence · the machine sees people like you ·
telling a man his future is itself an input.* **No character ever states all three.** The single allowed
near-statement is Karabo Ntuli's Reader's Note, and it is a question.

## Velocity checkpoints (anti-sag, binding)

- **Part Two is the danger zone** — it carries the argument. Every Part Two chapter must carry the clock
  *and* a personal cost (the abduction, the marriage, the payroll entry, the ledger). "Here is more of the
  case" gets rebuilt.
- Exactly **two detonations** (II.10, III.4). No more.
- **The Readers' interludes are not respites.** Each one advances the notebook, the dissent, or the
  recursion, and each one ends on a turn.
