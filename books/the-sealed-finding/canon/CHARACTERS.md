# CHARACTERS — *The Sealed Finding*

> Original cast. Each occupies a **function** in Dick's engine; **none** shares a life, scene, or line with
> his people (ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE). Voice law: **rhyme the function, never copy the person.** The
> noun-swap test is binding — swap two characters' names and the lines must stop fitting.

### Josiah Motaung — Director, founder

A detective in Katlehong through the Brownouts. In 2027 he wrote to three magistrates, twice each, about a
woman named **Beauty Ngobese** whose husband was going to kill her — he had the escalation, the means, the
pattern, the dates — and each time he was told, correctly, that no crime had been committed. She was killed
on a Sunday afternoon in front of her sister. He keeps the docket in his drawer, and he has never once told
anyone that he keeps it, including his wife, who has known for nineteen years.

He built the Directorate out of that, and he is not sorry, and the book must let him not be sorry.

**Voice:** economical, procedural, courteous to subordinates and short with peers; a man who says the
operational thing in an emotional moment because it is the only sentence available to him. Under pressure
he does not become eloquent — he becomes *accurate*. His failure mode is that he reads competence as
threat: he has spent nineteen years being the only person who could do this job, and the arithmetic of
that has quietly rotted him.

**Arc:** named → certain it is a frame → certain it is Cassim → certain it is Miriam → wrong about all of
it → offered the exoneration he has wanted for six days → and told what publishing it will cost → takes
the shot on purpose. **He is not redeemed and he is not damned. He is used, by a machine he built, exactly
as designed.**

*Not John Anderton:* different country, different founding wound, different marriage, different rank,
different ending. The only thing they share is a chair.

### Ayesha Cassim — Deputy Director

Seconded eleven weeks ago by the review committee, which is why Motaung cannot see her. Advocate before
she was an administrator; argued *against* the Act as a candidate attorney in 2044 and has never hidden it,
which is precisely why the committee wanted her. Runs meetings on time. Says "I don't know" in front of
her staff, which Motaung has never once managed.

**Voice:** precise, warm, unhurried; asks the question everyone else has agreed not to ask, without malice
and without dropping her voice. Her failure mode is that she believes an institution can be reformed from
inside it, and she will still believe it at the end of the book, in the chair, which is the coldest note
in the coda.

**Arc:** suspected → loyal throughout → the only person who tries, at real cost, to stop the thing → and
inherits the seat and the trap. **She never betrays him.** Not once, not partially, not in a way that can
be read as ambiguous on a second pass. The novella's cruellest craft move is that the paranoia is
*entirely* self-generated, and we keep it.

*Not Ed Witwer:* not a rival, not smug, not a man, not new to the argument.

### Miriam Motaung — Registrar of Findings

Keeper of the sealed drawer. Has copied every sealed finding since 2043 — eleven years, four hundred and
nine of them — into a ledger she keeps in a place the book does not disclose until Part Two. Not an
activist and not a whistleblower; she has never given one to anyone. She simply could not stand that
nobody was counting.

She and Josiah have been married thirty-four years and have the specific silence of two people who work in
the same building and have agreed, without ever discussing it, on one subject they do not raise.

**Voice:** dry, exact, unimpressed; the register of a woman who files things for a living and has therefore
read more of the truth than anyone with an office. Her failure mode is patience — she waited eleven years
for a moment to use the ledger, and the moment that arrives is her husband's.

**Arc:** her registry access against Grobler's name looks exactly like collusion → Motaung reads it wrong →
she is the one who puts Finding B in his hand → and she is the one who tells him what it will do to the
Estate's eleven thousand if it is published, because she is the only person who has counted them.

*Not Lisa:* she is not rescued, she has her own eleven-year campaign, and she is right before her husband
is.

---

### Cornelius "Neels" Grobler — chairman, Federation of Security Providers

Retired general; ran the East Rand through the worst of it and did it competently, which is not nothing.
Buried eleven of his own people. Watched the Act take his industry apart in four years and has spent the
decade since assembling a constitutional case, funded by an industry that would very much like its
240,000 jobs back.

**His case is correct.** Sealed findings are unconstitutional; the concurrence test has been broken since
2049; thousands are held on void warrants. **His motive is money.** Both. Always both. Any draft where the
reader can safely dismiss him has failed mandate #3.

**Voice:** courteous, unhurried, faintly amused; a man who has been underestimated so often he has made a
technique of it. Speaks Afrikaans when he wants a room to relax and English when he wants it minuted. His
failure mode is that he genuinely cannot tell the difference between his conscience and his interest, and
has stopped looking for the seam.

*Not Leopold Kaplan:* not military-restorationist, not a kidnapper by temperament, and his abduction of
Motaung is a compliance exercise with an indemnity form.

---

### W/O Dries Lombard — Directorate internal security

Twenty-two years at Motaung's shoulder. Drove him to Beauty Ngobese's funeral. On the Federation's payroll
since 2051 for reasons that are entirely legible: a daughter, a medical aid that stopped covering it, and a
first payment he told himself was a consulting fee.

**Voice:** warm, jokey, physically capable, uses first names with everyone including the Director. His
failure mode is that he is loyal to *Josiah* and has convinced himself that is different from being loyal
to the Directorate, and by the time the difference matters he is holding a firearm in a parkade.

*Not Fleming:* no ideology, no sneer, and the book grants him the dignity of a reason.

### The Readers

The house's hardest characters to write, because the temptation is to make them mystic. **They are shift
workers with a headache** (MYTHOS). Every scene in a suite must be furnished: a kettle, a lanyard, a chair
with a broken height adjustment.

- **Grace Sithole (A), 52.** Nineteen years, eleven thousand files, the last one signed at 04:11 on a
  Wednesday. Recruited in 2035 off a teaching post that had not paid since March. Her private religion is
  that she has been right every time, and it is a religion because it cannot be checked. **Voice:** steady,
  maternal in a way that is not soft, and completely certain, which by the end is unbearable to be near.
- **Wynand Loots (B), 41.** Broke in 2050 — a finding on a nineteen-year-old that he carried into a
  four-day breakdown — and was kept on, because recalibrating a Reader takes four years and the vacancy
  freeze is two per cent. Files fewer dissents than he used to, which is in his file as *improvement*.
  **Voice:** flat, over-precise, apologises before speaking; the man in the room who is *visibly the cost*
  of the system, and whom the system has classified as a maintenance item.
- **Karabo Ntuli (C), 26.** Three years in, best calibration scores in the programme's history, and keeping
  a private tally in a hardcover notebook, which is a disciplinary offence under her contract. She is the
  one who works out the recursion — not from a document, from having *felt* it in her own findings for
  months and been unable to say it, because she is not permitted to know what the others file. **Voice:**
  young, fast, technically fluent, and increasingly furious in a register that has nowhere to go. She writes
  the Reader's Note on Finding C, which is the best paragraph in the book, and it is a question.

Their common condition: **they are never told outcomes.** Play this as the quiet centre of every Reader
scene. They are the people who ended eleven thousand lives and are not permitted to know if they were right,
and they are the only people in the building who did not vote for that.

---

### Beauty Ngobese (1998–2027)

Appears only in a docket, in one memory, and in the last two pages. Killed on a Sunday afternoon in
Katlehong by a man everyone including the police knew was going to do it. **She is not a symbol and must
never be sentimentalised**: give her a wrong-sized jersey, an opinion about her sister's boyfriend, and a
day she was looking forward to. The book's whole moral weight rests on the reader agreeing, for at least
one chapter, that something should have been done — and then living with what was.

## Casting cautions (do NOT do)

- **No Anderton, Witwer, Lisa, Kaplan, Fleming as people.** The functions recur in any story about a state
  that punishes in advance; the lives are ours.
- **The successor is never disloyal**, not even briefly, not even in a scene that is later explained. The
  paranoia is the founder's own and it must have no external justification whatsoever.
- **The wife is not a rescue.** She hands him a document and a cost. What he does with them is his.
- **No Reader is a prophet.** If a Reader scene would still work with the machine removed, it is the wrong
  scene.
- **Nobody in this book is a monster.** Every person in it has a reason a reasonable reader can hold. The
  monster is the arithmetic (all-sides law, MYTHOS).
