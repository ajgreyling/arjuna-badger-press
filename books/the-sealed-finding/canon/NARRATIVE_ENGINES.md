# NARRATIVE_ENGINES — *The Sealed Finding*

> The machinery that keeps the book *moving*: clocks, vises, MacGuffins, B-stories, and the irony engine.
> Each entry answers "why does the reader turn the page." A procedural that stops moving becomes a
> position paper, and this book's ideas are strong enough to tempt it into becoming one.

## The master clock — six days

**Day One: Wednesday 4 November 2054. Day Six: Monday 9 November**, window expiring 24:00. Every chapter
head carries the date; every chapter carries at least one concrete sign of the day burning (a shift change,
a courier, the jacarandas dropping, a bank of storm on the Highveld that arrives on schedule).

The clock has a second hand the reader can see and the characters cannot: **the reader knows the window
was always going to close at the assembly**, because Finding A said "at or adjacent to a public gathering"
on page one.

## The vise (the book's primary engine — A-21)

Both available choices are indefensible, and the book's job is to tighten them until the reader cannot
choose either:

| If Motaung lets the sealed finding be read | If Motaung stops it |
|---|---|
| The concurrence test is proven void since 2049 | The Directorate stands |
| Every 2–1 warrant since is void | Thousands stay on void warrants |
| The Estate empties — **eleven thousand people** | The Estate stays full |
| Among them, a number who were correctly named | Among them, a number who were not |
| The Federation returns; the industry rebuilds | The machine keeps eating the poor |
| The murder rate is a matter of record | So is the murder rate |

Nobody in the book resolves this, including the author. The vise closes in Part Two and the shot is what
comes out of it.

## MacGuffins / leverage

| Engine | What it is | What it drives |
|---|---|---|
| **Finding A** | the warrant with his own name on it | all of Part One: the flight, the wrong suspicions |
| **The sealed finding (B)** | the document that exonerates him and voids the state | all of Part Two: everyone wants it, for opposite reasons |
| **Miriam's ledger** | 409 sealed findings, copied over eleven years | the true dissent rate; the only honest number in the country; Part Two's turn |
| **Finding C** | the finding that says he does it *knowingly* | Part Three: the reader holds it while Motaung walks toward it |
| **The Ngobese docket** | a 2027 case file in a drawer | the founding wound; the closing hand-off (PP-008) |
| **Karabo's notebook** | a private tally, a disciplinary offence | the recursion's discovery; the Reader's Note |

## The irony engine (the structural signature)

The reader is handed each finding at the head of its part. **No character holds more than one until the
end.** Every scene therefore runs on a gap: the reader knows the shape of the future the character is
walking into, and the tension is not *what happens* but *when he crosses from one finding into the next* —
which is a line no one can see, including him, including us on a second read. That irreducibility is the
point.

## Stakes ladder (rising)

1. **Personal** — a man's liberty, marriage, and reputation, inside six days.
2. **Institutional** — the Directorate's survival, and with it a policy the whole country has settled into.
3. **Moral** — the eleven thousand on the Estate, and whether a true thing may be suppressed because of
   what it would cost.

The book climbs rung by rung; the coda stands on the top rung and looks back down at the bottom one — a
single man on a lawn, handing over a docket. Smaller than the idea (STYLE_GUIDE).

## B-stories (the human ballast — anti-abstraction)

- **The Readers' thread.** Three sealed rooms, running under everything, converging on Karabo's note. This
  is where the book's grief lives; the procedural spine stays dry so this can be wet.
- **The marriage.** Thirty-four years and one subject not raised. Its silence is the book's quietest clock.
- **The named.** Three case cards, one per part — ordinary people, half a page, never mentioned again. They
  are the reason none of the argument is abstract.

## Anti-sag instrumentation

- **No beat without an engine.** Every PLOT beat must hook to at least one entry above. A beat that hooks
  to none is exposition and gets cut or rebuilt (STYLE_GUIDE velocity gate).
- **Two detonations only** (the recursion; the assembly). A twist every chapter is its own kind of sag.
- **The Part Two danger zone.** Part Two carries the book's ideas and is where a novel of argument goes
  grey. Every Part Two chapter must carry the clock *and* a personal cost — the abduction, the marriage,
  the payroll entry, the ledger. If a chapter is "and here is more of the case," it is rebuilt.
