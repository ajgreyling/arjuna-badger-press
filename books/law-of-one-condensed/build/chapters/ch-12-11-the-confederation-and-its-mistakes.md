## 11. The Confederation and Its Mistakes


Ra places itself within a grouping of higher-density social memory complexes that serve third-density planets: the Confederation, in the material's translation, and a name Ra treats as a rough approximation rather than a title.

Their constraint is absolute and it is the first distortion. **They may not interfere.** No proof, no rescue, no intervention. They may answer questions that are asked, and only in terms the asker can receive. This is not caution. It is the ethical core of the positive path, since a being cannot be helped into a choice it did not make, and the choice is the entire content of third density. Removing it would be the one genuinely destructive act available.

So they wait, and they are asked badly, and they answer partially, and the results are frequently a disaster. The material's own account of Confederation history is largely a catalogue of well-meant failures, and this is the most honest section in the whole document.

**Egypt.** Ra says it came to Egypt in physical form, taught, and gave the pyramids as instruments for healing and initiation. Within a generation or two the teaching had been captured by a priesthood, the instruments had become the machinery of an elite, and a body of knowledge intended to free people was being used to rank them. Ra describes this as its own error rather than the Egyptians', because it failed to anticipate what its help would become in the hands it left it in. It withdrew.

**The Law of One as religion.** Every attempt to give the teaching produced a hierarchy. Ra's diagnosis is that any transmission from an apparently superior source generates worship, worship generates intermediaries, and intermediaries generate exactly the separation the teaching was meant to dissolve. The failure is nearly structural.

**Contaminated transmissions.** Ra's claim that the reception of the commandments was penetrated by a negative source, and that a body of instruction meant to convey unity acquired an overlay of obedience, hierarchy, and a jealous deity, is among the material's more inflammatory statements. It is offered less an attack on any tradition than an illustration of the general problem: a channel is a channel, and what comes through it depends on the state of the instrument and on who else is transmitting.

The pattern is consistent enough to be the real lesson. **Help, delivered from above into third density, tends to become the opposite of itself.** Which is why the Confederation's default is silence, and why Ra spends so much of the sessions warning the Kentucky group not to build anything around what it was being told.
