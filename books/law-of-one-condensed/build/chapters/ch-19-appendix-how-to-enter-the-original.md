## Appendix — How to Enter the Original


If this book has done its job, you now have a frame, and the transcript becomes navigable rather than exhausting, because you can tell what a session is about before you're forty exchanges into it.

**The full text** is free at **lawofone.info**, searchable and cross-referenced by concept, with the original audio. It also hosts the Relistening Project: beginning in 2004, Tobey Wheelock went back to the original tapes and listened to every session against the published transcripts, recovering material that had never been printed and correcting errors. The site publishes a report of what changed. That corrected text is what you read there.

**llresearch.org** has all its books as free PDFs and ebooks. The edition to want is *The Ra Contact: Teaching the Law of One* (two volumes), which incorporates the relistening corrections. The original five-volume *Law of One* set is still in circulation and still fine, but it is the uncorrected text.

**A reading path, rather than page one:**

Start with the sessions on the Choice and on polarity. This is the spine, and everything else hangs from it. Then the veil material, where the system stops being cosmology and starts being about your actual life. Then the energy centres, the most immediately usable thing in the document. Then catalyst and the balancing practice.

Leave the archetypal mind for a second pass, and expect it to be hard. Leave the historical and technological material for last, or skip it. You now know why.

**Read it in small pieces.** The material's own structure is question-and-answer, and it rewards being read as it was produced: one exchange, then a pause. Reading it like a book is what defeats most people, including you the first time, and the fault is the format's rather than yours.

**Use the search.** Once you have a frame, the honest way to read this material is by question. Bring one you have, search the concept index for it, and read the four or five exchanges bearing on it across the three years. The repetition you found so wearing becomes an asset in that mode: you get to watch an idea get sharper as Don learns to ask better, which is its own quiet pleasure and the closest thing the transcript has to a plot.
