## 12. The Other Harvest


The negative path harvests too, and it also recruits.

The material describes a grouping of negative-polarity entities, the Orion group in its shorthand, pursuing on the large scale what a negative entity pursues on the small: the extension of control. Their method is *polarisation* rather than conquest. A negative harvest requires harvestable negative entities, and so the work is to move a population toward separation, hierarchy, and the conviction of its own specialness.

The characteristic instrument is **elitism**. The material identifies this as the signature to watch for, and it is a genuinely useful diagnostic. Teachings arriving from apparently elevated sources that emphasise the chosen status of the recipients, that distinguish an in-group of the awake from a mass of the sleeping, that convey urgency, secrecy, and an implied hierarchy: these are the shape of the negative transmission, regardless of how much true material they carry. And they often carry a great deal, because inaccurate information is a poor vehicle.

The distinguishing test the material offers is the *effect* rather than the content. Does the teaching leave the recipient more separate from other people, or less? Positive transmissions dissolve the boundary; negative ones fortify it while flattering the one inside.

The material also notes, coolly, that negative attention concentrates on those doing effective positive work, that the Kentucky group experienced this directly, and that the appropriate response is neither fear nor confrontation but a refusal to engage on the offered terms, since fear is itself the substance the negative path runs on. What is not fed does not stay.
