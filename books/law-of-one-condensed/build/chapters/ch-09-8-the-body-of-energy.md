## 8. The Body of Energy


The material describes a human being as an energy system before it describes it as anything else: a conduit through which light rises from the base of the body to the crown, encountering seven centres on the way. The scheme maps closely onto the chakra systems of Indian tradition, and the material treats that correspondence as unremarkable. The same structure has been described many times, and it is being described again here in the vocabulary of light.

The mechanical principle is what matters, and it is simple. **Light rises from below and must pass through every centre in turn.** A blockage anywhere starves everything above it. There is no bypass, no shortcut, no way to develop a high centre while a low one is jammed, and this single rule dismantles most of what passes for spiritual practice.

### Red: survival

The base. Survival, sexuality, the raw fact of physical existence, the animal will to continue.

The material treats red ray as almost never blocked, and is refreshingly unromantic about it. This is the intake valve for the whole system, and the energy that eventually becomes the highest spiritual experience an entity is capable of comes in *through here*. There is no version of the path that involves despising the body. Contempt for the physical is not asceticism; it is a blockage at the foundation, and everything above it goes hungry.

### Orange: the self among individuals

Personal identity in relation to other single beings. Self-worth, personal power, one-to-one relationship.

Blocked by the two failures of self-regard: the person who cannot value themselves, and the person who can only value themselves by diminishing someone else. Both are the same jam seen from opposite sides.

### Yellow: the self among groups

Family, tribe, workplace, nation. The self as a member of things.

The material's judgment here is blunt. This is where the overwhelming majority of humans live and die. Yellow ray is the density of loyalty, belonging, status, and the whole machinery of us-and-them, and it is not a low or shameful place to be. It is simply the last centre before the one that changes everything, and the traffic jam is enormous.

A large portion of human political and social life, on this reading, is an entire civilisation working the yellow ray and mistaking it for the summit.

### Green: the heart, and the bottleneck

Universal love. Not love of my people, my family, my kind, but love that has stopped checking credentials.

**This is the crossing.** Everything below green is the self arranging itself in relation to what it already identifies with. Green is where that stops, and where the entity begins to radiate rather than transact. It is also, the material says without much softening, where the human species is stuck: the great mass of us can love our own and cannot love beyond them, and the entire drama of third-density Earth is the drama of a green ray that will not open.

And the reason it won't is not that people are wicked. It is that opening the heart requires being hurt without closing again, repeatedly, on purpose, with no guarantee. Every unprocessed injury is a small closing. A life's worth of them is a sealed door: reasonable, well-defended, and fatal to further progress.

This is why the fifty-one percent threshold sits exactly where it does. It is not a measure of behaviour. It is a measure of *whether the door is open at all*.

### Blue: free communication

The honest expression of what one is. Not eloquence. *Transparency*: the capacity to say the true thing and to receive another's true thing without defending.

Blue ray opens only after green, and the ordering is not arbitrary. You cannot afford to be transparent until you have stopped needing to be liked. Most of what people call communication is negotiation conducted at the yellow ray.

### Indigo: work in consciousness

The gateway. Here the entity works directly on itself. This is the seat of the disciplines, of the deep intelligence beneath the veil, of everything the traditions call adept work. Ra calls it the gateway to intelligent infinity, and it is the centre through which genuine healing, genuine magic, and genuine contact operate.

Its characteristic blockage is unexpected and worth sitting with: **unworthiness**. The conviction that one is not fit for this, that the deep things are for other people. The material identifies self-contempt as the single most common obstruction at the gateway, the reason people who have done real work still cannot pass through.

### Violet: the reading

Violet is not a centre to be worked. It is a readout: the sum of all the others, the entity's vibratory state, unfalsifiable and not subject to opinion.

Which is a quietly ruthless piece of design. You cannot lie about violet ray. It is not an assessment anyone makes of you, including yourself. It is simply the total, and it is what is read at harvest.

### The practical upshot

The system is a plumbing diagram, and it explains a specific and very common failure: the person with a serious meditation practice, real insight, genuine hours logged, and a jammed orange ray, or an unexamined yellow-ray loyalty, or a green ray sealed shut by an injury from decades ago. All that indigo work draws on light that never arrives.

The material's advice follows directly and is not glamorous. **Work from the bottom.** Fix the money, the body, the resentment, the relationship you have been avoiding. The unglamorous lower work is not preliminary to the spiritual work. It *is* the spiritual work, and skipping it doesn't produce an adept. It produces a well-read person with a blocked heart.
