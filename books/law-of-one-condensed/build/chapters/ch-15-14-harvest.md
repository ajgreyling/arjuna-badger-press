## 14. Harvest


Everything converges here, and almost everything popularly said about it is wrong.

### It is not a judgment

There is no tribunal. No one is assessed by anyone. The material's account is that at the end of a cycle an entity encounters light of a particular intensity, and either can bear to remain in it or cannot. That is the entire mechanism.

Violet ray, the readout of the whole self, unfalsifiable and not a matter of opinion, determines what the entity can tolerate. An entity that has opened the green ray finds the light comfortable. An entity that has not finds it unbearable and withdraws, not from expulsion; it simply cannot stand there.

The material's image for what follows is a life review conducted by the entity itself, in the presence of its higher self and with the whole of the incarnation visible at once. No accusation is made. None is needed: the difficulty lies in *seeing clearly*, without the ordinary human machinery for looking away. The entity assesses itself, and the material says entities are usually far harsher on themselves than anything external would be.

### Earth is transitioning

The claim is that the planet itself has already begun the shift into fourth density, and that this is a process rather than an event, proceeding on a scale where a century is not a long time.

Ra was asked repeatedly for dates and repeatedly declined, which is one of the more credible things it did. Its stated reasons: the future is genuinely fluid, precision would constitute interference, and the instrument's own expectations would contaminate any answer given. What it did offer was a rough window and a great deal of hedging. The parts of the material that later readers hardened into a specific prophecy were not delivered as one. The prediction problem is dealt with honestly in Chapter 17.

### Most are not ready

Ra's estimate at the time was that the harvestable proportion of humanity was small.

What happens to the rest is the part almost always reported wrongly. They continue. Third density elsewhere, on another world configured for it, with the whole apparatus of forgetting and choice intact, and, critically, with nothing lost. No entity is destroyed, discarded, punished, or held against its will. Repetition is not damnation and carries, in the material's own framing, no shame at all.

The material is emphatic on this point in a way that most eschatologies are not: **there is no hell in this system, and no permanent loss.** The worst available outcome is another long turn through a school you have already attended, which is a real cost measured in time and difficulty, and is nothing whatever like condemnation.

### What it asks of you

Nothing dramatic, and this is where the material lands most quietly.

There is no preparation to undertake, no location to reach, no group to join, no date to prepare for. The harvest measures what you have become, and what you have become is built out of ordinary days: the green ray opened a little further by an act of patience nobody witnessed, a resentment finally set down, a person loved who did not earn it.

The material's entire practical instruction, after three hundred thousand words, reduces to something a great deal smaller than its cosmology. **Open the heart, and mean it.** Everything else in this book is explanation of why that is the thing, and machinery for understanding what is in the way.
