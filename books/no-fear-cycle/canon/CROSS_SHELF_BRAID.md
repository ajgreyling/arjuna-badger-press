## Transposition — *Honour's Edge* Battle-Council ("the Court")

The strike cruiser **Honour's Edge** carries a Mechanicus-pattern **Novahistorium battle-council** — a
chorus of cogitator voices Marines hear as **vox strata**, not personalities. In-universe it is Imperial
war-machine doctrine. Cross-shelf it is **the Court in a different form** (RELIC capstone language).

| Court agent | Ship stratum | Function in series |
|---|---|---|
| **Judge** | *Ordinance Authorisation* | Strike grid, interlock, "confirm holder" — primary orbit voice |
| **Wolf** | *Threat Perimeter* | Auspex, vector, hostiles; **speaks rarely**; cold numeric bursts |
| **Mother** | *Life Sign / Medicae* | Metaurus sus-an, squad skull icons, "declining" |
| **Fool** | *Manual Override* | **Human** sergeant/officer bypass when cogitator deadlock — Metaurus passing keys to Titus |
| **Librarian** | *Archive / Grid Doctrine* | VOG node history, Library of Lies briefings |
| **Atlas** | *Void Cartography* | Corridor navigation, node positions |
| **Mercury** | *Vox Warfare* | Comms degradation, lie-parchment jamming |

**Binding rule:** Marines never call it "the Court" on-page unless one line of Metaurus litany or
Syama foreword wink. They hear **channels**. Readers of the wider press recognise the braid.

## *Wolf Gets the Keys* — three beats across the cycle

### Beat A — Book I, Ch 1 *(on page)* — **the interlock passes**

**Engine:** Judge cannot hold ground truth on a dying sergeant; Fool (Metaurus) opens the gate manually;
**Wolf channel accepts Titus** as primary holder — ordnance keys, not bolter keys.

**40k skin:** `INTERLOCK: JUDGE — DECLINING` → `FOOL OVERRIDE: SERGEANT METAURUS` → `WOLF CHANNEL: PRIMARY
HOLDER — LT. TITUS` → `VOG NODE I: LOCK CONFIRMED`

**Emotion:** Metaurus chooses to sleep knowing the boy holds the crown he never wanted.

This is the **soft** Wolf-gets-keys — authority transfer, not violence POV-break.

---

### Beat B — Book IV, one chapter *(planned)* — **the interior break**

**Engine:** Jakobus Ellora / Mode 6 — marked POV break into Titus's interior during the Bladeguard
mission. Section rule + flag (*This is the inside.*). Present-tense italic or cold close-third procedural.
Page-time dilates against seconds.

**Interior tone:** NOT rage — **craftsman**. The child the sorcerer found, now a weapon doing sums.
*Enough and not one ounce past enough.* Snap back to Metaurus watching — **recalibration** — he has never
seen Titus the same way (mirrors Priya/Jakobus after Ellora).

**40k skin:** Titus clears a shrine corridor alone while Metaurus watches from sus-an carriage — knife and
bolter, no daemon, no quips. Horror that he doesn't become an animal; he becomes **exact**.

**One chapter only.** Never repeated. Same as Mode 6.

---

### Beat C — Book V finale *(planned)* — **Wolf fires the grid**

**Engine:** Fool (Metaurus's last act or recorded doctrine) + Wolf (Titus as threat-read who **wants the
keys least**) certify the **child signature** — seventh coordinate — while Judge opens the mass strike
once.

**Payoff:** The keys go home. The grid fires. The corridor closes. **Never repeated.**

## RELIC echo — Court withdraws

Book V, after mass strike: battle-council voices **go quiet** not from failure but from **honest limit** —
the grid is logged; no further authorisation exists. Mirrors Priya's Court naming the fence and stepping
back (RELIC ch-23).

One line, orbit, post-fire:

*"Ordinance complete. No further holder required."*

Silence. Titus alone on ash.
