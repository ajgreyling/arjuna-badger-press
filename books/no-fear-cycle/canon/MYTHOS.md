# MYTHOS — *The No-Fear Cycle*

> The binding "physics" and metaphysics of the 41st Millennium **as this series uses them.** Adapted from
> Games Workshop canon and the fan corpus gate — **literal in-world, never soft-pedalled**, yet **earned
> through physical detail** so it reads visceral rather than silly. This is what lets a lore-purist stay
> aboard without a lecture.

---

## Rule 1 — The Imperium is true, and it is monstrous

The Emperor, the Ecclesiarchy, the Chapter cult — all **exist and function** in-world. Marines believe.
The reader is not invited to find a better ideology. The series dramatises **duty without redemption** —
and finds meaning in **brotherhood within the horror**, not escape from it.

**Fail mode:** modern moral lecturing; "actually the Imperium is wrong and here's the good faction."

---

## Rule 2 — The body leads, the lore follows (binding — the Astartes move)

The reader earns the 40k-ness by **feeling the organ fail, the seal break, the bolt's physics** before
any proper noun lands. Name a daemon **after** the bird-eyes and the time-stop. Name Tzeentch **after**
the parchment lie condenses on rubble.

**Fail mode:** wiki exposition; characters explaining the Warp to each other; opening with a history of
the Horus Heresy.

---

## Rule 3 — Fear is the weapon; "no fear" is the exception, not bravado

Chaos sorcery in this series kills by **showing you what you love corrupted.** Metaurus falls because
Titus matters. Titus stands because the sorcerer finds **only a child** — not courage, not faith: **a
pre-linguistic self** before fear had vocabulary.

The McGuffin's final coordinate requires that same signature — not heroism, **ontology.**

**Fail mode:** Titus as power fantasy invincible; fearlessness as quip; anime defiance.

---

## Rule 4 — Silence and HUD are scripture

Following Syama Pedersen's Astartes law and the Secret Level episode's **wordless middle act:**

- Long stretches **without dialogue** are permitted and encouraged.
- **HUD text, status warnings, skull icons** may carry emotion equal to interior monologue.
- Orbital command speaks **liturgical brief** — coordinates as prayer.

**Fail mode:** banter-heavy squads; Marvel quips mid-bolter; narrator explaining what HUD already showed.

---

## Rule 5 — Sacrifice is local, victory is temporary

Marines die. Missions are suicide by brief. Orbital strikes **erase geography** and call it success. The
series-end grid strike **closes one corridor** — the galaxy remains damned.

**Fail mode:** saving the Imperium; Chaos permanently defeated; resurrection cop-outs.

---

## Rule 6 — Fan corpus enriches; official canon wins

When Lexicanum, Astartes aesthetic, Gaunt's Ghosts tone, or fic tropes conflict with GW primary sources,
**GW wins.** Fan corpus supplies **texture and demand**, not plot veto.

| Fan tribe demand | Series response |
|---|---|
| Organ/HUD detail | Rules 2 & 4 |
| Titus/Metaurus mentorship | Spine + Book IV |
| Military brotherhood | Metaurus skull icons; squad losses |
| No Chaos wank | Rule 3; bird-mutation as horror |
| The orbital strike payoff | VOG McGuffin — series-end mass fire |

**Fail mode:** fanon character inserted as canon; shipping overrides mission; Heresy-era retcon without gate.

---

## Rule 7 — The Library of Lies is physical

Lies **materialise** on thin-veil worlds — parchment, whispers, eggshell, fresh text of deceptions
happening light-years away. Destroying a relic **weakens** local Warp bleed; it does not empty the Library.

Books II–III treat each fragment as **evidence first, metaphysics second** — the Crichton move applied to
cosmic horror.

**Fail mode:** treating Chaos as abstract mood; deus ex machina warp storms.

---

## Rule 8 — The knife was always his

Titus's killing blade on Zsah'uj is **the same knife** Metaurus found on the boy in the battlefield
flashback (Secret Level / SM2 continuity). Objects **chain memory** across the series — not as magic items,
as **witnesses.**

Book IV (*Bladeguard*) honours this: weapons carry doctrine.

**Fail mode:** gear porn without history; interchangeable wargear.

---

## Rule 9 — The Court speaks in channels; the keys pass once (cross-shelf braid)

The strike cruiser *Honour's Edge* carries a **Novahistorium battle-council** — vox strata that map, cross-
shelf, to **The Court** (see `CROSS_SHELF_BRAID.md`): Judge authorises ordnance; Wolf reads threat;
Mother counts life; **Fool is human override** when cogitators deadlock.

**Wolf gets the keys** is transposed three times:

1. **Book I** — Metaurus (Fool) passes primary holder to Titus (Wolf channel); ordnance interlock.
2. **Book IV** — one POV-break chapter inside Titus's cold interior (Jakobus / Mode 6 prose equivalent).
3. **Book V** — mass strike certification; keys return; Court goes quiet at the fence.

**Fail mode:** chatty AI banter; Wolf speaking constantly; repeated override; naming "SAGE" or trilogy cast.
