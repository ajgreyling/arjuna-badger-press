# CHARACTERS — *The No-Fear Cycle*

> Canon-faithful extraction for the series cast. **Primary:** GW / *Space Marine 2* / *Secret Level*
> episode. **Secondary:** fan corpus voice laws. Sourced for **Ultramarines galactic-west corridor
> operations** — not a full Chapter roster.

---

## Lieutenant Demetrian Titus

- **Role:** protagonist; Ultramarines Lieutenant; the boy who knew no fear.
- **Canon facts:** Ultramarine-blue eyes **from childhood** (Community-confirmed); served Deathwatch
  penance; voiced by Clive Standen in SM2 and Secret Level; carries the **knife Metaurus found on him**
  as a boy.
- **Mind:** preternatural fearlessness is **not bravery** — it is an empty place where fear should live.
  Sorcerers find a **child**, not a hero. He learns doctrine late; he executes early.
- **Voice:** sparse; respectful to Metaurus ("old man" only when earned); no quips under fire.
- **Arc (series):** weapon → signatory of the final VOG coordinate → the exception the Imperium cannot
  replicate.
- **Flaws/cost:** abandons sarcophagus duty once to kill a tank (episode beat); trusts violence before
  protocol; may outlive every brother who taught him.

## Bladeguard Sergeant Metaurus

- **Role:** mentor; narrator of the Secret Level cold open; POV anchor for Prologue and parts of Book I.
- **Canon facts:** Bladeguard Veteran; armouring chamber with **Guilliman statue** (rank privilege);
  recruited Titus from a battlefield; Adewale Akinnuoye-Agbaje's voice — measured, paternal, grim.
- **Mind:** reads Titus as **salvation and risk** — the one who won't fall to Chaos, until the sorcerer
  shows him the fall anyway.
- **HUD (post-episode):** Larraman critical failure; Belisarian Furnace unresponsive; Oolitic inactive;
  sus-an primed; atmospheric seal compromised; battle-brother skulls mostly **red**.
- **Voice:** internal litany; brief orders; disappointment without cruelty; pride without speech.
- **Arc (series):** dying teacher → sus-an sleep or honoured death → doctrine passed to Titus.
- **Function:** the **Watson-frame inverted** — the experienced man who *cannot* solve the Warp problem;
  only the fearless boy can.

## The Astropath *(designation: Vox-Seven / "the one in the box")*

- **Role:** mission asset; Geller-analogue anchor; **dies in the episode** — persists as echo, flashback,
  and sarcophagus doctrine.
- **Canon facts:** Mark Sheppard voices astropath **and** orbital command — liturgical overlap by design;
  plugged into field generator "similar elements to Geller Field"; opens sarcophagus once, dies to
  sorcerer.
- **Series use:** Book II (*The Sarcophagus Road*) — other psykers in other boxes; the **pattern** is
  the cost. Book I — memory, vox fragments, guilt.

- **Canon facts:** rested in Lord of Change staff orb; bird-mutation cult; time-stop fear weapon; bisected

## Voice laws (binding for all new prose)

1. **Marines show status, not lore** — HUD, breath, weight; never wiki-speak.
2. **Metaurus may grieve in litany** — never in speech to the squad mid-fire. His interior runs long and recursive; he remembers while counting organs. He does not close thoughts on one-word snaps.
3. **Titus respects Metaurus before he loves him** — affection is **action** (dragging body, holding line). His interior is stimulus-response: see, move, count, confirm. No thesis statements; no aphorism closers.
4. **Kael speaks in measurements** — binary, then protocol. Not aphorisms. One beat where correct math costs time.
5. **Voren speaks in load order** — serial, clamp, mag-count. Techmarine register only.
6. **Everson speaks in clinical record** — full sentences, dissent on the slate, jaw tight when emotion leaks.
7. **Chaos speaks only to corrupt** — no friendly daemons; no moral ambiguity.
8. **Orbital command is liturgy** — coordinates are catechism.
9. **The child in Titus is sacred** — do not make him cute; make him **empty of fear, full of future.** State the empty-place fact once (Red Field); never restate.
