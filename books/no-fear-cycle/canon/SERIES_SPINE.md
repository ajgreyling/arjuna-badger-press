# SERIES SPINE — *The No-Fear Cycle* (5 novels, finite)

> The relay map, the **Veil Ordinance Grid (VOG)** McGuffin, and per-book handoffs. **Binding:** five books,
> no infinite escalation; the reader who finishes Book V receives **closure** — corridor sealed, mentor
> arc resolved, Titus's nature affirmed.

---

## The McGuffin — Veil Ordinance Grid (VOG)

**What it is:** seven orbital-strike coordinates along the **thin-veil corridor** in the galactic west.
Each coordinate requires:

1. **Ground truth** — a team reaches the apostate node under Geller-analogue cover.
2. **Psyker anchor** — a sarcophagus psyker holds the field long enough to **log uncorrupted numbers**.
3. **Certification** — orbit receives confirmation before Warp bleed rewrites the math.

**Secret Level destroyed Node VI (Zsah'uj)** and proved Titus can certify under sorcerer assault — but
**six nodes remain**, and the **seventh signature** cannot come from any psyker: it requires the
**pre-fear cognitive signature** only Titus carries (what the sorcerer found in his mind).

**Series-end payoff:** all seven fire as **one mass strike** — the orbital bombardment the episode
promised, scaled to **close the corridor**. Metaurus is dead, in sus-an, or witnessing from orbit. Titus
is no longer a boy. The Imperium gains **one less wound in its skin** — not victory. **Finality.**

```
NODE I ── Book I  (*Ordinance Pending*)      ── squad holds Zsah'uj aftermath; recover first anchor
NODE II ── Book II (*The Sarcophagus Road*)   ── escort chain; second psyker dies clean
NODE III ── Book III (*Fragments…*)           ── Library lies as evidence; third & fourth coords
NODE IV ── Book IV (*Bladeguard*)             ── Metaurus wakes or dies; fifth coord; the knife doctrine
NODE V–VII ── Book V (*They Shall Know*)      ── final assault; child-signature; MASS STRIKE
```

## Cross-book continuity locks

- Titus's **knife** appears in every book — same object.
- Metaurus HUD injuries **persist or resolve** — no magic heal between I and IV without Mechanicus cost.
- Skull icons **do not come back grey** unless explicitly recovered (no fake deaths).
- Orbital voice **same register** — liturgical, numeric.
- **No Primarchs on-page** — scale stays squad/planet.

---

## What we deliberately do not do

- No Horus Heresy flashback series.
- No Chaos Marine protagonist arc.
- No romantic subplot.
- No "Titus becomes Chapter Master" epilogue — **closure is the strike**, not promotion.
