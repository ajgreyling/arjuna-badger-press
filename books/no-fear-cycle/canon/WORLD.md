# WORLD — *The No-Fear Cycle*

> Binding geography and in-universe physics for the series. Inherits Games Workshop *Warhammer 40,000*
> official setting; this file is **series-specific** — the galactic west, the thin-veil corridor, and
> the operational reality of a Bladeguard squad post–Secret Level.

---

## The shape of this world

The 41st Millennium. The Imperium of Man spans a million worlds and is dying in every direction at
once. There is no hope in the modern sense — only duty, and the occasional clean kill.

This series is **narrow by design**: not the whole galaxy, but the **galactic west** — the frontier
near *Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2* territory and the Deathwatch Watch Fortress where Lieutenant
**Demetrian Titus** served penance after the first campaign. The war here is not the big Crusade
headlines. It is **suicide missions, relic hunts, and corridors where the Warp sits too close.**

---

## The thin-veil corridor

A chain of worlds where **reality's skin is thin** — not Eye-of-Terror thin, but enough that:

- **Vox and auspex degrade** unpredictably; targeters lie; orbital comms drop for minutes that cost lives.
- **Warp leakage manifests physically:** broken eggshells where no birds exist; parchment scraps — fresh
  lies told elsewhere in the galaxy, catalogued in the **Library of Lies** (Tzeentch) — materialise from
  sparks in the air.
- **Chaos cults flourish** on apostate relics: idols of change, bird-mutated heretics, sorcerers who
  kill with memory instead of blades.
- **Geller-analogue fields** (astropath sarcophagi, field generators on Mechanicus pattern) are the only
  reason Space Marines can walk these worlds without their minds being flayed open.

### Zsah'uj *(Node VI — the Secret Level world)*

- **Location:** far galactic west; near SM2 worlds; name pronunciation deliberately awkward (bird-beak
  consonants — fan in-joke from Community article).
- **Terrain:** rubble fields, traitor armour wreckage, temple-shrine to a **Lord of Change** idol;
  lesser Tzeentch daemon statuary at the margins.
- **State after the episode:** relic orbitally destroyed; sorcerer dead; **Warp disruption weakened but
  not gone**; cultist hordes still rising; Metaurus critical; Titus the last functional officer on the
  ground.

## Operational reality (what the prose must feel like)

### Space Marine biology as lived experience

Marines are not men in armour. They are **post-human weapons** whose bodies are organs and litanies:

| Organ / system | In-scene use |
|---|---|
| **Larraman's Organ** | wound clotting; "critical failure" = bleeding that won't stop |
| **Belisarian Furnace** | combat stim; "unresponsive" = cold, slow, mortal |
| **Oolitic Kidney** | toxin filter; inactive = poison and panic |
| **Sus-an membrane** | near-death hibernation; "primed" = the long sleep may take him |
| **Black carapace** | armour interface; seal integrity = void-capable or not |
| **Secondary heart / multi-lung** | HUD status lines; never lecture — **flash in crisis** |

### The armour as character

Power armour is **sacred machine and coffin**. HUD corners track battle-brother skull icons (grey live,
red dead). Atmospheric seal warnings. Ammunition trackers on plasma weapons (photonic hydrogen). Bolter
rounds shown **frozen mid-flight** in sorcerer time-stop — propellant, stabiliser, the physics fans
demand.

### Warp and psykers (grounded horror, not magic)

- Psykers **burn out, scream, die**; the astropath-in-a-box is a **consumable asset**, not a wizard.
- Sorcerers attack **memory and fear** — the weapon is intimacy, not lightning.
- Chaos wins when Marines **doubt their brothers** — Metaurus's vision of corrupted Titus is the perfect
  weapon because it uses love as leverage.

## Sensory texture

Recycled air inside a helm. Distant orbital vox like prayer. Bolter reports **felt through the soles**
before heard. Parchment lying on rubble that **wasn't there a second ago**. Bird cries from human throats.
The weight of a sarcophagus chain in a dead man's grip. Blue melta secondary flash. Silence when the
episode — and this prose — earns it.

---

## Constraints the plot must respect

- **Official continuity** — Titus post–SM2; Metaurus as Bladeguard mentor; no resurrection of dead episode
  characters except via flashback.
- **Grimdark honesty** — victories are local; the Imperium is not "saved"; a sealed corridor is a **good
  day's work**.
- **No Chaos sympathy** — corruption is horror, not aesthetic choice; bird-eyes on cultists are **body
  horror**, not cool.
- **Silence is load-bearing** — exposition is the enemy; the Astartes law applies: **show the HUD, show
  the wound, show the strike.**
