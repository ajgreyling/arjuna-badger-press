# FAN CORPUS — *The No-Fear Cycle*

> Map of the **heavy fan factions** and how this series serves them **without** letting fanon override GW.
> The collective corpus is **canon-enrichment**, not co-author. Use their demands as **quality gates.**

---

## Faction map

### 1. The Astartes / Syama Pedersen visualists

**What they love:** wordless combat; bolter physics; armour weight; HUD as emotion; no exposition;
Space Marines as **terrifying**, not relatable sitcom dads.

**Corpus sources:** *Astartes* (2018–2020); Syama's Secret Level layout contribution; *Astartes II*
(teaser aesthetic).

**Series response:** MYTHOS Rules 2 & 4; Prologue/Ch1 **silence-first**; frozen bolt detail; skull HUD.

**Fail them if:** quips; camera-aware banter; lore lectures; Marvel pacing.

---

### 2. Space Marine 2 / Titus continuity tribe

**What they love:** Clive Standen's Titus; Deathwatch penance; Metaurus mentorship; **the boy who knew
no fear**; respect for Guilliman orthodoxy; post-game "what happened next."

**Corpus sources:** SM2 campaign; Secret Level episode; Warhammer Community episode articles.

**Series response:** picks up **minutes after** the episode; Titus/Metaurus spine; blue eyes; knife origin;
"old man" earned.

**Fail them if:** Titus personality reset; Metaurus absent; contradicting SM2 beats.

---

### 3. Lexicanum / lore purists

**What they love:** correct organs; Belisarian vs Larraman; Geller fields; Tzeentch as schemer not cartoon;
Ultramarines as Codex not meme.

**Corpus sources:** Lexicanum; Warhammer Community "cool details" articles; codex chain.

**Series response:** WORLD organ table; Library of Lies physicality; tilting shield scavenged from wreck;
melta-secondary on corrupted reactor.

**Fail them if:** wrong organ names; "magic" without warp mechanism; Chapter culture as parody.

---

### 4. Gaunt's Ghosts / military SF brotherhood readers

**What they love:** squad as family; officers who **carry guilt**; convoy missions; dying well; competent
 evil.

**Corpus sources:** Dan Abnett; similar: *The Last Stand*, *Band of Brothers* fic crossovers on AO3.

**Series response:** Book II convoy; skull icons; Metaurus guilt over Titus vision; losses **felt** in HUD.

**Fail them if:** solo hero wins alone; deaths without weight; incompetent Imperium for cheap cynicism.

---

### 5. Black Library / Horus Heresy tone scholars

**What they love:** grimdark **as literature**; sacrifice; Imperial creed without satire; Chaos as **spiritual
 cancer**.

**Corpus sources:** *Horus Rising* restraint; *Gaunt*; *Eisenhorn* investigation edge (light touch here).

**Series response:** MYTHOS Rule 1 & 5; liturgical orbit; no hope — **meaning in duty**.

**Fail them if:** grimdark as joke; Chaos POV sympathy; Emperor as villain reveal.

---

### 6. Archive / fanfic brotherhood writers

**What they love:** hurt/comfort after battle; mentor/student; **sus-an sleep** angst; last rites; carrying
 wounded brothers; "old man" nicknames.

**Corpus tags (AO3):** `Space Marines`, `Ultramarines`, `Hurt/Comfort`, `Character Study`, `Angst`.

**Series response:** Metaurus/Titus **action-not-speech** bond; dragging body; sus-an primed; no slash
 (keeps faction without splitting sub-tribe).

**Fail them if:** emotional confession mid-firefight; out-of-character softness without earned beat.

---

### 7. Anti-woke grimdark purists *(handle with care)*

**What they want:** unfiltered Imperium; no modern morality; violence uncensored.

**Series response:** **Yes** to uncensored war; **No** to edgelord performative cruelty — grimdark is
 **honest**, not a culture-war flag. Rule 1: Imperium true and monstrous **in-world**, not as author sermon.

**Fail them if:** sanitised combat; fail **everyone else** if: cruelty without point; torture porn.

---

### 8. Animation / Secret Level anthology fans

**What they love:** Tim Miller/Blur production values **in prose**; short-story density; one clear mission;
 orbital strike button.

**Corpus sources:** Secret Level ep 5; Love Death Robots pacing.

**Series response:** finite five books (not bloated); each novel **one mission leg**; strike payoff at V.

**Fail them if:** bloated cast; multi-front epic; no strike.

---

## Easter-egg layer (optional, never load-bearing)

- Parchment lies referencing **current-year** deceptions (reader smirk — not plot).
- HUD lines echoing Community article details (photonic hydrogen tracker).
- Armouring chamber Guilliman statue — Metaurus rank flex once per series.
- Bird calls in cultist war cries — **always horror.**

## Satisfaction contract (the finisher's prize)

The reader who reads all five books receives:

1. **The mass strike** — VOG fired; corridor sealed.
2. **Metaurus resolved** — sleep, death, or witness with honour.
3. **Titus affirmed** — the child still inside; the weapon still useful.
4. **No dangling McGuffin** — seven nodes addressed; no "Book VI" tease.
5. **Secret Level continued** — the episode's silence and strike **honoured in prose**.
