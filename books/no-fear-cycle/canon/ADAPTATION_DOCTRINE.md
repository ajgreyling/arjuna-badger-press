# ADAPTATION DOCTRINE — *The No-Fear Cycle*

> **Binding for the whole series.** Unlike the Sherlock spin-off (PD ancestor → modern transpose), this
> project **inherits a licensed setting** and **fans' collective reading** — then writes **original
> prose missions** that must pass two gates: **GW-true** and **corpus-fair.**

## The test (apply to every choice)

Not *"what would fans want on Reddit?"* but:

> **"Does this beat honour official canon first, enrich with fan corpus second, and earn its emotion
> through physical detail rather than reference?"**

If a scene only works if the reader has seen Astartes, it **still fails** unless the prose **shows** the
weight without the homework. If a scene contradicts SM2, it **fails regardless of fic votes.**

## Transposition rules (Secret Level → novel)

The episode is **eighteen minutes of silence and one strike.** The novel must **not** pad with explanation.
It must **expand time inside silence** — the Astartes lesson applied to prose:

| Episode craft | Novel craft |
|---|---|
| Cold-open Metaurus narration | Prologue: HUD + litany only |
| Wordless middle | Chapters with **pages** without dialogue |
| Fear-visions | Book IV flashback; Book V callback — **once each, earned** |
| Orbital strike | Prologue + Book V finale — **rhyme, not repeat** |
| Titus vs sorcerer | Book V only — **do not re-fight in Book I** |

## Fidelity checks (gate-style, when live)

- [ ] Episode continuity preserved for Book I?

- [ ] Fan faction served **through craft**, not reference dump?
- [ ] Grimdark honest — no false hope, no Imperium-as-villain sermon?
- [ ] Titus fearlessness = **empty child**, not action-hero bravado?
- [ ] Metaurus mentor arc advancing per SERIES_SPINE?
- [ ] VOG McGuffin node logged this book?

## What we refuse (even if fic wants it)

- Chaos Marine Titus fall (Metaurus vision stays **false** forever).
- Primarch on-page.
- Inquisition comedy sidekick.
- Xenos alliance "better than Imperium."
- Romantic partners.
- Infinite series hooks.

## Foreword contract

The Syama Pedersen foreword is **homage voice**, not impersonation for commercial endorsement. It praises
**only what the prose earns**: silence, weight, correct cruelty, the child in Titus. If later drafts fail
the Astartes law, the foreword goes **stale** — rewrite prose, not flattery.
