# MYTHOS — *Lacework* (BINDING RULES)

1. **No maternity whodunit.** Lele knows before the book's present tense begins (CC-03). Any scene
   that plays "which of them is it?" as suspense for *her* is a violation. The reader's confirmation
   comes in Part III without theatre. The mystery of this book is culpability, not biology.
2. **DNA is real and present.** The book lives in a world where a cheek swab works. No plot beat may
   depend on genetic ignorance being possible in 2026.
3. **Indict the machine, never the people** (house all-sides law). The placement economy, the talent
   industry, the myth-making machinery around Marumo — all indicted as architecture. Mrs. April is
   not a villain. The foster system's workers are not villains. Marumo is not a villain. The four are
   not villains — and they are not excused. Both truths, always, unresolved.
4. **The work is sacred.** Every chapter contains real labour shown truly enough to be used as
   instructions. No montage. No "five years later she was successful."
5. **The named are people.** Lele is never a symbol the four contest; her chapters carry equal
   interiority from Part I (as the child in the machine) onward.
6. **No Conran text.** No scene, line, or paraphrase from *Lace*; the famous summons line does not
   appear in any form.
7. **Sensitivity gates.** Teen pregnancy and birth written from Mags's interiority, never clinically
   voyeuristic; Lele's exploitation at nineteen rendered by consequence, not depiction; race and
   class in the 1996 cohort written from inside each girl, all-sides law in force. Sensitivity read
   **required** before any publishing decision.
8. **Nothing live without the gate.** This project ships nothing — no PUBLISHED entry, no serial, no
   cover — until the estate/provenance notice stands in the foreword and AJ has signed off the
   attribution posture.
