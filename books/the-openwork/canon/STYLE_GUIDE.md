# STYLE_GUIDE — *Lacework*

## Register

Close third person, one POV per chapter, five POVs total (the four + Lele). Past tense both
timelines; the year heads every chapter (the interlace must never disorient). House typeface rules
apply downstream; on the page, the discipline is **procedural warmth** — the caring eye on real
work, Conran's true register transposed, never her sentences.

## The work register (the book's signature)

Labour is written with the specificity of a manual and the pace of drama: the exact form Els files at
court by 15:00; the rooms-division margin Priya defends; the rand-per-ton arithmetic under Mags's
replanting; Thandi's commissioning call sheet; Lele's masters-recapture clause. **Rule: if a
professional in that trade would snort, rewrite it.**

## The four voices (see CHARACTERS for laws)

Thandi declarative · Els precise/wry, Afrikaans under the English · Priya smooth-then-Durban ·
Mags sparse, farm-accurate. Lele: performed warmth with an audible off-switch; Belhar Afrikaans when
real. Dialogue carries the class and language textures of the country without phonetic caricature.

## The interlace mechanics

Past chapters may never explain a 2026 hole in the same part-section where the hole is opened; the
gap must breathe at least one chapter. Every 2026 chapter ends on a hole; every past chapter closes
one — for the reader only. Characters on the page never receive narration's knowledge.

## Prohibitions

No montage. No epistolary shortcuts except the envelope slips (which are artifacts, rendered
verbatim, our invention). No brand-name glamour cataloguing — the source's era did that; ours counts
costs instead. No sex written for the reader's appetite; intimacy is written for the character's
information, and Lele's exploitation is consequence-rendered only (MYTHOS 7).
