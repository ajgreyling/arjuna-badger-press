# Adaptation Doctrine — *Lacework*

> How this book is faithful to *Lace* without being a copy of it. The governing rule for every canon
> and manuscript decision in this project.

## The line we hold

Conran's novel is under copyright and we are not licensed. Therefore the fidelity is to **arcs, craft
and engine**, and the text is **ours**. Two failures to avoid, equally:

1. **The legal/ethical failure** — reproducing her plot, scenes, characters, or sentences; or the same
   with names swapped. A find-and-replace adaptation is a derivative work we have no right to make.
2. **The craft failure** — the same failure in a different coat. A borrowed skeleton with our flesh
   stretched over it reads as taxidermy. The book has to be **alive on its own ground.**

> **An arc is a shape. A scene is an expression.** "The grown child summons the four" is an arc. A
> film star in a Manhattan hotel demanding to know her mother, in Conran's words, is a scene — and it
> is hers. Ours happens in a Cape Town suite her friend's family owns, and the demand is different
> because the DNA result in her pocket makes Conran's question obsolete. Cover the ledger; invent the
> scene. **Conran's famous summons line never appears, quoted or paraphrased.**

## What we keep (the engine)

- **The pact as destiny** — one adolescent decision governing four whole lives.
- **The shared child** — jointly funded, collectively owned, individually lost.
- **The work on the page** — Conran's true radicalism; kept at full calibre.
- **The summons** — the lost child, now powerful, convening her makers.
- **The protected father** — a secret that armed itself to guard a man's myth.
- **The friendship that holds** — the ending belongs to the five, not to the reveal.

## What we invent (everything that is *expression*)

- **Every name, character, relationship, and backstory.** Thandi, Els, Priya and Mags are not Judy,
  Kate, Maxine and Pagan; Lelethu Grace is not Lili; Kagiso Marumo is not a prince. They share
  *functions* in the engine, not lives, scenes, or lines.
- **Every scene and beat.** The plot rhymes with the source's shape the way any pact-tragedy rhymes
  with any other; the events are ours, in places we built out of a country that exists.
- **The mystery mechanism.** Maternity whodunit **retired** — replaced by the anatomy of the pact:
  who decided, who paid, who stopped, who let the trail die. A different mystery that produces the
  same moral reckoning, which is the point.
- **The substrate.** South Africa 1994–2026: the first democratic school cohort, the placement
  economy, the tabloids, the hotel and wine trades, the streaming-era talent machine.

## The test, applied to every page

*If Conran's novel did not exist, would this scene still be a real scene — its own people, its own
logic, its own surprise?* If it only makes sense as a reference, cut it and build the real one.

## Provenance, disclosed

Per the house thesis (*immersion is the craft obligation; provenance is disclosed, never hidden*): the
foreword names the debt to Conran plainly, and the house attribution notice names the work, the
author, and the estate; states that this is published **in honor of the original**, is **not endorsed
by, authorized by, or affiliated with** them, and is **not for commercial release.** We do not pretend
the engine is ours. We pretend nothing.
