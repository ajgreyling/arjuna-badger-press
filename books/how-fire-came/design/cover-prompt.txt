HOW FIRE CAME TO THE PEOPLE — ILLUSTRATION SPECIFICATION
(Prompts for ChatGPT web image generator / OpenRouter image model. One book, one style.)
Children's Library shelf — Arjuna Badger Press. Original retelling of a San / Khoi fire-origin tale.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOUSE STYLE — paste this STYLE BLOCK at the top of EVERY prompt so all 13
spreads + the cover read as one book:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STYLE: Warm, painterly children's-picture-book illustration in the tradition of
hand-painted gouache and watercolour storybooks — soft visible brushwork, gentle
edges, deeply tender, a little wondrous. FIRELIGHT PALETTE: glowing ember orange,
honey gold and warm ochre as the light, set against the deep blue-grey of the
"cold time" and the rich indigo of the African night. In almost every spread the
single coal / flame is the one point of warm light, the thing the eye finds first.
NOT digital-slick, NOT 3D-render, NOT photoreal. Picture-book art a tired parent
and a small child read together by one lamp at bedtime.

SETTING (keep consistent): the open southern-African VELD and bush — golden grass,
stony kopjes, thorn trees, a shallow river, a great old mountain, an enormous sky.
Specific, warm, dignified. NEVER generic "jungle," never caricature.

CHARACTERS (keep consistent across every image):
- THE CHILD: a small Black / San-descended African child of the veld, around six or
  seven, short natural hair, an open steady kind face, simple soft skin-and-cloth
  wrap suited to the cold time. More careful than fearless — gentle-brave. The human
  hero who carries the fire. (This is a personalised keepsake; the child's NAME is
  added on the page text, never drawn into the image — no text in any image.)
- MANTIS: a real green praying mantis, twiggy and slender and characterful, riding on
  the child's shoulder — wise and warm, NOT a cartoon bug, NOT goggle-eyed; he reads
  as a gentle little elder.
- THE FIRE-BIRD: a magnificent great bird made of ember and gold, wings like glowing
  coals, eyes of quiet warm light. Glorious and awe-inspiring, NEVER frightening,
  NEVER a villain — careful and kind, the way fire is careful.
- THE PEOPLE: the cold-time veld village — grandmothers, fathers, mothers, babies,
  wrapped in karosses and skins, warm and specific and dignified.

THE OBJECTS: one small round CLAY POT with a soft nest of dry grass inside; one
single living COAL the size of a thumb — a small bright orange ember (the heart of
the whole book); later, soft GREY ASH.

FORMAT: landscape 3:2 spread, full-bleed, NO text in the image (the verse sits beneath
on the page). The FEELING must land with the sound off, before any words are read.

The first true night of fire. The village gathered close around a glowing fire, food

Cold grey morning after. The fire is out — only a soft heap of pale grey ASH where it

CLOSING IMAGE. Under a huge, deep, star-filled African night, the child gently banks
