# Chapter 2 — The Thornfield

---

*This chapter is deliberately empty.*

This is the Zulu youth's chapter — the boy of seventeen made into a weapon in the king's regiments,
whose fear and pride and cost belong here, beside the Voortrekker girl's and the English boy's. In the
finished book his voice will carry equal weight with theirs; the whole moral claim of *Brave and
Scared* depends on it.

It is empty here because it is **not yet written, and will not be written by me alone.** To imagine my
way into a Zulu boy's heart and publish it as truth would be the precise thing this book stands against
— the outsider speaking *for* the other. His chapter is being **co-created with a Zulu reader and
co-author.** Until that work is done, this seat stays open, in plain sight, rather than filled with a
guess.

If you can help write it true — see the note at the front of this book, and the book's page at the
press. The empty seat is an invitation.
