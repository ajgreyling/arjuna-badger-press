# Idrafti Evulekileyo — Nceda Funda Oku Kuqala

*Brave and Scared* ipapashwa apha **njengeidrafti evulekileyo, engagqitywanga**, ngabom.

Le yinoveli emalunga nonyaka ojikeleze iDabi lase-Blood River — 1838 — ebalisa ngamacala amathathu 
ngaxeshanye: intombazana yomtrekker, umfana ongumZulu, nomfana ongowesiNgesi, bonke baneminyaka emalunga 
neshumi elinesixhenxe, bonke besoyika, akukho namnye kubo ongumgxagxa ababexelelwe ukuba mabalindele. 
Isizathu sayo sokubakho kukuba iyala ukuthatha icala. Umphazamisi akasoze abe ngomnye wabantwana. 
Umphazamisi ngumatshini — uloyiko olusetyenziswa njengesikhali ngamandla — oguqula abantwana abasoyikayo 
babe ziintshaba, kumacala onke, phantsi kweflegi nganye.

Incwadi eyenza elo bango inoxanduva alunakukulubaleka: **onke amazwi amathathu kufuneka abe yinyani 
ngokulinganayo, kwaye akukho lizwi linokuba yintelekelelo yombhali yobomi bangaphakathi bomnye umntu.** 
NdingumAfrikaner. Intombazana yomtrekker nembali ebalisayo yeyam ukuyibhala. Umfana ongowesiNgesi 
usekwe kwingxelo ebhaliweyo kwaye uyidrafti esebenzayo. Kodwa ubungaphakathi bomfana ongumZulu — 
ukuvakalelwa kukwenziwa isikhali njengomntwana, ikratshi lakhe noloyiko nokuva ukuba ungowakhe neendleko — 
**ayiyoyam ukuyiqamba ndedwa.** Ukuyibhala ndedwa kuya kuba yinye kanye intshukumo yobukoloniyali le ncwadi 
ekhoyo ukuyala: abantu abanye bethetha *egameni* labanye beze babize oko yinyani.

Ngoko ke kule drafti, **elo lizwi lishiywe livulekile ngabom.** Apho isahluko sakhe besimele khona, uya 
kufumana isihlalo esingenamntu nengcaciso, hayi isenzo somthethi-ngelizwi. Iya kubhalwa — kodwa iya 
kubhalwa *kunye* nomfundi ongumZulu nomdali odibanayo, ayizukuhanjiswa phantsi. Loo msebenzi uyaqhubeka.

**Nabu ke ungena phi wena.** Ukuba ungumfundi ongumZulu, ungumbhali-mbali, uyinzala yalo naliphi na icala 
leli bali, okanye nje umntu onokundixelela apho le drafti ingavakali inyanisekile — ndiyakumema ungene. 
Ndixelele into endiyenze ngendlela embi. Ndixelele into irekhodi eyilibalayo. Ndincede ndenze isihlalo 
esingenamntu sibe yinyani. Kukho indlela yokufikelela kushicilelo kwiphepha lencwadi, kwaye kuya kubakho 
umbulelo obizwa ngegama kuzo zonke izandla eziyibumbayo.

Into oyibambileyo ngu-**Movement I** — ukuhamba, ngaphambi kokuqhekeka. Sisiqalo sencwadi, 
esipapashwe elubala ukuze abantu emalunga nabo bancede ukuyigqibezela.

Yifunde njengento eyiyo: ikhaliphile kwaye iyoyika, kwaye ayikagqitywa.

— *Andries J. Greyling*, Arjuna Badger Press

# Isahluko 0 — Isakhelo: Okugcinwe Yimbali

*Le yimbali echazwayo enyanisekileyo [F]. Ngumbono obanzi abantwana abangakwaziyo ukuwubona. Ichaza okugcinwe yimbali — nalapho imbali iphambuka khona, ibeka iindlela eziphambukileyo ecaleni komnye kwaye iyala isigwebo, ngokwemithetho yeNyaniso.*

---

Nantsi into enokuqinisekiswa.

Kwiminyaka emva ko-1834, amawaka abantu ababezibiza ngokuba ngamaAfrikaner — amafama, ubukhulu becala, bezithili zasempuma zeKoloni yaseKapa, abegazi lamaDatshi namaHuguenot namaJamani kwaye beneminyaka elikhulu elinamashumi amahlanu bawuMzantsi Afrika ngokuzalwa — balayisha konke ababenazo kwiinqwelo ezitsalwa ziinkabi baqhuba emantla, bemka kuphela kurhulumente ababemazi. Babenezizathu, kwaye izizathu zixhomekeke kumntu obuzayo, neyona nto yokuqala ekufuneka uyiqonde ngale nto yonke: ukuba phantse akukho nto inengxelo enye kuphela eyinyani, nokuba ukuba neengxelo ezimbini akusileleko lwembali kodwa yeyona nyaniso iqulethwe yimbali.

I-Crown yaseBritani yayithathe iKapa kumaDatshi ngokusisigxina ngo-1806, yayilawula ngendlela ubukhosi obulawula ngayo indawo ekude abangayithandiyo — ngemiyalelo evela eLondon, ngolwimi amafama ayengaluthethiyo, ngeenkundla ezazingasoloko zikholelwa umDatshi kunomNgesi okanye umntu omnyama kunomnye. Ngo-1834 i-Crown yaphelisa ubukhoboka kulo lonke ubukhosi, kwaye amafama ayenamakhoboka ahlawulwa ngamaphepha anokukhutshwa kuphela eLondon, nto leyo kumfama waseWinterberg yayifana nokungahlawulwa kwaphela. Babezioyisile iimfazwe kumda wasempuma baxelelwa ukuba ukoyiswa kwabo yayilityala labo. Baziva, ngendlela ethile yabantu abayekileyo ukuviwa, ukuba balawulwa ngabangengubo ababhajabhajela ngabo.

Ngoko ke bemka. *Siyahamba,* wabhala omnye wabo, *siya kwilizwe apho siza kuzilawula khona.* Bayibiza kamva i-Great Trek, bayenza into engcwele, bakha ilitye lenkcubeko labafazi bayo, kwaye nantso ingxaki kumntu ozama ukuyibona kakuhle — ngokuba into eyenziwe ingcwele iye yenziwa ilula, kwaye le yayingengolula. Babengengabo abahambi bangcwele begoduka kwilizwe elingenanto. Ilizwe lalingenabantu. Lalingazange libe lingenamntu nakuba kanye.

Emntla-mpuma, ngaphaya kweDrakensberg, kwilizwe eliluhlaza elinamanzi elehla lisiya elwandle olufudumeleyo, lalikho ubukumkani bamaZulu. Lalimalunga neminyaka engamashumi amahlanu njengobukumkani obunye kwaye yayingamandla amakhulu kakhulu kwisiqingatha esisemazantsi selizwekazi — urhulumente owakhelwe, njengarha rhulumente, ngengqondo nangegazi, enomkhosi omiyo owawulungiselelwe njengamabutho, i-*amabutho*, eyayinokubeka amawaka amadoda aqeqeshiweyo ebaleni ngokukhawuleza kunomkhosi waseYurophu kweli lizwekazi. Ukumkani wayo ngo-1837 yayinguDingane, owafika etroneni ngokubulala umntakwabo uShaka, nowayebukele iinqwelo ziqokelelana kwimida yakhe yasentshona ngendlela nakuphi na ukumkani abukele ngayo abangengubo abaxhobileyo befika emngceleni wakhe: njengombuzo owawuza kufuneka uphendulwe, uphendulwe nzima.

Bobabini aba bantu baboyika. Le yinto yesibini ekufuneka uyiqonde, kwaye imbali iyayixhasa kuzo zonke iinkalo. Abatrekki baboyika amandla abangenakulinganisa nabangawaqondiyo. Ukumkani wayesoyika into wayeyiqonda kakuhle kakhulu, ngokuba wayevile okwenzeka kwezinye iindawo xa kufika iinqwelo zingayeki ukufika — wayevile ngeKapa ngokwalo, nokuba iminyaka engamashumi amathandathu yokufika yayenze ntoni kubantu ababekhona kuqala. Uloyiko alusosizathu sokwenza kwabo bobabini. Kodwa yinjini phantsi kwayo yonke, kwaye imbali eshiya uloyiko ngaphandle ayikuxeleli inyaniso; ikuthengisela icala.

Kwaye emngceleni wayo yonke, phantse incinci kakhulu ukubonwa, kwakukho ubukho besithathu: i-Crown ababebaleka kuyo abatrekki, ngohlobo lwesixeko esincinci sabazingeli nabarhwebi abangamaNgesi phantsi kwitheku ababeyibiza ngokuba yiPort Natal, behleli ngokunyamezelwa kukumkani, berhweba ngendlovu, betshata nabafazi bamaZulu, betyikitya amagama abo kuyo nayiphi na iphepha elibonakala ngathi liza kubagcina bephila ngelo xesha — ngemva kwabo, kude ngaphaya kolwandle, ubukhosi obukhulu obake benziwa lihlabathi, obungekabi nandaba nazo zonke ezi zinto nobuya, ngokuhamba kwexesha, bunomdla omkhulu, kwaye buze nobuhlobo beminyaka engamashumi amathandathu kuhlobo lokucingo nohlobo lwenkampu noDingane wayengenakulucinga.

NgoFebruwari ka-1838, inkokeli yabatrekki egama linguPiet Retief yakhwela yaya kwindawo enkulu yokumkani neqela lamadoda angama-70 ukuya kuthabatha, wayekholelwa, isivumelwano esityikityiweyo somhlaba. Okwenzeka apho yindlela yayo yonke into elandelayo, kwaye kanye luhlobo lomcimbi le ncwadi ekhoyo ukuwubamba ngokunyanisekileyo, ngokuba kukho iingxelo ezimbini kwaye zombini zinobungqina kwaye umsantsa phakathi kwazo kulapho intlungu yonke ihlala khona. Enye ingxelo — ingxelo yabatrekki, ebhalwe kwilitye — ithi ubuqhophololo: ukuba ukumkani wabazisa wabakhulula izixhobo waza ke, ngelizwi, wathi mababanjwe barhuqelwe endulini yokubulala babethwe ngamazembe, abafazi nabasebenzi nomntwana kamva, ngegazi elibandayo, kungekho sizathu ngaphandle kokungathembeki kokumkani. Enye ingxelo ithi into enzima ukuyibhala: ukuba ukumkani obukele abangengubo abaxhobileyo bethatha umhlaba naphi na apho baya khona, osele ebabambe aba bangengubo ngokuthile okwakubonakala kuye njengobuxoki okanye ubusela beenkomo, wenza okwenziwa ngukumkani oyoyikayo kubuthunywa obuxhobileyo abugqibe ukuba lungumphambili wohlaselo — wabetha kuqala, wabetha kwingcambu, ngokuba wayekholelwa, mhlawumbi ngokulungileyo, ukuba akenzanga njalo, ubukumkani bakhe buya kuhamba ngendlela yaseKapa.

Imbali ayikuvumeli ukuba ukhethe. Imbali ikuvumela kuphela ukuba ume emsantsa.

Into engaphikiswa yinto eyalandela. I-impi yaya entshona kwiiveki ezalandela yawela kwiinkampu zabatrekki ezazisasazeke kwimilambo ngaphantsi kweentaba — kwindawo abatrekki abaya kuyibiza kamva ngokuba yiWeenen, eligama lamaDatshi elithi *ukulila* — yabulala abantu abangamakhulu: amadoda, nabafazi ababenokudubula njengamadoda, nabantwana abaninzi nabasebenzi ababengenawo. Yayiyintlekele kwaye yayicelwe ukuba ibe njalo. Yenza into eyenziwa ziintlekele: yathatha uhambo yayenza imfazwe, yathatha intlungu yayenza isizathu, yaqinisa into kubaphilayo eyayiza kuba nempendulo, kwiinyanga ezilishumi kamva, emlanjeni.

Loo mlambo wawuqukuqela, ngaphambi komhla we-16 kaDisemba 1838, phantsi kwegama lamaZulu: iNcome. Emva kwaloo mini abatrekki bayibiza ngokuba yiBloedrivier — uMlambo weGazi — kwaye igama lisigwebo, yiyo loo nto le ncwadi ingayi kulisebenzisa njengelayo, ngokuba incwadi ayikho kwicala. Ngaloo mini ikhomando yabatrekki abangaphantsi kwamakhulu amahlanu, emi ngemva komngcelelo weenqwelo ezibotshwe kunye kolwimi lomhlaba phakathi komlambo nedonga elinzulu, yadibana nokutshaja komkhosi wamaZulu omkhulu ngokuphindwe amatyeli, yawophula, iyure neyure, ngokudubula ngesixa kumadoda ayenezixhobo zomzimba namakhonto kuphela. Ngokuhlwa ikhomando yayiphulukene namadoda amathathu enzakele akukho namnye owafayo. I-impi yayiphulukene, ngokubala kwekhomando, mhlawumbi amawaka amathathu. Umlambo waba bomvu. Abatrekki bathi uThixo wabanika uloyiso ngempendulo yesifungo, bakha ukholo lwabo phezu koko kude kube yinkulungwane enesiqingatha. AmaZulu awukhumbula njengosuku isizukulwana samadoda ayo amancinci sachithwa kukudubula, kwaye akha usizi phezu koko olungenaso isikhumbulo.

Seso sisakhelo. Silungisiwe, sibhaliwe, nalapho siphambuka khona le ncwadi iya kukubonisa zombini iindlela eziphambukileyo kwaye ikuthembe ukuba uya kuthwala ubunzima bazo.

Kodwa isakhelo asiyobali, nobalo lwabafileyo asibabo abafileyo. Ngaphakathi kweso sakhelo sasimiyo kwakukho abantu, uninzi lwabo lwalusebancinci, uninzi lwabo loyika ngendlela ethile, epheleleyo enokoyikwa ngabantu abancinci kuphela — kubo ukuphela kwehlabathi akusisentetho kodwa ziindaba zenyaniso zentsasa. Le ncwadi imalunga nabathathu kubo. Ayiwazi amagama abo okwenene, ngokuba imbali yayicinga abantu abancinci abafanelwe kukubizwa ngamagama ngaphandle kokuba bekhokela okanye bafa ngendlela ephawulekayo. Ngoko ke ibanike amagama, nobomi, noloyiko ababenalo ngokuqinisekileyo, ibabeka phantsi kwisakhelo esinyanisekileyo ukubona into eshiywa ngubalo lwabafileyo.

Babeneminyaka efanayo. Babunjwe ngoomatshini abathathu abohlukeneyo. Akukho namnye kubo, xa ekugqibeleni umbona, oya kuba lutshaba owawuthenjisiwe.

Qala ngentombazana.

# Isahluko 1 — Ukuhamba

Ihashe kwafuneka lidutyulwe phambi kwesidlo sasekuseni, baye balinika uMarretjie ngoba wayenguye omntu othembekileyo ukuba angakwenza oko ngokuphumelela kwangoko.

Wayengakufuni oko. Yayiyinto engazange ithethwe ngaphandle kodwa wonke umntu wayeyiqonda: ukufuna kwakungenanto yakwenza nako, ngendlela ukufuna kwakungenanto yakwenza nezinto ezininzi ezifunwa kuwe ngulo hambo. Ihashe elingwevu laye phantsi ebusuku lomlenze walo ugobe ngendlela engafanelekanga emngxunyeni wobhubhane awayengenakho ukuwubona ebumnyameni, lele ngoku ekukhanyeni okungwevu-luhlaza phambi kokuba kuphume ilanga intamo yalo isoluleke emhlabeni obandayo iliso lalo lijikile limhlophe kuye, kwaye belilihashe elesibini elona lihle likatata wakhe iminyaka emithandathu kwaye lalimthwele umntakwabo uHendrik kwinkolo yakhe, kwaye wayelithanda, kwaye kwafuneka life, kwaye wayeneminyaka elishumi elinesixhenxe.

"Uzakwenza kakuhle," watsho utata wakhe. Yayingengombuzo kwaye yayingenabukrwada. Wayeyilayishe ngokwakhe i-*roer* wayinika yena sele imbumbulu ibekiwe, wabeka isandla sakhe emva kwentamo yakhe, kancinci, eyona nto isondele kububele obo busasa bungavuma, waza wahamba ukuya kujonga iinkabi kuba iinkabi zazo zazibagcina bonke bephila nehashe elingakwaziyo ukuma lalingekho. Wayeyiqonda le nkqubo. Wayesenza inkqubo yalo hambo okoko wayeneminyaka elishumi elinanye baphuma kwiTarka nayo yonke into ababenayo ikhwaza emva kweenkabi ezilishumi elinesithandathu unina ecula indumiso ukuze abantwana abancinci bangakhali. *Abadubuli abangaphumeleliyo balamba.* Umakhulu wakhe wayeyitsho ngohlobo abanye oomakhulu abathetha ngobabalo. Intombazana ekwaziyo ukudubula yayitya, nentombazana ekwaziyo ukudubula yayenza abanye batye, ngoko ke intombazana yayifunda ukudubula ngendlela eyayifunda ngayo ukuthandaza nokuphanda nokubeleka ithole nemikhono yayo isongiwe ukuya emagxeni, kuba i-veld yayingenandaba nokuba yintombazana, nengonyama yayingenandaba, nokusa okuzisa i-impi — nangona kwakungekabikho mpi ethe yafika, ngoko, kwilizwe eliphakamileyo elicocekileyo phambi kweentaba — ukusa kwakungeyikhathalele nako.

Waguqa ecaleni kwentloko yehashe elingwevu. Akazange azivumele ukukhawuleza, kuba ukukhawuleza yayiyindlela oyeka ngayo, ukuyeka yayiyindlela oyenza ngayo into ecocekileyo ibe yinto ekhohlakeleyo. Wabeka isandla sakhe esicaba kwithambo elide lobuso balo weva ukufudumala okusaphuma kulo, umphefumlo ungena uphuma ngokukhawuleza kunzima empomlweni omkhulu othambileyo, walisebenza igama lalo kanye, ngezantsi, igama eliyimfihlo eligeza awalithiyayo eneminyaka elithoba wangaze alixelele mntu, nendlebe yajika isiza kwilizwi lakhe. Wabeka umlomo waphi utata wakhe wayemfundisile khona, kumnqamlezo womgca ocingelelweyo ukusuka endlebeni ukuya kwiliso elichaseneyo, waphefumlela ngaphandle ngendlela ophefumlela ngayo ngaphandle ukuze uthule, walidubula, kwaye kwaba kakuhle, isandi sahamba sacaba sivela kwi-veld sabuya singavela ndawo, kuba kwakungekho nto apha ngaphandle ongavela kuyo, ingca kuphela ukuya kumda oyingqukuva wehlabathi.

Wayesele elila phambi kokuba umsindo ubuyayo ufe. Wazivumela, kanye ixesha elithathe ukuma, kuba wayekufundile nako oko — ukuba ungaba nemvakalelo okanye unokuyilwa nemvakalelo kodwa awunakwenza zombini uze ulayishe kwakhona, ukulayisha kwakhona yayiyinto ekugcina uphila, ngoko waba nemvakalelo ngokukhawuleza ngokupheleleyo waza wayibeka phantsi waphakamisa uphondo lwembumbulu. Ngethuba uHendrik esiza ngesantya, eneminyaka elishumi elinesine emhlophe ebusweni, ekuvile ukudubula esoyika ukuba ngabantu, wayesele ebuye ebuzweni i-*roer* ivuliwe phezu kwengalo yakhe wayekwazi ukuthi, ngelizwi elixengaxenga kancinci kuphela, "Yayiyihashe elingwevu. Umlenze. Yiya uncede uBawo nge-disselboom, umthi uphukile kwakhona," waza wabukela uloyiko luphuma kuye into embi nethuleyo ingene endaweni yalo, eyayikukuqonda, kufika kuye njengoko kwakufikile kuye, ukuba le yayiyindlela eyayiza kuba njalo ngoku. Ukuba izinto ozithandayo ziza kulala ebumnyameni zize zidutyulwe phambi kwesidlo sasekuseni, nokuba uya kuba nguwe oza kukwenza oko, nokuba usuku luya kuqhubeka emva koko lufune umsebenzi walo kuwe kungakhathaliseki.

Le yayiyinxalenye amadoda aya kuyibhala phantsi kamva aya kuyishiya ngaphandle. Babeza kubhala uhambo namadabi namagama eenkokeli nombhalo wezibhambathiso. Babeza kubaza abafazi ematsheni, bade bexhwalekile bebamangalisa, isikhumbuzo onokuhamba usijikeleze. Into ababengayi kuyifaka, kuba babengakwazi njani nangokuba yayingancomi mntu, yayiyindlela uMarretjie Pretorius awayeme ngayo engceni emanzi ngo-5:15 kusasa enehashe lakhe elithandwayo elifiileyo elipholileyo ezinyaweni zakhe nobomi bomntakwabo omncinci luxanduva lwakhe nosuku lonke olungenakwenzeka phambi kwakhe, weva — phantsi kwentlungu, phantsi kobuchule, phantsi kwazo zonke izinto uhambo olwamenzayo — umsonto omnye ocacileyo wento wayengasoze ayithethe ngaphandle waza ngobunzima anegama layo, eyayikukuba *wayesoyika lonke ixesha ngoku,* kwaye ebesoyika iminyaka emibini, nokuba ukusoyika kwakuphelelwe kukuvakalelwa yinto eyayiza kuphela.

---

Babesiya emantla kuba abantu abadala babesoyika, nangona abantu abadala babeyibiza ngezinye izinto.

Babeyibiza intando kaThixo, babeyibiza ukhangelo lomhlaba apho indoda inokuba yinkosi yayo, utata wakhe wayeyibiza, ngobusuku obubi, *ukubalekela iNgesi* — eyitsho ngendlela ongatsho ngayo igama lesifo, iNgesi, urhulumente we-smous ezantsi kwiKoloni owakhulula amakhoboka engabahlawulanga, owayeqhuba iinkundla zawo ngolwimi wayengakwaziyo ukululandela wanika isigwebo kwi-rooinek rhoqo, owaphulukana nefama yomzala wakhe ngemfazwe ne-*Xhosa* emfazweni waza wamxelela ukuba imfazwe yayilityala lakhe ngokuba elapho wayekhoyo. UMarretjie wayemncinci kwixesha elininzi lelo wayelikhumbula ngendlela okhumbula ngayo imozulu: uxinzelelo olude endlwini, utata wakhe ebuya kwi-*drostdy* enomqhele esandleni sakhe nomhlathi wakhe ubekiwe, igama elithi *Engelse* lithethwa ngezantsi emva kokuba abantwana bekufanele belele, ngendlela othetha ngayo into ekwintungo zophahla.

Wayengazithandi iNgesi ngendlela utata wakhe ayezithanda ngayo, kuba wayezange ahlangane nenye, kwaye awukwazi ukuthiya imozulu ngokufanelekileyo. Kodwa wayefundile ukuba ukuzoyika yayiyinyani enkulu yehlabathi labantu abadala, into ephantsi kwazo zonke ezinye izinto, nokuba uhambo ngokwalo — lo mzi ukhwazayo phezu kwamavili, iinqwelo zolulwe usuku olunesiqingatha sohambo kwindlela yeenqwelo ababeyinqumla kwilizwe kungekho nqwelo eyake yanqumla — yayiluloyiko oluneenkabi phambi kwalo. Babengenzi, nokuba iindumiso zithini, bekhwela besiya kwinto. Babebaleka besuka. Umhlaba ongaphambili wawulintlebendwane. Uloyiko olungasemva yayikuphela kwento eqinileyo, utata wakhe wayilayishe yonke intsapho kuyo wayiqhubela emantla kwilizwe wayengalaziyo, apho kwakukho abantu wayengazange ababone, belawulwa ngukumkani ogama lakhe amadoda alithethayo ngononophelo, ngendlela othetha ngayo igama lento ekwintungo zophahla.

Wayecinga ngako oko ngaphezu kokuba evuma. Wayecinga ngako ngelixa ekhwele — kwaye wayekhwela kakuhle, ngcono kunoHendrik, phantse njengamadoda, ehleli kwi-bay entsha utata wakhe wayemnike yona ukuthabatha indawo yehashe elingwevu ne-*roer* yakhe ijingiswe namehlo akhe esenza ngaphandle kokuxelelwa ukujonga okuqhubekayo okucothayo kwe-veld owayekufunda okanye ufe, ukufunda ingca ukubona intshukumo, imigca ye-donga, i-kopje enokufihla amadoda alikhulu okanye umhlambi we-eland okanye akukho nto konke konke. Amadoda ayemjongela phantsi. Wayeyazi ukuba bayenza njalo waza wabavumela, kuba kwakukho ukusetyenziswa kuko: umntu ojongwe phantsi ubona ngakumbi, ubuzwa imibuzo embalwa, angacinga iingcinga zakhe emva kobuso abanye abagqibe ukuba bulula. Umalume wakhe uGerrit wayembiza *ons skerpskutter*, umdubuli wethu obukhali, wayethetha njengohlobo loduko olunikwa inja ekrelekrele, waza wancuma akatsho nto wamdubula kakhulu kwi-*skiet-kompetisie* elandelayo ngeentaka ezimbini ezicacileyo waza wambukela egqiba ukuba yayilithamsanqa.

Into wayeyicinga, emva kobuso obulula, ekhwela emantla kwintlebendwane, yayile: ukuba wonke umntu wayemxelele ukuba makoyike bani, nokuba wonke umntu wayeqinisekile kakhulu, nokuba ukuqiniseka ngokwako kwakuqalile, kutshanje, ukumoyikisa ngaphezu kwento ababeqiniseke ngayo. Amadoda athetha ngamaZulu ngendlela athetha ngayo ngeNgesi — imozulu, ubumnyama, into ekwintungo zophahla — kwaye wayengazange ahlangane nenye yazo nayo, ngokwenene, ukuthetha nayo, waza waqaphela ukuba ukungahlangani akwenzanga mntu uqiniseke kancinci. Kwabenza baqiniseka *ngakumbi*. Ukuqonda into kancinci, kwakulula ukuqiniseka ngayo, kwaye lonke ihlabathi labantu abadala lalibonakala liqhuba kanye ngaloo mafutha, kwaye babewatshisa ngoku ukuqhuba iwaka leenqwelo kwingaziwayo.

Wayengenalo amagama ayo njengengcamango. Wayenayo njengemvakalelo, ngendlela wayenayo yonke into ebalulekileyo — ngokukhawuleza nangokupheleleyo, phantsi kobuchule. Kwakuya kuba yiminyaka, ukuba wayephila iminyaka, phambi kokuba akwazi ukuthi: *Ndicinga ukuqiniseka yinto eyingozi. Ndicinga sikulungele kakhulu ukubulala abantu esibale ngononophelo ukungababoni.* Eneminyaka elishumi elinesixhenxe wayenongesakhululeki kuphela kwayo, into encinci engalunganga wayeyithwele ngendlela othwala ngayo ilitye esihlatyini sakho ongekayeki ukuyikhupha, ekhwela emantla phantsi kwesibhakabhaka esiqhubeka ngonaphakade, esiya kwilizwe likakumkani, ne-*roer* yobomi bomntakwabo omncinci ijingiswe ecaleni kwesakhe i-*roer*, ekhaliphile kuba kwafuneka abe njalo nesoyika kuba wayengeyosidenge — zombini ngaxeshanye, lonke ixesha, eyayithetha nje, wayeqalisa ukuqonda, into eyayiza kuthetha ukuphila kwiminyaka eyayiza.

Iintaba zaphuma kwingqumbo ngosuku lwesithathu: iDrakensberg, luhlaza nenkulu namazinyo phezulu ngathi into eyalumayo. Ngaphaya kwazo kwakukulizwe likakumkani, nokuluhlaza, nolwandle olufudumeleyo amadoda athetha ngalo, nomhlaba owawuza kuba ngowabo kuba uThixo nentlebendwane yatsho njalo.

# Isituku sesi-3 — Itheku

UGeorge Biggar wayeneminyaka elishumi elinesibhozo kwaye ekude kakhulu nayo nantoni na, kwaye into angakwazanga ukuyiqhela, emva kweminyaka emibini ekupheleni komhlaba, yindlela ekuphela komhlaba okungakhathali ngayo ukuba ungubani.

Ethekwini ababebiza ngokuba yiPort Natal kwakungekho cawa yokuqinisekiswa kwaye kungekho mantyi wokoyika kwaye kungekho Ngiltani yokuxoxwa ngaphandle kwemiphefumlo embalwa eyayiyithwele entloko yazo ngathi yingqekembe esele igudile epokothweni — abazingeli, ubukhulu becala, kunye nomrhwebi okanye ababini, kunye noyise, owayebazise bonke apha ukuza kwenza imali ngeendlovu kwaye okwangoku wenze endaweni yoko igama, umkhuhlane, kunye nezihlobo ezininzi phakathi kwamaZulu, into uGeorge ayeqonde ukuba yiyo kuphela imali ekuphela komhlaba ekuyihloniphayo ngokwenene. Awunakutya iflegi apha. Wayebukele amadoda ezama. Umhlaba wawuthatha ubuntu bakho basesiNgesi kunye nokuqiniseka kwakho kwesiNgesi ngendlela amaza olwandle athatha ngayo ilitye eliphosweyo, ngaphandle kwenkcazo, wakunika kanye le nto ungayithwala uyidubule uyithengise, kwaye akukho nto ingenye, kwaye uGeorge — owayengumntwana ohlwempuzekileyo eNgiltani kwaye engowona uhlwempuzekile ngakumbi apha — wagqiba kwelokuba akakundixhalabisi kangako njengoko bekufanele ukuba andixhalabise. Kwakukho inyaniso kwindawo eyayingayi kuzenza ngathi iyakuthanda.

Wayengeyona ijoni. Uyise wayeseke umkhosi omncinci kunyaka ophelileyo, amaVolontiya asePort Natal, amadoda alishumi elinambini anemipu yokuzingela kunye neembono ezinkulu, kwaye yachitheka ngaphakathi kwexesha, into uGeorge acinga ukuba yeyona nto yesiNgesi eyenzekileyo oko bafika: *umbono* womkhosi, iyunifom yombono, kwaye ke imva kwemini yaseMzantsi Afrika iyinyibilikisa nje kuba kwakungekho nto apha yokuze ibe ngumkhosi *ochaseneyo* nayo apho umkhosi unokuba luncedo. Awona magunya okwenene etheku yayiyinkosi engaphakathi, imbalelwano yayo ababehleli kuyo, kunye nemozulu, kunye nemikhuhlane ecothayo evela echweba. Akukho nanye kwezo amadoda alishumi elinambini anemipu neflegi ayethetha nto konke konke.

Into uGeorge ayiqondayo — kwaye wayeyiqonda ngendlela le ntombazana imayile ezimakhulu ezimbini ukuya emazantsi-ntshona eyayiqonda ngayo ilitye-esihlatyini, njengemvakalelo phantsi kobuchule kunokuba ibe ngumbono ebenokuwuthetha — yayikukuba wayengekho kwibali lomntu. Kwaye uGeorge wema emaphethelweni ayo yonke le nto, inkwenkwe yesiNgesi elunxwemeni ekupheleni kweAfrika, ephethe iNgiltani engekho nkulu kunengqekembe, eluncedo kumatshini kanye ngoko anokukuthwala akudubule — okwakulixabiso elifanayo umatshini awalibeka kwintombazana yamaBhulu kunye nenkwenkwe yamaZulu elingana nayo ngobudala, nangona bonke bobathathu bengekazi ukuba bayabelana ngalo.
