# I-Draft Elivulekile — Ngicela Ufunde Lokhu Kuqala

*Amadlozi Anesibindi Nanesaba* lishicilelwa lapha **njenge-draft elivulekile, elingakapheli**, ngenhloso.

Le yinoveli emayelana nonyaka owandulela iMpi yaseNcome — 1838 — exoxwa ngamacala amathathu ngesikhathi sinye: intombazane yamaVoortrekker, insizwa yamaZulu, nomfana wamaNgisi, bonke baneminyaka engaba yi-17, bonke besaba, akekho kubo isilo ababetshelwa ukuthi bazosibhekana naso. Isizathu sokuba khona kwayo ukuthi iyenqaba ukukhetha icala. Isigelekeqe asisoze sibe ngomunye wabantwana. Isigelekeqe yimishini — ukwesaba okusetshenziswa njengesikhali ngamandla — ephendula abantwana abesabayo babe yizitha, kuwo wonke amacala, ngaphansi kwawo wonke amafulegi.

Incwadi eshilo lokho inomsebenzi engeke iwubalekele: **wonke amazwi amathathu kumele abe yiqiniso ngokulinganayo, futhi akukho zwi elingaba ngumcabango wombhali wokuphila kwangaphakathi komunye umuntu.** NgiyiBhunu. Intombazane yamaVoortrekker nomlando oxoxwayo kungokwami ukubhala. Umfana wamaNgisi usekelwe emibhalweni ebhaliwe futhi uyi-draft esebenzayo. Kodwa okungaphakathi kwensizwa yamaZulu — ukuthi kwazwakala kanjani ukwenziwa isikhali usengumfana, ukuziqhenya kwakhe nokwesaba nokuzwana nomphakathi nezindleko — **akusikho okwami ukuzakhela ngedwa.** Ukukubhala ngedwa kungaba yilowo mnyakazo wamakoloni le ncwadi ekhona ukuwenqaba: abantu abathile bekhuluma *egameni* labanye bese bekubiza iqiniso.

Ngakho-ke kule draft, **lelo zwi lishiywe livulekile ngenhloso.** Lapho isahluko sakhe kumele sime khona, uzothola isihlalo esingenalutho nencazelo, hhayi umdlalo womkhulumi ngelimu. Sizobhalwa — kodwa sizobhalwa *kanye* nomfundi ongumZulu nomakhi-ndawonye, hhayi kudluliswa phansi. Lo msebenzi uyaqhubeka.

**Yilapho-ke ungena khona.** Uma ungumfundi ongumZulu, umlando, inzalo yanoma yiliphi icala lale ndaba, noma umuntu nje ongangitshela ukuthi le draft iyiphi lapho ingakhali khona kahle — ngiyakumema ungene. Ngitshele ukuthi yini engiyenze kabi. Ngitshele ukuthi yini umbhalo awayikhohlwa. Ngisize ngenze isihlalo esingenalutho sibe yiqiniso. Kukhona indlela yokufinyelela umshicileli ekhasini lencwadi, futhi kuzoba khona ukubongwa okuqanjiwe kwesandla ngasinye esizololonga.

Lokhu okuphethe ngu-**Movement I** — ukuhamba, ngaphambi kokuhlukana. Yisiqalo sencwadi, sishicilelwe obala ukuze abantu okubhalwe ngabo bakwazi ukusiza ukuyiqeda.

Yifunde njengalokhu eyikho: inesibindi futhi iyesaba, futhi ayikaqedwa.

— *Andries J. Greyling*, Arjuna Badger Press

# Isigaba 0 — Uhlaka: Okubhaliwe

*Lona uhlaka lwamaqiniso oxoxwayo [F]. Umbono obanzi abantwana abangeke bawubone. Uxoxa okubhaliwe — futhi lapho umbhalo uhlukana khona, ubeka izindlela zombili ndawonye futhi wenqaba ukwahlulela, njengomthetho Wamaqiniso.*

---

Nansi into engaqinisekiswa.

Eminyakeni ngemuva kuka-1834, abantu abayizinkulungwane ezimbalwa ababezibiza ngokuthi ama-Afrikaners — abalimi, iningi labo, bezifunda zasempumalanga zeKoloni yaseKapa, amaDashi namaHuguenot namaJalimane ngegazi kodwa beneminyaka eyikhulu namashumi amahlanu bezalelwe eNingizimu Afrika — balayisha konke ababenakho ezinqoleni ezidonswa yizinkabi bahamba baya enyakatho, besuka kuhulumeni abake bazi kuphela. Babenezizathu, futhi izizathu zazincike kuwena ubuzwa ubani, okuyinto yokuqala okumele uyiqonde ngakho konke lokhu: cishe akukho lutho kulokhu olunomlando owodwa oqiniso, futhi ukuba nemilando emibili akusikona ukwehluleka kombhalo kodwa yiqiniso eliqiniso kakhulu eliqukethwe umbhalo.

UMbuso waseBrithani wawuthathe iKapa kumaDashi unomphela ngo-1806, wawubusa ngendlela umbuso obusa ngayo indawo ekude engayithandi — ngemiyalo evela eLondon, ngolimi abalimi abangazanga balukhulume, ngezinkantolo ezazingahlali zikholwa umDashi kunesiNgisi noma umuntu omnyama kunabo bobabili. Ngo-1834 uMbuso waqeda ubugqila embusweni wonke, abalimi ababenamagqila bakhokhelwa ngamaphepha okwakufanele akhishwe imali eLondon kuphela, okwakufana nokungakhokhelwa nhlobo kumuntu waseWinterberg. Babehlulwe ezimpini emngceleni wasempumalanga base betshelwa ukuthi ukuhlulwa kwakungamaphutha abo. Bazizwa, ngendlela ethile yabantu abaseyekile ukulalelwa, ukuthi babuswa abangazi ababadelela.

Ngakho-ke bahamba. *Siyahamba,* kubhala omunye wabo, *siya ezweni lapho esizozibusa khona.* Kamuva babiza lokhu ngokuthi i-Great Trek, bakwenza into engcwele, bakha itshe lesikhumbuzo labesifazane balo, kanti lokho kuyinkinga kunoma ubani ozama ukukubona ngokusobala — ngoba into eseyenziwe ingcwele iphinde yenziwe ilula, kanti lokhu kwakungalula. Babengeyona abahambi abahamba ezweni elingenalutho. Izwe lalingalingenalutho. Lalingakaze libe lingenalutho.

Enyakatho-mpumalanga, ngale kweDrakensberg, ezweni eliluhlaza elinezandla ezehla ziye olwandle olufudumele, kwakunombuso wamaZulu. Wawuphose ube neminyaka engamashumi amahlanu njengombuso owodwa futhi wawungamandla anamandla kakhulu engxenyeni eningizimu yezwekazi — umbuso owakhiwe, njengazo zonke imibuso, ngengxenye ngobuhlakani nangengxenye ngegazi, onebutho elihlelelwe lama-amabutho, elingafaka amadoda ayizinkulungwane eziningi aqeqeshiwe ensimini ngokushesha kunalo naliphi ibutho laseYurophu kulesi siqephu esincane sezwekazi elingaliphuphe. Inkosi yakhona ngo-1837 kwakunguDingane, owathatha isihlalo sobukhosi ngokubulala umfowabo uShaka, nowayebuka izinqola eziqoqana emingceleni yakhe yasentshonalanga ngendlela noma iyiphi inkosi ebuka ngayo abantu abangazi abahlomile befika emngceleni wayo: njengombuzo okwakuzodingeka uphendulwe, futhi uphendulwe nzima.

Bobabili laba bantu babesesaba. Lokho yinto yesibili okumele uyiqonde, futhi umbhalo uyakusekela kuzo zonke izinhlangothi. Abahambi babesesaba amandla abangakwazi ukuwalinganisa futhi abangawaqondi. Inkosi yayisesaba into eyayiyiqonda ngokuphelele, ngoba yayizwe okwenzeka kwenye indawo uma izinqola zifika zingami ukufika — yayizwe ngeKapa ngokwalo, nokuthi iminyaka engamashumi ayisithupha yokufika yenzani kubantu ababekhona kuqala. Ukwesaba akusona isizathu salokho okwenziwa noma ngubani wabo. Kodwa kuyinjini engaphansi kwakho konke, futhi umlando ongashiyi ukwesaba ngaphandle awukutsheli iqiniso; ukudayisela uhlangothi.

Futhi emaphethelweni akho konke, cishe kancane kakhulu ukubona, kwakukhona ubukhona besithathu: UMbuso abahambi ababubalekela, ngesimo sendawo encane engaziwa yabazingeli nabathengisi abesiNgisi phansi ethekwini ababelibiza ngokuthi Port Natal, behlala ngokubekezelela yinkosi, bethengiselana ngempondo zendlovu, bethatha amakhosikazi amaZulu, besayina amagama abo kunoma yiliphi iphepha elibonakala kungase libagcine bephila ngaleso sikhathi — futhi ngemuva kwabo, kude ngaphesheya kolwandle, umbuso omkhulu kunayo yonke eyake yenziwa umhlaba, owawungakanaki ngalokhu futhi, ngokuhamba kwesikhathi, owawuzoqala ukukhathalela kakhulu, futhi ulethe iminyaka engamashumi ayisithupha yohlobo locingo nohlobo lwenkambi ngisho noDingane wayengeke akucabange.

NgoFebruwari ka-1838, umholi wabahambi ogama lakhe linguPiet Retief wagibela waya endaweni enkulu yenkosi neqembu lamadoda angamashumi ayisikhombisa ukuyothatha, ngokukholwa kwakhe, incwadi esayiniwe yomhlaba. Okwenzeka khona yilokho okuyisisekelo sakho konke okulandelayo, futhi kuwuhlobo lomcimbi lolu bhuku olukhona ukuwubamba ngokweqiniso, ngoba kunemilando emibili futhi yomibili inobufakazi futhi igebe phakathi kwayo yilapho kuhlala khona inhlekelele yonke. Umlando owodwa — umlando wabahambi, oqoshwe etshe — uthi inkohliso: ukuthi inkosi yabadlisa futhi yabahlubula izikhali bese, ngegama, yathi mabaphathwe bahudulelwe egqumeni lokubulala babulawe ngemishiza, abesifazane nezinceku nengane kamuva, ngegazi elibandayo, kungekho sizathu ngaphandle kokungathembeki kwenkosi. Omunye umlando uthi into enzima ukuklwebha: ukuthi inkosi ebuke abantu abangazi behlomile bethatha umhlaba noma kuphi lapho beya khona, eseyibambe laba bantu abangazi kulokho okwakubukeka kuyo njengamanga noma ukuntshontsha izinkomo, yenza lokho inkosi esesaba ekwenzayo kuthunyelwa elihlomile esinqume ukuthi isandulela sokuhlasela — yahlasela kuqala, yahlasela empandeni, ngoba yayikholelwa, mhlawumbe ngokuqondile, ukuthi uma ingenzi njalo, umbuso wayo wawuzohamba ngendlela yaseKapa.

Umbhalo awukukuvumeli ukhethe. Umbhalo ukuvumela kuphela ukuthi ume egcekeni.

Okungaphikiswa yilokho okwalandela. Amabutho ahamba aya entshonalanga emasontweni alandelayo ahlasela izinkambi zabahambi ezazihlakazeke emifuleni ngaphansi kwezintaba — endaweni abahambi abayibiza kamuva ngokuthi Weenen — abulala abantu abangamakhulu ambalwa: amadoda, nabesifazane ababekwazi ukudubula njengamadoda, nezingane eziningi nezinceku ezazingakwazi. Kwakuyinhlekelele futhi kwakuhloselwe ukuba yinhlekelele. Futhi yenza into eyenziwa izinhlekelele: yathatha uhambo yalwenza impi, yathatha usizi yalwenza isizathu, yaqinisa okuthile kubasindile okwakuzothola impendulo, ezinyangeni eziyishumi kamuva, emfuleni.

Lowo mfula wawugeleza, ngaphambi komhla ka-16 kuZibandlela 1838, ngegama lesiZulu: iNcome. Ngemva kwalolo suku abahambi balibiza ngokuthi Bloedrivier — uMfula Wegazi — futhi igama liyisinqumo, yingakho lolu bhuku lungeke lulisebenzise njengalo, ngoba incwadi ayikho ohlangothini. Ngalolo suku iqembu labahambi elingaphansi kwamakhulu amahlanu, elalimi ngemva komjikelezo wezinqola eziboshelwe ndawonye olimini lomhlaba phakathi komfula nomgodi ojulile, lahlangana nokugadla kwebutho lamaZulu elikhulu kunabo izikhathi eziningi, lalephula, ihora ngehora, ngokudubula okuningi kumadoda ayenezinduku kuphela nezikhali zomzimba nomkhonto. Ngokuhlwa iqembu lase lilimele amadoda amathathu akekho ofile. Amabutho ayeshonile, ngokubala kweqembu, mhlawumbe izinkulungwane ezintathu. Umfula wageleza ubomvu. Abahambi bathi uNkulunkulu wabanika ukunqoba ekuphenduleni isifungo, bakha ukholo lwabo kukho ikhulu leminyaka nengxenye. AmaZulu akhumbula njengosuku lapho isizukulwane samajaha azo sachitheka kukudubula, akha usizi kukho olungenaso isikhumbuzo.

Lona uhlaka. Lugxilile, futhi lubhaliwe, futhi lapho luhlukana khona lolu bhuku luzokukhombisa zombili izindlela ziphinde zithembe wena ukuthwala umthwalo wazo.

Kodwa uhlaka akulona udaba, futhi ukubala abafileyo akusibo abafileyo. Ngaphakathi kwalolo hlaka olugxilile kwakukhona abantu, futhi iningi labo lalisha, futhi iningi labo lalisesaba ngendlela ethile, ephelele abasha kuphela abangasaba ngayo — okubona ukuphela komhlaba kungesisho kuphela kodwa izindaba zangempela zekuseni. Lolu bhuku lumayelana nabathathu babo. Alubazi amagama abo angempela, ngoba umbhalo wawungacabangi ukuthi abasha bafanele ukuqanjwa amagama ngaphandle uma beholile noma bafa ngokuphawulekayo. Ngakho lubanike amagama, nezimpilo, nokwesaba ababenakho ngempela, lubahlalisa ngaphakathi kohlaka lweqiniso ukubona lokho okushiywa ngaphandle ukubala abafileyo.

Babeneminyaka efanayo. Babunjwe yimishini emithathu ehlukene. Futhi akekho kubo, lapho ekugcineni ubabona, ozoba yisitha osithenjisiwe.

Qala ngentombazane.

# Isiqephu 1 — Ukusuka

Ihhashi kwakudingeka lidunyiswe ngaphambi kokudla kwasekuseni, futhi balinikeza uMarretjie ngoba nguyena owayekwazi ukulenza kahle ngokuzama kokuqala.

Wayengalifuni. Yilokho kungekho muntu owakusho kakhulu kodwa wonke umuntu wakuqonda: ukuthi ukufuna kwakungena ndawo kulokhu, njengoba ukufuna kwakungena ndawo ezinengi zezinto ezacelwa umhambi kuwe. Ihhashi elimpunga elingama-gelding lalilale ebusuku umlenze walo uphindekile ngendlela engalungile embobeni yonyawo ebengeke liyibone emnyameni, futhi manje lilele ekukhanyeni okuphuzi okuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka ngaphambi kokuphumana kwelanga intamo yalo yeluleke emhlabathini obandayo iso lalo liphenduke limhlophe limangele, futhi belihhashi lesibili likababa iminyaka eyisithupha belithwele umfowabo uHendrik ekuqiniselelweni kwakhe, futhi wayelithanda, futhi kwakumele life, futhi wayeneminyaka eyishumi nesikhombisa.

"Uzolenza kahle," kusho ubaba. Akuzange kube umbuzo futhi akuzange kube nesihluku. Wayeselilayishile i-*roer* yena ngokwakhe wayinika yona umgunyathi usuphelele, wabeka isandla sakhe emuva kwentamo yakhe, isikhashana, into esondele kakhulu othandweni ikusasa eyayingakuvumela, wase eyabheka inkomo ngoba izinkomo yizona ezazigcina bonke bephila kanti ihhashi elingakwazi ukuma lalinge lutho. Wayekuqonda ukubala. Wayesebala umhambi kusukela eneminyaka eyishumi nanye basuka eTarka nakho konke abanabantu bethutha kukhukhumala ngemuva kwesipani sezinkomo eziyishumi nesithupha unina ecula ihubo ukugcina abancane bengakhali. *Izibhamu ezingaphumi kahle zazilambe.* Ugogo wakhe wayekusho ngendlela abanye ogogo ababebonga ngayo. Intombazane eyayikwazi ukudubula yayidla, futhi intombazane eyayikwazi ukudubula yayigcina nabanye bedla, ngakho intombazane yafunda ukudubula ngendlela eyayifunda ngayo ukukhuleka nokukhanda nokukhipha ithole imikhono iphakanyiseke kuze kufike emahlombe, ngoba i-veld yayingenandaba ukuthi uyintombazane, nebhubesi lalingenandaba, nentathakusa eyayiletha i-impi — nakuba kwakungekafiki impi ngaleso sikhathi, ezweni eliphakeme elihlanzekile ngaphambi kwezintaba — intathakusa nayo yayingeke inandaba.

Waguqa eduze kwekhanda lempunga. Akazange azivumele ukushesha, ngoba ukushesha yindlela oyethuka ngayo, nokwethuka yindlela owenza ngayo into ehlanzekile ibe nesihluku. Wabeka isandla sakhe esicaba ethanjeni elide lobuso balo wezwa ukufudumala okwakusaphuma kulo, umoya ungena uphuma ngokushesha ubuthakathaka emakhaleni abanzi athambile, washo igama lalo kanye, ngezwi eliphansi, igama eliyisiphukuphuku langasese ayelinike lona eneminyaka eyisimfishane elingakhanga lamtshela muntu, indlebe yasondela ezwini lakhe. Wayesebeka umlomo lapho ubaba amfundise khona, ekuphambanisweni komugqa ocatshangwayo osuka endlebeni kuya esweni eliphambene, waphefumula ukuphuma ngendlela ophefumula ngayo ukuze uthule, wamudubula, futhi kwahlanzeka, nomsindo waphuma ucaba ku-veld wabuya engabuyeli lutho, ngoba kwakungekho lutho lapha eningeni olungabuya kulo, intshanyana kuphela kuya emkhawulweni oyindilinga womhlaba.

Wayesekhala ngaphambi kokuba umwangedwa ufe. Wazivumela, isikhashana sokuphela kokuma, ngoba nalokhoke wayesekufundile — ukuthi ungaba nomuzwa noma ungalwa nomuzwa kodwa awukwazi ukwenza kokubili futhi ulayishe, nokulayisha yikona okwakukugcina uphila, ngakho waba nomuzwa ngokushesha ngokuphelele wawubeka phansi wathatha uphondo lomgunyathi. Ngesikhathi uHendrik eza egijima, eneminyaka eyishumi nane ubuso bumhlophe, esezwe isibhamu esaba ngabantu, wayesesebuyisele ubuso bakhe i-*roer* ivuleke ngengalo yakhe futhi wakwazi ukusho, ngezwi elanyakazisa kancane kuphela, "Bekuyimpunga. Umlenze. Hamba uyosize uBaba nge-disselboom, ukhuni usephuke futhi," wambuka ukuvala okuphuma kuye nokunye okubi nokuthule okungena esikhundleni sakho, okwakuwukuqonda, okufika kuye njengoba kwakufikile kuye, ukuthi lokhu nje kwakuzoba kanjalo manje. Ukuthi izinto ozithanda zizolala ebumnyameni bese kudingeka zidunyiswe ngaphambi kokudla kwasekuseni, nokuthi uzobe ungumuntu okwenzayo, nokuthi usuku luyoqhubeka ngemuva kwalokho lufune umsebenzi walo kuwe kungakhathaliseki.

Lena kwakuyingxenye amadoda azoyibhala kamuva azoyishiya. Babezobhala uhambo nezimpi namagama abaholi nombhalo wezifungo. Babezoqopha abesifazane etshe legranayithi, abade bakhala benesibindi, itshe ongahamba uzungeze lona. Abangeke bakufake, ngoba babengazi kanjani nangoba kungajabulisi muntu, yindlela uMarretjie Pretorius ama ngayo otshanini olumanzi ngo-15 emva kwesihlanu ekuseni efavorite yakhe esefile iphole ezinyaweni zakhe nokuphila komfowenu omncane kungumthwalo wakhe nosuku lonke olungelula oluphambi kwakhe, futhi wezwa — ngaphansi kosizi, ngaphansi kwekhono, ngaphansi kwakho konke umhambi owamenza kona — intambo eyodwa ecacile yento wayengeke ayisho kakhulu futhi engenalo igama, okwakuwukuthi *wayesesaba njalo manje,* futhi wayekade esaba iminyaka emibili, nokuthi ukwesaba kwakungasazwakali njengento ezophela.

---

Babebheke enyakatho ngoba abadala babesaba, nakuba abadala bekubiza ngezinye izinto.

Bekubiza intando kaNkulunkulu, futhi bekubiza umkhankaso wezwe lapho indoda ingaba yinkosi yayo, futhi ubaba wayekubiza, ngobusuku obubi, *ukubalekela amaNgisi* — wakusho ngendlela ongasho igama lesifo, amaNgisi, uhulumeni we-smous eKoloni owakhulula izigqila wangakhokheli zona, owayeqhuba izinkantolo zawo ngolimi ayengalikwazi ukulandela wanikeza i-rooinek isinqumo njalo, owayelahlekiswe ipulazi lomzala wakhe empini yamaXhosa wamtshela ukuthi impi yayingeyakhe icala ngokuba lapho ayekhona. UMarretjie wayesemncane kulokho okuningi wayekukhumbula ngendlela okhumbula ngayo isimo sezulu: ingcindezi ende endlini, ubaba ebuya evela e-*drostdy* isigqoko sakhe esezandleni umhlathi wakhe umile, igama elithi *Engelse* likhuluma kancane ngemuva kokuthi izingane sezilele, indlela ongakhuluma ngayo ngento esophahleni.

Wayengazondi izingisi ngendlela ubaba azonda ngayo, ngoba wayengakaze ahlangane nenye ngempela, futhi awukwazi ukuzonda isimo sezulu ngendlela efanele. Kodwa wayefundile ukuthi ukuzesaba kwakuyiqiniso elikhulu lomhlaba wabadala, into engaphansi kwezinye zonke, nokuthi umhambi ngokwawo — lesi sizwe sonke esikhukhumayo esinamasondo, izinqola ezelule okwengxenye yosuku lokuhamba emgwaqeni wezinqola ababewusika ezweni elingakaze liwele inqola — kwakungukwesaba okunezinkabi ngaphambili. Babengabheki, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi amahubo athini, entwen ethile. Babebalekela kude. Izwe elingaphambili kwakungamahlebezi. Ukuvala ngemuva kwakuyinto eyodwa eqinile, futhi ubaba wayefake umndeni wonke kuwona wayishayela enyakatho ezweni alingalazi, lapho kwakunezizwe ayengazange azibone, ezibuswa yinkosi abamadoda ababesho igama layo ngokucophelela, ngendlela osho ngayo igama lento esophahleni.

Wayecabanga ngalokho ngaphezu kwalokho ayekuveza. Wayecabanga ngalokho ngesikhathi egibele — futhi wayegibela kahle, kangcono kunoHendrik, cishe njengamadoda, ehleli i-bay entsha ubaba amnikeze yona esikhundleni sempunga i-*roer* yakhe ilenga namehlo akhe enza ngaphandle kokutshlelwa ukubheka njalo okuhamba kancane kwe-veld owawufunda noma ufa, ukufunda utshani kokuhamba, imigqa ye-donga, i-kopje eyayingafihla amadoda ayikhulu noma umhlambi wamakhoza noma lutho nhlobo. Amadoda ayemlinganisela kancane. Wayazi ukuthi ayenjalo wayewavumela, ngoba kwakukhona ukusetshenziswa kukho: umuntu olinganiswe kancane ubona okuningi, ubuzwa imibuzo embalwa, angacabanga imicabango yakhe ngemuva kobuso abanye abanqume ukuthi bulula. Umalume uGerrit wayembiza ngokuthi *ons skerpskutter*, isibhamu sethu esihle, wayesho njengohlobo lokuncoma onikeza inja ehlakaniphile, wayemamatheka angasho lutho wamdubula nge-*skiet-kompetisie* elandelayo ngezinyoni ezimbili ezicacile wambuka enquma ukuthi kwakuyinhlanhla.

Futhi lokho ayecabanga, ngemuva kobuso obulula, ebheke enyakatho emahlebezini, kwakuyilokhu: ukuthi wonke umuntu wayemtshelile ukuthi abesabe bani, nokuthi wonke umuntu wayenesiqiniseko esikhulu, nokuthi ukuqiniseka ngokwako kwase kuqale, muva nje, ukumesabisa ngaphezu kwalokho ababeqiniseke ngakho. Amadoda ayekhuluma ngamaZulu ngendlela ayekhuluma ngayo ngamaNgisi — isimo sezulu, ubumnyama, into esophahleni — futhi naye wayengakaze ahlangane nanye yalezo, ngempela, ukukhuluma nayo, futhi waphawula ukuthi ukungahlangani akwenzanga muntu abe neqiniseko encane. Kwabenza babe neqiniseko *eningi*. Uma ungayazi into, kwaba lula ukuqiniseka ngayo, futhi wonke umhlaba wabadala wawubonakala usebenza ngawo lowo mafutha, futhi babewashisa manje ukushayela izinqola eziyinkulungwane engaziwa.

Wayengenawo amagama akho njengomqondo. Wayenakho njengomuzwa, indlela ayenakho konke okwakubalulekile — ngokushesha nangokuphelele, ngaphansi kwekhono. Kwakuyothatha iminyaka, uma ephila iminyaka, ngaphambi kokuba akwazi ukusho: *Ngicabanga ukuthi ukuqiniseka yikona okuyingozi. Ngicabanga sikuzimisele kakhulu ukubulala abantu esibaqashe ngokucophelela ukungababoni.* Eneminyaka eyishumi nesikhombisa wayenokukhathazeka kukho kuphela, into encane engalungile ayeyithwele ngendlela othwala ngayo itshe esicathulweni sakho ongakemi ukuyikhipha, ebheke enyakatho ngaphansi kwesibhakabhaka esiqhubeka unomphelo, ebheke ezweni lenkosi, ne-*roer* yomfowenu omncane elenga eduze kweyakhe, enesibindi ngoba kwakudingeka abe naso nesaba ngoba wayengeyena isiphukuphuku — kokubili kanyekanye, sonke isikhathi, okwakungukuthi nje, wayeseqala ukuqonda, kwakuzosho ukuthi uphila eminyakeni eyayiza.

Izintaba zaqhamuka ziphuma esiwulini ngosuku lwesithathu: uDrakensberg, oluhlaza olukhulu olunamazinyo phezulu njengento elumelayo. Ngale kwazo kwakuyizwe lenkosi, nokuluhlaza, nolwandle olufudumele amadoda ayekhuluma ngalo, nezwe elalizoba ngelabo ngoba uNkulunkulu namahlebezi atsho njalo.

Ubaba wathatha itomu eduze kwakhe wabheka izintaba isikhathi eside engasho lutho, wayebheka uhlangothi lobuso bakhe, ukwesaba nethemba behleli kulo ndawonye ngendlela ababehleli ngayo kukho konke manje, futhi akamtshelanga ngetshe esicathulweni sakhe. # Isahluko 3 — ITheku

UGeorge Biggar wayeneminyaka eyishumi nesishiyagalombili futhi ekude kakhulu nanoma yini, futhi into angakwazi ukujwayela, ngemva kweminyaka emibili esekugcineni komhlaba, ukuthi sekugcina komhlaba kungaze kwanake ukuthi ungubani.

Ethekwini ababebiza ngokuthi iPort Natal kwakungekho sonto ongaqinisekiswa kulo namantshi ongamesaba futhi kungekho England ongayikhuluma ngaphandle kwabantu abambalwa ababeyithwele emakhanda abo njengohlamvu lwemali oseluthambile ephaketheni — abazingeli, ikakhulukazi, nabathengisi ababili, noyise, owayebalethe bonke lapha ukuzozenzela imali nge-ivory kodwa okwamanje wayesenze isithunzi, umkhuhlane, nabangane abaningi phakathi kwamaZulu, okwabe uGeorge esekuqonda ukuthi yiyo kuphela imali eyayihlonishwa ngempela esekugcineni komhlaba. Awukwazi ukudla ifulegi lapha. Wayebabukile amadoda ezama. Umhlaba wawuthatha ubuntu bakho basesiNgisi nokuqiniseka kwakho kwasesiNgisi njengoba amagagasi ethatha itshe eliphonswe olwandle, ungaphenduli, bese ukunika ncamashi lokho ongakuthwala nokudubula nokuthengisa, lutho olunye, futhi uGeorge — owayengumfana ompofu eNgilandi futhi engumpofu nakakhulu lapha — wayenqume ukuthi akaphatheki kabi njengoba kwakufanele. Kwakukhona ubuqotho endaweni eyayingeke yenze sengathi iyakuthanda.

Wayengeyena isosha. Uyise wayeqoke ibutho elincane ngonyaka owandulela, iPort Natal Volunteers, amadoda ayishumi nambili anezibhamu zokuzingela nemibono emikhulu, futhi lalihlukane ngaphambi kwesizini, okwabe uGeorge ecabanga ukuthi yinto yasesiNgisi kakhulu eyake yenzeka selokhu bafika: *umqondo* webutho, umfaniswano womqondo, bese ntambama yaseNingizimu Afrika ivele iyancibilikisa ngoba kwakungekho lutho lapha elingaba yibutho *elilwayo* lapho ibutho lingaba wusizo. Amandla angempela etheku kwakuyinkosi ngaphakathi ezweni, ababe behlala ngomusa wakhe, nesimo sezulu, nemikhuhlane ehamba kancane ivela echibini. Akukho nakukodwa kulokho lapho amadoda ayishumi nambili anama-muskets nefulegi kwakusho khona lutho.

Lokho uGeorge ayekuqonda — futhi wayekuqonda njengoba intombazane emakhilomitha angamakhulu amabili eningizimu-ntshonalanga yayiqonda itshe esicathulweni sayo, njengomuzwa ngaphansi kwekhono kunomqondo ayengakwazi ukukukhuluma — ukuthi wayengeyona ingxenye yendaba yanoma ubani. AmaBoeri ayengena ezintabeni ayenaye uNkulunkulu wawo nesikhalazo sawo nezinqola zawo; inkosi yayinombuso wayo nokwesaba kwayo; uMbuso omkhulu owawugxilile uhlamvu lwemali ekhanda likaGeorge ngaphambi kokuba akwazi ukufunda wawunalo ifulegi, ndawana thize, kude, lingakanaki nhlobo ngaleliTheku kodwa ngolunye usuku lizokhathalela kakhulu. Futhi uGeorge wayemi emaphethelweni akho konke lokhu, umfana wasesiNgisi ogwini esekugcineni kwe-Afrika, ephethe i-England engangohlamvu lwemali, ewusizo emshinini ngenxa yalokho kuphela ayengakuthwala nokudubula — okwakuyintengo efanayo umshini owawuyibeka entombazaneni yamaBhunu nomfana wakwaZulu olingana naye ngeminyaka, nakuba bobathathu babengakazi ukuthi bayabelana ngayo.

NgoFebrayathi, uyise wayezomthumela ngaphakathi ezinkambini zamaTrekker emifuleni, ngoba amaBiggar ayezibandakanye namaBhunu futhi indoda inakekela abalingani bayo. UGeorge wayezohamba ngoba uyise wamtshela, njengoba intombazane yadubula ihhashi ngoba uyise wayitshele, njengoba umfana emkhangweni wameva egijima ngoba umshini wamtshela — izingane ezintathu, imishini emithathu, ngamunye enza into enzima ekuseni eyayicelwa ngoba ukuthi cha emshinini wakho kwakungakayibikho into noma yimuphi kubo owayazi ukukwenza.

Wayengakwazi konke lokho, ekuseni kakhulu kangako. Wayazi itheku, nephunga lokuhlehla kwamanzi, nokuthi wayekude kakhulu nanoma yini, nokuthi isekugcineni komhlaba sasingamnaki ukuthi ungubani — okwakungukuthi, wayeqala ukucabanga, iqiniso elikhulu futhi cishe elimnene kunazo zonke ayake wazitshela.
