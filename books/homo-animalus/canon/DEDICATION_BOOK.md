# Dedication — working copy

Canonical text lives in `build/chapters/ch-00-dedication.md` (that is the version that renders).
This file holds the intent behind it so it never drifts by accident in a later pass.

**Dedicated to: Andries J. Greyling Senior** — the author's father. Real name, real dedication,
same house policy as *Full Send, Klaus* and the letter to Lisel: some dedications are public
because the truth of them is the point.

**The facts this dedication is built from (do not embellish beyond these — they don't need it):**
- A dairy farmer at Grootvlei, and a bovine vet, and a marksman — "grand master" at all three,
  in the son's own word.
- Cows made way for him. AJ physically watched him push his own old cows aside to walk a path
  between them that they would gently open for him, or for others, unasked.
- Out in a grazing field, when his red Isuzu bakkie pitched up, the cows ran to it.
- AJ saw him cry exactly once: nursing a sick cow, he carried her a 2kg bag of soft brown sugar
  to help her. She did not make it.

**Handle with care (binding, per [[why-these-books-exist]]):** warts-and-all is the house ethos,
but a father is not a character to be improved for drama. Every detail above is reported as given,
nothing added. If AJ ever wants a line changed or pulled, that happens without argument — same
standing rule as the Lisel letter.
