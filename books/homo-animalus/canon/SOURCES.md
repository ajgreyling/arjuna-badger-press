# Sources / fact-floor (real-world claims, verify before quoting in prose)

- **Cristina Zenato** — Italian-born shark diver/conservationist based in the Bahamas (Grand
  Bahama) for ~30 years. Removes fishing hooks from wild Caribbean reef sharks by hand, using the
  sharks' natural **tonic immobility** reflex (gentle stimulation of the snout/ampullae of
  Lorenzini induces a trance-like stillness). Over 25+ years and 300+ hooks removed. Sharks she has
  worked with recognise her, approach her, and rest their heads in her lap. Active in the campaign
  that made the Bahamas a shark sanctuary in 2011. (Sourced 2026-07-29: cristinazenato.com,
  reefprotect.org, welovesharks.org, Gulf News.) Her own word for it is closer to "imprinting
  through presence and constant behaviour" than magic — cite that honestly, don't overclaim.

- **Dean Radin, *Real Magic* (2018)** — PhD, Chief Scientist at the Institute of Noetic Sciences;
  worked on the declassified US government "Stargate" remote-viewing program. Thesis: what
  cultures called magic across history (force of will / divination / theurgy) maps onto what
  parapsychology studies as psi (psychokinesis, telepathy, clairvoyance, precognition). Draws on a
  claimed ~3,000 lab studies over 150 years, incl. random-number-generator and meta-analytic
  telepathy studies. His own framing is "the evidence is far stronger than commonly assumed," not
  "proven beyond doubt" — the field remains genuinely contested in mainstream science; represent
  both the claim and the controversy.

- **Rupert Sheldrake** — biologist, theory of morphic resonance/morphic fields; author of *Dogs
  That Know When Their Owners Are Coming Home* (1999), a direct study of apparent
  anticipatory/telepathic behaviour in pets. The correct citation for the "my dogs know my mental
  state" thread.

- **Merlin Sheldrake** — mycologist (Rupert's son), author of *Entangled Life* (2020): fungal
  networks, mycorrhizal "wood wide web," and the case for a more interconnected, less
  individual-centred view of life. The correct citation for the "everything is communicating"
  thread.

- **Joe Dispenza** — chiropractor turned researcher/author (*You Are the Placebo*, *Breaking the
  Habit of Being Yourself*); meditation, neuroplasticity, heart-brain coherence. HeartMath
  Institute's coherence/heart-rate-variability research is the adjacent real science for
  "animals as co-regulators" (dogs/cats/horses entraining a human nervous system) — cite HeartMath
  separately from Dispenza, don't merge the two into one source.

- **Dolphin cognition** — mirror self-recognition studies (Reiss & Marino, 2001, bottlenose
  dolphins) are the real peer-reviewed anchor for "dolphins are sentient." Cite that specific
  study, not just the general claim.

- **"Animal magnetism"** — the historical term is Franz Anton Mesmer's (1770s), the discredited
  physical-fluid theory that nonetheless produced real hypnosis/suggestion effects and became the
  root of modern hypnotherapy. Keep the history straight: Mesmer's mechanism was wrong, but the
  phenomenon he stumbled onto (suggestion, trance, rapport) was real and is the ancestor of a real
  clinical field.

- **Animal worship / reverence in history** — candidates to research properly before drafting:
  Ancient Egypt (Bastet, Anubis, the Apis bull), the Hindu reverence for cattle and the deity
  forms of Nandi/Ganesha, San/Bushman rock art (eland, therianthropes), Norse (Odin's ravens and
  wolves, Freyja's cats), totemism broadly. Needs a real sensitivity pass before publication —
  same standard as every HBT sacred-material chapter.

- **Animals in psychedelic/shamanic accounts** — the jaguar and serpent motifs of ayahuasca
  visionary reports (documented in ethnobotanical/anthropological literature, e.g. accounts
  gathered by researchers of Amazonian ayahuasca use), and DMT-entity reports sometimes described
  in animal or animal-hybrid form. Report as documented first-person accounts and cultural
  cosmology, never as the author asserting literal metaphysical fact.

- **Islamic dhabihah (ritual slaughter)** — hadith (Muslim 1249; Ibn Majah 3172) establish that the
  knife must be sharpened before the animal is present and kept out of its sight, that one animal
  must never be slaughtered in view of another, and that the animal must not be dragged or pinned
  while the blade is prepared, all justified explicitly as minimising the animal's suffering.
  Cross-referenced against Jordan's Iftaa' Department fatwa and a 2023 Indonesian halal-slaughter
  regulatory decree, both of which restate the same "knife not sharpened near/in front of the
  animal" rule as active practice, not just historical text. (Sourced 2026-07-29.)

- **The Mundari people, South Sudan** — Nilotic agro-pastoralists whose Ankole-Watusi cattle (up to
  8 ft at the horn, ~$500/head) are the material and spiritual centre of their culture: guarded
  overnight at gunpoint, massaged with dung ash twice daily, slept beside. Dung ash is used as
  antiseptic/sunscreen/insect repellent on both people and cattle; cattle urine is used to wash
  hands, face, teeth, and hair, with the ammonia bleaching hair orange-red — regarded by the Mundari
  as aesthetically desirable, not accidental. (Sourced 2026-07-29: Africa Geographic, Wikipedia,
  Theirson Diary, GantNews/Tariq Zaidi photo essay, Times Now.)

- **Hippotherapy vs. therapeutic horseback riding** — hippotherapy is a clinical tool delivered only
  by a licensed PT/OT/SLP using the horse's three-dimensional gait (which closely mirrors human
  pelvic movement in walking) toward specific functional goals; therapeutic riding is
  instructor-led and teaches riding/horsemanship skills toward broader goals. Both are credentialed
  under PATH International. An RCT (Bass, Duchowny & Llabre, 2009, *Journal of Autism and
  Developmental Disorders*) found improved sensory processing and social/communication outcomes in
  children with autism after a course of therapeutic riding versus a control activity. A broader
  systematic review/meta-analysis of equine-assisted interventions for autism found genuine positive
  effects alongside real methodological weaknesses in the underlying study base (small samples,
  inconsistent protocols) — represent as encouraging, not settled. (Sourced 2026-07-29.)

- **Emotional support animals / service animal law** — Ventiko and Dexter the peacock were denied
  boarding by United Airlines at Newark in January 2018 (widely reported, e.g. contemporary news
  coverage). Comedian Maeve Higgins publicly described buying a fake service-vest for her dog Shadow
  on Irish television in 2018, then apologised after backlash from service-dog training
  organisations. The US Department of Transportation's Air Carrier Access Act final rule, effective
  January 2021, redefined "service animal" as a dog individually trained to perform a task for a
  person with a disability, explicitly excluding emotional support animals from mandatory free cabin
  accommodation going forward. (Sourced 2026-07-29.)
