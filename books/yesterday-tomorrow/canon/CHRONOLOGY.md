# Chronology — *YESTERDAY, TOMORROW*

> One novel. The story runs **2030 → 2034**, chronologically, from the cold open (L-29).
> Layer H is how the world got here and arrives only as fragments in scene.

---

## LAYER H — approach

| Date | Public | Hidden |
|---|---|---|

| **2029** | The consortium forms around weak-signal metrology and sovereign scientific compute | Nairobi begins the search-method work that will remove the GPU requirement |

| **2034** | Close | Averted. Markets recover. Governments deny everything. The encrypted file is opened |

> The gap between the cold open's file and the events it describes is the book's longest held
> question. Do not answer it early and do not let a character solve it as a puzzle — it resolves as
> an **implication**, once the reader knows what the machine can do.

## Continuity with *Afrika 2035*

*Afrika 2035* is one year downstream of the close. By then the 420 Code is the accepted unified
frame, the GPU bubble has burst, and the four quarters have formed around G. Nothing here may
contradict that end state, and this book never winks at it.

**The One Record / Lucid World distinction still binds (L-27):** *One Record* is the ingested corpus
— it survives, complete, in every local instance, and is what *Afrika 2035* means. What this novel
ends is the **join**: the single recombined causal view. A character who says "they deleted the One
Record" is wrong, and someone should correct them.
