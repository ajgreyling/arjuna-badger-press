## Relationship web

- **Dries ↔ Ama** — mutual respect expressed as arguments about whether discarded data is ever truly discarded. Emotional spine: **trust without total access.**
- **Dries ↔ Kiki** — builder versus verifier. He experiences her controls as obstruction; she sees his improvisation as an unaudited threat.
- **Lwazi ↔ G** — credentialled experimentalist versus outsider theorist. Each needs the other and resents that fact.
- **Sanna ↔ Ama** — Antarctica supplies the temporal signal; Ghana decides whether it is contamination. Their professional dispute becomes **the hinge of both books**.
- **Naledi ↔ Mercer** — sovereignty versus universal stewardship. Both can articulate real harms caused by the other's preferred model.
- **G ↔ Lucid** — creator of the corpus versus a machine that operationalises it. **Lucid must be able to refuse even G.**
- **Dries ↔ Hennie** — twenty years of the same office park. Dries has never once thought about where the money comes from, which is the point.
- **Kiki ↔ her own method** — the elegance that made the search possible is what made it universal, and universality is what made reflexivity global. Her arc is not betrayal; it is authorship.

## Character payoff tests

| Arc | Plant | Crisis test | Payoff action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dries:** observability → restraint | He insists "if it happened, log it" | The corridor is computable *because* everything is logged; observability is the mechanism, not the safeguard | He argues for a limit he cannot verify, and accepts a plan he cannot watch |
| **Ama:** neutrality → stewardship | She calls ingestion "plumbing" | Her neutral queue encoded who mattered, and fed the trading lane | She publishes her own queue's history, then decides what may never be ingested |
| **Kiki:** integrity → contestability | A valid signature ends arguments | Her method is everywhere, so everyone's priors agree, so nobody's disagreement is real | She argues for deliberate divergence — the opposite of the standard she built |
| **Lwazi:** recognition → independence | He rehearses a prestige lecture | Recognition is offered for silence and relocation | He publishes the killable raw derivation from Technopark |
| **Sanna:** isolation → relation | She speaks more easily to instruments than people | She holds the cold open alone for four months, then must send a claim without its evidence | She trusts a distributed human chain she cannot personally audit, and is trusted back |
| **Naledi:** secrecy → federated legitimacy | She locks the first result down | Secrecy is itself an intervention, and it raises the probability | She disperses authority across African jurisdictions, against every instinct and at the cost of her position |
| **Hennie:** provision → confession | He solves money problems nobody else will look at | The lane he opened is the leak | He tells them himself, before he is found, and it does not save him |

## Note on G (continuity)

**G is a principal**, working from Technopark — not offstage, not posthumous, not a consultant on a
video call. He refuses soft claims, asks what measurement would kill a thing, states the reflexivity
trap, and finds the blind spot in Act III. The alternative on the table
(posthumous notebooks) buys mystery at the cost of intellectual conflict and consent, and the bible
recommends against it. See `OPEN_DECISIONS.md`.

Continuity with the press shelf: G is the 420 Code originator of *Afrika 2035* — economics degree,
no physics degree, copyleft corpus, kill-switch discipline, zero appetite for worship. This book's G is
that man three or four years later, being asked to let the work leave his hands.
