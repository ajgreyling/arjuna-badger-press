The Why Files: The Scouring

In the green hills below Uffington Castle, a 3,000-year-old white horse runs across the chalk — a figure so old its meaning is lost, yet so well-kept it has never vanished. Every seven or so years, for a hundred generations, the people here have scoured the lines back into the hill, not knowing why — only that the horse must not fade.

**The interesting middle path:** The official story is ironclad: Bronze Age people dug metre-deep trenches, filled them with chalk, and set a shape in the hillside that only makes sense from the valley or the sky. OSL dating pins it to ~1380–550 BC. The skeptics are right: the folklore is a patchwork, the dragon tales are later, and maintenance is just tradition. But then comes the potato: try keeping a shape alive on a living hillside for even a century, let alone three millennia. Leave it alone, it vanishes in a generation. Yet here it stands. The hole: how does a maintenance act — not the meaning, just the *doing* — transmit unbroken across empire, plague, and war, when even the memory of why is gone? What is a tradition that outlives its sense? The wink: nobody alive knows what the horse was for, but every generation has agreed, without fail, to keep it alive.

**The chase:** The crew races a heritage consultancy, bankrolled and charming, who want to "restore" the horse to their vision of an ancient Britain — to fix the meaning, to claim the act. The crew want to read it; the consultants want to own it. The local lead is the community of scourers — and Edie Vane, returning from Book 1, stands as the bridge: she out-reads the crew, because only those who have carried the chalk know what it means to care for something long after the reason is lost. The ND lead is the Deaf face-reader, Frik, who sees what is carried in gesture and ritual, not words. Jakobus reads the made thing: not who made it, not what it "means," but what it *does* — it holds the hillside, it marks the land, it binds a people. In the finale on the chalk, the answer is not what the horse was, but what the scouring is *for*: care outlasting meaning.

**Back-matter promise:** Every date, trench, and OSL result is real. The unbroken chain is inferred, not documented. The meaning is lost, the tradition is alive, and the question stands: what do we keep, when we cannot remember why?
