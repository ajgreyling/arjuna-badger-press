THE CREW: 
- Frik (ND lead): Deaf, acute face-reader, communicates in BSL and gestures; sees ritual and care in action, not words. Curious, wry, never a caricature. 
- The LENS: Ex-war photographer, PTSD, always watching the room, the camera the series keeps asking to lower. Sees the moment others miss.
- Two other crew: One autistic, pattern-spotter, hyperfocus on landscape and timelines; one with ADHD, kinetic, improvisational, good at winning trust from locals. All ND wiring is lived, not a wound to be healed.
- Jakobus: The fixer, never POV, always the road, defers to crew and local, reads the made thing for function, not myth. 
THE LOCAL: 
- Edie Vane (returning from Book 1): Local teacher and lifelong scourer, her family has tended the Horse for generations. She out-reads the crew, holding the living knowledge of the scouring; she alone can name what the act is for. The community of scourers is a living presence, not background.
THE ADVERSARY:
- Heritage Consultancy: Bankrolled, charming, plausible; their lead (Dr. Rowan Leigh) is passionate and believes restoration is preservation. They are not villainous—clean-handed, genuinely want to "save" the Horse, but in fixing the meaning, they risk erasing the living act.
VOICES: 
- The crew's voices are distinct, neurodiverse, never caricatured. The local's voice is grounded, wry, and affectionate toward the land. The adversary is articulate, persuasive, a worthy opponent. Close-third rule: all internality flows through the crew, never Jakobus or the local at the turn.
