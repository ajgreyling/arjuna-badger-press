## 0. THE ONE-LINE SEED

> *Five men swore they'd built a machine that breaks the energy rules — a carburetor that sips, a disc
> that flies, a mirror that bends time. One held real patents. One was a real genius the State really
> buried. One stole the very electricity he said he made for free. The official story is the same for all
> five: **they were wrong, or they were frauds, and that's the end of it.** The only honest question left
> is the one the crew were built to ask of the written-off: **not were they right — but what were they
> reaching for, and who was glad when they went quiet?***

---

## 1. THE FRAME — the interesting middle path (LOCKED), and the §3 DEPARTURE this book documents

This book does **not** say "free energy is real and they're hiding it" (the credulous-believer drone —
Bible §8, a failure state). It does **not** say "cranks and con-men, every one, nothing to see" (the
smug-debunker sneer — also a failure state). Both miss the actual, human shock. The book lives in the
gap — but the gap here is **different from the slate's**, and that difference is the whole design:

- **The decoy = "free energy" itself.** The world's eye (and the genre's) is trained on the *miracle
  machine* — does the disc fly, does the carburetor really do 200 mpg. **That argument is a trap**, and
  the book knows it: the machines mostly **don't work**, and the book says so, plainly, on the record.
  The miracle is the misdirection. **The real thing is the *men* — and what was done to them.**
- **The official solve = "wrong, or fraud."** And for the *machines*, that's largely **TRUE** — the book
  reveres the truth of it (real patents that never worked; a disc with no photograph; a perpetual-motion
  claim that violates conservation). It earns the reader's trust as the sober one. *(Then: "…and yet.")*
- **THE CRACK (the real residue — and in this book it is almost always a *person*, not a machine):** the
  *official* story closes the file at "wrong/fraud" and **stops there** — and that *stopping* is the
  hole. Because the documentary record keeps **not** closing cleanly: a man with **four real patents**
  (Pogue) who simply **went quiet** and made oil filters and **refused, to his death, to say the one
  number**; a **real, gold-medalled astronomer** (Kozyrev) the **Gulag** really buried, who really
  **caught the Moon breathing**, and then spent his second life trying to prove time had a current; a
  vacuum that **measurably isn't empty** (the Casimir force, weighed to within a percent) wearing the same
  name as a thousand garage frauds. **The hole is never "the machine works." The hole is the *gap between
  how completely the world dismissed them and how little the dismissal actually explains.*** The residue
  is grief, refusal, and the one Russian paper nobody can replicate or quite throw away.
- **THE §3 DEPARTURE (documented here on purpose).** The line's firewall (Bible §3) is "the **local**
  out-reads the crew on their own ground; the answer is theirs to give." **Inventions have no indigenous
  ground.** So this book makes **the marginalised inventor themselves the "local"** — the disbelieved
  tinkerer, the buried astronomer, the jailed believer is **the *unheard* whose life's work the crew
  arrive to read, never to seize.** Where the inventor is dead (most are), the "local who out-reads the
  crew" is the **heir / keeper of the bench / surviving witness / archivist** — and the *answer* (**what
  was it *for*; what was *done* to them**) is **theirs to give or withhold.** The crew read the *pattern*
  across five graves; the keeper reads the *person.* This is the firewall kept **in spirit** (the win is
  the local's; the crew never own the place — and here "the place" is a human life). **The book states
  this departure in-text via the structure, never as a lecture.**
- **The real target = the *quiet*.** Not a buried temple this time (the slate's move) but a buried
  *silence*: the moment each man stopped talking. Pogue's. Kozyrev's, behind the Academy's verdict.
  Searl's, behind a prison door. **Somebody, sometimes, was glad of the quiet** — and the chase is the
  crew racing to read the silence before the people who profit from it tidy it away for good. *(The
  adversary weaponises the official story exactly as on the slate: "they were cranks" is the **best cover
  a real suppression ever had** — and the genius of the book is that it's **usually true**, which is what
  makes the **once** it isn't so hard to see.)*

## 2. THE STRUCTURE — the descent (LOCKED; this is the book's spine)

Unlike the slate's sites (twelve roughly-equal holes), the five devices **stratify by credibility**, and
the relay walks **down** the ladder — from the most documentable to the wholly indefensible. **The
descent IS the arc** (the site-books don't have this; it's the reason this is a book and not an
anthology). Full fact-floor: [`../../../canon/SUPPRESSED_TECH_DOSSIERS.md`](../../../canon/SUPPRESSED_TECH_DOSSIERS.md).

| Node | Device | The residue (what's real) | The register | What the crew *read* here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Pogue carburetor** | **Real patents**, real press, the editor's own retraction | **Documentable** — the grounded floor | *the lie in the documents* (Naila) + *the made thing* (Jakobus reads the exhaust-heated vaporiser — it's real engineering) |
| **2** | **Zero-point energy** | The vacuum **measurably** isn't empty (Casimir force) | **Real frontier** — strange without invention | *the real physics vs the word's abuse* — the crew learn the wonder is the truth, not the claim |
| **3** | **Kozyrev Mirror** | The **man** (Gulag, the Moon breathing); mirror unverified | **True grief** — the novelistic heart | *the truth on the body / the withheld* — the keeper reads **grief**, not telepathy |
| **4** | **Tachyon** | Real physics is wondrous; the **products** are word-theft | **The laundered word** — villainy is stolen vocabulary | *the grift* (the MONEY) + *the image/frame* (the LENS) — where a real word becomes a con |
| **5** | **Searl Effect Generator** | **No defensible physical residue** — only a man and a belief | **Indefensible** — the climax | *the made thing that isn't there* — **Jakobus's gift fails**; the cost lands |

**The arc the descent buys:** the crew walk from *a real patent you can hold* into *a man who believed so
hard it convicted him* — and **Searl is the climax**, the place the line's master question (*what is it
**for**?*) finally **has no answer the world will hand back.** That void costs someone (§5). This is the
**Yonaguni move** the slate already prizes ("Jakobus's gift meets its limit — a real character cost"),
made into a whole book — and the **emotional** climax, not just the structural one.

## 3. REAL-WORLD LOAD-BEARING FACTS (the *Why Files* "here's the hole" beats)

*(All sourced in [`../../../canon/SUPPRESSED_TECH_DOSSIERS.md`](../../../canon/SUPPRESSED_TECH_DOSSIERS.md) —
binding for real-vs-claimed. The short version, the beats the prose hangs on:)*

- **Pogue (the floor).** Four **real US patents** — **1,750,354 / 1,938,497 / 1,997,497 / 2,026,798**
  (1930–36) — readable, holdable, describing a genuine **exhaust-heated vaporizing carburetor.** The
  press said **200 mpg**; the **originating editor (R. Pepper, *Canadian Automotive Trade*) later called
  the report "entirely misleading"**; **Pogue himself (1953) denied 200 mpg "or even half that" — and
  refused to name the real figure.** Physics line: a vapor rig nudging a thirsty 1930s V8 into the
  **high-20s mpg is plausible**; **200 is thermodynamically impossible by a carburetor alone** — *the
  believable kernel and the impossible legend share a root.* **USE THE PATENTS STRAIGHT; the hole is the
  refused number and the quiet.**
- **ZPE (the frontier).** **½ℏω** is real; the **Casimir force is measured** (Lamoreaux 1997, ~5%; <1%
  since). You **cannot** run a lightbulb off it (ground state; Forward's battery is one-shot, ~milli-eV).
  The establishment's *real* embarrassment is the **opposite** of the fringe's: the **cosmological
  constant problem** (theory predicts ~120 orders of magnitude **too much** vacuum energy). **DARPA/NASA
  looked, openly, and found no free lunch** — Tang's *"a vacuum is a vacuum."* **The wonder is the
  truth.**
- **Kozyrev (the heart).** **Norilsk Gulag**; **two 10-year sentences** (the second handed down *inside*);
  **named in Solzhenitsyn**; **3 Nov 1958 — caught the Moon breathing** (Alphonsus spectrum — volcanic
  reading wrong, phenomenon class now respectable); **gold medal 1969; a lunar crater bears his name.**
  His "energy of time" theory was **formally rejected** (Tamm; the Academy, 1960). **Crucial attribution:
  Kozyrev did NOT build the "Kozyrev Mirror"** — the aluminium spiral was a **1990s Novosibirsk**
  invention (Kaznacheev; patent RU2122446) that **borrowed his name.** The Novosibirsk parapsychology
  experiments are **fringe, unreplicated.** **The man is true; the mirror is the unverified gadget — that
  asymmetry is the book.**
- **Tachyon (the laundered word).** Real tachyons: **never detected**; in real physics "tachyonic" means
  an **unstable vacuum** (the **Higgs was once tachyonic** — that's the Higgs mechanism), **not** a
  speedster. **OPERA (2011): a six-sigma faster-than-light headline undone by a loose fiber-optic
  cable** — science policing itself (Ereditato & Autiero resigned). **"Tachyon energy" products
  (pendants, "tachyon water," chambers) are pseudoscience word-theft — no mechanism, no evidence, no link
  to the physics.** The tell: the exact moment "tachyon" stops meaning *vacuum instability* and starts
  meaning *a thing you can buy that heals you.* **The villainy is vocabulary theft.**
- **Searl (the indefensible climax).** **No** reproduced demonstration in 60+ years (overunity violates
  conservation — *pseudoscience*, not "fringe-but-open"). The **documented, lethal fact**: he was
  **jailed for stealing the electricity he swore he generated for free** (*The Times*, 1982/83).
  **Heerfordt (1991): not one witness; discs hung on wires for photos; fake composites.** The one
  orphaned seducer: **Roschin–Godin (*Tech. Phys. Lett.*, 2000)** — claimed ±35% weight change, **never
  replicated, alleged a forgery.** The **"Law of the Squares" is magic-square numerology.** **Render him
  *tragic true-believer, never mocked* (§5). The narrator confirms nothing; the narrator never sneers.**

## 4. POV & CAST (LOCKED to the line's model — crew owns the POV; the inventor-as-local resolves §3)

- **The crew owns the POV** (Bible §3 / the model that crystallised in *The Belly Hill*). This book sits
  **later in the line** — the found-family is **established**; lean on the warmth and the cover-each-
  other's-blind-spots ensemble work that the assembly book earned. *(Running-order: a "special" outside
  the 12, like Marree Man — see §7.)*
- **The "local" is the inventor / the keeper of the bench (the §3 departure).** Each node, the crew defer
  to the one who out-reads them on **the person**: a **living heir, lab partner, apprentice, archivist, or
  surviving witness** who owns the *what-was-it-for / what-was-done.* The crew read the *pattern across
  five*; the keeper reads the *one.* **The win is the keeper's; the crew never seize the credit** — and
  the firewall's danger (clever outsiders cracking someone's mystery) is refused by making the "mystery"
  a **human life the crew have no right to.**
- **ND layer(s) that lead — rotate, per the slate's discipline (≥1 ND member present, humanised never
  caricatured, lived-experience veto):**
  - **Naila** (autistic pattern-cognition, the *Priya lineage*) leads the **Pogue** node and the **ZPE**
    node — she is the **potato-prover**: she reads the **real patents** and the **real physics**, and she
    is the one who can hold *"the kernel is real AND the miracle is impossible"* in one hand without
    flinching. Her gift here is **refusing the false binary** (it's not "fraud" *or* "suppressed" — the
    truth is stranger and sadder). *(Her cost shown too — the drowning-in-crowds, the relief of a clean
    number; sideways warmth; veto.)*
  - **The MONEY/fixer** (reformed insider, the GRIFT) leads the **tachyon** node — reads the **"tachyon
    energy" industry as the con it is**, from the inside, because he used to run grifts like it. His arc
    rhymes: *the man who sold the laundered word learning to tell it from the real thing.*
  - **Frik** (Tourette's, the LIE) recurs for the **over-reach punctures** — when a true-believer (or a
    crew member, §5) reaches past the evidence, Frik's involuntary blurt **names it** at the worst
    possible moment, gently and devastatingly. *(⚠ consultant veto; never swearing-for-laughs.)*
- **The LENS (ex-conflict photographer, PTSD — recurs every book, NOT the ND constant).** Load-bearing
  here, because the whole subject is **how a thing is framed**: the difference between the photo that
  **documents** a real patent or a real grief and the photo that **sensationalises** a man into a tabloid
  "SUPPRESSED GENIUS!" headline or **sneers** him into a crank. She reads **the image** — and at the
  **Searl** node she faces the hardest version of her own discipline: *the photographs are fakes, the man
  is real, and the kind thing and the true thing are not the same frame.* Her standing arc (*witness
  without consuming; lower the camera; ask before you shoot*) is, in this book, **applied to a man's
  dignity** rather than a place's.
- **The one who most wants to believe — the book's beating wound (§5).** One crew member (author's
  call — strong candidate: a *new* draw, or the **MONEY/fixer** carrying it personally) is the one for
  whom **the maybe needs to be a yes** — who needs Searl to be right, because if the written-off can be
  *right*, then so can they. **They are the one who has to let go.** This is the emotional engine of the
  descent.
- **Jakobus is the road.** He reads the **made thing** all book — Pogue's vaporiser (real), the lattice of
  a magnetic ring, the spiral of an aluminium mirror — asking *not who, not is-it-real, but **what is it
  for**.* And at the **Searl** climax **his gift fails**: there is **no made thing to read**, only a man
  and a belief, and for once the master question **has no answer the world will hand back.** That failure
  **costs him** (the Yonaguni cost, deepened). Far from home throughout (the humility engine — default
  now); brother/uncle register with the crew; his field-kit and tells carry per the SSOT; **he defers —
  the keeper owns each win, and at the end the *not-knowing* is owned by everyone.** *(Honour the
  tattoo-acquisition continuity; no ink unearned.)*
- **Close third, rotating among the crew** (the model) — never the keeper/inventor (seen from the crew's
  POV, out-reading them from there).

> (HBT `canon/characters/`); the crew pool (`the-unheard/canon/CHARACTERS.md`).

## 5. THE SEARL PROBLEM — the dignity contract (LOCKED: *tragic true-believer, never mocked*)

Searl is the one node with **zero defensible physics** and a **real, recently-dead (2018) man.** The book
holds him by a hard contract — this is the line's load-bearing-humanity law (Bible §4) applied to an
*adult fringe inventor* instead of an ND crew member, and it is **just as inviolable:**

1. **The narrator confirms nothing and sneers at nothing.** The SEG never works on the page; the book
   says the physics is impossible (conservation), plainly. But **the man is rendered with full dignity** —
   his **70-year obsession** is shown as *the most human thing in the book*: the need for the maybe to be
   a yes, in a man the world had already filed under "crank."
2. **The irony is tragedy, not punchline.** *Jailed for stealing the electricity he swore he made for
   free* (*The Times*, 1982/83) is the tragedy at the **centre** of him — a man so sure of a generator
   that wasn't there that he bypassed the meter feeding the bench where he was building it. Played for the
   ache, **never** the laugh. (Frik may *name* the contradiction — that's the LIE's function — but the
   *register* stays grief.)
3. **The crew member who most wants him to be right is the one who must let go (§4).** The cost of the
   climax is **internal**: not "the device is debunked" (the reader knew that at chapter one) but *"I
   needed the written-off to be right, and this one wasn't, and I have to love him anyway."* That is the
   book's deepest beat, and it is **about the crew**, earned by the descent.
4. **The real-name question (author's call, flagged).** Two clean options, both honour the contract:
   **(a)** use **Searl by name** for documentary frisson (he's public, the *Times* record is real) —
   higher dignity-bar, but the book can carry it *because* the contract above is absolute; **(b)** a
   **fictional analogue** who lives the Searl *beats* (the disc, the conviction, the orphaned Russian
   paper, the San Diego "soon") so the book can be tender without litigating a real grave. *(Default lean:
   the fictional analogue for the **inventor we get close to**, with the real Searl/Pogue/Kozyrev present
   as the **documented historical bedrock** the crew are reading — i.e. the real men are the **record**,
   the analogue is the **living keeper** we sit with. This keeps the dignity floor and the documentary
   frisson both.)*
5. **Roschin–Godin is the in-world seducer, not a payoff.** The one orphaned paper (real journal,
   unreplicated, alleged forgery) is **exactly** what a smart, hungry crew member grips — *"but there was
   this one Russian paper…"* — and it **dissolves** when gripped. Use it to dramatise the over-reach (§4),
   never to tip the narrator's hand.

> If the Searl node ever reads as "ha, what an idiot," it has failed the line (the smug-debunker sneer,
> Bible §8). If it ever reads as "…and maybe the disc *did* fly," it has failed worse (the credulous
> drone). **The only honest register is: a real man, a real need, an impossible machine, held with love
> and never confirmed.** That is the whole book in one node.

## 6. THE CHASE (the propulsion — the line's template, ported)

A **clean-handed adversary** races the crew to *take* / *tidy away* what they're racing to *read.* The
recurring *kind* (not necessarily one outfit): an **energy/extraction concern, a "heritage of science"
foundation, a patent-aggregator, or a private collector's cut-out** — buying up **the benches, the
notebooks, the orphaned patents, the last witnesses** before the heirs and the record can be protected.
They weaponise the **official story** (the rhyme with the whole line): **"they were cranks"** is the
**best cover a real suppression ever had** — and the book's hardest, truest blade is that **the cover is
usually right**, which is what makes the **once** it isn't nearly invisible. Give the adversary a **mortal
want and clean hands**, never a moustache (masterpiece-campaign doctrine). *(Strong engine for the want:
the adversary doesn't want the machines to **work** — they want the *question* gone, because the
**question** is what's worth money / what's dangerous to a portfolio / what a dead patron staked a
reputation on. They are buying **silence**, the same silence each man fell into — the adversary as the
*institutional* version of the quiet.)*

**The season-spine hook (light, optional, reverse-payoff — Bible §6):** the slate's never-confirmed
*someone-got-here-first* throughline can **touch** this book — the crew find that **someone has already
been to these benches**, bought these notebooks, *filed the same silence* across five graves on three
continents — the same **shared-hand** whisper as Marree Man's American-English clues and Newark's shared
unit. **Plant it; never confirm it's one hand.** The fish wink, at season scale. *(This is also the
natural bridge if the line opens a second season — the line pivoting from anomalous places to anomalous
people, the inventors arc.)*

## 7. THE "WHAT IS IT FOR" (the grounded turn — re-opens wonder, never closes it)

The crew can establish, per node, *who* (the man), *is-it-real* (the machine: mostly **no**, and the book
says so), and *how* (the real mechanism where there is one — Pogue's vaporiser, the Casimir geometry).
The book turns on **what it's for** — and because the machines mostly **don't work**, the answer is **not
a working function** (the slate's move) but a **human one**, grounded and physical, that **re-opens** the
wonder instead of closing it:

- **Pogue:** what the carburetor was *for* was never 200 mpg — it was a Depression-broke mechanic's bid to
  **matter**, and the **refused number** is a man protecting the one true, modest thing (the high-20s that
  was *real*) from a press that had already turned it into a lie he couldn't live up to. *The function is
  dignity.*
- **ZPE:** what the vacuum is *for* — the real wonder — is that it **isn't empty**, and the honest awe of
  *"we weighed it and still can't run a bulb off it"* is **larger** than any free-energy fantasy. *The
  function is humility before a real frontier.*
- **Kozyrev:** what the mirror was *for* was **never telepathy** — it was a man who'd lost everything to
  the camps trying to prove **time has a current**, because if it did, then nothing that fell into the
  past was truly gone. *The function is grief.* (The keeper hands the crew **that** reading; the narrator
  never declares the mirror works.)
- **Tachyon:** what the *word* was for — in real physics — is to name **a vacuum about to fall** (the
  Higgs); what the *product* is for is to **launder credibility into a sale.** Holding both is the node.
  *The function is the difference between wonder and a con.*
- **Searl:** what the disc was *for* has **no answer the world will hand back** — and that void is the
  climax. The crew (and Jakobus, whose gift fails here) are left with **only the man**, and the "what is
  it for" collapses into *what was he reaching for, and why couldn't he stop* — which the book answers not
  with a function but with **love and not-knowing.** *The function is the maybe itself, held to the last
  page.*

**The keeper hands each reading; the narrator never declares it proven.** The wonder gets **larger**, not
smaller — and in this book it is almost always **a person**, not a machine.

## 8. TONE CONTRACT (LOCKED — same as the line, with this book's particular weight)

- Play it **STRAIGHT**, like *The Why Files.* Official story first, full respect — and for the *machines*,
  the official "wrong/fraud" is often **true**, said plainly and with love for the men anyway. Then the
  hole (which is almost always **a person**, not a machine). Then the wink. Let the reader sit in the
  *maybe* — and here the *maybe* lives in **the silence and the grief**, not in "does the disc fly."
- **Pro-skeptic, pro-dreamer, pro-wonder, all at once.** The book is **harder on the machines and softer
  on the men** than any other in the line — that is its signature, and its defence against woo. **The fish
  heckles the *claims*; nobody sneers at the *people*.**
- **The ND crew is humanised, never caricatured** — the inviolable floor. **Naila does pattern and
  arithmetic, not miracles** (she's the one who holds *real-kernel-AND-impossible-claim* without
  flinching); Frik's blurt punctures **over-reach**, never a person's dignity; the warmth and the cost
  both real.
- **The inventor-as-local out-reads the crew** (§4); the **person is not a puzzle-box for the band's
  growth** (the §3 firewall, ported to human lives — a man's silence is not the crew's to solve, only to
  *witness* and hand back to his keeper); the wonder serves **the men** (and, the HBT-tourism echo made
  human, sends curiosity and dignity *toward the written-off* — the back matter could point a reader at
  the *real* patents, the *real* Slavic-Review history, the *real* Casimir measurement: *go and check; the
  record is open*).
- **This is the line's *saddest* book** — and the joy is **the crew**, the found-family of the written-off
  recognising themselves in five more written-off men. That recognition is the warmth. Have fun **in the
  van, between the graves.** This is still the cranked line. **Roll the tape.**

## 10. TITLE CANDIDATES

- ***The Quiet Men*** (the silence each fell into; plain, grounded, a little haunting — very Why Files) ·
  ***The Refused Number*** (Pogue's, and the book's — what the world wouldn't hear) · ***Run on
  Nothing*** (the free-energy promise + the Searl irony, jailed for stealing the power he ran on nothing)
  · ***The Law of the Squares*** (Searl's numerology — beautiful, ironic, names the reach that outran the
  proof) · ***What the Vacuum Owes*** (the ZPE frontier; the debt that can't be collected) · ***The Men

  true).
