# MYTHOS RULES — *The Quiet Men*

> Not a "mythos" in the *History Before Time* sense (there is **no** in-world-true mythos here, and never will be). These are the **binding rules of the maybe** for the line's one people-not-places volume: how the book keeps the checkable thing real, runs the potato engine, stays wonder-not-conspiracy, holds the §3 firewall in spirit, designates which mysteries stay open, and **never resolves the maybe in narration.** Bound by the series bible (§0.1, §2, §3, §8) and the fact-floor (`SUPPRESSED_TECH_DOSSIERS.md` §0, §F).

---

## 1. THE CHECKABLE-THING-IS-REAL RULE (the fact-floor is law)

Every "hole" the book leans on is a **genuinely-cited, real-world** unresolved or strange fact — and in this book the hole is **almost always a person, not a machine.** Binding:

- **No fact in prose may exceed the dossier.** The dossier (`SUPPRESSED_TECH_DOSSIERS.md`) is the single source of real-vs-claimed. Where it says *claimed / cannot verify / reported-not-verified*, the prose says so too (the Roschin–Godin forgery allegation; the Dikson numbers; the prison-sentence length; the Imperial Oil stock wobble).
- **The five real holes (the residue the book actually stands on):** Pogue's **four real patents** + the editor's own retraction + the **refused number**; the **measured Casimir force** (Lamoreaux ~5%, then <1%) and the fact it still won't run a bulb; Kozyrev's **documented Gulag, the gold medal, the lunar crater, and the Moon breathing** (3 Nov 1958, Alphonsus); the **once-tachyonic Higgs** and the **OPERA loose cable**; and at Searl, the **documented conviction** (*The Times*, 1982/83) and Heerfordt's *not one witness.* These are the load-bearing checkables. The two that can take any skeptic's punch: **Pogue's patents** and **the real ZPE/Casimir physics.**
- **Fringe stays labelled as fringe, in-world.** Free-energy suppression of Pogue (no evidence); "ZPE generators" (fail on conservation); "tachyon energy" products (no mechanism, no evidence, word-theft); the SEG (perpetual motion — pseudoscience, not "fringe-but-open"); torsion-field / Novosibirsk parapsychology (unreplicated). **A character may over-reach on any of these — that is a flaw to dramatise — but the narrator's verdict is always the real residue + the maybe.**
- **The maybe lives only where the real residue earns it.** It earns it on the **men** (Pogue's refusal, Kozyrev's grief, Searl's need) and on the **real frontier** (the vacuum that isn't empty). It does **not** earn it on "does the disc fly," "does the chamber heal," "can you run a bulb off the vacuum" — those are answered **no**, plainly.

---

## 2. THE POTATO ENGINE (inverted for this book)

On the slate the potato is *how hard the easy build is* — the daylight croppers making a wobbling mess. **Here the "easy" version is the dismissal**, and the potato is the moment the tidy *crank/fraud* verdict **visibly strains against the documentary residue:**

- **The potato move, per node:** state the dismissal straight and (for the machine) affirm it's usually true — *then show the residue that the dismissal can't quite cover.* Four real patents that don't vanish when you call Pogue a crank. A gold medal and a crater that don't vanish when you call Kozyrev a mystic. A force weighed to within a percent that doesn't vanish when you call ZPE woo. **The potato is the dismissal that can't quite close the file.**
- **Most chapters have a potato:** the beat where the official "wrong/fraud" is offered, taken seriously — and then strains, on the record, in one concrete, checkable detail. Land it once; do not caption it.
- **The engine's hardest truth:** the dismissal is **usually right.** The book banks that honesty (it is the defence against woo) — which is precisely what makes the **once** the dismissal doesn't fully close (the refused number, the breathing Moon) land with weight instead of as special pleading.

---

## 3. WONDER-NOT-CONSPIRACY (the adversary is human, the wonder is grounded)

- **The adversary is a fallible human want, never an omnipotent cabal** (bible §6; masterpiece-campaign doctrine). They are buying **silence** because the **question** is dangerous to a portfolio or a patron's legacy — not because the machines work. Clean hands, mortal want, a face. Never a moustache.
- **The "suppression" is the cover, and the cover is usually true.** The book never confirms a grand suppression of working tech, because (per the dossier) there is **no evidence** any working device was ever buried. The conspiracy is the *believers'* and the *adversary's weapon*, dramatised — **never the narrator's verdict.**
- **The wonder is grounded and re-opening, never woo.** The "what is it for" answers are **human and physical** (dignity, humility, grief, the difference between wonder and a con, love-and-not-knowing) — each one **re-opens** the wonder instead of closing it. The forbidden registers (bible §8) are all failure states; the two deadliest here are the **credulous drone** ("…and maybe the disc *did* fly") and the **smug sneer** ("ha, what an idiot"). Either kills the book.

---

## 4. THE §3 FIREWALL, KEPT IN SPIRIT (the documented departure)

The line's firewall (bible §3) is "the **local** out-reads the crew on their own ground; the answer is theirs to give." **Inventions have no indigenous ground.** So this book **documents a departure on purpose** (seed §1, dossier §0):

- **The marginalised inventor — or the heir / keeper of the bench / surviving witness / archivist — IS the "local."** The disbelieved tinkerer, the buried astronomer, the jailed believer is the **unheard** whose life's work the crew arrive to **read, never to seize.**
- **The keeper out-reads the crew on the person, every node.** The crew read the **pattern across five graves**; the keeper reads the **one.** The *answer* — *what was it for; what was done to them* — is **theirs to give or withhold.** The win is the keeper's. **The crew never hold more of the camera than the keeper** (the LENS makes this literal — Edith is the camera the book keeps asking to lower, here trained on a man's dignity rather than a place's).
- **A human life is not a puzzle-box for the band's growth.** A man's silence is not the crew's to solve — only to **witness and hand back.** If a node ever reads as "clever outsiders crack the dead inventor's mystery," it has failed the line and must be rebuilt.
- **The book states the departure via structure, never as a lecture** (seed §1). No character explains "the inventor is our local now." The descent *enacts* it.

---

## 5. DESIGNATED MYSTERIES (what stays open — forever)

These are **load-bearing maybes.** They are **never resolved**, by any character's certainty or by the narrator:

1. **The refused number (Pogue).** What real mileage his rig actually made — the modest true figure he protected to his death. *Open forever.* (The wink: ninety years on, the one thing nobody can produce is the one number he refused to say.)
2. **What Kozyrev's mirror was "for."** The narrator **never** declares the mirror works (it is the unverified gadget); the *keeper* hands the crew the reading that it was for **grief**, not telepathy — and even that is the keeper's gift, not a proven fact. *The maybe is the grief, held open.*
3. **The one Russian paper (Roschin–Godin).** Real journal, ±35% claimed, never replicated, alleged a forgery. It is the **in-world seducer** that **dissolves when gripped** (Tomi grips it). It **never pays off**; the narrator never tips its hand. *Open — and that openness is the cost.*
4. **What Searl's disc was "for."** The climax's void. **No answer the world will hand back.** The "what is it for" collapses into *what was he reaching for, and why couldn't he stop* — answered not with a function but with **love and not-knowing.** *The maybe itself, held to the last page.*
5. **The season-spine whisper (someone-got-here-first).** Whether one hand bought every bench and filed the same silence across five graves. **Plant; never confirm it's one hand.** *A maybe at season scale.*

---

## 6. THE MAYBE IS NEVER RESOLVED IN NARRATION (the discipline that is the book's grief)

- **The narrator confirms nothing and sneers at nothing.** No machine works on the page. The physics-impossible ones are stated impossible, plainly (conservation; the ground state has no lower rung; "tachyon energy" has no mechanism). But the narrator **never** says a man was a fraud-and-that's-all, and **never** says a man was right.
- **Over-reach belongs to characters, never the narrator.** A true-believer, a grieving keeper, or a crew member (Tomi) may reach past the evidence — and the book lets the reader **see** the reach (Frik's blurt names it; the paper dissolves). The narrator stays cold-eyed on the residue and warm on the person.
- **The Searl contract is the inviolable case** (seed §5): *if the Searl node reads as "ha, what an idiot," it has failed the line; if it reads as "…and maybe the disc did fly," it has failed worse. The only honest register is: a real man, a real need, an impossible machine, held with love and never confirmed.*
- **The discipline is the subject, not a tic** (dossier §0). The never-confirm is not a rule the book obeys; it is **the grief the book is about** — five men who needed the maybe to be a yes, and a crew who need it too, and a narrator who will not give it to any of them, because giving it would be a lie, and the lie is what was done to the men in the first place.

> **One-line test for any draft beat:** *Does it let the reader sit in the maybe (pass), or does it hand them a verdict the maybe shouldn't carry (fail)?* And: *Is the machine still a hole, the man still held with dignity, and the narrator still silent on whether he was right?* If yes, roll the tape.
