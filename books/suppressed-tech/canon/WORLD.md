## 2. NODE 1 — POGUE (the documentable floor)

- **Real bedrock (public, holdable).** **Charles Nelson Pogue** (b. 15 Sep 1897, d. 1985), self-taught Canadian mechanic, Winnipeg. **Four real US patents** — **US 1,750,354 / 1,938,497 / 1,997,497 / 2,026,798** (1930–36), readable on Google Patents — describing a genuine **exhaust-heated vaporizing carburetor** (air bubbled through liquid fuel, atomising nozzles, a heating chamber warmed by the engine's own exhaust). ⚠ The fringe typo **"1,759,354" is wrong** — it is 1,750,354; never repeat the typo.
- **The hole (genuine, unfilled).** The mileage was **never independently, instrumentally verified.** Press said **200 mpg** (a Ford V8, Winnipeg, 1933); a Winnipeg→Vancouver run "on 14.5 gallons." The **originating editor — R. Pepper, *Canadian Automotive Trade*** — later called the report **"entirely misleading."** And in **1953 Pogue himself denied claiming 200 mpg "or even half that"** — and **refused to name the real figure.**
- **The physics line (the seam).** A vapor rig nudging a thirsty 1930s V8 into the **high-20s mpg is physically plausible**; **200 / 130 / even a doubling is thermodynamically impossible by a carburetor alone** (a gallon holds a fixed ~33.4 kWh; a carburetor only *mixes*; modern combustion already exceeds 97%). The believable kernel and the impossible legend **share a root** — that is the seam to write on.
- **Fringe, labelled.** Oil/auto-industry suppression: **no evidence** (Wikipedia, Snopes, Straight Dope concur). Pogue's own 1953 claims of break-ins and threats: **uncorroborated** — a character's mouth, never the narrator's.
- **The keeper.** A fictional **heir / archivist of the Pogue papers** (a granddaughter, an estate solicitor's box, a small-museum volunteer who has the patent folders) — out-reads the crew on *who Pogue was* and *why he refused the number.* The "what is it for" she gives: **dignity** — a broke mechanic protecting the one true, modest thing (the real high-20s) from a press that had turned it into a lie he couldn't live up to.
- **The ground.** A Winnipeg lock-up / a prairie museum back room; the four patent documents on a reading-room table; a 1936 newspaper on microfiche; the rumoured oil-filter business — the "200-mpg man" selling oil filters (true-enough to ache, thinly sourced — flag it as such in-world).

## 3. NODE 2 — ZERO-POINT ENERGY (the real frontier)

- **Real bedrock (textbook; state it straight, with love).** ZPE is the lowest possible energy of a quantum system, and it is **not zero** — the harmonic oscillator sits at **½ℏω**, forced nonzero by the **uncertainty principle.** Empty space is a seething background of fluctuations, **not** a true void. No controversy.
- **The measured wonder (the hole that's a triumph).** The **Casimir effect** — two uncharged plates in vacuum attract because the plates exclude vacuum modes (force/area ∝ 1/a⁴). **Measured:** **Lamoreaux (1997, *Phys. Rev. Lett.*)** to ~5%; **Mohideen & Roy (1998)** to <1%. **The vacuum is real and it pushes.**
- **The crux (the exact real/fringe line).** You **cannot** run a lightbulb off it: it is the **ground state** — no lower rung to fall to, no gradient to exploit. The one honest exception — **Forward's "Casimir battery" (1984)** — is **one-shot**, can't be cycled, and pays out at **bond-energy scale (~milli-eV).** Mechanical work *once*, in sub-micron geometries: yes. Cyclic free energy: no.
- **The irony to exploit.** The establishment's *real* embarrassment is the **opposite** of the fringe's: the **cosmological constant problem** — theory predicts vacuum energy **~120 orders of magnitude too LARGE** ("the worst theoretical prediction in the history of physics"). They aren't hiding the vacuum's energy; they're embarrassed there's far too much of it in theory and nobody knows where it went.
- **The legitimate edge (a character can stand here, unimpeachably).** **DARPA's Casimir Effect Enhancement program (2008–~2010)** — real, peer-reviewed, explicitly **not** free energy (stiction; frictionless MEMS bearings). The deflating on-record quotes are gold: **Hong Tang (Yale): "a vacuum is a vacuum"**; **Marc Millis (NASA): "clever ways of broaching some really hard questions," not "good energy schemes."** NASA's **Breakthrough Propulsion Physics** (1996–2002) concluded **"no breakthroughs appear imminent."** The **dynamical Casimir effect** — real photons coaxed from the vacuum (Wilson et al., *Nature* 2011, Chalmers) — is real, *but you pay for every photon.*
- **The keeper.** This node's "local" is the **living physics itself** and the people who hold it honestly — a fictional **working physicist / a DARPA-or-NASA-adjacent engineer** (in the lineage of the real Tang/Millis quotes) who out-reads the crew on *the real wonder vs. the word's abuse*, and a fringe **"ZPE generator" seller** as the foil. The "what is it for": **humility before a real frontier** — *we weighed the vacuum and still can't run a bulb off it*, which is larger than any free-energy fantasy.
- **The ground.** A university basement lab; a torsion-pendulum rig under fluorescent light; a maker-faire booth selling an "over-unity box"; the gap between the two.

## 4. NODE 3 — KOZYREV (the true grief — the novelistic heart)

> **Source-tier warning (binding, from the dossier).** Tier 1 — **Kozyrev the man and astronomer — is genuinely well-documented** (peer-reviewed *Slavic Review*; Britannica; contemporary *NYT/Science*; modern lunar literature). Tier 2 — **the Mirror and the Novosibirsk experiments — is thin and fringe-dominated.** Treat every Tier-2 claim as *claimed.* Lean on Tier 1.

- **Real bedrock / the gold (Tier 1).** **Nikolai Aleksandrovich Kozyrev** (1908–1983), respected Soviet astrophysicist (Pulkovo). **The Gulag is fact, and worse than the short version:** swept up in the **1936–37 purge of Soviet astronomers** (McCutcheon, *Slavic Review* 50(1), 1991 — at Pulkovo, 13 of 28 senior staff vanished; colleagues Numerov and Gerasimovich were **killed**). Arrested Nov 1936, **10 years**; Norilsk labor camps; a **second 10-year sentence added Jan 1941** *while already imprisoned*; released Dec 1946. **Named in Solzhenitsyn's *Gulag Archipelago*** as a scientist who did theoretical physics in his head, in the camps. **Gold medal (International Academy of Astronautics, 1969); a lunar crater Kozyrev** and **asteroid 2536** bear his name.
- **The hole (the Moon breathing).** **3 Nov 1958**, Crimean Astrophysical Observatory: Kozyrev obtained **spectrograms** of crater **Alphonsus** he read as gas outgassing — the **first spectroscopic catch of a transient lunar phenomenon.** Reception split (the *NYT* "incontrovertible"; Kuiper: genuine images, flawed analysis). **Honest modern status:** the *volcanic* reading was wrong, **but the phenomenon class is now respectable** (Alphonsus is a recognised outgassing/TLP site). **He really did catch the Moon breathing — he just misread the breath as fire.** (Do not overclaim as full vindication.)
- **His theory (real that he proposed it; NOT accepted physics).** **"Causal mechanics"** treated **time as an active physical entity** with a flow, a direction, a fluctuating "density" — and (his real blind spot) held that **stars are powered by "the energy of time," not fusion** (he refused to accept fusion). **Publicly attacked in *Pravda* (1959) by Nobel laureate Igor Tamm**; a **USSR Academy commission (Jan 1960)** found it lacked rigour; **never replicated.** State it as *what he claimed.*
- **The Mirror — the crucial attribution (get it right).** **Kozyrev did NOT build "the Kozyrev Mirror."** The aluminium spiral was a **1990s Novosibirsk** invention — **Vlail Kaznacheev**, Institute of Clinical and Experimental Medicine, **Russian patent RU2122446 (1998)** — that **borrowed his name in homage.** The man's theory (1950s–70s) ≠ the apparatus (1990s). The Novosibirsk parapsychology experiments (Trofimov; ISRICA; the Dikson "global experiments") are **fringe, unreplicated, not peer-reviewed**; the English origin is the popular 1970 paperback *Psychic Discoveries Behind the Iron Curtain.* **Present no mirror result as established.**
- **The keeper.** A fictional **heir / archivist of the man** — or a **keeper of the Novosibirsk legacy** who has the institute's papers and the truth of the attribution — who **out-reads the crew on grief, not telepathy.** The "what is it for" she gives: the mirror was **never** for telepathy; it was a man who lost everything to the camps trying to prove **time has a current**, because if it did, **nothing that fell into the past was truly gone.** *The function is grief.* (The keeper hands the crew that reading; the narrator never declares the mirror works.)
- **The ground.** A Siberian institute with the heating off; a box of the man's papers and the gold medal; the aluminium spiral standing in a cold room like a question; the *Slavic Review* article on a laptop screen in the van.
- **Cannot verify (say so plainly).** Dikson outcome numbers; internal legal details of Kozyrev's case; the "½ billion roubles" figure (single popular source).

## 5. NODE 4 — TACHYON (the laundered word)

- **Real bedrock / the real physics (wondrous, genuinely strange).** A **tachyon** is a *hypothetical* faster-than-light particle (Feinberg, 1967; earlier Bilaniuk–Deshpande–Sudarshan) with **imaginary rest mass**; **never detected** (decades of searches, the LHC included). The causality scandal is real: a faster-than-light signal lets you message your own past (the **"tachyonic antitelephone"**; Benford, Book & Newcomb 1970: it happens *"if and only if it does not take place — a genuine paradox"*).
- **The under-appreciated hole (the reframing, the wonder).** In modern QFT a **"tachyon" is the quantum of a field with negative mass-squared — which signals an *unstable vacuum*, not a fast particle.** The field rolls down off a hilltop (**tachyon condensation**) and its quanta become ordinary positive-mass particles. **The Higgs field was technically tachyonic** before electroweak symmetry breaking — *that is the Higgs mechanism.* "Tachyonic" now mostly means **this vacuum is about to fall over**, not *this thing outruns light.* More interesting than the sci-fi version, and entirely defensible.
- **The true cautionary tale (the best real anecdote — it checks out precisely).** **OPERA (Sept 2011):** muon neutrinos reported arriving **~60 ns early** over **730 km (CERN→Gran Sasso)**, at **six-sigma.** The community didn't buy it. **Feb–Mar 2012:** the cause was **a loose fiber-optic cable** (plus a clock-oscillator effect); ICARUS measured light-speed; spokesperson **Ereditato** and coordinator **Autiero resigned**; CERN confirmed consistency with light. *A six-sigma faster-than-light headline, hunted down, traced to a plug that wasn't screwed in all the way.* Self-correction worked — and cost two careers their leadership.
- **The fringe (unambiguous: pseudoscience, word-theft).** A New Age **wellness industry** stole the word: **David Wagner & Gabriel Cousens, *Tachyon Energy* (1999)**; Wagner's **Advanced Tachyon Technologies (ATTI, 1990)** and its "Tachyonization" process. **"Tachyonized" pendants, water, belts, shoe inserts, chambers.** The verdict, plainly: **no relationship to the hypothetical particle, no evidence it exists, never tested, no plausible mechanism.** A marketing term, full stop. The tell to dramatise: the **exact moment** "tachyon" stops meaning *vacuum instability* and starts meaning *a thing you can buy that heals you.* The honest residue on the healing side is **zero**; the villainy is **vocabulary theft.**
- **The keeper.** Two-sided: a fictional **physicist / science communicator** who out-reads the crew on the real wonder (and tells the OPERA story right), and — crucially — the **con-artist or true-believer running the "tachyon" booth/chamber**, whom **Yusuf (the MONEY/fixer) out-reads from the inside**, because he used to run grifts exactly like it. The "what is it for": the **difference between wonder and a con** — the word, in real physics, names a vacuum about to fall; in the product, it launders credibility into a sale. Holding both is the node.
- **The ground.** A wellness expo with a "tachyon chamber" you lie inside for 40 minutes; a physics lecture hall; the fiber-optic cable story told over a service-station coffee; the receipt for the pendant.

## 6. NODE 5 — SEARL (the indefensible — the climax)

> ⚠ **NOT "fringe-but-open" like Yonaguni.** The conservation objection is decisive: the SEG is a perpetual-motion / overunity claim — **pseudoscience.** No independent, controlled, reproduced demonstration in **60+ years.** The book uses Searl **because** there is no defensible physical residue. Rendering: **tragic true-believer, NEVER mocked** (LOCKED — seed §5). The narrator confirms **nothing** and sneers at **nothing.**

- **Real bedrock / the documented man (the residue is *human*).** **John Roy Robert Searl** (b. 2 May 1932, Wantage; d. 4 Dec 2018, aged 86). RAF ~1950–53; electric-motor-rewinding trade background. Ran **SISRC** in the UK; from ~2014 partnered with **Fernando Morris** in San Diego (**SEG Magnetics, Inc.**, 2016, still "about to" productise). A real **~70-year obsession** (a 1946 "dream" asserted to his death). The company and the followers are real; the working product is not.
- **The documented, lethal fact (it cuts both ways).** Searl was **convicted of abstracting (stealing) electricity by bypassing his domestic meter**, in a "vendetta" against the electricity board that escalated to a custodial sentence. Anchors: ***The Times*, 26 Nov 1982** ("Electricity thief risked blackout of the South"); ***The Times*, 5 Jan 1983** ("Engineer jailed for vendetta"). **The man who swore he had built a generator that ran on nothing was sent to prison for stealing electricity from the mains.** A documented fact, not a smear. **In the book's register this is the tragedy at the centre of the man — never the punchline.**
- **The device (all CLAIMED; none demonstrated).** Concentric **fixed magnetic rings** with **magnetised rollers** orbiting ~1–2 mm clear (frictionless); a materials stack magnetised per the **"Law of the Squares"** — **magic-square numerology with ZERO standing in electromagnetism.** Claimed chain: rollers self-accelerate → over-unity output → weight loss / anti-gravity → the **Inverse-G-Vehicle / "levity discs"** (50+ craft claimed; the flagship ~6-ft disc that "flew/levitated ~1968" and craft "lost during testing," i.e. flew away). **No photo or video of the 1968 flight exists.** (The viral "2175 mph" stories are unsourced blog myth — do not dignify them.)
- **The evidence problem — the precise answer is NO.** No working device, ever, under independent controlled conditions. **Heerfordt's 1991 investigation** could **not find a single witness** who had seen a SEG or IGV operate; found discs **hung on wires for photos**; documented **fake composite photos.**
- **The one orphaned "validation" (the in-world seducer — and its fatal flaw).** **Roschin & Godin**, ***Technical Physics Letters*, 2000 (vol. 26, pp. 1105–1107)** — a real, minor, peer-reviewed Russian journal — claimed a one-ring "Magnetic Energy Converter" showed **weight change up to ±35%**, self-acceleration, ~6–7 kW. **Never independently replicated**; the device gone; **in 2004 the paper was publicly alleged to be a forgery** (the journal reportedly apologised — *reported, not re-verified*). **A single, unreplicated, ~25-year-old paper of disputed authenticity** — *real enough to seduce a smart character, hollow enough that it never pays off.* The book's perfect *"but there was this one Russian paper…"*
- **The "suppression" narrative — self-reported, undercut by its own record.** What is documented is a **prosecution** (stealing electricity), **not a suppression**; the motive ("to bury free energy") is purely Searl's assertion. No documentary evidence any agency confiscated a *working* generator — because none independently existed. Label as self-reported.
- **The keeper (the book's heart — a fictional analogue).** The living keeper here is **a fictional Searl-analogue believer** — an aging inventor or an apprentice/heir who lives the Searl *beats* (the disc, the conviction, the orphaned Russian paper, the San Diego "soon") — so the book can be tender without litigating a real grave (seed §5.4). The real Searl is the **record**; this keeper is the **person we sit with.** The "what is it for" has **no answer the world will hand back** — and that void is the climax. The keeper, out-reading the crew on the *man*, hands them not a function but the only true thing: *what he was reaching for, and why he couldn't stop.* The answer is **love and not-knowing.**
- **Where Jakobus's gift fails.** He reads the *made thing* all book. Here **there is no made thing to read** — only a man and a belief. For the first time in the line, the master question has no answer, and the failure **costs him** (the Yonaguni cost, deepened).
- **The ground.** A San Diego industrial park unit with a half-built ring on a bench; a roller-door storage lock-up of magnetised parts and ring-binders of the Law of the Squares; the *Times* clippings; the one Russian paper printed out and underlined.
