# CHARACTERS — *The Quiet Men*

> The travelling ND crew (≥1 ND lead, load-bearing, never caricature; the LENS recurs), **Jakobus** (the road, no POV), the **keeper(s)** who out-read the crew and own the answer (the §3 departure: the inventor or the heir/keeper IS the local), and the **clean-handed adversary.** Voices, wiring-not-wounds, arcs.
>
> **SSOTs (load, never re-derive):** the crew pool — `the-unheard/canon/CHARACTERS.md` (provisional names set in drafting: **Frik / Naila / Bashir / Mira / Yusuf / Edith**); Jakobus + the Beast — `history-before-time/canon/characters/`. Every name and neurology is **PROVISIONAL**, drawn to be real and dignified, **subject to the lived-experience + in-culture sensitivity read that is the FLOOR** (bible §4.7; lived-experience consultants have a real veto, per book).
>
> **THE LOAD-BEARING LAW (inviolable, bible §4 / Unheard §0):** the neurology is rendered FROM THE INSIDE — real texture, real cost, real dignity — **humanised, NEVER caricatured.** No quirk for the audience's comfort; no meltdown as a thrill; no savant magic; no inspiration close-up; never comic relief *at the neurology's expense.* Each travels for a **competence** and a **want**, never as a broken person seeking a cure. **No one is here to be healed.** **And in this book that same law extends to the five inventors** — the Searl contract (§5 below) is the humanity law applied to an adult fringe inventor, just as inviolable.

## 0. THE CLOSE-THIRD RULE (stated, binding)

**Close third, rotating among the crew — never the keeper, never the inventor, never Jakobus.** The keeper and the dead inventors are **always seen from the crew's POV, out-reading the crew from there**; the reader never enters the inventor's head — we meet each man through the one who kept or loved him. POV-of-the-chapter goes to whichever crew layer leads that node (the casting grid below). The book sits **later in the line** — the found-family is **established**; the warmth and the cover-each-other's-blind-spots ensemble work are *banked*, not re-earned here.

## 2. NAILA — THE SYSTEM (the potato-prover) — *ND lead, load-bearing*

- **Wiring (how she sees, not a wound):** autistic pattern-cognition (the *Priya lineage*, distinct from Priya — lead-heritage rule). She reads the **shape of a system at a glance**: the four patents as a real engineering progression, not a fraud's prop; the Casimir measurement as a clean result; the *too-clean ledger* of who is buying the benches. **Her gift in this book is refusing the false binary** — it is not "fraud" *or* "suppressed"; the truth is stranger and sadder, and she is the one person who can hold both halves without flinching.
- **Voice:** precise, literal, economical; warmth shown sideways (a number laid down like a gift, a correction that is actually a kindness). She does pattern and arithmetic — **never miracles.**
- **Cost (real, never sanitised):** drowns in crowds with no exits (the wellness expo at Node 4 is physically punishing for her); the **relief of a clean number** in a world of noise is her oxygen — which is exactly what makes the **refused** Pogue number, and the *un-replicable* Roschin–Godin paper, ache for her: the world keeps handing her holes where a number should be.
- **Arc across the descent:** she begins on solid ground (a patent she can hold) and is walked, node by node, toward the place where there is **no number to check** (Searl) — and her arc is **holding the line of rigour while loving the men anyway.** She is the crew's conscience against the credulous drone: she will not let a maybe become a yes. ⚠ lived-experience veto.

## 3. TOMI KELLER — THE WOUND (new ND draw) — *the descent's emotional engine*

- **Wiring (how he sees):** ADHD + a maker's compulsion — the kind of mind that has built and abandoned forty machines in a garage, that **sees the elegant version of every device instantly** and falls for it just as fast. Provisional; lived-experience read decides the exact profile. **His wiring is why he's brilliant in a lock-up and why he needs the written-off to be right** — never a deficit, never a thing to heal.
- **Why he travels (competence + want):** the competence — he can **rebuild a man's bench from the parts and the notebooks**, get a dead rig to at least *turn*, read the maker's intent in the solder. The want — **he needs the maybe to be a yes.** The world filed him under unhirable; if five disbelieved men can be *right*, then his whole life isn't a series of abandoned garages. He is the reader's surrogate for the credulous pull, given a body and a dignity.
- **Voice:** fast, funny, over-committed, leaps three steps ahead; the one who says *"but if you just—"* and is already reaching for the soldering iron. Warm, generous, infuriating, loved.
- **Arc (the book's deepest beat):** he is the one who grips the **Roschin–Godin paper** — *"but there was this one Russian paper…"* — and the one it dissolves under. At **Searl**, where the gift fails and the void opens, **Tomi is the one who has to let go**: not "the device is debunked" (the reader knew that at chapter one) but *"I needed the written-off to be right, and this one wasn't, and I have to love him anyway."* That is the climax's internal cost, and it is about the crew. ⚠ lived-experience veto.

> **Frik's blade lands on Tomi at least once** (the LIE puncturing the over-reach of a *crew member*, gently and devastatingly) — the moment Tomi reaches past the evidence and Frik's involuntary blurt names it before anyone can soften it. This is the crew covering each other's blind spots, and it hurts because it's true. Never played for the laugh; the register is love.

## 4. JAKOBUS — THE ROAD (no POV; the gift that fails)

*(SSOT: `history-before-time/canon/characters/JAKOBUS_SWART.md` — do not re-derive; all series-wide tells carry.)*

- **Role (LOCKED):** the **road, never the lead.** No POV; seen only from outside; **off home ground the entire book** (the humility engine — far from his African mastery, among Canadian prairie, Siberian cold, California sprawl). He **gathered this crew** and travels with or ahead of them; **brother / uncle** register (default; no romance unless a later book earns its own).
- **His gift here:** he reads the **made thing** all book — Pogue's exhaust-heated vaporiser (real engineering, and he says so, with respect), the lattice of a magnetic ring, the spiral of an aluminium mirror — asking *not who, not is-it-real, but **what is it for.*** Where there's real making (Pogue's vaporiser, the Casimir geometry) his read is sure and grounding.
- **The failure (the climax — the Yonaguni cost, deepened to a whole book):** at **Searl** there is **no made thing to read** — only a man and a belief. For the first time in the line, the master question **has no answer the world will hand back**, and Jakobus's gift **fails.** He cannot read what was never made. The failure **costs him** — and the cost is part of how the book hands the *not-knowing* to everyone, not just to Tomi.
- **Tells that carry (per the SSOT, used sparingly, iceberg never exposition):** the shades / uniform-rule; the **no-gun rule**; ***Sawubona*** (a courtesy handful of each keeper's own register — even the dead man's idiom, the trade-shop English of a 1930s mechanic, the camp-Russian of a Gulag survivor's daughter); the field kit (the cayenne tin, the always-clean hanky, the Beast's weeping water bag); **Stoffel the honey-badger as carried legend**; music-as-metronome; the **giving doctrine** (he feeds people without thinking); coins-for-knives. **Honour the tattoo-acquisition continuity — no ink shown unearned.** The Beast shipped or sourced per leg; that he's far from home with it is itself a recurring beat.
- **He defers.** The keeper owns each win; at the end, the **not-knowing is owned by everyone.** He carries, stands guard, reads the made thing into the keeper's waiting hand — and at Searl, for once, has nothing to put there. *(Reverse-payoff, optional: no camera in the book ever gets a clean frame of him — Edith's lens included; the man no frame can hold, in the book most about framing.)*

## 5. THE KEEPERS — the §3 departure (the inventor / heir / keeper IS the local)

**Inventions have no indigenous ground**, so the marginalised inventor — or, where he is dead (most are), the **heir / keeper of the bench / surviving witness / archivist** — **is the "local"**, the *unheard* whose life's work the crew arrive to **read, never to seize.** The crew read the **pattern across five graves**; the keeper reads the **one person.** The *answer* — **what was it for; what was done to them** — is **theirs to give or withhold.** The win is the keeper's; the crew never own the place, and here "the place" is a human life.

**Real men = documented bedrock; the living keeper at each node is a fictional analogue** (seed §5.4), so the crew read the real record and *sit with* an invented keeper where the book needs tenderness.

- **Node 1 — Pogue's keeper:** a fictional **heir / archivist of the Pogue papers** (granddaughter / estate / small-museum volunteer) with the patent folders. Out-reads the crew on *why he refused the number.* The answer she gives: **dignity** — a broke mechanic guarding the one true modest thing from a press that had made it a lie he couldn't live up to. **Withholds, then gives** (the turn: she lets them read the file only once she trusts they won't sensationalise him).
- **Node 2 — ZPE's keeper:** the **living physics**, embodied in a fictional **working physicist / Casimir-experiment hand** (the Tang/Millis lineage) who out-reads the crew on *real wonder vs. the word's abuse* — and a fringe **"ZPE box" seller** as the foil. The answer: **humility before a real frontier.** (Here the "keeper" defends the *truth*; the gift is being told the wonder is the truth, not the claim.)
- **Node 3 — Kozyrev's keeper (the heart):** a fictional **heir / archivist of the man** (or a keeper of the Novosibirsk legacy who knows the truth of the attribution). Out-reads the crew on **grief, not telepathy.** The answer she gives: the mirror was **never** for telepathy; it was a man who lost everything to the camps trying to prove **time has a current** — *because if it did, nothing that fell into the past was truly gone.* **The function is grief.** (She hands them that reading; the narrator never declares the mirror works.) She gets the crucial attribution *right* and out-reads the crew on it — **Kozyrev did not build the mirror** — which is itself the keeper out-reading the pattern-readers on the person.
- **Node 4 — Tachyon's keeper:** two-sided — a fictional **physicist/communicator** (the real wonder, the OPERA story) and the **booth-running con-artist or true-believer** whom **Yusuf out-reads from the inside.** The answer: **the difference between wonder and a con.**
- **Node 5 — Searl's keeper (THE CLIMAX):** a fictional **Searl-analogue believer** — aging inventor / apprentice / heir living the Searl beats (the disc, the conviction, the orphaned Russian paper, the San Diego "soon"). Out-reads the crew **on the man.** The answer has **no function the world will hand back** — only *what he was reaching for, and why he couldn't stop.* **The keeper gives the crew the man, not the machine** — and the turn is that this gift is *enough*, even as the proof is nothing. **This is where the win is most emphatically the keeper's, and the not-knowing becomes everyone's.**

> **The keeper is never a puzzle-box for the crew's growth** (the firewall in spirit, ported to human lives). A man's silence is not the crew's to solve — only to **witness and hand back** to his keeper. If a node ever reads as "clever outsiders crack the dead inventor," it has failed (bible §3).

## 6. THE FIVE INVENTORS (the documented bedrock — held with the Searl contract)

The real men are present as the **record** the crew read (public, cited). They are not POV and not "keepers" we sit with — but they are **rendered with the full dignity floor.** Compressed (full facts in WORLD / the dossier):

- **Charles Nelson Pogue** — the broke Depression mechanic with four real patents who **refused the number** and (reportedly) sold oil filters. Held as a man protecting a modest truth from a lie the press made of it.
- **Nikolai Kozyrev** — the gold-medalled astronomer the Gulag buried, who **caught the Moon breathing** and reached for time itself, refusing fusion. The true grief at the book's heart. Never a mystic-punchline; a brilliant, grief-shaped, post-camp mind.
- **The ZPE physicists (Casimir, Lamoreaux, Forward, Tang, Millis)** — real, honest, openly studying the vacuum and openly saying it won't run a bulb. Held with respect; the wonder is theirs.
- **The OPERA team / Ereditato & Autiero** — real scientists who chased a six-sigma ghost, found a loose cable, and **resigned.** Held as science policing itself, at a human cost — not as fools.
- **John Searl** — the real, recently-dead (2018) man with the 70-year obsession and the conviction for stealing electricity. The **documented bedrock** behind the fictional keeper we sit with. **The Searl contract governs every appearance** (§5 of the seed): *tragic true-believer, NEVER mocked; the irony is tragedy, not punchline; the narrator confirms nothing and sneers at nothing.*

## 7. THE ADVERSARY — clean-handed, mortal want, never a moustache

- **The kind (not necessarily one outfit):** an **energy/extraction concern, a "heritage of science" foundation, a patent-aggregator, or a private collector's cut-out** — buying up **the benches, the notebooks, the orphaned patents, the last witnesses** before the heirs and the record can be protected.
- **The want (clean and mortal):** they don't want the machines to **work** — they want the **question gone.** The question is what's dangerous to a portfolio, or what a dead patron staked a reputation on, or what a sale depends on never being asked. **They are buying silence — the institutional version of the quiet each man fell into.** The adversary is the *systemic* form of the book's grief.
- **The weapon (the line's rhyme):** they **weaponise the official story.** *"They were cranks"* is the **best cover a real suppression ever had** — and the book's hardest blade is that the cover is **usually right**, which is what makes the **once** it isn't nearly invisible. The adversary doesn't have to lie; they only have to be **right four times out of five** and confident the fifth won't be noticed.
- **A face, not a cabal:** a single, plausible, deniable human — a foundation officer, an acquisitions lawyer, a smooth fixer with a real reputation and a real boss — with a fallible want (a career, a debt, a patron's legacy to protect). Frik may **blurt the adversary's lie** at the worst moment (the LIE's function). Never omnipotent; never cartoon.
- **Season-spine whisper (light, optional, reverse-payoff — bible §6):** the crew keep finding **someone has already been to these benches** — bought these notebooks, **filed the same silence across five graves on three continents.** The same *someone-got-here-first* whisper as Marree Man's American-English clues and Newark's shared unit. **Plant it; never confirm it's one hand.** The fish wink, at season scale. (This is also the natural bridge to a possible Season 2 — the line pivoting from places to people.)

## 8. THE FOUND-FAMILY WARMTH (the reason it's a show, not a thesis)

The crew is the first place each of them is **not** the problem in the room. The comedy is **real and warm** (*Reservation Dogs / We Are Lady Parts* register, never BBC-distance, never the inspiration poster): Naila laying down a number like a small precise gift; Tomi three steps ahead with a soldering iron; Frik's misfires landing (never at the neurology's expense); Yusuf reading a grifter and half-admiring the craft; Edith lowering the camera before anyone asks. They **cover each other's blind spots** — Frik blurts the lie / Naila proves it in the patents and the physics / Yusuf knows who's paying / Edith knows which frame would steal a dead man's dignity and lowers it / Tomi gets the rig to turn / Jakobus reads the made thing and stands guard. **No one of them could do it alone** — and that is also why the **keeper** completing the read is no insult to the crew: the read is **always a relay, and the last, decisive hand is the keeper's.** In the line's saddest book, **the joy is the van** — the found-family of the written-off recognising themselves in five more of the world's written-off men.
