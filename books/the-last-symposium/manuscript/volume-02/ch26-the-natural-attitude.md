# Chapter 26 — The Natural Attitude

Husserl found sugar in a bowl labelled SALT and treated it as a philosophical success.

Hume had switched the labels before breakfast to test whether the new method survived contact with tea. Kant called the experiment discourteous. Mateo called it a waste of two labels. Husserl tasted the white grains, laughed once and put the containers side by side.

“The error was not perception,” he said. “I saw the label perfectly.”

“A distinction of immense comfort to the tea,” Hume said.

By eight, the council had adopted Husserl's three columns for one session only. Noah projected them in the briefing room: what was directly presented, what was inferred or institutionally asserted, and what had to change before noon.

The first subject was the nineteen protected allocations.

Mara placed the instruments on the table. Each was a six-page document containing a person identifier, a continuity classification and a reserved-capacity token. Transfer conditions were redacted. Site assignments were not named.

“We possess places,” Marx said.

“You possess documents representing places,” Husserl corrected.

“People outside possess neither.”

“Also correct.”

Husserl asked them to suspend—not deny—the assumption that the documents referred to actual shelter capacity. The earpieces preserved *epoché* after three failed renderings: pause, abstention, withholding. A blue flag hovered beside the word on the transcript.

“We do not erase the world,” he said. “We set aside, for examination, the unearned certainty with which we take it up.”

Descartes leaned forward. “Then you begin where I began.”

“No. You seek an indubitable foundation. I ask how the world is given meaning in experience.”

“Through a mind.”

“Already you have smuggled in a box and called it mind.”

Hume covered his mouth with his cup.

The method helped. Once the protected places were treated as claims rather than underground chairs waiting in darkness, questions multiplied usefully. Who issued the tokens? Which database enforced them? Could one token be transferred? Did a token reserve food, air, floor area, medical load or merely administrative priority? Mateo drew five different failure points on the display. Léa identified a signature chain hidden in the document metadata. Descartes requested verification from each issuer instead of one consolidated assurance.

For an hour, nobody argued whether philosophers deserved to live. They investigated what *having a place* meant.

Then Elena received a message.

She read it without moving. Nia's agricultural-skills petition had been moved from pending to expired because supporting evidence was incomplete at the review cutoff. The evidence requested was a certification held by an institution closed after a government continuity seizure.

“Put it in the first column,” Husserl said gently.

Elena looked at him. “My daughter is not an appearance for your method.”

“No.”

“Her application expired.”

“That is the institutional statement.”

“It changes what doors open for her.”

“That consequence is real whether their account is just or not.”

She stood. The camera nearest the door turned to follow her.

Husserl removed his earpiece. The room's speech collapsed into several languages, none addressed to him in a form he could follow. He watched Elena's hand tighten around her wrist display, Mara shift her weight toward the console, Marx begin to rise, Samira signal him back. Gesture did not make their arguments transparent. It did make the fantasy of a neutral pause harder to maintain.

He replaced the device.

“How long does an appeal take?” he asked.

Mara said, “Normally seventy-two hours.”

“And the relevant decision?”

“The preliminary agricultural cohort locks at noon.”

“Then suspension is not equally available to everyone.”

Kant said, “A deadline cannot convert an unjust procedure into a just one.”

“No,” Mara replied. “It converts an unfinished procedure into a decision.”

The argument escaped the columns. Marx demanded a moratorium on all worker classifications. Mara said the council lacked authority over external allocation bodies. Elena demanded that Project Mnemosyne certify staff competencies it had relied upon. Mateo named twelve workers whose employment records understated their actual functions. Noah began drafting a general principle until Elena told him to stop making one delayed application represent everybody.

Husserl went to the wall and erased **WHAT CHANGES BEFORE NOON**.

In its place he wrote:

**WHO BEARS THE COST WHILE WE EXAMINE?**

“That is inference,” Descartes said.

“It is a question,” Husserl replied. “Questions disclose what our first description concealed.”

At nine-twenty, the council issued no survival principle. It made one bounded demand: Mnemosyne would immediately provide workers with certified records of the survival functions they actually performed, including work omitted by their contracts.

Mara authorised the records internally. She refused to promise the outside system would accept them.

Elena sat again.

The sugar remained in the salt bowl until Mateo switched the labels back. By then nobody trusted the names on the allocation forms quite as much as they had at breakfast.
