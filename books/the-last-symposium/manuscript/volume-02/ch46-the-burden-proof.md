# Chapter 46 — The Burden Proof

Elena arrived at council with a toolbox and placed it on the charter draft.

“Move it,” Mara said.

“Prove you can.”

The box was not locked. It held two battery packs, a respirator motor, water filters, hand tools and a coil of cable—the support kit Mateo’s team had assembled for a medically complex shelter resident. Mara could have lifted it with both hands. She understood the trap and did not touch it.

“This is theatre.”

“So are your capacity numbers. Mine has handles.”

The council had spent the morning turning Jo’s demands into a principle. Kant’s version prohibited treating bodily condition as a basis for lesser standing. Levinas objected that equal standing remained too abstract if the face before them could still be sacrificed to a total. Hume wanted language capable of surviving scarcity. Aristotle distinguished equal worth from identical provision. Marx demanded that every scarcity claim disclose the ownership and design decisions that produced it.

Gadamer tried to weave the positions together. Elena stopped him.

“Before you fuse anything, say who gets to reject the result.”

“Every participant,” he said.

“Then it never leaves the room. Try again.”

Noah displayed the actual publication chain. The council could issue an advisory principle. CERN’s executive board could attach it to the charter package. Bunker authorities could adopt, alter or ignore it. Allocation vendors could convert it into compliance language. There was no sovereign switch.

“Then the power here is interpretation after publication,” Gadamer said.

“And procurement before it,” Mateo said from the doorway.

He had returned from medical wearing no visible marker of the tremor. Samira had adjusted his work schedule. Two technicians covered his overnight calls. The staffing change appeared on the operations board as a cost increase.

Mateo opened the toolbox and assembled the respirator circuit. Without the correct adapter, the battery could not power the motor. He held up a small printed coupling worth less than the lunch being served.

“This piece changes the capacity equation. So does a second power bus. So does cross-training. So does locating medical rooms near maintenance. The model treats the bunker as finished and the body as the variable.”

“Some bunkers are finished,” Amina said.

“Then they finished making choices.”

Mara received an alert and turned her wrist away from the cameras. Elena saw the movement.

“What changed?”

“The Asterion forecast has not changed.”

“That is not what I asked.”

Three governments had moved their selection deadlines forward by forty-eight hours in response to civil unrest. Mara disclosed it after a pause long enough to become part of the answer.

Urgency entered the room like smoke.

Husserl asked them to separate the new deadline from the physical catastrophe. Nietzsche accused the states of manufacturing necessity. Hume said manufactured deadlines could still produce real closed doors. Descartes wanted confirmation from independent channels. Noah supplied two.

Mara placed a clean draft on top of Elena’s toolbox.

“We publish today.”

Elena read it. “No.”

The word was quiet.

The principle called disability a protected characteristic and required proportionate accommodation where resources permitted. It was recognisable, respectable and almost empty.

“Where resources permit means the people who withheld the resources may cite the withholding,” Elena said.

Kant agreed. Mara told him perfection was becoming complicity with delay.

“A false universal is not improved by speed,” he replied.

Nietzsche asked Jo’s cooperative to draft the first sentence. Jo refused. “We gave you conditions. Do your own work where everyone can see it.”

So they worked.

They replaced *disability* as the principle’s foundation—not because disability disappeared, but because the category allowed supposedly independent people to imagine the rule belonged to somebody else. Samira listed infancy, injury, illness, sleep, ageing, grief and ordinary reliance on food systems. Merleau-Ponty added that dependence was not merely a temporary defect in an otherwise self-contained body. Levinas resisted making ethics depend on a shared trait: the other person did not need to resemble one’s future self to command responsibility.

At eighteen ten they had three sentences:

**Bodily dependence is not an exception to human life but one of its universal conditions. No person may be assigned lesser standing because survival requires care, assistance, adaptation or association with others. Any authority claiming that inclusion is materially impossible bears the burden of disclosing its constraints, prior design choices and feasible alternatives to those affected, with independent appeal.**

Hume circled *universal*. “Every life begins dependent. Most end so. Between those points we merely become talented at forgetting.”

Nietzsche crossed out *lesser* and wrote it back in. “They will praise this as pity.”

“Let them be wrong in public,” Jo said through the speaker.

Mara asked for the vote.

Heidegger had been silent throughout. “The burden is placed on the authority that declares impossibility.”

Elena looked at him. “Yes.”

“Then I support it.”

“Your support does not clean it.”

“No.”

The council approved the text with two abstentions and no opposition. Worker representatives approved it separately, conditional on the source ledger and appeal mechanism remaining attached. The distinction appeared in the record.

At nineteen hundred Mara transmitted the principle unedited.

For eleven seconds, the communications wall showed only the upload bar.

Then three bunker systems returned automated compatibility warnings.
