# Philosophy Payloads and Research Gates

This file prevents entertaining simplification from becoming false teaching. It is not a substitute for
reading primary texts and reputable scholarship before drafting each chapter.

## Four-part accuracy gate for every philosophical scene

Before prose:

1. **Claim:** what is the strongest historically grounded version of the idea?
2. **Context:** in which work and period does it belong?
3. **Opponent:** what is the strongest objection present in the scene?
4. **Person:** could this retrieved individual plausibly know, endorse, or phrase it at the chosen date?

If any answer is uncertain, mark the scene `[RESEARCH]`; do not fill the gap with a famous quotation.

## Source hierarchy

1. Primary work in a reputable edition or translation.
2. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy / Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy for orientation and bibliography.
3. Peer-reviewed specialist scholarship and serious intellectual biography.
4. University lectures or scholarly companions.
5. Wikipedia only to model what the characters themselves see—not as the final authorial authority.

## Quotation law

- Draft dialogue in character; do not collage famous quotations.
- If an exact historical quotation is essential, verify wording, translation, date, and source.
- A translator's modern phrasing should not automatically become the earpiece's output.
- Never assign a later editor's slogan to the historical person without making the mediation explicit.

## Aristotle payload

Chapters: 1–4, 8, 16, 20–24.

Claims to carry:

- inquiry begins from the world as encountered and from distinctions among kinds;
- explanations may address material, form, source of change, and purpose without these being interchangeable;
- virtue is formed by habituated action and practical judgement, not rule possession alone;
- humans flourish in political association, but Aristotle's account includes exclusions and hierarchies;
- equal treatment and just treatment are not always identical within his framework.

Research gates:

- exact relationship among *Politics*, *Nicomachean Ethics*, household rule, citizenship, slavery, and women;
- mature Lyceum biography, family, habits, health, and likely spoken languages;
- how to dramatise four causes without anachronistically treating them as a physics competitor;
- precise historical moment that includes required works;
- the limits of “humans are political animals” in translation and context.

Red-team question: when does practical classification become naturalisation of power?

## Descartes payload

Chapters: 5–8, 13–16, 23–24.

Claims to carry:

- methodic doubt suspends claims that can be doubted to find a secure foundation;
- dreaming and deception challenge sensory warrant, not the immediate occurrence of thought;
- the cogito is an enacted certainty, not merely a syllogistic catchphrase;
- mind/body distinction is more complex than contempt for embodiment;
- a rebuilt epistemology must still explain trust in clear ideas, God, memory, and the external world.

Research gates:

- retrieval date relative to *Discourse*, *Meditations*, *Principles*, correspondence with Elisabeth;
- Descartes on embodiment, passions, medicine, animals, and automata;
- biography in the Netherlands, routines, religious commitments, and temperament;
- how translation logs interact with first-person certainty without becoming pseudo-neuroscience;
- strongest contemporary and Princess Elisabeth-style objection available within story chronology.

Red-team question: is the story rewarding paranoia because one camera system glitched?

## Hume payload

Chapters: 8–13, 20, 23–24.

Claims to carry:

- experience does not logically guarantee that the future resembles the past;
- causal necessity is not directly perceived; repeated conjunction and mental habit matter;
- scepticism can be mitigated by ordinary life rather than logically defeated;
- moral judgement depends on sentiment and shared human responsiveness, not deduction alone;
- one cannot derive an ought merely by sliding from descriptions without explanation.

Research gates:

- retrieval date and relationship between *Treatise*, *Enquiries*, essays, histories, and autobiographical tone;
- Hume's actual social style, health, food, humour, and religious reputation;
- moral sentiment versus crude emotivism;
- testimony, miracles, probability, and when these apply to the petition/forecast;
- Hume's racism and the edition/date of the infamous footnote; decide when legacy confrontation occurs.

Red-team question: does genial uncertainty become permission for the comfortable to delay?

## Kant payload

Chapters: 13–20, 24.

Claims to carry:

- autonomy is self-legislation through reason, not doing whatever one desires;
- moral worth concerns acting from duty, but outcomes are not simply invisible to practical judgement;
- humanity must be treated as an end and never merely as a means;
- universalisation tests maxims, not superficial descriptions of acts;
- public right and ethical virtue are related but not identical.

Research gates:

- exact retrieval date relative to *Groundwork*, Critiques, *Metaphysics of Morals*, political essays;
- Kant's routine without repeating embellished folklore;
- lying examples and scholarly disputes; avoid making them the whole ethics;
- hospitality, cosmopolitan right, punishment, emergency, and political legitimacy;
- Kant's writings on race, colonialism, women, and how views changed or did not change over time.

Red-team question: when does universal procedure conceal the standpoint of the rule-maker?

## Nietzsche payload

Chapters: 17–24.

Claims to carry:

- genealogy asks how values arose, whose needs they served, and what forms of life they express;
- ressentiment is not merely resentment but a value-producing reversal associated with blocked action;
- critique of pity is not a prohibition on all care;
- self-overcoming and affirmation do not equal domination of others;
- nihilism is a diagnosis and danger, not a simple programme;
- “survival” may be a lesser value than the quality and creative force of life.

Research gates:

- exact 1887 retrieval window, symptoms, medication, eyesight, diet, housing, and relationships;
- *Genealogy*, *Beyond Good and Evil*, *Gay Science*, *Zarathustra* chronology;
- reliable scholarship on sister Elisabeth's later editorial role—reserved for legacy movement;
- pity/Mitleid, rank, nobility, power, and translation choices;
- letters and biography for ordinary voice distinct from published prophetic register.

Red-team question: does provocation expose power or merely shift work onto somebody else?

## Marx payload

Chapters: 21–24.

Claims to carry:

- commodities and polished systems conceal social relations and labour;
- alienation concerns relations among worker, activity, product, others, and species-being—not boredom alone;
- class is a relation to production and power, not an income personality type;
- ideology is not merely a conscious lie; social arrangements make certain appearances seem natural;
- praxis requires collective activity, not superior interpretation by one intellectual.

Research gates:

- retrieval date relative to *Capital* Volume I, later manuscripts, political events, health and family;
- domestic reliance on Jenny Marx, daughters, Engels, and household labour—must complicate his insight;
- worker organisation and strike tactics he could plausibly know;
- distinctions between early alienation texts and mature political economy;
- Marx's journalism and harmful views, to be confronted later rather than scrubbed.

Red-team question: is Marx listening to Elena or converting her into proof of Marx?

## Catastrophe science gates

Must be resolved before any quantitative prose:

- whether a West Antarctic fictional caldera can plausibly be monitored and modelled as proposed;
- what precursors scientists can and cannot interpret;
- useful eruption magnitude range without “supervolcano” sensationalism;
- atmospheric aerosol pathways, temperature effects, crop sensitivity, and duration;
- regional variation: global catastrophe does not mean identical conditions everywhere;
- realistic shelter types, ventilation, energy, food, medicine, sanitation, agriculture, and governance;
- construction lead times and why probability creates action before certainty;
- communication risks and how a forecast changes markets and conflict even if wrong.

If the Asterion mechanism fails expert review, retain the social architecture and replace the physical
catastrophe before chapter prose. Do not bend real science merely to preserve the provisional caldera.

## CERN and retrieval gates

- fictional programme areas must not be confused with real CERN operations;
- decide whether CERN is builder, host, energy source, or political cover;
- define retrieval energy/resource cost consistently;
- define conservation, duplication, and causal-history implications enough to prevent accidental plot holes;
- decide why only one person per day and why no repeat retrieval;
- establish how bodies receive immunity, microbiome, language interface, and safe modern exposure;
- preserve metaphysical uncertainty even if engineering rules are operationally clear.

## Cultural and historical sensitivity gates

- Philosophers encounter societies profoundly shaped by colonialism, slavery, industrial capitalism, world
  wars, genocide, feminism, decolonisation, and technologies they did not know.
- Do not let the locked-down Wikipedia become an excuse for instant expertise.
- Let modern staff have intellectual authority over their own domains.
- The male-European selection is an institutional choice with consequences, not a neutral “best minds” list.
- Later expansion must not use one woman, African, Asian, Islamic, or colonised thinker as corrective decoration.
- Historical prejudice should create credible relationship and legitimacy problems without turning the
  project into a parade of gotcha moments.
