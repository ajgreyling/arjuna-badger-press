# Chapter Skeletons — Volume One: *The House of Reasons*

These are drafting cards, not prose. Scene counts may flex, but every chapter must preserve its causal
turn, philosophical payload, relationship movement, and continuity output.

## Chapter 1 — The First Animal

**POV:** Dr Mara Voss → Aristotle after retrieval  
**Time:** Day 1, 05:40–08:00  
**Locations:** control room; retrieval chamber; medical suite  
**Chapter job:** make resurrection procedural, frightening, and embodied; establish Aristotle as observer.

Scenes:

1. Mara watches final retrieval checks while governments argue remotely about public disclosure.
2. Aristotle wakes in pain, hearing translated voices before understanding the device producing them.
3. Samira establishes bodily consent one action at a time; Aristotle tests her role through questions.
4. Mara gives the catastrophe mandate. Aristotle refuses to discuss salvation before knowing the kind of
   association CERN has created.
5. He notices that everyone calls him “the first” rather than “Aristotle.”

Philosophy in action: knowledge begins from observation; humans as political animals; purpose inferred
from organised activity.  
Misconception to avoid: Aristotle instantly reciting later textbook categories.  
Relationship movement: Mara expects an adviser; Aristotle treats her as evidence.  
Turn: the examined authority becomes the examiner.  
End hook: he asks to see where he will live before he agrees to learn how the world ends.  
Continuity output: throat pain; left-wrist medical band; translation still lags on abstract nouns.

## Chapter 5 — The Second Beginning

**POV:** Descartes  
**Time:** Day 2, dawn  
**Locations:** retrieval chamber; medical suite  
**Chapter job:** make methodic doubt emotionally credible rather than theatrical.

Scenes:

1. Descartes experiences awareness before he can organise the unfamiliar sensory field.
2. Translation voices arrive without visible mouths; he suspects dream, delirium, or deception.
3. Samira lets him verify simple bodily and environmental regularities rather than arguing him into trust.
4. Aristotle introduces himself; Descartes cannot establish that the name denotes the historical man.
5. Mara presents the mandate. Descartes asks what proposition survives if every presenter is false.

Philosophy in action: staged doubt; search for an indubitable foundation.  
Misconception to avoid: “Descartes thought nothing exists.” Doubt is methodological, not casual disbelief.  
Relationship movement: Aristotle offers testimony; Descartes treats testimony as a claim needing grounds.  
Turn: resurrection turns the dream argument into a lived emergency.  
End hook: Descartes asks for solitude, then realises solitude cannot verify that he is alone.  
Continuity output: he refuses the tablet and covers the earpiece with his hand when thinking.

## Chapter 9 — A Reasonable Breakfast

**POV:** Hume

**Locations:** kitchen  
**Chapter job:** introduce Hume through appetite, humour, and ordinary inference.

Scenes:

## Chapter 24 — A Seat at the Table

**POV:** Mara → Aristotle  
**Time:** Day 6, evening–Day 7 dawn  
**Locations:** whole house; dining room  
**Chapter job:** resolve the volume question through changed participation, not ideological victory.

Scenes:

1. Non-medical service stops. Food, waste, climate controls, and translation support degrade in bounded ways.
2. Mara offers worker consultation but refuses voting standing, citing the philosophers' mandate.
3. Kant's persons-as-ends rule, Aristotle's political association, Hume's dependence on convention,
   Descartes' distrust of curated evidence, Nietzsche's attack on moralised privilege, and Marx's material
   analysis converge for different reasons on opening the council.
4. Workers enter. Elena asks the philosophers to determine their own bunker eligibility before anyone else's.
5. Aristotle yields his usual chair and brings another from the wall. No consensus follows; the room has
   become more legitimate and harder to manage.
6. The machinery starts for Husserl. Mara announces the retrieval will proceed despite the unresolved strike.

Philosophy in action: politics as shared association; legitimacy beyond expertise; ideas converging without synthesis.  
Relationship movement: Aristotle corrects an action rather than apologising abstractly; Mara loses control
but preserves the experiment by accepting the loss.  
Turn: the object of policy becomes a participant in making it.  
Final hook: Husserl's arrival will ask everyone to suspend assumptions just as they have finally chosen sides.  
Continuity output: workers have standing; service stoppage suspended, not settled; seventh room prepared.
