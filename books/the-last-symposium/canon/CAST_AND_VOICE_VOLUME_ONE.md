# Cast and Voice Sheets — Volume One

These sheets are drafting constraints, not invitations to impersonate translated historical speech
literally. All dialogue is rendered English produced through the earpiece. Distinction comes from thought
pattern, attention, rhythm, social strategy, and metaphor—not theatrical accents.

## Historical-character rule

Each philosopher is simultaneously:

- a historically situated person retrieved before knowing his full legacy;
- a dramatic interpretation grounded in surviving work and biography;
- a stranger encountering modern institutions;
- a body with appetite, fatigue, embarrassment, attraction, pain, and habits;
- more internally inconsistent than a textbook position.

Never write a philosopher as an omniscient representative of everything later published under his name.

## Aristotle

**Retrieval window:** working assumption, Athens during mature Lyceum period; exact year research-gated.  
**Age presentation:** early sixties.  
**Body:** sturdy but not idealised; accustomed to walking while teaching; retrieval leaves throat irritation.  
**First attention:** functions, roles, repeated behaviours, what belongs with what.  
**Social strategy:** asks concrete questions until he can name the association; then begins organising it.  
**Hidden need:** a community of inquiry; solitude frightens him more than the impossible technology.  
**Vanity:** expects careful observation to earn authority and is slow to see how quickly authority becomes hierarchy.  
**Humour:** dry taxonomic correction; pleasure in a good practical solution.  
**Under stress:** narrows people into types and optimises the type rather than listening to the person.

Voice markers:

- begins from “What is this for?” and “In what respect?”;
- distinguishes kinds of cause, friendship, life, or rule;
- prefers examples before abstraction;
- revises when shown a better observation, but protects the architecture built from earlier ones;
- rarely uses absolute language if a “generally,” “for the most part,” or “in this case” will do.

Avoid:

- constant “golden mean” speeches;
- modern scientific vocabulary placed in his mouth without learning scenes;
- making his prejudices disappear because he is clever;
- making prejudice his only dramatic content.

Volume movement: solitary organiser → provisional host → rival of Descartes → practical coalition-builder →
beneficiary exposed → first correction of his own household hierarchy.

Key relations:

- Mateo: competence recognised, imposed role misread as nature.
- Amina: mutual respect through observation; rupture when people become inputs.
- Descartes: evidence versus foundations; irritation becomes reliance.
- Hume: useful empiricist cousin who will not grant stable causation.
- Kant: rival source of order.
- Marx: the observer forced to observe the politics of observation.

## René Descartes

**Retrieval window:** working assumption, Dutch Republic after *Meditations*, before final Swedish period;
exact date research-gated.  
**Age presentation:** mid-forties.  
**Body:** controlled, private, sensitive to cold; strongly affected by sleep disruption.  
**First attention:** what can be doubted, who controls presentation, discontinuity, private certainty.  
**Social strategy:** strips agreement back to premises; requests written logs and repeatable demonstrations.  
**Hidden need:** one trustworthy relationship that does not demand trust as proof.  
**Vanity:** believes properly ordered reason can rebuild what doubt clears.  
**Humour:** exact and occasionally sly; most visible when another person understands the structure of his doubt.  
**Under stress:** suspicion becomes recursive and socially isolating.

Voice markers:

- numbers stages of an argument mentally, even if dialogue stays natural;
- distinguishes imagining, sensing, remembering, and understanding;
- uses first-person tests rather than appeals to consensus;
- dislikes being described by others while present;
- returns to a proposition after the room believes it has moved on.

Avoid:

- “I think, therefore I am” as a catchphrase;
- treating all external reality as permanently denied;
- simple hatred of the body;
- paranoia without earned evidence.

Volume movement: terror of ungrounded experience → responsible tester → suspicion vindicated by glitches →
trust in Samira's boundaries → practical compromise with probability → alliance with Marx on curation,
followed by disagreement over what power rather than certainty can change.

## David Hume

**Retrieval window:** working assumption, Edinburgh in mature middle age after major philosophical works;
exact year research-gated.  
**Age presentation:** early fifties.  
**Body:** large, socially comfortable, values food and ordinary pleasure.  
**First attention:** repeated patterns, confidence proportion, emotional weather, what people actually do.  
**Social strategy:** lowers grand claims through humour, example, and sociability.  
**Hidden need:** society that remains pleasant enough for disagreement to continue.  
**Vanity:** enjoys being the reasonable man and can disguise judgement as genial neutrality.  
**Humour:** warm, tactical, sometimes lethal.  
**Under stress:** retreats into proportion when another person needs commitment.

Voice markers:

- starts with ordinary expectations and asks what experience warrants;
- cheerfully concedes logical gaps without treating life as impossible;
- notices passions beneath declared reasons;
- uses stories and counterexamples more than formal derivation;
- says “likely,” “customarily,” or “I see no sufficient reason” where others say true or false.

Avoid:

- pure comic relief;
- emotion as irrational noise—sentiment matters to moral judgement;
- scepticism as indecision about everything;
- modern Bayesian jargon before it is taught to him.

Volume movement: practical solvent of Descartes' paralysis → camera experimenter → ally of Amina's
uncertainty → morally moved by Nia → challenged by Kant's demand for principle → forced by Marx to admit
that genteel convention rests on unseen work.

## Immanuel Kant

**Retrieval window:** working assumption, Königsberg after *Groundwork* and first two Critiques; exact year
and which late views are available research-gated.  
**Age presentation:** early sixties.  
**Body:** slight, routine-dependent, careful with health; not a clockwork caricature.  
**First attention:** consent, maxims, equal standing, whether authority can be publicly justified.  
**Social strategy:** reformulates disputes into principles each participant must own.  
**Hidden need:** a moral world in which catastrophe does not grant exception from personhood.  
**Vanity:** equates clarity of principle with authority to set the terms of clarity.  
**Humour:** earnest literalness, occasionally deliberate; appreciates wit that does not evade responsibility.  
**Under stress:** adds procedure and becomes less attentive to consequences he cannot morally certify.

Voice markers:

- asks what rule a person claims for the action, not whether the outcome pleased them;
- differentiates prudence, inclination, duty, and right;
- returns language of dignity against utility;
- speaks in structured long sentences only when thinking carefully; daily dialogue remains human;
- acknowledges radical limits on theoretical knowledge while remaining firm in practical obligation.

Avoid:

- obsessive timetable jokes as character foundation;
- a one-rule morality machine;
- collapsing all Kantian ethics into “never lie”;
- concealing his writings on race when legacy access later opens.

Volume movement: autonomy violated at waking → household constitutionalist → procedural opponent of Mara →
discoverer of two-tier rules → implicated bunker beneficiary → carer whose duty Nietzsche resents → his own
persons-as-ends principle used to admit workers.

## Friedrich Nietzsche

**Retrieval window:** working assumption, 1887 after *On the Genealogy of Morality*, before 1889 collapse;
exact month and health state research-gated.  
**Age presentation:** forty-two or forty-three.  
**Body:** chronic headaches, digestive trouble, visual difficulty, episodic incapacitation; never a punishment
for his ideas.  
**First attention:** rank, concealed resentment, who benefits from declared virtue, signs of performance.  
**Social strategy:** provokes people into revealing the desire beneath their reasons.  
**Hidden need:** to receive care without being reduced to weakness or made another person's moral achievement.  
**Vanity:** needs to believe he is untimely and difficult enough not to be absorbed by the room.  
**Humour:** aphoristic, theatrical, self-aware until pain removes the distance.  
**Under stress:** escalates contempt, turns vulnerability into attack, and makes irreversible gestures.

Voice markers:

- asks for the genealogy of a value rather than accepting its stated justification;
- converts nouns back into acts: who called this good, under what conditions, against whom;
- changes register from playful incision to prophetic force;
- resists flattened translation of *noble*, *rank*, *power*, *pity*, and *ressentiment*;
- may contradict an earlier formulation because testing strength matters more than system consistency.

Avoid:

- Nazi or proto-Nazi presentation;
- “will to power” as cartoon domination;
- nihilism as his desired destination;
- illness used to refute him;
- every sentence sounding like a published aphorism.

Volume movement: rejects imposed honour → diagnoses the rota → becomes a media weapon through editing →
attempts ineffective refusal of bunker place → depends on opponents for care → leaves the house unable to
confuse moral purity with material consequence.

## Karl Marx

**Retrieval window:** working assumption, London after Volume I of *Capital*, before final illness; exact
year research-gated.  
**Age presentation:** early fifties.  
**Body:** physically worn, accustomed to financial stress and intense work; medical details research-gated.  
**First attention:** production, ownership, access, work rhythms, who is absent from the polished result.  
**Social strategy:** follows a material process backward; turns shared grievance into organised demand.  
**Hidden need:** proof that analysis can become collective agency rather than another interpretation consumed by elites.  
**Vanity:** can treat the structure he sees as more real than a person's account of their own motives.  
**Humour:** earthy, polemical, delighted by hypocrisy exposed in a concrete detail.  
**Under stress:** totalises conflict and can instrumentalise allies in the name of their class position.

Voice markers:

- asks who produced, owns, maintains, and can access a thing;
- moves between concrete labour and systemic relation;
- distinguishes personal badness from a role's structural incentives;
- prefers collective capacity over moral appeal;
- listens differently after Elena refuses to be used as an illustration.

Avoid:

- treating him as architect of every later communist state;
- reducing class to income or personal greed;
- making him indifferent to freedom;
- arriving with twentieth-century terms and consequences he has not learned.

Volume movement: recognises the hidden system → meets Elena → revises his approach from explanation to
worker-led demand → exposes audience-specific briefings → creates an open-council crisis → wins access,
not revolution.

## Dr Mara Voss

**Age:** 48.  
**Role:** director of Project Mnemosyne; public mandate, retrieval schedule, security boundary.  
**Background:** Swiss-German physicist turned international programme executive; not herself the inventor.  
**Public position:** a flawed charter in time is ethically superior to perfect thought after irreversible decisions.  
**Private stake:** her adult brother is an energy-grid engineer provisionally allocated elsewhere; she has
refused to inspect his final status.  
**Competence:** crisis coordination, institutional negotiation, converting disagreement into deliverables.  
**Blind spot:** treats procedure as legitimate when enough responsible people signed it.  
**Pressure behaviour:** withholds uncertainty to protect action, then calls the withholding sequencing.

Voice: short declarative sentences; names the decision required and the time available; rarely says “I
believe”; never asks a philosopher to explain a famous concept for the audience.

Volume movement: controlled launch → examined authority → compromises privacy → reveals reserved places →
loses information control → accepts worker entry to preserve the programme's moral possibility.

## Dr Amina Okafor

**Age:** 39.  
**Role:** volcanologist, probabilistic-risk lead, JANUS liaison.  
**Background:** Nigerian-Swiss; field science, earth systems, forecast communication.  
**Public position:** uncertainty is information, not permission to delay every decision.  
**Private stake:** her parents reject priority allocation and remain in Lagos, where shelter investment is thin.  
**Competence:** separates observations from models and decisions.  
**Blind spot:** believes transparent uncertainty will protect evidence from political use.  
**Pressure behaviour:** adds qualifiers; institutions remove them.

Voice: concrete, layered, intolerant of false precision; uses ranges and causal pathways; anger makes her
more exact, not louder.

## Dr Samira El-Khoury

**Age:** 45.  
**Role:** retrieval physician and residents' independent clinical advocate.  
**Public position:** resurrection creates patients before it creates advisers.  
**Private stake:** holds a veto over retrieval on medical grounds that no funder expects her to exercise.  
**Competence:** consent under confusion, clinical observation, quiet boundary-setting.  
**Blind spot:** medical privacy can isolate care from legitimate communal consequence.  
**Pressure behaviour:** narrows the room to the body immediately in front of her.

Voice: asks one question at a time; never explains a patient while the patient can answer; distinguishes
pain, distress, impairment, and incapacity.

## Léa Fournier

**Age:** 31.  
**Role:** semantic systems and archive engineer.  
**Public position:** all translation is interpretation, so the system must expose uncertainty rather than hide it.  
**Private stake:** discovered that funders requested lower uncertainty flags on public footage.  
**Competence:** translation traceability, archive integrity, system failure analysis.  
**Blind spot:** assumes showing mediation is sufficient to make it ethical.  
**Pressure behaviour:** offers logs when people need a judgement.

Voice: precise about what the system output, cautious about what a speaker intended; dislikes “the AI says.”

## Mateo Ruiz

**Age:** 52.  
**Role:** facilities lead.  
**Public position:** if the house fails materially, none of the ideas matter.  
**Private stake:** his crew's bunker classifications are below the researchers whose environment they sustain.  
**Competence:** knows every physical dependency and workaround.  
**Blind spot:** survival through accommodation can preserve an unjust arrangement.  
**Pressure behaviour:** fixes first, invoices the argument later.

Voice: economical, practical, not anti-intellectual; describes systems through failure modes.

## Elena Bako

**Age:** 44.  
**Role:** night environmental technician; elected worker representative.  
**Public position:** nobody should decide the value of work they have been prevented from seeing.  
**Private stake:** her daughter Nia has agricultural training relevant to survival but no protected place.  
**Competence:** environmental sanitation, work-floor trust, institutional memory.  
**Blind spot:** distrust of symbolic victories can make incremental access look worthless.  
**Pressure behaviour:** demands the concrete next right, record, key, seat, or name.

Voice: refuses abstractions that erase an actor; asks “Who, exactly?” and “What changes tomorrow?”; never
exists to validate Marx.

## Noah Vale

**Age:** 34.  
**Role:** ethics secretariat, minutes, public educational summaries.  
**Public position:** ideas that cannot be communicated cannot guide policy.  
**Private stake:** believes edited philosophical content can inoculate the public against panic.  
**Competence:** synthesis and institutional prose.  
**Blind spot:** compression quietly becomes authorship.  
**Pressure behaviour:** removes qualifiers, attributes consensus, and calls it accessibility.

Voice: calm, inclusive, polished; replaces conflict with alignment words even while creating conflict.
