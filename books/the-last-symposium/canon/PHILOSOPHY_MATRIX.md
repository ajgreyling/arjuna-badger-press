## Productive alignments

- **Evidence caucus:** Aristotle, Hume, Husserl, Wittgenstein. They agree that the briefing is inadequate,
  but disagree on whether better observation, disciplined description, or conceptual therapy can fix it.
- **Duty caucus:** Kant, Aquinas, Anselm. They share moral seriousness; divide over revelation, autonomy,
  and whether species survival can override prohibition.
- **Embodiment caucus:** Merleau-Ponty, Husserl, Heidegger, Gadamer. They resist reducing survival to data,
  then fracture over method, politics, and the possibility of genuine understanding.
- **Other-first caucus:** Levinas, Camus, Gadamer, Merleau-Ponty, sometimes Kant. They oppose dehumanising
  pre-emption but cannot agree whether reciprocity or evidence matters.
- **History-and-power caucus:** Hegel, Marx, Sartre, Heidegger. They see the crisis as historically made;
  Marx attacks the others for turning material power into abstraction.
- **Immanence pair:** Spinoza and Nietzsche reject comforting transcendence, but sharply differ over calm
  understanding, power, pity, and affirmation.

## High-voltage clashes

- Plato vs Marx: rule by the knowledgeable vs ownership of the conditions that manufacture knowledge.
- Descartes vs Merleau-Ponty: mind-centred certainty vs bodily perception.
- Hume vs Anselm: evidential restraint vs reason reaching necessary existence.
- Kant vs Nietzsche: universal duty vs genealogy and self-overcoming.
- Hegel vs Camus: history's rational movement vs refusal to justify present murder by future meaning.
- Heidegger vs Levinas: ontology's priority vs ethics before ontology, sharpened by Heidegger's politics.
- Sartre vs Camus: responsibility through committed action vs limits on violence in the name of justice.
- Gadamer vs Marx: dialogue and horizon-fusion vs ideology, ownership, and asymmetrical power.
- Aristotle vs Wittgenstein: classification of stable kinds vs suspicion that language manufactures the puzzle.
- Aquinas vs Spinoza: providential personal God and created order vs God/Nature without divine personality.
