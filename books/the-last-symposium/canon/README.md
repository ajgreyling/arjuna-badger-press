## Before drafting Chapter 1

The narrative architecture is complete, but factual research is intentionally not faked. Clear these
gates first:

- choose and verify each philosopher's exact retrieval date, age, health, known works, and biographical state;
- expert-check the provisional Asterion super-eruption mechanism and consequences;
- lock what fictional relationship CERN has to Project Mnemosyne;
- establish a translation convention for proper names and untranslatable philosophical vocabulary;
- decide whether the near-future calendar year is stated or kept implicit.

Once those are cleared, Chapter 1 can be drafted directly from its card and the drafting protocol.
