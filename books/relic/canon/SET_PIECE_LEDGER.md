# SET-PIECE LEDGER — *African Gold*

> Binding canon for the quest-relay spine. The adventure analogue of *Sacred Shadows*' EVIDENCE
> DECK: where book 2's deck items were **artifacts of proof**, this book's items are **set-piece
> nodes** — each a self-contained location/sequence the protagonist enters with a *key* and
> leaves with the *next key*. **Every chapter must advance, plant, or pay off at least one node.**
> The reader should finish able to name 7–9 set-pieces and what each one *cost* and *unlocked*.
> `storygraph/setpieces.py` ingests this; the gate asserts the **key-chain is unbroken** (every
> key-out is a later key-in; no orphan keys; no locked door whose key wasn't planted earlier).

Each node carries: **location · key-in → key-out · the cost · the obstacle (physical + puzzle) ·
the read (who decodes what) · plant → payoff · escalation rank.**

> NOTE: node specifics below are the **canonical skeleton**; PLOT.md expands them into beats. Keep
> the *structure* (the shape, the key-chain, the costs, the escalation) binding; refine the
> texture during drafting. Locations and the gold-key chain are canon; flavour is flexible.

---

## THE RELAY — node shape (every node obeys this — Architecture §3.1)

```
ARRIVE → OBSTACLE → READ → COST → KEY → HANDOFF
```
- **ARRIVE / OBSTACLE / KEY / HANDOFF** = relay mechanics (propulsion; the place resists, then
  yields, then forces the next place).
- **READ** = the trilogy's brain (Priya: agent/intent · Arin: mechanism · Leila: symbol).
- **COST** = what the node *takes* (time, trust, a body, a piece, a wound) — keeps the relay
  accumulating weight, not just locations.

## THE NODES

### Node 1 — DURBAN / KZN · *the crumb that won't rest* — rank 1
- **Location:** Priya's home ground; an engineering yard / an assayer's back room.
- **Key-in:** none (the inciting find). **Key-out:** the first **worked-gold piece** that "rings
  wrong" — too pure, formed to a geometry that means *function*, not coin.
- **Obstacle:** mundane-but-tightening — the piece shouldn't exist; someone wants it back;
  records don't match. Puzzle: Priya clocks the geometry isn't decorative.
- **Read (Priya):** the agent behind the design — *someone made this to do something.*
- **Cost:** her quiet life; she's now visible to the watchers.
- **Plant → payoff:** plants the gold-as-key thread (GOLD.md) → pays off across the whole relay.
- **Handoff:** the piece's provenance points down — into the deep mine.

### Node 2 — WITWATERSRAND DEEP MINE · *depth* — rank 2
- **Location:** ~4 km down; heat, pressure, the descent register (re-skinned from *RESONANCE*).
- **Key-in:** the gold piece's provenance. **Key-out:** a **map/marker** to Vredefort + a second
  gold element; first hint of a *vitrified bore* in the deep rock.
- **Obstacle:** the mine itself (heat, a flooding working, a collapse); puzzle: the old workings
  follow something *older* than the mining.
- **Read (Arin enters / mechanism):** the bore is *made* smooth — not geology. (His *RESONANCE*
  tunnel means something now.)
- **Cost:** a near-miss that costs time and nerves; the adversary's hand first shows (a staged
  route, a watched dig).
- **Plant → payoff:** plants the vitrified-tunnel crumb's payoff (CROSSBOOK_CRUMBS) → pays at
  Node 3.
- **Handoff:** the marker forces Vredefort. **(End Act I.)**

### Node 3 — VREDEFORT IMPACT STRUCTURE · *the waveguide* — rank 3
- **Location:** the oldest scar on Earth; deep strata; the vitrified tunnel as a **waveguide**.
- **Key-in:** the mine marker. **Key-out:** a **tuning element** + the bearing to Adam's Calendar;
  proof the bore *guides vibration*.
- **Obstacle:** the tunnel (a waveguide is a death-trap if it's *driven*); puzzle: it only reads
  correctly if you treat it as acoustics, not architecture.
- **Read (Priya + Arin):** waveguide, not tunnel — *"someone made it smooth on purpose."*
- **Cost:** real danger; a piece of trust or a body; the mystery deepens (designated: the
  vitrification process is *not* explained).
- **Plant → payoff:** vitrified-tunnel crumb **pays off** here; plants "the sites ring."
- **Handoff:** the bearing forces Adam's Calendar.

### Node 4 — ADAM'S CALENDAR (Mpumalanga) · *the instrument's ear* — rank 4
- **Location:** the real stone arrangement; in-world a landscape **resonator**.
- **Key-in:** the Vredefort bearing. **Key-out:** the **decode of the tuning** (how the gold drives
  the stone) + the inheritance-trail route up Africa; **Leila enters** with the Brotherhood thread.
- **Obstacle:** the site is an instrument that *resists being misplayed*; puzzle: alignment +
  acoustics + stone properties (reached by measurement, never by citing authority — MYTHOS_RULES).
- **Read (Leila + Priya):** symbol + system — the stones are placed to *ring*; the gold is the
  string.
- **Cost:** exposure — playing the site draws the adversary; a relationship strains over *should
  we even be doing this.*
- **Plant → payoff:** plants the planetary-instrument inference → pays at Convergence.
- **Handoff:** the inheritance route forces the leg up Africa.

### Node 5 — GREAT ZIMBABWE / up-Africa leg · *inheritance* — rank 5
- **Location:** Great Zimbabwe and the gold-route cultures.
- **Key-in:** the inheritance route. **Key-out:** the **Brotherhood contact** + confirmation the
  worked gold is **scattered on purpose** (the key dispersed).
- **Obstacle:** human, not just physical — the inheritors' guardians; the adversary's network
  closing; puzzle: which "ruin" remembers the Builders and which is just a ruin.
- **Read (Leila):** the inherited memory — who kept the gold and why.
- **Cost:** the decoy/key flip begins — what they thought was a treasure economy is a custody
  system; a betrayal-by-evidence beat (their assumptions were wrong).
- **Plant → payoff:** GOLD.md Layer A/B decoy→key flip **begins**.
- **Handoff:** the Brotherhood points to Aksum / the seat.

### Node 6 — AKSUM / the Brotherhood seat · *the key revealed* — rank 6
- **Location:** the Brotherhood's seat (Leila's ground; *Sacred Shadows* carry-over).
- **Key-in:** the Brotherhood contact. **Key-out:** the **full tuning set assembled** (the relay's
  prize) + the bearing to the convergence site; the **midpoint reframe** lands.
- **Obstacle:** the order's caution (they've kept it asleep for centuries) + a radical keeper who
  wants it used; puzzle: assembling the scattered gold into one working key.
- **Read (all three):** the machine is **planet-scale**; the gold is a key to a *switch.*
- **Cost:** the order's secret is now in the open; the adversary moves; an ally pays a real price.
- **Plant → payoff:** GOLD.md decoy→key flip **completes** (reverse-payoff for book-2 readers).
- **Handoff:** the assembled key points to Egypt / the flooded Nile. **(End Act II.)**

### Node 7 — EGYPT / approach to the flooded dam · *the drowned threshold* — rank 7
- **Location:** the Aswan-adjacent reservoir; the drowned temple field.
- **Key-in:** the assembled key + bearing. **Key-out:** access to the **coupling chamber** + the
  realisation the instrument is *live-capable.*
- **Obstacle:** water — depth, visibility, pressure, the dam's own machinery; the adversary
  already staged here; puzzle: the temples are **coupling chambers**, not tombs (water as acoustic
  medium — MYTHOS_RULES Rule 3, honest physics).
- **Read (Priya + Arin):** how water + stone + gold geometry transfer energy.
- **Cost:** the most dangerous crossing yet; a near-drowning; the ancient-layer braid pulls tight.
- **Plant → payoff:** plants the convergence; the two-timeline braid begins to fuse.
- **Handoff:** the chamber forces the descent into the instrument's heart.

### Node 8 — THE COUPLING CHAMBER · *convergence* — rank 8
- **Location:** the drowned temple's heart; where the instrument can be tuned.
- **Key-in:** chamber access + the assembled gold key. **Key-out:** the **machine made whole** —
  it *can* be played; and the grounded **reveal** (purpose by effect/inference, not lecture).
- **Obstacle:** the instrument itself (a planetary resonator is lethal if mishandled); the
  adversary has leverage to force the switch; puzzle: completing the coupling *without* committing
  to switching it on.
- **Read (the Court recognises it — MYTHOS_RULES Rule 5):** the network is an instrument; here is
  what it acts on (designated mystery opens, grounded).
- **Cost:** the braid fuses at full cost; the choice becomes real and imminent; someone is in the
  adversary's hands.
- **Plant → payoff:** the planetary-instrument inference **pays off**; the braid **closes.**
- **Handoff:** the choice — Node 9. **(End Act III.)**

### Node 9 — THE CHOICE · *consent, not conquest* — rank 9 (climax) · **the threshold (Rule 7-T)**
- **Location:** the chamber / the switch.
- **Key-in:** the whole machine, tunable. **Key-out:** the instrument's **fate** — settled by
  consent over control, **once the machine reveals it answers only to consciousness**.
- **Obstacle:** the adversary's logic (someone must hold the switch) + the Brotherhood's caution
  (keep it asleep) + the physical climax — then the **turn**: the machine will not answer code, AI,
  augmentation, or the Court. The puzzle stops being technical and becomes the one thing Priya's
  tools can't solve: to operate it she must **set the tools down** and meet it as bare consciousness.
- **Read (Priya):** refuses the adversary's *logic*; the third path — understand-then-consent — and
  then the hardest read of all: that *understanding* itself (her instruments, her systems) is the
  thing she must release. The Court reasons to its own boundary and **steps aside** (pays off
  RESONANCE).
- **Cost:** the honey-badger price — Priya foregoes technology entirely at the threshold; someone
  pays; hope earned through cost, not triumph. **The mythos breaks here on purpose** (MYTHOS_RULES
  Rule 7-T) — shown, never explained.
- **Plant → payoff:** the trilogy's question **answered in action**; the consciousness-control turn
  pays off R's made-mind thread and REV's stewardship thread; sets up the epilogue's reverse-payoff
  seeds + the C-resonance relinquishing.
- **Handoff:** resolution → epilogue (the trilogy clicks shut; a chosen relinquishing of tech/AI for
  this domain).

## THE KEY-CHAIN (gate asserts this is unbroken)

```
[inciting find] → N1.gold-piece → N2.mine-marker → N3.tuning-element+bearing
   → N4.tuning-decode+route → N5.brotherhood-contact → N6.assembled-key+bearing
   → N7.chamber-access → N8.machine-whole → N9.fate
```
Every arrow is a key-out feeding the next key-in. **No orphan keys. No locked node without its key
planted in an earlier node.** A break here is a hard gate failure.

## ESCALATION (gate asserts monotonic rank + forced handoffs)

Ranks 1→9 must not regress; each node out-stakes the last; **every handoff is forced** (a closing
window, water, a pursuer, a collapse) — never "they decided to go to the next place." A node that
doesn't escalate or whose handoff is unforced is flagged.

## COST DISCIPLINE (gate-checked via setpieces ingest)

Every node has a **non-empty COST**. A node that yields its key for free (no time/trust/body/
piece/wound paid) is flagged — that's the theme-park failure mode the blueprint exists to prevent.
