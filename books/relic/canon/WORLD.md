# World — African Gold

> Binding geography, institutions, and the grounded-ancient tech. Real places used honestly
> (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 6); travel/distance/depth physically plausible (the two-layer timeline gate
> cross-checks this). The map *is* the relay: the quest runs from KwaZulu-Natal up the spine of
> the continent to Egypt, following **inheritance** (who remembered the Builders) not origin.

---

## The shape of the world

A real, present-day Earth in which a deep-antiquity engineering civilisation (**the Builders** —
MYTHOS_RULES Rule 1) left infrastructure across Southern Africa and up the continent, and in
which the inheritors — dynastic Egypt, Aksum, Great Zimbabwe, the gold-trade cultures, and a
hereditary Brotherhood — half-remember it. Nothing about the surface world is fantastical. The
extraordinary is **buried, drowned, or hidden in plain sight as "ruins."**

### Node settings (grounded; each a real or real-adjacent place)

1. **Durban / KwaZulu-Natal** — Priya's home ground. Indian-South African Durban; the harbour,
   the humidity, the engineering yards. Where the crumb finds her and won't let her rest.
2. **The Witwatersrand deep mines** — the real, deepest mines on Earth (~4 km); Archean gold
   (GOLD.md). The set-piece of *depth* — heat, pressure, the descent register carried from
   *RESONANCE*'s mine-rescue, re-skinned. The first worked-gold piece surfaces here.
3. **The Vredefort impact structure** (Free State) — the world's oldest/largest verified impact
   crater (~2 Ga). Real geology; the anomaly that ties the region's deep gold and unusual strata
   to the Builders *by inference, never assertion* (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 6). The **vitrified tunnel**
   crumb from *RESONANCE* connects here — a waveguide in the deep rock.
4. **Adam's Calendar** (Mpumalanga) — the real Tellinger-popularised stone arrangement; in-world a
   landscape-scale **acoustic resonator** ("the instrument's ear"). Reached through measurement
   (acoustics, stone properties, alignment), not by citing authority.
5. **The inheritance trail up Africa** — Great Zimbabwe; the Aksum/Ethiopia leg (Leila's ground;
   the Brotherhood's seat); the gold-route cultures. Travel here is real and costed.
6. **Egypt / the flooded Nile dam** — drowned temples beneath dam waters (an Aswan-adjacent
   invented-but-plausible reservoir): **coupling chambers** where water, stone, and gold geometry
   meet (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 3; water as acoustic medium used honestly). The convergence and climax.

> The relay's geography is itself a clue: it traces **gold and inheritance** from source
> (Witwatersrand) through the oldest scar (Vredefort) and the instrument's ear (Adam's Calendar)
> up to where the inheritors built their grandest rememberings (Egypt) — exactly the path the
> Builders' network would force you to walk to assemble the key.

## The grounded-ancient tech (what the Builders left)

All effects are acoustic/resonant/structural (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 3). The world contains:

- **Vitrified conduits / waveguides** — glass-smooth bores (the *RESONANCE* tunnel) that guide
  vibrational energy with low loss. Made smooth by a process the book treats as advanced directed
  energy and **never fully explains** (designated mystery).
- **Landscape resonators** — stone arrangements (Adam's Calendar and sisters) placed for
  vibrational properties; the sites "ring."
- **Coupling chambers** — the drowned temples; where water + stone + gold geometry transfer
  energy efficiently.
- **The worked gold** — the tuning elements / keys (GOLD.md): refined past nature, formed to
  precise geometry, surviving geological time because gold does not corrode.
- **The planetary instrument** — the inferred whole: a network of resonators that, gold-tuned and
  driven, acts on something atmospheric/geophysical. Its purpose is the Act III mystery.

## Institutions & powers

- **The Brotherhood** (new form — CHARACTERS.md) — hereditary keepers of the key; the untraceable
  gold economy is their cover. Seat in the Aksum/Ethiopia leg; cells along the inheritance trail.
- **The adversary's institution** — a patient, distributed body (state-adjacent / heritage-
  authority / private intelligence — pin in PLOT.md) that wants the switch under control. Not a
  lone villain; a standing apparatus with a most-articulate face.
- **AugmenTech** (from *RESONANCE*) — Priya's former employer; the bridge to the Court/SAGE
  lineage and to Arin. Its augmentation tech may equip the relay (grounded gear, not super-suits).
- **Local craft cultures** — miners, geologists, Egyptologists, assayers; the dedication's
  "mastery without credential" woven as texture at every node.

## Physical-plausibility constraints (gate-checked)

- **Travel & distance**: the KZN → Witwatersrand → Vredefort → Mpumalanga → up-Africa → Egypt
  route must respect real distances and travel times; the present-day quest clock (TIMELINE.md)
  stays monotonic and consistent with these.
- **Depth & environment**: deep-mine heat/pressure, dam-water depth/visibility, desert logistics —
  honest. People need air, light, and time; the relay's velocity comes from stakes, not from
  ignoring physics.
- **The acoustics must close**: any resonant effect references the established mechanism
  (waveguide / resonator / coupling chamber / gold tuning). New "powers" that aren't vibrational
  are a `nonresonance_force` flag (MYTHOS_RULES).
- **Geology stays real**: Vredefort age/scale, Witwatersrand gold, Adam's Calendar location and
  stone — factual; Builder connections are inferred *from* the geology, never imposed over it.
