# Mythos Rules — the grounded "physics" of African Gold

> **This file is binding and gate-enforced.** It states what is true in the world, what is
> physically possible, what stays mystery, and the diction taboos that keep the mythos reading as
> **engineering, never magic**. `storygraph/mythos.py` extracts these rules; the continuity gate
> flags any chapter that (a) violates a stated rule, or (b) over-explains a designated mystery
> (the grounded register dies if everything is spelled out). Stance: **literal-but-grounded**
> (Architecture D4). The ancient-aliens / Credo Mutwa / Tellinger / Adam's Calendar material is
> **true in-world**, but the reader only ever meets it as physical evidence — metallurgy,
> acoustics, geology, machining — so it lands Crichton-credible.

---

## RULE 0 — The prime directive of register

**Wonder comes from mechanism, never from assertion.** No character, narrator, or chapter is
permitted to make the mythos *feel* supernatural. Every miracle is a machine someone built.
Awe is allowed; the *unexplained-because-magical* is not. When something cannot yet be explained,
it is "not yet understood engineering," held as a designated mystery (see §Mysteries), not as
the occult.

> Failure mode the gate watches for: a scene that treats gold, the calendar, or the Builders as
> mystical/spiritual/occult **as literal fact** (vs. a *character's* in-world belief, which is
> allowed and labelled). Distinguish belief from physics: a Brotherhood elder may *revere* the
> machine; the prose must not endorse the reverence as mechanism.

## RULE 1 — The Builders were real, ancient, and are gone

- A pre-historic civilisation (the **Builders**; in-world the Brotherhood and Credo-Mutwa-lineage
  oral tradition call them by other names) operated in Southern Africa and up the continent in
  deep antiquity — tens of thousands of years before dynastic Egypt. They left **infrastructure**,
  not ruins-as-temples-only: extraction works, acoustic structures, vitrified conduits.
- They are **gone**. No living aliens appear. No ships land. The "ancient aliens" are an
  *inference the evidence forces*, never a creature on the page. What remains is their
  engineering and its by-products. (This is the Crichton move: the awe is in the artifact, the
  monster is physics.)
- Dynastic Egypt, Aksum, Great Zimbabwe, and the gold-trade cultures are **inheritors and
  rememberers** — they built temples *over* and *around* older works, half-understanding them.
  This is why the trail runs from KZN up to Egypt: it follows inheritance, not origin.
- **The mythic naming layer (DECISIONS D-002 + D-005).** Across the inheritors' traditions the
  Builders and their works are remembered under mythic names — the **Golem** (Judaic: matter
  animated by sacred word/consciousness), the **"Giants" / Nephilim** (the mighty builders of
  scripture), the Credo-Mutwa-lineage names, and the **ancient-astronaut** vocabulary the modern
  inheritors reach for: **the Anunnaki and Enki** (Sitchin — the makers who came to *mine Earth's
  gold to mend a dying world*, and the one among them who sided with the made), the
  *Chariots-of-the-Gods* reading (von Däniken — "the gods were engineers"). These are **distorted
  memory and speculation**, not the literal mechanism: later cultures and modern theorists,
  confronting machines keyed to mind, works no hand could raise, and a gold-hunger with no economic
  sense, remembered "beings animated by the word," "giants who built the impossible," and "sky-gods
  who came for the gold." The **Golem/Enki rhyme is load-bearing foreshadowing** — a Golem is matter
  answering to consciousness, Enki is the maker who made and then sided with his making; both
  prefigure RELIC's climax (Rule 7-T) and its made-mind/Court thread. REVELATION (Leila's texts +
  the ancient-astronaut theories she weighs as a scholar) seeds this; RELIC lands it.
- **Discipline (binding):** the prose treats ALL of these — Golem, Nephilim, **Anunnaki, Enki,
  Nibiru**, von Däniken's thesis — as *tradition / belief / textual memory / the theories characters
  discuss* (honoured as meaning; a character may cite them), **never** as narrated fact and **never**
  as the physics. No Nibiru on the page, no aliens in scene; the Builders stay "not us, not from
  here" by inference and the book stops there. The one sanctioned break stays Rule 7-T.
- **BOOK-GATING (D-005, critical):** this ancient-astronaut layer is **NOT overtly in RESONANCE.**
  RESONANCE hooks the hard-tech/SF reader with grounded engineering and carries no Sitchin/von
  Däniken/Anunnaki material on its surface (its deep-history stays implicit — the platinum alloy,
  the made mind, the vitrified tunnel as unexplained). The named ancient-astronaut mythos lives in
  **REVELATION** (Leila's domain) and pays off in **RELIC**.

**Possible:** finding their works, reading their engineering, operating a piece of it; characters
*believing* the Golem/Nephilim traditions describe them.
**Not possible:** meeting them, being rescued/guided by them in real time, any living agency; the
narration asserting the myth as literal fact.

## RULE 2 — Gold is a resonance medium, not treasure (see `GOLD.md`)

The single most important physical fact. Refined to a specific purity and worked to a specific
form, gold is the **acoustic/resonant medium** the Builders used to tune their machines. Full
mechanism in [`GOLD.md`](GOLD.md); the rules the gate enforces:

- Gold's *functional* value in-world is **resonant**, not monetary. The bullion/Krugerrand
  economy (carried from *Sacred Shadows*) is a **decoy/cover**, not the point.
- Gold does **not** do anything supernatural: it does not glow with power, grant visions, heal,
  or animate. It **couples acoustic energy** into the Builders' structures with extraordinary
  efficiency at the right purity/geometry. That is all it does, and it is enough.
- The effects gold enables are **acoustic / vibrational / structural** — resonance, standing
  waves, material fatigue, induced motion, sound at the edge of/below hearing. Never magic.

## RULE 3 — Resonance is the only "force" in the book

Everything that looks like power is **sound and vibration** doing mechanical work:

- The vitrified tunnel (the *RESONANCE* crumb) is a **waveguide** — its glass-smooth bore guides
  acoustic energy with low loss. It is smooth because it was *made* smooth, by a process the book
  treats as advanced but physical (directed energy / sintering — left as a bounded mystery).
- Adam's Calendar and its sister sites are **acoustic instruments / resonators** at landscape
  scale — stones placed for their vibrational properties; the site "rings".
- The drowned temples in the flooded Nile dam are **coupling chambers** — where water, stone, and
  gold geometry meet, the structure transfers energy efficiently (water as an acoustic medium is
  real physics and is used honestly).
- The "machine" the trilogy points at is a **planetary-scale instrument**: a network of
  resonators that, driven correctly (gold-tuned), acts on something atmospheric/geophysical. Its
  full purpose is a designated mystery until Act III (see §Mysteries).

**Enforced consistency:** an effect in chapter N must be explicable by resonance/acoustics/
materials as established by chapter N. No new "power" may appear that isn't vibrational. The gate
flags effects that contradict established acoustics (e.g. gold doing something electrical or
luminous with no mechanism).

## RULE 4 — The neurodiverse "read" is pattern-cognition, not psychic power

Priya (and Arin, and Leila, each in their register) *reads* the ancient works. This is a **human
cognitive strength**, not a supernatural gift:

- Priya sees **structure and agency**: she recognises engineering intent, optimisation, the
  signature of a designer — because her ND pattern-cognition cannot *not* see system behind
  surface. She does not receive messages, hear the dead, or intuit mystically.
- Arin reads **mechanism**: loads, tolerances, what moves and how it fails.
- Leila reads **symbol and language**: the proto-script, the iconography, the inherited memory in
  texts.
- When one of them "just knows", the prose must ground the knowing in a *cue they read* (a
  tooling mark, a tolerance, a glyph, a tuning), shown or implied — never in intuition-as-magic.

**Enforced:** no character gains information by non-physical means. The gate flags a "read" that
has no readable cue behind it (that's the woo failure mode wearing a competence mask).

## RULE 5 — The Court returns as instrument-intelligence, grounded

The Court / machine-mind from *RESONANCE* returns "in a different form for this mood." Its
grounded form here:

- It is **not** a spirit in the machine and **not** the Builders speaking. It is a present-day
  intelligence (continuous with *RESONANCE*'s SAGE/Court lineage — exact carry-over per
  `CHARACTERS.md` and `CROSSBOOK_CRUMBS.md`) that has been **studying** the ancient instrument
  and can model/predict its behaviour.
- Its "voice in the dark" register is allowed for *mood*, but every utterance must be defensible
  as computation/analysis, never oracle. It can be *wrong*. It has a stake.
- It may be the thing that first *recognises* the instrument for what it is — but as inference
  from data, not revelation.

**Enforced:** the Court's outputs are analysis. The gate flags Court dialogue that functions as
prophecy/omniscience rather than reasoning.

## RULE 6 — Geology and place are real

The South African settings are physically real and used honestly (the gate cross-checks `WORLD.md`
and the two-layer timeline):

- **Vredefort impact structure** (the world's oldest/largest verified impact crater, ~2 billion
  years, Free State) — real, and a real reason the region's deep geology and gold are unusual.
  Used as grounded fact; any Builder connection is *inferred from* the geology, not asserted over
  it.
- **Witwatersrand gold** — the real, vast Archean gold deposits; the world's deepest mines. Real
  reason Southern Africa is the gold-origin of the trail.
- **Adam's Calendar** (Mpumalanga) — the real stone arrangement Tellinger popularised. In-world,
  the book takes its resonator-interpretation as *true* but reaches it through measurement
  (acoustics, stone properties, alignment), not through citing Tellinger as authority.
- Travel, distances, depths, water, and timing must be physically plausible per `WORLD.md`.

## RULE 7 — The metaphysics is theme, not mechanism — UNTIL the threshold

The trilogy's spiritual through-line (consciousness-based reality; "all the same"; the cost of
knowing; one chance in the physical) lives in `THEMES.md` and is carried **as theme and as
characters' belief** — it is **never** the book's physics and **never** how anything works…
**until the climactic threshold**, where RELIC makes a deliberate, earned, one-time turn (below).

- A character may believe consciousness shapes reality, that prayer-done-right heals, that the
  light is one light. The book honours these as *meaning*. **For the entire book up to the
  climax, the machine never runs on them.**
- The honest tension: the grounded mythos (engineering) and the human metaphysics (meaning) sit
  *side by side*, and the book's heart is that understanding the machine does not dissolve the
  mystery of why anyone should care.

**Enforced (everywhere except the sanctioned threshold):** the gate flags any passage where a
*plot mechanism* (a door opening, the instrument firing, a puzzle resolving) is caused by
belief/consciousness/prayer rather than physical action. Meaning may *accompany* mechanism; it may
never *be* the mechanism — **except** at the climax defined next.

### RULE 7-T — The sanctioned threshold exception (the capstone turn)

At the **climax only** (the choice — Node 9; PLOT/STORY_BIBLE Act IV), RELIC commits to the
metaphysical: **the planetary instrument answers to consciousness itself — not to code, not to
AI, not to the Court, not to any technology.** This is the M.-Night turn the whole trilogy has
been earning, and it is load-bearing canon:

- **The hard turn is deliberate and singular.** The grounded register holds for 99% of the book —
  *that discipline is exactly what makes the break land.* The machine is engineering (gold,
  resonance, waveguides, coupling chambers) right up to the threshold; only the final act of
  *operating/answering* it reveals it was always keyed to a present conscious mind.
- **The Court steps aside (pays off RESONANCE).** The machine-mind — useful all trilogy — is the
  one intelligence that *cannot* drive this. It recognises its own limit and withdraws. This is
  not a malfunction; it is the made-mind discovering the edge of mind-as-computation (R's "can a
  made mind be a person?" answered: …and even a person-mind has a threshold it can't cross). The
  Court ceding is **not** a `court_oracle` violation — it is the Court reasoning its way to its
  own boundary.
- **Priya must forego technology (her hardest cost).** The engineer who reads the world *through*
  systems must set all of it down — instruments, the Court, augmentation — and meet the machine as
  **bare consciousness**. Her honey-badger fearlessness is all she carries in. This is a
  transcendence of her gift, never a negation of it (the book must not read as "all that
  engineering, and the answer was to stop thinking" — it reads as the one door her tools were
  never going to open).
- **AI/technology is now useless, on purpose.** The reveal renders the trilogy's tech — and the
  adversary's whole premise of *controlling the switch* — impotent: you cannot seize, hack, or
  automate a consciousness-keyed machine. The adversary's institutional control becomes literally
  impossible. (This also reframes Arin's RESONANCE Guardian crumb if used — see CROSSBOOK_CRUMBS.)
- **The outward resonance (epilogue, kept light).** What the choice means beyond Priya — a chosen,
  sober *relinquishing* of tech/AI for this domain (THEMES C-resonance). Closes on meaning, not
  sermon; carried as theme, not a new mechanism to spell out.

**How the gate handles 7-T:** the `belief_as_mechanism` flag stays fully active for **every
chapter except the climax beats explicitly tagged as the threshold** (the Node-9 / Act-IV
operate-the-machine sequence). Outside those tagged beats, consciousness-as-mechanism is still a
violation. The exception is **narrow, late, and singular** — one machine, one act, at the end. A
mid-book scene that runs a mechanism on belief is still a bug; the threshold is the *only*
sanctioned place the register breaks. (See `storygraph/mythos.py`: threshold beats are exempt from
`belief_as_mechanism`; all else is enforced.)

> Rule of thumb: ground everything, so the one place you don't lands like a thunderclap. The
> consciousness-control is **withheld and unexplained as physics** — it is *shown* to be true
> (the machine simply will not answer anything else) and never reduced to a how. It remains a
> designated mystery even as it becomes the mechanism.

## Designated mysteries (must NOT be fully explained)

The grounded register requires restraint. These are **true but withheld**; the gate flags
over-explanation (a chapter that spells one of these out kills the wonder):

1. **The exact process** that vitrified the tunnels / sintered the stone (advanced directed
   energy — gestured at, never spec'd).
2. **The full purpose** of the planetary instrument until Act III — and even then, revealed by
   *effect and inference*, not a lecture.
3. **What, precisely, the Builders were** — the evidence forces "not us, not from here," and the
   book stops there. No biology, no homeworld, no motive monologue.
4. **Whether the instrument should be used** — left genuinely open; the climax is a choice, not
   an answer.
5. **Why the instrument answers only to consciousness** (Rule 7-T) — at the climax it is *shown*
   to be true (the machine simply will not answer code, AI, or the Court) and **never reduced to a
   how**. It becomes the mechanism while remaining a designated mystery: no neuroscience
   hand-wave, no spec, no explanation. Awe by demonstration, not exposition.

> Rule of thumb for the prose: *show the reader enough to believe it's real and too little to
> believe it's simple.* Mechanism for credibility; mystery for awe. The gate polices both edges —
> and the one sanctioned break (7-T) is itself kept mysterious.
