## PROLOGUE — the ancient layer (cold open)

The machine being **built**, not found. Deep antiquity, the Builders' register (third-person,
distant, present-tense-cold). Gold sung into stone — a tuning element seated, a resonator brought
into tune, a single moment of the planetary instrument coming alive. **No exposition, no names we
know.** The reader doesn't understand it yet; they feel its scale and its intention. This is the
first strand of the two-layer braid (TIMELINE.md) and the book's promise: *this was made, and it
worked.* End on the act of *leaving it asleep* — a choice the present will have to make again.

## ACT I — IGNITION (Ch ~1–5 · Nodes 1–2)

**Ch 1 — Priya, in her element.** Open on competence: she reads a system, she's right, she's dry,
she's alone by choice. Establish the ND gift as superpower *before* any mystery. Establish she
left AugmenTech and the wound that drove it (RESONANCE backstory, shown not lectured). Establish
voice (clipped, exact, funny). End: the crumb arrives — a worked-gold piece that "rings wrong"
(Node 1 key-out), via Theo / an assayer / the Court lineage.

**Ch 2–3 — Node 1 (KZN).** Priya can't un-see the geometry. The piece shouldn't exist; records
don't match; someone wants it back. She clocks: *function, not coin.* First watchers. The
provenance points down — the deep mine. (Arin's name surfaces; the tunnel he survived is suddenly
relevant.)

**Ch 4–5 — Node 2 (Witwatersrand deep mine).** The descent set-piece (heat, pressure, a flooding
working). **Arin enters** — mechanism reader; he *felt* the made-smooth bore in book 1. The old
workings follow something older. Key-out: a marker to Vredefort + a second gold element + the
first hint of a vitrified bore. **Cost:** a near-miss; the adversary's hand shows (staged route).
**Turning point (end Act I):** she's proven it's real and *made*, and she's not alone in looking.
The marker forces Vredefort.

## ACT II — THE RELAY (Ch ~6–13 · Nodes 3–6)

**Ch 6–7 — Node 3 (Vredefort).** The vitrified tunnel **pays off** (CROSSBOOK_CRUMBS:
`crumb:vitrified-tunnel`). The waveguide; the oldest scar; the bore guides vibration. Puzzle: read
it as acoustics, not architecture. Key-out: a tuning element + the bearing to Adam's Calendar.
Cost: real danger; the vitrification process stays a designated mystery. The Court's lineage first
*recognises* something (analysis, not oracle).

**Ch 8–9 — Node 4 (Adam's Calendar).** The instrument's ear; the site rings. **Leila enters** with
the Brotherhood's gold-thread (Sacred Shadows carry-over). Puzzle: alignment + acoustics + stone,
by measurement. Two women reading different layers of one artifact — the book's quiet thesis.
Key-out: the tuning decode + the inheritance route. Cost: playing the site draws the adversary; an
ally strains over *should we even be doing this.*

**Ch 10–11 — Node 5 (Great Zimbabwe / up-Africa).** Inheritance leg. The inheritors' guardians;
the adversary's network closing. The **decoy→key flip begins** (GOLD.md Layer A/B): the treasure
economy is a *custody* system. Betrayal-by-evidence — their assumptions were wrong. Key-out: the
Brotherhood contact + confirmation the gold is scattered on purpose.

**Ch 12–13 — Node 6 (Aksum / Brotherhood seat).** The key revealed. The order that kept it asleep;
a radical keeper who wants it used (internal argument, not a monolith). Assembling the scattered
gold into one working key. **Midpoint reframe lands:** the machine is *planet-scale*; the gold is a
key to a **switch**. The book-2 gold thread **flips** (reverse-payoff completes). Key-out: the
assembled tuning set + the bearing to Egypt. **Turning point (end Act II):** the adversary moves to
control the switch; an ally pays a real price; the ancient-layer braid is now tight. The key forces
Egypt.

## ACT III — CONVERGENCE (Ch ~14–19 · Nodes 7–8)

**Ch 14–16 — Node 7 (Egypt / the flooded Nile dam).** The drowned threshold. Water set-piece —
depth, visibility, the dam's machinery; the adversary already staged here. Puzzle: the temples are
**coupling chambers**, not tombs (water as acoustic medium, honest physics). Key-out: chamber
access + the realisation the instrument is live-capable. Cost: a near-drowning; the braid pulls
tight.

**Ch 17–19 — Node 8 (the coupling chamber / convergence).** The machine made whole — it *can* be
played. **The grounded reveal:** the network is a planetary instrument; its purpose shown by
**effect and inference**, not a lecture (designated mystery opens — MYTHOS_RULES). **The two-layer
braid FUSES** — the prologue's act and Priya's moment are the same act across time; the gate
asserts this closure. The Court recognises the whole (analysis). Cost: the choice becomes real and
imminent; someone is in the adversary's hands. **Turning point (end Act III):** the instrument can
be tuned; the adversary has leverage to force the switch.

## ACT IV — THE CHOICE (Ch ~20–23 · Node 9 + resolution)

**Ch 20–22 — Node 9 (the choice) · THE THRESHOLD (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 7-T).** Ideological climax
first, physical second. Priya refuses the adversary's *logic* (managed knowledge; someone-must-hold-
the-switch); both the adversary's control and the Brotherhood's caution are rejected; her third
path: **understand-then-consent** (the *Sacred Shadows* stewardship thesis, evolved into
technological consent). Then **the turn**: at the moment of operating the instrument, the grounded
mythos breaks open — the machine will not answer code, AI, augmentation, or the Court. It is keyed
to a present conscious mind. *(Shown, never explained — designated mystery #5.)*
- **The Court steps aside (pays off RESONANCE).** The machine-mind reasons to its own boundary and
  withdraws — the made-mind finds the edge of mind-as-computation (`crumb:the-court` lands).
- **Priya foregoes technology (her hardest cost).** She sets down instruments, the Court,
  augmentation, and meets the machine as bare consciousness — honey-badger fearless, alone. A
  transcendence of her gift, not a negation.
- **The adversary is unmade by the physics, not a fight** — you cannot seize/hack/automate a
  consciousness-keyed machine; their whole premise collapses.
- The honey-badger price is paid; someone is lost or broken. Hope earned through cost, not triumph.

**Ch 23 — Resolution.** The instrument's fate settled honouring consent over control; the trilogy
turns on a chosen, sober **relinquishing** of tech/AI for this domain (THEMES C-resonance). What
Priya keeps: the integrity of having understood it first — and of having set the understanding
*down* at the end. The allies part changed.

## EPILOGUE — the trilogy clicks shut

The ancient layer lands its last fragment. The **reverse-payoff seeds** plant: the *RESONANCE*
title double-meaning (gold = the thing that resonates — `crumb:resonance-gold`); the *Sacred
Shadows* gold thread completed (`crumb:gold-was-never-wealth`); the consciousness-relinquishing as
benediction (`crumb:all-the-same`). A newcomer feels a clean, resonant ending; a series reader feels
three books lock together. *Per ardua ad magnum.*
