## FROZEN — the six per-act epigraphs (authored into the act-opening chapters 2026-06-05)

Density is **6 (per-act)**, deliberately lighter than REVELATION's 18 — sparse epigraphs keep a
propulsive adventure-thriller's velocity. Each is *slant, never summary*: it means more on re-read
than on first read, and never foreshadows the chapter's turn. Attributions are soft and *in-world*
("as the chroniclers told it") so real material stays texture, never a citation under authority.
Full rationale: `academic/feedback/RELIC_epigraphs_proposal.md`.

**Prologue (ch-00) — the Builders' register** *(the canon's own example line; ties `crumb:left-asleep`)*
> *They did not write it down. They tuned it into the stone, and left it asleep.*
> —from the Builders' register *(reconstructed; the Brotherhood's rendering)*

**Act I — Ignition (ch-01) — gold as the inheritor's measure** *(Aksumite-coin angle, Leila's thread;
chosen over the Genesis/Havilah line, which read too scripture-as-authority for a book that resists it)*
> *He struck his own coin, and on it put a king and the moon, and called the gold his own.*
> —of the kings of Aksum, as the old chroniclers reckoned them

**Act II — The Relay (ch-06) — the scale of African gold** *(Mansa Musa's 1324 hajj; nods to the series name)*
> *He gave away so much gold in Cairo that its worth did not recover for a generation.*
> —of the king of Mali's road to Mecca, as the chroniclers told it

**Act III — Convergence (ch-14) — make the stone answer** *(the mortarless kingdoms; grounds the chamber reveal)*
> *The old builders did not raise their walls against the stone. They raised them to make the stone answer.*
> —a remark on the mortarless kingdoms, attributed to a mason whose name is lost

**Act IV — The Choice (ch-20) — consent; the cost of knowing** *(the climax's moral architecture as principle;
echoes the keepers' in-prose inscription)*
> *A thing that can only be taken by the one who understands it cannot be stolen, and cannot be given. It can only be chosen.*
> —the late inscription, the keepers' hand *(in-world)*

**Epilogue (ch-25) — the trilogy clicks shut** *(fires `crumb:all-the-same` — one light, refracted)*
> *One light, and all these windows.*
> —a saying of the gold-road, as travellers passed it down

## Rules for any epigraph used

- **Earns its place** — tone or grounded clue; never ornament.
- **Grounded, not occult** — even the ancient-register lines read as engineering/intention, not
  magic (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 0).
- **Respectful texture** — real cultural/historical material (Credo Mutwa lineage, Kebra Nagast,
  Tellinger's Adam's Calendar, gold history) is *in-world texture*, never a real-world claim and
  never under a named living authority (NAMES deprecated).
- **House motto stays in front matter** — not used as a chapter epigraph.
- **Freeze chosen epigraphs here** once selected, so the merge step renders them consistently.
