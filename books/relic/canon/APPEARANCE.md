## Priya Ellis (the boss; "Priya Ellis")

- **Eyes:** Not described.
- **Face / skin / age:** Prose never describes her face or complexion directly; canon gives ~35, Durban Indian–South African. She narrates her own heritage at length (Tamil/Hindu great-great-grandmother off an indenture ship; "she was the fold") (ch-01) but supplies no physical self-portrait. States her own age implicitly: "over thirty-five years" / "thirty-five years" (ch-14, ch-20).

## Coherence flags

- **Arin's ear(s) — single vs both.** At Vredefort the blood is "a thin line of blood running from his ear" (singular, ch-07), then he says "Both, probably." Subsequent chapters treat it as **one** perforated ear: "his good ear" (ch-08), cotton/gauze "in his ear" (singular, ch-09, ch-10, ch-11), and "one side of his head is wadded with gauze" (ch-10). Minor drift: the wound is established as bilateral once (ch-07) but consistently played as a single packed ear thereafter. Likely intentional (one worse than the other) but worth a continuity note.

- **Sibu's tenure contradicts itself.** Ch-01 introduces Sibu as "a kid... who'd been hired three weeks ago" (line 5). Ch-03 then says "He'd worked for her three years" (line 21). **Three weeks vs three years** for the same character, two chapters apart. This is a genuine prose contradiction — flag for fix.

- **Priya has almost NO physical self-description.** Across 26 POV chapters the prose gives the lead no hair length, no hair colour, no eye colour, no build/height, no facial features — only "sweat-flat hair" once (ch-23). Not a contradiction, but a coherence gap: any future cover/illustration/audio note has nothing prose-anchored to work from. Same gap for Arin (no hair/eyes/skin colour) and Leila (hair length/colour and eyes unstated). Flag as missing-data rather than drift.

- **The "maker" in the prologue (ch-00) is deliberately faceless.** Singular "the maker" plus unnamed "others"; no gender, build, or features — consistent with the Builders being "inferred, never on the page" (NAMES.md). Not a drift; noted so a reader doesn't mistake the absence for an omission.

- **Hennessy: secondhand "suit / good shirt" (ch-02) vs onscreen dive rig (ch-18+).** No contradiction — different scenes — but note his entire characterisation is voice/diction; he has zero fixed physical description, so any later "appearance" beat has no anchor.

- **Priya's hand tremor has two distinct causes, kept separate correctly.** Stress/"reach"-tremor (ch-11–14, while carrying the key) vs the dive-burn/cold injury to the right hand (ch-19–23). The text is careful to distinguish them ("the tremor had only ever been the not-knowing," ch-21); no drift, but they are easy to conflate — noted for editors.

No hair-colour, eye-colour, or build contradictions exist in the prose, because those attributes are essentially never stated. The only hard contradictions found are **Sibu's three-weeks/three-years tenure** and the **minor one-ear/both-ears** wobble for Arin.
