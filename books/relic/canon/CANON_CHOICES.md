# Canon Choices — African Gold (locked author decisions)

> These are the binding decisions that **gate outline generation** — the outline stage and every
> draft must obey them. They mirror Architecture's locked-decision table (D1–D12) in canon form.
> Loaded **last** in `CANON_ORDER`, so they bind hardest. Changing one of these means changing the
> book; record it in `ARCHITECTURE.md` first, then here.

---

## The locked choices

1. **Title / series.** Working title **AFRICAN GOLD**; series *Gold*; the **cinematic capstone**
   of a loosely-connected trilogy with *RESONANCE* (R) and *Sacred Shadows* (SS). Title may evolve;
   the capstone role does not.

2. **Series binding: soft trilogy capstone.** Reads **100% standalone** for a newcomer; openly the
   finale for a series reader. Plants **reverse-payoffs** (CROSSBOOK_CRUMBS) that reward reading R
   and SS — crumbs **reward, never require**.

3. **Structural blueprint: quest-relay / set-piece chain.** Deliberately unlike R (Weir
   crisis-loop) and SS (Dan-Brown chain-of-proof). Every node: ARRIVE → OBSTACLE → READ → COST →
   KEY → HANDOFF (SET_PIECE_LEDGER). The StoryGraph `template:quest-relay` enforces it.

4. **Mythos stance: literal-but-grounded.** The ancient-aliens / Credo Mutwa / Tellinger / Adam's
   Calendar material is **true in-world**, revealed only through **hard physical evidence**
   (metallurgy, acoustics, geology, the vitrified tunnel) so it reads Crichton-credible — **never
   woo**. Enforced by MYTHOS_RULES + the mythos gate.

5. **Protagonist: Priya Ellis, sole POV, amplified.** The sharp, dry-witted, fiercely independent
   ND engineer from R (editors praised her; she was underused there). She appreciates men and
   relies on none. Her ND pattern-cognition reads the agent behind ancient design. The book grows
   her interiority, her own goal/stakes, and her banter beyond R.

6. **Co-stars, no shared POV: Arin Ndlela + Leila Aziz.** Real arcs and scenes; the camera stays
   on Priya. The Brotherhood returns in a new form (keepers of the key); the Court returns in a new
   form (instrument-intelligence, grounded — MYTHOS_RULES Rule 5).

7. **Gold = resonance key, not bullion.** Gold's functional value is **acoustic** — it tunes the
   ancient machines (the literal hinge back to R's title *RESONANCE*; GOLD.md). The bullion /
   Krugerrand economy (from SS) is the **decoy**; the real gold is functional. The decoy→key flip
   is a mid-book reverse-payoff.

8. **Two-layer narrative.** A propulsive present-day relay over a thin ancient-layer thread
   (prologue + fragments) that **fuses in Act III** (TIMELINE Layer A/P; the two-layer gate asserts
   the braid closes).

9. **ND / father DNA: ethos only.** ND pattern-cognition as the key; mastery-without-credential and
   honey-badger fearlessness as **theme/texture**. The dedication and crest live in repo front
   matter (`DEDICATION.md`, `CREST.md`), **not** in the story; the father's name is never a
   character (NAMES deprecated).

10. **Theme substrate (carry as theme, never preach, never mechanism).** Consciousness-based
    reality; anti-dogma; "all the same"; one chance in the physical; heaven/hell = eternity living
    with the full knowledge of your impact. Meaning beside mechanism — never the plot's physics
    (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 7; THEMES).

11. **Length: ~90–100k words** (`--target 95000`), ~20–24 chapters, prologue + 4 acts + epilogue.
    The broadest, most cinematic of the three.

12. **The climax is consent, not conquest.** Not a fight over a relic — a choice about **who gets
    to decide what humanity switches on**. Priya rejects both the adversary's control and the
    Brotherhood's caution; her third path is understand-then-consent. Hope earned through cost, not
    triumph; ending is continuation, not closure.

## Hard constraints for the outline stage

- **Structure:** prologue (ancient cold open) + Act I (Nodes 1–2) + Act II (Nodes 3–6) + Act III
  (Nodes 7–8) + Act IV (Node 9 + resolution) + epilogue. ~20–24 chapters.
- **Every chapter** advances/plants/pays off at least one **set-piece node** (SET_PIECE_LEDGER) and
  respects the **key-chain** (no orphan keys).
- **Escalation monotonic**, **handoffs forced**, **every node costs** (no free keys).
- **Ancient fragments** anchored and landing; **braid closes** in Act III (TIMELINE).
- **Mythos grounded** — no rule violated, no designated mystery over-explained (MYTHOS_RULES).
- **Crumbs reward, never require**; none load-bearing for a newcomer (CROSSBOOK_CRUMBS).
- **POV:** sole close-third on Priya; Arin/Leila no POV.
- **Voice & register** per STYLE_GUIDE; cast distinct per CHARACTERS.
