- **The father's name as a character name.** *Andries Jakobus Greyling* / *A.J.* belongs to the
  dedication and crest (repo front matter), **not** to any character on the page (Architecture D9).
  The ethos is woven; the name is not used.

  *material/lineage* informs the in-world mythos (grounded, invented); they do not appear as
  people, and the book makes no real-world claim under their authority.
