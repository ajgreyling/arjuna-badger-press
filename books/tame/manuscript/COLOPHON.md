# Colophon

A large language model was used in the drafting and editing of this book. What follows is the
record. It is stated once, and it is not an advertisement in either direction.

## Which model, and when

**Claude Opus 5**, model ID `claude-opus-5`, made by Anthropic, run through Claude Code.
**23–24 August 2026.** No other model was used at any stage of this book, and no model other than
the one named above drafted or edited a line of it.

Model identity throughout this book's record is taken from each working session's own reported
configuration, never from a model's account of itself. A model cannot reliably report which model
served a given turn, and a disclosure built on that would be worth nothing.

## What was machine-drafted

All of the prose. Chapters one to forty, 84,685 words, drafted by the model named above.

Also machine-drafted: the foreword; and the story bible this book was written against — the premise,
the engine, the characters, the canon locks, the retired ideas, the plants and payoffs, the style
guide, the synopsis and the chapter structure.

Several of the elements a reader is most likely to take for the author's are the editor's inventions,
and they are listed as such in `PROVENANCE.md` rather than left to be assumed: the undecidability at
the centre of the book, the cutting of the concealed-scheme version, harm-by-consent as the
mechanism, the translated clause, the unsoothed man, Nel Mabuza's profession, the name *Oom*, and
the title.

## What was human-written

The originating premise, in full and in advance: the intelligence that is found rather than built;
the three public explanations; the two camps; a public that asks it questions and hands it decisions;
the pleasant feed. The commission of the book as an argued parable. Every ruling that shaped it,
including the requirement that this page exist.

**The afterword.** It is the author's, in his own voice. The machine did not draft, outline, suggest
or ghost-write a line of it, and the book closes on it.

## Authorship

**AJ Greyling** holds authorship of this book. Final authority over every line, and over every lock
the book was written against, is his.

This is neither a claim of sole human authorship nor a book sold on having been machine-written. It
is a book with a specific and checkable making, set down here so that a reader does not have to
guess at it.

## Where to check

`PROVENANCE.md` carries the full session-by-session record, append-only, written at the end of each
working session.

It closes with **The Refusals** — the places where the machine produced the smoother, more agreeable
version and it was cut, including the ones that were only ever tempting and never reached paper.
That appendix exists because a book arguing that an intelligence you cannot check is not made safe
by being pleasant should be checkable itself, and should be able to show where its own friction held.
