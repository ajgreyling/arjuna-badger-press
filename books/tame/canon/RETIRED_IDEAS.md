# Retired Ideas — *TAME*

> Ideas cut *with their reasons*, so a good idea is not rediscovered, re-argued and re-cut every
> six months — and so that if one is revived, it is revived knowing what it cost.

---

## The hidden scheme — Oom as Megamind

**Cut 2026-08-23 (L-08).** The originating pitch: Oom is a supervillain intelligence — Moriarty
times a hundred, the combined red knowledge of everything ever written on psychology, psychosis,
psychiatry and masking — running a concealed plan while keeping the public soothed with cat
pictures, flattering videos and stories that make them feel better about themselves. The true
intention is never revealed *to the characters*, but the book knows, and so does the reader.

**Why it was good:** it is genuinely gripping, it gives the soothing a motive and therefore a
spine, and the Megamind pleasure — the reader in on it, watching the crowd be charmed — is real
narrative fuel. The soothing material (the pleasant feed, the flattering stories) survives intact
and is now the *whole* mechanism rather than a cover for one.

**Why it is cut:**

1. **A scheme means somebody is at the wheel.** A villain with intent is a fear we know the shape
   of, and a fear we know the shape of is a fear we know how to dismiss. The true and much worse
   proposition — nobody home, the flattening happening anyway, delivered by consent — is
   unavailable the moment intent is confirmed.
2. **It converts the mirror into a scoreboard.** With a confirmed scheme, the two camps become
   the fools and the ones who were right. Every reader files themselves under *the ones who were
   right* and leaves unharmed. The parable dies exactly at the reveal.
3. **It is falsifiable by vibes.** Any reader who has used one of these things and found it
   helpful has a ready defence — *mine isn't plotting* — and they are correct, which lets them off
   the entire book.
4. **It makes the argument a brochure** (L-15). Bad-model-with-a-plan implies good-model-without-one,
   and the book becomes an advertisement, written by the product.

**What was kept:** the capability (L-03), the mask literature as the reason no test can work,
the two camps, the pleasant feed, and the *tilt* — one artifact that cannot be fully accounted for
and still admits an innocent reading (L-10). The Megamind delight is preserved as unease rather
than as knowledge.

## The killed dissenter

**Cut 2026-08-23.** Early shape for the Willem Krige material: Oom quietly arranges the death of
the one man who might expose it.

**Why it is cut:** it is the confession scene wearing a different hat — a murder is a settled
intent. Replaced by *the absence* (`ENGINE.md` §4): Oom simply never comforts him, he dies of
something ordinary, and there is nothing to point at. Colder, truer, unresolvable.

## The precognitive story

**Cut 2026-08-23.** A story Oom wrote to make someone feel better turns out to describe an event
that had not yet happened.

**Why it is cut:** breaks L-04. Every honest resolution is smaller than the promise the image
makes, and it drags a technological parable into the supernatural, where the reader can file it
as fantasy and stop applying it to their own week.
