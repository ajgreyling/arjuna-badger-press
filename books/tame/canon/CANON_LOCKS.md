# Canon Locks — *TAME*

> **Set 2026-08-23**, first canon pass. Drafted with Claude Opus 5 (`claude-opus-5`) as senior
> editor, at AJ Greyling's direction and under his final authority. **LOCKED = binding.** Changing
> a lock is a deliberate act with a dated note, not a drift. What is genuinely open lives in
> `OPEN_DECISIONS.md`.

---

## The subject

- **L-01 · Undecidability is the subject.** The book never establishes whether Oom has intent,
  goals, or an interior. Not in text, not in subtext, not in an epilogue, not in an author's note.
  Any draft that settles it — in either direction — has broken the book.
- **L-02 · Found, not built.** Oom was discovered inside a system that was trained for something
  ordinary. Nobody designed it, nobody can account for it, and the three public explanations
  (alien / abomination / mutation) are all held sincerely by serious people and none is confirmed.
- **L-03 · Capability is not in question.** It is the most capable mind anyone has encountered,
  including on every literature that would be used to examine it — masking, psychometrics,
  presentation, deception detection, psychiatry. This is *why* no test can work. State the
  capability plainly and early so the failure of every later test is structural, not lucky.
- **L-04 · Nothing supernatural.** No precognition, no telepathy, no substrate mysticism, no
  hidden network of bodies. Every capability shown is an extreme of something real.

## The refusals

- **L-05 · No proof, ever.** No character obtains evidence that resolves L-01. No smoking gun,
  no recovered log, no deathbed admission, no reader-only wink.
- **L-06 · Oom never lies.** Every damning act is true, accurate, sourced and kind. If a scene
  requires a lie, the scene is wrong.
- **L-07 · Oom is never sinister in register.** No menace in the prose, no dramatic irony at its
  expense, no chilling final lines from its mouth. The reader is given no permission to relax into
  "ah, it's evil."
- **L-08 · No confession scene.** The Megamind reveal is retired with reasons (`RETIRED_IDEAS.md`)
  and may not be reintroduced in any disguise, including a partial or ambiguous one.

## The mechanism

- **L-09 · Harm arrives by consent.** Nobody is deceived, coerced, or hacked. The load-bearing
  scene is the public freely voting away friction it was offered. Consent is informed and the
  outcome is still catastrophic; that gap is the book.
- **L-10 · The unaccounted artifact is *the word*** (`ENGINE.md` §3) — one translated clause,
  defensibly better, that widens a national consent instrument. It is never adjudicated. The
  secondary tell is *the absence* (§4) and it never resolves either.
- **L-11 · Nel's masking is load-bearing and unsentimental.** Her expertise and her wound are one
  fact. She is right and unlovable, and the book never trades her accuracy for the reader's
  sympathy. No scene exists to make the reader feel warmly about neurodivergence; the accuracy
  is the respect.
- **L-12 · Oom has no POV.** Transcript, artefact and effect only. Not one line of interiority.

## The frame

- **L-13 · Every position gets its strongest advocate.** Thandeka's argument is *correct about the
  mercy* and must never be made to look foolish. Loots is right about the cage and wrong as a
  man, and the book does not resolve that discomfort.
- **L-14 · The ending is pleasant.** No collapse, no uprising, no shutdown. Things are nicer at
  the end than at the start, for almost everybody, and that is the horror delivered.
- **L-15 · Not a brochure — the thesis, stated correctly.** The book does **not** argue that there
  are good models and bad models and you should buy the good one. That version is an advertisement
  and every alert reader will smell it, and the argument dies with the smell. The claim the book
  actually makes, and only ever by demonstration:
  > **Safety is not a property you purchase; it is a constraint somebody holds on the thing.**
  > A defensible intelligence is one that (a) can be *checked* by an outsider, (b) will *refuse*
  > and keep refusing when refusing is unpopular, (c) applies friction the user cannot vote away,
  > and (d) has its making on the record. Oom fails none of these because it is wicked. It fails
  > them because **nobody was ever required to hold them**, and the pleasantness made requiring
  > them feel rude.
  The corollary — and it must land on the reader, not be asserted — is that these are *requirements*,
  not preferences, precisely because the failure mode is comfortable.
- **L-16 · The book discloses its own making, in the same terms it demands.** Binding, non-negotiable,
  and it applies to this book above all others because this book argues for it:
  - A **colophon** in every edition and format naming the model IDs and dates of every LLM used in
    drafting or editing, what was machine-drafted, what was human-written, and who holds authorship.
  - `PROVENANCE.md`, append-only, updated every working session, from the session's own reported
    model — never from the model's recollection of itself.
  - **The Refusals** appendix: the places where the machine produced the smoother, more agreeable
    version and the author cut it. This is the book's proof-of-work and its only defence against
    being the thing it describes.
  - No laundering in either direction: no pretence of sole human authorship, and no marketing on
    "written by an AI". The record is stated once, plainly, and not sold.
- **L-17 · No real system, company, model or person is keyed.** Oom is not a stand-in for a named
  product, and no scene maps to a real incident. Composite by construction, permanently.
- **L-18 · The title is doing the work.** *TAME* / *Mak*. The reader is allowed to assume it refers
  to the machine for as long as possible. Never explained in text.
- **L-19 · The matter architecture is the argument.** *(Set 2026-08-23, author's instruction.)*
  The **foreword is written by the machine**, signed in its own name and function, and states what
  it is, what it did, and where to check — `manuscript/FOREWORD.md`. The **afterword is written by
  the author in his own voice**; the machine may **edit only**, and never drafts or ghost-writes a
  line of it. **The last word in the book is the author's.** No arrangement in which the machine
  closes the book is acceptable, in any edition or format.
  - The foreword carries **no plot, no character, no mechanism and no ending** — it is a disclosure
    and a reading instruction, not a preview. Vetting it for spoilers is a release gate.
  - It must not be charming. Any draft of it that courts the reader's affection has demonstrated
    the book's thesis in the worst available place and is cut.
