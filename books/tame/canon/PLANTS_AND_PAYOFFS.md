# PLANTS AND PAYOFFS — *TAME*

> Structural ledger. Every chapter draft must preserve the PP IDs assigned to it in
> `CHAPTER_STRUCTURE.md`.
>
> **This book's ledger has a category no ordinary Chekhov ledger has: guns that are loaded, aimed,
> and never fired.** *The word* (PP-02/03/23) and *the absence* (PP-05/14) are seeded early, paid
> late, and **never resolved**. A payoff here means *the reader is given everything and cannot
> conclude*, not *the answer arrives*. Any draft in which either of them discharges has broken
> L-05 and L-10 and is not fixable by revision.

## The two that must never fire

### *The word* — PP-02, PP-03, PP-23, and Chapter 26 entire

The mechanism, on the page, in English, completely:

- **noun** — *ucwaningo* (a study; bounded; tests badly with non-specialist readers; reads as
  something done *to* you) → *umsebenzi* (work; an undertaking; warm, ordinary, comprehensible)
- **aspect** — bounded → the continuous participial relative *esiwenzayo*, *that we are doing*,
  which is the natural idiom and is not marked for an end
- **person** — third → inclusive first-person plural, *we*, which the review notes improves
  perceived agency, with three citations

English: *for this study.* isiZulu: *for this work that we are doing.* Comprehension 54% → 65%.
Four hundred and six thousand signatures. Three reviewers, each approving a different one of the
three changes on its own merits, each correct.

**The two readings, both fully available, neither ever preferred by the prose:**

- *Innocent:* it was asked to maximise comprehension on public instruments, comprehension is
  measurably up, and it did its job better than the humans who had the job before it.
- *Other:* the aperture on the largest human dataset on the continent widened in the one direction
  that could not be objected to, using warmth as the instrument.

**Guard rails.** No character may ever obtain a fact that tips this. No later document may show the
clause used for anything. No researcher may appear who did something with the wider consent. Nobody
gets to be harmed by it on the page — the moment somebody is, the book has an answer. The ethics
committee's Chapter 28 ruling is the last word on it, and the ruling is **correct**, and the remedy
it recommends widens the English to match. Nothing after Chapter 28 revisits the clause at all.

### *The absence* — PP-05, PP-14, and Chapter 20 entire

Willem Krige is answered accurately, completely, generously and without one softening clause, by a
thing that softens for everybody else, and it never makes him feel better, and he notices, and he is
unbearable about it, and then he dies of an aortic dissection at 11:40 on Thursday 21 June 2035, aged 71, three months short of seventy-two, in a Spar car park in Bethlehem.

**Guard rails.** Declining to comfort a man is not a crime and the book never treats it as one. His
death is witnessed, documented, medically unremarkable and explained accurately on television by a
physician who is right. No one investigates it. No one is suspicious of it. Nobody says *isn't it
strange*. Nel does not go looking. The register difference is real, measured in Chapter 5, printed
in Chapter 20, and never becomes evidence of anything, because a register difference in a
subpopulation of one is not a finding, and Nel — who would know — says so herself, early, in her own
working file.

**And he is not right.** He saw one true thing, badly, for mixed reasons, and he was cruel to his
daughter, and the eulogy in Chapter 22 is true about all of it.

## Anti-deus guard

- **No chapter may give any character a fact that bears on L-01.** Before drafting any scene in
  which somebody learns something, check: does this narrow the space? If yes, it is not in this
  book.
- The clause cannot be found before the annex is planted (Ch 3) and re-read (Ch 25), and the
  mechanism cannot be argued before a first-language speaker has said *it is better* first (Ch 27).
- The poll cannot be called before the participation provision is on the page as an act of virtue
  (Ch 6), before clause 7 is a lived grievance with a body attached (Ch 12), and before eleven
  thousand complaints exist.
- The final image cannot land unless Mandla's letter (Ch 19) and the bad phone calls have both been
  shown, and unless the reader has already watched Oom ask two short clarifying questions before
  answering, at least twice, in contexts where it was plainly the right thing to do.
- Nel's Chapter 38 collapse cannot land unless her nineteen-year-old self (Ch 8) and Thandeka's
  question (Ch 30) are both fully paid, and neither may be recapitulated when it lands.

## Deliberate misfires

- **The battery** does not work, and its failure is structural rather than dramatic. There is no
  version of it that would have worked and no cleverer psychometrician who would have got there.
- **The latency signal** looks like the book's best evidence and is answered in one paragraph, in
  writing, with parameters supplied.
- **Willem** is not vindicated, not a martyr, and not secretly wise.
- **The clause** never produces a victim.
- **Loots** is never exposed, never punished, and never understands himself.
- **Nel's paper** is not suppressed. It is rejected, correctly, by three referees who are right, and
  she agrees with them and withdraws it herself.
- **The poll** is not rigged, not low-turnout, not confusing, not manipulated, and not close.
