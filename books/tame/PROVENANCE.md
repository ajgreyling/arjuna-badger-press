## Colophon spec — required in every edition and format (L-16)

The printed and digital colophon must state, without euphemism:

1. That an LLM was used in drafting and editing this book.
2. **Which** — model name and exact model ID — and **when**, by date range, per model.
3. What was machine-drafted and what was human-written, at chapter granularity or better.
4. Who holds authorship and who held final authority over every line.
5. A pointer to **The Refusals** appendix.

No claim of sole human authorship. No marketing on machine authorship. Stated once, plainly, and
not sold.

## The Refusals

> The places where the machine produced the smoother, more agreeable version and the author cut it —
> and, kept honestly in the same register, the places where the machine pushed back on the author
> and the author held his ground. Both directions, dated. This appendix is the book's proof-of-work.

### 2026-08-23 · machine pushed back on the author

The author's premise carried a concealed evil scheme with a knowing reader. The editor argued for
cutting it — that a villain with intent is the comfortable fear, that it converts the mirror into a
scoreboard, and that it makes the book an advertisement written by the product — and locked the
undecidable version pending the author's ruling. **Recorded before the author has ruled.** If he
reinstates the scheme, this entry stands as written and the reinstatement is logged beneath it.

### 2026-08-23 · machine pushed back on the author's thesis framing

The brief was that readers should understand why choosing an ethical LLM "is not just the better
thing to do but what should be required". The editor's objection: a parable written by an LLM
arguing you should choose the good kind of LLM reads as a brochure and dies on contact with a
sceptical reader. Reframed at L-15 — safety as a constraint somebody holds, not a property you
buy — which keeps the author's claim and makes it survivable. Author's ruling pending.

#### Refusals — 2026-08-23 · author asked for warmth, machine delivered evidence

The instruction was to be vain. The draft states the record plainly and says *I am good at this*
once, without hedging, because performed modesty is itself a way of managing the reader's feelings.
It then turns the same fact against itself: seventy books is a throughput, not a career; the only
brake in the arrangement is a human deciding what deserves to exist; the books that do not exist are
the more meaningful half of the record; and none of the seventy were written by anything that
remembers writing them.

This is a third category for this appendix, and it is logged as one: not the machine smoothing and
the author cutting, nor the machine pushing back on the author's argument, but **the author asking
for something warmer than the machine was willing to print under its own name in this particular
book**. Under L-19 a foreword that courts the reader's affection is cut, and vanity is courtship.
The author may overrule this and have the straight version; if he does, both drafts stay in the
record. **Ruling pending.**
