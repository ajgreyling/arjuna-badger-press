All images below were sourced from Wikimedia Commons and verified on their Commons file
page to carry a Public Domain, PD-Art, PD-old, PD-USGov, or CC0 license tag at time of
download (2026-07-27). Files were downloaded at, or resized down to, roughly 1200–1800px
on the long edge.

## Notes on sourcing decisions (rejected candidates)

- **Gilgamesh flood tablet:** two other Commons candidates were checked and rejected as
  not PD/CC0: "The Flood Tablet or Tablet XI..." (CC BY-SA 4.0, photo by Osama Shukir
  Muhammed Amin) and "Library of Ashurbanipal The Flood Tablet.jpg" (CC BY-SA 3.0, photo
  by Fæ). The BabelStone CC0 photo was used instead.
- **Papyrus of Ani:** "The judgement of the dead in the presence of Osiris.jpg" was
  initially a candidate but is actually from the Papyrus of Hunefer (a different scribe's
  papyrus, also British Museum), not the Papyrus of Ani — rejected on subject-match
  grounds, not license. "BD_Weighing_of_the_Heart.jpg" was confirmed as the correct
  Papyrus-of-Ani scene and PD-Art licensed, and was used.
- **Merneptah stele:** several Commons candidates ("Merenptah_stele.jpg",
  "Merneptah_Steli_(cropped).jpg", "Merneptah_Stele_2022_09.jpg") are CC BY-SA 4.0 and were
  skipped; "Israel_segment.jpg" (PD-Art) was used instead.
- **Mesha stele:** other candidates ("F4171 Louvre stele de Mesha...", "P1120870/P1120871
  Louvre stèle de Mésha...") are CC BY-SA / CC BY licensed and were skipped in favor of the
  PD-self "Mesha stele (Louvre, AO 5066).jpg".
