# Picture Credits — Homo Credens, Part 1 (Deep Prehistory)

All images below were sourced from Wikimedia Commons and verified on the file's Commons page to
carry a Public Domain, PD-old, PD-self, or CC0 license tag at time of download (2026-07-27).
CC-BY and CC-BY-SA candidates were deliberately excluded per sourcing policy, even where they were
otherwise the best/highest-resolution option (this affected Göbekli Tepe pillar photography, most
Lion-man photography, and all Çatalhöyük excavation/artifact photography reviewed).

---

### sungir-clothing-beadwork-reconstruction.jpg
- **Subject:** Reconstruction of Sungir burial clothing showing ivory bead ornamentation (Upper
  Paleolithic, ~30,000 BP), photographed at a museum display.
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Clothes_1_-_Sungir_-_Vladimir_Palaty.jpg
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
- **Original file:** 3,292 × 4,951 px

### sungir-twin-burial-model.jpg
- **Subject:** Museum model/reconstruction of the Sungir "twin burial" (the two juveniles buried
  head-to-head with thousands of ivory beads and grave goods).
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Twin_burial_model_-_Sungir_-_Vladimir_Palaty.jpg
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
- **Original file:** 5,008 × 3,241 px

### sungir-ivory-disk-ornament.jpg
- **Subject:** Carved mammoth-ivory disk ornament from the Sungir burial assemblage, museum
  display photograph.
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disk_-_Sungir_-_Vladimir_Palaty.jpg
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
- **Original file:** 2,495 × 1,900 px

### chauvet-cave-lions-painting-replica.jpg
- **Subject:** Painting of lions from Chauvet Cave, photographed from a museum replica
  (Anthropos Museum, Brno) since the original cave is closed to the public.
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lions_painting,_Chauvet_Cave_(museum_replica).jpg
- **License:** Public domain (self-released by photographer; also tagged PD-Art/PD-old-100 and
  Public Domain Mark 1.0)
- **Original file:** 2,015 × 1,721 px

### lion-man-hohlenstein-stadel-figurine.jpg
- **Subject:** Photograph of the Löwenmensch ("Lion-man") ivory figurine from Hohlenstein-Stadel
  cave, Aurignacian, ~35,000–40,000 BP (Museum Ulm).
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lion_man_photo.jpg
- **License:** Public domain (photographer-released, no conditions)
- **Original file:** 216 × 540 px
- **Caveat:** This is a small file (below the ~1200px target). Every other Lion-man photograph
  checked on Commons (Loewenmensch1/2, Löwenmensch 2022 01/02, LöwenmenschRekonstruktion URMU,
  Musée-forum de l'Aurignacien, Lion headed man, Lonesee Schautafel) was CC-BY-SA or CC-BY and was
  excluded. This was the only verified PD option found; a higher-resolution genuinely PD/CC0 photo
  of the figurine was not located.

### gobekli-tepe-pillar-before-roof.jpg
- **Subject:** T-shaped megalithic pillar at Göbekli Tepe, photographed before the site's
  protective roof structure was built.
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GobeklitepeHeykel.jpg
- **License:** Public domain (PD-self — released by copyright holder/uploader)
- **Original file:** 977 × 1,560 px

  after checking the full Category:Çatalhöyük listing plus targeted searches (overview shots,

  contributor (with attribution) if the print edition can accommodate that license, or
  commissioning/using a different (non-Commons) PD source (e.g. an old out-of-copyright
  Mellaart-era publication scan) for this subject.
