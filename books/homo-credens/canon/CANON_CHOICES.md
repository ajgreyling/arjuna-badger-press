# Homo Credens — canon choices

> The deliberate decisions. Each one is a call that could reasonably have gone the other way,
> so each is recorded with its reason — otherwise a later pass "fixes" it back.
>
> Sourced from `00-front-matter.md`, `../STYLE.md`, `OUTLINE.md`, and
> `../../TRILOGY-SUMMARY-FOR-G.md`. **Binding.**

## Scope and shape

**1. The book stops at midnight, 31 December 1900.**
Not 1914, not 2000. The stop is a cliff on purpose: the century that would announce religion's
death opens with more humans praying than at any moment in the species' record, and Victorian
confidence about what came next is left hanging on the century's edge. The 20th century "gets
its own treatment elsewhere."
*Open question:* that "elsewhere" is currently Pilgrimage Era X only. No book covers it.

**2. Twenty-two chapters in five parts.** The parts are acts (see `SPINE.md`), not bins.

**3. No S/B/R/C axioms by name.** Credens is the phenomenon; *Verificans* is the audit. The
axioms may be **foreshadowed only** through "record technology" language. Naming them here
collapses the trilogy's structure, which depends on each volume being the falsifier for the one
before it.

**4. It is a history of religion — not a theology, not an exposé.** No tradition is the hero;
none is the villain.

## Method (the editorial covenant)

**5. Verbatim public-domain scripture, with translator and edition cited.** Never invented
quotation. Historical persons may be paraphrased; quotation marks are reserved for words we
actually have. Prefer `../../corpus/*.jsonl` and `study_bible.json` — real sourced verses across
28 traditions — over memory.

**6. When sources disagree, print both citations.** Never a smoothed average, never a silent
pick.

**7. "We do not know" is a preferred move, not an admission.** Scholarly debate is flagged
in-line. A contested reconstruction is never presented as settled.

**8. Wry, never snide.** Irony yes; mockery of believers no.

**9. Fringe material is a subject, never a source.** Where press companions cover the same
ground from a speculative angle (*The Princely Offspring* on Mesopotamia), Credens stays
scholarly and uses them for texture only. Same discipline as the anunnaki compendium.

**10. The reader contract:** readable aloud at dinner **and** survives a hostile fact-check.
Both halves, or it fails.

## Structure per chapter

**11.** Epigraph (one verbatim verse, cited) → cold-open scene, 300–600 words → thesis
paragraph → 4–7 titled sections → closing bridge, 100–200 words.
*Observed:* chapters run 6–8 sections, slightly over spec at the top end. Left as-is.

**12. Bridges are mandatory.** Each chapter hands off to the next per `OUTLINE.md`.

## Catalogue discipline

**13. *Homo Animalus* is NOT trilogy Book I.** Separate press memoir/nonfiction about animals.
Shares the "Homo …" gag and a cognitive-kinship rhyme with ch-01 only. **Keep it off the
Ter Probatus SKU.**

**14. ch-16 "The Recitation" collides with the Jakobus/HBT novel *The Recitation*.** Different
works. Keep disambiguated in all catalogue copy.

**15. Box set is *Homo Ter Probatus*** — "the thrice-tested human," believe → audit → build.
Register-breaks like "Three Homos" are rejected. *Homo Trismegistus* was floated and dropped:
Hermes Trismegistus is patron of exactly the unfalsifiable claim Book II audits, and the name
would misfile an evidence-drunk trilogy into the esoterica shelf.

## Drafting method — and its known cost

**16. Each chapter was written single-shot by its own author-agent, with `OUTLINE.md` as the
only continuity mechanism.** Brief: *do not contradict another chapter's brief; cover your
beats; trust your neighbours to cover theirs.*

This is fast and it produces genuine variety. It also produces the failure mode that
**twenty-two independent agents sharing one outline will re-anchor on the same phrasings** —
cross-chapter verbatim drift, and evenness of register. The same pattern was measured and cut
in *The Prophet and his Brother* (981 em-dashes, 120 duplicate sentences across 24 chapters).

**Credens has not been scanned for it.** `arjuna-badger-press/tools/prose_tics.py` runs free and
local against any `build/chapters/` tree; see `docs/craft/LLM_TELLS.md` for the house contract.
Do this before print lock.
