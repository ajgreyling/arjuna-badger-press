# *The Long Silence* *(working title)*

An **unauthorised homage** to Liu Cixin's *Remembrance of Earth's Past* (the *Three-Body* trilogy) — a
craft exercise under the Arjuna Badger Press house tooling. **Not licensed. Not for commercial release.**
The book borrows the *engine* of the trilogy (the **Dark Forest** as moral physics — survival first,
finite matter, the chain of suspicion, the technological explosion, and the silence that follows) fused to
the reader's own hook — the **broadcast-folly**: a species clever enough to split the atom and foolish
enough to broadcast its address to a cosmos of silent hunters. Every name, world, device, and sentence is
**original**.

Provenance is disclosed, never hidden — same standing as the press's *Warhammer 40,000* fan novel
([`../no-fear-cycle/`](../no-fear-cycle/)) and its PKD homage ([`../the-scramble/`](../the-scramble/)). See
[`canon/ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md`](canon/ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md) for the line we hold: **take the engine,
never the text.**

## The two craft mandates (why this book exists)

This homage is built to **fix two named faults** of the translated source:

1. **Native English, not translated culture.** The original sometimes read as "Chinese dressed in
   English." This reads as prose *born* in the language — idiom, rhythm, a built and specific world.
2. **No sag.** The original had stretches that stalled, where theory was *told*. This **protects velocity**
   — every clause of the Dark Forest law is discovered under pressure and paid for in scene.

Plus the source's gift, kept at full calibre: **idea-as-detonation** — one "perfect"-bar set-piece per
movement (the droplet/sophon bar AJ named).

## Substrate & structure

Near-now realist Earth sliding into hard-SF deep time. A **deep-time relay**: no single protagonist
survives; the baton passes across three movements, each runner learning one clause of the law and dying
before the sentence is finished, while the **reader** assembles the whole. The aliens are withheld — a
consequence, never a character (no-woo, MYTHOS).

| Movement | Rhymes | Engine |
|---|---|---|
| **I — THE HAIL** | *Three-Body* | Earth becomes loud; a deliberate hail; the reply is a countdown; the watcher arrives |
| **II — THE LONG CENTURY** | *The Dark Forest* | the law deduced under pressure; the long defence; the single-object weapon's one pass; mutual-exposure deterrent |
| **III — THE SILENCE KEPT** | *Death's End* | deep time; deterrence fails; the final cosmic stroke; the heat-death coda |
