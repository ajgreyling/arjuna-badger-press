> **Type:** homage / craft exercise · **Lineage:** Liu Cixin, *Remembrance of Earth's Past* (the
> *Three-Body* trilogy, 2008–2010) — theory, structure, and craft only · **Status:** scaffolding ·

  **unauthorised homage and craft exercise** under the Arjuna Badger Press house tooling — *not* a
  licensed adaptation and **not for commercial release**. No claim of ownership; no commercial use
  implied. (Same standing as [`../no-fear-cycle/`](../no-fear-cycle/PROJECT.md), an unauthorised
  *Warhammer 40,000* fan homage, and [`../the-scramble/`](../the-scramble/PROJECT.md).)
- **What we borrow:** the **theory and the engine**, not the text. Specifically — the **Dark Forest**
  as moral physics (chain of suspicion + technological explosion → the rational silence of every
  surviving civilization); the **broadcast-folly** (a species advertising its location to a cosmos of
  listeners); the deep-time scale-shift from one human lifetime to the heat-death horizon; and the
  trilogy's signature move of detonating a single *idea* with the force of a set-piece (the way the
  source did with the droplet and the sophon).
- **What we do NOT do:** transcribe Liu's plot beat-for-beat with names swapped; reproduce his scenes,
  dialogue, sentences, or named devices; or "make minor changes and substitutions" to his text. That is
  a derivative work and a craft failure. **All prose, characters, scenes, places, and specific beats are
  original Arjuna Badger Press work** — the way the No-Fear novels are original prose over a borrowed
  world. Fidelity is to the *machine of the book*, not its letters. See
  [`canon/ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md`](canon/ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md).

## The author's brief (this IS the craft mandate — captured 2026-06-22)

The reader (AJ) loved the trilogy and the Dark Forest theory; called the droplet/sophon set-pieces
**"perfect"** — that calibre of spectacle is the bar. Two faults of the source this retelling exists to
**fix**:

1. **"Chinese dressed in English."** Parts of the translated original read as transposed culture rather
   than native idiom — stiffness that didn't cross the language. *The Long Silence* must read as **native
   English**, born in the language, not carried into it. (See [`canon/STYLE_GUIDE.md`](canon/STYLE_GUIDE.md).)
2. **The sag.** Parts of the source were **boring** — the engine stalled. This book **protects velocity**:
   no dead middle, no lecture-as-chapter, every idea earns its page. (The africangold velocity rule.)

*Ball Lightning* is explicitly **not** a model — do not chase its register.

## Premise (renamed, modernized, original)

Late this century, a junior analyst at a continental radio-astronomy array notices that humanity has,
without deciding to, become **loud** — a planet that split the atom and then spent eighty years
broadcasting the fact in every direction: weapons-test signatures, leakage, and finally a deliberate,
triumphant **hail** sent by people who thought a silent universe meant an empty one. The reply, when it
comes, is not a reply. It is a countdown.

What humanity learns across the rest of the book — through a contact generation, a long defensive
century, and a final deep-time coda — is the single law the array's data implied from the first page:
the universe is not empty and not friendly; it is a **forest at night**, full of armed hunters who have
all reached the same conclusion, that the only safe neighbour is a quiet one, and the only certainly
safe neighbour is a dead one. The tragedy is not that the law is cruel. The tragedy is that humanity was
clever enough to build the radio and not wise enough to keep its mouth shut.

## The structural engine (what makes it *this* book)

**The deep-time relay.** No single protagonist survives the arc; the book is a baton passed across a
contact generation, a defensive century, and a final witness at the deep-time horizon. Each runner
learns one more clause of the Dark Forest law and dies before the full sentence is spoken. The reader
assembles the whole law the characters only ever glimpse — the same negative-space contract The Scramble
uses (the reader holds what the cast cannot).

**The idea as detonation.** The trilogy's genius was making a *concept* land like an explosion (the
sophon unfolding; the droplet's single pass). This book keeps that calibre: each movement turns on one
clean, devastating idea-reveal, dramatised as event, never delivered as lecture (the anti-sag rule).
