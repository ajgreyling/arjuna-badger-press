# ADAPTATION (supreme rule)

- **Rhyme** with the trilogy's spine; **do not transcribe** its plot, names, devices, or lines.
- No analyst is Ye Wenjie; no strategist is Luo Ji; no inheritor is Cheng Xin; no cop is Da Shi.
- Our watcher is NOT the sophon; our weapon is NOT the droplet; our deterrent institution is NOT the
  wallfacers; our final stroke is NOT the dark-forest strike. Keep the *function*, never the *form*.
- Provenance is disclosed in front matter; never hidden.

# THE TWO MANDATES (the reason this book exists — binding on every page)

| Mandate | Rule |
|---|---|
| **#1 Native English** | Prose born in English — idiom, rhythm, dialogue native. No transposed syntax, no line that reads as a translation. World is its own built place, not relocated-China, not US-default. |
| **#2 No sag** | Theory is dramatised, never lectured. Every clause of the Dark Forest law is discovered under pressure and paid for in scene. No info-dump standing in for a scene. No dead middle. |

# IDEA-AS-DETONATION (the source's gift — keep at "perfect" calibre)

- One set-piece per movement at the droplet/sophon bar: a concept made physical and inevitable.
- Trust the image; never caption the awe. The reader feels the floor drop because they *understood*.
- Do NOT stage a detonation in a non-detonation chapter (paces the awe; one per movement only).

# DARK FOREST PHYSICS (MYTHOS — binding)

- Engineering, never woo: physics + game theory only. No telepathy, no benevolent elders, no loophole.
- The aliens are **withheld** — a consequence, not a character. No alien POV, no explanatory alien speech.
- Never let a character *say the theme*. No one announces "the universe is a dark forest" as a thesis;
  they live the consequence and the reader names it. (Negative space, §28.)

# THE RELAY (structure)

- No single surviving protagonist; the baton passes across movements. Bond with each runner, then bury
  them. Restore interiority — concepts never outrun the people carrying them.

# PLANTS & PAYOFFS (fire what you hang)

Cover every **THREAD** ID listed for this chapter. Plant quietly; do not label PP-IDs in prose. Do not
fire a gun before its plant beat. Do not leave a listed PAYOFF unfired.

# CRAFT DOCTRINE (binding)

- **Embody, don't state** (§16): cut the sentence that explains the beat you just dramatised.
- **Body before thought** under pressure (§8); syntax **breaks** under peak stress (§18), not refines.
- **One quotable closer per scene, one mouth** (§7).
- **No LLM tells:** ration "not X, but Y"; kill em-dash addiction; specific nouns over smooth abstractions
  (smooth abstraction is how both the sag and the translation-stiffness creep in — double-duty rule).
- **End on motion or turn**, not a thematic summary. The coda is **smaller than the idea**.

# CHARACTER VOICES (§7 — differentiate by failure mode, not vocabulary)

- **The Analyst:** precise, observational; dread enters through what she *notices*, not what she says.
- **The Sender:** articulate, sympathetic; the folly must be arguable — reader half-agrees before the
  countdown.
- **The Strategist:** cold, lateral, game-theorist's clarity; carries the hardest ideas, so the prose
  around him must *move*.
- **The Cop / ballast:** plain, dry, profane-warm — the native-English tuning fork; the human thing in a
  room of cosmic abstraction.
- **The Inheritor:** tender, morally exposed; hope and ruin in the same mouth.
