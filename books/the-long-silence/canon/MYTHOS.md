# MYTHOS — *The Long Silence* (binding rules)

> The house rule is **engineering, never woo** (Craft Doctrine non-negotiable #1). This is hard SF in the
> source's tradition: every cosmic horror must be **physics and game theory**, not magic. The Dark Forest
> lands as *the way the universe actually is*, deduced and inescapable — not as a mood. Nothing here is
> supernatural. Everything here follows from the axioms.

## The Dark Forest — the physics of the book (Liu's theory, our words)

The whole engine derives from two axioms and two mechanisms. They must be *demonstrated* in scene
(discovered, argued, paid for), never lectured (STYLE_GUIDE mandate #2).

**Two axioms.**
1. **Survival is the first need of any civilization.** Everything else is negotiable; existence is not.
2. **Civilizations grow and expand; the universe's matter is finite.** Demand rises; supply does not.

**Two mechanisms that turn the axioms lethal across cosmic distance.**
1. **The chain of suspicion.** You cannot know whether a distant civilization is benign or hostile, and
   it cannot know it of you, and *neither can know what the other has concluded* — and that uncertainty
   does not cancel out; it **compounds** across light-years of delay and cultural strangeness. Goodwill
   cannot be verified at distance; misjudgement is fatal.
2. **The technological explosion.** A civilization that is backward when you detect it may, in the
   centuries your signal takes to arrive and a reply to return, **leap past you**. A harmless-looking
   neighbour is a future threat you cannot rule out.

**The conclusion (the law).** Given the axioms and mechanisms, the only move that is rational *and* safe
toward any newly-revealed civilization is the **pre-emptive, anonymous strike** — destroy it before it can
grow, and do so without revealing your own position, lest a third hunter do the same to you. Therefore
every civilization that has reasoned this far has gone **silent**, and listens. The cosmos is a forest at
night full of armed hunters; the one who lights a match is not greeted. He is shot.

**The corollary (the broadcast-folly — the title, the thesis).** To broadcast your location is to light
the match. A species that splits the atom and *then* spends a century shouting on radio has the power to
be found and the foolishness to ensure it. The Hail is humanity striking the match on purpose. **The
Long Silence** is the discipline humanity never had and the cosmos has always kept.

## The hard lines (no-woo, capped reveals)

- **No magic, no telepathy, no benevolent elders saving us.** The horror is that the law is *correct*.
  There is no secret friendly civilization, no loophole of love. (Hope, where it exists, is human and
  costly — Craft Doctrine L-08.)
- **One level of reveal per device, capped** (ANTI_PATTERNS §6). No conspiracy-behind-the-conspiracy. The
  enemy is not a villain with a speech; it is *the logic itself*, embodied.
- **The aliens are never anthropomorphised into comprehensibility.** They obey the law; their interiority
  is mostly withheld. They are a *consequence*, not a character with motive we can argue with.

## Our original devices (rhyme the function, never the form — see SOURCE_NOTES table)

All four are now LOCKED (full detail in CANON_CHOICES); each is physically grounded and **not** a renamed
sophon/droplet/strike. Summary:

- **The Net** (rhymes: sophon). A swarm of self-replicating relativistic microprobes dispatched ahead of
  the threat. Jobs: (1) watch Earth at fine grain via uncountable tiny sensors; (2) sabotage *one* class of
  human progress — corrupting high-energy-physics results at the detector level so we can't make the leap.
  Obeys lightspeed; partially mappable and fightable; menace is *patience and reach*, not omniscience. NOT
  folded-proton-computers, no FTL.
- **The Filament** (rhymes: droplet — Movement I detonation). A single monomolecular carbon nanofilament
  strung taut across a transit corridor by Net microprobes. The weapon is the *geometry*: anything crossing
  at speed is sliced cleanly into sheets before it registers a cut. **This is the "ship cut by the wire"
  set-piece AJ asked to keep — kept at full calibre, re-grounded as our cable/ship/crew** (CANON_CHOICES
  Filament spec; ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE).
- **The single-object weapon** (rhymes: droplet — Movement II detonation, the LARGER event). One object,
  one pass, total, against a fleet. The Filament in Movement I is its terrible first demonstration on one
  vessel; this is the full droplet-grade horror (PP-007). Physics: a Net-built relativistic kinetic body,
  unanswerable, indifferent. NOT a mirror-finish probe.
- **The Reckoners' deterrent** (rhymes: wallfacers). A dead-man system that, if Earth is struck,
  broadcasts the **enemy's** location to the whole forest — mutual exposure. Works only while the species
  will pull the pin on everyone. Original institution (NOT four secret strategists).
- **The Quietus** (rhymes: dark-forest strike — Movement III). A cheap lightspeed beam of engineered dust
  that scours the inner system to bare rock in an afternoon. The horror is that it is *cheap*: Earth was
  never worth more effort than a swept floor. Original device and cost (NOT a dimensional collapse).

## The one sanctioned scale

The realist, grounded register holds the book so that the **deep-time coda** can break every human scale
at once — the heat-death horizon, the law proven absolute, a final small human act measured against an
indifferent cosmos. That is the book's "thunderclap," earned by everything else staying rigorous and
human-sized (house Rule 0).
