## The reader-contract payoff (negative space)

Across all three movements the reader assembles the full Dark Forest law from clauses each runner only
glimpses (SEED_STORY relay). The prose **never states the whole law as a thesis**; the title and the
final stroke do the saying (STYLE_GUIDE §embody-don't-state). The single allowed near-statement is in the
Strategist's mouth, partial and contested, late in II.

## Velocity checkpoints (anti-sag, binding)

- **II is the danger zone** (where the source sagged). Each II beat must carry the deterrent's rising
  tension *and* a runner's personal stake. If a II chapter is "and here is more theory," it is rebuilt.
- Each movement earns exactly **one detonation** at the "perfect" calibre (I.5, II.5, III.3). No more — a
  set-piece every chapter is its own kind of sag.
