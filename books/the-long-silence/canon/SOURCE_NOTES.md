# SOURCE_NOTES — *The Long Silence*

> **SSOT for fidelity.** What the source trilogy *is*, what we keep, and — critically — **where it
> sagged**, because this book is built to fix the sag. This file is the north star: if a beat is not in
> the source's *engine*, it is ours to invent; if it is in the engine, our version must **rhyme**, never
> transcribe.

## The source

Liu Cixin, ***Remembrance of Earth's Past*** — the trilogy known by its first volume:

1. ***The Three-Body Problem*** (2008; Eng. tr. Ken Liu, 2014) — first contact established; the listening
   begins; the betrayal that makes Earth a target; the alien observation/sabotage net arrives.
2. ***The Dark Forest*** (2008; Eng. tr. Joel Martinsen, 2015) — the **Dark Forest theory** stated in
   full; the long century of defence; the strategist who deduces the law and uses it as a deterrent.
3. ***Death's End*** (2010; Eng. tr. Ken Liu, 2016) — deep time; the deterrence fails; the safety-notice
   strike; the dimensional collapse; the heat-death horizon.

## Where the source SAGGED (this book's repair brief — binding)

The retelling exists partly to out-craft these. Naming them so we don't repeat them:

1. **Translation stiffness ("Chinese dressed in English").** Stretches read as transposed culture and
   syntax, not native idiom. **Repair:** prose born in English; setting is its own world, not a relocated
   anywhere; dialogue and rhythm native. → STYLE_GUIDE mandate #1.
2. **The lecture-sag.** Long expository runs where theory was *told*, not *dramatised* — the engine
   stalled and the page went dead. **Repair:** every clause of the law is discovered under pressure and
   paid for in scene; no chapter is an essay. → STYLE_GUIDE mandate #2; PLOT velocity gate.
3. **Thin character interiority in places.** Concepts sometimes outran the people carrying them. **Repair:**
   the deep-time relay gives each runner a full, finite life; we bond before we bury. → CHARACTERS.

## What we keep at "perfect" calibre (AJ's bar)

The droplet/sophon set-pieces were called **perfect**. The bar for our equivalents (the observation-net
arrival; the single-object weapon's one pass; the final cosmic stroke) is *that_* — clean, physical,
inevitable, dread delivered as event. STYLE_GUIDE §"idea-as-detonation" governs.

## Not a model

***Ball Lightning*** (Liu, 2004) is **explicitly excluded** as a register reference per the author brief.
Do not borrow its tone or structure.
