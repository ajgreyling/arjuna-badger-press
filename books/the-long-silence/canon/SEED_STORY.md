## One sentence

A species clever enough to split the atom but foolish enough to broadcast its address learns, across one
loud first contact, one long defended century, and one deep-time reckoning, the single law of a universe
full of armed listeners — **the wise stay silent, the loud do not last** — and the tragedy is that we
were smart enough to build the radio and not wise enough to keep quiet.

## Why this book exists

Liu's trilogy is the best account in the form of the **Dark Forest** — the cosmos as a night woods full
of silent hunters, where revealing your position is suicide. We keep the **theory** (the two axioms, the
chain of suspicion, the technological explosion, the silence as survival) and fuse it to the reader's own
hook — the **broadcast-folly**: humanity hanging a neon welcome sign on a dark and dangerous street. We
invent every scene, name, device, and sentence: same standing as [`../no-fear-cycle/`](../no-fear-cycle/)
and [`../the-scramble/`](../the-scramble/).

**This book also exists to fix two named faults of the source:** the "Chinese dressed in English"
translation stiffness (ours reads as **native English**) and the **sag** (ours **protects velocity** —
theory dramatised, never lectured). See [`ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md`](ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md).

**North star:** the *engine* in [`SOURCE_NOTES.md`](SOURCE_NOTES.md). If a beat is not in Liu's engine,
it is ours; if it is in the engine, it must **rhyme**, never transcribe.

## The deep-time relay (our structural signature)

No single protagonist survives the arc. A baton passes across a **contact generation**, a **defensive
century**, and a **final witness** at the deep-time horizon. Each runner learns one more clause of the
Dark Forest law and dies before the whole sentence is spoken; the **reader** assembles the law the cast
only ever glimpses. (The negative-space reader-contract, as in [`../the-scramble/`](../the-scramble/).)
