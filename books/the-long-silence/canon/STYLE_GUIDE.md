# STYLE_GUIDE — *The Long Silence*

> The voice laws. This book has **two craft mandates that are unique to it** (the reasons it exists at
> all): it must read as **native English**, and it must **never sag**. Everything below serves those two,
> plus the house non-negotiables.

## Mandate #1 — Native English, not translated culture (binding)

The source sometimes read as "Chinese dressed in English." This book is **born in English**.

- **Idiom, rhythm, dialogue native to the language.** No transposed syntax; no sentence that reads as a
  careful translation of a thought first had in another tongue. If a line could only have been written by
  translating it, rewrite it until it could only have been written *in* English.
- **The world is its own place, not a relocated one.** We do not Americanise and we do not transliterate.
  Invented, specific, native-feeling place-names, institutions, and texture (WORLD). Specificity is the
  cure — generic "world English" reads as flat; *built* English reads as native.
- **The ballast character** (the cop) is the proof-of-mandate: his dry, profane-warm, unmistakably-English
  idiom in a room full of cosmic abstraction is the tuning fork. If his lines sing, the book is native.

## Mandate #2 — No sag, ever (binding)

The source had boring stretches where theory was *told*. This book **protects velocity**.

- **Theory is dramatised, never lectured.** Every clause of the Dark Forest law is *discovered under
  pressure, argued by people with something at stake, and paid for in consequence*. No chapter is an essay
  with a character's name on it. (The africangold velocity rule.)
- **No info-dump standing in for a scene.** If a passage is explaining instead of happening, cut it and
  build the scene that makes the reader *deduce* it.
- **The dead-middle guard.** Movement II (the long century) is where the source sagged worst. It must run
  on the deterrent's rising tension and the relay's human stakes, not on exposition. Velocity gate in
  PLOT.
- **Modulation, not constant peak.** Vary voltage; plain pages between the detonations (§3, §12). Anti-sag
  is not the same as breathless — it is *no wasted page*, which includes earning the quiet ones.

## Idea-as-detonation (the source's gift — keep at "perfect" calibre)

AJ called the droplet/sophon set-pieces **perfect**. Our equivalents (the observation-net arrival; the
single-object weapon's one pass; the final cosmic stroke) are staged at *that* bar:

- A concept made physical and inevitable. The dread is in the *logic completing*, not in adjectives.
- Clean, concrete, unanswerable. Trust the image; never caption the awe (ANTI_PATTERNS §16). The reader
  should feel the floor drop because they *understood*, not because we told them to gasp.
- One detonation per movement, earned by the grounded pages around it (MYTHOS §"one sanctioned scale").

## The deep-time register (the scale-shift)

- The book begins human-sized and ends cosmic. As the scale opens, the prose widens — but **the human
  detail never disappears**; the horror is the small thing measured against the vast one. Never let scale
  become abstraction-for-its-own-sake (that *is* the sag).
- The coda is **smaller than the idea** (§20, L-08): a single human act at the heat-death horizon. No
  paragraph explaining what it means.

## House non-negotiables (inherited from Craft Doctrine)

1. **Embody, don't state.** Cut the sentence that explains the sentence before it (§16). Never let a
   character *say the theme* — no one announces "the universe is a dark forest" as a thesis; they live the
   consequence and the reader names it.
2. **Body before thought** under pressure (§8); under peak stress syntax **breaks**, not refines (§18,
   §22).
3. **One quotable closer per scene, in one mouth** (§7). Not every paragraph ends on a mic-drop.
4. **No LLM tells:** ration the "not X, but Y" reframe (LLM_TELLS); kill em-dash addiction; cut half the
   "the way…" similes; restore the specific noun over the smooth abstraction. (Smooth abstraction is also
   how the sag and the translation-stiffness both creep in — so this rule does double duty here.)
5. **Engineering, never woo** (MYTHOS). The cosmic horror is physics and game theory, rendered correctly.

## The ending guard (§20, §26, L-08)

The law is proven absolute, but the last weight is a **human** one, **smaller than the cosmos** that
dwarfs it. Hope, if any, is costly and uncertain — never pharmacological, never a loophole. The dedication
carries the final note, and it is **original**.
