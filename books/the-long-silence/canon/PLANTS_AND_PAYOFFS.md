## Misfire guard (Chekhov in reverse)

- **No deus ex machina.** Nothing introduced late may save Earth that wasn't planted. Hope, if any, is
  costly and seeded (PP-008), never a rescue device.
- **The withheld gun.** The hunters' nature is the one "gun" deliberately *never* fired in full — its
  non-firing is the design (MYTHOS). Do not pay it off with an explanation.
