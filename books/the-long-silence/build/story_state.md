  closes by stating outright that she will be merciful, "and that was how the long silence ended." Movement
  III pays this off.

## Reader knows vs cast (negative-space law)
- The prose has **never stated** "the universe is a dark forest" as authorial thesis. Ridgeway says it ONCE
  in ch-07 — in-scene, qualified, contested, in his own mouth (the single sanctioned near-statement,
  PP-009). The reader otherwise assembles the law from the bubble + countdown + thumb + line + the failed
  kneel. Hold the negative-space discipline into III; let the Quietus and the coda DO the saying, not a
  narrator.
- Aliens remain WITHHELD — no POV, no speech, no motive beyond arithmetic. "No malice in it" recurs as the
  watchers'/narrator's inference (ch-05, ch-08, ch-10), never the alien's voice. Keep this in III: the
  Quietus has no villain behind it.

### III craft cautions (binding)
- **Do NOT make Esme's mercy a flaw to be scolded.** It must read as the best of the species (rhyme Hale):
  the all-sides law — indict the machine (the forest/the arithmetic), not the merciful woman. She is right
  that mercy is the best thing about her AND it dooms everyone; hold both.
- **The coda is smaller than the idea.** After the Quietus, end on a single human-scale act, not a cosmic
  summary. The dedication carries the last note.
- **Keep velocity to the end** — III is the shortest movement; no deep-time abstraction-sag. Bond (Esme)
  before we bury (everyone).
