# *Verdigris*

**Original sci-fi horror / eco-weird**, on the Arjuna Badger Press shelf. No borrowed IP — this is
original work, and carries no estate or attribution notice (unlike the Faithful Modern homages).

The warming Earth turns fungal. A heat-adapted consortium — a behaviour-hijacking lineage, an
aflatoxin lineage, an entheogenic lineage — spreads spores that hollow living people and drive them
like puppets. Humanity's one reliable weapon is **copper**: a colloidal mist, oligodynamic, that
scours the growth clean — and cumulatively poisons everyone who breathes it, turning survivors
verdigris-green at the wrist and throat.

Against the war stands **Dr. Ezra Mokwena**, a mycologist who comes to read the bloom not as an
invasion but as the planet's immune response to a species that lost its humanity — the magic
mushrooms rising in every ruin an offer to give it back, which the copper is scouring away with the
horror. The book never tells you whether he is a prophet or a poisoned man.

> **The engine:** two complete readings — *invader* and *immune response* — held alive to the last
> page, and an ending that resolves the plot while refusing to arbitrate the question. See
> [`canon/THE_TWO_READINGS.md`](canon/THE_TWO_READINGS.md).
