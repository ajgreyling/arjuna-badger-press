# NARRATIVE_ENGINES — *Verdigris*

## 1. The ambiguity engine (the whole book)
Two complete readings held in superposition to the final page (THE_TWO_READINGS). Every datum is
built to fit both; the drama is not "which is true" resolved, but a man forced to *act* under
irreducible uncertainty. This is the engine every other engine serves.

## 2. The copper clock
Survival runs on a spray schedule, and the schedule is a poison dose. The book's ticking clock is
Ezra's own greening throat (PP-02): every chapter he stays sprayed, his liver dies a little; every
chapter he skips to hear the bloom, his defence drops. The countdown is not to an explosion — it is
to the moment he can no longer tell revelation from toxicity, and neither can we.

## 3. The two-narrator conceit
The clinical human voice and the hyphal chorus (STYLE_GUIDE). The chorus is a *loaded gun that never
fires a checkable round* — its power is that it might be real and can't be trusted. Rationed hard.

## 4. The dispossession engine (horror)
Horror by proximity and loss, not swarm. The reader is made to love a person (Thabo, PP-05) and then
watch them hollowed, then watch something wearing them come partway back. Every scare is personal;
the book earns dread by attachment, never by numbers.

## 5. The vise
Both jaws, always: to spray is to save a body and maybe scour the cure; to withhold is to keep faith
and maybe damn the room to hollowing. No chapter lets one jaw open. The ending closes the plot and
leaves the vise shut on the reader.
