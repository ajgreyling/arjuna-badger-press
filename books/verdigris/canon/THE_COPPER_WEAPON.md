# THE_COPPER_WEAPON — *Verdigris*

> Copper is the one thing that works. It is also slowly killing everyone who uses it. Both facts are
> load-bearing; neither may be dropped for convenience.

## Why copper (real mechanism)

Copper is **oligodynamic**: copper ions disrupt microbial membranes and denature proteins at very low
concentrations. This is why hospitals trial copper surfaces and why the metal has been an antimicrobial
for millennia. Against fungi it is a genuine, cheap, un-evolvable-around fungicide at sufficient
dose — the consortium cannot easily select resistance to a bare metal ion the way it can to an
organic drug. **That is why humanity's last reliable weapon is a metal, not a medicine.**

## The mist (the deployment)

- **Colloidal copper mist** — suspended copper nanoparticles/ions, aerosolised. Sprayed on surfaces,
  perimeters, wounds, lungs, whole cities. It scours established growth and denies spores a foothold.
- **The liturgy of survival:** people mist their airways on a schedule; doorways, vents and skin are
  treated; "the corps" are the spraying crews, part exterminator, part clergy. A sprayed zone is a
  *clean* zone. The smell of copper is the smell of safety, and everyone is sick of it in both senses.

## The cost (the trap)

Copper is an essential trace element and a **cumulative poison above it**. Chronic overload is real —
Wilson's disease is the genetic version; here it is acquired, at civilisational scale:

- **Verdigris skin** — green settling at wrists, throat, the folds where the mist pools; a visible
  dose meter you wear.
- **Copper toxicity syndrome** — hepatic strain (the same liver the aflatoxin attacks — the cure and
  the disease fight over one organ), tremor, mood collapse, a metallic depression the culture calls
  "the green mood."
- **The cruel symmetry:** to stay unpuppeted you must poison the liver the bloom is also trying to
  destroy. Survival is a managed suicide. The corps who spray most, live cleanest and die soonest.

## The economy and the politics

- Copper becomes the reserve currency and the strategic resource; the sprayed cities are the ones who
  hold the mines and the refineries. Everyone outside the copper perimeter is exposed.
- **The class line is a copper line.** Who gets misted, and how green they're allowed to get before
  they're pulled from the crews, is the book's inequality engine — the poor spray the rich clean and
  green out first. (WORLD, all-sides law.)
- **The heresy is also economic:** if Ezra is right that the bloom is the cure, the entire copper
  order — mines, corps, currency, the men who own it — is a machine for scouring away salvation to
  keep selling the poison. The book lets that indictment stand *and* lets the terror of un-sprayed
  puppeting stand. Both jaws.

## Binding

- Copper always costs. No scene may use it as a clean fix. Every spray is a withdrawal from a body.
- Copper's efficacy is real and never secretly fake — the horror is not that the weapon doesn't work,
  it's that it works *and* may be scouring away the only thing that could end the war.
