# PLOT — *Verdigris* (beat sheet, scaffold)

> Crosswalk: [PP-#] = PLANTS_AND_PAYOFFS. Chapter counts provisional; ~110k over ~32 chapters.
> Governing rule on every beat: keep both readings alive (THE_TWO_READINGS).

## Part I — SPORE (ch 1–10)

1. A spraying run. The copper liturgy shown as competent, sacred, exhausting. Ezra mixes; Nomsa
   leads. The taste of copper as safety. [PP-07]
2. A hollowing on the page — a stranger dispossessed by aflatoxin; the corps performs the mercy
   (copper-hollowing to stop sporulation). Horror established as loss, not gore. [PP-04]
3. Ezra at work: the mycologist who loves the thing he kills; his old wood-wide-web research. His own
   throat greening. Lock the city (CC-07). [PP-01, PP-02]
4. Thabo established — crew member, friend, the reader made to love him. [PP-05 plant]
5. The refinery; Priya Sun; the clean Reading-A data; the copper economy and its class line. [PP-06]
6. Thabo is exposed and hollowed. The book's first gut-punch; dispossession up close. [PP-05]
7. Ezra begins the grief-map, off-hours, obsessive. Nomsa warns him off romanticising. [PP-03]
8. A puppeted zone where the psilocybin strain has colonised — hosts that are *not* behaving like
   puppets. Ezra can't explain it. First crack in Reading A.
9. The mercy of someone Ezra knows; the human cost of the weapon lands on him.
10. **Part turn:** Thabo, hollowed, *partly returns* where the entheogen strain took hold. Reading B
    detonates. The hyphal chorus, first interstitial — deniable. [PP-05, PP-09]

## Part II — MYCELIUM (ch 11–22)

- Ezra's heresy forms: the bloom as immune response; the grief-map as evidence (Priya's rebuttal:
  warm wet undefended ground). The debate dramatized, both sides landing. [PP-03, PP-06]
- He tests the entheogen strains on himself; skips copper to "hear" the bloom; throat past the
  warning line. Visionary chapters in clinical control — enlightenment or encephalopathy, unbanked.
  [PP-02] Chorus rationed.
- "Thabo" deepens the ambiguity — is it him, or the lure wearing him. Ezra's love tips him fully.
- Nomsa vs Ezra ruptures: she relies on his science, he's preaching the thing she sprays. Priya moves
  institutionally against him — not as villain, as a scientist protecting a load-bearing truth.
- The Green Seer rumour surfaces; the largest bloom named as a place with an intelligence, maybe.
  [PP-08]
- **Part turn:** the copper order announces a sterilization run on the great bloom. To Ezra: burning
  the cure. To Nomsa/Priya: killing the biggest sporulation source before it hollows a city.

## Part III — FRUIT (ch 23–32)

- Ezra crosses the collarbone line for good — off copper, defenceless, the tie-breaker gone. [PP-02]
- The approach to the great bloom: beauty and lethality one frame; safety-smell inverted. [PP-07]
- The Green Seer: lucid, greened past survival — the offer, if it is one, rendered so both readings
  hold. [PP-08] Chorus's last interstitial makes a nearly-checkable statement — [PP-09] cut before
  check.
- Nomsa's tally; she's asked to mercy the Seer; mercy vs murder collapse. [PP-04, PP-10]
- **Ezra's choice:** he stops the sterilization — chooses faith over the sure thing — and receives
  **no confirmation** the bloom was benign. [CC-05]
- **Coda:** green. A wrist, a bell, a face. The plot resolved; the question refused. Last line
  withholds the verdict.
