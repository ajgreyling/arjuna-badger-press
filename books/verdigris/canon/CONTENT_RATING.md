# CONTENT_RATING — *Verdigris*

> Adult sci-fi horror. Gates below are binding; a sensitivity/craft read is required before any
> publishing decision (MYTHOS 9).

## Body horror
Present and central, but **intimate not spectacular** (CC-02, STYLE_GUIDE). The hollowing is rendered
through loss of a known person, not gore volume. No torture-for-effect; the horror is dispossession.

## Drug content
Entheogens (psilocybin and a wider real pharmacopeia) are load-bearing and treated with clinical
seriousness (MYTHOS 7) — never glamorised as recreation, never demonised as a warning poster. The
book must not read as either advocacy or DARE pamphlet. No dosing instructions or how-to detail.

## Despair / mental-health
The setting is bleak (mass dispossession, a poison-to-survive economy, "the green mood" copper
depression). The floor rule (MYTHOS 8): the credible possibility of grace keeps it tragic, not
nihilistic. Ezra's deterioration is not romanticised as beautiful madness — it costs him.

## Ethics / real-world
- **Inequality is indicted structurally** (the copper class line), never mapped onto a real ethnic
  or national group as villain. All-sides law.
- Warming/climate content is extrapolative fiction, not a policy claim; the science floor keeps it
  honest without lecturing.
- Southern African setting (CC-07) written from inside, with the house's ground-truth care.

## Not present
No sexual violence. No child endangerment as spectacle. No reanimated-dead gore tradition.
