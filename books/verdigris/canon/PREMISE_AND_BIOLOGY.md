# PREMISE_AND_BIOLOGY — *Verdigris*

> The science floor. Extrapolated one turn past the present, never invented from nothing. Every
> capability must trace to a real mechanism. If a beat needs biology that doesn't ratchet from
> today's, the beat is wrong, not the science.

## The warming that opened the door

The human body's chief defence against fungi is not immunity — it is **heat**. Most fungi cannot grow
at 37 °C; our warmth is our wall. Two pressures breach it:

1. **A warming planet selects for heat-tolerant fungi.** Every degree the world gains narrows the gap
   between ambient temperature and body temperature, and trains fungal lineages upward toward the
   mammalian ceiling. (This is the real, current *Candida auris* thesis, pushed forward.)
2. **Past the wet-bulb threshold**, human core temperature can no longer be shed in large regions for
   parts of the year. Bodies run hot, then can't. The wall thins from both sides at once.

**In this book:** decades of overshoot warming have produced fungal lineages that grow *at and above*
human body temperature. The wall is gone.

## The complex (the enemy, biologically)

Not one organism — a **consortium**, which is why it's unbeatable by any single measure and why
"is it invader or immune-response" is even askable:

- **A behaviour-hijacking lineage** — kinship of function with *Ophiocordyceps* (the "zombie-ant
  fungus"), heat-adapted and generalised to mammals. It does not think; it *chemically edits
  behaviour* — driving hosts to climate and posture that maximise sporulation. This is the
  "zombify."
- **An aflatoxin-bearing lineage** — kinship with *Aspergillus flavus*. Aflatoxins are real, potent
  hepatotoxins and neurotoxins. In the consortium they do the neurological damage that makes a
  hijacked host tractable: they don't animate the dead, they **hollow the living** — liver failure,
  encephalopathy, the personality scoured out, the body left drivable. This is the horror's true
  register: not reanimation, **dispossession**.
- **An entheogenic lineage** — kinship with *Psilocybe*. Psilocybin is a real serotonergic agonist
  that measurably increases neural connectivity and, in trials, empathy and ego-dissolution. In the
  consortium it does something the other two do not: where it colonises a host, the hollowing
  **reverses** — sometimes. This is the seed of Ezra's heresy and the book's whole ambiguity.

## The hijack, mechanically (no magic)

Spores enter via lung and mucosa. The behaviour-lineage establishes and secretes; the aflatoxin
lineage does the neuro-hepatic damage; the host's executive function degrades while motor and
autonomic function persist — a person emptied but walking. "Puppet" is accurate: no will, retained
capacity. The terror is proximity to the living, not the dead.

## The entheogen strains (the other door)

Alongside the collapse, **psychoactive fungi and plants erupt at unprecedented scale** — psilocybin
mushrooms in every ruin, and (Ezra's widening study) other entheogens filling the disturbed ground:
kinship with the tryptamine and β-carboline pharmacopeia the book treats seriously and never
name-drops as counterculture set-dressing. Their spread is *correlated with the same warming and
disturbance* that spread the killer strains — the same bloom, two fruiting bodies. Whether their
empathy-restoring effect is (a) coincidence, (b) the consortium's genuine repair function, or (c)
Ezra's aflatoxin-poisoned pattern-matching, **the book never resolves** ([`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md)).

## Hard science lines (binding)

- No reanimation of the dead. Hosts are **living and hollowed**, never corpses walking.
- No telepathy, no literal fungal "voice." The mycelial chorus is a *stylistic* register, not a
  claim the organism speaks (STYLE_GUIDE). Any in-world "signal" is mediated through a drugged human
  brain and is therefore always deniable.
- Copper's mechanism is real oligodynamic toxicity, not a plot convenience — see
  [`THE_COPPER_WEAPON.md`](THE_COPPER_WEAPON.md).
