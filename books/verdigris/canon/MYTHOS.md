# MYTHOS — *Verdigris* (BINDING RULES)

1. **Never resolve the two readings.** Invader and immune-response both survive to the last page. Any
   scene that hands the reader a clean verdict is a violation. (THE_TWO_READINGS, CC-04/05.)
2. **The science floor holds.** Every capability ratchets from real mechanism (heat ceiling,
   oligodynamic copper, aflatoxin neuro-hepatotoxicity, serotonergic entheogens). No magic, no
   reanimation, no telepathy, no fungus that states a checkable fact. (PREMISE_AND_BIOLOGY.)
3. **Copper always costs.** No clean fix, ever. (CC-03.)
4. **The hollowed are people.** Individual, recent, loved — never a horde. Dispossession, not gore.
   (CC-02.)
5. **No strawmen on either side.** The copper order genuinely saves people; the heresy is genuinely
   seductive; both are held by good people with evidence. Indict the *machine* of cruelty/inequality
   that caused the warming — never the individuals inside the response. (House all-sides law.)
6. **The mycelial chorus is deniable style, not a speaking character.** Rationed, never checkable.
   (STYLE_GUIDE.)
7. **Drug content is serious, never glamorised or demonised.** Entheogens are treated with the same
   clinical respect as the copper — mechanism, cost, ambiguity. Not a trip, not a warning poster.
   (CONTENT_RATING.)
8. **Despair has a floor.** The book is bleak and must not be nihilist-for-effect: the possibility of
   Reading B — that grace is real and offered — is what makes the copper tragic instead of merely
   grim. Keep the offer credible or the horror is just misery.
9. **Nothing live without the gate.** No PUBLISHED entry, no serial, no cover until CANON_CHOICES are
   locked (esp. CC-07 setting) and the content-rating posture is signed off.
