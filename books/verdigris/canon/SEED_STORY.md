# SEED_STORY — *Verdigris*

## One sentence

In a world warmed until the fungi could finally wear us, humanity's one weapon is a copper mist that
poisons the sprayer as surely as the spore — and the mycologist who mixes it comes to believe the
bloom is not an invasion but the planet's grief made biological, the mushrooms rising everywhere a
last offer to give back the humanity we burned away, which the copper is scouring off the Earth
along with the horror.

## The title

**Verdigris** — the green patina copper grows as it corrodes. It is the color of the mold and the
color of the cure at once: the salvation and the rot share a pigment. It is also what the sprayed
survivors turn, at the wrists and the throat, where the mist settles: a nation going green two ways,
and unable to tell which.

## The structural spine

- **Part I — SPORE (the world as it is).** Ground truth: the warm Earth, the puppeting, the copper
  liturgy of survival. Ezra as a working mycologist for the spraying corps — the horror rendered
  procedurally, the aflatoxin hijack shown on a person the reader has come to like. Ends with the
  first thing Ezra cannot explain: a puppeted host that the psilocybin strain *un-puppets*.
- **Part II — MYCELIUM (the heresy).** Ezra reads the bloom as response, not attack — mapping where
  it surges (the sites of cruelty), testing the entheogen strains, going off the copper himself to
  hear the mycelium's "signal." His body greens; his mind opens; the reader cannot tell revelation
  from aflatoxin psychosis, and neither can he. The two-narrator conceit fully engaged.
- **Part III — FRUIT (the choice with no clean side).** The spraying corps moves to sterilize the
  largest bloom — the one Ezra believes is the offer's fruiting body. To save the bodies is to burn
  the cure; to spare the bloom is to accept mass puppeting on faith. Ezra acts. The book does not
  tell you he was right. Last image: green, and a question.

## The engine in one line

Every page must keep both readings alive — *invader* and *immune response* — and the ending must
refuse to arbitrate. (See [`THE_TWO_READINGS.md`](THE_TWO_READINGS.md).)
