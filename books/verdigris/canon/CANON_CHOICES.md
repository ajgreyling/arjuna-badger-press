# CANON_CHOICES — *Verdigris* (LOCKED)

## CC-01 — The enemy is a consortium, not one fungus.
Three lineages (behaviour-hijack / aflatoxin / entheogen), which is what makes both readings possible
and copper the only universal counter. No single-organism simplification. (PREMISE_AND_BIOLOGY.)

## CC-02 — The hollowed are living, never dead.
"Zombify" = dispossession of a living person, not reanimation of a corpse. Horror register is
proximity and loss, not gore-swarm. (Binding — WORLD, CONTENT_RATING.)

## CC-03 — Copper works, and poisons the user.
Efficacy real and never faked; toxicity real and never skipped. Every spray costs the sprayer.
(THE_COPPER_WEAPON.)

## CC-04 — The two readings never resolve.
Reading A (invader) and Reading B (immune response) both stay fully evidenced to the end. Neither
author-endorsed. (THE_TWO_READINGS — the referee file.)

## CC-05 — The ending resolves the plot, not the question.
Ezra makes a choice at the great bloom in Part III (recommendation: he stops the sterilization but
does **not** get confirmation the bloom was benign — he chooses faith over the sure thing, and the
last image withholds the verdict). Amendable, but the *withholding* is not.

## CC-06 — Anti-derivative rule.
This is original IP. Influences (fungal-apocalypse fiction, ambiguous-invader ecohorror, entheogen
literature) are acknowledged as *engine kinship* in PROJECT.md and never transcribed. If a scene,
line, or beat reads as a reference to a specific existing work — a named property, a signature
set-piece — it is cut and rebuilt from our own world. Test: would this scene exist, whole, if that
work didn't? If no, rebuild it.

## CC-07 — Setting. **LOCKED: Cape Town.**
Cape Town on the habitable southern edge — ocean on one flank, the Cape Fold mountains on the other
(genuinely coastal-highland), the winelands and the drowned Flats within reach. House ground; warming
and inequality lived, not imported. The copper order is centred on the harbour and a refinery in the
industrial south; the great bloom (Part III) grows over the drowned Cape Flats — the book's site of
grief (PP-03), where apartheid geography put the poor on the low wet ground the sea took first.

## CC-08 — Ezra's status.
Never certified sane or ill. Revelation and aflatoxin/copper damage rise on one curve. No lab result
and no vision is ever the tie-breaker.
