# WORLD — *Verdigris*

## The warm Earth

Decades past the overshoot. The wet-bulb line has eaten the tropics for parts of every year; the
survivable band has narrowed and moved. The setting is a coastal-highland city on the edge of the
still-habitable — real-feeling, unglamorous, patched. (Recommend a Southern African littoral: the
house's ground, and the warming/inequality textures are lived, not imported. Confirm in
CANON_CHOICES.) The world is not post-apocalyptic rubble; it is **functioning and terrified** — a
civilisation that has organised itself entirely around one metal.

## The copper order

- **Sprayed cities.** Perimeters, vents, transit, skin — all on copper schedules. Green at the wrist
  is normal; green at the throat is a warning; green past the collarbone gets you pulled from the
  crews. A clean-smelling street is a safe street.
- **The corps.** Part exterminator, part clergy, part sanitation. They spray the puppeted zones, mist
  the newly exposed, and perform the "mercy" — copper-hollowing a host too far gone, to stop the
  sporulation. Nomsa's crew is the reader's window.
- **The mines and the refinery.** The strategic centre; copper is currency and survival both. Priya
  Sun's research arm sits here. Who owns the copper owns the air.
- **The inequality.** The poor spray; the rich stay clean and un-green. Exposure is distributed
  downward. This is the machine the book indicts — not the individuals, the *architecture* by which
  some breathe copper so others needn't (all-sides law).

## The green wild

Outside the perimeter: the bloom. Not a jungle — a **transfiguration**. Ruins fruiting; the drowned
slums and the old camps furred and luminous; psilocybin and stranger entheogens standing in the
disturbed ground like a garden nobody planted. Beautiful and lethal in the same frame — the book's
signature image. This is where Reading B lives, and where the hollowing waits.

## The sites of grief (PP-03)

The bloom's surge maps — Ezra claims — onto where human cruelty concentrated: the border camps, the
abandoned flood districts, the places the copper order wrote off. Reading B: the correction goes
where the pathology was. Reading A: those are just the warm, wet, undefended, un-sprayed places
where any fungus would thrive. **The map fits both. That is the point.**

## Texture rules
- No zombie-movie hordes. The hollowed are individual, close, and were recently people. Horror by
  proximity and dispossession, never by swarm spectacle.
- Copper is everywhere sensory: the taste, the smell, the green, the metallic depression. The world
  is narrated through the weapon.
