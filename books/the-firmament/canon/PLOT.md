## MOVEMENT I — THE FLAT WORLD (≈40%) · *dread by accretion*

**Function:** live inside the flat cosmology at face value; accrete seams until the world's coherence
becomes unbearable; ignite the walker with the un-fittable verse.

- **I.1 (ch-01) — the world, lived from inside.** The walker, keeper-caste, at her work near the
  machinery. The warm analog amber (WORLD; PP-007). The day-track's too-regular light registered as
  beauty, not yet as wrongness (PP-001). The ice wall as taboo edge of creation (PP-002). We take it all
  *straight* — the reader must believe the flat world.
- **I.2 — the first seam.** A name gone from a list; a day the record says never happened (PP-012). A
  wall-walker reassigned and not seen again (PP-003). She notices; she is not yet afraid.
- **I.3 — the plant.** The senior keeper (PP-009), his quiet faith; the un-redacted page pressed into her
  hand — the *"upon nothing"* verse (PP-004). It will not fit a floor she can stand on. The grit enters
  the pearl.
- **I.4 — the closing.** The record begins editing around *her* (PP-012); the ration responds; she feels
  the tending tighten (soft clock, NARRATIVE_ENGINES). She starts, against every taboo, to *watch* the
  light and the wall.
- **I.5 — DETONATION (Mv I set-piece): the disappearance witnessed.** She sees, or all-but-sees, how a
  seam-reader is "reassigned" — the machine's no-malice tending made undeniable (PP-003/012, Dark-City
  kinship). The world is *maintained*, and maintenance eats the curious. The question is now unbearable:
  she must go to the wall.

## MOVEMENT II — THE SEAM (≈35%) · *courage; the walk to the wall*

**Function:** the transgression. Walk to the edge of everything and touch it to see if it holds.

- **II.1 — resolve.** She commits to the wall. Cost named: this is the unforgivable act; there is no
  coming back to the world as it was. The plant's faith carried with her (PP-009 relay).
- **II.2 — the approach.** Through the keeper-caste's grey complicity (G2) — people keeping the peace,
  understandable and wrong. The cold ring; the machinery of the perimeter; the frost (WORLD "the seam").
- **II.3 — the 13th-Floor edge.** At the wall the world feels like it thins to seam — dread and
  recognition (MYTHOS: feeling, not literal render). She finds not the end of creation but a **seal** —
  a made thing, with a *far side* (PP-002).
- **II.4 — DETONATION (Mv II set-piece): the breach.** The wall opens. Present-tense, spare, no captioned
  awe (STYLE). She steps through. Cut, or hold, on the threshold — the last image of the made world
  behind her and the first unfiltered thing ahead.

## MOVEMENT III — THE ROUND (≈25%) · *fulfilment, then vocation, then doorway*

**Function:** the verses come true as sensation-first recognition; solitude becomes purpose; the series
door cracks.

- **III.1 — no roof.** Outside. First the *wrongness*: no firmament, uncontrolled light and weather
  (PP-001). Sensation before meaning (STYLE). The green, silent, recovered Earth (I-Am-Legend).
- **III.2 — the verses land.** The horizon curves — *the circle of the earth* (PP-005). She walks and the
  end never comes — *the ends of the earth* recede (PP-006). Recognition, not citation; the devout
  vindicated (fulfilment law).
- **III.3 — DETONATION (Mv III set-piece, the keystone): _upon nothing_.** The ground she stood on all
  her life — the floor she could never square with the verse — turns out to *hang upon nothing*
  (PP-004). The keystone fulfils. The redactors lied; the text never did; the plant was right; she
  carries his faith into the open. **The book's cleanest, quietest detonation.**
- **III.4 — the deep-time ache + the vocation.** She grasps the frozen-period gap: centuries gone; she
  stepped out of a diorama (PP-007). The founders' love aches (PP-008). And the seedbank's purpose falls
  to her — the empty Earth is a field to plant (PP-010). Solitude → vocation.
- **III.5 — the doorway (the HAM reveal, LEAN but CONCRETE).** Back inside, or in a sealed/forbidden room
  she can now reach, she finds the founders' **hardened radio room** — dials, a hand-mic, and a **logbook
  of call-signs** from the other colonies, the entries marching down the page and then stopping, decades
  ago, one silence at a time (PP-011; BACKSTORY §3). She does not fully understand it. She works the set
  anyway — and, as Book I closes, **one call-sign answers.** The world was never as empty, or as alone, as
  either the flat teaching or the graveyard-Earth made it seem. Book I's arc (breach → fulfilment →
  vocation) is complete; the world has just become infinite. (Hinge to Book II — SERIES / BOOK_II_PREMISE.)
