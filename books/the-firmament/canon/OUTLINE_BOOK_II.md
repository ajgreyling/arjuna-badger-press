## ACT ONE — ASSEMBLY (the Court forms; blocs along wounds) · ch 1–5 · ~30k

### ch-01 — The far end of the wire ◻ ~6k
Open on the OTHER colony — the ensemble whose call-sign answered Neva at the end of Book I. Ivo (or the
comms nerd) working the hardened HAM set; the shock of a voice from a dead world. Establish this colony's
"efficient truth" (a *different* redaction). Introduce the first Court members through the crisis of
first contact. Function: bridge from Book I; the reboot's inciting spark. (BACKSTORY §3, SERIES bridge.)

### ch-02 — Roll call ◻ ~6.5k
The Court assembled — introduce the Twelve through work, not exposition. Dawid's order, Marika's warmth,
Ronel's distance, Steve's jokes. Blocs begin to form along wounds (SYNERGY §1). The scale of the task
named: they can *hear* other colonies now; how do you actually rebuild? Function: cast + stakes. Boer/Amish
grit foregrounded.

### ch-03 — The manuals ◻ ~6k
They have the discovered tech and the manuals but not the *craft* — the manuals read like scripture, half-
understood (PP-014). First rebuild problem: getting anything to run. Ivo the Magician in his element.
Early comic friction (feel-vs-fix: Tomas wants Ivo present; Ivo wants the bench). Function: the "geeks
geek out" register established; PP-014.

### ch-04 — Diesel and dirty oil ◻ ~6.5k
The diesel husbandry set-piece: decades-old sludged fuel filtered/centrifuged/biocided back to usable;
old mechanical engines coaxed alive; the army trucks *start.* A genuine victory scene (competence-joy).
Steyn + Ivo + Steve; Dawid's logistics. Function: first real win; the tactile heart of the reboot.
(BOOK_II diesel; grit.)

### ch-05 — The vinyl party ▣ (scene drafted — ~1,830w; expand to full chapter ~6k)
KEYSTONE. Valve amp + vinyl + truck battery; grief and joy in one room; the Court's guards drop; the
SynergyEngine eases (the bond the later crisis will strain). Steve carries the ache; *ons lag, want anders
huil ons.* Reuel's absence/coldness noted at the edge (foreshadow). Function: the found-family sealed;
the tonal thesis. **Draft exists (manuscript/book-ii/scene-vinyl-party.md) — expand + place.** SET_PIECE.

## ACT TWO — PRESSURE (a crisis hardens the blocs; Reuel exploits it) · ch 6–10 · ~31k

### ch-06 — Efficient data over analog ◻ ~6k
The comms problem deepens: they can *talk* over noisy HF but must push *data* to coordinate colonies —
hand-rolled packet radio, error-correcting codes, compression (real craft, dramatised). Ivo's arc engine.
The trust problem surfaces: how do you know who's really on the wire? (Comms/Crypto — Elsabé/the Sentinel.)
Function: the rebuild's central technical spine; PP-014.

### ch-07 — Who's on the wire ◻ ~6.5k
The trust/crypto crisis: a colony's identity can't be verified; a voice claims something that can't be
proven (or is a ghost, or a trap). Elsabé's suspicion vs. the group's hope (SYNERGY: trust-vs-verify).
Reuel's colony makes contact — persuasive, ordered, *certain.* The Shadow enters the political field.
Function: introduce the antagonist-idea (control); the moral spine begins. (Rhymes with web-of-trust.)

### ch-08 — The resource cliff ◻ ~6.5k
A real crisis — a failed crop, a fuel shortfall, a medical emergency (Hettie's arc), or a colony that
went dark. The Order Bloc hardens; rationing bites; Dawid tightens. Reuel's "control is the only safety"
starts to *sound reasonable.* Function: the pressure that tests every bond; blocs harden (SYNERGY
trajectory: Pressure).

### ch-09 — Two thrones ◻ ~6k
Marika (the true authority) vs. Dawid (the formal one) vs. Reuel (the seductive one). The house's real
politics erupt. A ⚡ tie resolves (Ronel/Steyn, or Ivo/Elsabé's quiet warmth — a fade-to-black intimacy
whose *aftermath* re-wires the blocs; CONTENT_RATING). Function: the SynergyEngine at full tension;
intimacy pays off as politics, not act.

### ch-10 — The martial forms come alive ◻ ~6k
Out in the broken world, real danger (a hostile party, a survival crisis) makes the kept combatives
*practical again* — the liturgy-keeper discovers the prayer was always a craft (PP-013 fires). A turn for
the Liturgy-keeper and a proof of the master theme (a true thing, meaning restored by the world). Function:
the martial rhyme pays; raise physical stakes. PP-013.

## ACT THREE — THE FRACTURE (the house nearly breaks) · ch 11–14 · ~26k

### ch-11 — The scapegoat ◻ ~6.5k
Reuel moves to consolidate the Order Bloc by isolating someone — Jorik the Innocent painted as a liability
(SYNERGY §4). Marika and Neva block it. Dawid tempted toward Reuel's certainty (the ⚡ soul-tie). Function:
the political knife; the Bregman thesis under maximum threat.

### ch-12 — Ronel gone ◻ ~6k
The Huntress self-isolates at the worst moment (her wound); her absence in the crisis is *felt* — the house
learns it needs the one who needs no one. Hettie's care finally reaches her through a wound, not a want
(SYNERGY: need-vs-self-reliance resolves). Function: the self-reliance arc turns; a bond remade under
fracture.

### ch-13 — Marika at the end of her strength ◻ ~6.5k
The Queen, who burns herself down for everyone, breaks — or nearly. The Heart bloc's engine falters. The
group must hold *itself* without her for the first time. A death, or near-death, lands (matched restraint,
CONTENT_RATING). Grief carried in galgehumor; Steve's armour tested again. Function: the darkest hour;
the found-family's real cost.

### ch-14 — Dawid at the gate ◻ ~6.5k
The pivot of the whole book's soul: Dawid, tempted, stands where he could *become* Reuel — impose the boot
"for their own good." The Ruler and his Shadow face to face (the ⚡ tie's climax). Function: set up the
choosing; everything rides on his refusal. (MYTHOS: the argument the series is having.)

## ACT FOUR — THE CHOOSING (matured, not won) · ch 15–18 · ~23k

### ch-15 — He refuses ◻ ~6k
Dawid refuses to become Reuel — chooses dignity over domination; discipline as a gift offered, not a boot
(his arc completes, Bregman). The Order Bloc splits from the Shadow. Function: the moral spine resolves;
the turn toward the light.

### ch-16 — The choosing ◻ ~6k
Not won by force — the crisis is met by people choosing each other across the fault lines. The blocs don't
dissolve; they *mature.* The rebuild-coordination succeeds because trust was extended, not compelled (the
trust/crypto problem resolves as *chosen* trust; SYNERGY climax). Reuel defeated by cooperation, not
conquest — or he leaves, unpersuaded, a seed for later. Function: the Bregman payoff.

### ch-17 — What we rebuild ◻ ~5.5k
The aftermath: the colonies coordinating; the net alive; the first real institution built *with dignity.*
Neva's bridge-role fulfilled — old world and new reconciled in her. The manuals now living craft; the
forms now skill; the scripture now fulfilled (all three registers of the master theme land — SERIES).
Function: the reconstruction begins in earnest.

### ch-18 — Under one sky ◻ ~5.5k
Close. The Court, changed, choosing to stay a Court. A final galgehumor beat and a final ache (the vinyl,
maybe, one more time — the dead danced with, the living dancing on). The series doorway: the *other* arks,
the other cut books, the reconciliation-under-one-sky still ahead (PREMISE §7). Function: standalone
resolution + series horizon. Grief and joy, one last time, in one room.
