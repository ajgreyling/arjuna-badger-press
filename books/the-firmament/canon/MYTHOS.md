# MYTHOS — *The Firmament*

> The binding rules of the world. Break these and it is a different (worse) book.

## The floor: no-woo, no-software

- **Physical enclosure only.** The Vault is a real sealed structure — real dome, real cold, real light
  on a real track. It is **not** a computed dream, not a Matrix in the literal sense. The Matrix kinship
  is the *waking into a truer world*; the mechanism is a wall, not a wire.
- **No magic, no telepathy, no prophecy-that-comes-true-because-it's-magic.** The verses come true
  because they were **accurate descriptions of the real world all along** (SCRIPTURE) — not because
  scripture is sorcery. Fulfilment is *physics + honest text*, never the supernatural.
- **The 13th-Floor "edge" is a feeling, not a literal render.** When the walker nears the wall and the
  world feels like it's thinning to seam, that is *dread and recognition* rendered in prose and in the
  real machinery of a real perimeter — never actual wireframe, never "the sim glitching."

## The all-sides law (house canon — binding here)

- **Indict the machine, not the people, and never belief itself.** The targets are: the *redaction*
  (the cutting of the true book), and the *autopilot* (the dead automation enforcing a closed world).
  The believers, the devout, and the scripture are **never** the target.
- **The flat teaching is never debunked.** The walker learns the world was *bigger*, not that her elders
  were *wrong.* Every scene preserves this. (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS hard rules.)
- **The founders and the faithful are loved.** Competence and kindness at the root; cruelty only ever
  emergent. (CHARACTERS.)

## The fulfilment law (the soul — binding)

- **The reveal vindicates faith; it does not embarrass it.** The un-redacted verses *fit the round
  Earth.* Faith survives, enlarged. The casualty of the reveal is the **lie of enclosure**, not the
  believer's trust.
- **The text never changes; only the world it fits does.** No retcon. The same words that consoled a
  sealed people now describe an open one.

## The withheld

- **No autopilot POV, no villain monologue, no explanation-scene for the machine.** Its menace is that
  it has no plan — only maintenance. (Anti-lecture; CHARACTERS.)
- **The other arks stay mostly off-page in Book I.** One lean seed only (CANON_CHOICES G5). The
  reconciliation of all the cut books under one sky is the *series* horizon, dramatised later, never
  info-dumped here.

## The influence-kinship line (what we keep vs. what we never touch)

We keep the **machines** of our influences — never their letters, names, characters, or scenes:
- **Silo** → the sealed community that cannot conceive of outside; the forbidden-knowledge taboo; caste
  and ration. *(Not: silos, the specific society, any named character or reveal.)*
- **Dark City** → reality tended by an unseen hand; the one who remembers. *(Not: the Strangers, tuning,
  Shell Beach, any character.)*
- **The 13th Floor** → the edge of the world as seam; nested reality. *(Not: the specific sim-tech, the
  plot, any character.)*
- **The Matrix** → the breach into a truer, freer, harder world. *(Not: red pills, agents, the specific
  mythology, any character.)*
- **I Am Legend** → the last one awake in a dead world. *(Not: the plague, the vampires, the character.)*
- **Early-70s enclosed-ark SF (Silent Running / Space:1999 / The Starlost)** → the analog voyage that is
  a set; the retrofuture texture. *(Not: their plots, ships, or characters.)*

All prose, world, cast, cosmology-specifics, institutions, and beats are **original**.
