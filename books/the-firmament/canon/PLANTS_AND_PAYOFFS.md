## Deliberate misfires (marked, so drafting doesn't "fix" them)

- **No weapon detonation.** Unlike a war-SF book, this book's "perfect-bar" set-pieces are *not*
  violence — they are the **breach** (PP-002) and the **fulfilment** (PP-004/005/006). Do not invent a
  monster or a battle at the wall to "raise stakes." The stakes are epistemic and spiritual. (STYLE /
  MYTHOS.)
- **The other arks do not appear in Book I.** PP-011 is a seed only. Resist the pull to stage the
  meeting; it belongs to the series.
