# THE COURT — the Twelve (*The Firmament* sequence, full ensemble loadout)

> The complete Jungian-12 Court: every archetype seat filled, every human chemistry covered. This is the
> **full loadout** — Jungian archetype · star sign · MBTI · wound→defence · the grit · friction vectors.
> Governed by HUMAN_SPINE_AND_ARCHETYPES (people-first, flaw-first) and DARK_HUMOUR_AND_REGISTER (SA
> galgehumor). **Astrology is bible-full, prose-winked** — signs are a *friction-design tool* and an
> in-character joke-register ("typical Scorpio"), NEVER a mechanism. Signs were chosen to *engineer* the
> clashes; the friction is written from the WOUND, and the sign is just how the crew teases it.

## The shared substrate: Boer / Amish grit

Every one of the Twelve is cut from the same cloth beneath their differences — **the grit of sealed,
self-sufficient, faith-shaped, hard-handed people.** Trekboer stubbornness and Amish plainness fused by
twelve sealed generations: they *make* rather than buy, *mend* rather than replace, distrust softness and
spectacle, work with their hands, bury their dead themselves, and carry an inherited, weathered faith
(the redacted scripture, the kept liturgy). They are not delicate and they are not naive — they are
**capable, blunt, enduring, and dry.** This is the floor under all twelve loadouts. No one here whimpers.

## The women (binding — grit, not glass)

The female seats carry **equal weight, equal flaw, equal command** — no whimpering princesses, no rescue-
bait, no dessert. They are competent, embodied, dangerous, and fully *people*: they sweat, they're built
like women who *work*, they lead and they fail and they carry the same wounds the men do. The "Lara Croft
with a real chest" brief = **capable AND embodied AND never a fantasy object** — desire, if it appears, is
mutual and human, never the camera leering. (See THE HUNTRESS especially.)

## THE TWELVE

### 1. THE RULER — *the King seat* · ♑ Capricorn · ISTJ
- **Name (working):** *Dawid* (the Sergeant-Major, HUMAN_SPINE).
- **Wound→defence:** watched a chain of command break and something in himself come loose; **imposes
  order relentlessly** so he never meets that man again. Order as armour.
- **Grit:** the trekboer patriarch — duty, hierarchy, the yoke carried without complaint.
- **Friction:** ⇄ THE REBEL (Huntress) and THE OUTLAW; his Capricorn granite grinds on their fire. Softens
  only for THE CAREGIVER. **Arc:** discipline as a *gift offered,* not a boot (Bregman).

### 2. THE QUEEN / SOVEREIGN — *the Queen seat* · ♌ Leo · ENFJ
- **Name (working):** *Marika.* The one who actually *holds the group together* — moral centre, the room's
  true authority even without the rank Dawid clings to.
- **Wound→defence:** lost the people she was responsible for once; now **cannot stop mothering the whole
  group**, leads by warmth and will, burns herself down for them. Radiance as duty.
- **Grit:** the Boer matriarch — the *tannie* who runs the whole farm and everyone knows it; grief-steel
  under grace.
- **Friction:** ⇄ THE RULER (two thrones, one room — Leo warmth vs Capricorn cold; the central power
  tension); allies with THE SAGE. **A woman of full command — glass nowhere in her.**

### 3. THE WARRIOR — *the Knight seat* · ♈ Aries · ESTP
- **Name (working):** *Steyn.* First through any door; the body that acts while others deliberate.
- **Wound→defence:** froze once when it mattered and someone died; now **never hesitates**, courts danger
  to prove the freeze is dead. Action as absolution.
- **Grit:** the commando outrider — reckless, loyal, tireless.
- **Friction:** ⇄ THE SAGE (act-now vs think-first) and gets on *too* well with THE OUTLAW (they egg each
  other into stupidity). The "poor Steve's Sagittarius soft ass" energy lives near here — see §note.

### 4. THE MAGICIAN — *the Magician seat* · ♒ Aquarius · INTP
- **Name (working):** *Ivo* (the HAM / soldering-iron nerd, HUMAN_SPINE).
- **Wound→defence:** people are unpredictable and hurt you; **machines are honest.** Buries himself in the
  fixable, uses competence to dodge intimacy. The one who resurrects the radio, rediscovers data-over-
  analog.
- **Grit:** the Amish tinker — the man who can make a dead thing live with a hot iron and patience.
- **Friction:** ⇄ THE LOVER (who needs him *present,* not at the bench); ⇄ THE SENTINEL (his the-signal-is-
  just-a-signal naivety vs her paranoia). **Arc:** the wire he fixes is a wire *between people.*

### 5. THE SAGE — *the Sage seat* · ♍ Virgo · INTJ
- **Name (working):** *Elsabé.* The strategist; sees three moves ahead; the cold clean mind.
- **Wound→defence:** was betrayed by feeling once; now **trusts only what she can verify**, keeps counsel,
  guards the group's decisions like a chess clock. (Overlaps the Comms/Crypto function — suspicion as
  care.) Analysis as armour.
- **Grit:** the schoolmistress-surveyor precision; nothing wasted, nothing assumed.
- **Friction:** ⇄ THE WARRIOR (think vs act); ⇄ THE INNOCENT (her cold reads his hope as foolishness).
  **Another woman of full command; her edge is her mind, and it *cuts.***

### 6. THE REBEL / THE HUNTRESS — *the Rebel seat* · ♏ Scorpio · ISTP · **the "Lara Croft with a real chest"**
- **Name (working):** *Ronel.* The bushcrafter-hunter; keeps them all alive off the land; the most
  physically capable person in the Court and she knows it.
- **Wound→defence:** failed by people and systems; learned her own two hands and the veld never betray
  her. **Radical self-reliance**, keeps everyone at arm's length, doesn't *need* the group — the problem
  in a book about rebuilding *together.* Scorpio: intense, private, stings when cornered, loyal to the
  death once she lets you in.
- **Grit:** the Boer huntress — rawhide-tough, weathered, built like someone who carries carcasses and
  chops wood; **embodied and dangerous, never ogled.** Desire around her is mutual and adult or it isn't
  in frame at all.
- **Friction:** ⇄ THE RULER (won't be commanded — the spine clash); her Scorpio claws come out at THE
  WARRIOR's swagger ("there goes Ronel, claws out, nipping at Steyn's soft ass"). **Arc:** learns needing
  people isn't the weakness the wound taught her.

### 7. THE LOVER — *the Lover seat* · ♓ Pisces · ENFP
- **Name (working):** *Tomas.* The heart of the group; the one who *feels* everything and says it; binds
  the found-family with open affection.
- **Wound→defence:** loved and lost, hard; now **loves loudly and constantly** because the alternative —
  guarding it — nearly killed him. Openness as defiance of grief.
- **Grit:** even the tender one is *hard* — Boer-tender, which is love expressed through labour and
  presence, not words alone.
- **Friction:** ⇄ THE MAGICIAN (needs Ivo present); ⇄ THE SAGE (his heart vs her ice). The Court's
  emotional truth-teller — when Tomas cries, the room knows it's real.

### 8. THE FOOL / JESTER — *the Fool seat* · ♐ Sagittarius · ESFP · **the register-keeper**
- **Name (working):** *Steve* / *Stoffel* (the Fixer / *oom*, HUMAN_SPINE). *(Note: "Steve/Sagittarius" is
  the archetype AJ named in the friction example — this is that seat.)*
- **Wound→defence:** has buried more than his share; carries an unnamed grief. **Fixes anything with wire
  and a joke, and laughs at the funeral.** *Ons lag, want anders huil ons.* Humour as armour.
- **Grit:** the plaas-oom who mends the truck, the fence, and the mood, all before lunch.
- **Friction:** his deflecting jokes infuriate THE RULER (who wants gravity) and wound THE LOVER (who needs
  him to *feel it*). Sagittarius soft-ass = the good-natured target the sharper signs tease. **Arc:** the
  page the joke *fails* him and he finally cries = the most human in the book (DARK_HUMOUR rule 2).

### 9. THE CAREGIVER — *the Caregiver seat* · ♋ Cancer · ISFJ
- **Name (working):** *Hettie.* The one who feeds, tends, doctors, remembers everyone's wounds; the glue
  no one notices until she's gone.
- **Wound→defence:** could not save someone she nursed; now **cares for everyone compulsively**, gives
  past her own limit, hides her own needs. Service as penance.
- **Grit:** the Amish healer-mother — endless practical competence, iron under the softness.
- **Friction:** softens THE RULER; clashes with THE HUNTRESS's self-sufficiency (Hettie *needs* to be
  needed; Ronel refuses to need). **Not a servant — a pillar; her care is strength, not meekness.**

### 10. THE INNOCENT — *the Innocent seat* · ♉ Taurus · ISFP
- **Name (working):** *Jorik.* The youngest; the one who still believes, still hopes, still sees the world
  clean. The Court's conscience and its future.
- **Wound→defence:** hasn't been broken *yet* — his defence is **stubborn faith in people and goodness**,
  which reads as naive until it turns out to be the thing that saves them (Bregman: the innocent is
  *right*). Hope as quiet iron (Taurus immovability).
- **Grit:** the devout farm-boy — plain, steady, unbudgeable once planted.
- **Friction:** ⇄ THE SAGE and THE SHADOW (their cynicism vs his hope — the book's moral argument). **Arc:**
  keeps his innocence *by choice,* not ignorance — the hardest kind.

### 11. THE CREATOR — *the Creator seat* · ♎ Libra · INFJ
- **Name (working):** *Neva* — **the Book I protagonist**, carried into the Court as the one who *walked
  out first* and now must imagine the new world into being (the re-seeder, PP-010). The visionary-builder.
- **Wound→defence:** transgressed everything she was raised to hold sacred and was *right* — carries the
  guilt and the authority of the one who broke the world open. **Builds, plans, envisions** the rebuild;
  balances the Court (Libra) because she has seen both sides of the wall. Creation as redemption of the
  transgression.
- **Grit:** the keeper-caste devout turned pioneer; faith *enlarged,* not lost (the fulfilment soul).
- **Friction:** the bridge figure — ⇄ everyone, belongs fully to neither the old world nor the new. Her
  Libra balance is tested by the Court's harder poles. **The thread that ties Book I to Book II.**

### 12. THE SHADOW / THE RULER'S DARK TWIN — *the Shadow seat* · ♊ Gemini · ENTJ
- **Name (working):** *Reuel.* The one who chose, or would choose, **the boot** — the colony (or the voice
  on the wire) that decided rebuilding means *control*, order imposed by force, the flare's lesson read as
  "power is the only safety." The Court's necessary darkness — not a cartoon villain but the *road not
  taken,* the Bregman thesis's antagonist.
- **Wound→defence:** the same founding trauma as everyone — but he answered it with **domination** instead
  of trust. Two-faced (Gemini), persuasive, genuinely believes he's the only adult in the room. Control as
  survival.
- **Grit:** the hard-line commandant — the Boer laager instinct turned tyrannical; grit *without* grace.
- **Friction:** ⇄ THE INNOCENT and THE QUEEN (his cynicism vs their faith in people — the book's central
  moral fight); a dark mirror of THE RULER (Dawid could *become* Reuel if his fear wins). **The Court's
  shadow made flesh — the argument the whole series is having.**

## The friction map (hinted, never algorithmic — the crew *teases* the signs)

The clashes are written from the **wounds**; the star signs are how the Court jokes about each other, the
way a real crew does. Sample register (for the drafting voice — DARK_HUMOUR):

- *"There goes Ronel, Scorpio claws out, nipping at poor Steyn's Sagittarius soft ass again."*
- *"Two thrones and one kettle — watch the Leo and the Capricorn decide who pours."*
- *"Ivo'd rather marry the radio. Aquarius. What do you expect."*
- *"Don't let the Virgo plan the party or we'll all be issued clipboards."*

The **poles** that power the book:
- **Order vs Freedom** — Ruler (Dawid ♑) ⇄ Rebel/Huntress (Ronel ♏) + Outlaw-energy.
- **Faith in people vs Control** — Innocent (Jorik ♉) + Queen (Marika ♌) ⇄ Shadow (Reuel ♊). *The moral spine.*
- **Feel vs Fix** — Lover (Tomas ♓) ⇄ Magician (Ivo ♒) + Fool (Steve ♐).
- **Act vs Think** — Warrior (Steyn ♈) ⇄ Sage (Elsabé ♍).
- **Need vs Self-reliance** — Caregiver (Hettie ♋) ⇄ Huntress (Ronel ♏).
- **The bridge** — Creator (Neva ♎) ties old world to new; belongs to neither pole, tests all of them.
