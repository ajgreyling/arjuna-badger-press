# TITLES — *The Firmament*

> Author said "let me recommend." Candidates by angle, then the recommendation with reasons. Working
> folder slug is `the-firmament` regardless; the final title can change before publish.

## The scripture angle (leans into the hook)
- **Upon Nothing** — from the keystone verse (*He hangeth the earth upon nothing*). Spare, strange,
  unforgettable; means "sits on a floor" inside and "hangs in space" outside — the whole book in two
  words.
- **The Circle of the Earth** — the same-verse-both-worlds line; elegant but softer.
- **The Ends of the Earth** — the ice-wall edge that turns out not to be an edge; evocative but common.
- **The Waters Above** — the firmament/weather line; lyrical, more oblique.

## The enclosure angle (leans into the dystopian sleeper)
- **The Firmament** — the dome that is real load-bearing structure and real sky both. Clean, resonant,
  double-valued, and it *is* the working title. Reads as SF and as scripture at once.
- **The Ice Wall** — concrete and cold; strong but tells less of the soul.
- **The Flat** — brutal and modern; risks sounding glib.
- **Seedbank** — accurate but dry; buries the awe.

## The reveal angle (leans into the waking)
- **The Round** — the answer-word; punchy but gives the game away.
- **The Long Noon** — the day-track light that never truly sets; beautiful, oblique, deep-time-flavoured.
- **After the Flare** — clear but generic post-apocalypse.

## RECOMMENDATION (Klaus)

**Primary: _Upon Nothing_.**
Reasons:
- It is the **keystone** of the whole engine (PP-004): the one verse that *cannot* fit the enclosure and
  so had to be cut hardest — and the one whose fulfilment is the book's cleanest detonation. The title
  *is* the reveal, hidden in plain sight, exactly as the verse was.
- It reads three ways and all are true: the world resting on *nothing* (the lie of a floor), the earth
  hung on *nothing* (the round truth), and the faith the redactors tried to found on *nothing* (and
  failed, because the text held). That triple-valence is the book.
- It's spare, ominous, and unlike anything on the shelf — carries the literary-dystopian register
  without a genre-tell.

**Strong alternate: _The Firmament_** (keep as working title / series-friendly). If AJ wants the
enclosure-forward, scripture-and-SF-at-once read, this is the safe, resonant choice and it's already the
folder slug — zero-cost to keep.
