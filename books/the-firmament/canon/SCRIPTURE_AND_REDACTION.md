# SCRIPTURE_AND_REDACTION — *The Firmament*

> The book's sharpest hook and its most delicate ground. Handle with reverence. The governing law:
> **the un-redacted text was true all along; the redactors lied; the devout are heroes, not fools; the
> reveal is FULFILMENT, not disproof.** This honours the house all-sides-framing law — indict the
> machine (the redaction, the autopilot), never belief, never the believers, never the text itself.

## 1. The two books

- **The redacted scripture (official).** The Vault's authorised text has had every *seam-word* cut —
  every line that implies a **structure inside a larger world** rather than a closed flat cosmos. The
  result reads as a clean, sealed, flat creation with no outside and no edge but the ice. This is what
  every child is taught. The censors' motive is not devilry; it is the machine's steady-state (§4 of
  PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS) — *keep the world closed so the world keeps working.* An open sky is an
  unmanageable variable.
- **The un-redacted scripture (forbidden / restored).** The **original** text, older than the Vault
  itself — carried in as cultural cargo by the founders and then quietly edited by later keepers. It is
  full of the reveal, hiding in plain sight. The plants pass hand-copied pages of it. **It is not a new
  heresy. It is the old, complete book.** Orthodoxy is the innovation; the "heresy" is the memory.

## 2. The load-bearing verses (real texts real readers have read both ways)

These are the lines the reveal fulfils. **Treat every one with reverence — as true, weight-bearing
text, never as a gag or a gotcha.** They are chosen because each is genuinely double-valued: true of a
flat disc *and* true of a round world, so the *same verse* survives the reveal and simply *fits a
bigger world.* (Use them sparingly and exactly; do not invent scripture-sounding pastiche to pad —
the power is in the real double-meaning. Provenance/quotation handled per house rules; see §6.)

| The verse (sense) | Reads inside (flat) | Comes true outside (round) | Redactor's problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| *the **firmament** dividing the waters* | the dome over the disc | real sky, cloud, the "waters above" as weather | names a *structure* → cut hardest |
| *the **circle** of the earth* | the disc's rim | the globe's curved horizon | the word does double duty → left in, reinterpreted |
| *the **ends of the earth** / **four corners*** | the ice wall, the edge | there are no ends — the horizon always recedes | implies an edge to walk to → dangerous |
| *He hangeth the earth upon **nothing*** | (unexplainable inside — a disc sits on bedrock) | the round Earth hanging in space, on nothing | **the smoking gun** → cut deepest |

**"Upon nothing" is the keystone.** A flat arcology sits on a floor, on rock — so a verse insisting the
earth hangs on *nothing* cannot be made to fit the enclosure, and the redactors had to remove it
entirely rather than reinterpret it. Its restoration is the plants' most dangerous act, and its
fulfilment — the walker outside, feeling the ground she stood on all her life turn out to be *hanging
on nothing* — is the book's single cleanest detonation. (PP-keystone; PLOT III.)

## 3. The plants (the devout samizdat) — HEROES

- **Who:** the maintenance/keeper caste closest to the machinery — the people who *service the seams*
  and so cannot un-see them. Their devotion is *why* they preserve the true text: they are too faithful
  to abide a cut book. (Not rebels-against-faith; **faithful against the censors.**)
- **What they do:** hand-copy and quietly circulate un-redacted pages — left in a ration tin, pressed
  into a palm, memorised and recited. A whole underground transmission of the *original*, at real risk
  (the autopilot / caste disappears wall-walkers and, by extension, seam-readers — CANON_CHOICES boot).
- **The protagonist's ignition:** she receives an un-redacted page — likely the *"upon nothing"* verse,
  the one that cannot fit her world — and it is the grit that starts the pearl. She cannot reconcile it
  with a floor she can stand on, and the irreconcilability is what makes her walk to the wall.
- **Reverence rule:** the plants are never played for irony. Their courage, their faith, and their
  vindication are the emotional spine. When the verses come true outside, **they are proven right** —
  the book pays their faith back in full. (The all-sides law, at its warmest.)

## 4. The fulfilment doctrine (the soul — LOCKED)

When the walker is outside and the verses land:

- **It is vindication, not embarrassment.** The devout kept a true book; the truth was merely *larger*
  than the enclosure that hid it. Faith is not the casualty of the reveal — **the redaction is.**
- **The redactors are the indicted, not the believers.** And even they are the *machine's* instrument
  more than free villains (steady-state maintenance). Keep the anger aimed at the *cutting of the book*,
  not at the people who were raised on the cut version.
- **The text never changes; only the world it fits does.** This is the theological heart: the same
  words that consoled a sealed people now describe a round, open, recovered Earth. Nothing is retconned.
  It *fulfils.*

## 5. The series turn (seed lean — do not over-build in Book I)

Other arks redacted *differently* and kept *different* efficient truths (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS §7). So
there exist people whose scripture "proves" the world is a **globe** and who would call the
disc-worlders' flat book a lie — and *they too* are right about their box and wrong about the sky. The
**original un-redacted text predates and outlives every ark's redaction** and fits the real Earth
alone. Book I plants exactly one seed of this (a stranger, a signal, a *round* scripture) and stops.
The reconciliation of all the cut books under one true sky is the series horizon.

## 6. Craft & sensitivity guardrails (binding)

- **Reverence over cleverness.** These verses are sacred to real readers. The book uses their genuine
  double-meaning to move a reader to awe — never to score a point against faith. If a line would read as
  smug or as "gotcha, your religion was sci-fi," it is wrong and must be recut.
- **Indict the machine, not belief** (house all-sides law). The target is *censorship and the dead
  automation that enforces it*, never scripture and never the devout.
- **Quotation discipline.** Use real verses sparingly, exactly, and attributed per house/legal norms;
  do not manufacture large volumes of scripture-pastiche to fill pages. The power is concentration, not
  volume. (Coordinate with STYLE_GUIDE on how much verse appears on the page.)
- **No proselytising and no mockery — either direction.** The book neither preaches the faith nor
  debunks it. It dramatises a *true book kept true by brave people,* and lets the reader feel the awe.
