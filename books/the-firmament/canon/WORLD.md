# WORLD — *The Firmament*

> The lived texture of the flat world, the machine that tends it, and the round Earth beyond the wall.
> Defers to PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS for the *why*; this file is the *feel*.

## The Vault (the World) — the flat disc

- **Shape as cosmology.** A broad, low disc floor under a domed roof (**the firmament**), lit by a
  single engineered light that runs a track from rim to rim (**the day-track**). Rise, noon, set —
  the schedule is the sky. Night is the track parked and dimmed. This is creation, physics, and clock,
  all one. (It is *accurate* — of a disc. Never staged as a lie.)
- **The ice wall.** The cryogenic seedbank perimeter runs as a cold ring around the inhabited disc: a
  literal wall of frost and machinery marking the edge of the world. To touch it is taboo; to walk into
  it is not done. The keeper-caste service it; everyone else keeps away. Beyond it — the people are
  taught — is nothing, the end of creation. (It is the freezer, then the seal.)
- **The fields and the ration.** A closed farm under the day-track; recycled water; rations dispatched
  on a schedule no living hand is seen to set. Scarcity is real and managed, and the management is
  invisible — which is exactly how the autopilot hides in plain sight.

## The frozen period (1970s-analog, REAL — the amber)

- **Objects & idiom.** Analog and tactile: dials and toggles, magnetic tape, printed record on
  fan-fold paper, warm plastic and Formica, hand-woven textile, valve-glow and CRT. Legible technology —
  a child can see how a switch works, which is *why* the machinery invites the keeper-caste to notice
  seams.
- **Culture in amber.** Twelve generations of *drift within a frozen frame* — dialect, ritual, folk
  memory, and myth all evolve; the *idiom* (aesthetic, assumptions, objects) does not turn over. The
  people feel modern to themselves and are, to an outside eye, centuries preserved.
- **Why it matters on the page.** The reader should feel the *slight wrongness* of a world that never
  updates — the seam the walker learns to read (Dark-City / 13th-Floor). And it loads the deep-time
  gun: outside, the real Earth has moved on by centuries.

## The tending hand (the autopilot) — Dark-City kinship, no malice

- The Vault runs hands-free: light, cold, air, water, ration, record — all maintained by a dead
  generation's automation that simply *continued.* No throne, no priest-king necessarily — a *system*.
- **How it shows without a face:** the too-regular light; the record that quietly corrects itself; the
  ration that responds to behaviour; the wall-walkers and seam-readers who are "reassigned" and not seen
  again. The dread is bureaucratic and total precisely because *no one chose it.* (Boot = CANON_CHOICES
  G2; harden before drafting past ch-02.)

## The society (Silo kinship — caste, ration, taboo)

- **The keeper/maintenance caste.** Closest to the machinery; service the seams; devout; the source of
  both the plants (SCRIPTURE) and, ambiguously, the enforcers (the boot). The protagonist is of this
  caste (recommended, G1).
- **The taboo.** Not a law so much as an unthinkability: one does not touch the ice wall, one does not
  read the un-cut book, one does not ask what tends the light. The most effective control is the one
  that never has to be enforced because it cannot be *conceived.* (The quietest, most insidious boot —
  woven under the automated one.)

## The outside (Movement III) — the round Earth

- **Green, silent, recovered.** Centuries after the flare, nature has reclaimed the surface; a
  ruined-and-overgrown world, beautiful and empty (I-Am-Legend). The first thing that is *wrong* to the
  walker is that there is no roof — and then that the horizon *curves the wrong way* and never ends.
- **The verses come true here** (SCRIPTURE §2): the circle of the earth; no ends to walk to; the
  firmament peeled back to real weather and real sky; and the keystone — the ground she stood on all her
  life turning out to hang *upon nothing.*
- **The seed of the others** (one, lean — G5): a signal, or a second wall on the far horizon, or a
  stranger carrying a *round* scripture the disc-worlders would call heresy. Planted; not resolved.

## Sensory palette (for the drafting pass)

- **Inside:** warm, dim, close, hum of machinery, smell of recycled air and growing things, the
  day-track's slow arc, frost-breath at the wall. Sound of *maintenance* always just under hearing.
- **The seam:** cold, geometric, too-regular; the wall's machinery; the place where the tended world
  shows its edges.
- **Outside:** overwhelming — wind, weather, distance, uncontrolled light, birdsong in a dead city,
  the vertigo of a sky with no ceiling and a ground with no floor beneath it.
