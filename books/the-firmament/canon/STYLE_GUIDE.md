# STYLE_GUIDE — *The Firmament*

> Voice laws for drafting. The register: **grounded, sensory, close, unhurried in the world and sudden
> at the seam.** Literary dystopian, not pulp; awe delivered clean, never captioned.

## The register

- **Close third, present-leaning in the two detonations.** Live inside the walker; the world is only as
  large as she can see — which is the whole point in Movement I. (POV = CANON_CHOICES G1.)
- **Native and earthed.** Concrete nouns, working verbs; the tactile analog world (WORLD) does the
  characterising. No floating "world English," no lecture, no as-you-know dialogue.
- **The amber shows in the objects, not in narration.** Never say "it was like the 1970s." *Show* the
  toggle, the tape, the fan-fold record, the warm plastic. The reader dates the world; the prose never
  does.

## The three anti-sag rules (velocity — the africangold rule)

1. **Idea as detonation, never as lecture.** Every reveal is *dramatised as event* — the walker seeing,
   touching, remembering — never explained in a speech or an essay-chapter. If a paragraph starts
   teaching the reader the premise, cut it and stage it instead.
2. **No dead middle.** Movement I earns its slowness by *accreting seams* (the too-regular light, the
   disappeared, the verse that won't fit) — dread is motion. If a scene neither tightens the dread nor
   advances the walk to the wall, it is cut.
3. **Trust the image; do not caption the awe.** The cleanest moments — the wall opening, the sky with no
   roof, the ground hanging on nothing — land hardest with the *least* narration. Let the reader
   understand a half-beat before, or with, the walker. Never write "and she realised the profound
   truth that…" Show the thing; stop.

## The reveal-craft (the fulfilment must LAND, not lecture)

- The verses come true **as sensation first, meaning second.** She feels the horizon curve before she
  names it *the circle of the earth.* She feels the ground fall away — the vertigo of *upon nothing* —
  before the verse surfaces in her. The scripture arrives as *recognition*, not citation.
- **Reverence over cleverness** (SCRIPTURE §6). If a line reads as "gotcha, your religion was sci-fi,"
  it is wrong. The tone is *awe and vindication*, the devout proven right.
- **Concentration, not volume.** Actual verse text appears sparingly and exactly (SCRIPTURE §2, §6);
  most scripture is felt, alluded to, carried in the walker's memory — not printed in bulk. The keystone
  ("upon nothing") gets the fullest weight; the rest touch and go.

## Sentence-level

- **Short at the seam, longer in the world.** Let sentences breathe in the warm interior; let them
  fracture and quicken at the wall and in the emergence.
- **No genre-tell clichés.** No "little did she know," no prophetic portent, no chosen-one grammar
  (CHARACTERS). The dread is earned by *observation*, not by narrator foreshadowing.
- **Silence and space.** This is a book about a sealed world and a lonely one; use white space, pauses,
  the unsaid. The loudest thing outside is how quiet it is (I-Am-Legend).

## Do-not list

- No autopilot voice / villain monologue (MYTHOS).
- No "it was all a simulation" software framing — it is a *physical* enclosure (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS).
- No debunking of the flat teaching — only enlargement (all-sides / fulfilment law).
- No info-dump of the other arks — one lean seed (CANON_CHOICES G5).
- No captioned awe. Trust the image.
