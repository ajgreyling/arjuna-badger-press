# BACKSTORY_AND_PROGRAMS — *The Firmament*

> The founding history: why the Vaults were built, why they were built *flat and sealed*, and why a
> hardened radio net links them. This file grounds PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS in real, plausible history and
> gives the redaction a **motive that is trauma, not villainy** — deepening the all-sides law.

## 1. The scare (real history, extended)

- **The precedent: the Carrington Event, 1859.** A solar superflare struck a pre-electric world and still
  set telegraph lines sparking, burned operators, made aurorae bright enough to read by at midnight. Real.
- **The near-miss: July 2012.** A Carrington-grade coronal mass ejection crossed Earth's *orbit* and
  missed the planet by days. Real. Estimates of a direct modern hit ran to civilization-scale damage —
  grids, satellites, transformers that take years to replace.
- **The book's extension (near-future, plausible):** a decade of **rising solar activity** and one
  close-enough strike — a partial hit that browns out continents for months, kills satellites, and, more
  than anything, *frightens the institutions that model these things.* Not the end of the world; the
  **proof** that the end is a coin-flip and the coin is being flipped faster. The fear of a *second
  Carrington*, arriving on a wired planet, becomes the founding dread.

## 2. The programs ("under other auspices" — LOCKED: parallel, mutually aware)

The scare does not produce one grand ark; it produces **many quiet ones**, each built by a different
government or coalition, each dressed as something ordinary and funded through something else:

- a **seed and gene vault** (agricultural continuity),
- a **continuity-of-government bunker** (civil-defence),
- a **deep research station** (science budget),
- a **strategic reserve / archive** (heritage or resource auspices).

Every one of them, whatever its cover, is quietly built to the **same hidden spec: solar-flare
hardening** — deep siting, heavy shielding, no exposed grid dependence, closed life-support, and a
**self-sustaining sealed interior** meant to ride out a catastrophe of unknown length. They are Vaults
wearing other clothes.

- **LOCKED — they knew of each other.** The programs were aware of one another and coordinated at the top:
  a **deliberate distributed survival architecture** — spread the arks, spread the risk, and *link them*,
  so that if the sky fell, the pieces of civilization could find each other again. This is *why the radio
  net exists at all* (§3). It was designed in, by founders who thought of everything they could.

## 3. The hardened net (the HAM link — the hinge to Book II)

- **What it is:** a **solar-hardened HF/HAM radio link** connecting the Vaults — deliberately *analog*,
  deliberately *low-tech*, because analog HF is exactly what survives when satellites and grids do not.
  Long-wave, bounce-off-the-ionosphere, a mode that worked in 1930 and will work after the sky burns.
  (Fits the frozen-period tech — PREMISE §5 — with zero magic.)
- **Why it was built:** the founders' distributed architecture (§2) needed a nervous system that could
  survive the very disaster it was built for. The HAM net is that nervous system: the arks' phone line to
  each other, hardened to outlast everything above ground.
- **What became of it:** after the flare killed the operators and the outside world, the arks fell silent
  one by one — not destroyed, just *unmanned*, their people sealed into cosmology-not-schematic
  (PREMISE §4). The net went dark, colony by colony, call-sign by call-sign, as each ark forgot it had a
  radio room at all.
- **Book I payoff (LOCKED: a dark set + a dead log):** the walker finds, sealed and forbidden, a **hardened
  radio room** — dials and a hand-mic and a **logbook of call-signs** from the other colonies, the entries
  marching down the page and then stopping, decades ago, one silence at a time. And as Book I closes —
  after the fulfilment, after *upon nothing* — **one of them answers.** The world was never as empty, or as
  alone, as either the flat teaching or the graveyard-Earth made it seem. (PP-011 made concrete; the hinge
  to Book II.)

## 4. What this does to the redaction (motive = trauma, not villainy)

The scare reframes the closing-of-the-sky as an act that *began* in mercy:

- The founders sealed out the sky because **the sky nearly killed everyone.** "There is nothing above the
  firmament" started as protection — do not yearn for the thing that fell on us; the roof is your friend.
- Later hands (PREMISE §4, the autopilot + keeper-caste) hardened that protection into orthodoxy and cut
  the book (SCRIPTURE) to keep the world closed and manageable. But the *root* was fear, not power.
- So the indictment stays aimed at the **machine and the redaction**, and even *they* inherited a founding
  trauma. Nobody in this history is a cartoon. (All-sides law, at its most grounded.)

## 5. The martial rhyme (a founding practice calcified — LOCKED as a theme)

Same engine as the scripture, in the *body* instead of the book, and it belongs here because it is
founding history:

- The programs included **survival and combatives training** — real, practical skill for people who might
  have to fight and live rough in a broken world (bushcraft, self-defence, discipline, small-unit drill).
- Twelve sealed generations with **no enemy and nowhere to fight** hollowed the practice of its purpose.
  The forms were kept — perfectly, reverently — but the *why* was cut away, exactly as the seam-words were
  cut from scripture. The combatives became **liturgy**: a religion of movements whose meaning is lost,
  practised because the ancestors practised it.
- **The payoff (spans both books):** in Book II, out in the broken world, the forms become *practical
  again* — and the descendants who kept them as prayer discover their prayer was always a weapon and a
  craft. The liturgy re-earths into skill. (A hopeful mirror of the scripture's fulfilment: a preserved
  true thing, meaning restored by contact with the world it was made for.)
- Book I plants this lean (the walker's caste keeps such forms as ritual — one or two grounded beats,
  never a lecture). Book II fires it. (CHARACTERS; SERIES.)

### 5a. The register of the forms (the tonal key — LOCKED 2026-07-21)

**Governing simile: they do the forms with the gravity of Amish kin raising a barn.** Not sport, not
display, not a dojo's crisp aggression — *communal sacrament.* The whole caste moving together in
the same slow shapes at the same hour, wordless, grave, wholly absorbed, because the ancestors did
and because doing it together is what makes them a people. A barn-raising is the exact note: hard
physical work performed as worship, by everyone, in unison, with no one in charge and no one idle,
and a quiet joy underneath the seriousness. The forms are *that.*

Binding consequences for drafting:
- **The killing purpose is fully forgotten, and this is the sleeper knife in the body.** A movement
  that is, in its origin, a throw / a choke / a strike / a break is performed as a *blessing* — named
  for a season, a gesture of thanks, a shape in a prayer. The reader raised on any grappling art will
  *recognise the technique* under the liturgy (the hip that loads a judo throw; the frame that is a
  krav-maga defence; the "way of no way" flow) exactly as the flat-earther recognises his proof-texts
  — and feel the same floor drop later when Book II re-earths the prayer into a weapon. Same bait,
  same UNTIL, in the flesh instead of the book.
- **Reverence, never irony** (mirrors the scripture rule). The forms are beautiful and the devotion
  real. We never wink that "it's really fighting." The recognition is the reader's to make; the
  characters make it only in Book II, out in the world.
- **Source arts stay off-page as *names*** — no character says "judo" or "krav maga." Those are the
  founders' vanished vocabulary. On the page the caste has its own liturgical names for the shapes
  (working: *the Standing, the Yielding, the Turning-away, the Way-of-No-Way*), and the founders'
  hybrid origin (jiu-jitsu / judo / krav maga / wushu / the way of no way) lives only here in canon.
- **Amish/Boer grit substrate** (THE_COURT_TWELVE) makes this consistent cast-wide: plain, hard-
  handed, faith-shaped people to whom disciplined communal physical practice *is* devotion already.
