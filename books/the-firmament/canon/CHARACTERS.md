# CHARACTERS — *The Firmament*

> Original cast. Names are placeholders until locked (CANON_CHOICES G3). Voice laws below are binding.
> The anti-trope rules matter: no chosen-one, no slacker-savant, no villain who twirls. The system is
> the antagonist; the people are people.

## The Walker — protagonist (recommended: single close-third POV)

- **Working name:** *(TBD — G3)*. A young woman of the **keeper/maintenance caste**.
- **Why her:** close enough to the machinery to *see the seams*; devout enough that an un-redacted verse
  *wounds* her rather than merely intriguing her; ordinary enough that her courage costs her and is not
  a birthright. She is **not** a chosen one and **not** a secret genius. She is faithful, competent, and
  unable to un-know what she has seen. That is the whole engine of her.
- **Arc:** remembering → transgression → fulfilment. She receives the *"upon nothing"* verse (SCRIPTURE),
  cannot reconcile it with a floor she can stand on, and the irreconcilability walks her to the wall.
  Outside, her faith is not broken but **enlarged** — the words she was given come true, and she becomes
  the seedbank's living purpose: the one who must re-seed. (Her mercy / her burden is a series thread.)
- **Do NOT:** make her a rebel-against-faith. Her devotion is *why* she breaches; she is too faithful to
  abide a cut book.

## The Plant — the one who hands her the page

- A senior keeper; devout, patient, has passed the un-redacted text for years at real risk. Likely does
  not live to see outside — a runner in the relay whose faith the walker carries through the wall. His
  courage is quiet and total; he is the book's conscience. (Never played for irony — SCRIPTURE reverence
  rule.)

## The Keeper-caste / the boot (morally grey — G2)

- Not a person but a *pressure*, embodied in one or two faces: a caste-elder or overseer who half-knows
  the world is tended, and keeps the flat order for rations, peace, and the terror of the alternative.
  **Complicit, not evil.** Some are true believers who would be as shattered as anyone by the truth. The
  reader should understand them, even pity them — the machine wears their faces because someone has to
  service it.

## The Autopilot — the antagonist with no face

- The dead operators' automation. It is *characterised* only through its effects: the too-regular light,
  the self-correcting record, the reassignment of seam-readers. **No voice, no POV, no explanation
  scene.** Its menace is patience and reach, not malice. (MYTHOS: no villain monologue, ever.)

## The Founders — the loved dead

- Present only in the Prologue and in the un-redacted text they carried in. Competent, kind, grieving
  people sealing their children away from a dying world with the best cosmology-model and the best
  machine they had. The book must *love* them — every cruelty downstream is emergent, never their intent.

## The Stranger (Movement III seed — lean, G5)

- If used: a survivor from *another* ark, carrying a *round* scripture — proof, planted and unexplained,
  that other efficient truths and other cut books exist. One scene, one seed, no resolution. (Series.)

## Voice laws (binding)

- **Native, earthed, no lecture.** No character explains the premise to the reader. The physics
  (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS) is *dramatised*, never delivered as a speech. (Anti-sag; STYLE_GUIDE.)
- **The devout are never comic relief or dupes.** Reverence is a *character* law, not just a theme one.
- **No chosen-one grammar.** No prophecy that names her, no secret bloodline, no "you were always meant
  to." She chooses; it costs; that is all.
- **The machine never speechifies.** No AI villain explaining its plan. Its horror is that it has none —
  only a steady-state it maintains.
