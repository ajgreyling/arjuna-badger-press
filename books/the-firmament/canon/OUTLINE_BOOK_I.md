## MOVEMENT I — THE FLAT WORLD (dread by accretion) · ch 1–7 · ~44k

### ch-01 — The near wall ✅ (~925w drafted; extend to ~6k)
Neva at her keeper's service; the analog amber; the too-regular light; the taboo wall; Ines gone, her
readings rewritten in a clear hand that isn't hers. The first seam, taken straight. PP-001, PP-002,
PP-007, PP-012. *(Drafted opening; needs extension to full chapter length — the day's work, the caste,
her mother, the texture of the world believed.)* ◻ extend

### ch-02 — The clear hand ◻ ~6k
The world lived fully, so the reader *believes* it. Neva's home, her caste, the ration, the rhythm of
days on the track. She quietly tries to find where Ines went; every door is soft and closed. Introduce
the plant (senior keeper) at a distance — a man who watches her noticing. Submerged galgehumor of sealed
people (DARK_HUMOUR, Bk I register). Function: deepen belief + first unease. PP-012, PP-003 (seed).

### ch-03 — What the keepers keep ◻ ~6k
The service in full: the frost-line, the machinery, the liturgy of the work. The kept combatives/ritual
shown lean (PP-013 seed, martial rhyme). The redacted scripture as lived faith — Neva devout, unquestioning,
warm. Establish what she'd be *losing.* Function: make the faith real and loved before it's tested.

### ch-04 — The plant ◻ ~6.5k
The senior keeper makes contact. The un-redacted page pressed into her hand — the *"upon nothing"* verse.
It will not fit a floor she can stand on. His quiet, total faith; the risk he carries. The grit enters
the pearl. PP-004 (plant), PP-009. Detonation-adjacent emotionally. Function: ignition.

### ch-05 — The verse that won't fit ◻ ~6k
Neva cannot un-see it. She starts, against every taboo, to *watch* — the light, the record, the wall.
The record begins editing around *her* (soft clock tightens). She finds another un-redacted fragment;
the underground hinted (samizdat). PP-004, PP-012, PP-005/006 (seeded as "circle"/"ends" she now reads
differently). Function: obsession; the world starts to feel tended.

### ch-06 — The tightening ◻ ~6.5k
The ration responds; a warning, soft and bureaucratic. The plant is at risk. Neva torn between faith and
the crack in it. The keeper-caste's grey complicity shown (G2) — people keeping the peace, understandable
and wrong. Function: raise cost; the machine's no-malice pressure. PP-003, PP-012, PP-009.

### ch-07 — DETONATION: the disappearance ◻ ~6k
Neva witnesses (or all-but-witnesses) a seam-reader "reassigned" — the machine's tending made undeniable.
Possibly the plant himself, or the last person she trusted. The world is *maintained*, and maintenance
eats the curious. The question becomes unbearable. **Mv I set-piece.** PP-003, PP-012, PP-009 (relay:
his faith passes to her). Function: she must go to the wall.

## MOVEMENT II — THE SEAM (courage; the walk) · ch 8–12 · ~38k

### ch-08 — Resolve ◻ ~7k
The unforgivable decision. Cost named: no coming back to the world as it was. She carries the plant's
faith and the un-redacted pages. Grief and terror. Maybe a farewell she can't explain (her mother). PP-009,
PP-002. Function: commit.

### ch-09 — The approach ◻ ~7.5k
The journey to the wall through the caste's complicity — evading the tending, the record, the soft
enforcement. The cold ring nears; the machinery of the perimeter. Fear as motion. Function: rising dread,
the world's edge made physical. PP-002, PP-012.

### ch-10 — The frost ◻ ~7k
At the wall. The 13th-Floor edge: the world feels like it thins to seam — dread and recognition (feeling,
never literal render). She reads the cold like her father taught her and finds *wrongness* — the wall is
a made thing. Doubt and awe war. Function: the threshold approached. PP-002.

### ch-11 — The seal ◻ ~7k
She finds not the end of creation but a **seal** — a door, a mechanism, a *far side.* The full weight of
what this means. Perhaps the radio room glimpsed here (PP-011 seed reinforced) — a forbidden space she now
dares. Function: proof the world has an outside; final courage gathered. PP-002, PP-011.

### ch-12 — DETONATION: the breach ◻ ~6.5k
The wall opens. Present-tense, spare, no captioned awe. She steps through. Hold on the threshold — the
made world behind, the first unfiltered thing ahead. **Mv II set-piece.** PP-002. Function: the point of
no return; end of the sealed world.

## MOVEMENT III — THE ROUND (fulfilment · vocation · doorway) · ch 13–16 · ~28k

### ch-13 — No roof ◻ ~7k
Outside. Sensation before meaning: no firmament, uncontrolled light, weather, *distance.* The vertigo of
a sky with no ceiling. The green, silent, recovered Earth (I-Am-Legend). Terror and wonder. PP-001.
Function: the world made real and overwhelming.

### ch-14 — The circle of the earth ◻ ~7k
The verses come true as recognition, not citation. The horizon curves (*circle of the earth*); she walks
and the end recedes (*ends of the earth*). The devout vindicated — fulfilment, not disproof. She feels
the frozen-period gap begin (centuries gone). PP-005, PP-006, PP-007. Function: the soul of the book
starts to pay.

### ch-15 — DETONATION: upon nothing ◻ ~7k
The keystone. The ground she stood on all her life turns out to *hang upon nothing.* The verse the
redactors cut deepest fulfils. The redactors lied; the text never did; the plant was right; she carries
his faith into the open. **Mv III set-piece — the cleanest, quietest detonation.** PP-004. Function: the
book's central payoff.

### ch-16 — The answering call-sign ◻ ~7k
The deep-time ache lands fully (PP-007) — she stepped out of a diorama the world left behind. The
founders' love aches (PP-008). The re-seeding vocation falls to her (PP-010): the empty green Earth is a
field to plant, not a punishment. Then — the HAM reveal: the hardened radio room, the logbook of
call-signs gone silent, and **one answers** (PP-011). Book I's arc complete (breach → fulfilment →
vocation); the world becomes infinite; hinge to Book II. Function: standalone resolution + series
doorway. PP-007, PP-008, PP-010, PP-011.
