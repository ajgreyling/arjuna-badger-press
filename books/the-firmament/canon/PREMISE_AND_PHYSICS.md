# PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS — *The Firmament* (SSOT)

> The single source of truth for *why the world is flat and the cosmology is honest.* Every drafting
> decision defers to this file. The governing law: **nobody lied; the flat teaching is an accurate
> description of the enclosure, mistaken across generations for a description of the universe.**

## 1. Why flat is the efficient shape (this is the knife — keep it grounded)

A closed, self-sustaining world has to do four things, and a **flat disc under a dome** does all four
better than a sphere or a sprawl:

- **Shield.** Radiation and thermal load are managed over a single broad plane with one thick roof (the
  "firmament") rather than wrapped around a ball. One up, one down. Simple mass budget.
- **Pressure & air.** A low disc with a domed ceiling gives a shallow, stable atmosphere column — easy
  to circulate, easy to scrub, no deep gradients. (A sphere is the efficient *pressure vessel*; a disc
  is the efficient *habitat floor*. Different arcologies chose differently — see §7.)
- **Light.** One engineered light source on a track over a flat plane lights every field evenly. Rise
  and set are just the track's ends. Day and night are a *schedule*, and the schedule is scripture.
- **Cold storage.** The seedbank — the whole *point* of the ark — must stay cryogenic. The vault is run
  as a **perimeter ring** around the inhabited disc: the cold border is the literal edge of the world.
  **This is the ice wall.** Beyond it is not "the end of creation" — it is the freezer, and past the
  freezer, the seal.

So the founders' cosmology — *flat disc · dome above · light on a track · ice at the rim* — is not
myth dressed as physics. It **is** the engineering spec, taught as creation because, to anyone born
inside, it *is* creation. (Do not let a character "expose" it as a lie. It was never a lie.)

## 2. The arcology (the machine)

- **Name (working):** *the Vault* to its keepers; *the World* to everyone else. (Final name a canon
  gate — TITLES / WORLD.)
- **Purpose:** humanity's seedbank-and-survival ark — genetic and cultural cargo meant to outlast a
  catastrophe and **re-seed** the Earth. The people are not passengers to a destination; they are the
  *living half of the seedbank*, kept alive so that something human survives to plant the rest.
- **It never went anywhere.** (Buried-Earth arcology, LOCKED.) It is *on* the round Earth the whole
  time — sealed, shielded, waiting out the disaster. The "long voyage" the retrofuture idiom implies
  (§5) is a voyage through **time**, not space. That is a deliberate misdirection the reveal corrects.

## 3. The flare (the severing)

- A **solar superflare / coronal event** — the catastrophe the ark was built against — struck near or
  after the sealing. It killed the operators and the outside world alike: the crews who understood the
  Vault as a *machine* died, and with them the knowledge that it *was* one.
- What survived: the inhabited disc, sealed and shielded, and the people in it, who from that day
  forward had only the *inside* frame — the cosmology, not the schematic.
- Timing is a gate (CANON_CHOICES): the cleanest version is that the flare came **during or just after
  the sealing generation**, so the founders' children already inherited cosmology-not-schematic.

## 4. The autopilot (why it kept working — the horror of no villain)

- The Vault was engineered to run **hands-free** for the long survival window. It did its job *too
  well.* With the operators dead, it simply **continued** — light on the track, rations dispatched,
  cold held, air scrubbed, record kept — for twelve generations.
- **The system is the antagonist, and it has no malice.** It enforces the flat order not out of cruelty
  but because that is its programmed steady-state: it maintains the world it was told to maintain.
  (Dark-City kinship: reality tended by an unseen hand — here the hand is a dead man's automation.)
- Where the dystopian boot comes from is a **canon gate the author asked me to riff** — see
  CANON_CHOICES §"The boot." Working blend: *automated orthodoxy* (the system disappears wall-walkers
  and corrects records as maintenance routines) braided with an inherited *maintenance-caste* who
  half-keep the order for rations and peace. Neither is a mustache-twirl; both are the machine.

## 5. The frozen period (the retrofuture is REAL — LOCKED)

- The Vault was conceived, built, and sealed in a **1970s-analog technological era** — tactile, analog,
  CRT-and-toggle, Formica-and-macramé retrofuturism. This is **not a stylistic mood; it is a preserved
  period**, run forward by machines that never updated it.
- Consequences that must show on the page:
  - **The tech idiom** is analog and legible — dials, tape, switches, printed record. (Also *why* the
    cosmology is a 1970s cosmology-model: the founders encoded the science they had.)
  - **Culture is in amber.** Twelve generations of drift *within* a frozen frame — dialect, ritual, and
    myth evolve, but the *idiom* (the objects, the aesthetic, the assumptions) does not turn over.
  - **Deep time is the hidden clock.** Outside, centuries have passed and the Earth has moved on. The
    walker steps out of a diorama the real world left behind. The emergence is not into *space* but into
    *the future* — the I-Am-Legend silence plus the vertigo of the outdated survivor.

## 6. The outside (LOCKED direction: others survived)

- The flare killed *most* of the world; the Earth the walker emerges onto is **green, silent, recovered
  by nature** — an I-Am-Legend graveyard reclaimed. The seedbank's re-seeding purpose now, terribly,
  falls to whoever walks out.
- **But the Vault was not the only ark.** Other sealed worlds survived, each built to its own efficient
  geometry and keeping its own "true" cosmology (§7). Book I *seeds* this; it does not resolve it. First
  contact between arcologies is the series/deep-time horizon (NARRATIVE_ENGINES).

## 7. The other arcologies (series horizon — seed lean, do NOT over-build in Book I)

The single richest idea in the stack, kept mostly off-page in Book I:

- **Every ark was built flat/round/curved for its own efficiency, and each cosmology is a true
  description of its own hull:**
  - **The disc-worlders** (our Vault): *flat under a firmament, ringed by ice.* Correct — of a disc.
  - **A sphere-hull ark** (the efficient *pressure vessel*): its people *know* the world is a globe and
    think the flat-worlders are deluded. Correct — of a sphere.
  - **A cylinder-hull ark** (O'Neill/Rama efficiency): its people *know* the world curves up on both
    sides overhead. Correct — of a cylinder.
- **The payoff:** every arcology's *redaction* is a different local lie; the **original, un-redacted
  scripture predates all the arks** and fits *none* of the boxes and *all* of the real sky. The round
  Earth is the one place where the disc-worlders' and the globe-worlders' cosmologies *both* fail and
  the oldest text *both* survive. Fulfilment, at the scale of a civilization. (Do not stage the meeting
  of arks in Book I — a single true seed is enough: a signal, a second wall, a stranger with a *round*
  scripture the disc-worlders would call heresy and we now know is *also* true-of-a-box.)

## Hard rules (binding on drafting — the no-woo floor)

- **No magic, no simulation-as-software.** This is *physical* enclosure — a real sealed structure, real
  cold, real dome, real light-track — NOT a Matrix-style computed dream. The Matrix kinship is the
  *waking*, not the mechanism. The 13th-Floor "wireframe at the edge" is a *feeling* rendered as the
  physical seam of a real wall, never literal rendering. Keep it buildable.
- **The founders were competent and kind.** The Vault is good engineering by people trying to save
  their descendants. Any cruelty is emergent (the autopilot, the redaction), never designed-in malice.
- **The flat teaching is never "debunked."** The walker does not learn the elders were *wrong*; she
  learns the world was *bigger.* Every scene must preserve this. (SCRIPTURE_AND_REDACTION; MYTHOS.)
