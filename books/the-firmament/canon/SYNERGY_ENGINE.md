# THE SYNERGY ENGINE — *The Firmament* sequence

> The relationship continuity layer. Sibling to the **Continuity Engine** (which tracks *facts* — who
> knows what, what's been established): the **SynergyEngine** tracks *people* — who aligns, who grates,
> who wants whom, who's rising, who's isolated, and how all of it **shifts** across the arc. Referenced
> throughout drafting as a living layer, the way the crew narrates each other in shorthand:
> *"Jakobus's Id is ruling him again today."* Big-Brother-house real: nothing static, everything political.
>
> **Governing laws:** built from WOUNDS (THE_COURT_TWELVE, HUMAN_SPINE), seasoned by signs (bible-full,
> prose-winked), voiced in SA galgehumor (DARK_HUMOUR). The politics are *emergent from character*, never
> assigned. A tie can flip; that's the point — the SynergyEngine records the *current* state and its
> trajectory, and drafting updates it as scenes land.

## How to use it (the shorthand law)

- **In prose:** characters *read each other's state out loud* — psychology-as-teasing. The template is
  AJ's line: **"[Name]'s [faculty] is ruling [them] today"** — *"Ronel's Id is off the leash again," "the
  Sergeant's Superego's got the conch," "Tomas is all heart and no brakes this morning."* It's how the
  house talks; it's also live continuity (the reader tracks who's in which state).
- **In the room (Klaus/author):** before drafting a scene, check the current **alignment state** below
  and the **live tensions** — who should be circling whom *right now*, given what's just happened. Update
  the state after the scene. This file is the source of truth for "who's on good terms this chapter."

## 1. THE POWER BLOCS (the house factions)

Real houses form blocs. These are the standing alignments — they hold *until* stressed, then re-form.

### The Order Bloc — *"the ones who want a plan"*
Dawid ♑ (Ruler) · Elsabé ♍ (Sage) · Hettie ♋ (Caregiver, reluctant) · leans **Reuel ♊** (Shadow) —
which is the danger. Wants structure, rationing, chain of command, the wall watched. Competent and
suffocating. **Its fault line:** Reuel keeps trying to push it from *order* toward *control* — and Dawid,
who fears becoming Reuel, is the swing vote for the whole book's soul.

### The Freedom Bloc — *"the ones who want to breathe"*
Ronel ♏ (Huntress) · Steyn ♈ (Warrior) · Steve ♐ (Fool) · leans **Tomas ♓** (Lover). Self-reliant,
allergic to command, alive to the moment. Keeps the group human but can't build an institution to save
its life. **Its fault line:** Ronel won't lead and won't be led, so the bloc has no centre — it's a mood,
not a movement.

### The Heart / the Centre — *"the ones who hold it together"*
Marika ♌ (Queen) · Tomas ♓ (Lover) · Jorik ♉ (Innocent) · Hettie ♋ (Caregiver). The moral gravity.
Marika is the *true* authority (over Dawid's *formal* one). This bloc is the Bregman thesis embodied —
it wins the book by keeping people choosing each other. **Its fault line:** it runs on Marika, and Marika
is burning herself down (her wound).

### The Unaligned — *"the wildcards"*
**Neva ♎ (Creator/Bridge)** — belongs to no bloc by design; the swing conscience; she's seen both sides
of the wall so she can broker any of them. **Ivo ♒ (Magician)** — checked out into the workbench; votes
with whoever leaves him alone; a bloc of one until someone needs the radio.

## 2. THE ALIGNMENT MAP (dyads — the real politics)

> A→B pairwise. **Strength:** ⧻ strong · ⁓ warm · ~ neutral · ✕ friction · ⚡ volatile (both).
> These are the *starting-state* ties (Book II, ensemble assembled). Trajectory arrows show where the arc
> pushes them.

### Alliances (the load-bearing bonds)
- **Dawid ⧻ Elsabé** — the Order Bloc spine. He commands, she plans; mutual respect, no warmth. → *tested
  when her cold logic serves Reuel's ends and Dawid must choose.*
- **Marika ⧻ Hettie** — the two who feed and hold the group; the quiet matriarchy that actually runs
  things while the men argue rank. → *deepens; the real government of the house.*
- **Steyn ⧻ Steve** — the two who make each other laugh and make each other stupid; brothers-in-mischief.
  → *the bond that costs them when a joke goes too far in a crisis.*
- **Neva ⁓ Jorik** — the bridge and the innocent; she protects his hope because she remembers having it.
  → *she becomes the one who keeps him from breaking when the world does.*
- **Ivo ⁓ Elsabé** — two cold minds who trust competence over feeling; the only two who *don't* find each
  other exhausting. → *a late, unexpected tenderness — two armoured people, armour to armour.*

### Frictions (the standing grievances)
- **Dawid ✕ Ronel** — *the spine conflict.* Order that cannot command freedom. He needs her (best
  survivor); she despises being needed-as-a-tool. → *forced to save each other; grudging respect, never
  ease.*
- **Elsabé ✕ Jorik** — her cynicism reads his hope as childishness; his faith reads her cold as cowardice.
  → *the book's moral argument, in miniature; he changes her more than she changes him (Bregman).*
- **Reuel ✕ Marika** — the two true powers, one dark one warm; the house's real election. → *the climactic
  political fight — control vs. the choosing-each-other.*
- **Ronel ✕ Hettie** — the huntress who needs no one vs. the carer who needs to be needed; each is the
  other's reproach. → *Hettie's care finally reaches Ronel through a wound, not a want.*
- **Tomas ✕ Ivo** — the heart that needs presence vs. the man at the bench; *"come out of the workshop,
  Ivo" / "the workshop is the only place that makes sense."* → *Ivo learns the wire is between people.*

### Volatile (the ⚡ — could go either way, and does)
- **Ronel ⚡ Steyn** — Scorpio claws and Aries swagger; reads as antagonism, is half attraction. *"There
  goes Ronel, claws out, nipping at Steyn's soft ass."* → *see romantic currents; they circle.*
- **Dawid ⚡ Reuel** — the Ruler and his Shadow; Dawid is drawn to Reuel's certainty and repelled by where
  it leads; *Reuel is what Dawid becomes if fear wins.* → *the book's soul rides on Dawid refusing him.*
- **Neva ⚡ Reuel** — the Bridge who's seen the truth vs. the Shadow who'd rebuild the boot; he *wants her*
  (her authority legitimises him); she's the one who can unmask him. → *a chess game; she must not be
  seduced by his order.*

## 3. THE ROMANTIC / SEXUAL CURRENTS (adult, human, never leering)

Big-Brother-house real means desire is in the room. Handled per THE_COURT_TWELVE (mutual, embodied,
never ogled), DARK_HUMOUR (the crew *notices and teases*, warmly), and **CONTENT_RATING** (New Adult
18+, **fade-to-black**).

> **The ceiling (binding — CONTENT_RATING):** sex is *real and named*, never *choreographed on the page.*
> The camera cuts before the act; the book draws the **consequences**, not the anatomy. And this is where
> the SynergyEngine earns its keep: a coupling matters for **what it does to the house** — the shifted
> blocs, the jealousy, the betting pool, the morning-after politics — far more than for the act. **Imply
> the act; draft the aftermath.**

- **Ronel ⚡ Steyn** — the slow-burn antagonists. Two Id-led bodies who fight because they can't say the
  other thing. If it lands, it's combustible and probably bad for them both; the house has a *betting pool*
  (galgehumor). *Ronel would rather die than admit it; Steyn would announce it at dinner.*
- **Tomas → (open)** — the Lover loves everyone a little; his open heart is a current that unsettles the
  guarded ones (Elsabé, Ronel). Not predatory — *generous*, which is its own kind of pressure.
- **Ivo ⁓ Elsabé** — the quietest, truest possibility: two people who've armoured against feeling,
  recognising themselves in each other. A slow, adult, unshowy warmth. The house would *never* see it
  coming (and that's the tenderness).
- **Marika — widowed-of-the-group** — her love is maternal-total, spent on everyone, leaving nothing for
  herself; a quiet ache the book honours (her wound). Any romantic thread for her is *late* and *earned.*
- **Reuel — desire as leverage** — he doesn't love; he *acquires.* His interest in Neva is political
  seduction (§2). Keep it menacing, never titillating.

## 4. THE ISOLATES & THE SCAPEGOAT DYNAMIC (the darkest house politics)

Houses turn on someone. Track who's currently *out*:
- **Ivo** drifts to isolation by choice (the bench) — the house tolerates it until a crisis needs him and
  resents that it does.
- **Reuel** engineers *others'* isolation — he's the one who'd nominate a scapegoat to consolidate the
  Order Bloc. Watch him move against **Jorik** (the innocent is the easiest to paint as a liability) — and
  Marika/Neva blocking it is a recurring political beat.
- **Ronel** self-isolates and the house lets her — until her absence in a crisis is felt, and they learn
  they *need* the one who needs no one.

## 5. THE TRAJECTORY (how the politics SHIFT — the arc)

The SynergyEngine's whole point: the map *moves.* Rough arc for the drafting to honour and update:
- **Assembly (early Bk II):** blocs form along wounds; friction is comic and low-stakes; the betting pool,
  the teasing, the vinyl party ease it (SET_PIECE_VINYL_PARTY — the bond the later crisis strains).
- **Pressure (mid):** a real crisis (a failed colony contact, a resource cliff, a death) hardens the blocs;
  Reuel exploits it to push Order→Control; alliances are tested; the ⚡ ties resolve one way or another.
- **The fracture (late-mid):** the house nearly breaks — a scapegoating, a betrayal, Marika at the end of
  her strength, Dawid tempted toward Reuel. The Freedom Bloc and the Heart nearly lose.
- **The choosing (climax):** it's *not* won by force. Dawid refuses to become Reuel; the Heart holds;
  people choose each other across the fault lines (Bregman). The politics don't *resolve* — they *mature*.
- **Update rule:** after each drafted scene, revise the affected dyads' state here. This file is never
  "done"; it's the current standings.
