# THE SYNERGY ENGINE — *The Firmament* sequence

> The relationship continuity layer. Sibling to the **Continuity Engine** (which tracks *facts* — who
> knows what, what's been established): the **SynergyEngine** tracks *people* — who aligns, who grates,
> who wants whom, who's rising, who's isolated, and how all of it **shifts** across the arc. Referenced
> throughout drafting as a living layer, the way the crew narrates each other in shorthand:
> *"Jakobus's Id is ruling him again today."* Big-Brother-house real: nothing static, everything political.
>
> **Governing laws:** built from WOUNDS (THE_COURT_TWELVE, HUMAN_SPINE), seasoned by signs (bible-full,
> prose-winked), voiced in SA galgehumor (DARK_HUMOUR). The politics are *emergent from character*, never
> assigned. A tie can flip; that's the point — the SynergyEngine records the *current* state and its
> trajectory, and drafting updates it as scenes land.

## How to use it (the shorthand law)

- **In prose:** characters *read each other's state out loud* — psychology-as-teasing. The template is
  AJ's line: **"[Name]'s [faculty] is ruling [them] today"** — *"Ronel's Id is off the leash again," "the
  Sergeant's Superego's got the conch," "Tomas is all heart and no brakes this morning."* It's how the
  house talks; it's also live continuity (the reader tracks who's in which state).
- **In the room (Klaus/author):** before drafting a scene, check the current **alignment state** below
  and the **live tensions** — who should be circling whom *right now*, given what's just happened. Update
  the state after the scene. This file is the source of truth for "who's on good terms this chapter."

Houses turn on someone. Track who's currently *out*:

  resents that it does.

## 5. THE TRAJECTORY (how the politics SHIFT — the arc)

The SynergyEngine's whole point: the map *moves.* Rough arc for the drafting to honour and update:
- **Assembly (early Bk II):** blocs form along wounds; friction is comic and low-stakes; the betting pool,
  the teasing, the vinyl party ease it (SET_PIECE_VINYL_PARTY — the bond the later crisis strains).
- **Pressure (mid):** a real crisis (a failed colony contact, a resource cliff, a death) hardens the blocs;
  Reuel exploits it to push Order→Control; alliances are tested; the ⚡ ties resolve one way or another.
- **The fracture (late-mid):** the house nearly breaks — a scapegoating, a betrayal, Marika at the end of
  her strength, Dawid tempted toward Reuel. The Freedom Bloc and the Heart nearly lose.
- **The choosing (climax):** it's *not* won by force. Dawid refuses to become Reuel; the Heart holds;
  people choose each other across the fault lines (Bregman). The politics don't *resolve* — they *mature*.
- **Update rule:** after each drafted scene, revise the affected dyads' state here. This file is never
  "done"; it's the current standings.
