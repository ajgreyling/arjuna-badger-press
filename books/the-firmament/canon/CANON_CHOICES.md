## LOCKED (2026-07-19, scaffold)

- **Standing.** Original IP, shelf book, series-capable. No borrowed characters/settings/devices;
  influence-kinship only (Silo · Dark City · The 13th Floor · The Matrix · I Am Legend · early-70s
  enclosed-ark SF). (PROJECT / MYTHOS)
- **The knife.** The flat cosmology is **true of the enclosure**, not a lie: flat is the efficient
  shape for a sealed world (shield / air / light-track / cold-perimeter). **Nobody lied; the believers
  are never fools.** (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS §1 — binding.)
- **The enclosure.** A **buried-Earth seedbank arcology** (the Vault). It never left Earth; the "voyage"
  is through time, not space. (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS §2)
- **The severing.** A **solar superflare** killed the operators and the outside world; the autopilot ran
  the sealed disc for **twelve generations**. The system is the antagonist and it has **no malice**.
  (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS §3–4)
- **The retrofuture is REAL.** The Vault is a **1970s-analog period frozen at the flare** — preserved,
  not stylistic. It dates the enclosure and powers the deep-time gap. (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS §5)
- **The soul: FULFILMENT, not disproof.** The un-redacted verses **come true** outside; the devout are
  **vindicated**; the redaction is indicted, not belief. (SCRIPTURE_AND_REDACTION §4 — binding.)
- **The hook: redacted vs un-redacted scripture; devout plants pass the original.** Orthodoxy is the
  innovation; the "heresy" is the memory. The **"upon nothing"** verse is the keystone. (SCRIPTURE §2–3)
- **The outside: others survived.** Earth is a green, silent, recovered graveyard (I-Am-Legend), AND the
  Vault was not the only ark — other efficient-geometry arks kept other true cosmologies. Book I **seeds
  once, resolves nothing.** (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS §6–7)
- **Structure.** Single volume, ~110k, three movements: **THE FLAT WORLD · THE SEAM · THE ROUND**, ≈
  I:40% / II:35% / III:25%. One "perfect"-bar detonation per movement (PLOT).
- **No-woo / no-software.** Physical enclosure, not a computed dream. The Matrix kinship is the *waking*,
  not the mechanism; the 13th-Floor "edge" is the physical seam of a real wall, never literal rendering.
  (PREMISE_AND_PHYSICS hard rules; MYTHOS)

## LOCKED — backstory & series (2026-07-19, second pass)

- **The founding scare = Carrington.** Real history (1859 Carrington Event; the 2012 near-miss) extended
  into a near-future of rising solar activity + a partial strike that *frightens the institutions.* The
  fear of a second Carrington on a wired planet is the founding dread. (BACKSTORY_AND_PROGRAMS §1)
- **The programs: parallel, mutually aware.** Multiple governments/coalitions built arks "under other
  auspices" (seed-vault, continuity bunker, research station, strategic reserve) — every one secretly to
  the same spec: **solar-flare hardening + a sealed self-sustaining interior.** They knew of each other
  and built a **deliberate distributed survival architecture**, linked by a hardened radio net.
  (BACKSTORY §2)
- **The hardened HAM net.** A solar-hardened, deliberately *analog* HF/HAM link between the arks — the
  founders' nervous system, built to outlast the disaster. Went dark colony-by-colony after the flare.
  (BACKSTORY §3; fits frozen-period tech, no magic.)
- **Redaction-as-trauma, not villainy.** "Nothing above the firmament" *began* as mercy (the sky nearly
  killed everyone); the machine + keeper-caste later hardened it into orthodoxy and cut the book. Root is
  fear, not power. Deepens the all-sides law. (BACKSTORY §4)
- **The martial rhyme (theme, LOCKED).** Founding combatives kept for twelve enemy-less generations
  calcified from *survival skill* into *liturgy* — the same "true thing, meaning cut away" engine as the
  scripture, in the body. Book I plants it lean; Book II re-earths it. (BACKSTORY §5)
- **G4 CLOSED — flare timing.** The flare struck **during/just after the sealing generation**, so the
  founders' children already inherited cosmology-not-schematic. (Matches the Prologue.)
- **G5 CLOSED — the Book-I series seed = the HAM reveal.** Not an abstract signal: the walker finds a
  sealed/forbidden **hardened radio room, a logbook of other colonies' call-signs gone silent decades
  ago — and, as Book I closes, one answers.** Concrete, earned, the hinge to Book II. (BACKSTORY §3; PP-011)
- **Series shape.** Book I standalone (dread→awe); **Book II = the reboot** (competence-joy: The Martian ×
  Captain Fantastic × Bregman's *Humankind*) — coordination/logistics of rebuilding civilization from
  manuals, analog radio, diesel, and hands. Premise captured; not scaffolded yet. (SERIES; BOOK_II_PREMISE)

## OPEN — gates to harden before the relevant movement drafts

### G1 · POV & the walker (Klaus recommendation below — needs AJ's yes) — **before Movement I**
Author said "let me recommend." **Recommendation: single close-third POV, one wall-walker, present-
leaning in the breach and the fulfilment.** Reasons:
- The Truman/Silo/Matrix engine is *strongest as one person's floor dropping* — braided POV would leak
  the twist and trade shock for dread; this book wants the shock of *remembering with her.*
- The reader must take the flat world at face value in Movement I; a single interior keeps us honest
  inside her frame with no outside vantage to spoil it.
- The deep-time / other-arks horizon can be seeded through *her* discovery (a signal, a second wall) —
  it does not need its own POV in Book I.
- **Recommended protagonist:** a young woman of the **keeper/maintenance caste** — close enough to the
  seams to start remembering, devout enough that the un-redacted verse *matters* to her, ordinary
  enough that her courage is human, not chosen-one. (CHARACTERS; the anti-"slacker-savant" rule.)
> *Decision needed from AJ:* confirm single-close-third + caste-outsider heroine, or steer.
