## The through-lines (what binds the sequence)

1. **A true thing, preserved past its context, meaning cut away — then restored by contact with the
   world.** The master theme, in three registers:
   - **the book** (scripture redacted, then *fulfilled* outside — Book I),
   - **the body** (combatives kept as liturgy, then *re-earthed* as skill — Book II, BACKSTORY §5),
   - **the machines & manuals** (frozen-period tech and its manuals, *rediscovered* as living craft —
     Book II: efficient analog data transfer, diesel husbandry, comms/crypto).
2. **Every ark kept a different efficient truth** (PREMISE §7). The disc-worlders' flat cosmology, a
   sphere-hull ark's globe, a cylinder-hull ark's curved sky — each true of its box, all reconciled only
   under the original un-redacted sky. Reaches full force in Book III+.
3. **The founders' distributed architecture** (BACKSTORY §2): the arks were *built to find each other
   again.* The HAM net is the nervous system; rebooting it is rebooting civilization.
4. **All-sides / fulfilment / no-woo** (MYTHOS) hold across every book. Indict the machine, never belief;
   vindicate, never mock; physical, never magic.
5. **People-first** (HUMAN_SPINE_AND_ARCHETYPES) — *the premise is the backdrop; competent, flawed people
   facing a new reality together are the story.* The master law, above every clever idea, in every book.
6. **The register: SA galgehumor** (DARK_HUMOUR_AND_REGISTER) — *ons lag, want anders huil ons.* A binding
   voice across the sequence; submerged in Book I's dread, full-voiced in Book II's reboot.

## The bridge (Book I → Book II)

Book I ends on **the answering call-sign** (BACKSTORY §3): a dark radio room, a dead log, and then one
voice. Book II opens on the far end of that wire — a *different* colony's ensemble — and the two threads
converge on the shared problem: **you can hear each other now; how do you actually rebuild?**
