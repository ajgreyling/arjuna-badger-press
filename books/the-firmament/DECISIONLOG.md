## Session 2026-07-21 — sleeper opening + martial-liturgy beat

### D-001 — POV committed: *sealed-in with Neva* (purest sleeper)
- **Call:** Reader knows exactly and only what Neva knows. Flat cosmology reads literally true on the
  surface; the machine-meaning is decoded only on reread.
- **Why:** AJ chose this explicitly (AskUserQuestion). It is the strictest sleeper and the one that
  makes the "flat-earther gets a hard-on UNTIL — science, bitch" bait-and-switch land hardest.
- **Consequence:** the founders'-POV Prologue cannot survive (it arms the reader on page one).

### D-002 — CUT the founders' Prologue
- **Call:** Removed `manuscript/PROLOGUE.md`; preserved verbatim in
  `canon/CUT_PROLOGUE_founders_pov.md` with a header explaining the cut and what to mine it for.
- **Why:** It is the Dark-City *theatrical-cut spoiler-voiceover* — the exact thing AJ said the
  opening must NOT do ("never plays on the fact that they thought their bunker was the firmament").
- **Reversible?** Yes — full text retained in canon. Never restore to the front; mine for the Book I
  midpoint reveal and Book II founder-flashbacks.

### D-003 — Resleepered ch-01: cosmology as double-edged truth, never authorial assertion
- **Call:** Rewrote flat-earth lines so each is literally true on the surface AND mechanically true
  underneath. Added: the **warm seam** in the wall that "breathes" (holy to Neva) but runs
  "dead-straight, and turned once, square" (built, to the reader); the **light that never varies in
  400 years** = *faithful* to her, *scheduled* to us ("a thing described that exactly… is the kind of
  thing you build"); Psalm 19's *the sun runs his course* as plainly watchable truth.
- **Why:** The prior draft asserted flat-earth as *authorial* fact ("the world was flat"). A sleeper
  needs the surface true and the machine buried. No narrator ever tips it.

### D-004 — Scripture upgraded to THREE strata (two mercies, one accident)
- **Call:** Rewrote `canon/SCRIPTURE_AND_REDACTION.md` from a two-book model to three:
  **Stratum 0** — real flat-earth proof-texts used AS-IS (mechanically true of the machine);
  **Stratum 1** — founders' *geo/sol-centric adapted* bibles (first mercy: spare the children the
  bigger world); **Stratum 2** — plants' *un-redacted original firmament* bibles (opposite mercy:
  don't leave them a tainted book). Expanded the load-bearing verse table with the full real proof-text
  set (firmament, waters above, circle, four corners, foundations-that-shall-not-be-moved, sun-runs-
  his-course, upon-nothing).
- **Why:** AJ's note — "good meaning people who built the place left geo/sol-centric adapted bibles /
  well-meaning dufuses left original firmament bibles to not leave them a tainted bible." The horror
  is two loves that both meant to save her, colliding, a dead machine enforcing the crueler one.

### D-005 — ch-03: the plant passes Neva an un-redacted leaf (*Job 26:7*)
- **Call:** Sarel palms Neva a hand-copied leaf at the Lighting feast — *"he hangeth the earth upon
  nothing"* — the one verse that fits no world she can stand on. Not heresy: *nonsense*, which means
  there's a world it's true of. Closed the chapter on Neva sitting between the two mercies.
- **Why:** Canon §3 — the un-redacted page is the grit that starts the pearl. Sarel was already coded
  as the man who almost says the forbidden thing; making him the plant is the economical call.
