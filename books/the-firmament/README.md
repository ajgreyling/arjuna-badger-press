# *The Firmament* *(working title — see canon/TITLES.md)*

An original dystopian sleeper. For twelve generations they knew the truth: the world is a flat disc
under a firmament, ringed by ice. They were right — about a world the size of a lifeboat. When the last
seal fails, one of them walks through the ice wall and out onto the round Earth, where the old,
forbidden verses come true one by one.

**The knife:** the flat cosmology was never a lie. Flat is the efficient shape for a sealed seedbank
world (shield · air · light-track · cold-perimeter), so the founders' teaching was an *accurate
description of the enclosure*, mistaken across generations for a description of the universe. Nobody
lied; the believers are never fools. The heresy is not "the earth is round" — it is "the world is
bigger than the world."

**The soul:** the reveal is **fulfilment, not disproof.** The un-redacted scripture the devout risked
their lives to pass — *the circle of the earth; he hangeth the earth upon nothing* — comes true outside.
The redactors are indicted; faith is vindicated.
