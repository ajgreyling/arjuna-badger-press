# Project — *The Firmament* *(working title)*

> **Type:** original dystopian sleeper · **Lineage:** original IP — engine-kinship only (below) ·
> **Status:** scaffolding · **Length:** one novel (series-capable) · **Substrate:** a sealed post-flare
> seedbank arcology, frozen in a 1970s-analog period, run by autopilot over a graveyard — breached at
> last onto the round, recovered Earth.

## The one-line

For twelve generations they knew the truth: **the world is a flat disc under a firmament, ringed by
ice.** They were right — about a world the size of a lifeboat. When the last seal fails, one of them
walks through the ice wall and out onto the round Earth, where the old, forbidden verses come true one
by one.

## The real story (the thesis that governs everything below)

**The premise is the backdrop; the people are the point.** The flat earth, the flare, the HAM net, the
diesel — all of it is the *stage.* The play is **competent people, left alone together, facing a new
reality, being messily, flawed, funny, frictional human about it.** Survival is merely the pressure;
character under pressure is the plot. And the register that carries it is **South African galgehumor —
_"Ons lag, want anders huil ons"_** (we laugh, because if we don't, we cry). This thesis sits *above*
every clever idea in the canon; when a scene serves the premise but reveals nothing human, it is the
wrong scene. See [`canon/HUMAN_SPINE_AND_ARCHETYPES.md`](canon/HUMAN_SPINE_AND_ARCHETYPES.md) and
[`canon/DARK_HUMOUR_AND_REGISTER.md`](canon/DARK_HUMOUR_AND_REGISTER.md).

## The engine (what makes it land)

The flat cosmology is **not a lie and its keepers are not fools.** A sealed arcology is built *flat*
because flat is the most efficient shape to shield, pressurise, and light a closed world; the "ice
wall" is the real cryogenic seedbank perimeter (the vault runs cold); the "firmament" is the real
load-bearing dome; the "sun" is a real engineered light on a real track. So when the founders taught
their children *the world is a flat disc under a firmament, ringed by ice,* **they were describing
their actual home with perfect accuracy** — a true description of the *enclosure*, mistaken across
generations for a description of the *universe*. Nobody lied. They were correct, about the wrong-sized
world. The heresy is never "the earth is round." The heresy is **"the world is bigger than the world."**

## Provenance & standing (original IP)

- **Original work.** All world, cosmology, cast, institutions, scenes, and beats are original Arjuna
  Badger Press work. No borrowed characters, settings, or named devices. This is a shelf book, not a
  homage or craft exercise.
- **Engine-kinship, not IP.** The book stands in a *tradition* — the sealed-community sleeper — and
  wears its influences openly at the level of **feeling and shape**, never text: **Silo** (the sealed
  world that cannot conceive of outside; the forbidden-knowledge taboo; the caste-and-ration order);
  **Dark City** (a reality *maintained* by an unseen hand; the one who starts remembering); **The 13th
  Floor** (drive to the edge and the world turns to unrendered seam — the wall is where the set ends);
  **The Matrix** (the breach into a truth worse and freer than the dream); **I Am Legend** (the last
  one awake in a dead world); and the analog-voyage-that-is-a-set texture of early-70s enclosed-ark SF
  (**Silent Running**, **Space:1999**, **The Starlost**). We keep the *machines of those stories*, not
  their letters. See [`canon/MYTHOS.md`](canon/MYTHOS.md).

## The author's brief (the craft mandate — captured 2026-07-19)

- **The flat truth was structurally the most efficient** — that is the whole knife. No villain, no dupe.
  The redaction and the autopilot are the machine; the believers (especially the devout) are never the
  fools. (Honours the house **all-sides-framing law**: indict the machine, not the people.)
- **The reveal is FULFILMENT, not disproof.** When the walker steps onto the round Earth and the
  un-redacted verses come true — *the circle of the earth; he hangeth the earth upon nothing* — the
  devout are **vindicated**, not mocked. The redactors lied; the *text* never did. Faith survives the
  reveal, transformed. This is the book's soul and it is binding. See
  [`canon/SCRIPTURE_AND_REDACTION.md`](canon/SCRIPTURE_AND_REDACTION.md).
- **The 70s-analog world is REAL, frozen at the flare.** Not a stylistic mood — a *preserved period*.
  It dates the enclosure, explains the tech idiom and the cosmology-model, and sets up the **deep-time**
  gap: they step out of a diorama the real Earth left behind centuries ago.
- **Others survived.** The arcology was not the only ark. Other sealed worlds kept *other* efficient
  truths (a sphere-hull people who "know" the world is round; a cylinder-hull people who "know" it
  curves up on both sides) — each a true description of its own box, each heretical to the others, all
  reconciled only outside, under the original un-redacted sky. Series/deep-time horizon; drafted lean,
  seeded early.

## The structural engine (what makes it *this* book)

**The set ends at the wall.** The book's governing image, fused from the 13th-Floor edge, the Silo
taboo, and the Dark-City maintained-world: everything the protagonist has ever called *the world* is a
tended enclosure, and the ice wall is where the tending stops. The dread of Movement I is the slow
recognition of *seams* — the too-regular light, the redacted verses, the disappeared wall-walkers, the
period that never changes — and the courage of Movement II is walking to the seam anyway.

**The verses come true in real time.** The trilogy-grade detonation of this book is not a weapon; it is
a *fulfilment*. The un-redacted scripture the plants risked their lives to pass is proven — line by
line — the moment the walker is outside. The reader should feel the older, deeper text *land* as event,
never as sermon (STYLE_GUIDE: idea-as-detonation; no captioning the awe).
