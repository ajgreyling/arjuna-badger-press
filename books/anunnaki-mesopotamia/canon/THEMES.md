- Theme(s) in action, never spoken:  
  *Survival by adaptation* — a conquered people taking their conquerors’ stories and making them their own  
  *Restoration of genius* — wonder handed back to the written-off, not the sky  
  *The “allowed to say maybe” gift* — the reader is left in the gap, able to say “I don’t know,” which is the beginning of wonder  
  *The humanity law* — ND crew see what others don’t because of their wiring, not in spite of it; the local’s dignity is inviolable; scripture is treated with reverence, never sneer  
  *Transmission* — stories survive by being retold, repurposed, and inherited, not in pure form but through contact and need  
- Dignity contracts:  
  Never sneer at scripture or the faithful; never sneer at those who dream of aliens; the residue is always human, not a spaceship  
  The crew are never “fixing” themselves or the local; the story is not outsiders cracking the code, but a restoration of credit
