## With thanks

This book was written in the spirit of a curiosity I learned from someone else: the discipline of
taking the official story seriously, telling it straight and with real respect, finding the one
honest place it does not quite close — and then trusting the reader to decide.

**With genuine thanks to AJ Gentile**, whose work lit that way of looking for me. The stories here
are my own, the people invented or freshly drawn; what I borrowed is only the *posture* — eyes open,
sneer off, wonder intact. I am grateful for it, and I hope I have done it honour.

And a quieter thanks to the keepers of the clay — the decipherers who proved, under seal, that the
dead script could be read; the scholars who showed, without ever once sneering at anyone's faith,
that the first chapters of the most-read book on Earth have a Mesopotamian genealogy; and the
Assyrian, Iraqi, and Kurdish people who are the living heirs of the place where writing began. The
wonder in this book is *theirs.* So is the achievement the genre tried to hand to the sky. I have
tried to hand it back.
