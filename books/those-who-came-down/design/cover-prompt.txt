### `those-who-came-down` — *Those Who Came Down*  (The Anunnaki, as the dreamers tell it)
> Portrait book-cover art, 2:3, cinematic matte painting, painterly realism, epic scale. NO text,
> no lettering, no typography anywhere. Ancient Mesopotamia at deep dusk: the flat marsh plain of
> the Edin between two rivers, reed beds and dark water channels catching the last bronze light.
> Low on the horizon hangs one enormous unfamiliar star — far brighter than Venus — casting a long
> gold track across the wet plain. From high in the indigo sky a single thin column of white-gold
> fire descends toward the marshes, a vertical burn like a falling sun, its light mirrored in the
> water. A stepped ziggurat stands small and dark on the far horizon. In the foreground, on a low
> mud-brick platform, a lone robed figure stands with his back to us, watching the descent. Very
> faint gold dust shimmers in the upper air, like pollen caught in light. Palette: clay-red, dusk
> gold, deep indigo shadow. Mood: austere, monumental, quiet, elegiac — awe without menace. No
> spaceships with visible hulls, no chrome, no cartoon aliens, no fantasy armour, no glowing runes.
> Leave the top 40% of the frame relatively calm and dark so title typography can sit over it.
