# MYTHOS_RULES — *Those Who Came Down*

> Committed mythos: the fringe is true **in this book's world**, full stop. The discipline is not
> ambiguity (that's the debunk twin's job) but *physics of the wonder* — what is allowed to be true
> here, and what stays mysterious.

## True in-world

1. **Nibiru** crosses every 3,600 years (one sar). Its sky is thinning; gold beaten to dust and
   lofted into the high air holds the warmth in. That is why they came. No other motive is offered.
2. **The Anunnaki** are long-lived, not immortal; they age by their own year, the sar. They eat,
   tire, grieve, and can die (Geshtu-e does).
3. **The making is real**: clay of the Abzu quickened with the blood of a slain god, carried in
   fourteen wombs. But the walkers of the Abzu were already upright, already clever-handed, already
   burying flowers with their dead **before** any god touched them. The making *hurried* something;
   it did not create the spark. This is binding.
4. **The flood is the crossing's tide** — the returning world drags the sea. The gods' crime is
   silence, not weather-making.
5. **What the gods gave** is measured and small: the crucible, the wheel's trick, the calendar of
   crossings. **What the humans made** is everything else — beer, the city, kingship's ceremonies,
   the walls, and writing. No god may be shown teaching a human to write.

## Stays mysterious

- What Nibiru looks like from its own ground; the deep politics of An's court.
- How the quickening works. Ninmah's hands are shown; the mechanism is not.
- Whether Enki's last lingering (Tablet XII) is body, memory, or story. Never resolved.

## Forbidden

- Cabals, bloodline management, "they are still among us," reptiles, grievance.
- Any scene where human achievement is handed down whole from the sky (the pillar, inverted here
  as in-world fact: the gods themselves are startled by what the clay does unprompted).
- Confirming or denying the neighbouring books. The shelf disagrees on purpose.
