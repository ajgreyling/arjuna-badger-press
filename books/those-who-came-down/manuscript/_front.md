# About this book

This is a fiction, and it is a particular kind of fiction: the story the late-night shelf has been
telling in fragments for fifty years, told once, whole, and committed. In these pages the
crossing-world is real, the gold is why they came, and the worker was made. The house has two other
books on this same clay. *The Walls of Uruk* tells the poem plainly, as the scribes wrote it.
*The Princely Offspring* follows the evidence and shows, wedge by wedge, why none of this happened.
Read either of them after this one and you will feel the shelf disagree with itself. That is not an
accident. Some stories are worth telling from inside even when the clay says otherwise, because the
hunger that wants them true is itself a true thing, and it deserves better than cardboard.

Told in twelve tablets, after the old custom.
