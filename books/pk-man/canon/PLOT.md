# Plot — *INTERFACE ONE* — 20 sequences

> Each sequence: a concrete problem, a testable result, an institutional consequence, a character
> cost. Two strands, alternating and never mixed (O-38): **[P] present, 2036–37, past tense,
> documents only** · **[E] erased, 1955–1987, present tense, full interiority, no corroboration
> possible.**
