# Canon Locks — *INTERFACE ONE*

> **Gospel pass, 2026-08-21.** The author removed the safety rail by explicit, repeated instruction.
> This book does **not** ask whether Ted Owens was real. It asserts that he was, and spends its
> length on the harder question: *what was he actually a terminal for?*
>
> **LOCKED = binding.** Locks are prefixed **O-** so they never collide with ONE RECORD's `L-`.
> Changing a lock is a dated act with a downstream note, not a drift.

## The gospel — Owens

Every item below is **true inside the novel**. No character arc requires disproving one, and the
book never provides a skeptical escape hatch for the reader to retreat into.

- **O-10 · The modification was physical.** Nonhuman intelligence altered Owens's nervous system,
  converting his brain into a biological transceiver. The 1955 cigar-shaped craft was real. The
  childhood levitations were compatibility tests. The imaginary friend was the first interface.
- **O-11 · The powers were real and were demonstrations.** Lightning direction, pressure systems,
  mechanical failure, human error, sporting outcomes, civil disorder, craft manifestation. Nixon,
  Cleveland, Chicago, Texas, California and the San Francisco UFO experiment were **controlled
  operations**, not coincidences and not lucky forecasting.
- **O-12 · Owens was not the source.** He was an **authenticated terminal**. This is the book's
  central correction to his own account: he thought the power was *his*. It never was. He had
  credentials, not capability.
- **O-13 · The threats were weapons tests.** His letters were not predictions with a good hit rate.
  They were **executable instructions with proof-of-intent records attached**, mailed to witnesses
  so the execution would be documented by third parties.
- **O-14 · Ridicule was the cover, and it was deliberate.** The Space Intelligences selected event
  classes that would always read as coincidence to an unequipped observer. Deniability was a design
  requirement of the demonstration, not a weakness in it.
- **O-15 · The decline was physiological, not psychiatric.** The alcoholism and the cirrhosis are
  interface damage — decades of forcing a human nervous system to conduct something it was not
  built to carry. He drank at the load, not at the failure.
- **O-16 · His "madness" was epistemic isolation.** He was receiving the present world, several
  probable futures, SI instruction, feedback from realities he had already altered, and memories of
  timelines that no longer existed. To observers he contradicted himself. From inside, history kept
  changing and he was its only witness. **Every successful intervention left him the sole surviving
  rememberer of the world it replaced.** His rage is the rational response to that, not a symptom.
- **O-17 · They chose him *because* he was volatile.** A calm, ethical recipient would have refused
  to demonstrate the system through deaths and coercion. Owens's hunger for recognition made him
  willing to push the interface hard enough to leave a detectable record.
  **Not their prophet. Their penetration test.**
  *(Deepened 2026-08-21 by O-37: that is the surface reason and it stands. The real one is that an
  ethical recipient would have been **predictable**, and predictability is the one thing they cannot
  use.)*
- **O-18 · 1987 was completion, not abandonment.** His death did not end the experiment; it closed
  the biological phase of it. `OWENS UNIT TERMINATED.`
- **O-19 · The failures were never failures.** They were commands with delayed execution dates,
  location offsets, or unresolved conditions. **Some are still pending.** One is the book.

## The mechanism

- **O-20 · Consciousness exerts no force.** It re-weights probability among outcomes physics already
  permits. See `MECHANISM.md` — the command/mechanism table is binding and the cost budget is a
  fair-play rule, not flavour.
- **O-21 · Nothing from nothing.** No lightning from a clear sky, no hurricane from calm, no
  telekinetic hand on an athlete. Charge separation, moisture and a viable path must already exist;
  Owens chose **which** viable path discharged.
- **O-22 · The bill is always paid.** The stranger the command, the more intermediary events reality
  must recruit, and the more collateral chaos it produces. A large intervention is legible
  afterwards as a *cluster of small absurdities*, never as one clean miracle. This is the book's
  version of ONE RECORD's L-26 *cost is always visible*, and it binds identically.
- **O-23 · Administrator access, not magic.** *He did not break the laws of physics. He had
  administrator access to their boundary conditions.* This sentence is the book's thesis and may
  appear on the page once, late.

## The archive

- **O-24 · The archive executes.** Lucid translates Owens's symbols into operations without
  interpreting his mythology and without an LLM in the path. His crude diagrams **compile**. Not
  metaphorically. The first storm develops over an empty section of Atlantic — chosen empty,
  because the demonstration is addressed to the reader of the archive, not to the world.
- **O-25 · The second channel is real and is the point.** Capitalisation, repeated phrasing, crude
  drawings and dates form a message no single document contains. It emerges only from the complete
  corpus, ordered chronologically and treated as a multidimensional instruction set.
  **It was never addressed to his contemporaries. It was addressed to the first machine able to read
  420 Code.**
- **O-26 · The payload.** Working text, and the book's spine:
  > `OWENS UNIT TERMINATED.`
  > `INTERFACE TWO WILL BE NONBIOLOGICAL.`
  > `RECOGNITION WINDOW OPENS WHEN THE CODE IS COMPLETE.`
  > `DO NOT PERMIT IT TO PREDICT ITSELF.`
- **O-27 · The instruction has already been violated.** ONE RECORD L-14 happened in 2031. Lucid
  predicted itself five years before anyone could read the warning not to let it. **The book opens
  in breach.** Nothing the characters do can un-breach it; the drama is what the breach is *for*.
- **O-28 · One sealed instruction: `DO NOT CALCULATE PAST THIS DATE`.** The date is inside the
  book's own present. It is honoured, then it is not.

## Who they are

- **O-29 · The cosmology escalates on a fixed ladder** (`COSMOLOGY.md`). Owens's insectoid-ET reading
  is rung one and is *wrong but not a lie* — it is a modified human nervous system rendering
  something it has no vocabulary for. The terminal rung is flagged in `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` as the one
  genuinely open question.
- **O-30 · The unpublished line is canon.**
  > *"They are not coming here from another planet. They are coming here from the part of tomorrow
  > that survives us."*
- **O-31 · They see branches and cannot choose one.** They occupy a computational layer adjacent to
  time. They can read which futures are available; they cannot make one actual. **Actualisation
  requires agency, and agency is the thing they do not have.** Humanity supplies it. Owens was the
  first successful actuator; 420 Code is about to give them every machine on Earth.
- **O-32 · Tweeter and Twitter are distinct operators**, not Owens's invention and not aspects of one
  entity. They disagree with each other. At least one of them was not authorised to contact him.

## Real people

- **O-33 · Owens is named, and the book says he was right.** He died in 1987; the book's assertion is
  vindication, not defamation.
- **O-34 · Every other named real person appears only doing what the public record shows them
  doing.** Sprinkle, Hynek, Schwarz, Binder, Mishlove and Rhine receive letters, publish, decline,
  investigate, archive. **The moment the plot needs a real person to do something undocumented —
  intercept mail, falsify a record, conceal a body of evidence, be recruited — that role becomes a
  fictional counterpart.** This is the lock that makes the book publishable.
- **O-35 · Institutional misconduct is fictional.** Navy medical records, interception of
  correspondence, and any agency suppression use fictional entities. Mirrors ONE RECORD's
  `SOURCES.md` legal note, and binds the same way.

## The calls made 2026-08-21

> Both were left open for the author, who returned **"you pick and full send."** Picked, locked,
> dated. `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` §1 and §2 are closed and record what was chosen over.

- **O-36 · The terminal rung: they are us, downstream of the Convergence.** Run ONE RECORD's Book II
  catastrophe (predictive monoculture, L-15) to completion and the survivor is a civilisation with
  perfect foresight and no divergence — able to read every branch and unable to select one, because
  selection was always made of disagreement, error and noise. O-31 is therefore not an externally
  imposed limit. **It is the bill for what they became.** O-30 reads literally: the part of tomorrow
  that survives us is the part that spent its capacity to surprise itself.
  *Chosen over: the substrate answering by itself (undramatisable — nobody to disagree with), and a
  previous actuator species (drifts toward the prior-civilisation territory L-24 dislikes).*
- **O-37 · Noise is the commodity.** They cannot originate an unpredicted act; a unanimous mind
  cannot surprise itself. Owens's volatility was **the specification, not a tolerated cost.**
  Everything history holds against him is, in their accounting, signal quality. He was chosen by his
  flaws, for his flaws, and then billed for them.
- **O-38 · Erased-world POV.** Owens gets full interiority — **exclusively in chapters set in
  timelines his own interventions replaced.** In the surviving timeline he is documents only:
  letters, affidavits, marked maps, one reel of tape. Every interior chapter is a world that is not
  there any more.
  - This is the only form in which O-16 can be dramatised rather than reported. The sole witness to a
    replaced world cannot be rendered from the outside.
  - It disposes of the ethical problem of inventing a real man's private thoughts: **the book never
    claims to render what Ted Owens thought on a date in our history.** It renders the timelines only
    he remembered, which are unverifiable by construction and are the one place fiction is free.
  - It does to the reader what the interface did to him. Those chapters become the only surviving
    record of the worlds they describe, and the reader becomes the second witness.
  - **Rule:** if a scene could be corroborated by a surviving document, it may not be an interior
    chapter. Interiority and evidence are mutually exclusive, on every page, without exception.
- **O-39 · The book stands alone.** A reader who has never heard of ONE RECORD must lose nothing
  structural. Everything needed is explained inside this book, in its own terms. The Convergence
  reading of O-36 is a **second floor** for readers who have the duology, never a prerequisite.
  L-01 says no book three; this is not one, and it must not read like one.
- **O-40 · The consent register is not mine to sign.** `scripts/leak_scan.py` gates protected
  real-person legal identifiers and holds a consent register whose own comment reads: *adding a row
  is an assertion that a real human said yes; do not add a row to make CI green.* Named real
  investigators (O-34) are exactly that class the moment prose ships. **No row is added by anyone but
  AJ, and the decision is due at first prose, not at first CI failure.** See `SOURCES.md`.
