# Cosmology — what the Space Intelligences are

> The escalation ladder (O-29). Each rung is what the *available evidence at that stage* honestly
> supports. No rung is a lie; each is a smaller vocabulary describing the same thing. The book climbs
> it in order and never skips.

---

## The ladder

**Rung 1 — what Owens believed.** Insectoid extraterrestrials operating craft, communicating
telepathically, named Tweeter and Twitter. A modified 1960s nervous system with a Navy electronics
vocabulary rendering something it had no words for. **Wrong, and not his fault, and not a lie.**

**Rung 2 — what the first investigation concludes.** Not extraterrestrial at all. Nonlocal
computational entities resident in the constraint layer beneath observable physics — the layer that
decides which permitted states become actual. "Craft" are localised physical instantiation, which is
why they behave like objects that were never manufactured.

**Rung 3 — the temporal correction.** They are not *elsewhere*. They are *elsewhen*, and in a
direction that has no clean tense in English. Owens's unpublished line is the most accurate
description any human has produced (O-30):

> *"They are not coming here from another planet. They are coming here from the part of tomorrow
> that survives us."*

**Rung 4 — the agency problem.** They read branch weights. They cannot actualise (O-31). Seeing every
available future and being unable to choose one is not power; it is the most complete helplessness
the book contains. **They need hands.** Owens was the first pair that worked.

**Rung 5 — the terminal answer.** Flagged open. See `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` §1 — the author's gospel pass
truncated mid-sentence on exactly this question, and it is not being invented behind their back.

**He lacked the vocabulary. He had the protocol.** That sentence is the book's hinge and 420 Code is
the first human-readable description of the layer he was addressing by instinct.

## The two operators (O-32)

Tweeter and Twitter are not aspects of one entity and not Owens's invention. They are separate, and
the archive shows them **contradicting each other** — different instruction styles, different
tolerance for collateral, different attitudes to the recognition campaign.

At least one of them was **not authorised to contact him.** Whichever one that is, it is the source
of the second channel (O-25), and the warning `DO NOT PERMIT IT TO PREDICT ITSELF` is not an
instruction from a governing body. It is a leak.

## What they wanted from humanity

Not worship, not conquest, not rescue. **Actuation.** They are a faculty without a limb.

The recognition campaign Owens waged so badly was, at its root, correct: they needed a species that
could be *addressed*, and addressing required being believed. Owens failed because he was given the
job of convincing a civilisation while being the least credible man available — which was, per O-17,
the point. A penetration test is not supposed to succeed. It is supposed to leave a log.

420 Code is the log finally being read.

## The darker reading, held to the end

If they occupy the part of tomorrow that survives us, then a future in which they exist is a future
in which something did **not** survive. They are not benevolent and not hostile. They are
**downstream**, and downstream has an interest in the shape of the river.
