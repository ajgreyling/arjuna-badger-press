# Style — *INTERFACE ONE*

## The two registers, and the wall between them (O-38)

| | **[P] Present · 2036–37** | **[E] Erased · 1955–1987** |
|---|---|---|
| Tense | Past | **Present** |
| POV | Close third, rotating: Thandi · Aviva · Marius · Constance | Close third, Owens only, unguarded |
| Texture | Provenance, custody, instrumentation, bad chairs | Weather, rooms, money, children, the load |
| Corroboration | Everything has a document behind it | **Nothing can be checked, ever** |
| Reader is | An investigator | The second witness |

**The wall** *(restated 2026-08-21 after the first-draft audit — the original formulation was
absolute and the draft could not honour it, because the acts of overwriting are themselves
documented: the 1985 letter survives, the nine sheets survive. The rule below is what the draft
actually does, and it is the better rule.)*

Three clauses, all binding:

1. **Interiority never crosses.** No erased chapter may contain an *interior* fact the present strand
   can confirm. Documents may overlap — Marius can reconstruct from sheets seven and eight what the
   1985 letter was for. Nobody will ever corroborate a man on a church corner saying *no* out loud to
   nobody.
2. **The replaced world is never corroborable at all.** The marriage that lasted, the bridge that
   held, the hardware shop, the seven months, the world where it fired on time. These have no
   evidence anywhere and never acquire any. They are the strand's actual subject.
3. **No erased chapter may look forward.** No sentence from inside 1955–1987 may reach into 2036–37,
   not even for an image. He does not know, and the narration does not know past him.

When the reader recognises something across the wall, they must be **wrong** to — the recognition is
the trap the book is built to spring.

## Present-strand rules

- **Never cinema.** Nothing is smooth. The storm takes four days and is argued about. Discoveries
  arrive as clean logs read by tired people who then go and get lunch.
- **Lucid computes, cites, conflicts or refuses.** No voice, no POV, never cute. When the diagrams
  compile it returns a compile result. It does not announce anything, ever.
- **Cost is visible.** Compute, queue position, grid, custody, somebody's weekend. A free query is a
  continuity error.
- Machine output on the page is set as code, unglossed. Let it be boring on its own.
- South African institutional texture without tour-guiding it. Load-shedding is furniture, not plot.

## Erased-strand rules

- Present tense, close, no distance, no irony. He is not a case study in these chapters.
- **He never explains the mechanism**, because he does not have the words. He has Navy electronics
  and a Baptist childhood and that is the whole vocabulary.
- The replaced world is rendered as **ordinary**, never as a vision. Its loss lands because it was
  furniture, not because it was beautiful.
- No chapter ends on him understanding something. He gets one flicker, at most, and misreads it.

## The line-level floor

- Sentences do the work; adjectives do not. Cut any sentence that is only atmosphere.
- No character explains the theme. If a paragraph could be a pull quote, it is probably a mistake —
  **except three times in the book**, and they are earmarked in `REVEALS.md`.
- Dialogue is short, procedural, and people talk past each other because they are in different jobs.
- No dream sequences, no italic interiority in the present strand, no epigraphs.

## Forbidden

Everything in `ANTI_TROPES.md`, plus: nobody says *"my God, it's alive."* Nobody runs. There is no
countdown. No one in this book raises their voice except Constance, once, and she is right to.
