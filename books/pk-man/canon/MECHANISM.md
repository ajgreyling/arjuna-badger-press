# Mechanism — how the interface actually works

> Binding. This file is what keeps the gospel from becoming a magic system with no floor. Owens is
> terrifying **because** he is bounded, not despite it. Fair play (O-20…O-23) binds here the way
> ONE RECORD's L-25 binds the duology: every late solution uses a principle demonstrated earlier at
> smaller scale, and no rule is concealed to manufacture a twist.

---

## The correction at the centre of the book

Owens believed he had **PK power** — mind exerting force on matter. He was wrong about the
machinery and right about everything else.

G's physics eventually establishes that consciousness exerts no force at all. It **re-weights
probability among outcomes physics already permits.**

> He did not break the laws of physics. He had administrator access to their boundary conditions.

## The command table

Owens's own vocabulary, left of the line; what was actually issued, right of it. This table is
canon and scenes are written against it.

| Owens's command | Actual mechanism | Precondition that must already exist |
|---|---|---|
| *Send lightning there* | Selects one viable discharge path from many | Charge separation, moisture, a path |
| *Punish this city* | Amplifies thousands of independent low-probability failures | A city's ordinary failure rate |
| *Make the winter warm* | Sustains a compatible atmospheric attractor | The attractor must be reachable |
| *Sabotage the team* | Introduces tiny neuromuscular errors at decisive moments | Athletes already near their error floor |
| *Send UFOs* | Requests instantiation from the SI network | Their willingness — not his to compel |
| *Cause strange behaviour* | Weakens neurological probability stability locally | Populations, not individuals |
| *Predict an event* | Samples a high-probability adjacent future | The branch must already be weighted |
| *Cause an event* | Pushes actualisation toward a sampled branch | Everything above |

**Prediction and causation are the same operation at different amplitudes.** This is why Owens
could never tell his own foresight from his own will, and why his letters read as threats and
forecasts interchangeably. He was not being evasive. The distinction does not exist at the interface.

## The cost budget (O-22)

Reality does not grant a command; it **pays** for one. The bill is drawn from the same probability
budget the outcome came from.

- Small, well-conditioned command → one clean-looking result, cheap, deniable.
- Large or badly-conditioned command → reality must recruit long chains of intermediary events.
- The recruited events are **not** dramatic. They are absurd: wrong luggage, a stuck relay, four
  people mishearing the same word, a dog in a stairwell, a clerk who transposes two digits.

> **A large intervention is legible afterwards only as a cluster of small absurdities, never as one
> miracle.** The Cleveland and Chicago campaigns are the reference cases: read the trivia, not the
> disaster.

This gives the book its detection method. You do not find Owens's fingerprints in the catastrophe.
You find them in the **statistically impossible amount of stupid** surrounding it.

## What it costs the operator

- Sustained connection is metabolically and neurologically expensive. Alcohol is not escapism here;
  it is the only sedation Owens found that let a loaded interface idle (O-15).
- Cirrhosis is the terminal bill. The organ that clears the body's load fails first.
- **Timeline feedback** (O-16) is the worst of it: he retains memory of replaced worlds. Aircraft
  that crashed and now did not. A president never elected. A city flattened by a storm that exists
  only in his nervous system. He is accused of lying about worlds only he remembers.

## Hard limits — the book never violates these

1. **No creation.** Nothing is conjured. Everything actualised was already available.
2. **No precision on individuals.** Weather, crowds, machinery and error rates — yes. A named
   person's specific fate — no. The interface addresses *distributions*.
3. **No instant results.** Actualisation takes as long as the physical process takes. Storms take
   days. This is what made his timing look sloppy and his hit rate arguable.
4. **No compulsion of the SI network.** Craft manifestations are requests. Sometimes refused.
5. **No un-doing.** A pushed branch cannot be pulled back. Owens's worst hours are the ones after a
   command lands harder than he meant it to, with the previous world still in his head.
6. **The operator is never the source** (O-12). Cut the credentials and he is a sick man in a
   rooming house with correct memories of nothing anyone else can find.
