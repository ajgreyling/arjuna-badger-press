## Primary references

- **Jeffrey Mishlove, *The PK Man: A True Story of Mind Over Matter* (2000)** — the indispensable
  archive-based narrative and the source of most Owens chronology. Mishlove is a **living author**;
  the novel must not put words, acts or conclusions on him beyond his published record (O-34).
- **Psi Encyclopedia, "Ted Owens"** — incident-by-incident chronology with citations and criticism.
  Fastest route to the documented objections, which this book uses as texture (`ANTI_TROPES.md`).
- **Ted Owens, *How to Contact Space People*** — his own voice and cosmology, first-hand.
- **Ted Owens, *Flying Saucer Intelligences Speak*** — the earlier, less embittered mythology.
- **Otto Binder, *Saga* articles c. 1970–71** — contemporary reporting predating Mishlove's
  retrospective; valuable because it is not downstream of the later framing.
- ONE RECORD's own `books/the-record/canon/` — 420 Code, Lucid, the corpus/graph distinction, and
  `PHYSICS_ANCHORS.md` (Landauer foundation, Vopson fringe — L-07 binds here too).

## Fact-check gate — before any draft ships

Everything in the left column of `OWENS_LEDGER.md` needs verification against primary sources, not
against this folder and not against the author's gospel pass. Specifically flagged as **unverified
in-repo** and requiring a checked citation:

- Naval service dates and rating; the nature and dates of the Duke/Rhine association
- Publication dates of both Owens books, and the *Saga* article dates
- Date, wording and recipient of the Nixon prediction
- Dates and recipients of the Oct 1972 letters; the Texas broadcaster warnings
- The San Francisco window as given to Mishlove, and what was actually reported
- Death date, cause and circumstances

> The gospel (O-10…O-19) is not subject to this gate. **The dates are.** A book that asserts Owens
> was right cannot afford to be wrong about when he mailed something.

## Legal and editorial

- **Owens (d. 1987) is named** (O-33). The book's claim about him is vindication.
- **All other named real persons appear only within their public record** (O-34). Where the plot
  requires an undocumented act — interception, falsification, recruitment, medical concealment — the
  actor is a **fictional counterpart**, without exception.
- **All institutional misconduct is fictional** (O-35): naval medical records, mail interception and
  any agency suppression use invented entities. Mirrors ONE RECORD's `SOURCES.md` note.
- Living relatives and descendants of Owens and of his correspondents are **out of scope entirely**.
  No composite child, no estate scene keyed to a real family member.
