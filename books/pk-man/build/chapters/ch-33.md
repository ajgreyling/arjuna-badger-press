# Chapter 33

Thandi's decision was circulated at 08:11 on the thirtieth of September and it was one page.

> I have decided not to authorise the execution of segment four, and not to authorise the evaluation of what it does.
>
> My reason is not that I believe it is dangerous. I have no basis for believing that and neither does anyone here.
>
> My reason is that I cannot construct an account of this decision that does not end with four people in a converted laundry deciding it. I have tried for eleven days. Every version I write is a good argument made by people with no standing, and I have read a great many good arguments made by people with no standing, because they are in the boxes downstairs.
>
> Dr Kotze is right that if we do not do this, somebody worse will, in some number of years. I have weighed that and it does not move me, because *somebody worse will do it anyway* is the reasoning of every person who has ever done something first.
>
> The material stays. Nothing is destroyed. The description is published in full, including the ledger, including the misses, including everything that makes him look like what he was. If in five years there is a body with standing that wants to take this up, it will find every page where we left it and a complete account of why we stopped.
>
> I am aware that this is a decision to be the people who did not find out. I would like it recorded that I know exactly what I am giving up and that I think it is worth it.
>
> T. Mokoena, Director. 30 September 2037.

Marius read it on the train and wrote back four words: *Correct. I disagree. Correct.*

Pieter printed it and put it in the file.

Aviva read it at her desk at twenty past eight and sat for a while and then went down to the basement and did the day's work, which was a box of shipping manifests from Walvis Bay, and she got through eleven of them and did them well.

The first of October was a Thursday.

The guard expired at midnight, in the sense that a date in a notation expires, which is to say that nothing whatever happened. There was no event. Aviva was at home, asleep, and she has been clear that she was asleep and that she did not lie awake.

She came in at eight and worked until six.

At six she did not go home.

She has given four accounts of the next four hours and they are consistent and none of them is flattering and she has never once tried to make it sound like anything other than what it was.

She did not tell herself she was authorised. She knew precisely what Thandi's memorandum said and she had read it eleven times that day and she could have recited the fourth paragraph. She did not construct a justification and she has been contemptuous, in every subsequent account, of the suggestion that she did.

What she has said is this:

*I had wanted to know for nineteen months and the guard had lifted that morning and I was the only person in the building.*

She submitted `evaluate/seg4` at 21:40 on the first of October 2037 under her own credentials, with the scope set to calculation only and execution explicitly disabled, which she did not do to protect anybody: it was the job she wanted; she wanted to know what it did, and she has never claimed the scoping as a mitigation.

It ran for three hours and fifty-one minutes.

She sat in the imaging room for the whole of it with the lights off and the door open, and at about eleven she went and stood in the corridor outside the old laundry for a while, listening to the racks, which made the sound they always made.

It returned at 01:31.

The report was nineteen pages. The first page was eleven lines.

```
JOB 2037-10-01-0009  evaluate/seg4 [scope: calculate. execute: DISABLED]

operation class ......... installation
target .................. addressee (per corpus)
effect .................. extends addressee from
                          read-only over outcome
                          distribution to write
                          over outcome distribution
reversible .............. no
arguments bound ......... 9 of 9

execution timestamp ..... 2037-10-01
  ORIGINAL VALUE ........ 1987-12-28  [overwritten]
  overwrite is IN-CORPUS, same hand, same
  notation, sheets 2-9 of segment 4.
  sheets 2-8 are the derivation.
```

Aviva read the first page four times.

Then she read the ninth line four more times, which is the one everybody reads four more times.

`ORIGINAL VALUE 1987-12-28`

She got up and went and found a wall calendar from 1987 in the reference shelf, which took her nine minutes, because she needed to see it on paper and not on a screen, and she has never been able to explain that either.

The twenty-eighth of December 1987 was a Monday.

It was also, and she did not need the calendar for this, and she checked it anyway, twice, from two sources, standing up, at ten to two in the morning in an empty building in Cape Town: the day Theodore Owens died in a rooming house in Philadelphia of cirrhosis of the liver, aged sixty-seven, with nobody in the room.
