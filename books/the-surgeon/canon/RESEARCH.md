# *THE SURGEON* — Research Notes & Reality Deltas

> What the book bends, and what it must get right. Sensitivity load-bearing items flagged ⚑.

## Reality deltas (deliberate, lightly fictionalised)

- **Stellenbosch veterinary faculty.** In reality South Africa's only veterinary school is
  Onderstepoort (University of Pretoria). A second school has been publicly mooted, with
  Stellenbosch among the candidates. The novel runs slightly ahead of reality: SU's new
  veterinary faculty exists and he is in its first cohorts — young institution, prestige
  hunger, systems still soft enough for a brilliant insider to exploit (dispensary audit
  culture, new-building keys). One line of hand-waving in-text maximum; never a footnote.
- **SAPS structure.** Violent-crimes / Hawks handoff simplified for pace; keep docket
  culture, resourcing strain, and rank forms (Captain, Warrant Officer) right. Deon Meyer
  altitude of procedural realism.

## Must-get-right

- **Geography:** Stellenbosch–Franschhoek–Paarl–Somerset West triangle; Helshoogte pass;
  Atlantic Seaboard strip (Clifton, Camps Bay, Bantry Bay, Sea Point). Drive times matter
  to the alibi mechanics — over the mountain is his moat.
- **Phi-mask / golden-ratio beauty metrics:** real cosmetic-industry discourse (Marquardt
  mask lineage) — the book uses it as his liturgy and quietly discredits it (φ irrational;
  the mask's own scientific standing is contested). Research the *rhetoric*, not procedures.
- **Psychopathy vs. sociopathy (locked in STORY_BIBLE):** he is the cold, organised,
  primary-psychopathy profile (PCL-R Factor 1 register: glibness, grandiosity, absence of
  remorse/empathy, shallow affect) — not the hot, impulsive colloquial "sociopath."
  Formal frame: ASPD with psychopathic and narcissistic features.
- **Veterinary-vs-human surgical tells:** the case breaks on suture pattern/material
  recognisable to a clinician as animal-practice work. Keep at recognition level (what a
  dermatologist would *notice*), never at instruction level.

## ⚑ Sensitivity & handling (binding)

- **The race-and-beauty doctrine is his pathology.** The book must render the "African
  beauty is the apex" creed as fetish-objectification — the colonial gaze wearing a
  compliment — and dismantle it on the page through characters with standing (Nkosi
  chapters). Never leave it charismatic and unanswered. Consider a sensitivity read for the
  Nkosi POV and the doctrine chapters before publication.
- **Non-consensual medical violation:** the horror is consent and selfhood, not gore.
  Victim interiority is mandatory (STORY_BIBLE Rule 3). No sexual violence on page or as
  implication.
- **Non-procedural rule** (STORY_BIBLE HOUSE RULES) applies to research too: do not gather
  or encode dosing/technique detail into canon or prose. Premise-level accuracy only.
