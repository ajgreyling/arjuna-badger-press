# THE SURGEON — Style Guide (working)

The contract for the prose. Where a chapter and this differ, this wins.

- **POV braid:** the Surgeon in **first person present-tense-adjacent** (the Dexter register —
  intimate, amused, confiding; the reader as the only person he never lies to, which is
  itself his best lie). Counterweight chapters (Nkosi, Naidoo, victims) in **close third,
  past tense** — plain, warm, unshowy. The style gap *is* the moral argument: his beauty is
  performance; their plainness is truth.
- **His voice:** cultured, funny, precise; wine-and-anatomy vocabulary; Machiavelli quoted
  the way other men quote rugby scores. Never camp, never mustache-twirling. The sentences
  seduce, and the content indicts — maximum distance between how it sounds and what it says.
  He self-mythologises with the golden ratio, π, Fibonacci; the prose lets the mathematics
  quietly contradict him (φ is irrational — his gospel cannot terminate).
- **Victim chapters are sacred ground:** interiority, names, an *after*. No lingering on
  bodies; the camera stays on selfhood, consent, the mirror. If a sentence aestheticises a
  victim the way *he* would, cut it — the counterweight chapters must never borrow his gaze.
- **Non-procedural rule (binding, from STORY_BIBLE HOUSE RULES):** no drug names/doses/
  routes/sourcing, no surgical or anaesthetic technique, nothing liftable. Cellar scenes run
  on dread, tenderness-horror, and consequence: the tray's click, the sleeping face, the
  marker's cold nib — never the how.
- **The ideology on the page:** his race-and-beauty doctrine appears only inside his POV or
  in scenes where it is being seen through (Nkosi's chapters). It is never left charismatic
  and unanswered across a Part boundary.
- **Setting texture:** Cape specificity, lightly worn — Stellenbosch oaks and lecture halls,
  Franschhoek harvest, Helshoogte pass, the Seaboard's gym-café strip, SAPS docket-and-
  loadshedding realism (Deon Meyer altitude, not tourist-brochure altitude). Afrikaans and
  isiXhosa phrases used naturally, unitalicised, meaning carried by context.
- **Chapter shape:** short-to-mid chapters landing on turns, not lulls; every Part ends on
  an uncrossable threshold. Epigraph option: fragments of *The Prince* and/or phi-mask
  "measurements" as section marks — decide once at drafting, apply uniformly.
- **Length target:** full novel, ~85–100k. The braid runs roughly 60/40 [S]/[C] in Parts
  I–II and inverts to 40/60 by Part V — the book slowly takes the microphone away from him.
- **The last word is never his** (binding, PLOT.md coda).
