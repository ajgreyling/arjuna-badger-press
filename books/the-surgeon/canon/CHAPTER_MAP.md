# *THE SURGEON* — Locked Chapter Map (drafting authority)

> **STRUCTURE (locked 2026-07-24): a TWO-HANDER.** Two POVs, strictly alternating, in the
> Deon Meyer register: **[S]** the Surgeon, first person · **[D]** Captain Gideon Loots,
> close third. Every other character — victims, partner, witnesses — appears **in scene
> only**, never as POV. (Sole exception: ch. 3, Margaux's chapter, already drafted and
> published; it stands as the book's one witness chapter and is not repeated.)
>
> 24 chapters, 5 Parts, ~70k words, ~2,400–3,200 per chapter, single-shot drafts.
> **Series intent:** Loots is a recurring detective; *The Surgeon* is Book I.
> No decision below is reopened during drafting.

## The two men (the whole novel is their argument)

**CHRISTIAAN "CHRIS" VERMEULEN — the Surgeon.** 29. Final-year vet, Stellenbosch; Franschhoek
wine-farm childhood, thin old money; rejected from medicine by one mark. Cold, organised
primary psychopathy; charm as instrument; Machiavelli as scripture. Corrects bodies.

**CAPTAIN GIDEON "GIDI" LOOTS — the detective.** 51. Was the best violent-crimes detective in
the Western Cape — a closer, a man who could read a room like a text. **Disgraced:** eighteen
months ago he beat Ryan Adonis, who had killed a four-year-old, half to death in a holding
cell at Bellville South, on camera, with two witnesses. Not fired (the union, the optics, the
child) — buried: stripped of the group commander's post, shunted to a satellite office,
"under supervision," fitness-for-duty reviews he keeps failing to attend. **Divorced:** Elmarie
remarried a quantity surveyor in Durbanville; daughter **Ilse**, 19, first-year at Tygerberg
(medicine — the wound in the wound), takes his calls about one time in four. Drinks Klipdrift
and Coke in a rented duplex in Parow with his mother's stoep chairs and no curtains. Sleeps
four hours. Brilliant and knows it, which is half the problem.

**The mirror (the book's thesis):** both men decided, privately, that the world was wrong and
that their hands were the correction. One breaks bodies to punish; one perfects them to
worship. Neither asked. Loots is the only person alive who understands Vermeulen from the
inside, and hates it, and that is why he catches him — and why the catching costs him.

## Locked supporting cast (scene only, never POV)

## The 24 chapters

**PART I — THE KINDNESSES (1–6)** · *"Everyone sees what you appear to be; few experience what you really are."*
1. **[S] The Kindnesses** ✓ — Margaux's scar; the creed; the gold hour over Helshoogte.
2. **[S] The Faculty of Second Choices** ✓ — campus, Krige, Anri, the ledger, the letter, the door.
3. **[witness] The Lost Evening** ✓ — Margaux: shame-logic, the small wrongnesses, the mirror.
4. **[D] The Man Who Broke a Man** — Loots entire: the cell video, Parow, Klipdrift, Ilse's
   unanswered call, Delport handing him the joke docket nobody wants. He reads it and does
   not laugh.
5. **[S] Subtraction** — Cherise Bekker's mole; his pride in the closing; the hunger after.
6. **[D] The Orphan Docket** — Loots works it: Margaux's statement, a second unreported case,
   the water-and-two-paracetamol marker only he sees. First sentence of his profile.

**PART II — THE IMPROVEMENTS (7–12)** · *"Men in general judge more by the eye than by the hand."*
7. **[S] The First Addition** — Demi Louw's lips; phi liturgy; he crosses without noticing.
8. **[D] The Woman Who Knew** — Demi; the disbelief machinery; Loots is the first person in
   her life to say *I believe you* and mean it. His sobriety begins here, badly.
9. **[S] Stocktake** — Anri's variance query; the charm working hard; he enjoys it.
10. **[D] The Impossible Excision** — Naidoo; animal-practice suture material in human skin;
    Margaux re-interviewed; the case acquires a spine.
11. **[S] The Chapel** — the cellar converted for architecture; liturgy, not technique.
12. **[D] The Sculptor** — the press gets it wrong; the flattering nickname; a taskforce that
    treats it as a joke; Nkosi assigned to babysit him. Loots humiliated and right.

**PART III — THE DOCTRINE (13–16)** · *"Men are so simple that he who deceives will always find someone who lets himself be deceived."*
13. **[S] The Creed** — the doctrine entire and rotten; Bianca Steyn's pigment work.
14. **[D] What Worship Costs** — Nkosi names the fetish; Loots hears his own defence in the
    suspect's logic and cannot unhear it. The mirror chapter.
15. **[S] The Variance** — Anri pulls three months of records; he manages her; architecture scheduled.
16. **[D] Over the Mountain** — geography triangulated: Seaboard hunting, Winelands living.
    Ilse answers once. The profile becomes a man.

**PART IV — THE ARCHITECTURE (17–20)** · *"If an injury has to be done to a man it should be so severe that his vengeance need not be feared."*
17. **[S] Load-Bearing Beauty** — Nadia Prinsloo; the chapel in full; monstrous tenderness.
18. **[D] What Did Not Wake** — the catastrophe from the outside: ICU, the family, the frozen
    face. Loots's hands want the old answer. He walks out to the parking lot instead.
19. **[S] The Material Was Wrong** — theory over guilt; blames her proportions; selects Anri.
20. **[D] Warrant Mathematics** — the key print, the Chenin, the dispensary variance, the
    faculty. The Sculptor gets a name on a docket.

**PART V — THE MASTERPIECE (21–24)** · *"Fortune is the arbiter of half our actions, but she leaves the other half to us."*
21. **[S] The Glass She Never Drinks** — Anri; the method fails; the improvisation; the crack.
22. **[D] North-Wester** — the storm, the flat, the empty hook, the farm; the Ruger left in
    the safe on purpose.
23. **[S] Irrational** — the chapel; the work that cannot terminate; Anri's own stubbornness
    buys the minutes; the breach. Taken alive, unfinished.
24. **[D] The Mirror, After** — the cell's steel mirror; the ledger and the letter; Ilse's
    call; Nadia's slow year; and Margaux in good light, whose last line is not about him.
