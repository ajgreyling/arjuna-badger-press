  wine-farm childhood, thin old money; rejected from medicine by one mark, eleven years ago.
- **Margaux Theron** — ch. 1 victim (eyebrow scar), 31, brand strategist, Camps Bay. Recurs
  ch. 3 (her POV), ch. 7 (Naidoo's patient), ch. 24 (coda — the last line of the book is hers).

- **Demi Louw** — ch. 6 victim (lips; first *addition*), Sea Point. First to KNOW (ch. 8).
- **Bianca Steyn** — ch. 11 victim (pigment doctrine), Clifton wellness person.
- **Nadia Prinsloo** — ch. 16–17 victim (implant architecture). THE CATASTROPHE: she
  **survives with permanent harm** (facial nerve damage — half her face frozen; fogged
  memory). Not a death. His mathematics blames her proportions.
- **Anri Fourie** — classmate, dispensary steward; sharp, unglamorous, immune to the charm.
  Notices the ledger maths (ch. 10, 15). **She is the chosen Masterpiece (ch. 19)** — his
  doctrine broken by his own appetite; dual motive (silencing + the work). **She does not
  drink alcohol** (planted ch. 2, fired ch. 21 — his method needs the glass; he improvises,
  and the improvisation is his first imperfection).
- **Prof. Danie Krige** — his mentor; "jy's gebore hiervoor" is the refrain that curdles.
- **Dr. Priya Naidoo** — Sea Point dermatologist; finds the impossible excision (ch. 7).
- **Capt. Zintle Nkosi** — SAPS violent crimes; thesis scene ch. 13; closes the net.
- **W/O JP "Vissie" Visser** — Nkosi's legwork partner (SAPS texture).

## The 24 chapters

**PART I — THE KINDNESSES** · *"Everyone sees what you appear to be; few experience what you really are."*
1. **[S] The Kindnesses** — DONE. Margaux's scar; the creed; the gold hour over Helshoogte.
2. **[S] The Faculty of Second Choices** — his world: campus, Krige, Anri (won't drink,
   runs the dispensary count with him), the flat, the ledger, the letter, the locked cellar
   door on the family property he "caretakes." Ends: the wanting from ch. 1 given a name.
3. **[C] The Lost Evening** — Margaux POV: the morning after; shame-logic; the month of
   small wrongness; ends the moment she sees the scar is gone.
4. **[S] Catalogue of Vandalism** — Seaboard safari; doctrine outer shell (contempt for the
   bought face); Cherise Bekker marked in the ledger.
5. **[S] Subtraction** — Cherise's mole; his pride in the closing work (suture gun planted);
   post-op hunger: subtraction no longer fills him. Part threshold crossed.

**PART II — THE IMPROVEMENTS** · *"Men in general judge more by the eye than by the hand."*
6. **[S] The First Addition** — Demi Louw's lips; phi liturgy in full; rapture; he crosses
   mid-chapter without noticing (the reader notices).
7. **[C] The Impossible Excision** — Naidoo finds surgical work Margaux swears never
   happened; material/pattern she can't place; laughed at twice.
8. **[C] The Woman Who Knew** — Demi knows; the disbelief machinery (GP, boyfriend,
   comments, her own mirror); the docket with nowhere to live.
9. **[C] The Orphan Docket** — Nkosi takes it; Visser legwork; second unreported case;
   water-and-paracetamol pattern-marker.
10. **[S] Stocktake** — Anri queries a variance in the count; the charm working hard for
    the first time; he enjoys it; threshold: planning hours-long work.

**PART III — THE DOCTRINE** · *"Men are so simple that he who deceives will always find someone who lets himself be deceived."*
11. **[S] The Creed** — the doctrine entire and rotten: African beauty as apex never to be
    altered, the Seaboard as vandalised property he restores; Bianca Steyn's pigment work.
12. **[C] The Sculptor** — the press gets it wrong (lifestyle piece, flattering nickname);
    he reads his own coverage at a tasting, quoting Machiavelli to laughter.
13. **[C] What Worship Costs** — Nkosi thesis chapter: victim interviews; she names the
    fetish (worship and mutilation, same seizure); Naidoo's file reaches her; signatures join.
14. **[S] The Chapel** — converting the cellar for architecture; instruments, theatre
    build-out (non-procedural: dread and liturgy only); doctrine hardening into destiny.
15. **[C/S] The Variance** — Anri pulls three months of dispensary records; his managing of
    her (split chapter, her third/his first); threshold: architecture scheduled.

**PART IV — THE ARCHITECTURE** · *"If an injury has to be done to a man it should be so severe that his vengeance need not be feared."*
16. **[S] Load-Bearing Beauty** — Nadia Prinsloo; the chapel in full; hours under; monstrous
    tenderness at maximum altitude.
17. **[C] What Did Not Wake** — THE CATASTROPHE: Nadia survives, half her face frozen,
    memory fogged; family POV carries the cost; the reader's exit engineered since ch. 1.
18. **[C] Taskforce** — the case goes real; Naidoo vindicated; geography triangulated
    across the mountains; Winelands profile forms.
19. **[S] The Material Was Wrong** — flight forward into theory; he blames her proportions;
    selects the Masterpiece: **Anri**. His own doctrine breaks and he calls the breakage
    destiny.
20. **[C] Warrant Mathematics** — Nkosi: the key, the Chenin CCTV, dispensary variance,
    Demi's ID, the "concerned acquaintance" pattern; the net has a name and a face.
21. **[S] The Glass She Never Drinks** — Anri; the method fails (she doesn't drink); the
    improvisation; the first crack in the immaculate; the drive to the chapel.
22. **[C] North-Wester** — Nkosi and Visser over Helshoogte in the storm; warrant executed
    at the flat; the ledger's empty hook — and the farm caretakership surfaces.

**PART V — THE MASTERPIECE** · *"Fortune is the arbiter of half our actions, but she leaves the other half to us."*
23. **[S/C] Irrational** — COLLISION at the chapel; the masterpiece cannot be finished
    (φ non-terminating; the improvisation compounds); Anri's own agency matters (she is
    never merely rescued — her left-handed stocktake stubbornness buys the minutes);
    arrest mid-work: **endings (a)+(b) fused** — taken alive, unfinished, and the last
    face his algorithm ever assesses is his own in the cell's steel mirror.
24. **[C] The Mirror, After** — coda: Nadia's slow therapy; Naidoo publishing; Nkosi's next
    docket; Anri back at the count; Margaux in good light, touching her eyebrow — the last
    line is hers, and it is not about him.
