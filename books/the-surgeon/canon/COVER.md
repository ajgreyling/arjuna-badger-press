## Concept — the golden spiral in the vineyard

Not a scalpel-and-blood cover (the genre's cliché, and it would glamourise him). The image
is his delusion seen from above: **dark Winelands vine-rows curling into a perfect golden-
ratio spiral**, aerial, dusk-to-night, the Stellenbosch mountains a black wall behind. The
spiral reads as beauty first and wrongness second — exactly the book's move. One cold
accent: a thin gleam of stainless steel light along the spiral's innermost curve, where the
rows tighten to a point that never terminates.

## Mood

Sun-drowned noir gone dark: deep greens and near-black, one gold thread. Elegant, geometric,
unsettling in the second glance. Empty charged sky top-third for title; dark foreground
bottom-quarter for author line. Accent gold `#C9A227` (surgical-lamp gold, harvest gold).
