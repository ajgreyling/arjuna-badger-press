# Codex Medica :  Style & House Rules

## What this book is

A **research constitution**, not a novel and not a pharmacopoeia. Voice: plain, exact, slightly
formal. Prefer tables for controlled vocabularies. Prefer callout blocks for binding rules.

## Binding notices (must appear in every public edition)

1. **Non-clinical status.** Documents knowledge. Does not diagnose, dose, prescribe, or instruct
   preparation of hazardous, intoxicating, abortifacient, or high-risk substances.
2. **Three layers never collapsed.** Witnessed tradition · interpreted mechanism · tested outcome.
3. **CARE before FAIR.** Open is not the default when openness would reproduce extraction.

## Words that are load-bearing

- *Medica* means materia medica (historical register), never "see a doctor via this book."
- *Claim* is a normalized proposition, not a fact.
- *Use report* is one source's one practice.
- *Band* is an editorial publication grade after adjudication, not a star rating of a culture.

## What never goes on the page

- Personalized treatment advice
- Reconstructed preparation instructions when the source is incomplete
- Secret / sacred / initiated knowledge without documented authority
- Efficacy rankings that treat RCTs as a measure of cultural worth

## Typography

House render gate: Atkinson Hyperlegible. No em dashes in new press prose. Tables over prose lists
when the source used a grid.
