# Book XIV :  Research Programme

## 39. Phased build

| Phase | Deliverable | Exit criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 • Constitution | This research bible, governance board and controlled vocabularies | Custodian and scientific review |
| 1 • Pilot corpus | 50 deeply sourced monographs across 5 cultural/ecological regions | ≥90% provenance completeness |
| 2 • Comparative atlas | Claim lineages, convergence maps and safety registry | No unresolved public restrictions |
| 3 • Research engine | Prioritized validation studies with community partners | Benefit-sharing and preregistration |
| 4 • Living edition | Public, community and restricted access layers | Sustainable governance and review cycle |

## 40. Pilot portfolio design

Select common, culturally important and ecologically secure taxa: not only exotic or psychoactive plants.

Include positive, null, hazardous and abandoned practices to reduce survivorship bias.

Represent oral and textual traditions, women’s knowledge, domestic medicine and specialist practice.

Pair at least one African regional cluster with comparable but independently sourced traditions elsewhere.

Include one deliberately difficult identity problem and one restricted-knowledge case to test governance.

## 41. Governance roles

| Role | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Community council/custodians | Consent, attribution, restriction, interpretation and benefit-sharing |
| Ethnobotany editor | Source fidelity, transmission mapping and comparative method |
| Taxonomy lead | Identity, voucher and synonym resolution |
| Clinical-methods lead | Evidence eligibility, bias and statistical synthesis |
| Safety lead | Toxicology, interactions, misuse and escalation |
| Conservation lead | Harvest impact, threatened taxa and publication risk |
| Data steward | CARE-aligned permissions, provenance, versioning and audit |
| Independent ombud | Grievance, correction, conflict and takedown pathway |
