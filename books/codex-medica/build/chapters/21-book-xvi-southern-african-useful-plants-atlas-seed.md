# Book XVI : Southern African Useful Plants Atlas (seed)

> **Edition 0.3 agenda.** This atlas is the living Southern African centre of gravity
> locked in Book XV. It follows the publicly catalogued 20-chapter use taxonomy of
> van Wyk & Gericke, *People's Plants* (Briza, 2nd ed.), as a **research agenda only**.
> See `canon/SOURCES_PEOPLES_PLANTS.md`: cite, do not copy. No text, anecdote, dose,
> or photograph from that book appears here.

Each row below is an **open-source starter** drawn from public ethnobotanical, SANBI,
agricultural and conservation records. A row is not a monograph. A row is not
permission to forage, dose, or prepare. HOLD rows are named so the corpus can refuse
them honestly.

**Seed size:** 62 taxa ·
31 open · 20 caution · 11 hold ·
25 plated · 37 awaiting freely-licensed plates.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Band | `open` = public food/craft/low-hazard; `caution` = documented use with safety/trade/claim risk; `HOLD` = intoxicant, poison, sacred-restricted, or high-toxicity until review |
| Plate | Whether a PD/CC identification image is already in `design/images/` |
| Anchor | Public literature/database trail (not People's Plants page numbers) |

Southern Africa* (2nd ed.). Pretoria: Briza Publications. Secondary literature and
the atlas agenda (~700 plants across these 20 chapters). The steward's physical copy
is the desk reference; the Codex expands from **independent open sources** and
custodian consent, never by transcription.
