# PLANTS AND PAYOFFS — *The Unnumbered*

> Every plant, where it goes off, and — the important column — **whether it is allowed to be
> noticed when planted.** A plant the reader clocks as a plant is a trick. This book's plants are
> all disguised as texture, and most of them are disguised as *kindness*.

## Rules

- **No plant may be flagged twice.** If a thing is mentioned again before it pays, it is a
  signpost, not a plant. Cut the second mention.
- **The kindnesses are the load-bearing ones** (1, 3, 5, 13). This is on purpose: in a book about a
  state that does not keep records, the things that survive are the things somebody kept because
  they loved someone.
- **#9 is a moral obligation, not a craft one.** Track Mmapula. A book that lets a woman with no
  surname fall out of its own accounting has argued the opposite of its thesis.
