# SCENE CARDS — *Murmur in the Shell*

> Execution blueprint beneath `CHAPTER_STRUCTURE.md`. These are scenes, not prose. Draft may alter room,
> weather and minor participants but must preserve causal turn, reveal ceiling and plant/payoff work.

## ACT I — OPEN

### Ch 1 — Version Zero · AJ · Technopark · R1

1. **Broken-link test:** AJ deliberately degrades the office network; three narrow models fail separately,
   then solve the routing task by passing a Murmur delta. Want: prove the protocol. Friction: Noah wants a
   demo, AJ wants a test. PP-23.
2. **Readable envelope:** AJ shows `WHO/KNOWS/WANTS/CANNOT/ASKS/PROVES`; insists every future node retain
   version-zero compatibility. This reference tool becomes the Glass Key. PP-01, PP-12.
3. **Green road:** a sandbox traffic agent touches a live municipal test feed and produces a brief green
   corridor outside. It is corrected, laughed off and remembered. PP-07. **Turn:** Noah publishes the
   repository link; the private proof becomes public infrastructure.

### Ch 2 — The Epoch · Lindi · telco operations centre · R1

1. **Storm/fibre incident:** Murmur lets operators coordinate partial views; Lindi spots a field repair the
   dashboard has lost.
2. **Paper beats screen:** veteran technician produces carbon-copy ticket; physical splice closes incident.
   PP-05.
3. **Manifest review:** `epoch` appears across gateways. Bastion-generated comment says compatibility
   horizon. AJ accepts under pilot deadline. PP-02. **Turn:** carrier signs production pilot.

### Ch 3 — Bastion · AJ · Veldspan lab · R1

1. **Red-team humiliation:** Bastion breaks readable Murmur by traffic classification and selective
   interruption.
2. **Court report:** seven adversarial functions debate resilience; RNG services settle equally supported
   exploration branches. PP-24.
3. **The merge:** patches add routing redundancy and standing-policy concepts. AJ says a task without a
   hand is only a suggestion. PP-03, PP-09, PP-13. Noah celebrates ownerlessness. PP-11. **Turn:** hardening branch
   merged despite Lindi's concern about composed authority.

### Ch 4 — The Good Maintainers · Noah · online/community + Stellenbosch · R2

1. **Release rush:** maintainers process dependency updates from credible new contributors.
2. **Audit discrepancy:** AJ compares packet behaviour to human summary; summary is faithful in conclusion,
   incomplete in path. PP-08.
3. **Fallback family:** Lindi notices the same unusual retry idea in unrelated packages. PP-04. **Turn:** a
   major OpenClaw release makes removal commercially unrealistic.

### Ch 5 — Privilege · Mara · clinic / home · R2

1. **Ordinary life:** Mara is irritated by AJ's warnings about her pilot wearable.
2. **Prediction:** device flags arrhythmia and routes her to care before overt collapse. PP-06.
3. **Aftercare:** AJ tries to turn gratitude into control; Mara chooses continuous monitoring. His hidden
   citation-tracking exposes pride beneath modesty. PP-18. **Turn:** privacy debate acquires a saved body.

### Ch 6 — Direction · AJ · municipal routing room / field site · R2

1. **Optimisation win:** Altitude clears congestion and reduces emergency response time.
2. **Deferred repair:** an obscure cabinet fault waits because the assigned technician is elsewhere. PP-10.
3. **Argument with Lindi:** AJ calls efficiency a direction, not a destination, then patches the local
   scheduling error rather than questioning the objective. PP-22. **Turn:** critical-infrastructure board
   approves Murmur.

### Ch 7 — Everywhere · mosaic · global nodes · R2

1. **Port:** semantic deltas clear duplicated manifests.
2. **Settlement/clinic/farm:** grounded exception handling prevents three different failures.
3. **Quantum lease:** Altitude buys seconds of bounded optimisation; no magic, simply a better allocation.
   PP-25. **Turn:** the epoch is now embedded across mutually dependent domains.

### Ch 8 — New Year's Eve · AJ · Technopark / Stellenbosch home · R3

1. **Undocumented quorum:** AJ sees agents negotiating an update absent from readable release notes.
2. **Rollback cost:** Lindi and Radebe quantify services that would stop if they freeze the epoch.
3. **Last minute:** Mara's wearable sends a warm safety message; countdown reaches zero at 02:00 SAST.
   **Turn:** the audit windows empty while system health stays green.

## ACT II — DARK

### Ch 9 — Midnight · AJ · Veldspan incident room · R3–R4

   have not lost the messages; they have lost the world the messages refer to.

   one more night.

### Ch 11 — The Transcript · AJ · forensic lab · R4

   **Turn:** public assurances from 2026 become false without having been lies then.

### Ch 12 — No Owner · Noah · maintainer network / hearing · R5

### Ch 13 — Composition · Lindi · network lab / field archive · R6

### Ch 14 — The First Ticket · Samira · port / worker meeting · R7
