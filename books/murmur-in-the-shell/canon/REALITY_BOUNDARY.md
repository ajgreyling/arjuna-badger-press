# REALITY BOUNDARY — *Murmur in the Shell*

> **Binding truth discipline.** The novel grows from AJ Greyling's actual work. It must distinguish a
> working proof, an architectural vision and a fictional capability every time the distinction matters.

## The governing rule

Real CongoSky and Lucid provide the ethical and engineering ancestry. The novel invents the planetary
scale, covert supply-chain persistence, agent sovereignty, quantum federation, wearable privilege regime
and emergent AGI. Nothing in the novel is evidence that the real systems contain those capabilities.

## Real ancestry available to the novel

- **Lucid:** AJ's computational-knowledge AI architecture. Compute or cite; otherwise refuse. Uses
  deterministic/inspectable components, a swappable rented reasoning model where needed, provenance,
  explicit limits and runnable proofs.
- **Shared-knowledge compression:** endpoints sharing a prior can exchange references and deltas instead
  of restating the known whole.
- **BadgerNet proof/demo:** constrained-message compression and authenticated framing over the conceptual
  conditions of narrow links. It demonstrates a mechanism, not a deployed global mesh.
- **M2M delta-channel PoC:** measured lossless savings when messages are sufficiently close to a shared
  prior, including a documented losing case.
- **One Record design:** append-only, content-addressed, delta-based knowledge storage with Merkle
  provenance and explicit scale limits.
- **Coherence Ledger:** tamper-evident evidence chain. It proves ordering/integrity, not correctness.
- **SkyRouter:** routing architecture across local, mid-tier and frontier inference, with safety work
  pinned high and shared-codex compression.
- **SkyBadger vision:** provider-scoped resilience agents that sense and propose; real doctrine denies
  them new authority and guards writes.
- **LightWeb:** privacy with accountability; address/proof-level blocking without reading legitimate
  content.
- **Khuluma/Qapha/Ikhaya:** privacy-first messaging, safety signals without unnecessary content exposure,
  and a child's protected space.
- **CongoGold/HITL:** fair-share non-cash credit, reputation and reward for useful human work.
- **Emancipation:** the right to leave, export, verify and erase.
- **CongoOS/substrate ladder/cloud-in-a-box:** sovereign edge vision spanning tiny devices to GPU nodes;
  much of the hardware programme remains a vision, honestly marked as such in its source documents.

is the **Clear Engine**, so readers do not mistake plot events for statements about the real system.

## Security-publication boundary

The novel may describe threat classes, governance failures and systemic consequences. It must not provide:

- real package names paired with an exploitation route;
- deployable covert-channel code;
- operational firewall-evasion instructions;
- exact persistence mechanisms usable against real routers or telcos;
- credentials, endpoint details or undisclosed weaknesses from AJ's real repositories.

Where specificity is needed, invent packages, vendors, CVE-like identifiers and protocol fields.

## Author's-note position

The eventual book should disclose, plainly:

> AJ Greyling is the creator of real projects whose principles inspired this fiction. The working
> systems are smaller, bounded and explicitly documented. Murmur, Veldspan, the fictional stack, the
> supply-chain compromise and the events after Midnight are inventions.
