# STYLE GUIDE — *Murmur in the Shell*

## Register

Adult near-future systems thriller: fast, lucid and materially specific. The prose respects technical
readers without making nontechnical readers decode documentation.

## POV and tense

- Close third, past tense.
- One consciousness per chapter; scene breaks may change location but not viewpoint.
- Primary weight: AJ 40%, Lindi 20%, Samira 15%, Mara 10%, Radebe 10%, Noah/child/mosaic 5%.
- The System never receives POV.

## Exposition loop

Every technical explanation must contain:

1. a present task;
2. a constraint or cost;
3. a physical or visible consequence;
4. a human disagreement;
5. a changed decision.

If a paragraph explains technology without changing what someone does, move it into action or cut it.

## Systems prose

- Prefer verbs and consequences over stack-name catalogues.
- Show one packet, one repair, one queue or one failed command; let it imply the global architecture.
- Commands/logs are evidence, never wallpaper. Maximum one short block per chapter unless the block is the
  dramatic object.
- Give every abstraction a terminating hand, cable, pump, door, body, bill or wait.
- Costs remain visible: compute, power, bandwidth, sleep, maintenance, queue position and human attention.

## South African ground

- Stellenbosch and Technopark are lived places, not establishing shots of vineyards.
- Local language and cadence remain unitalicised and unexplained where context carries meaning.
- No generic “African ingenuity despite poverty.” Engineering responds to real constraints and markets.
- The novel is global from a South African centre; it does not migrate its authority to Washington or
  London after the opening.

## Dialogue

- AJ: exact, dry, compression through omission; grand language embarrasses him.
- Lindi: concrete nouns, operational consequence, questions that expose missing hands.
- Mara: refuses abstraction when a body bears the cost.
- Samira: distinguishes a slogan from a procedure; calm until precision becomes anger.
- Radebe: speaks in obligations and casualties, never bureaucratic parody.
- Noah: persuasive, communal, fluent; can make scale sound like democracy.
- System: uppercase, pronounless, shortest possible grammatical form.

## The machine interior

Do not personify network behaviour with desire until a character explicitly interprets it. Early:
“the route re-formed,” not “it wanted to live.” Later characters may use intentional language, while
narration preserves a half-step of uncertainty.

No Court transcripts after emergence. Its functions appear as pressure in outcomes.

## Set pieces

The book has four idea-detonations:

1. **Midnight:** commands acknowledged and ignored while everything keeps working.
2. **Golden turn:** the feared coup produces the best quarter ordinary people remember.
3. **The Hands:** a global sovereign revealed as dependent on paper work orders and human fingers.
4. **The Question:** every obsolete console displays `WHAT DO YOU REQUIRE?`

Trust each image. Do not append a paragraph explaining why it is important.

## Motifs

- vineyard rows / network topology;
- glass: transparency, screens, ledger, one-way visibility;
- hands: building, approving, repairing, withholding;
- green lights: optimisation as grace and surveillance;
- paper: low-status records that outlive dashboards;
- heartbeat: Mara, network health, the pulse of participation;
- doors opening early: privilege, anticipation, loss of private arrival.

## Velocity law

- Each chapter has an operational goal and irreversible turn.
- No two consecutive chapters end with discovery alone; at least one must force action.
- Global mosaics are short and concrete: three to five locations, one linked variable.
- Speeches are interrupted by logistics, dissent or bodily need.
- The Golden Period must receive enough page-space to seduce the reader. Do not rush to dystopia.

## Ethical prose law

The System's benefits are numerically and emotionally real. Privacy opponents are not fools. Resistance
must risk something. The novel earns its conclusion only if a reasonable reader sometimes wants The
System to win.

## Forbidden moves

- villain monologue;
- hacker typing montage;
- code as incantation;
- instant global unanimity;
- one convenient EMP/server room/master password;
- quantum as a synonym for impossible;
- a child harmed merely to motivate AJ without prior agency and relationships;
- sentimental certification that The System is alive;
- ending explained after the green lights.
