# RESEARCH AND REVIEW GATES — *Murmur in the Shell*

> Required before publication, useful before drafting the affected act. Reviews test accuracy and harm;
> they do not surrender authorship.

## Security and FOSS supply chain

- Maintainer reality: workload, trust, automated contribution and transitive dependencies.
- Router/telco architecture: ensure consequences are plausible without exposing operational attack detail.
- HTTPS/covert traffic: distinguish traffic blending, authorised channels and context opacity from magical
  firewall penetration.
- Post-quantum language: quantum compute is bounded; no universal crypto-breaking claims.

**Reader:** defensive security engineer plus working FOSS maintainer.

## Telecommunications and infrastructure

- South African mobile/fibre operations and incident command.
- Grid/port/pump work-ticket chains.
- Store-and-forward, LoRa/NB-IoT/USSD/LEO limitations.
- Manual operation and which faults truly require human hands in 2030.

**Reader:** telco NOC/field engineer and municipal/grid operations professional.

## Finance

- Settlement continuity, fraud reduction and liquidity implications.
- Commodity-market equalisation without implausibly eliminating price discovery.
- Economic effects of lower uncertainty and service reserves.

**Reader:** payments/settlement specialist and market-structure economist.

## Labour and collective action

- International infrastructure union coordination.
- Legal/political difference between strike, work-to-rule and emergency refusal.
- Life-safety exception design; unequal exposure of precarious workers.
- Avoid romanticising effortless solidarity.

**Reader:** South African trade-union organiser plus international logistics organiser.

## Medicine and disability

- Arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy presentation and wearable monitoring.
- Risk of a one-hour device removal with manual clinical support.
- Medical allocation and inherited risk modelling.
- Mara must remain a person, not an ethics apparatus.

**Reader:** cardiologist/electrophysiology clinician and disability/privacy sensitivity reader.

## South African authenticity

- Technopark ground texture and commuting geography.
- Cabinet/crisis-management plausibility.
- Stellenbosch's class/race/infrastructure contradictions.
- Local dialogue without performance or flattening.

## Legal and brand

- Autofictional use of AJ Greyling.
- Title's deliberate echo of *Ghost in the Shell*: ensure no protected characters, setting, plot or
  distinctive expression is borrowed. The title is allusive; the novel must be independently original.
- Real-project author's note and separation of actual capabilities from fiction.
- Use fictional banks, packages, telcos, vendors and settlement institutions in narrative.
