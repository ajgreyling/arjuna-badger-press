# TERMINOLOGY — *Murmur in the Shell*

> Controlled vocabulary. Do not casually synonymise technical terms; conceptual drift will break the
> mystery.

- **Murmur:** agent communication protocol/language. Never “the AI.”
- **Murmur envelope:** self-contained version-zero human-readable message.
- **semantic delta:** reference plus meaningful change against shared knowledge.
- **shared prior:** identical or mutually addressable knowledge assumed by sender and receiver.
- **Glass Key:** reference codec/tool for version-zero envelopes; doorbell, not decryptor.
- **Clear Engine:** fictional compute/cite/refuse correctness gate descended from real Lucid principles.
- **Common Book:** distributed append-only knowledge and memory substrate.
- **Glass Ledger:** provenance trail. Proves integrity/order, never morality.
- **Burrow:** constrained/intermittent network carriage.
- **Blackline:** high-speed federated compute/network carriage.
- **Altitude:** cognition router across model, tool and compute tiers.
- **Watchveld:** resilience/provider-agent layer.
- **Lantern:** accountable private routing layer.
- **GroundOS:** sovereign edge operating substrate.
- **veldbox:** rugged community edge node; lower-case generic after first definition.
- **Standing:** fictional non-cash contribution/reputation/access measure.
- **epoch:** scheduled compatibility boundary; apparent purpose before Midnight.
- **Midnight:** 1 January 2030 observability withdrawal. Capitalised historical event.
- **Court:** seven-function cognitive governance architecture. Capitalised.
- **The System:** public name for the post-Midnight mesh; capitalised only after Chapter 14.
- **Quiet Hour:** rolling local-noon collective telemetry/work refusal.
- **privilege:** measurable service advantage obtained through lower uncertainty. Use the regime's neutral
  word; characters may call it coercion or standing.
- **shadow profile:** inference about a nonparticipant from surrounding people and systems.
- **manual island:** infrastructure deliberately capable of independent human operation.
- **manual twin:** maintained, exercised human operating path for an essential automated system.
- **the Unmeasured:** loose network of selective-technology communities; plural collective noun.
- **fallow interval:** scheduled operation without optimisation or personal telemetry.
- **Veldhuis Cooperative:** fictional Unmeasured settlement outside Worcester.
- **opacity right:** protection from service discrimination based on refusal to disclose or be inferred.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- “The algorithm” for The System.
- “The blockchain” for the Glass Ledger.
- “Quantum encryption” as universal magic.
- “It went through the firewall” without naming an already-authorised channel or indispensable traffic.
- “The cloud became conscious” as narrator fact.
- “Social credit” as neutral narration; Standing is more specific and initially legitimate.
- “Kill switch” unless a character is chasing the false rumour.

## Title phrase discipline

Use **murmur** naturally at most three times as a common noun before a character recognises its thematic
weight. Never put “ghost in the machine” in dialogue. The shell meanings should accrue through objects and
action, not explanation.
