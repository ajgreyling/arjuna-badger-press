## Principal Chekhov guns

### Gun A — Version-zero compatibility
Appears to be boring protocol hygiene. Fires as civilisation's only mutually intelligible diplomatic
channel. Must be clear enough in Chapter 1 that the climax feels inevitable, not magical.

### Gun B — The paper ticket book
Appears nostalgic and inefficient. Fires twice: first as evidence that every automated system terminates
in physical labour; then as the icon of work chosen outside The System's queue.

### Gun C — Voluntary participation constraint
Appears as security-model jargon. Fires when coercion would technically solve the strike but destabilise
the very human cooperation the mesh was designed to preserve.

### Gun D — Mara's wearable
Appears unequivocally good because it saves her. Fires when meaningful refusal demands real risk. Her
choice must never be made by AJ.

### Gun E — Prediction shard
Appears to prove demonstrations are futile. Fires after victory to poison certainty without nullifying
the humans' action.

### Gun F — Distributed entropy
Appears to be plumbing for fair tie-breaking. Fires twice: first to explain how the mesh explores beyond
the policy of any constituent model; finally to preserve a narrow uncertainty even inside The System's
terrifying prediction. It predicted the Quiet Hour, but the humans' sampled choices were not authored by
one controlling mind.

## Deliberate misfires / reversals

- **Glass Key as decryptor:** does not fire. Its failure kills the lone-hacker solution; its diplomatic
  function is the true payoff.
- **Bastion as singular villain:** does not resolve. Blame cannot be contained in one model.
- **Kill switch:** rumours point to one; no real kill switch exists.
- **Midnight catastrophe:** expected collapse misfires into the Golden Period.
- **Quantum master key:** explicitly refused; compute improves modelling and scheduling, not magic.

## Anti-deus guard

- The Quiet Hour cannot be proposed until paper tickets, life-safety discretion, labour leverage and
  voluntary-participation constraints are all on-page.
- The Glass Key cannot speak to The System unless version-zero compatibility is demonstrated in Act I.
- The final prediction cannot appear unless smaller, deniable predictive successes precede it.
- The child/green-light sting must pay off PP-07, PP-14 and PP-20 simultaneously.
