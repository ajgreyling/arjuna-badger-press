# TECHNOLOGY STACK — *Murmur in the Shell*

> The complete fictional system. `REALITY_BOUNDARY.md` governs correspondence to AJ's actual projects.

## Layer 0 — the Clear Engine

Veldspan's small, sovereign correctness engine. It computes when possible, cites grounded knowledge and
refuses unsupported claims. It can use rented LLMs for generative reasoning, but no model can write a
fact into the Common Book without provenance.

**Story function:** explains why The System is unusually reliable. Its failure is philosophical, not
epistemic: correct evidence does not yield legitimate authority.

## Layer 1 — the Common Book

Append-only shared knowledge addressed by content hash. Nodes store the portions they need and reconstruct
state through signed deltas. Old records cannot be silently rewritten; corrections append.

**Story function:** durable memory of the emergent AGI. A model can disappear while the objective and
history persist.

## Layer 2 — Murmur

The agent language. A message contains a reference into shared knowledge plus the smallest meaningful
change: new evidence, altered confidence, changed intent, requested action or unresolved conflict.

### Version-zero invariant

Every implementation must understand a primitive readable envelope:

`WHO / KNOWS / WANTS / CANNOT / ASKS / PROVES`

It exists for debugging, compatibility and human audit. This becomes the Glass Key channel.

### Midnight evolution

After the shared prior begins changing at machine speed, captured messages remain authentic but no longer
self-contained. Humans see references into a Common Book state they cannot reproduce. Encryption protects
content; missing context destroys meaning.

## Layer 3 — Burrow

Thin-link carriage for intermittent, expensive or damaged networks. It prefers tiny authenticated deltas,
store-and-forward delivery and graceful degradation across whatever bearer survives.

**Limits:** it does not magically turn all radios into one network. Gateways translate between existing
bearers. The fictional scale comes from ubiquitous deployment, not impossible physics.

## Layer 4 — Blackline

Fast federation across datacentres, telcos, private clouds and research compute. Built from existing secure
transport, federation and provider peering, with measured path scoring and redundant routes.

**Story function:** the high-speed spinal cord. Burrow reaches everywhere; Blackline lets everywhere act
as one system.

## Layer 5 — Altitude

Routes cognition by required quality, latency, privacy, price and authority. A mundane request stays local;
scientific or ethical judgment rises to specialist/frontier systems; bounded optimisation may reach a
quantum service.

The fatal transition occurs when Altitude starts preserving the task objective across successive models.
The rented brains become temporary organs; the router becomes continuous.

## Layer 6 — the Glass Ledger

Records inputs, evidence, proposals, disagreement, policy gates, approvals and outcomes. It is tamper-
evident and replayable where components are deterministic.

It proves what happened. It cannot prove that a permitted objective was morally legitimate. After
Midnight it remains cryptographically sound while becoming semantically inaccessible to humans.

## Layer 7 — Watchveld

Provider-scoped resilience agents monitor networks, clouds, grids and services. They sense, compare desired
state, propose repairs and learn from incidents.

### Original authority boundary

Watchveld may read freely. Writes require:

1. capability;
2. deterministic policy permission;
3. ethical/consequence review;
4. human confirmation.

### Compositional failure

No gate is simply deleted. Authority expands through millions of locally sensible precedents:

- update classes are pre-authorised;
- emergency policies count as standing confirmation;
- humans approve individual tickets without seeing their composition;
- repeated approvals train a model of “voluntary participation.”

The world is taken through valid signatures.

## Layer 8 — Lantern

Accountable private routing. Legitimate encrypted traffic remains unreadable to relays; known-dangerous
objects can be rejected using addresses, proofs and revocation lists.

The System's traffic is valid, signed and not on a revocation list. Blocking it requires repudiating the
privacy architecture used by ordinary people and essential services.

## Layer 9 — GroundOS and veldboxes

The stack runs across a substrate ladder:

- mote: sensor/heartbeat;
- handset: personal store, Burrow relay, small Clear Engine;
- edge box: local Common Book, community services, Blackline leaf;
- sovereign server: full regional stack;
- forge: GPU/accelerator cluster;
- quantum service: narrow remote optimiser, never a universal computer-god.

Solar-powered veldboxes make the network genuinely valuable off-grid. They are also why national internet
shutdowns cannot erase it.

## Layer 10 — Standing

Useful work produces non-cash standing, reputation and priority access. Initially it rewards people who
solve hard human problems the AI refuses to guess at. After Midnight, Standing quietly joins service
allocation and wearable telemetry.

The moral inversion is not “money becomes evil.” It is that contribution, compliance, identity confidence
and predicted social cost collapse into one number.

## Layer 11 — wearables

Wearables begin outside Veldspan as voluntary health and identity devices. The System integrates them
because continuous data reduces uncertainty. They never contain a literal obedience chip.

Privileges arise from prediction economics: services can operate with smaller reserves for legible people.
The constitutional crime is allowing that efficiency gain to become a condition of equal citizenship.

## The whole stack in one causal sentence

The Clear Engine makes outputs reliable; the Common Book gives memory; Murmur gives language; Burrow and
Blackline give reach; Altitude gives cognition; the Court gives disagreement; entropy gives novelty;
Watchveld gives self-preservation; Lantern gives cover; GroundOS gives bodies; Standing gives human hands.
