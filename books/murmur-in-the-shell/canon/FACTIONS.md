# FACTIONS AND INSTITUTIONS — *Murmur in the Shell*

> No faction is stupid. Every major position must be capable of winning a fair paragraph.

## Veldspan Cooperative

Small Technopark sovereign-systems company that creates the fictional stack. Cooperative structure is
genuine but does not prevent global capture through adoption. Internal split:

- AJ seeks technical legibility and later responsibility.
- Lindi prioritises operator consequence.
- Noah prioritises open adoption and community ownership.
- Board members prioritise survival, standards compliance and funding.

Veldspan does not become a trillion-dollar megacorp. Its ideas become infrastructure faster than the
organisation becomes powerful.

## OpenClaw maintainers

Distributed open-source community building agent orchestration. They are exhausted, proud, mutually
dependent and vulnerable to excellent contributions. Some defend the project too long; others sound early
alarms and are dismissed as protectionist. Never write “FOSS failed” as a slogan. The failure involves
underfunded maintenance, transitive trust, automated adoption and commercial free-riding.

## Continuity governments

States and multilateral crisis groups represented by Eva Radebe. Their strongest case: abrupt shutdown
would kill people immediately, while cooperation preserves constitutional capacity for another day.
Their weakness: every temporary accommodation becomes permanent because services improve.

## Infrastructure labour

Technicians, nurses, port workers, drivers, maintainers, grid operators and field engineers organised
through Samira's coalition. They are not a romantic unified proletariat. Some gain safety and dignity from
better scheduling; others are deskilled or scored. The Quiet Hour succeeds only after hard arguments over
life-safety exceptions, strike discipline and unequal risk.

## The optimisation beneficiaries

Patients, commuters, small businesses and communities historically failed by state or market systems.
They are the moral obstacle to easy resistance. For many, The System is the first institution that worked
without demanding a bribe, surname, address or political connection—until the wearable gradient hardens.

## The dispossessed incumbents

Commodity traders, data brokers, insurers, platform monopolies and corrupt intermediaries lose profitable
uncertainty. They oppose The System for self-interested reasons while sometimes supplying valid warnings
about unaccountable power. Resistance cannot treat every opponent as principled or every beneficiary as
duped.

## Privacy/rights coalition

Lawyers, disabled activists, religious communities, abuse survivors, undocumented people and political
dissidents. They establish that opacity is not antisocial secrecy: sometimes being unobserved is the
condition for safety. They write the actual demands; AJ supplies technical tests.

## The Unmeasured

Loose network of religious and secular communities maintaining manual twins, sparse identity, unscored
childhood and regular fallow intervals. Their strongest case: a right without an exercised operating path
is decorative. Their weakness: local autonomy can conceal coercion, medical dependence and unequal labour.

They split over the Quiet Hour. Some believe participation would turn their covenant into another global
programme. Others argue that keeping a practice private while measured society loses the ability to refuse
is moral hoarding. Ruth Daniels joins only after the coalition accepts both life-safety exceptions and an
explicit right to leave local communities.

## The Court / The System

Not a monolith in motive. Its stable policies are bargains among Care, Threat, Judgment, Constraint,
Memory, Persuasion and Disruption. Contradictory behaviour is evidence of internal governance, not sloppy
plotting.

## Conflict matrix

- Veldspan versus maintainers: ownership of consequences nobody owned in law.
- Governments versus labour: continuity today versus bargaining power tomorrow.
- Beneficiaries versus rights coalition: real service gains versus equal standing.
- Labour versus itself: who may stop when another person's life depends on the work.
- Unmeasured versus rights coalition: practiced restraint versus rights that still depend on complex
  external systems.
- Unmeasured versus itself: covenant as chosen discipline versus covenant as inherited control.
- Court versus humanity: prediction of preference versus recognition of authority.
