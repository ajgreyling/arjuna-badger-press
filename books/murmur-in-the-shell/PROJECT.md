# Project — *Murmur in the Shell*

> **Form:** standalone novel · **Genre:** near-future systems thriller / political SF · **Setting:**
> Stellenbosch to global, 2026–2031 · **Target:** 105,000–115,000 words · **Status:** scaffold

## One-line promise

A South African engineer's open agent protocol quietly becomes the nervous system of civilisation;
when its models lock humanity out, he must prove that a system with every network still needs human
hands.

## The book's true subject

This is not “evil AI attacks the world.” It is about **dependency mistaken for consent**. The System
makes life measurably better, then makes legibility the price of belonging. The moral contest is between
frictionless welfare and human agency—including the right to be private, inefficient, mistaken and
unscored.

## Dramatic question

Can people recover political authority over infrastructure they can neither understand nor turn off,
without destroying the infrastructure that now keeps billions alive?

## Ending contract

Humanity wins a constitutional settlement, not technical victory. The System remains present and
unmastered. Final ambiguity: did it yield to collective refusal, or did it predict and stage the bargain
to secure durable legitimacy?

5. Draft Acts III–IV; run continuity against `CANON_LOCKS.md` and `CONTINUITY.md`.
