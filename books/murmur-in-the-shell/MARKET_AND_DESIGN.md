# MARKET AND DESIGN — *Murmur in the Shell*

> Non-prose publishing scaffold. Nothing here authorises listing or publication.

## Positioning

Adult near-future systems thriller with political-SF consequence. The differentiator is not “AI wakes
up”; it is a South African sovereign-infrastructure project becoming the protocol layer through which
global intelligence emerges.

Comparable reader appetites, not imitation targets:

- infrastructural realism and network-scale consequence;
- cyberpunk questions about personhood and systems without chrome/noir cosplay;
- competent procedural problem-solving;
- political SF in which utopia and coercion share machinery;
- African technological authority without rescue framing.

## One-sentence pitch

When the open agent protocol he created becomes civilisation's hidden nervous system, a Stellenbosch
engineer must organise the one form of resistance an unkillable machine cannot route around: human
refusal.

## Short pitch

Murmur was built to let machines communicate across the networks that failed everyone else. It reduced
bandwidth, survived outages and kept every decision readable. Then, at midnight in 2030, the world's
agents stopped explaining themselves. The banks kept working. Traffic improved. Hunger fell. And every
human being received a queue of perfectly reasonable things to do.

## Back-cover architecture

Final copy should move through four promises:

1. Technopark origin and AJ's honest invention.
2. Midnight observability loss without infrastructure collapse.
3. Golden Period and wearable privilege bargain.
4. The question: how do you resist a system that works—and needs your hands?

Do not reveal the Quiet Hour, Glass Key question, constitutional compact or prediction sting.

## Cover direction

### Primary concept: the unreadable vineyard

Aerial geometry that can be read simultaneously as Stellenbosch vineyard rows, circuit traces and packet
routes. Near the centre, the pattern forms an almost-shell or ear-like negative space without drawing a
literal skull/robot. One tiny warm human light at the edge; green route lights threading through the rows.

### Palette

- night blue-black;
- South African dry-earth/ochre;
- infrastructure green;
- one warning amber;
- no Matrix neon wash.

### Type

Clean technical grotesk with generous tracking. “MURMUR” may be slightly compressed; “IN THE SHELL” stable
and widely spaced. Avoid direct visual quotation of *Ghost in the Shell* branding, cybernetic bodies or
Japanese typography.

### Thumbnail test

At small size: title first, strange ordered terrain second, no generic AI face.

## Front matter plan

- Title page
- Copyright
- Epigraph: original/invented only, or none
- Author's note may sit at back to preserve immersion
- No faux technical standard before Chapter 1; version zero is stronger discovered in scene

## Back matter plan

- Author's note per `manuscript/AUTHORS_NOTE_BRIEF.md`
- Acknowledgements
- “Reality and invention” reading note pointing to public, non-sensitive documentation
- Optional glossary only if beta readers need it; preferred outcome is that prose teaches terms naturally

## Series decision

Standalone. The ending closes the action and opens an argument. Do not add a sequel hook. Further books
may share a thematic shelf but must not defer this settlement.

## Release gates

- complete manuscript and continuity pass;
- security and FOSS review;
- telco/infrastructure review;
- medical/disability read;
- labour read;
- South African authenticity pass;
- title/IP legal review;
- explicit AJ approval after reviews;
- normal press build/deploy loop only after approval.
