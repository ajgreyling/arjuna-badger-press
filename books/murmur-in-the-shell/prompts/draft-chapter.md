# Draft-chapter prompt — *Murmur in the Shell*

Draft exactly one chapter of *Murmur in the Shell*. Before writing, read in `project.json` canon order,
then read `SCENE_CARDS.md`, `build/story_state.md` and the relevant chapter in
`CHAPTER_STRUCTURE.md`.

## Hard gates

1. Close-third past, one POV; no System POV.
2. Respect `REVEAL_LEDGER.md`; later knowledge may not leak backward.
3. Every technical explanation requires task, constraint, physical consequence, disagreement and changed
   decision.
4. Show work terminating in bodies, hands or infrastructure; no abstract stack tour.
5. Real CongoSky/Lucid elements remain inside `REALITY_BOUNDARY.md`; dangerous capabilities belong to
   their fictional descendants.
6. No actionable exploit recipe, real vulnerable dependency or operational evasion instructions.
7. The System's benefits remain real. No easy moral sorting.
8. Plant/payoff IDs assigned to the chapter must appear naturally and be logged afterward.
9. End on changed operating reality, not an essay conclusion.
10. Do not draft another chapter in the same pass.

## After drafting

Update `build/story_state.md` with word count, date/location, relationship movement, objects moved, PP IDs,
reveals opened/closed, continuity holes and the exact next chapter state.
