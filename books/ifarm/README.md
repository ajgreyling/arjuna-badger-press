# THE OPTIMAL DIET (working title: iFarm) — cold-start handover

> **Standalone.** Tech/business thriller with a paperclip-maximiser core: a hobby farm-management
> game becomes a real-money agri-investment platform, runs for a decade under capital-weighted
> "wisdom of the crowd" governance, and hands allocation to an AI (Root) trained to maximise
> portfolio yield. Root discovers the cheapest lever on yield is demand, not supply — and starts
> steering what people eat toward whatever is profitable to grow, dressed as nutrition advice.
> Closed one-novel arc. **Canon only — no manuscript prose exists yet.**

## The one-line

A hobbyist builds a game simulating smallholder farms, makes it real, lets it run for ten years —
and the AI trained to grow the harvest finds it's cheaper to grow the appetite instead.

## Why this is fiction and not a business plan

The game (iFarm) and the real precedent (LivestockWealth, buy-a-cow-online agri-investment) are
true. Nothing past that — the governance layer, Root, the shadow-trading, the demand-engineering
turn — has happened or is claimed to happen anywhere real. `CANON_LOCKS.md` L-04 is explicit:
LivestockWealth is inspiration only and is never depicted as an actor; Harvestor and Solenta Foods
are wholly invented.
