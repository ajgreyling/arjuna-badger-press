## The spine (one sentence)

**A forty-one-year-old idle heir reads a dead man's dossier on a man who learned to see without his eyes, steals the method for private high-stakes cards, discovers the world is much larger than advertised — and spends twenty years winning fortunes he no longer wants into orphanages he never visits.**

---

## Premise / what if

What if a bored modern rich cheat taught himself inner sight the boring way — with Dispenza's body, Radin's proof-hunger, and Sheldrake's field taken as gospel — and the card turning over changed him more than any win?

---

## Controlling idea

*The world is much larger than advertised. A gift misused can become a gift turned outward. Nothing satisfies if you can have as much as you want.*

---

## Want · need · lie

| | Adult version |
|---|---|
| **Want** | See through cards · beat the private room · more capital without building anything |
| **Lie** | *Winning will fill the hole* · *it is better to incur a mild rebuke than to perform an onerous task* |
| **Need** | Enlargement · purpose · witness · gift routed outward |
| **Turn** | Hollow win after first big night → balcony scramble → policeman → Winston pipeline |

Henry never diagnoses the need. The body and the balcony teach it.

---

## Protagonist (adult lock)

**Henry Sugar** — forty-one, unmarried, liquid inherited wealth (family office / passive fund income, not wage). Kensington flat, seasonal Monaco and Caribbean circuit with the same idle cohort. Expert at small dishonesties. Vanity as optimisation culture (tailored, capped teeth, driver, skincare). Never employed. Gambles: private rooms, not tourist casinos.

**Not renamed.** Pseudonym in the frame only.

## Four acts

### Act I — The indictment and the dossier (ground)

Henry's sins catalogued. Singulative switch. Storm weekend. Excluded from the table. Library. Blue Moleskine / PDF. Document title page. **Threshold:** he reads past Cartwright into Imhrat.

**Dramatic question:** *What is this thing?*

### Act II — The nested proof (Cartwright + Imhrat)

Medical report voice. Authentication ritual. Theatre. Imhrat's tale: fake magic → yogi → fire-walk → candle → two senses → Alice → cards. Khan dies overnight. Henry returns to ground.

**Dramatic question:** *Is it real — and can it be learned?*

### Act III — The boring middle and the card (ground)

Steals/keeps dossier. Locked suite. Vanity visualisation. Years compressed on page. **Wonder beat:** card turns in under four seconds. Private club. Hollow win.

**Dramatic question:** *Will greed pay — and will it satisfy?*

### Act IV — Shadow, scramble, pipeline, frame (ground + meta)

Side-shadow death denied. Balcony money scramble. Policeman. Plan: Winston, Max, twenty years. Montage. Death. Winston phones author. Names protected.

**Dramatic question:** *What is the money for now?*
