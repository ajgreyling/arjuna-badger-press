# THEMES — *Henry Sugar*

## Primary — The world is larger than advertised

The card turn. Wonder without irony. The reader's childhood capacity for enlargement, restored for
one page in an adult body.

## Secondary — Greed as motor, gift as finish

Henry does not begin good. The yogi's path was never about morality lectures — it was about
**seeing**. Charity arrives **after** sight, not before.

## Secondary — Nested truth

Stories inside stories; documents inside clubs; the account within the account — each layer told
straight so the next can land.

## Press substrate (carry, never preach)

Consciousness-based reality · anti-dogma · somatic truth before metaphysical claim · honest
skepticism outside the wonder beat, full belief inside it.

## Real magik (with a k)

Radin's *Real Magic* as nonfiction spine; Dahl's card turn as fable delivery. **Magic** = trickery
(stage magician, marked deck). **Magik** = mind-matter anomaly, extended perception, consciousness
not confined to the skull — the wonder beat, no wink.

## Mind not in the skull (Sheldrake)

Sheldrake's *Science Set Free* names what Imhrat and Henry **live**: consciousness is not a bulb
inside the cranium. **Morphic resonance** = yogic lineage as habit of perception. **Extended mind** =
inner sight reaching the card before the eyes do. Planted ch-09 · paid ch-13 · wonder ch-14 (PP-A02).

## Common people, uncommon capacity (Dispenza)

Dispenza's *Becoming Supernatural* (2017) is the **training spine**: ordinary humans rewiring
through breath, elevated emotion, and repetition until the body does what the culture says it cannot.
Henry is the subtitle — a common cheat learning the uncommon. Planted ch-09 (Imhrat) · ch-11–13
(Henry boring middle) · never cited at the card turn (PP-A03).

## Origin: chick, robot, REG (Peoc'h)

Before the three gospels landed as names, **René Peoc'h's** chick-and-tychoscope experiments made
mind-not-in-skull **visceral**: day-old imprinted chicks associated with biasing a random robot's
path toward them. Author inference (not one published trial): if a chick can pull an REG, what of
**12 or 144 trained meditators** turning inner eye on one person's wellbeing? Thematic pressure on
Dahl's **gift outward** — not a new plot superpower. See ch-98 · `SOURCE_NOTES` Tier 4b.
