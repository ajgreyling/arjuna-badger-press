# STORY_BIBLE — *Henry Sugar*

> **Creative driver (LOCKED):** Henry Sugar wonder — Dahl's register when the card turns over.
> **Physics (LOCKED):** Dispenza · Radin · Sheldrake as gospel inside the world.

---

## Premise

**Henry Sugar** — wealthy, bored, clever, amoral enough to cheat at cards — finds a document that
contains another man's life: **Imhrat Khan**, who wanted to see without eyes and found a yogi willing
to teach the boring way. Henry reads for advantage. He stays for years. When he first sees through
the playing card, the prose goes quiet — and the reader, for a page, believes the world is much
larger than advertised.

---

## Protagonist

**Henry Sugar** — not renamed. Adult register: forty-ish, liquid wealth, no vocation, expert at
small dishonesties. He is **not** seeking enlightenment. He wants to win. Dahl never mocked the yogi;
we never mock Henry's greed **during** the wonder beat.

---

## The three gospels (in-world physics — not footnotes)

| Voice | Role in the book |
|---|---|
| **Joe Dispenza** | **Common → uncommon via the body** — heat, breath, elevated emotion, months of nothing before click. North star: *Becoming Supernatural* (2017). ch-09 Imhrat somatics · ch-11–13 Henry training (PP-A03) |
| **Dean Radin** | Psi as real — blind protocols, presentiment, the lab evidence Henry half-reads and half-feels |
| **Rupert Sheldrake** | **Mind not in the skull** — morphic fields, extended mind, attention as real; the "field" Henry learns to listen to at the click (ch-09, ch-13, ch-14). North star: *Science Set Free* (2012) |

These are **true in the world**. Characters may argue; the narrative **does not** debunk at the proof
beat.

---

## Nested tale (Imhrat Khan)

Told **straight** — documentary hush, sentences lengthen, the club frame falls away. The yogi is
never a punchline. The training is physical before metaphysical: sweat, failure, migraine, nothing,
nothing, *click*.

---

## Tone contract

See [`STYLE_GUIDE.md`](STYLE_GUIDE.md). Short version: paranoia and worldly cynicism allowed
**between** wonder beats; **forbidden during** the card turning over.

---

## End state

Henry can see. Henry cheats less, gives more. The club men who heard the story are not the same.
The reader should finish **enlarged** — not merely entertained.
