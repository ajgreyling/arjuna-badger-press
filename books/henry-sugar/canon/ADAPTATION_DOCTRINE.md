# Adaptation Doctrine — *Henry Sugar* (deep craft fidelity, not reskinning)

> **Binding for the whole book.** Same rule as The Reichenbach Files: **transpose the engine, not
> the props.** Dahl's story is not "a man learns magic" — it is a **nested documentary**, a **greedy
> protagonist**, **years of boring practice**, a **first small impossible proof**, and a **gift turned
> outward** — with **no mockery of the yogi at the moment it works**.

## The test (apply to every choice)

Not *"what's the modern equivalent of a typed manuscript?"* but:

> **"What is Dahl's engine — arc, moral, nested structure, wonder contract — and what adult life
> produces that same engine now?"**

If a change only swaps a surface, it has failed. If it changes a surface because the deep structure
demanded an adult vehicle, it has succeeded.

---

## Inherited — must be reproduced

- **The arc** — boredom → document → nested tale → training → first proof → escalation → charity.
- **The moral** — the world is larger than advertised; gifts misused become gifts turned outward.
- **The structure** — club frame; nested Imhrat Khan account told straight; return to frame.
- **The craft** — documentary hush in the nested tale; Dahl warmth toward Henry's flaws; **reverence
  at the wonder beat**; physical specificity before the impossible.

---

## Transposed — surfaces only where structure demands

Communication, wealth, gambling venues, the document's provenance — rendered in adult materials
(PDF, estate sale, private game) **only where the inherited engine needs a modern vehicle**.

---

## The adult substrate (Dahl was of his moment; we are of ours)

Dahl's Henry lived among post-war British leisure-class boredom. Ours lives among **liquid wealth,
attention surplus, and spiritual marketplace noise** — carried as **character interior**, never
lectured:

- **Wealth without purpose** — the engine of Henry's boredom (hedge-fund restlessness, not country-house ennui).
- **The consciousness literature as lived physics** — Dispenza, Radin, Sheldrake taken **as gospel**
  in-world: not cited as self-help, but **how the training actually works** on Henry's body and
  perception.
- **Skepticism outside, belief inside** — Henry doubts like an adult; the prose **does not** when
  the card turns.
