# STYLE_GUIDE — *Henry Sugar*

> Primary touchstone: Roald Dahl, *The Wonderful Story of Henry Sugar*.
> Secondary texture: Dispenza somatic awe · Radin experimental psi · Sheldrake field intuition.

---

## The Henry Sugar contract (binding)

Henry begins as a cheat. He stays a cheat a long time. **Never mock the yogi, the training, or the
moment it works** — only Henry's motives. When Henry first sees through the playing card, the prose
goes quiet and the reader becomes a child who has just been told the world is **much larger than
advertised**.

---

## Voice

- **Extradiegetic frame:** intrusive author — indictment, side-shadow, true-story contract; addresses the reader.
- **Iterative portrait:** habitual Henry (sins, vanity list, motto) before the singulative switch.
- **Nested tale:** documentary hush — slower, longer sentences; Cartwright then Imhrat told straight.
- **Training:** physical before metaphysical — heat behind the eyes, sweat, hours of nothing.
- **Wonder beat:** **no wink**, no debunk voice, no ironic distance.

---

## Dahl craft tools (binding — see CRAFT_MAP.md)

- **Side-shadowing** before the charity turn — draft the "competent fiction writer" death; deny it.
- **Parallel construction** — at least one medical anaphora chain (*we must… we must…*).
- **Singulative switch** — explicit hand-off from type-portrait to plot.
- **Resonant image** — money scramble (one note, then all).
- **Hyperbolic grammar** — lists, sums, durations stated plainly.

## Machine-tell taboos

- No chakra lecture — centres as **sensation sequence**, not infographic.
- No wellness-influencer cadence during practice or proof.
- No "she realised" discovery crutch — she *sees*; the reader sees with her.
- No **bibliography voice** at the wonder beat (ch-14, Imhrat card proof) — no URLs, no "studies show."

## Science in prose (C-027 — binding)

**Bring as much reported real science into the prose as possible.** See [`SCIENCE_IN_PROSE.md`](SCIENCE_IN_PROSE.md).

- **Documentary nested tale:** Price 1935 protocols, fire-walk temperatures, Kuda Bux skin-sensitivity
  claims, medical publish ambition — **on the page**, not deferred to backmatter.
- **Henry's adult memory:** half-remembered Radin/RNG/presentiment clips, McMoneagle cultural ghost,
  Peoc'h chick-and-robot as intrusive thought — **mapped onto** the dossier as he reads.
- **Training chapters:** Dispenza somatics and Sheldrake field **dense** in ch-09, ch-11–13.
- **Wonder beat exception:** ch-14 = phenomenology only (body before mind); science **felt**, not cited.
- **Author frame (ch-01, ch-16, ch-19):** most explicit; may name Peoc'h, REG, consciousness-not-in-skull.

Backmatter duplicates on purpose. The reader who never opens ch-98 still got the science **in the story**.

## Tone balance

```
[ Adult cynicism | Wealth boredom | Greed as motor ]     ← frame
                        ↓
              Henry Sugar wonder                         ← engine (proof beats)
                        ↓
     Dispenza body-awe · Radin psi · Sheldrake field    ← dense in prose (not sermon)
                        ↓
              ch-98 backmatter                         ← duplicates & cites (err on side of duplication)
```

The reader should finish **lighter** than cynical literary fiction normally allows — enlarged, not
merely survived.
