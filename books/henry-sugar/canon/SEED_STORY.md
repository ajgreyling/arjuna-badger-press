## One sentence

A bored rich man who cheats at cards reads a nested tale of a yogi who learned to see without eyes,
does the boring years of practice, and discovers — without irony — that the world is much larger than
advertised.

---

## Why this book exists

Dahl wrote Henry Sugar for everyone, including children who could feel the card turn. This retelling
keeps that wonder contract for **adults** who already suspect the official story is too small — and
gives the training a physics they can follow home: somatic practice (Dispenza), experimental psi
(Radin), morphic / extended mind (Sheldrake). Not preached. **Lived on the page.**

**North star:** Dean Radin's *Real Magic* (2018) — **real magik with a k**. Stage magic is Imhrat's
disillusion; card-marking is Henry's sin; **magik** is the card turn played straight: consciousness
as fundamental, perception not skull-local, anomalies documented under blind protocol. This novel is
Radin's thesis as Dahl fable.

**Sheldrake's lane:** Radin proves the anomaly; Sheldrake locates the mind. *Science Set Free* (2012)
names the dogma this book refuses: that consciousness is only inside brains. Morphic habit + extended
perception = how inner sight **knows** without eyes (PP-A02).

**Dispenza's lane:** *Becoming Supernatural* (2017) — **how common people do the uncommon**. Henry
is the common man; inner sight is the uncommon. Somatic sequencing, breath, heat, boring years =
ch-09 Imhrat texture · ch-11–13 Henry montage (PP-A03). Not wellness sermon — **lived in the body**.

**Science in prose (C-027):** maximise reported science **on the page** — Price protocols, presentiment
felt, Peoc'h chick thought, McMoneagle boredom — see `SCIENCE_IN_PROSE.md`. Backmatter duplicates.
