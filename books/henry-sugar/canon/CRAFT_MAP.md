# CRAFT_MAP — *Henry Sugar* (Dahl source → adult retelling)

> **Source:** Roald Dahl, *The Wonderful Story of Henry Sugar* ([1977 collection](https://archive.org/details/wonderfulstoryof0000dahl_l7r9); Heinemann 1979 ed.). Real-life seed: Pakistani mystic **Kuda Bux** and Dr Cartwright's 1935 medical report.
>
> **Method:** Name every binding craft element from the original; map each to our retelling. Original prose is **not** copied. Structure and engine are **faithful**.

---

## I. Architecture (named levels)

Dahl builds a **matryoshka** — four diegetic floors below the ground story, one meta floor above. We reproduce the **embedding**, not the exact props.

| Craft term | Dahl (original) | Our retelling |
|---|---|---|
| **Extradiegetic narrator** | Dahl-as-author at open and close; "I agreed to write it up" | Same contract: authorial frame, identity protected, **true-story persuasion** without copying Dahl's voice |
| **Ground story (intradiegetic)** | Henry Sugar's life — greed → training → casino → charity → death | Same arc; adult Henry (liquid wealth, private games) |
| **Hypodiegetic level 1** | Henry reads *A Report on an Interview with Imhrat Khan* (1934) | Scanned PDF / dead estate / forgotten file — same **found-document shock** |
| **Hypodiegetic level 2** | Dr **John Cartwright**'s medical report — Bombay, theatre show, authentication | Named physician report; modern Mumbai or retained Bombay texture; **clinical testimony voice** |
| **Hypodiegetic level 3** | **Imhrat Khan**'s autobiography inside the report | Told straight — documentary hush; **Banerjee → referral → training** preserved |
| **Hypodiegetic level 4** | Imhrat reads **Alice's Adventures in Wonderland** as proof-of-sight | Intertextual proof beat — different text or same PD Alice; **hand-as-sensor** read-aloud |
| **Frame ascent (meta close)** | **John Winston**, Swiss accountant, phones Dahl after Henry's death | Accountant / executor calls author; **posthumous revelation frame** |
| **Story-within-story-within-story-within-story** | Slap Happy Larry counts **five** diegetic levels | **Minimum four nests** before Henry acts; do not flatten |

**Press glossary cross-links:** *Adaptation — transposition vs reskinning* · *caption-after-image* (invert: wonder **before** footnote).

## II. The true-story contract (metafiction / tall tale)

| Element | Dahl | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| **Tall-tale persuasion** | Reader persuaded this "really happened" | Same — but honest about our retelling status in front matter; **in-story** contract stays |
| **Intrusive authorial narrator** | Steps in to judge Henry, side-shadow, geolocate Curzon Street | Adult register; same **author-on-the-shoulder** at indictment and close |

| **Geolocated specificity** | Curzon Street, Mayfair balcony, Guildford manor | One real street, one real balcony or window — **maps-clickable** anchor |
| **Hyperbolic arithmetic** | £6,600 = house or car; £144 million; 20 orphanages | Modern equivalents stated plainly — **money as measurable wonder** |

## III. Opening movement (before plot)

Dahl does **not** open in a club. He opens with **indictment**, then **iterative portrait**, then **singulative switch**.

| Craft element | Dahl | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| **Character indictment (Deadly Sins register)** | Greedy, proud, slothful — stated bluntly before story proper | Same moral clarity; adult sins (selfishness, vanity, sloth) — **no softening** |
| **Iterative time** | "Henry was forty-one… wealthy… unmarried… too selfish…" — habitual life | Compressed class portrait: what Henry **always** does |
| **Singulative switch (overt)** | *"And now that you've got a rough idea of the sort of person Henry Sugar was, I can begin my story."* | **LOCKED LINE-TYPE:** explicit hand-off from type to event |
| **Personal motto** | *"It is better to incur a mild rebuke than to perform an onerous task"* | Henry's own words — cheat the effort, not the story |
| **Class portrait of idle wealth** | Inherited money; seasonal migration; friends equally rich; urge to grow richer | Fund peers, yacht weeks, **attention surplus**, same hunger |
| **Physical vanity catalogue** | Tailor, aftershave, capped teeth, mole removed, Ferrari, manicure | Adult grooming / optimisation culture — **same exhaustive list rhythm** |
| **Sloth as comic sin** | Never worked a day; friends find motto hilarious | Never built anything; **mild rebuke** as life philosophy |
| **Gambling cheat texture** | Sleeping-powder in opponent's tortoise (Danny echo) | One small cheat vignette before yoga — **establishes method** |

## XI. Adult physics (Dispenza · Radin · Sheldrake as gospel)

Mapped **inside** Dahl's existing slots — not a replacement structure.

| Dahl slot | Original fill | Our fill |
|---|---|---|
| Candle / breath work | Yogi concentration | **Dispenza:** somatic rewiring, heat, elevated emotion |
| Two senses of sight | Inner skin-vision | **Radin:** blind protocol, presentiment, mind-matter lab evidence Henry half-remembers |
| Field / habit / non-local | Implicit in yogi lore | **Sheldrake:** morphic resonance, extended mind — **how inner sight "knows"** |
| Doctor publish ambition | Western medicine captures miracle | Radin beat: **peer review vs lived proof** |
| Side-shadow death | Violates yogi code | Same — **personal gain corrupts the gift** (shadow ending only) |

**Rule:** gospel is **true in-world**. **Maximise reported science dramatised in prose** (C-027 —
see `SCIENCE_IN_PROSE.md`). ch-14 wonder beat stays quiet: phenomenology only, no citations.
Backmatter duplicates; it does not replace on-page science.
