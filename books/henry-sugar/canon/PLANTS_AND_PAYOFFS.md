# PLANTS_AND_PAYOFFS — *Henry Sugar*

> **Scope:** Every Chekhov's gun, plant, payoff, echo-rhyme, side-shadow, and frame-close in Dahl's
> *The Wonderful Story of Henry Sugar* ([source](https://archive.org/details/wonderfulstoryof0000dahl_l7r9)),
> mapped to our retelling. **MacGuffins, want/need, spine, stakes:** [`NARRATIVE_ENGINES.md`](NARRATIVE_ENGINES.md).
>
> **Press rules:** L-10 (fire what you hang) · plant must precede payoff · wonder before footnote.
> See [`docs/craft/terms/plant-payoff.md`](../../../docs/craft/terms/plant-payoff.md).

**Legend**

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **GUN** | Chekhov's gun — object/faculty shown early; must fire |
| **PLANT** | Quiet setup |
| **PAYOFF** | Setup fulfilled |
| **ECHO** | Structural rhyme (same engine, different valence) |
| **SHADOW** | Side-shadow — competent-fiction counterfactual (not the true ending) |
