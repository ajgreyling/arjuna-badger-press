## Act I — the scratch (ch-01–06)
1. **The Light Was Doing the Thing** *(written)* — Mark, the photographer's eye, shoots a midden in the
   dunes east of the Goukou river mouth at **Stilbaai** (Blombos country) at golden hour; the camera
   catches what the eye skated past — a scored ochre stone with a wrong gold gleam. He texts *J.*
   (Artifact leads. Wound planted: the surface-skater.)
2. **A Man Who Drives Like He's Closing a Drawer** *(written)* — Jakobus arrives on home ground (the
   Beast). Reads the *frame,* not the rock; "drawing with light → the oldest drawing on earth." Boat vs
   Beast counter-melody. The door opens: they go to dig. (HANDOFF: the find is not theirs — locals + the
   Order who are allowed to dig.)
3. **The Dune Face** *(written)* — they go to the midden; Mark photographs the ochre in situ; Jakobus reads
   the stratigraphy (it washed out from *under* the shells = far older). First real OBSTACLE: the dune is
   actively eating the site, and a storm is coming — the evidence is washing into the sea. COST: race the
   weather; you can't just dig, it's not yours, but it won't survive the week.
4. **The Right Hands** *(written)* — Jakobus brings in a **Khoisan descendant / custodian of this coast**
   (LOCKED: the expert who LEADS; the find is theirs; Jakobus witnesses, Mark's eye serves). The custodian
   reads the cross-hatch as *language older than language* — the mark that means *we were here, we thought,*
   the thing Blombos proved. READ: the artifact is of the Blombos/Pinnacle-Point family, ~70–100k years, and
   it should NOT be a portable, *placed* stone — someone carried it here and set it.
5. **Pinnacle Point / the cave** — the bearing (the gold fleck) points **west** along the coast toward
   **Mossel Bay / Pinnacle Point** (earliest modern human behaviour, heat-treated tools, first ochre) — or
   first to Blombos itself in the cliffs nearby. KEY: a second mark, in situ, deeper — the relay confirmed.
   (Blombos is local; Pinnacle Point ~90 min west; Klasies River / Nelson Bay Cave run the spine east.)
6. **The Adversary surfaces** — the Court (in its new Cape form) / a collector or a clean-handed company
   wants the ochre (the gold, the provenance) before the determination lands. The familiar deniable
   theft. Mark's photographs become the thing that can't be erased — the evidence the dune was eating.

## Act II — the dig (ch-07–14)
- Blombos itself (in the limestone cliffs a few km from the Stilbaai find): the documented oldest *drawing*
  on earth, the engraved ochre — the real site as anchor; the fictional find as the *placed* twin that
  rewrites where the symbol-makers ranged. The braid: the gold-keyed ochre points the bearing *off the
  edge of Africa, southward* (the series relay running on, the way Calendar of Stone's ran north).
- Mark's arc deepens: the man who never stayed is staying. The boat (his lightness) vs the cave (the deep
  he's being made to go into). The cost of knowing. **Jakobus's home-ground wound** (the Cape he clawed out
  of; Paarl; the dop-stelsel coast) surfaces, slow — note Stilbaai is the Overberg/Garden-Route *edge* of
  that world, not Paarl itself; he is closer to home here than anywhere in the series but not *on* it, which
  suits the slow surfacing better than the old Gordons Bay framing did.
- A Beast set-piece on the real **southern-Cape passes near the find** — e.g. the climbs over the coastal
  range inland of Stilbaai/Mossel Bay, or the Garden Route passes (Robinson, Outeniqua, the Seven Passes
  road toward George) — escape / race / the one-shot trick, used once. *(NB: "Clarens Drive / the roller-
  coaster of curves" from Mark's blog is the **Gordons Bay → Rooi Els** False Bay road, ~400 km west of the
  new site — DON'T use it here unless the route deliberately swings back to the Overberg; pick a pass that
  actually belongs to this coast.)*

## Act III — the surface and the deep (ch-15–20)
- The find handed to its people; the determination; the locals own the win; the Order witnesses; Mark's
  photographs are the record. Mark chooses to *stay* — the surface-skater who finally let a place go deep.
- The gold bearing runs south, off the edge of the world, the relay's next door opening (the series
  continues; reverse-payoff: Jakobus's red Pilbara pebble may surface; the man-no-camera-can-hold beat
  pays off — the one photograph Mark is allowed, or pointedly is not).
- Theme as EFFECT only: the deep past belongs to everyone; to truly see a thing — a place, a man — you
  have to stop skating and let it go deep on you. Never spoken.
