# STYLE_GUIDE — *Scratching the Surface*

**Inherits, in full and as binding, the STYLE_GUIDE of *The Calendar of Stone***
(`books/history-before-time/books/book1-africa/canon/STYLE_GUIDE.md`). Read it first.

Book-specific notes layered on top:
- **POV: Mark Harpur, close third, only.** His instrument is the *eye* — composition, the decisive
  moment, the difference between the accidental and the intended. The narration sees the way a
  photographer sees: light first, frame, the thing the eye skates past that the camera doesn't.
- **The artifact leads through the LENS.** The checkable thing is often *what the camera caught* — the
  geometry too regular for erosion, the OSL date, the ochre chemistry. Wonder lands after.
- **The boat is the counter-melody to the Beast.** Clean/silent/forward vs heavy/diesel/carried. Two
  freedoms; neither wins; they recognise each other. Use lightly.
- **Mark Harpur ≠ Mark of Paarl** (the engine-whisperer). Two different Marks; keep them distinct.
- **Khoisan heritage: sensitivity-read floor.** The find is the custodians'; they lead; the Order
  witnesses; Mark's eye serves; Jakobus is the road. Never "they couldn't have made it" — they made the
  first marks any human ever made.
