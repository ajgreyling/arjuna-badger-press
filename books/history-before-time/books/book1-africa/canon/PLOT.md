# PLOT — *History Before Time*, Book I (Africa) — relay skeleton

> The **quest-relay spine** (Q2). Structure is binding; texture is flexible. Each node:
> **ARRIVE → OBSTACLE → READ → COST → KEY → HANDOFF.** Every chapter advances, plants, or pays off a
> node. This is a *skeleton* from the wizard, not a beat sheet — beats get expanded during outlining.
> Sites are candidates (see WORLD.md); confirm/sequence with the author.

---

## Act I — The buried truth & the offer

**Node 0 — DURBAN / JOHANNESBURG · the ruin and the recruitment** *(rank 1)*
- **OPENING (LOCKED — Q8): open IN THE WOUND.** Page one is her public ruin or its raw aftermath —
  the hearing, the retraction, the lab result no one will publish. Tense, human, low-glamour; earns
  the reader's love for her *before* the Order or any wonder arrives. Voice and stakes first; marvel
  later.
- **Arrive:** Jennefer on the margins of the field — her anomalous date a cautionary tale; scraping a
  living; the wound raw (and rhyming, per Q10, with an older not-belonging wound).
- **Obstacle:** her own self-doubt + a world that has filed her under "crank."
- **Read (Jennefer):** she still believes the data — and hates that she's stopped saying so.
- **The find (LOCKED — Q11):** the thing that ruined her = **an impossibly early date on worked
  stone** (organic material sealed in/under a worked structure, dated to tens of thousands of years).
  Buried by the field as "contamination/error." This is the relay's first key.
- **The offer:** the **Order** approaches with proof her date was **real** (a corroborating
  artifact/reading the establishment never saw). *"You're not crazy. Come with us."*
- **Cost:** to follow, she must risk the last shred of her credibility (and trust strangers who claim
  a centuries-old secret).
- **Key-out:** the proof-object + a bearing to **Adam's Calendar.**
- **Handoff:** she goes. **(End Act I.)**

## Act II — The relay up Africa

**Node 1 — ADAM'S CALENDAR (Mpumalanga) · the stone instrument** *(rank 2)*
- **Arrive:** the standing stones at dawn; the Order's working ground.
- **Obstacle (physical + puzzle):** the site only reads correctly as an *instrument* — alignment,
  acoustics, stone properties — not as decoration. (Reached by measurement, never authority — Rule 2.)
- **Read:** Jennefer's rigour cracks it; the site *rings* / aligns in a way that can't be coincidence.
  Her first earned moment of **wonder** (the recovering skeptic, converted by the artifact).
- **Cost:** the rival surfaces; playing the site exposes them; a first taste of what they're up against.
- **Key-out:** a reading that points **down** — into the deep-gold country (why the builders cared).
- **Handoff:** the bearing forces the Witwatersrand.

**Node 2 — THE WITWATERSRAND DEEP-GOLD COUNTRY · why they came** *(rank 3)*
- **Arrive:** the deepest workings on Earth — heat, dark, pressure.
- **Obstacle:** the mine itself + the rival's reach (a watched dig, a staged hazard).
- **Read (Jennefer + the field investigator):** the first **worked-gold** object that "rings wrong" —
  too pure, formed to a *function*, not coin. The gold thread (Rule 7) ignites.
- **Cost:** a real near-miss; trust tested; possibly a betrayal-by-evidence (an assumption proven
  wrong). The vindication she craves starts to feel bigger — and more dangerous — than her career.
- **Key-out:** a marker + the inheritance question pointing **up the continent.**
- **Handoff:** the trail forces Great Zimbabwe / the up-continent leg.

**Node 3 — GREAT ZIMBABWE / up-continent · inheritance** *(rank 4)*
- **Arrive:** the stone city of the gold-trade cultures.
- **Obstacle:** human as much as physical — the rival's network closing; the puzzle of *which ruin
  remembers the builders.* Local custodians hold the key the textbooks lost (experts of their place).
- **Read (Jennefer + a custodian + the Order):** oral memory + physical evidence converge — the truth
  is **bigger than one continent** (the series thread reveals its scale).
- **Cost:** the rival forces a reckoning; someone or something is lost; Jennefer must choose proof over
  safety.
- **Key-out:** confirmation that the chain runs **beyond Africa** (the Book II handoff: Indus/Egypt).
- **Handoff:** to **Aksum** (Node 3b) for highland confirmation, then south to climax.

**Node 3b — AKSUM (Ethiopia) · highland confirmation** *(rank 4b — LOCKED; ch-14)*
- **Arrive:** obelisks; keeper **Tewodros**; inheritance road Ma names in ch-20.
- **Obstacle:** time, altitude, borders; Vorster regrouping while they are north.
- **Read:** southern rubbing + gold on highland stone — bearing **confirms Egypt**.
- **Texture:** open **chilum** (Asher / Rasta travellers); substance middle-road beats.
- **Handoff:** south to Sekuru; **The Cost** (ch-15).

## Act III — The reckoning & the choice

**Climax — the rival vs. the wonder**
- The antagonist moves to **own or loot** the central site/find (strip it, patent it, vault it).
- **Jennefer's choice (the want, resolved):** not just to *get* her vindication, but to **stand on the
  truth out loud** — protect the wonder for everyone (the Order's vow) over claiming it for herself or
  letting it be hoarded. The personal arc (fear → courage) and the plot (extraction vs. shared wonder)
  resolve in the *same* beat.
- **Cost & grounding:** a real price; the deepest mechanism stays a **designated mystery** (Rule 3) —
  she wins enough to be vindicated and to protect the place, not to "solve" deep antiquity.

**Resolution — vindicated, and the door opens**
- Her name (and her data) begin to turn; she is *changed* — no longer needing the crowd's permission.
- The **gold thread** and the wider truth point onward: the Order's quest leaves Africa. (Standalone
  ending **and** an open door — Series Bible's order-independent-but-rewarding rule.)
- **Back matter (binding):** *The Real Places in This Book* — Adam's Calendar, the Cradle/Wits gold
  country, Great Zimbabwe, Mapungubwe/Vredefort if used — what to see, what's real vs. invented.
  *(The tourism mission's payoff.)*
