## The shape of this world

Present-day Earth — but one in which the **fringe is fact**: a high-knowledge phase of humanity
(and/or its visitors) existed **before recorded time**, and left **infrastructure**, not just art,
across Africa. Nothing on the surface is fantastical; the extraordinary is **buried, drowned, or
hiding in plain sight as "ruins."** The visitors came for **gold** (the Sitchin thread, the series'
literal engine). The Order has tracked it for centuries.

## The relay map (Book I — where the set-pieces live)

The quest runs **up the spine of southern Africa**, following *inheritance and gold*, not a single
dig. Candidate nodes (real places — confirm/sequence in PLOT):

### Node settings (grounded; each a real, visitable place)

- **Adam's Calendar (Mpumalanga)** — the real Tellinger-popularised stone arrangement; in-world a
  landscape-scale **stone instrument / calendar** whose alignments and acoustics are the order's
  opening proof. Reached through *measurement* (alignment, stone properties), never by citing
  authority. **The anchor site of Book I** (and the working-title source).
- **The Witwatersrand deep-gold country** — the real deepest gold mines on Earth; in-world the answer
  to *why they came.* Depth, heat, and the first **worked-gold** object that "rings wrong" — too
  pure, formed to a geometry that means *function*, not ornament. (Soft link to the African Gold
  trilogy's gold-as-key.)
- **Great Zimbabwe / the up-continent leg** — real stone city of the gold-trade cultures; the
  *inheritance* question: which "ruin" remembers the builders, and which is just a ruin. The trail
  that will, across the series, climb toward Egypt and beyond.
- *(Candidate adds)* the **Vredefort impact structure** (oldest scar on Earth), **Mapungubwe**
  (the golden rhino; real SA gold-culture treasure) — strong, true, on-theme options for nodes.

## The protagonist's languages as a relay tool (Jennefer's gift, used honestly)

Jennefer's multilingualism (Afrikaans, English, isiXhosa, Sesotho fluent; Nguni passable; some
Swahili) is **not decoration — it's how she advances nodes** the others can't:

- **Reading the sites:** inscriptions, place-names, and the *roots* of words (her shampoo-bottle
  philology) become clues — etymology as evidence, used carefully and never as magic.
- **The custodians:** at each node the local keepers are the true experts; she earns their trust and
  their knowledge **by speaking to them in their own tongue.** (Honours the anti-poison pillar and the
  tourism-dignity theme — and gives her a competence no foreign "expert" could fake.)
- **The up-Africa leg:** her passable Swahili is the literal bridge as the relay climbs north — and
  the seed of the wider series, where the languages multiply. (Ties to the studio's a13n/Swahili
  mission off the page.)

## The grounded "physics" of the wonder (what makes it credible, not silly)

All effects are **physical and witnessable** (see MYTHOS_RULES): stone worked to anomalous
tolerances; acoustic/resonant properties of arranged stone; astronomical alignments; metallurgy and
gold purity; anomalous dates on a lab report; subsurface voids on ground-penetrating radar. The world
*shows the artifact*, then lets the awe land. The deepest mechanisms (who the builders were, exactly
how) remain **designated mysteries** — named, never fully explained.

## Sensory texture

The high-veld at dawn; Mpumalanga mist over standing stones; the heat and dark and pressure of a deep
mine; the dry-grass hugeness of the Zimbabwe plateau; dust, stone, gold-light. The land is a
character — and an **invitation** (the tourism mission lives in how lovingly place is rendered).

## Constraints the plot must respect

- **Real geography, honest distances.** Travel is real and costed; sites are where they really are.
- **Respect for the living.** Sacred sites are sacred in-world; local custodians are the experts.
- **Wonder, not conspiracy.** Institutional resistance exists but is *human and fallible*, never an
  omnipotent cabal. The villainy is greed (the collector), not a global cover-up.
- **The grounding rule.** No astonishing claim without a witnessable artifact behind it.
