# EPIGRAPHS — *History Before Time: The Calendar of Stone* (Book I)

> Same system as *Revelation*: a **book-level epigraph** (front of book) + **per-chapter** short
> multilingual quotes from "the old ones / the ancestors / the makers," original-language + English
> translation, each rhyming with its chapter's beat. Real/invented mix, lightly handled; never let an
> invented line pose as a checkable historical quote. Format slots between a chapter's `# Title` and
> its first prose line:
>
> ```
> > <original-script line(s)>
> >
> > *<English translation, italic>*
> >
> > — <attribution>
> ```

## BOOK-LEVEL EPIGRAPH (front of book — LOCKED)

> **Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu.**
>
> *A person is a person through other people.*
>
> — Zulu proverb (the principle of *ubuntu*)

**Why:** it is the philosophical parent of the book's *Sawubona* spine ("I see you" / "because you
see me, I am here") and the exact shape of Jennefer's arc — the woman told she came from *nobody* who
learns she is from *everybody,* made real by being seen. It also states the series pillar in five
words: we are each other's; the past, like the self, belongs to all of us. **It also appears once in
the prose** (ch-04, in the *Sawubona* conversation — Ubuntu is named as the bigger idea *Sawubona*
lives inside). Spelling is canon: *Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu* (do not "correct").
