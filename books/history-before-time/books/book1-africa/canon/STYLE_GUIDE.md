# STYLE_GUIDE — *History Before Time: The Calendar of Stone*

> Voice rules for the draft. Inherits the hard-won craft doctrine from the African Gold project
> (dramatize, don't declare; write 10–15% *less* declared meaning; vary cadence; kill machine-tells).

## Register
- Cinematic, propulsive, sensory **adventure-thriller** that loves landscape and stone. Dan Brown /
  James Rollins pace; Wilbur Smith's love of African place; early-Crichton artifact-credibility.
- **Close third, Jennefer only.** We are always behind her eyes; we know only what she knows. Her
  rigour, dry wit, and recovering-skeptic resistance colour the narration.
- Short, hard-pulling chapters. End on a turn, a question, or a door opening.

## The grounding rule (binding — Q7)
- **The artifact LEADS, the claim FOLLOWS.** Show the checkable physical thing first (a date, a
  tolerance, an acoustic reading, a GPR void); let the wonder land *after*. Never hand-wave.
- **Wonder, not conspiracy.** Awe and invitation, never paranoia/grievance/woo. Skeptics respected.
- **Anti-poison pillar:** African genius is the *source*; locals are the experts; the Order learns
  *from* them. Never "they couldn't have built it."

## Identity, carried right (binding — Q10/Q13)
- Jennefer's heritage, the misspelled name, the dop-stelsel, the languages: **strong undercurrent,
  never a lecture.** Felt in gesture, gut, and choice; a few earned beats; never misery-spectacle.
- Jakobus's *Sawubona* (mirroring as **respect, not technique**), his ND mind, his wound, his
  blending-in advantage: handle clear-eyed; reveal the wound slow, never a dump.

## Machine-tell taboos (from the de-LLM campaign — avoid)
- The **"Not X. Y." reframe** as a load-bearing crutch (e.g. "Not a question. A command."). Use at
  most rarely, never at every climax.
- Hollow **em-dash reframes**; triple-beat lists as default rhythm; "the way she X'd" anaphora.
- **Stating the theme aloud.** The theme ("the deep past belongs to everyone") is NEVER spoken by a
  character. It's the book's *effect*, carried by image and action.
- Over-explaining the marvel after it's shown (trust the reader; let the object do the work).
- Even, uniform register — **vary** sentence length and texture; let quiet beats breathe and set-
  pieces accelerate.

## Texture sources (use, lightly)
- SA detail done honestly (Cape Town cosmopolitan mishmash, Afrikaans/isiXhosa code-switching,
  the high-veld, Mpumalanga mist, deep-mine heat, the Zimbabwe plateau). "Bakkie" etc. as natural
  texture, never as a load-bearing implausibility.
- Jennefer's languages and Jakobus's land-reading are *tools that advance the plot*, not decoration.

## Length target
- A complete, readable draft: ~18–22 chapters, ~2,000–3,500 words each (≈ 55–75k). Lean and
  propulsive over padded. Word count is a guide, never a floor.
