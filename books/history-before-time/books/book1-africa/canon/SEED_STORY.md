## The one-line

Dr. Jennefer Abrahams — a disgraced Cape Town archaeologist, professionally buried for a date that
couldn't be real, who isn't even sure what she is — is found by a secret order who know she was right,
and, with **Jakobus Swart** — their charming, dangerous, freedom-haunted fixer — led site by site up
the spine of Africa toward proof that the continent's deep past is older, and greater, than the
textbooks allow.

## Title (locked) & provisional specifics

- **Title (LOCKED):** ***History Before Time: The Calendar of Stone*** (Book I). Anchors to Adam's
  Calendar (the signature real site) + deep time + points the reader somewhere to go.
- **Protagonist heritage (LOCKED):** **Mixed / "Coloured" South African** — a community with its own
  layered relationship to ancestry, belonging, and erasure (Khoisan, Cape Malay, African, European
  roots; a history of being told it has no single heritage to claim). This deepens her wound from
  *career* to *identity*: she isn't reclaiming one lineage, she's confronting the idea that she's owed
  none. (See CHARACTERS + THEMES.)
- **Protagonist (LOCKED — Q13a):** **Dr. Jennefer Abrahams** (may go by Jenna / Jen). "Jennefer" =
  *meant* to be Jennifer, **misspelled phonetically by her semi-literate father on her birth
  certificate** — a lifelong identity-marker (a name never quite the right shape). **Abrahams** = a
  real Cape-Coloured surname + a subtle nod to the cross-series Brotherhood of Abraham.
- **Heritage (LOCKED — Q10), privately uncertain:** Afrikaans / Khoi / Cape Malay / a bit of Xhosa by
  family lore — then a **genetic test reveals Italian** (the **Italian POWs who built Bain's Kloof
  Pass** and stayed post-WWII; the cigarettes-and-snacks-into-the-train-carriages kindness, now in her
  DNA). "Owed no single heritage" turns out to be the wrong question.
- **The gift (LOCKED):** a **knack for languages** (cosmopolitan Cape Town) — fluent Afrikaans,
  English, isiXhosa, Sesotho; gets by in Nguni; passable Swahili; learned word-roots from **shampoo-
  bottle ingredient lists**. Her superpower on the relay *and* the theme made flesh; ties to the
  studio's Swahili/a13n mission.
- **Age / stage:** early-to-mid 30s; brilliant, early-career-derailed rather than senior.

## Craft locks from the wizard (additional)

- **Grounding mechanism (LOCKED — Q7):** *show the artifact, let the data speak.* Every "the ancients
  did X" is **earned first** by a physical, checkable thing (a lab date, a tolerance, an acoustic
  reading, a GPR void). The artifact LEADS; the claim FOLLOWS. Jennefer's rigour is the reader's proxy.
- **Opening (LOCKED — Q8):** *open in the wound.* Page one is her public ruin (or its raw aftermath) —
  the hearing, the retraction, the result no one will publish — a tense, human, low-glamour scene that
  earns her **before** the Order and the wonder arrive.
- **The inciting find (LOCKED — Q11):** *an impossibly early date on worked stone.* She dated organic
  material sealed within/under a deliberately worked stone structure to **tens of thousands of years**
  — far before "agriculture/architecture" should exist (the Göbekli-Tepe gut-punch, on African soil).
  The "find" is a **date**, not a glamorous object — which is exactly why the establishment could bury
  it as contamination/error. Lab-checkable (serves Q7); points straight at Adam's Calendar; the
  Order's proof that it's real is the relay's first key.
- **Theme statement (LOCKED — Q12):** ***"The deep past belongs to everyone — the story of where we
  came from was never theirs to ration, and claiming it is how the disinherited come home."*** Never
  said aloud (THEMES rule); every scene serves it. Fuses her vindication + her Cape-Coloured belonging
  wound + the anti-poison pillar + the shared-wonder/tourism mission into one spine.

## The hook (act-one shape)

She is scraping a living on the margins of the field — her name a cautionary tale — when the order
approaches with a single artifact or reading that proves her ruined find was *real*, and the trail of
it points to **Adam's Calendar** and the deep-gold country. To follow it she must risk the one thing
she has left: the last shred of her credibility. She goes.

## The thread that binds the series (planted here, paid across the saga)

The **gold** — what the visitors came for — surfaces in Book I as the first link in a chain that runs
up Africa and across the later continents (Indus, Egypt, Antarctica, the Americas). Credo Mutwa
stands behind the order as its venerated prophet-figure; African oral memory is treated as a primary
source (the §2 anti-poison pillar made structural).

## The promise to the reader

Heart-in-throat adventure that loves the land; a brilliant African woman vindicated; and a closing
urge to **go and stand where it happened.** (The bucket-list back matter is the mechanism — see the
Series Bible §9 and §0.5.)
