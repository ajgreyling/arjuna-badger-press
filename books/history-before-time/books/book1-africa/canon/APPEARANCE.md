## Coherence flags

- **Ma Tshabalala — BUILD CONTRADICTION (prose-internal).** ch-04 introduces her as "built **small and straight**, and she wore her years like authority rather than weight." ch-17 describes her as "a **big woman, broad through the shoulders**, with hands that had done physical work every day of her life." Small-and-straight vs. big-and-broad is a direct physical contradiction in the same book. Recommend picking one (the ch-04 "small and straight" was the first-impression establishing beat; ch-17 "big woman, broad through the shoulders" reads as the more grounded later description). Flag for author resolution.

- **Jakobus — head-hair length not on the page (prose vs. SSOT, minor).** JAKOBUS_SWART.md specifies "dark hair cut short, greying at the temples." The prose confirms the salt-and-pepper **beard** ("kept just short of unruly," ch-03) and the **greying temples** (ch-20), but never states his head-hair length or that it is dark/cut short — it is only ever implied (a cap in ch-06). Not a contradiction; a gap. No prose contradicts the sheet on Jakobus's build, eyes (grey/green/blue shifting + light-sensitive), olive weathered skin, waistcoat, shades, blade kit, stones, or the uniform-rule subversion (ch-15) — all consistent with the SSOT. ✓

- **Jakobus eyes — assumed colour vs. revealed (intentional, not a drift).** ch-03 keeps the shades on and gives no eye colour; ch-04 reveals "a cool tired grey, grey going faintly green"; ch-16 gives the full shifting grey/green/blue. This is a deliberate staged reveal, not an inconsistency — noted so it isn't mistaken for drift.

- **Jennefer — total absence of hair/eye/build description (prose gap, not a contradiction).** The sole-POV lead is never physically described beyond age (34), heritage/complexion, and a "bad knee." No hair length/colour/style, no eye colour, no build/height anywhere in 20 chapters. This is internally consistent (nothing to contradict) but leaves illustrators/continuity with nothing to anchor — flag as a deliberate gap to fill in canon if a physical description is ever wanted. CHARACTERS.md likewise gives no hair/eyes.
