# EPIGRAPHS — *The Songlines of Stone* (standalone, Australia)

> Revelation-style: book-level epigraph + per-chapter (deferred to polish). **Binding care line
> (this book's spine):** sacred/secret knowledge (men's/women's business, restricted songlines,
> closed stories) is **off-limits** — the book walks the reader *up to* certain doors and not through
> them. **This applies doubly to the epigraphs:** use ONLY what is openly, publicly shareable — deep
> time, country, the *fact* of continuous memory — and **never invent, attribute, or quote a sacred
> or specific Aboriginal song/story.** Where an in-world "old voice" is used, it is the **author's
> own framing of an openly-acknowledged idea,** clearly not posed as a real restricted text.

## BOOK-LEVEL EPIGRAPH (front of book — LOCKED)

> *The country was here before the story, and the story has been here longer than anyone who would
> doubt it. Sixty-five thousand years is not a long time to the rock. It is only a long time to us.*
>
> — author's framing *(in-world; not a traditional text)*

> *They held the shape of a coast that drowned ten thousand years ago, and held it in true, and
> handed it forward, and handed it forward, and handed it forward — until a man with a machine that
> reads the seabed went looking, and found the old coast exactly where the keeping said it would be.*
>
> — author's framing *(in-world; gestures at the real, science-corroborated reality of deep oral
> memory — NOT a specific song or sacred account)*

**Why:** it states the book's spine — **the oldest continuous knowing on Earth, treated as a knowing**
— and the real, astonishing, *publicly documented* phenomenon the plot turns on (Aboriginal oral
traditions that preserve coastlines drowned at the end of the last Ice Age, since corroborated by
marine science). Crucially it is framed as the **author's own words about an openly-shareable idea,**
so nothing restricted is touched. **A dedication/acknowledgement to the Traditional Owners and a
"this book does not speak for, or disclose the knowledge of, any community" note belong in the front
matter** (and the closing *Real Places* gazetteer makes the real/invented line explicit).
