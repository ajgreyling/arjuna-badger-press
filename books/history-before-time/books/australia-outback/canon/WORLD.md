# WORLD — *The Songlines of Stone*

> Binding geography and the in-world "physics" of the wonder. **Real places used honestly** (MYTHOS_RULES
> Rule 6); the relay *is* the map — but the *specific* lease, company, route, and any sacred site are
> deliberately **composited and unmappable**. The land is the book's largest character.

## The shape of this world

Present-day Australia — **no fantastical layer.** The "wonder" is the *true* one: the **oldest continuous
living culture on Earth (65,000+ years)** and the staggering sophistication of its knowledge of country —
water, fire, sky, sea-memory, navigation, engineering — most of it hiding in plain sight as "landscape,"
"rock art," or "oral story," and most of it *checkable* once you know to look. The extraordinary is not
buried by aliens; it is **carried by the living descendants**, and the science keeps arriving late to what
they already knew. The threat is ordinary and real: **extraction** — iron ore, the blasting of country,
the box-ticking erasure of heritage — set against the deep time of the continent.

## The two time-layers (binding)

- **PRESENT DAY** — Nell's thread. The Pilbara lease, the relay down the songline, the fight for the rock-
  art country. The world of utes, drill rigs, native-title offices, Ranger stations, sat-phones, and heat.
- **THE SALTWATER DEEP (~12,000–10,000 years ago)** — the deep-thread interludes, on the **same country**
  when it was a coastal plain a day's walk from a sea now drowned. Post-glacial sea-level rise is the deep
  thread's engine: within a single lifetime the water comes up, the old freshwater springs and the hunting
  plains go under, and a family sings the lost places into memory so the children will know. **No
  fantastical element; just weather, water, kinship, and the act of making a song that will outlast the
  sea.** (Care line: the deep thread never depicts ceremony/sacred content — MYTHOS_RULES Rule 2.)

## The relay map (where the set-pieces live)

The quest runs **down a songline** — a deliberate inversion of the African book's *up the spine*: here the
relay follows **water and the rising-sea memory inland and across country**, from the Pilbara rock-art
coast toward the Centre, with two southern "proof" nodes that the custodians' network points Nell toward.
The route is **fictional/composited** (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 6) but every node is anchored to a **real
place-type** the reader can later visit honestly.

```
THE PILBARA LEASE / MURUJUGA-EDGE ROCK-ART COAST  (the job, the recruitment-by-humbling, the threat)
        │   (deep-thread rhyme: the family on the drowning coastal plain)
        ▼
THE DROWNED SPRING / OFFSHORE  (sea-level memory checks out on bathymetry/sonar — the first key)
        │
        ▼
THE SOAKAGE COUNTRY / WATER-FINDING LEG (the elders out-read the rig; "two ways of knowing water" ignites)
        │
        ▼
THE STONE ARRANGEMENT / SKY-CALENDAR NODE  (solstice alignment; the Emu in the Sky; reading the year)
        │
        ▼
THE ENGINEERED-WATER NODE (Budj-Bim-class hydrology: "hunter-gatherers" built this — anti-poison proof)
        │
        ▼
THE DEEP-ANTIQUITY NODE (Mungo/Madjedbebe-class dates + repatriation: who remembers, who is believed)
        │
        ▼
BACK TO THE LEASE — THE BLASTING CLOCK  (the climax: the survey that protects vs. the survey that clears)
```

### Node settings (each anchored to a real, honestly-renderable place-type)

- **The Pilbara rock-art coast (Murujuga-edge).** Anchored to the **real Murujuga / Burrup–Dampier
  petroglyph landscape** — the world's largest rock-art gallery (1M+ engravings, some estimated tens of
  thousands of years old, including extinct **thylacine/megafauna** and one of the oldest human-face
  images), red Pilbara rock, iron-ore country, **and the real, mournable fact that industry has already
  destroyed a portion of it.** In-world: the **edge** of such a field sits on Carrow's lease — public
  knowledge that it's significant, a company plan to blast anyway, and Nell sent to make the paperwork
  clean. **The book invents the specific lease; it points readers to the real, Aboriginal-guided Murujuga
  National Park tours.** This is the anchor node and the title's source (songlines + stone).
- **The drowned spring / offshore node.** Anchored to the **real, documented sea-level oral traditions**
  (Nunn & Reid, 2015, *Australian Geographer*: 21 stories around the coast, datable to **7,000–13,000 years
  ago** by coastal-contour reconstruction). In-world: a song names a freshwater spring/island now under the
  sea; Nell cross-checks it against bathymetry and a post-glacial sea-level curve, then *confirms it on
  sonar* — **oral tradition as datable observation.** The first artifact that converts her. (Deep-thread
  rhyme: we have *seen* that spring on dry land, and seen the family name it as the water rose.)
- **The soakage / water-finding leg.** Anchored to **real Western/Central Desert water knowledge**:
  **gnammas** (rock-holes), **soakages** (sand wells), **mound springs**, **mallee-root water**, the
  **water-holding frog**, and the trained reading of slope, sheen, vegetation, ant-lines, bird-flight, and
  fig-root to water. In-world: the company's drill grid fails; the elders read the country to water no
  instrument found. The author's "tattoo to find water" is rendered as the **trained hand and eye and the
  song that names the water** — knowledge carried in body and verse, **not a magic mark** (MYTHOS_RULES
  Rule 7). The honest, astonishing truth beats the gimmick.
- **The stone arrangement / sky-calendar node.** Anchored to **real Aboriginal astronomy**: a
  **Wurdi-Youang-class** stone arrangement whose sightlines mark **solstice and equinox sunset**; the
  **Emu in the Sky** (the dark dust-lanes of the Milky Way) read as a seasonal calendar that *predicts the
  country's behaviour* (when the emus lay, when to burn, when the water comes). In-world: Nell surveys the
  alignment to the degree and watches the prediction come true — the sky as **applied science**, the
  world's oldest, still working.
- **The engineered-water node.** Anchored to **Budj Bim** (real: **Gunditjmara eel-trap aquaculture**,
  6,600+ years, first site **World-Heritage-listed for cultural significance alone**, 2019). In-world: a
  channel-and-weir hydrology in rock that Nell, the hydrogeologist, re-derives and finds *flawless* — the
  unkillable refutation of "they were only hunter-gatherers." (Used as a **proof node**, geography honest;
  the real Budj Bim is named in the gazetteer as visitable via Gunditjmara-run tours.)
- **The deep-antiquity node.** Anchored to **Lake Mungo / Willandra** (real: **Mungo Man ~62,000 yrs**,
  **Mungo Lady ~40–42,000 yrs**; **earliest known cremation and ochre burial on Earth**; ancestors
  **repatriated and reinterred by their descendants**, Mungo Man 2017) and **Madjedbebe** (~65,000-yr
  occupation). In-world: the date that ends every "couldn't have" argument, and the **repatriation** that
  makes the moral point bodily — *who remembers, and who is believed.* (Geography honest; the real Mungo is
  visitable via the park connected to the **Mutthi Mutthi / Barkandji / Ngiyampaa** Traditional Owners.)

## The in-world "physics" of the wonder (what makes it credible, not silly — and never woo)

All effects are **physical and witnessable** (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 1): **hydrology** (aquifers, soakage
recharge, weir flow), **bathymetry and post-glacial sea-level curves**, **astronomical alignment and
heliacal risings**, **rock-art dating** (mineral crust, superimposition, depicted extinct species),
**OSL/radiocarbon** dates, **acoustic/echo** in a gorge. The world **shows the artifact, then lets the awe
land.** The deepest mechanisms — *how* a 12,000-year observation stayed word-perfect; the full extent of
the songline net; the deepest age of first arrival — remain **designated wonder-mysteries** (named, never
solved). The **sacred content** is a permanent **care-line mystery** (never depicted at all — Rule 2).

## The protagonist's expertise as a relay tool (Nell's gift, used honestly)

Nell's **hydrogeology** is **not decoration — it's how she advances nodes** the company men can't, and the
ground on which she meets the elders as a *peer who is humbled*:
- **Reading water by instrument** (resistivity, the drill log, the sat-map of recharge) — which the country
  repeatedly **outsmarts**, forcing her to learn the elders' reading.
- **The convergence** (Rule 7): her instruments and the songline locate the **same water**, including the
  *drowned* freshwater of the old coast — the proof that the culture got there first and was right.
- **Testimony as the final tool:** her professional credibility — the very thing she was hired to spend on
  the company's behalf — becomes, at the climax, the instrument that can **protect** the country in the
  heritage/legal arena. She advances the last node not with a drill but with a **signature.**
- **Language / protocol literacy (acquired, humbly):** she does *not* arrive speaking Language; she learns
  the **protocols** — when to wait at the vehicle, not to photograph, not to ask, to be quiet — and that
  learned restraint is itself a tool that earns the custodians' trust (and keeps the care line intact).

## Sensory texture (the land as character — the "Wilbur Smith of Australia" register)

The Pilbara at first light, red rock going to furnace by ten; the iron tang of haematite dust and diesel;
spinifex hiss and the impossible blue; the silence so large it has a sound; the night sky **un-citied** —
the Milky Way thick enough to read, the **dark Emu** lying along it. The drowned coast's green water and
the ghost of a plain beneath it. The Centre's mulga, mound-spring reed-green against red, the soakage's
first cup of cold water from white sand. Salt, ochre, smoke (a cool-season burn done right, the country
*managed* not wild). Heat, distance, water-as-life. The land is an **invitation** (the tourism mission
lives in how lovingly the public places are rendered) — and, at the sacred thresholds, a **withheld
weight** the reader feels and respects.

## Constraints the plot must respect

- **Real geography, honest distances, real heat and water.** Travel in the Pilbara/Centre is real and
  costed; water is life and is never trivial; the blasting clock and the legal calendar are real mechanisms.
- **Real places real; sacred places fictional/withheld.** No scene functions as a map to a real protected
  or sacred site (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 6); the gazetteer separates real-and-visitable from invented from
  withheld-by-design.
- **Respect for the living, absolute.** Custodians are the experts and the authorities; sacred knowledge is
  off-limits content (Rule 2); the anti-poison pillar holds in every beat (Rule 5).
- **Wonder, not conspiracy.** The opposition is human and fallible — a balance sheet, a regulator, an
  ambitious man — never an omnipotent cabal (Rule 4).
- **The grounding rule.** No astonishing claim without a witnessable, checkable artifact in front of it
  (Rule 1).
