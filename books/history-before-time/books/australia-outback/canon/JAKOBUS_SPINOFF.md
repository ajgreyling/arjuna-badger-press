## 1. How the Order enters (the mechanism)

**Pulled in by a checkable anomaly, not a treasure.** Nell's heritage survey produces a result she
can't explain and won't bury: a songline verse, sung by Nana May, **names a freshwater spring now
drowned ~30 m under the sea** — and Nell's own bathymetry confirms a spring sits *exactly* there,
where the coast stood at the last glacial low. Deep memory, verified by instruments. Her honest
filing crosses the threshold the Order watches: **a site where an impossibly-old way of knowing tests
TRUE.** The Order wants the *songline-as-data* witnessed and mapped onto the **Sahul** keystone
(Australia–PNG–Tasmania as one drowned ice-age continent), and the gallery kept safe before the mine
erases the evidence. Vow unchanged: *we do not conquer the past; we remember it and keep it safe.*

**Jakobus is sent — explicitly OFF home ground.** The Order's African water-and-desert man is
dispatched to a desert that is **not his.** That dissonance is the point of sending *him*: the
humility is the lesson the Order itself is learning — it must defer to local keepers, not master the
place.

**The "someone" sent with him:** a **Torres Strait Islander keeper** — a Tagai navigator / sea-Country
knowledge-holder (working name **Esme Pau**, Mer lineage; provisional, set on cultural advice). She is
the Order's *correct* placement here: standing that Jakobus and Nell will never have, kin-protocol
access to Daniel and Nana May, and she extends the Order's reach to the **sea** half of Sahul. A fellow
expert, never a sidekick.

**Nell stays the lead (the REVELATION model holds).** Nell breaks the case (the science that proves
the site engineered/ancient/real); **Daniel and Nana May own the knowledge;** Jakobus **is the road** —
the field-protector who keeps the party alive and stands between the keepers and the camp's muscle.
**POV stays Nell-primary, single close third** (canon-locked). He does not become the lead; he becomes
the spine that carries them.

## 3. Jakobus in a desert not his (the humility engine — dramatise, never narrate)

Four wrongnesses + one transferable competence:
- **Water (the deepest wrong).** His Kalahari instinct — find the dry course, dig the outside bend —
  is *right in Africa and nearly kills them here.* Pilbara water hides in rock (**gnamma** holes under
  a slab he'd never think to lift), and **named waters are responsible** — kept secret as Law. The man
  who reads water like scripture must **ask permission to drink.**
- **Fauna.** His threat-grammar is calibrated for *charisma* (buffalo, lion, weight-and-eyes). The
  Pilbara kills small and from below — a mulga snake in a bootprint. He learns to walk **looking
  down** — a daily humiliation for a horizon-reader, accepted.
- **Navigation.** No brute-force dead-reckoning. Country is navigated by *song*, in sequence. He must
  admit a route exists he **cannot perceive, only follow, and only if invited.**
- **Plants / heat.** His pharmacopeia is foreign; his pacing too generous for a drier, older, more
  total heat. Nana May corrects him without a word. *(Ties to the locked heat-inversion: he owns cold,
  is beaten by real heat — and this is the hottest, most foreign furnace he's ever stood in.)*
- **Where he still helps (transferable):** he reads **people** better than anyone (tastes the drugged
  salt as *wrong* before he reasons it; clocks the chit-economy's tells; sorts captor from broken);
  runs and keeps the Beast alive; his survival *discipline* (calm under load, water-as-principle, long
  patience) ports cleanly. And **"Sawubona / I see you"** is revealed as the one skill that was always
  the whole skill — **deference as his deepest competence, not weakness.**

## 3a. Stoffel, the cayenne tin, and the kit that fails (the humility engine, made concrete)

*(Full law on the series sheet — `canon/characters/JAKOBUS_SWART.md`, "Stoffel the honey badger" + "The
field-kit." Here: how each lands in THIS book. POV stays Nell; she watches a competent man meet a land
that doesn't take his competence — and watches the few things that still work.)*

- **Stoffel is the LEGEND he carries, not an animal on the page (Africa-vs-Australia rule, LOCKED).**
  The honey badger stays in Africa; here Stoffel is the **story Jakobus tells** and the **resistance he
  half-trusts.** Two load-bearing uses:
  - **The venom gamble (the spin-off in one beat).** Stoffel survives African elapid venom (a real
    receptor mutation — see series sheet). Australia's killers (mulga, brown, taipan, death adder) are
    **also elapids** — so the African rule *probably* carries, **but was never built or tested for this
    country.** When the snake beat comes (it sharpens §3's "Fauna" wrongness), the unspoken question is
    Stoffel's whole meaning turned on the man: *does what kept you alive at home keep you alive here —
    or is "probably" a thin margin to bet a life on?* Nell, the scientist, may half-know the biology;
    let the **uncertainty** carry it, never a lecture. Keep the receptor detail off-page.
  - **The carried-badger parable, lived (LOCKED — the Dirkie-Uys image).** The badger that follows a
    man past all sense until, worn out, the man *carries it* — relentlessness met with respect, the
    strong one who carries. In the Outback **Jakobus lives it from the inside**: on the forced
    flight/walkabout-mirror he is the one near the end of himself in a country that is not his, and the
    grace he's always *given* he must now *receive* — carried, guided, sung the way out by people placed
    to do it. Plant the badger story early (his, lightly); pay it off without ever naming the parallel.
- **The cayenne tin — the competence that DOES port (LOCKED).** His leaf-lore is useless here (§3,
  "Plants"), but the **tin of ground cayenne** travels and works anywhere: the disarming **wimpy-eggs**
  quirk early; the **field coagulant** packed into a real wound later (a beat apart, so the quirk
  re-reads). One of the small, true things that crosses every border — including this one.
- **The hanky + the Beast's hessian water bag (LOCKED).** The always-clean **handkerchief** (dressing /
  filter / wet-wrapped evaporative cooling) and the Cruiser's **weeping hessian water bag** (looks
  broken, pours the coldest water in camp) are his **portable cooling-and-care doctrine** — and they
  *do* work in the Pilbara furnace, a quiet counterpoint to everything that doesn't. The hessian bag is
  the African-desert cousin of the gnamma/zeer logic: **cold earned by letting a little go.** Good
  recurring visuals; strangers eye the dripping bag like neglect, then drink the coldest water there.
- **Exposure, not sterility — the floor under his ease (LOCKED).** Why he'll drink from the communal
  cup and eat what's offered while the wary go thirsty: his gut and nerve are built by **exposure**, not
  caution (series sheet). In a camp that controls people through the **salt pot** and the **store**, his
  instinct to *share food and trust the offered cup* is both characterisation and a quiet act of
  resistance — the body version of *Sawubona.* (He still tastes the drugged salt **wrong first** — §3 —
  because trust is not the same as being fooled.)

> **Drafting guard for all of the above:** these are *Nell watching*, not Jakobus narrating. Each is a
> SHOWN habit that does double work — quirk + competence, gear + theme. Never a survival-manual aside,
> never the parallel spelled out. The constant is the inversion: **his African mastery is total and
> nearly useless here; the few small true things travel; the legend goes where the man goes.**

## 4. The commune thriller (antagonist spine, integrated)

**Uhuru Station** — a defunct pastoral lease sold as an off-grid "wellness/rewilding retreat,"
marketing *uhuru* (freedom) to the burnt-out and broke. Actually a **labour camp**: the off-books
workforce that does what a licensed crew legally can't — clearing tracks across heritage Country and,
the real job, **pre-blast "salvage" stripping of the galleries before the heritage determination
lands.** Uhuru is the mine's **deniable hand.**

Control architecture (grounded, no torture-porn):
- **The salt pot.** Communal meals are the only food; the salt is dosed with a sedative-hypnotic —
  foggy evenings, easy compliance. **Jakobus tastes it wrong first.**
- **The chits.** No wages — **carnival-ride stubs and laminated chits**, redeemable only at the camp
  store. This **rhymes with the series' control-of-provenance villain doctrine** (the diamond/De Beers
  con): make a thing artificially scarce, control who may touch it. The mine scrips provenance of
  *Country*; Uhuru scrips provenance of *bread.*
- **Distance as the wall.** Walk out unprepared and you die — the captors' whole bet, and the trap the
  Order man inverts.
- **The kangaroo-ears beat (LOCKED — the visceral keystone).** A drug-dulled, protein-starved resident
  drags a days-rotten roadkill 'roo to butcher; **the ears come off in her hands.** The moment the
  reader understands these people are being killed by *neglect*, not melodrama. Visceral, grounded,
  never gore-for-thrill.

**Tie to mine + Order:** the determination is about to rule the gallery un-blastable (Nell's
hydrogeology + the song-checked drowned spring prove it real); the mine needs the art *gone* first. The
**walkabout crosses Uhuru's perimeter** — and the camp is broken not by the outsider's force but by the
**locals' knowledge**: a freed resident is guided out by reading the land the captors thought was a wall.

## 5. The walkabout + the knife

**Walkabout as expertise, never soul-quest.** A structured journey along Dreaming tracks — law,
navigation, obligation at once, each verse naming a soak/ridge/food in sequence (the oldest GPS on
Earth). Plot uses:
- **Daniel is mid-initiation** — a lawful obligation to walk his grandfather's songline to "sing up"
  the threatened gallery before the blast. That's *why* he's away from camp — the structural reason
  Nell is left with only the song to read the land.
- **The Uhuru escapees** can only cross to help via the songline's water sequence; **Nana May "sings
  the way out."** Wrong verse, missed soak, death.
- **Jakobus & Nell's forced flight MIRRORS a walkabout without claiming to BE one.** Jakobus
  (neurodiverse) locks the verse-sequence as **pattern** (his gift) but is explicit it is *borrowed,
  sacred, not his to own.* Nell confirms each named water is *really there* — song checked against
  science. Wonder, grounded.
- **Climax:** the walkabout's endpoint **IS the gallery** the mine wants to blast — arriving completes
  Daniel's law AND saves the art. The Order's vow bows to keepers who never needed saving, only
  witnessing.

**The big blade — survival, not display (LOCKED).** In Egypt the kukri was craft kit, given away; in
Australia a **different large fixed-blade** is the thin margin between living and dying. When the Beast
is lost and they flee on foot into 45°C, the knife is **water from roots, butchering, shelter,
signalling, last-ditch defence** — never theatre. *"Where I'm from it's craft. Here it's the thin
margin."* But — the line that keeps it humble — **the knife doesn't know where the water is. That you
learn by walking it, in order, with someone placed to take you.**

## 6. Guardrails (HARD lines — gate-enforceable)

> **See also the WHITE-SAVIOUR FIREWALL** in `canon/characters/JAKOBUS_SWART.md` (the 7-point binding
> checklist: locals lead/own the win; he's useless on their ground; the Order witnesses-not-claims;
> he's not here to save anyone; no deficit/noble-savage framing; sensitivity read is a floor; when in
> doubt the agency goes to the local character). This book is the **highest-risk** in the series for
> it (Aboriginal + Torres Strait Islander material), so the firewall is doubly binding here.

1. **Never depict secret/sacred knowledge.** Characters *withhold* — "that's not mine to tell,"
   "you're not placed to hear that." Withholding is dignity, not a tease. **No fabricated real songs,
   sacred designs, or ceremony** — invent fictional Country/people; never script an actual sacred verse.
2. **Never conflate peoples.** Aboriginal nations (250+, name a specific *fictional* people), **Torres
   Strait Islanders (Melanesian, sea-Country)**, and **PNG (sovereign, 800+ languages, Kuk independent
   agriculture)** are distinct — say all three; never homogenise.
3. **No deficit framing, no white-savior, no trauma-spectacle.** Nell *learns from* the experts and
   never rescues the culture; locals lead, decide, own the win. The kangaroo-ears beat is neglect made
   visible, not gore.
4. **Avoid naming/depicting the recently dead** (cultural protocol).
5. **No lost-white-race / aliens-built-it.** The achievement is *theirs*; the Order witnesses, never
   claims. The Dreaming is a rigorous, **checkable** knowledge system, not mysticism.
6. **Sahul / Sea-Country is the deep-time hinge:** the drowned Australia–PNG land bridge is the *same
   physics* as Nell's drowned spring — extend the Crichton spine to the sea, with Esme the correct keeper.
7. **Sensitivity read is a FLOOR, not a nicety:** Aboriginal + Torres Strait Islander cultural
   consultants engaged before publication. All people/place names above are **provisional**, set on advice.
