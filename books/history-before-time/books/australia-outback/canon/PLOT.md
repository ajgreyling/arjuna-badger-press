# PLOT — *The Songlines of Stone* — relay skeleton

> The **quest-relay spine.** Structure is binding; texture is flexible. Each node:
> **ARRIVE → OBSTACLE → READ → COST → KEY → HANDOFF.** Every present-day chapter advances, plants, or pays
> off a node. The **Saltwater deep-thread interludes** are placed to *rhyme* with the node they illuminate
> (marked ❖). This is a *skeleton*, not a beat sheet — beats expand during outlining. Sites are anchored to
> real place-types but the specific route is **fictional/composited and unmappable** (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 6).
> **The care line (Rule 2) governs every node: wonder and plot advance through the *public* artifact;
> sacred content is honoured and never depicted.**

---

## Act I — The job, the humbling, and the first impossible thing

**Node 0 — THE PILBARA LEASE · the ruin, the contract, and the threat** *(open here)*
- **OPENING (LOCKED — open IN THE WOUND, then on COUNTRY).** Page one is Nell at her lowest: the
  tailings-dam ruin still on her — broke, scapegoated, professionally radioactive — taking the only
  contract anyone will give a pariah: a **cultural-heritage and water survey** for **Carrow Resources**,
  which everyone understands is meant to come back clean and clear the ground. Tense, human, low-glamour;
  earns the reader's investment in her *before* any wonder. **Then** the second note: she arrives on the
  lease and the **country hits her** — the red rock, the scale, the silence with a sound — and, against her
  will, her **palm finds the ground** (her mother's gesture; she doesn't yet know it). Voice and stakes and
  place first; marvel later.
- **Arrive:** Nell on the lease; the company men; the rig; the brief to box-tick. The edge of a vast
  **petroglyph field** (Murujuga-class) on the lease — public knowledge it's significant, a plan to blast
  anyway.
- **Obstacle:** she's the enemy and she knows it — sent to manage the very custodians (Daniel, Nana May,
  the mob) whose country she's there to help clear; they owe her nothing, tell her nothing, and watch.
- **Read (Nell):** she still reads water better than anyone — and she clocks, uneasily, that the company's
  certainty about "empty country" is the same certainty that overruled her about the dam.
- **The find / first key (LOCKED):** her own bores come up **dry** where her maps say water; her
  instruments *fail* — the first crack in her empiricist armour, and the thing that forces her to
  **listen.**
- **Cost:** to do the job at all she must ask the custodians for help she has no right to expect —
  swallowing pride, and risking the last of her credibility on people the company wants her to overrule.
- **Key-out:** an invitation (grudging, on **their** terms) to be shown *one* thing — and a bearing toward
  water her grid missed.
- **Handoff:** she goes with them. **(End Act I.)**
- ❖ **Deep-thread interlude 1 (Waraki):** the coastal family on a **green plain by the sea** — abundance,
  kin, the freshwater spring at the heart of their country. The first, golden note of the world that is
  about to drown. (Rhymes with Nell standing on the same country, now arid, instruments dead.)

## Act II — The relay down the songline (the conversion)

**Node 1 — THE DROWNED SPRING / OFFSHORE · oral tradition checks out** *(the conversion node)*
- **Arrive:** the custodians take Nell to the coast; a **story/song names a freshwater spring now under the
  sea.** She's skeptical — *a story.*
- **Obstacle (puzzle + her own resistance):** the claim is "impossible" by her training — until she does
  the one thing she trusts: **she measures.** Bathymetry, a post-glacial sea-level curve, then a boat and
  **sonar.**
- **Read (Nell):** the sonar returns a spring/feature **exactly where the song says, at exactly the depth
  the sea-level curve predicts for ~10,000+ years ago** (the Nunn–Reid result, dramatised). Oral tradition
  as **datable observation.** Her armour breaks — the recovering skeptic's **first earned wonder.**
- **Cost:** she can no longer pretend the country is "empty" or the custodians "primitive" — which means she
  can no longer, in conscience, write the clean report Carrow is paying for. The job and her livelihood
  start to pull against the truth.
- **Key-out:** the realisation that the **songline is a navigable record** — and that it runs *inland*,
  node to node, water to water.
- **Handoff:** the bearing forces the soakage country.
- ❖ **Deep-thread interlude 2 (Waraki):** the **sea rising** within Waraki's lifetime — the spring going
  brackish, the plain shrinking, the family's dawning grief. (Rhymes directly with the drowned-spring
  sonar.)

**Node 2 — THE SOAKAGE COUNTRY · two ways of knowing water become one** *(the anti-poison node)*
- **Arrive:** dry country; the company's drill grid has **failed**; Nell is out of instrument answers.
- **Obstacle:** survival-stakes — they need water, and the maps are useless.
- **Read (Nell, humbled):** **Nana May reads the country** — slope, sheen, ant-lines, fig-root,
  bird-flight, the trained hand — and **walks them to a soakage** (sand well / gnamma) that no instrument
  found. The author's "water-finding" fascination rendered **honestly**: knowledge in body and song, **not
  a magic mark** (MYTHOS_RULES Rule 7). Nell — *who has never been wrong about water* — was wrong, and the
  elder was right, **first, for 65,000 years.** The anti-poison pillar, dramatised, never declared.
- **Cost:** the gift comes with a **line**: there are places on this leg Nell **may not go / photograph /
  ask about** ("this is women's country," "wait here"). She must honour the care line **without
  entitlement** — and the cost is real: it means the easy, complete "evidence" she'd want for a legal fight
  is partly **withheld by right**, and she must accept that. (The care line as *plot cost*, not exposition.)
- **Key-out:** trust earned; a bearing to the **stone arrangement** up-country.
- **Handoff:** the songline points to the sky-calendar node.

**Node 3 — THE STONE ARRANGEMENT / SKY-CALENDAR · reading the year** *(the wonder node)*
- **Arrive:** a **Wurdi-Youang-class stone arrangement** on high country; the wide un-citied night sky.
- **Obstacle (physical + puzzle):** the site only "reads" as an **instrument** — solstice/equinox
  sightlines, the **Emu in the Sky** in the Milky Way's dark lanes as a **seasonal calendar** — not as
  decoration. (Reached by *measurement and listening*, never authority — Rule 1.)
- **Read (Nell + Daniel):** Nell **surveys the alignment to within a degree**; the elders read the Emu and
  **predict the country's behaviour** (the burn, the laying, the coming water) — and are **proven right.**
  The sky as **applied science**, the world's oldest, still working. Her wonder deepens into respect.
- **Cost:** the **rival surfaces** — Carrow learns Nell has "gone native," and the **PR/legal smear
  begins** (her ruined credibility weaponised); and the **community-division** strand bites (some of the
  mob want the jobs/royalties and resent the fight). The cost is human and legible, never a cabal.
- **Key-out:** Nell grasps that the songline's nodes are **one continuous body of evidence** — and that the
  oldest, hardest proof lies at the deep-antiquity and engineered-water nodes.
- **Handoff:** the trail forces the engineered-water node.
- ❖ **Deep-thread interlude 3 (Waraki):** the family **deciding to move inland** — and the elder insisting
  the lost places be **sung, not just remembered**, so the children will always know. The **making of the
  song.** (Rhymes with the present-thread realisation that the songline *is* the record — and seeds the
  climax.)

**Node 4 — THE ENGINEERED-WATER NODE · "hunter-gatherers" built this** *(anti-poison proof)*
- **Arrive:** a **Budj-Bim-class** channel-and-weir hydrology in rock.
- **Obstacle:** to read it, Nell must apply her *own* discipline against the deepest "couldn't have"
  assumption in her field.
- **Read (Nell, hydrogeologist):** she **re-derives the hydrology and finds it flawless** — deliberate,
  engineered, millennia old. The **unkillable refutation** of "they were only hunter-gatherers." (Real Budj
  Bim: 6,600+ yrs, World-Heritage-listed for cultural significance alone.) She is now *certain*, on her own
  empirical terms, and her conversion is **complete** — measuring has become listening, and listening has
  become *advocacy.*
- **Cost:** certainty makes her **dangerous to Carrow** — and the company escalates: a move to **strip the
  heritage protection / fast-track the approval / split the community with royalty money.** The **blasting
  clock starts.**
- **Key-out:** Nell resolves to gather the one form of evidence that can stop a blast in the legal arena —
  which requires the **deep-antiquity node.**
- **Handoff:** to the deep-antiquity node, under time pressure.

**Node 5 — THE DEEP-ANTIQUITY NODE · who remembers, and who is believed** *(the moral fulcrum)*
- **Arrive:** a **Mungo/Madjedbebe-class** site — dates, burial/cremation evidence, the deep antiquity of
  occupation; and the living fact of **repatriation** (ancestors returned from museum drawers and
  reinterred by their descendants).
- **Obstacle:** the **clock** (Carrow's approval/blast) and the **care line** collide — the strongest legal
  evidence might require touching what is **not for telling**, and Nana May/Daniel **draw the line.** Nell
  must win the fight **without trespassing** — find a way to protect the country that does not betray the
  very thing she's protecting.
- **Read (Nell + custodians):** the **OSL/radiocarbon dates** and the **drowned-spring + songline record**
  converge into a body of *public* evidence overwhelming enough to matter — **without** any sacred content.
  And the **repatriation** lands the moral point bodily: this is not "ruins," these are **ancestors**, and
  the descendants are **here, and remember.**
- **Cost:** the deepest cost of the book — Nell is offered a **devil's bargain** (Carrow: drop the fight,
  your name cleared, your debts gone, one clean report and you're whole again) at the exact moment the
  community is **divided** and the lawful path looks like losing. She must choose her *old* want
  (vindication, money, being whole) or her *new* one (the country, the people, right relationship).
- **Key-out:** she **chooses the country** — and commits to **testify**, spending her credibility instead
  of selling it.
- **Handoff:** back to the lease, to the climax and the blast.
- ❖ **Deep-thread interlude 4 (Waraki) — the braid closes:** Waraki, now older, **sings the finished song**
  of the drowned spring to the next child — the knowledge entrusted forward. **The same
  flat-palm-to-the-ground gesture** we've seen in Nell and her mother. (Rhymes with the climax: the song
  Waraki made 400 generations ago is the evidence Nell is about to stand on. Never spelled out; felt.)

## Act III — The reckoning and the choice (personal arc + plot resolve in ONE beat)

**Climax — the survey that protects vs. the survey that clears**
- **The arena (binding — fused beat):** the resolution is **not** a gunfight; it is the **heritage / legal
  / public reckoning** where the blast is decided — and Nell's **personal arc and the plot resolve in the
  same action.** She stands up and **testifies to what she now knows**, presenting the *public* convergence
  (drowned spring on sonar matching a 10,000-year-old song; the solstice alignment; the engineered
  hydrology; the deep dates), **on the custodians' terms and within the care line** — *the woman who
  measures, finally listening, and using the very credibility Carrow tried to destroy to protect the
  country.* The personal (measuring → listening; shame → right relationship) and the plot (extraction vs.
  custodianship) land in **one beat.**
- **The custodians lead it, not Nell (binding — anti-poison pillar at the climax):** the decisive authority
  is **Daniel and Nana May and the mob** — Nell's testimony is the *outsider's corroboration that finally
  gets the room to listen*, **not** the white expert who saves the day. The win belongs to the custodians;
  Nell's role is to have *earned the right to stand beside them.* (No saviour. The country is protected
  because its people were right, and were finally believed.)
- **Carrow's true villainy:** his **override** — the one moment he *sees* the drowned-spring proof, half-
  understands what he's about to silence, and **chooses the share price anyway.** Greed as a failure of
  listening — the inverse of Nell's arc.
- **Cost & grounding:** a **real price** — the win is **partial and hard** (a stay, a re-listing, country
  protected *this time*; not every threat ended forever; the community-division leaves scars; perhaps a
  piece of country, or Nell's old life, is not recoverable). The **deepest mechanisms stay designated
  mysteries** (Rule 3) and the **sacred stays unseen** (Rule 2) — she wins enough to **protect the place**,
  not to "solve" or expose it.

**Resolution — belonging by listening; the door open to country**
- The blast is stopped (this time); the rock-art country stands; the **repatriation/heritage** thread
  closes with the ancestors and the country honoured. Carrow is checked, **not cartoonishly destroyed** (a
  balance sheet takes a hit, a project stalls — human stakes, human outcome).
- **Nell is changed:** not vindicated-for-herself in the way she came starving for, but **remade** — a
  taught outsider finally trusted to **stand rightly on country as a guest.** Her last gesture is her
  mother's — **palm flat on the ground** — and this time she **understands it**, and is at peace with it
  (homecoming to *humility and right relationship*, **never** a claimed identity — see CHARACTERS
  guardrail). Quiet, earned, never a speech.
- **The door open (own-series potential):** the songline runs on, beyond this country; the custodians'
  network and the deep record stretch across the continent. **Standalone ending *and* an open door** — a
  next country, a next threat, a next outsider taught to listen.

## Subplots (each ties back to the theme statement)

1. **The community division (jobs/royalties vs. country/law) — major.** Not everyone in the mob agrees;
   some want the work and resent the fight. **The book's truest, least-villainous conflict** — honours the
   reality and refuses a monolith (CHARACTERS). Resolved not by Nell but *within* the community, on their
   terms.
2. **Nell's tailings-dam past / vindication-she-came-for.** The old want (clear her name, win back the
   doubters) is **set up to be refused** — she learns she no longer needs the crowd's permission. The
   regulator/colleague from her old life is the face of it.
3. **Nell ↔ her mother / the gesture.** The inheritance-of-listening thread, carried **in the body,
   surfaced in a few quiet beats**, paid off at the climax and resolution. **Handled with maximum restraint
   and on cultural advice; never a "secretly Aboriginal" reveal** (binding — CHARACTERS guardrail).
4. **Nell ↔ Daniel — respect/alliance → friendship (NOT romance).** Dramatises outsider-earns-trust and
   the anti-poison pillar; **no saviour in either direction** (relationship rule, CHARACTERS).
5. **The watched drink.** A light, controlled goldfields-inheritance undercurrent for Nell — never
   misery-spectacle; optional (author's call).

## The deep-thread braid (binding — the three-scene thesis, never declared)

The whole book's argument is carried by **one image across deep time**, in (at least) three placed scenes,
**never explained:**
1. **Deep thread:** Waraki / the ancestral family **name the freshwater spring into song** as the sea
   rises (interludes 1–4).
2. **Present:** **Nana May sings / holds** that same country and its water in living memory (Nodes 1–2).
3. **Present:** **Nell's sonar pings the drowned spring** exactly where the song says (Node 1; paid off at
   the climax as evidence).
Plus the **flat-palm-to-the-ground gesture** linking Waraki → Nell's mother → Nell. The structure *is* the
theme: the oldest continuous culture on Earth, remembered forward across 400 generations, and right.

## Back matter (binding — the mission's payoff and the respect spine)

A closing **author's note + gazetteer**, three required parts:

1. **"The Real Places in This Book" (the tourism mission).** Separate **real & visitable** (Murujuga
   National Park via the **Aboriginal-owned/guided tours**; **Lake Mungo / Willandra** via the park
   connected to its Traditional Owners; **Budj Bim** via **Gunditjmara-run** tours; **Ubirr / Kakadu**; the
   **documented sea-level oral traditions** and the Nunn–Reid study; **Wurdi Youang** and Aboriginal
   astronomy resources) — from **debated** — from **invented / composited** (Carrow Resources, the specific
   lease, all named characters, the **specific songline route and any sacred site — fictional and
   deliberately not mappable**). State plainly **which sacred locations are withheld by design**, and why.
2. **The binding sensitivity-reader / consult-the-real-custodians note (NON-NEGOTIABLE).** State, in the
   author's own voice, that: this is a **work of fiction** that **does not depict any secret or sacred
   knowledge** (men's/women's business is honoured, never revealed — MYTHOS_RULES Rule 2); that the
   manuscript **was / must be read and guided by Aboriginal cultural advisors and sensitivity readers
   before publication**; that it follows **ICIP** and **free, prior and informed consent** principles; that
   the characters are **fictional and not spokespeople** for any real community; and that **authority over
   Aboriginal knowledge and country belongs to Aboriginal people.** (This note is **load-bearing** and a
   genuine pre-publication step, not boilerplate.)
3. **"Go, listen, and book the right tour."** Point readers toward **Aboriginal-owned tourism, Ranger
   programs, land councils, and reputable organisations** — the right way to encounter this country — and a
   short, plain word on **why some doors stay closed, and why that is right.** (The tourism-dignity theme's
   payoff; never preached in the novel, made explicit only here.)
