## Priya Ellis (the lead; "engineer", "the Indian one")
- **Role:** POV (sole close-third) / lead
- **Build & height:** No height or frame is given anywhere in the prose. She is physically undescribed as a body — the book renders her entirely through cognition, voice, and the right-hand scar. (all chapters)
- **Hair:** Never described — no length, colour, or style stated in any chapter. (none)
- **Eyes:** Never given a colour. (none)
- **Face / skin / age:** **34 years old** (ch-1, ch-2, ch-7: "thirty-four years old"). South-African-Indian, Natal-Indian / Tamil indenture descent; brown skin "in that particular range" — she registers her own face as matching the Mumbai crowd's "register" (ch-3). Has the inherited **"family chin"** that Murugan and his line carry (ch-13: "you have the family chin"). (ch-1, ch-3, ch-13)
- **Habitual clothing:** **Boots** are her one fixed wardrobe signature — "the boots came off first" when packing (ch-3); Jakobus's parting line "Wear the boots. The ground's the whole point" (ch-3); she reads people clocking her as foreign partly "in the boots" (ch-3); boots in the surf at Mahabalipuram (ch-15, ch-16). Trousers (wipes hand/wrist on "her own trouser leg", ch-1, ch-7). A jacket with a hip pocket (ch-19). Otherwise unspecified.
- **Wardrobe by scene:** Not tracked in detail; boots are the through-line. Barefoot/sandals-in-hand at the village shore in the final chapters (ch-17, ch-19) — deliberate, "you take them off at the edge of the lane."
- **Carried / props:**
  - **Ouma's tin** — a dented Nestlé condensed-milk tin from a Durban corner shop, ~100 yrs old, English script on one face and four sheared Tamil letters (a girl's name, cut where the lid begins) on the other; carried wrapped in a rolled sock in her bag the whole book (ch-1, ch-2, ch-7, ch-9, ch-13, ch-17, ch-19). Decides to keep carrying it, not leave it (ch-17, ch-19).
  - **The gold** — Africa's resonance-gold, carried "zipped flat against her spine in a flat case" (ch-2, ch-16).
  - Field kit: tone meter / tone generator, notebook, charger cable (ch-7); a **yellow dosimeter** (Anu's, cigarette-packet sized) clipped to her collar on the glass plain (ch-12).
  - A hard silver-cornered travel case/bag, "scarred silver at the corners," packed by weight (ch-3).
  - **The copper plate** (Murugan's half of the manual) — she takes custody to carry it north (ch-13, ch-17).
  - Phone (recurring; she streams the Mahabalipuram reveal live, ch-16).
- **Source chapters:** 1, 2, 3, 7, 9, 12, 13, 15, 16, 17, 19

## Coherence flags

- **Jakobus's vehicle — RESOLVED (was a SSOT contradiction).** ch-1 originally read **"a dusty Hilux double-cab,"** which conflicted with the locked silhouette in `JAKOBUS_SWART.md`: his vehicle is **the old Land Cruiser "The Beast"** (yellow OME shocks, snorkel, larger-than-stock tyres, the German sound system). **Fixed:** ch-1 (chapter + BOOK.md + novelcrafter) now reads **"a battered, dust-grey Land Cruiser double-cab,"** with Priya's engineer's-eye clocking the sleeper bones (yellow shocks, snorkel) — paralleling Jennefer's first read of the same truck in Book I ch-4 and confirming, for a reader of both, that it is the same vehicle. A Land Cruiser 79-series double-cab bakkie is a real configuration, so "bakkie/double-cab" holds; it is simply **not a Hilux.**

- **Jakobus shades-off "uniform rule" subverted in ch-2 — verify intent.** Per the SSOT, he takes the shades off **for uniforms** (and for the people who've earned the bare-eyes intimacy). In **ch-2** he takes them off indoors, at evening, "with the walls up… and yet here he was taking the glasses *off* with the walls up, which she didn't have a slot for" — there is no uniform present, only Priya. This is *intended* characterisation (giving Priya the earned bare-eyes), consistent with the SSOT's "the people who matter" clause and the ch-1 precedent (he removes them when she asks). **Not a contradiction — flagged only so the deliberate off-uniform removals (ch-1, ch-2, ch-11, ch-18) aren't "corrected" as drift.**

- **Near-identical names: "Devaraj" (kindly archivist, ch-9/15/18) vs "Devraj Anand" (antagonist, ch-8/14/16).** One letter apart ("Devaraj" vs "Devraj"). This is almost certainly a typo risk / reader-confusion hazard rather than intended. **Flag for the author to confirm the two spellings are deliberately distinct** (good archivist = "Devaraj"; villain = "Devraj"). If unintended, rename one. No same-scene collision occurs, which mitigates it, but ch-14/16 put "Devraj"/"Anand" and the memory of "Devaraj" close in the reader's mind.

- **"Sodhi" vs "Anand" — two distinct antagonist figures, consistently handled.** Prose keeps them separate: **Sodhi** = the TV broadcast face / the money who "shouts on the television" (ch-6, ch-8 "Sodhi shouts on the television… This one signs the cheques," ch-10, ch-14, ch-17); **Anand (Devraj)** = the quiet man who chairs/signs and appears in person (ch-8 on). No drift found — recorded here only because CHARACTERS.md names neither and a future editor might conflate them.

- **Priya is almost entirely physically undescribed (cross-book check needed).** The PROSE gives her **no hair (length/colour/style), no eye colour, and no build/height** — only **age 34**, brown South-African-Indian skin, the inherited **"family chin,"** and the **burn scar on the heel of her right hand**. CHARACTERS.md likewise gives no physical description (defers to the African Gold bible). **For cross-book continuity: confirm her hair/eyes/build against the African Gold canon and Book I, because this book establishes none of them and will not constrain the other books — but also must not contradict them.** The only hard physical facts to carry across: **right-hand burn scar (heel of palm)** and **the heritable "family chin."**

- **The right-hand burn scar is load-bearing and internally consistent.** Established ch-1/ch-7 (foundry flask, her twenties), matched to the depot register "*Scar, right hand, burn*" (ch-9), echoed by three women of the line (ch-13) and Murugan's own right-hand scar (ch-13, ch-19). All cites agree it is the **heel/palm of the RIGHT hand.** No drift. (Flagged positively so it's protected across edits.)

- **Anu's injury continuity (ch-16) is clean.** Cable-tie wrists + "thin line of dried blood at her hairline" (ch-16), wrists "chafed raw" after (ch-16), still referenced in ch-17 ("wrists chafed raw"). Consistent.

- **No hair/eye drift found** for any character who *is* described, because most supporting cast are given only partial description (white surf-hair for Devaraj is consistent ch-9/15/18; grey for Murugan ch-13; salt-and-pepper for Jakobus throughout). No character's stated hair/eye/build value changes between chapters.
