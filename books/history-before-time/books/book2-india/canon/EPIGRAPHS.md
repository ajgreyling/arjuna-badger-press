## BOOK-LEVEL EPIGRAPH (front of book — LOCKED)

> **यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत।**
>
> *Whenever right withers and wrong grows strong, I take form again.*
>
> — Bhagavad Gita 4.7

> *The self that asks "where am I from" is not the self the stone answers. Come empty. The rock does
> not read your passport.*
>
> — attributed to the makers *(in-world)*

**Why:** Gita 4.7 is the dharma-restored promise (real, attributable) and the engine under Priya's
whole arc — the disinherited daughter who turns out to be exactly who the moment needed; and the
makers' line names her wound (origin/belonging) and its answer (the self is not the village or the
ocean crossed) without the book ever lecturing it. *(Transliteration fallback if Devanagari can't be
verified at typeset: yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānir bhavati bhārata.)*
