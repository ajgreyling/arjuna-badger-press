## ACT I — THE ROOTS & THE ROAD (South Africa → Mumbai)
- **Node 0 — the call to India.** Open on Priya (in her *African Gold* register — sharp, dry,
  independent), drawn into the Order's road as it follows the bearing off the edge of Africa. *Opening
  options:* (a) open IN HER WOUND, mirroring Book I — a beat that shows the "too Indian for here, never
  been there" ache before the wonder; or (b) cold-open on a marvel and pull back. **Lean (a)** for
  series consistency. **Jakobus brings her in (BOOKEND CAMEO — LOCKED, see CHARACTERS/SERIES_BIBLE):**
  the relay handoff from Africa is Jakobus's scene — he's the one who carries the bearing across, who
  vouches for the Order, who gets her on the plane (his uniform rule plays at the airport / the way
  in). Then **he steps back**: India is Priya's and the local companion's to navigate. Jakobus is the
  *thread, not the guide.* **Key-out:** a reason only Priya can read what waits in India → she goes,
  telling herself it's the machine.
- **Arrival — Mumbai / the homecoming vertigo.** The sensory wall; faces like family among strangers;
  the mother-tongue half-remembered. Establish the diaspora ache (recognition + alienation). **Here
  Jakobus hands off to the Indian companion** (the local field foil) and the Indian keeper — the
  experts of this ground. The Indian
  keeper makes contact. **(End Act I.)**

## ACT II — THE RELAY THROUGH INDIA
- **Node 1 — ELLORA / Kailasa (anchor).** ARRIVE the temple carved whole from one mountain. OBSTACLE:
  it only reads as the makers' instrument if you grasp that it was carved by someone who could *see the
  finished form inside the rock* — a subtractive, no-errors-possible feat. READ (Priya): her
  pattern-cognition resolves the geometry/acoustics; her first earned wonder; the engineer meets the
  thing she can't control. COST: the antagonist's hand shows; exposure. KEY-OUT: a reading pointing to
  the older layer / the vitrified place.
- **Node 2 — AJANTA / the older layer.** What the rock remembers; the painted caves; a quieter node
  that deepens the mystery and the brain-layer (the first hint that the *texts* are a manual).
- **Node 3 — THE VITRIFIED PLACE (the "ancient nuclear" beat).** ARRIVE a site of fused ground +
  anomalous radiation. OBSTACLE/READ: the *Mahabharata* weapon-and-sickness texts read like a record;
  Priya measures (grounding rule HARD — readings on the page, cause designated a mystery). COST: real
  danger / a real price (escalation). The dread of what the makers — or the inheritors — could do.
  KEY-OUT: the coupling-chamber idea (water + stone) → the shore.
- **Node 4 — MAHABALIPURAM / the drowned shore temples.** ARRIVE the shore temples; the submerged
  structures. READ: water + stone + geometry = a coupling chamber (ties forward to Egypt's drowned
  temples from Book I's bearing). The brain-layer pays off: the machine demands the Gita's discipline
  and Priya — neurodivergent, control-reflexed — must *let the grasping self go* to read it. COST: the
  hardest yet. KEY-OUT: confirmation the chord runs on (Egypt / next book).

### Threaded through Act II (subplots)
- **The roots thread:** Priya traces her indenture ancestry — a village, a name, maybe living kin. A
  meeting with relatives she never knew (recognition + the gap the ocean made). Build to a small,
  earned, possibly devastating beat. **Undercurrent, never lecture** (kala pani, caste handled with
  care).
- **The field-companion thread (the Indian foil):** Priya's real travelling dynamic through India is
  with the **local companion** (fresh, India-specific). Whether it stays platonic or carries a charge
  is open — but keep it distinct from the Jakobus sibling-register.
- **The Jakobus seam (bookends only):** the **sibling-love** register from Book I — warm, teasing, the
  healthy never-acted-on tension; Priya clocks it at once and finds it sweet — appears at the **bookend
  cameos** (the handoff in, the handoff out to Egypt / the climax), not through the body of the book.
  (See Book I CHARACTERS + SERIES_BIBLE; same rule.)

## ACT III — THE RECKONING & THE CHOICE
- **The antagonist's move** (Vorster's reach / a heritage-extractor / **the ideologue who'd weaponise
  the past** — richest option): an attempt to own, loot, or *twist* the makers' legacy. If the
  ideologue: he wants it as *proof* of a supremacist myth — the pillar's exact poison.
- **Climax (title payoff — *The Temple in the Rock* or chosen title).** Priya's choice: not just to
  read the machine / find her roots, but to **refuse to let the deep past be hoarded or weaponised** —
  to give it to everyone (echo of Book I's "give it away to keep it safe") AND to resolve her own wound
  in the same beat: she stops needing a *place* to be from, because the Self was never displaced (the
  Gita's answer, *experienced* not stated). Grounding held (deepest mechanism stays a mystery).
- **Resolution.** Priya changed — home in herself, not in a country; roots found *and* transcended. The
  chord points onward (Egypt / next book). **Jakobus returns for the closing bookend** — the relay
  handoff *out* toward Egypt (his promised road), the sibling-register warm at the seam. Standalone
  close + open door.
- **Back matter — The Real Places in This Book.** Ellora/Kailasa, Ajanta, Mahabalipuram, the vitrified-
  site reality, the kala-pani/indenture history — real vs. debated vs. invented; the anti-poison +
  anti-weaponisation pillar stated plainly; *go and see.* (Tourism mission.)
