# MYTHOS_RULES — *History Before Time*, Book II (India)

> Inherits the binding contract from [Book I / the series default](../../book1-africa/canon/MYTHOS_RULES.md).
> Same seven rules. Listed here are the **India-specific applications and one added rule.**

## Rules 1–7 (carried verbatim from Book I)
1. The fringe is true in-world (deep antiquity was advanced; the makers left infrastructure).
2. **The artifact LEADS, the claim FOLLOWS** — every marvel earned by a witnessable physical thing first.
3. Designated mysteries stay mysterious (who the makers were / exactly how — named, never fully solved).
4. Wonder, not conspiracy (awe not paranoia; resistance human/fallible; villain = greed *or ideology*, not a cabal).
5. **African (and Indigenous) genius is the SOURCE, never explained away** — and, in India, **Indian
   genius likewise.** The temples, the texts, the metallurgy are *theirs.* The "aliens did it" move is
   forbidden; visitors/makers were inheritance-partners, not civilisers. The deep past belongs to
   everyone — *and was built by the people of each place.*
6. Real places, used honestly (the reader can go; the gazetteer separates real/debated/invented).
7. The gold thread is the series engine (the makers' tuned-gold network binds the continents).

## India-specific applications of Rule 2 (what "the artifact leads" looks like here)
- **Rock-cut temples:** the *witnessable* marvel is the carving itself — Kailasa at Ellora removed
  from a single monolith, top-down, ~200,000 tonnes, to *reveal* a finished temple. Show the
  engineering reality (the cuts, the scale, the impossibility of error in a subtractive top-down
  carve) before any maker's-instrument claim.
- **The "ancient nuclear" beat:** show the **vitrified ground**, the **anomalous radiation
  readings**, and the **textual evidence** (Mahabharata weapon-and-sickness passages) — *measured,*
  on the page — before any inference. Then designate the cause a mystery (Rule 3). **Do NOT** let the
  prose assert "it was a nuclear war"; let the reader stand where Priya stands, with the readings in
  hand and the hair on her arms, and decide.
- **Vimana texts:** treat as *texts* (the Vaimānika Śāstra etc. are real documents of contested
  origin) and physical-engineering questions, not as flying-saucer spectacle.

## Added Rule 8 — The brain-layer is dramatized, never lectured (India-specific, binding)
The **Atman/Brahman + Bhagavad Gita** material is the philosophical *key* to the makers' instrument
here (WORLD.md). It MUST be carried through **Priya's lived experience** of reading the machine — the
discipline of dropping the grasping, controlling self so the pattern resolves — and **never** delivered
as a guru's monologue, a seminar, or an info-dump. The Gita is shown to *work,* on the machine and on
her, the way Book I showed the calendar *ring.* Rule of thumb: if a character explains Atman/Brahman
in a speech, cut it and make Priya *feel* it instead.

## Added care-line (binding) — respect + the anti-weaponisation guard
Hindu/Buddhist/Jain sacred sites, texts, and living tradition are treated with **deep respect**, never
exoticised or used as set-dressing (doubly binding: the lead is reclaiming this heritage). And the
real-world danger that "ancient India was advanced" gets weaponised into **nationalist/sectarian
pseudo-history** is something the book may *feature as an antagonist's sin* but must always **refuse**:
the target is the ideology that hoards the past for one people, never a faith or a people. The pillar
holds — the deep past belongs to everyone.
