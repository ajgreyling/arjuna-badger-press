***History Before Time: The Rose in the Rock*** — a **Jakobus-led wonder-of-place** volume: the Nabataean
city carved from rose sandstone, walked as a man who reads *what a made thing is for* rather than who

## Locked decisions (author seed — revisit freely)

- **Register: NOT woo.** No ancient aliens, no "they couldn't have carved it." The Nabataeans are
  brilliant hydraulic engineers and master stoneworkers; the wonder is **real engineering in real
  sandstone** — the Siq, the Treasury, the tomb façades, the cistern system that made a desert city
  live. Anti-poison pillar: **living Jordanian/Petra custodians out-read Jakobus** on what the place
  means; he witnesses, they own the answer.
- **POV:** close third on Jakobus throughout (matches *Silver Thread* / *Recitation* / *The File*
  register). Iceberg voice — the power is in what he won't say.
- **Spine:** arrive as the fixer on a quiet commission (documentary, heritage survey, or a friend's
  errand — TBD) → walk the Siq → read the water engineering → the tomb-as-promise → the cost of a
  city built on trade when the trade moves → leave with the rose dust in his kit and no theory to sell.
- **Scale:** target ~25–35k (Jakobus-Book register — propulsive but not HBT-length).
