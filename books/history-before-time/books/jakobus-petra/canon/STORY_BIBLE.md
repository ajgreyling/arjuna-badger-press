# STORY_BIBLE — *The Rose in the Rock* (Petra)

> Jakobus-led wonder-of-place. Inherits HBT series doctrine; Jakobus SSOT wins on character.
> **Title locked:** *History Before Time: The Rose in the Rock*.

## Premise

Jakobus takes a small job in Jordan — escort a photographer, verify a heritage survey, settle a
friend's debt with a guide — because the road goes through Petra and he was always going that way.
He expects the postcard. He gets **volume**: the Siq's slot geometry, the Khazneh's misnamed face,
and the real wonder — **a desert city kept alive by water engineering** when the trade moved and the
rain got stranger.

## Theme

Permanence is **paid for** — in stone, in maintenance, in the people who still live beside the
wonder. The Nabataeans didn't magic a city out of sandstone; they **owned the rain** long enough to
cut a tomb like a promise. (Carry as theme, never preach.)

## Tone

Literary travel-adventure — propulsive like *The Jakobus File*, iceberg like *The Recitation*.
Cinematic sensory register; wonder not conspiracy. The dread is heat, checkpoints, and what tourism
does to a living place — not paranoia.

## POV & form

**Single close third — Jakobus only.** ~16 narrative chapters + ch-99 backmatter. Short chapters,
strong site-to-site pulls.

## Content bounds

- Anti-poison pillar: Nabataean genius real; Jordanian/Bedouin custodians are experts.
- No woo: no aliens, no hidden library, no secret chamber rewrite.
- Sensitivity read before publication for Bedouin/Jordanian portrayal.

## Promise to the reader

Walk the Siq with a fixer who reads made things; leave wanting to see the cisterns, not just the
Treasury — and understanding what it costs to keep a wonder alive.
