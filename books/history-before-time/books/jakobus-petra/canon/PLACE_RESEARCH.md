## Site

**Petra** — Nabataean capital, Ma'an Governorate, Jordan. UNESCO World Heritage (1985). Primary approach
via the **Siq** (narrow gorge; ~1.2 km). Signature façade: **Al-Khazneh** ("The Treasury") — ~40 m Hellenistic-Nabataean tomb façade. **Not a treasury** — misnomer; interior tomb chamber.
