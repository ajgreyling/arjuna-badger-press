# THEMES — *The Rose in the Rock*

> Embody, never state.

## Permanence has a maintenance bill

The tombs outlasted the trade route; keeping the **city alive** is a daily job — water, custody,
tourism, people.

## What is it for?

Jakobus's gift applied to a city: the answer is **water and trade**, not treasure.

## The postcard vs the cistern

Spectacle (Khazneh) vs function (hydraulics) — the book rewards the second look.

## The anti-poison pillar as respect

Genius belongs to the people who inherit the place; the outsider's job is to witness and defer.

## Iceberg fixer

Competence without domination — he fixes the pump, not the narrative.
