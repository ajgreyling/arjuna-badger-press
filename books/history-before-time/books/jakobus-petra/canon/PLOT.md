# PLOT — *The Rose in the Rock* — wonder relay skeleton

> Each node: **ARRIVE → OBSTACLE → LOCAL READ → COST → what remains → move on.**

## Act I — The commission & the approach (ch 1–4)

**Node 1 — Amman / Wadi Musa · the job**
- Arrive: small commission (photographer + heritage survey); Jakobus as fixer/driver.
- Obstacle: heat, bureaucracy, Lena wants the iconic shot list.
- Read: Sami hired — knows Jakobus's type; names the real tour vs the brochure.
- Cost: Jakobus pays for tea, commits to Sami's route not Lena's.
- Handoff: dawn entry tomorrow.

**Node 2 — The Siq · volume without explanation**
- Arrive: slot canyon; sound, shade, rose stone.
- Obstacle: tourist crush; Lena's impatience.
- Read: Sami on the gorge as flood engineering, not scenery.
- Cost: Jakobus slows them — loses Lena's goodwill briefly.
- Handoff: Khazneh at exit.

## Act II — The misnamed face & the real system (ch 5–10)

**Node 3 — Al-Khazneh · not a treasury**
- Arrive: the postcard façade.
- Obstacle: crowd, guides selling myths.
- Read: Sami — tomb, not hoard; the wonder is the face, not a hidden room.
- Cost: Jakobus wanted the romance too; accepts correction.
- Handoff: into the city — follow the water.

**Node 4 — Hydraulics · the cistern tour**
- Arrive: staff area / exposed cistern and pipe segment (custodian access).
- Obstacle: Youssef's rules; Lena bored until the camera finds texture.
- Read: Sami on dams, settling basins, ceramic pipes — ASCE-grade marvel.
- Cost: Jakobus fixes a pump; owes a favour; seen as useful not exotic.
- Handoff: Royal Tombs before the heat peak.

**Node 5 — Royal Tombs · promises in stone**
- Arrive: cliff of façades; trade wealth made permanent.
- Obstacle: heat, Lena fading; a tourist collapses — Jakobus first aid.
- Read: permanence vs living town — who pays to keep it?
- Cost: emotional — the father's ghost in "built to last."
- Handoff: Monastery climb or lose the light.

## Act III — The climb & the living town (ch 11–15)

**Node 6 — Ad-Deir · the long climb**
- Arrive: 850 steps; body cost.
- Obstacle: Lena's kit; Jakobus carries; Sami sets pace.
- Read: largest façade — same question, larger scale; emptiness inside.
- Cost: knees, water; the Beast parked below.
- Handoff: descent to Wadi Musa night.

**Node 7 — Wadi Musa · still lived in**
- Arrive: town lights; not a museum diorama.
- Obstacle: commission ends; Lena got her shots; Sami's cousin's wedding or shop.
- Read: tourism economy, custodian families, B'doul continuity.
- Cost: Jakobus leaves the good knife? (no — smaller gift: film, fix, promise to send prints).
- Handoff: road out.

## Act IV — The leaving (ch 16–17)

**Node 8 — King's Highway south · what he carries**
- Arrive: driving; silence.
- Obstacle: checkpoint — shades off; papers.
- Read: internal — water before spectacle; the fixer's read upgraded.
- Cost: none dramatic — the quiet cost of leaving.
- Close: he has the cistern in his head, not the Treasury.
