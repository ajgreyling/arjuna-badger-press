# CHARACTERS — *The Rose in the Rock*

> Jakobus: see series SSOT `JAKOBUS_SWART.md` (injected by engine). All other names **PROVISIONAL**.

## Jakobus Swart (POV)

Fixer on a small commission. His question — *what is it for?* — runs the spine. He catches himself
wanting the Treasury romance and accepts the correction. Iceberg throughout.

## Sami al-Hadid *(placeholder)* — local guide

B'doul-descended custodian; knows the **water system** better than any brochure. Out-reads Jakobus on
what Petra means to people who never left. Sharp, dry, not a mascot. Owns the telling.

## Lena Voss *(placeholder)* — photographer

Commission cover; German documentary photographer. Competent, impatient with myth. Her lens wants the
postcard; Sami and Jakobus keep steering her to the cistern.

## Youssef *(placeholder)* — park official

Checkpoint bureaucracy; not villain — procedure. Jakobus takes shades off, produces papers, fixes a
pump at the staff house as favour.

## Cast rules

- No one lectures on Nabataean history in blocks — knowledge through work and argument.
- Sami corrects misnamings ("Treasury") in passing, not as thesis.
- Jakobus carries gear, pays for tea, refuses the hero shot.
