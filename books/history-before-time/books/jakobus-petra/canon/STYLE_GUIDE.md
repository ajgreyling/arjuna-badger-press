# STYLE_GUIDE — *The Rose in the Rock*

> Inherits HBT voice from Book I (`book1-africa/canon/STYLE_GUIDE.md`), adapted for Jakobus sole POV.

## Register

- Literary travel-adventure; propulsive, sensory; loves stone and heat.
- **Close third, Jakobus only.** Iceberg — power in what he won't say.
- Short chapters; end on turn, cost, or forced move.

## Grounding rule

- Artifact leads, claim follows. Wonder not conspiracy. Anti-poison pillar.
- No woo. Hydraulics marvel is the load-bearing wonder.

## Jakobus voice

- Competence through action (fixes pump, carries kit, shades off at checkpoint).
- Dry humour spare; never Marvel quip.
- Emotion through body and silence — not named.
- ND pattern-read shown as **work** (wrong first read, then correction).

## Machine-tell taboos

Same as HBT: no "Not X. Y." crutch; no caption-after-image; no theme stated aloud; vary cadence.

## Length

~16 narrative chapters ~1,600–1,800 words + ch-99 (~1,000) ≈ 28k total.
