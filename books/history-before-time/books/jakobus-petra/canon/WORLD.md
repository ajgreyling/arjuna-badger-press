# WORLD — *The Rose in the Rock*

## When & where

**Present day** (wandering-years placement — post main HBT arc; Jakobus has Litany tattoo from Egypt).
**Jordan:** Amman → King's Highway → **Wadi Musa** → **Petra Archaeological Park** → Siq → city centre
→ Royal Tombs → Monastery (Ad-Deir) climb.

## The site (binding geography)

- **Siq:** ~1.2 km slot canyon; approach axis to city centre.
- **Al-Khazneh:** first major façade at Siq exit; tomb façade (~40 m).
- **Street of Facades / Royal Tombs:** east cliff; Urn Tomb, Silk Tomb, Corinthian Tomb, Palace Tomb.
- **Great Temple, theatre, colonnaded street:** city centre.
- **Ad-Deir (Monastery):** northwest climb (~850 steps); largest façade (~47 m).
- **Hydraulics:** dams in wadis above city; cisterns and ceramic pipes throughout — the custodian's
  tour beat is **here**, not at the postcard façade.

## Access reality

- Tourist tickets, early-morning entry, bus parking, official guides, unofficial fixers.
- Heat, water bottles, donkeys for hire, crowding at Khazneh.
- Jakobus blends — Afrikaans/English/Arabic fragments; sunglasses-off for uniforms.
