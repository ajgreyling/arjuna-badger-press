# MYTHOS_RULES — *The Rose in the Rock*

> Wonder-of-place contract. No HBT ancient-visitors mythos in this volume — Petra's marvel is **human
> engineering + trade + custody**.

## Rule 1 — The engineering is real and Nabataean

The hydraulics, rock-cut architecture, and trade wealth are **settled fact**. Never "they couldn't
have." Never ancient aliens.

## Rule 2 — Artifact leads, claim follows

Show the pipe segment, the cistern lintel, the dam scar in the wadi **before** the wonder lands.
Jakobus reads function from physical evidence.

## Rule 3 — The Treasury misnomer is a character beat

Popular name vs actual function (tomb façade) — dramatised through Sami's correction and Jakobus
accepting the less romantic truth.

## Rule 4 — No hidden history rewrite

No secret chamber that changes archaeology. Unexcavated areas may be **named** as open questions, not
entered as plot prizes.

## Rule 5 — Living custodians are the authority

Sami (and community) out-read the outsider. Anti-poison pillar binding.

## Rule 6 — Tourism is part of the story

Crowding, wear, economic dependence — shown honestly, not as lecture. Wonder does not require
ignoring the present.

## Rule 7 — Real places, honest gazetteer

ch-99 separates real / debated / invented for reader trust.
