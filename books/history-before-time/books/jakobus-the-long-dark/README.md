# *The Long Dark* — the grid-down Jakobus novel

***History Before Time: The Long Dark*** (working title) — the **fourth Jakobus-Book**, and the one that
**carries his death.** Where *The Silver Thread* is the Kalahari and the gift of **peace**, and *The
Recitation* is the Sahara and the gift of **patience**, this is **home — South Africa — and the year the
grid does not come back on.** The present-tense Jakobus (not a backstory volume): the fixer at the end of
the road, at sixty, in the catastrophe he was always ready for.

## Locked decisions (author's call)

- **The collapse IS his death-year — 2030, aged 60.** Braids directly into the locked death/wake canon;
  the book **claims the canonical Wake at the Beast**, staged as a **bookend frame** — the **prologue**
  opens on the bush assassination + the Wake's first beats (battery dies, cut to black, told from outside
  Jakobus), the body runs the grid-down arc under that known shadow, and the **finale** completes the Wake
  (the heartbeat brought back, dawn, *Sawubona*). The "battery dies → someone he taught brings the
  heartbeat back" beat becomes the *literal architecture* of the book.
- **He is killed by the Forger** — a master forger of the adversary's *control-of-provenance* world (the
  thematic anti-Jakobus), who reveres him and gives him a clean, reverent, unseen death (rusk half-chewed,
  coffee in hand, no fear) + a calling card stating it plainly. **NOT the Brotherhood of Abraham or the
  Order** (keepers, not killers, who owe him a bloodless debt — faith-dignity firewall). No revenge-kill;
  the death **proves** the Bregman thesis by contrast. Full bible [`canon/THE_FORGER.md`](canon/THE_FORGER.md).
- **4th Jakobus-Book** on the own-work `jakobus-books` shelf (sibling to *Silver Thread* / *Recitation* /
  *The File*).
- **Heavy & accurate technical layer — Dartnell-grade** (*The Knowledge*). Fuel goes bad on a clock
  (petrol gums first, diesel lasts a year-plus), the grid cascades and won't black-start, water follows
  power, the real survival science is the texture. Get it right; the gate enforces it.
- **The Bregman thesis is the SPINE, embodied never preached.** Anti-*Lord of the Flies*: the collapse
  keeps promising the Golding story and keeps delivering the *Humankind* one — crowds default to mutual
  aid; the predators are real but few and beaten by community + decency, never by a lone hard man with a
  gun. **That last resolution is forbidden** (it would betray the thesis).

## Register (binding — inherited from *The Sheltering Desert*)

*Great real bushcraft; not always a fun read; real story; real respect.* Unsentimental; the prepper-
fantasy earned down to truth (the water that never ends, the diet crisis, the insulin death the kit can't
prevent, a body failing at sixty). Real-bushcraft authority is **Ray Mears, not Bear-Grylls theatre** —
true, unshowy, never performed. Close-third on Jakobus, iceberg voice — but this is *his* book and his
end, so we are allowed closer to the cost than the cameo books ever were. **No woo** (the grounded
register, not the HBT ancient-aliens one).
