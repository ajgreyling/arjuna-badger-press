# *The Broken Crescent* — the unvisitables Jakobus novel

***History Before Time: The Broken Crescent*** — a **Jakobus-led wonder-of-place** volume: the ancient
sites of Iraq, Afghanistan, Syria, and the surrounding belt — **the wonders the brochure can't sell**
because war, checkpoints, and risk keep the tourists away. Binding seed:
[`canon/SEED_STORY.md`](canon/SEED_STORY.md); site floor:
[`canon/PLACE_RESEARCH.md`](canon/PLACE_RESEARCH.md). Jakobus SSOT:
[`../../canon/characters/JAKOBUS_SWART.md`](../../canon/characters/JAKOBUS_SWART.md).

## Locked decisions (author seed — revisit freely)

- **Register: NOT woo.** No treasure-hunt, no Ark-in-a-hole, no cabal. The wonder is **what remains**
  — Ur's ziggurat footing, Babylon's rebuilt gate beside real rubble, Bamiyan's empty niches, Palmyra's
  scarred columns, Mes Aynak's copper hill above a Buddhist monastery — and **the living people** still
  custodianing, still losing, still there.
- **Witness-not-saviour (BINDING).** Jakobus goes where the tourist can't; he **does not** rescue the
  sites or the people. Local guides, archaeologists, and community custodians **out-read him** on every
  site. The book's job is to send the reader's attention and respect toward the place — not to make the
  fixer the hero of someone else's catastrophe.
- **POV:** close third on Jakobus throughout. Iceberg voice; the closed-border backstory earns the
  checkpoints without being unpacked.
- **Spine:** a chain of witness-stops (not a treasure relay) — each node: ARRIVE → CHECKPOINT/OBSTACLE →
  LOCAL READ → COST → what remains → move on. No single "prize" at the end; the coda is **what the reader
  will never walk but can still honour.**
- **Scale:** target ~30–40k (upper Jakobus-Book register — more road, more nodes).
