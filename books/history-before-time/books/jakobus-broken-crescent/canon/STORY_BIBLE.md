# STORY_BIBLE — *The Broken Crescent*

> The unvisitables belt — witness-not-saviour; propulsive loss.

## Premise

Jakobus walks the shadow list — Ur, Babylon, Mosul, Bamiyan, Palmyra — on a chain of witness-stops.
No Order, no gold, no treasure. Deadline: each site still disappearing. He goes to **see what remains**
before the next mortar, mine, or looting truck takes the rest.

## Theme

Wonder does not require a visa — but **custody** does, and the people holding it are not props.

## Tone

Propulsive loss (rhyme with File, not quiet Recitation). Conflict through consequence. Dignity firewall.

## POV

Jakobus close third. ~18 narrative chapters + ch-99. ~35k words.

## Binding

At every site, local custodian out-reads him. He carries gear, pays tea, refuses hero shot.
