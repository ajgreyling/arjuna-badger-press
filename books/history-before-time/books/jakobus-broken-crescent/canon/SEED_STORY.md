## The one-line

The ancient wonders the package tour will never sell — **Ur, Babylon, Bamiyan, Palmyra**, the sites behind
checkpoints and headlines — and a man who has walked worse roads goes in anyway, **not to save them but
to witness what remains** before the next mortar, mine, or looting truck takes the rest.

## Premise

Every travel brochure has a **shadow list** — the places too dangerous, too policed, too broken for the
decent tourist. This book is that list, walked by Jakobus on a chain of witness-stops. **No Order, no gold
thread, no treasure relay** — the propulsion is **deadline and loss**: each site is still disappearing
(damage, looting, climate, neglect, war), and the fixer's job is to **see it honestly** and hand the
seeing to the reader who cannot go.

**Candidate nodes (author trims before outline):**

| Node | What remains / the witness |
|------|---------------------------|
| **Ur** (Iraq) | Ziggurat of Ur — rebuilt façade, real antiquity, checkpoint reality |
| **Babylon** (Iraq) | Saddam-era rebuilt gate beside ancient rubble; UNESCO damage reports |
| **Nineveh / Mosul** (Iraq) | Assyrian lamassu looting and restoration stories |
| **Bamiyan** (Afghanistan) | Empty niches where the Buddhas stood; the valley still lived-in |
| **Mes Aynak** (Afghanistan) | Buddhist monastery under a copper deposit — archaeology vs extraction |
| **Palmyra** (Syria) | Columns scarred by conflict; custodian courage |
| **Yemen / Socotra / other** | optional — author selects by access story and sensitivity |

Each node runs: **ARRIVE → CHECKPOINT/OBSTACLE → LOCAL CUSTODIAN READ → COST → what remains → move on.**

There is **no prize** at the end. The coda is the reader understanding that **wonder does not require a
visa** — but custody does, and the people holding it are not props in a white man's road story.

## Jakobus here (lead — witness-not-saviour)

He has the closed-border backstory (iceberg). He knows checkpoints, fixers, the sunglasses-off tell, the
no-gun doctrine, the village cup. He uses all of it **to get in**, not to dominate.

**BINDING:** at every site, a **local guide, archaeologist, or community custodian out-reads him** on what
the place means and what it needs. He carries gear, stands guard, pays for the tea, refuses the hero shot.
The book **sends curiosity toward the place** — back matter separates real / contested / invented and
names responsible tourism and heritage NGOs where possible.

Rhyme with *The Recitation* (Sahara, patience) and *Silver Thread* (witness without taking) — but the
register here is **propulsive loss**, not quiet seasons.

## The checkable wonder (witnessable; guardrail)

Each node must be **sourced** — UNESCO damage assessments, documented looting/restoration, on-the-ground
access reports. No invented "secret chamber" discoveries. The marvel is **what is still standing** and
**who is still keeping it**.

## Guardrails (INVIOLABLE)

1. **Witness-not-saviour** — Jakobus does not rescue sites or people; he witnesses and defer.
2. **Anti-poison pillar** — Mesopotamian, Buddhist, and Islamic heritages are **real genius**; never
   "they couldn't have" and never "thank the outsider for noticing."
3. **No treasure hunt** — no Ark, no bullion, no cabal, no looting-as-plot-reward.
4. **Conflict shown through consequence** — not explained, not adjudicated; living people in frame.
5. **Multi-faith + conflict-zone sensitivity read** with veto before publication.
6. **No woo** — grounded register throughout.
