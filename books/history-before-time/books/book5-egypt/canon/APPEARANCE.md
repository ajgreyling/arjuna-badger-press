## Layla (the girl who bends light; "the little one"; *ya bint*)
- **Role:** co-POV protagonist (the living key)
- **Build & height:** ordinary, slight; small as a child ("small enough that the men... called her *the little one*", ch-1). Capable restorer's hands — patience and eye, the maker's attentiveness (ch-2). No height/build emphasis in prose.
- **Hair:** worn under a headscarf, often the **green scarf** (her favourite), which slips off her hair when she runs/laughs (ch-4 "the green scarf slipped half off her hair"; ch-6, ch-7). Hair long enough to come loose dancing (ch-13 "her own headscarf coming loose"). Exact length/colour never stated in prose beyond implied dark (see flag).
- **Eyes:** **"enormous dark serious eyes"** — but this is the **four-year-old** Layla (ch-1). Adult eye colour not restated; consistent (dark) by implication, never contradicted.
- **Face / skin / age:** brown skin ("one brown finger", ch-1; "one brown finger" again ch-4). **Age jump is the spine: 4 → 26.** Prologue at four (ch-1); "Twenty-two years later" (ch-1) → 26 ("twenty-six years of being a background figure", ch-4/ch-7/ch-9; "twenty-six and ringing mountains", ch-11). Ordinary, un-notable looks by design ("ordinary in every visible way").
- **Habitual clothing:** Cairene everyday dress + **green scarf**; later a **wetsuit** for the dive (ch-14 "wetsuited, white-faced"; ch-15 neoprene glove). Her own headscarf is identity, not tradecraft — explicitly contrasted with Jakobus's shemag (ch-13).
- **Wardrobe by scene (only if it changes):** ch-1: child, against a crate in the desert. ch-2–13: green scarf / Cairo dress. ch-14–16: wetsuit, full-face dive kit.
- **Carried / props:** planishing hammer (the small one, handle worn to her grip, ch-2/ch-17); at the end she carries the **red firestarter paracord strand** Jakobus gives her (ch-17).
- **Source chapters:** ch-1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17.

## Coherence flags

- **Layla — hair length/colour never stated in prose (minor gap, not a contradiction).** The eyes are vividly fixed at **age four** only ("enormous dark serious eyes", ch-1); adult hair is only ever rendered via the green scarf and "came loose" (ch-13). No drift, but the ledger cannot confirm adult length/colour from prose — they are *implied dark* and never contradicted. CANON (CHARACTERS.md) also leaves hair/eye specifics open, so no canon-vs-prose conflict. **Recommend** an author call if a downstream book needs a fixed adult description.
- **Tarek — "cousin" (ch-2) vs "brother" (ch-5 onward).** ch-2 prose: "*Tell Mama...*" and Tarek as the one she lives/works beside; Dalia in ch-4 says "My — Tarek... My cousin. The workshop." But from **ch-5** he is consistently "her brother" ("they had brought the boy, Tarek... because you did not leave a family"; "My brother is in this room because of me", ch-5) and remains *brother* through the climax and epilogue (ch-9, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17). **This is a genuine relationship-label drift in the prose: cousin in ch-2/ch-4, brother from ch-5 on.** Flagging for resolution — pick one (the arc leans hard on *brother* as the hostage stakes, so ch-2/ch-4 "cousin" is the likely error).
- **Ben — eye colour never given.** Not a contradiction; only "sandy" hair (ch-4) is fixed. No flag needed beyond noting the gap.
- **Roeg — no fixed hair/eye colour anywhere.** Only "light tan", "younger... better-looking", "white teeth", and the **cream linen suit** (consistent across ch-6/7/13). No drift; description is deliberately gloss-over-void. The suit is the one load-bearing constant and it holds.
- **Jakobus — cross-checked against JAKOBUS_SWART.md (SSOT). No contradictions found.**
  - **The bush knife (SSOT lifecycle: given away ch-13, never seen/referenced after as a literal object).** Prose **conforms exactly**: clocked first by Ben in ch-11 ("the **fixed blade** on Jakobus's thigh first"); Layla counts it ch-11 ("That." nodding at his thigh); **given to the Tuareg elder ch-13** ("the lasting respect of an old man to whom he'd given a knife... his thigh was bare, the whole rig gone, knife and sheath both... handed across the fire entire... never mentioned, then or after"). **No literal knife appears after ch-13.** ✓ Clean.
  - **Shades = grey-green-blue shifting eyes, kept behind wraparound shades.** Prose conforms: pale/quick (ch-3), unnameable colour in firelight (ch-11), "a pale that had nothing to do with the light" when penned in (ch-13). The ON/OFF state machine moves constantly **by design** and (per PROP_CONTINUITY.md scan) has **no within-scene contradiction**. ✓ Clean. One worth an eye: **ch-3** he is in sunglasses in the car, removes them in the Garden City flat ("The sunglasses were off now") — both shown, legal.
  - **Build (soft belly, average, "Steve from accounting"), beard (salt-and-pepper), waistcoat (never without it), paracord band, shemag, perfume, stones, no-gun rule, Land Cruiser sleeper (built-in system, doors-open dance ch-13), nitrous-once, naked-ocean splash (bad swimmer), full-moon wakefulness** — all present in prose and all SSOT-consistent (ch-11, ch-13 carry the bulk). ✓ No drift.
