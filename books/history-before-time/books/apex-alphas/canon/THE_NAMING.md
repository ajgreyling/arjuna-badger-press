# Names — house rule

**Nobody in the house is renamed.**

The narration, dialogue, broadcast feed, dossier cards, and supporting canon use each resident's real,
publicly recognisable name. A familiar given name or surname may be used after introduction when it is
unambiguous, but the house does not assign replacement names or invented nicknames.

This keeps the reader oriented and leaves the burden where it belongs: on what each person does in the
room, not on decoding a second roster.

## Writing rules

- Use the dossier card for the full introduction, then the canonical short form above.
- Do not create a second house roster, nickname ceremony, or replacement-name subplot.
- Descriptive phrases may still identify an unnamed person before the reader learns who they are, but
  once introduced the narration uses their real name.
- Keep *sawubona* as Jakobus's greeting and the book's motif. It is not Levinas's name.
