# Modern Marvels — the ordinary future, met sideways

*A running list. The rule: **casually.** Never a set-piece about The Wonders Of Technology. Somebody
from another century meets an ordinary object of ours in the middle of doing something else, and the
scene is about the person, not the object. If a marvel needs a paragraph of explanation to land, it is
the wrong marvel.*

---

## The three tiers of knowing, and why they are different scenes

The book has three separate instruments for a person meeting the world's version of themselves, and
they must never be collapsed into one:

| Instrument | What it holds | What it does to a man |
|---|---|---|
| **The Court's archive** | Everything. The whole species' record, opened on the first day of the bargain. Private, total, unsentimental. | **Devastates.** See [[LEGACY_LEDGER]]. Einstein's letter. Marcus's wall. The empty box. |
| **The tablets** | Six and a half million encyclopaedia articles. Public, secondhand, summarised, frequently wrong. | **Stings, and is funny.** *"This is what people say happened. Some of it's wrong."* |
| **The feed** | What eight billion people are saying about them *right now*. | Reserved. Not yet used. Hold it in case Book Two needs a third turn of the screw. |

## The tablets

Children's tablets, rubber bumpers in four colours, battery you cannot get at, **one app**, locked: an
offline encyclopaedia mirror, updated Sundays. No browser, no messages, no feed. Andrew hands them out
like hymn books with one sentence of framing and then leaves the room.

Established reactions (ch-15):

- **Smug:** the Furnace (*father of modern philosophy*, read aloud three times) · JP (declined the
  Nobel) · Threes (had to be taken aside by the Doctor a second time) · the Nose (*Caesar salad*, until
  he scrolls down to *Kaiser* and *Tsar*)
- **Disgusted at the inaccuracy:** the Clock (the murderer at the door, in the summary box, out of four
  hundred thousand words) · the Owl (thesis–antithesis–synthesis, three boxes and arrows, a formula he
  never once wrote) · Fritz (the sister, the assembled book, the walking stick in the museum, and *that
  which does not kill us* on a shirt — it is a joke about convalescence)
- **Took it better than anyone expected:** Canterbury (*a dead argument gets one paragraph and no
  enemies*) · Keeper (the unused train ticket in his pocket — *nobody would print it in a novel*)
- **Would not look:** Sid (found elephants instead; never searched himself in eleven months) · Filters
  (*I have already seen a wall in a gymnasium*) · Martin (read to the bottom, where the 1933 section runs
  longer than the work, said nothing, handed the tablet back and asked not to be given another)
- **Looked up something else entirely:** Mo (*proprioception*, then *mirror neurons* — four hours of
  pleasure, sixty years of laboratories proving him right about the knee) · the Bracket (searched
  *phenomenology*, found his assistant's name above his, put the tablet in a kitchen drawer where it
  stayed) · Sawubona (a town in Lithuania, a wood outside it, a number in the third sentence — then
  cleared the search box so the next man would not find it)
- **The one that costs the most:** Sapor, who read *priority dispute* and the other man's name, and
  then asked the Court for a copy of a letter he had not yet written, and was refused, and asked again
  on the Thursday, and was refused again, and did not ask a third time.

## Marvels still on the shelf

*Use sparingly. One per chapter at most, always in passing, never explained.*

| Marvel | Who meets it | The beat |
|---|---|---|
| **The dishwasher** | Josh | Already used, and used correctly: he is *fixing* it, not marvelling at it. This is the model for all of these |
| **The espresso machine** | Sapor | Already the load-bearing one. Not a marvel to him for four minutes; then a *system*, and then a ledger, and then a door into the least bindable man in the house |
| **Hot water, on demand, indefinitely** | Sid · Kong · the Ox | The one the ancients actually stop over. Not the tablets. A tap that gives hot water forever and nobody has carried anything |
| **Ice** | the Nose | In a jug, in summer, unremarked by everybody else at the table. He asks where it is kept. There is no ice house |
| **Reading glasses** | Prof · the Chair · Broad | The Court grows them to prescription in an afternoon. Three men who spent their last decades unable to read comfortably, and one of them has to leave the room |
| **The lift** | Anselm | Does not trust it for nine days. Uses the stairs. Then uses it, once, and afterwards uses nothing else |
| **Anaesthetic** | anybody, told in passing by Bas about a knee reconstruction | The single fact from the future that most reliably silences a table of people who lived before 1846 |
| **The washing machine** | the whole rota | Nobody makes a speech. It simply means the rota has eleven duties instead of twenty-six, which is why there is time for a Porch at all |
| **Antibiotics** | Keeper (tuberculosis) · Sapor · the Grinder (glass dust, forty-four years old) | Never as a gift. Always as arithmetic: *how long would I have had* |
| **A photograph of the Earth from outside** | everybody, once, on the Doctor's evening | Held to the end of Book One's material. When it comes it is not a marvel scene; it is an argument |
| **Recorded music, on demand, all of it** | Kong · Prof | They agree that music governs a state, they disagree about how — and then somebody hands them a device containing every song ever recorded and asks them what they would like. Neither of them can answer for a full minute |
| **The microwave** | the Wardrobe demonstrates it | Comedy. Four ancients watching a Springbok prop heat a pie. No commentary |

## Rules

1. **No speeches.** Nobody says *what a marvellous age.* If a character starts to, another character
   cuts them off, because the house has no patience for it.
2. **The user is never impressed by the right thing.** Sid finds elephants. Mo skips himself for
   proprioception. Sapor is not amazed by the machine — he is annoyed by the grind setting.
3. **Marvels are doors into people, or they are cut.** The dishwasher exists so that a technician can
   ask a stranger's name at twenty past five in the morning.
4. **Never explain how it works** unless the person meeting it is the one who explains it — and then
   let them be *wrong* if that is truer.
