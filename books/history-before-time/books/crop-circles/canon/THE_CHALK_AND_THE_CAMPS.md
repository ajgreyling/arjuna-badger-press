# LOCKED — the ground, the hill figures, Bath, and the camps (binding for *The Field of Doors*)

> Author lived in Wessex **four years.** This is memory, not research. The chalk is his. Two things
> are now binding additions to the spine, plus one fact that goes **on record** in this book harder
> than anywhere else in the series.

## 1. The hill figures = the ANCIENT crop circles (LOCKED — the missing spine piece)
The white chalk hill figures are the **same medium, made permanent**: a people cutting enormous,
deliberate, *useless-beautiful* figures into the white skin of the land **for the sky to read.** They
are the ancient version of the signpost system the crop marks are the temporary version of.
- **The Westbury White Horse** (Bratton Down, below the Iron-Age hillfort) — the chalk cut to bone.
- **The Cerne Abbas Giant** — "**the tall man**" (author's words). The giant figure on the Dorset
  hill. (Real dating now ~late-Saxon, but the PRACTICE of cutting figures into chalk for the sky runs
  back to the Uffington Horse, Bronze Age, ~3,000 years.)
- **The Uffington White Horse** (just over the Oxfordshire line) — the oldest, ~Bronze Age, the
  proof the medium is **millennia older than the 1991 hoax.** This is the book's hard anchor: *people
  have been drawing giant deliberate marks into this chalk for the sky to see since before Stonehenge
  was finished.* The crop circle is not a modern prank inventing a medium — it is the **latest entry
  in the oldest sky-writing tradition in Britain.** The hoax hides inside a 3,000-year-old habit.
- **USE:** Jakobus / Edie read the hill figures as kin to the formations — same impulse, same
  audience (the sky), same useless-beautiful deliberate mark that means *we were here and we thought.*
  Rhymes hard with the Cape book's "first deliberate mark = that's when we became us." The buried
  signs point DOWN; the hill figures are the same hand pointing UP. The land written both ways.

## 2. Bath / Aquae Sulis (LOCKED — set-piece; "my single best day on British soil")
The Roman baths over the hot spring — **but the Romans didn't make it first.** The Celts worshipped
**Sulis** at the spring long before; Rome built its temple over hers and renamed her Sulis Minerva
(the same colonial over-writing the whole series inverts — the conqueror builds his monument on the
older people's holy place and takes the credit). The hot water comes up from **deep in the same
chalk-and-limestone aquifer** the buried sites sit on — **the land writing in WATER instead of
wheat.** Use as an Act-II beat (the deep water; the over-written goddess; the aquifer as the thing
the whole map is really about). Carry the author's "best day on British soil" as Edie's or Mark's-
register awe, never stated as autobiography.

## 3. THE CAMPS — on record, prose + note, biting harder here (LOCKED — AUTHOR'S DIRECTIVE)
**Documented historical fact (verify-clean):** Britain invented the **modern concentration camp**
during the **Second Anglo-Boer War (1899–1902)**. Under Kitchener's scorched-earth policy the
British military interned Boer **civilians — overwhelmingly women and children** — and, in **separate
camps**, tens of thousands of **Black Africans**. Roughly **26,000–28,000 Boer women and children
died** (the majority under sixteen) of disease and starvation; the Black African camps had their own
enormous, long-uncounted toll (estimates **20,000+**). **The term "concentration camp" enters wide
use from this war.** **Emily Hobhouse** exposed it to the British public and was vilified for it. **The
Crown has never formally apologised.**

**How it lands in THIS book (author's call: BOTH + bite harder):**
- **In prose — cold, ONCE, through Jakobus.** He is standing on the Crown's home chalk, protecting
  the buried ancestors of the people whose empire **invented the camps his people died in**, and the
  fact surfaces **once**, flat, undetonated — the iceberg — but this is the **one book where the
  gentle man's anger is closest to the surface.** It draws blood **once.** No speech, no grievance
  monologue, no lecture. A single quiet unanswerable fact, said on this ground, and let to sit. The
  restraint IS the indictment — but here the restraint has a blade in it.
- **In back matter — a signed author's-note ON RECORD, in AJ's own voice**, stating the facts and
  the figures (Boer AND Black African dead; the coining of the term; Hobhouse; the un-returned
  apology) as **fact, for the record** — cool, exact, unanswerable, separate from the fiction so the
  fiction can't soften it. (Mirrors how the series already carries its heavy true things in back
  matter.) See `DEDICATION_BOOK.md` is the front; the camp note is **back** matter.
- **GUARD:** still never woo, still never a misery-spectacle, still never the theme spoken. The camp
  fact is not the book's *plot* — it is one cold true beat and one cold true note. The book stays the
  crop-circle hunt. But the fact goes on record, named, sourced, here, harder than anywhere else.

## 4. THE SURRENDER THAT WASN'T + THE ONE-SHOT FATHER (LOCKED — AUTHOR'S DIRECTIVE, both prose + note)
**Two more facts on record, this book, bite-harder register (author's call: "let the pride show").**

**(a) Vereeniging — the Boers were never beaten in the field; they WROTE terms (verify-clean).** The
empire could not beat Boer riflemen on the open veld (3 years; farmer + Mauser + horse vs the largest
empire on earth). It only "won" by burning farms and caging women/children. The war ended **31 May
1902 at Vereeniging in a NEGOTIATED TREATY, not unconditional surrender** — Boers secured a **general
amnesty, Dutch in schools & courts, £3 million to restock the burned farms, eventual self-government.**
"Beaten people sign what's put in front of them; my people wrote their own terms." The pride is
EARNED and allowed to be felt (still sourced, still not a lecture). Sources: Britannica/Wikipedia/
King's College/O'Malley(Mandela) archives — Treaty of Vereeniging.

**(b) The one-shot father (AJ's REAL father — PRIVATE truth, put on record as HIS in the note; carried
by JAKOBUS as his father's in the prose).** The marksmanship in the DNA (a people who went hungry if
they couldn't shoot) came down to AJ's dad, **a dairy farmer and a hunter** (NOT a policeman — author
correction 2026-06-13). His marksmanship was known, so the **local policemen would come and fetch him
in the police van** and the group would go out together — police friends as companions, NOT the
father in uniform; the van is present but is NOT the load-bearing "vehicle sworn to stop them" irony.
At night they **cut game fences on rich private land, poached game to feed hungry poor Afrikaner
families in the dorps.** **THE RULE: ONE SHOT a night, never more** — one shot in the Transvaal dark
reads as a *surprise/thunderclap* (man on his stoep forgets it); **two+ shots = a poacher** (now he's
listening, reaching for the phone). One animal, one clean shot, one family fed, **never greedy** (greed
= caught = families starve + a friend loses his job). **Discipline as mercy.**
**THE FAMILY HAD ENOUGH (author correction 2026-06-13): NOT poor — "not rich, but always more than
enough to eat."** The sacrifice is **pure choice, not shared desperation:** a man with a full freezer
emptying it for strangers who had nothing. They came home often to an **empty deep-freeze + stripped
pantry**; **Dad said nothing** (closed it, made coffee); **Mom said nothing but KNEW** — a family with
nothing is eating tonight; a hungry child goes to bed full. It made for a **hard month** (Mom works out
how to feed her own on what's left), never hunger. Never named aloud; lived in the silence. *"The
freezer's empty. Somebody's eating. Say nothing. Make the coffee."*
- **PROSE (ch-06 "One Shot"):** Jakobus tells it as HIS father's — folds into his existing DNA (the
  marksman-who-refuses-the-gun, the favour given-and-never-named, *Sawubona*, the wound). Cold,
  earned, no speech. Read off Pyne's shotgun ("a man who'll only fire once is a particular kind of
  man"). The book never names AJ.
- **NOTE (ch-99):** on record as AJ's OWN father, in his voice, named — the dairy-farmer-and-hunter
  fetched by his police friends, the one shot, the silent freezers, the family that had enough and gave
  anyway — alongside the Vereeniging pride. Mirrors how the camps land (prose + note).
- **GUARD:** PRIVATE family truth → transformed in prose (it's Jakobus's father there, not literally
  AJ's), literal & owned only in the signed note. Never misery-spectacle; the silence IS the dignity.

## 5. THE MILITARISED SCHOOLING — the soil the one-shot grew in (LOCKED; in the note)
Added to the back-matter note (between Vereeniging and the one-shot father), as REAL grounding for
the marksmanship-in-the-DNA theme. AJ's real apartheid-era boys' high school = **Bethlehem
Voortrekker Hoërskool** (Bethlehem, Free State). The ordinary half of that schooling was militarised:
**mandatory cadets (*kadette*)** with real drill + rank; a ***Seunsdrilpeloton*** (boys' drill
platoon) that **won inter-school cadet competitions** ("Wenners van Groep 25 Kadetkompetisie"; a
schoolboy named **"beste junior drilsersant"**); and a **safe in the school holding .22 rifles** for
shooting competitions (marksmanship as an ordinary school sport). Documented in the school's own
yearbook (the Seunsdrilpeloton/Kadetkompetisie page). The note frames it WITHOUT nostalgia ("a hard,
militarised, fearful edge … I have spent a life walking away from the parts that deserved it") — the
point is that the Boer hunger-and-veld marksmanship was *deliberately kept alive* in every Afrikaans
boy, and came down to the father. **Cross-book note:** this SAME real schooling (Bethlehem + the
cadets/.22 + the separate **Verrykingsonderwys** enrichment at the **Bloemfontein sentrum**) also
grounds the standalone novella *The Loneliest People in the World* — see [[the-loneliest-novella]];
there it's the world the fictional boy is pulled OUT of. The VO programme name is **Verrykingsonderwys**
(verified from AJ's 1983 *Voortrekker* yearbook masthead — NOT "Verrykende Onderwys").

## 6. THE OLIVER'S CASTLE FOOTAGE — the real "won't lie down" hook (LOCKED; back-matter note only)
Added to the back-matter note (ch-99), in the opening "here are the real things, go look yourself"
cluster, as the one piece of real footage the author points the reader at. **Verify-clean facts:**
**11 August 1996**, beneath **Oliver's Castle** hillfort on **Roundway Down above Devizes, Wiltshire**
(a few miles from the book's setting). A ~**24-second video** shows **balls of light moving low over a
wheat field while a formation appears beneath them** — the thing *forming under the camera*, not a
dawn-photographed finished circle. Filmed by **John Wabe** (a Bristol animation-studio employee) under
the alias **"Jonathan Wheyleigh."** **The official verdict was *hoax*:** the lights argued to be CGI
(**no motion blur, no inter-field flicker** — render-tells, per John Huckvale / AVP Studios; Colin
Andrews concluded hoax). **BUT — author's honest register (this is why it's in the book):** the case
**never cleanly closed** — the promised confession was **never properly aired** (Andrews says a Japanese-
TV confession went unbroadcast), **nobody reproduced it with period tools**, and the tape **still
circulates and is still argued over** 25+ years on. **GUARD — the note must NOT claim it is real.** The
exact, load-bearing stance: *"never satisfactorily shown to be false"* ≠ proven true; "I am not telling
you it is real… go and watch it, decide for yourself, the answer is allowed to stay a *maybe*." This is
the book's whole "the answer stays a *maybe*" spirit, on record. Sources: thecroppie.com (2023, "The
Video That Won't Go Away"), cropcircleresearch.com (Enigma 11 analysis), National Geographic *Paranormal?
Crop Circles* (2005). **Register: wonder + intellectual honesty, never woo, never "proof of aliens."**

## 7. "A FIELD FULL OF SECRETS" — the patterns-as-engineering hook (LOCKED; back-matter note only)

schematic*, not as "who made it."** **Verify-clean facts:** ***A Field Full of Secrets*** (2014 doc,
~82 min), directed by/starring **Charles Maxwell** (with Dax Phelan). The film's people **read the crop
geometry as the blueprint for a machine** ("some kind of machine"; framed in coverage as a power plant /
new kind of engine), **combined ELEVEN formation designs** into one model, and **actually tried to
physically build it** — drawings to engineers, metal cut, many parts **outsourced/fabricated** because
they couldn't make them in-house. **Outcome ambiguous/unresolved** — the film itself poses it as "change
the world, or simply waste an enormous amount of time and money."
- **WHY IT'S THE KEYSTONE:** it is the literal-engineering thesis of the series enacted on screen —
