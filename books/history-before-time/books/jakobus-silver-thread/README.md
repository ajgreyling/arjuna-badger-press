# *The Silver Thread* — the Jakobus backstory novel

***History Before Time: The Silver Thread*** — the human, grounded prequel to the Jakobus arc: the
years between the Border War and the man we meet in Book I. Seed canon and binding guardrails:
[`../../canon/characters/JAKOBUS_BACKSTORY_BUSH.md`](../../canon/characters/JAKOBUS_BACKSTORY_BUSH.md);
acknowledgement + naming guidance: [`../../canon/SAN_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT.md`](../../canon/SAN_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT.md).

## Locked decisions (author-deferred; chosen to draft — revisit freely)

- **Register: NOT woo.** No ancient aliens, no impossible object. The one numinous image — the
  tracker's *silver thread* — is rendered as embodied expertise (Liebenberg), never magic.
- **POV:** close third on Jakobus throughout. The San are full people with agency — experts, teachers,
  funny, ordinary — never seen-through-a-white-lens props (anti-poison pillar).
- **Spine:** the war → the betrayal (Schmidtsdrift/Platfontein) → the deep-Kalahari kin → the unmaking
  → the costly leaving → peace.
- **Community:** the tracker is **!Xun** (Bushman Battalion lineage, 201 Bn / Omega); the bush healing
  happens among Kalahari kin still living close to the old ways.

## ⛔ Sensitivity-read gate (BINDING before this is anything but a draft)

- **Every San character name in this draft is a FICTIONAL PLACEHOLDER** — not a real person, not a
  verified !Xun/Khwe/Ju name. Lock names with the chosen community.
- The Border-War history (31/201/203 Bn, Omega, Schmidtsdrift, Platfontein) and **all San cultural and
  spiritual content** must be verified and routed through a San sensitivity read (see seed §Guardrails).
- Click letters (ǀ ǁ ǂ ǃ) are used lightly and must be confirmed at typeset.
