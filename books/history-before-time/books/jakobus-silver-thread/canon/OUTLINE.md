# *The Silver Thread* — outline (as drafted)

> The complete arc, seventeen chapters, four parts. Drafted close-third on Jakobus. Grounded, never
> woo. Read the seed first: [`../../../canon/characters/JAKOBUS_BACKSTORY_BUSH.md`](../../../canon/characters/JAKOBUS_BACKSTORY_BUSH.md).

## Cast (ALL San names are fictional placeholders — sensitivity-read gate)

- **Jakobus Swart** — Free State boy, SADF conscript-then-operator; the man being unmade. (SSOT.)
- **Kxao** — !Xun tracker, 201 Bn; saves Jakobus's life in the war; later parked at Schmidtsdrift; takes
  Jakobus to his Kalahari kin. The teacher of the silver thread. *(fictional name)*
- **ǂOma** — old man, the clown-grandfather; teaches the children by becoming the animals. *(fictional)*
- **Be** — grandmother; the band's spine; tells the fire-stories. *(fictional)*
- **Tashe** — a boy; Jakobus's small mirror; learns the thread alongside him. *(fictional)*

## Parts

- **I — The War (1–3):** the same Bible; the tracker who reads the ground; independence and the betrayal.
- **II — The Noise (4–5):** the years he can't be still; the father's death; finding Kxao at Schmidtsdrift.
- **III — The Bush (6–15):** the road north; *a place they just call home*; insulting the meat; the
  clowns; the silver thread; the elephant vow; the moon/gemsbok and the mantis; the gift; the night the
  wound opens; the calmest man.
- **IV — The Leaving (16–17):** what he leaves behind (the costly gift); *Sawubona* — the man who walks
  into Book I.

## Themes carried (embody, never state)

Peace as the gift beneath all gifts · reduced-to-a-word (Boesman / the mantis misnamed) · the genius and
contentment of the marginalised · giving-away-what-you-love-is-love · the silver thread as the origin of
his reads-land-and-people competence.
