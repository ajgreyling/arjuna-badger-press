**Gita arc position:** Book III = **Krishna's teaching.** Arjuna's paralysis (Book II) is broken;
now the character must learn to act without ego-grasping. The rock-cut temples are the physical
embodiment of this: they were made by people who could already see the finished form inside the
rock — subtractive, no-errors-possible, requiring a mind that acts without hesitating over outcome.

## Author's decisions needed (before deep-dive)

1. **The protagonist** — who is native to the Deccan? Options:
   - A Marathi archaeologist (Maharashtra, Pune/Aurangabad) — insider to the Ellora/Ajanta sites
   - A Muslim conservation architect from Hyderabad — Deccan Sultanate history, the Muslim layer of the region (the Bidar/Golconda thread), complex religious plurality shown from *inside* it
   - A Jain scholar (Jainism has a major presence at Ellora — Cave 32 is Jain) — the ahimsa / non-attachment thread runs directly into the Gita's teaching
   - Author's call

2. **The relay spine** — what does Book III add beyond Book II?
   - Ellora's three-faith cliff (Cave 1-12 Buddhist / 13-29 Hindu / 30-34 Jain — in one mountain) is the argument: plurality coexisting. This is the physical version of the theme.
   - The Deccan Sultanates (Muslim kingdoms that built syncretic culture) as a *second* plurality argument — India's Islam is not alien, it's one of the layers of the rock.
   - Vijayanagara (Hampi) — the last great Hindu empire's ruins; the tension between preservation and destruction.

3. **The antagonist** — different from Book II's. Options:
   - A Hindu nationalist who wants to erase the Buddhist and Jain caves from Ellora's story (the re-writing-of-history angle — historically real)
   - A mining/infrastructure interest threatening the sites (the extraction mirror)
   - An outsider academic who wants to claim the discovery as Western knowledge

4. **How Priya threads through** — she should appear but NOT carry the POV. A cameo or a relay handoff scene; she passes the bearing to the Book III protagonist. The sibling-love dynamic with Jakobus can appear briefly.
