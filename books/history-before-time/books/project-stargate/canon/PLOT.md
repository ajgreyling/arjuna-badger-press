## BOOK I — ~18 chapters, ~55k (balanced braid → first proof)

### ACT I — The ledger (Ch. 1–6)

1. Cold open: session tape — dead viewer's voice; Mara's employee ID.
2. Wound flash: termination; manifest anomaly; report buried.
3. Mac finds her — strip-mall office; badge cache forty days.
4. **First sit (sketch seed — LOCKED)** — blind coordinate; ice-door sketch; in the margin a **hinge /
   symbol / line** she did not draw and does not remember drawing. Mac goes quiet. Henry Sugar hush.
5. Handler — polite reinstatement offer.

### ACT II — Goats + practice (Ch. 7–12)

7. **Nested tale (LOCKED)** — Mac tells **McMoneagle Typhoon** (Sept 1979): sketch, dismissal, satellite
   confirmation; Dahl hush; then cut to First Earth Battalion absurdity.
8. Lena — observer effect as protocol; **Vatican Consulta Medica parallel** (rapid/complete/lasting);
   first failed resonance (body cost).
9. Handler tightens; Mara trains in secret.
10. Desert facility glimpsed / approached — program still live (setup for Book II).
11. Goats set-piece — psychic soldier comedy → horror.
12. Midpoint: she chooses to stay — not for clearance, for **the next session**.

### ACT III — First proof (Ch. 13–18)

13.–15. Escalation — hunt, second session, Mac's sketch wall grows.
16. **Book I climax — Henry Sugar proof #1 (LOCKED):** session under pressure; margin detail **matches**
    classified hinge/architecture she has not been shown; card turns; prose quiet; she **believes**.
17. Handler closes — she runs with Mac; the sketch page pinned to his wall.
18. Close: Antarctica on the horizon; she is no longer the woman who only trusted ledgers.
