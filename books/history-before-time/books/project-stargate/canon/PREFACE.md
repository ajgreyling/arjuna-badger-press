# PREFACE — factual front matter (binding)

> **LOCKED:** Each volume opens with a **non-fiction Preface** — declarative, stated **as fact** —
> before epigraph → Ch. 1. Primes readers new to mind-over-matter. **Publication drafts:**
> `build/PREFACE.book1.md` · `build/PREFACE.book2.md` · `build/PREFACE.book3.md`

---

## Function

| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| **Preface** | What is **on the record** — volume-specific factual priming |
| **Epigraph** | Fiction register (*Stranger in a Strange Land*) |
| **Chapter 1** | Story; Mara may doubt what the reader already knows |

The preface is **author factual voice** — calm, specific, no wink. Each volume ends with a **fiction boundary** paragraph + Source Notes pointer.

---

## Tone rules

- **Declarative** — institutions and events stated as fact where documented
- **Specific** — dates, names, acronyms
- **Length** — ~1,000–1,400 words per volume
- **Serpo (Book II)** — legend with paperwork; **not** stated as historical fact
- **Book III** — clear **invented** list (door, warm sector, sketch completion) vs **documented** (Highjump, Piri debate, ice logistics, observer-effect distinction)
