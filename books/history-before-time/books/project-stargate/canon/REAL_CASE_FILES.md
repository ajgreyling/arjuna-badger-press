## Tier 1 — anchor cases (plant early; trilogy spine)

### Joe McMoneagle & the Typhoon submarine (September 1979) — **DOCUMENTED** (details contested)

**The story:** Tasked blind, McMoneagle described activity inside a Soviet building near the White Sea.
He sketched a **massive submarine** — double hull, angled missile tubes (~18–20), scale so large NSC
staff reportedly **dismissed** it (no submarine that size in US intel). He described blast of a
channel to water; launch ~four months out. **January 1980:** satellite imagery showed launch of the
**Typhoon-class** — largest submarine ever built — matching several sketch details.

**Google bait:** "McMoneagle Typhoon submarine", CIA STAR GATE reading room, Dr. Edwin May accounts.

**Contested:** Prior intel leak? Selective memory? Session transcripts show monitor prompting. **Still
real:** declassified program, dated sessions, sketch, later hardware match — the shape of the story
is on record.

**In-fiction:** Mac's nested tale (Book I, Ch. 7 energy). Mara finds session ID in archive. Source Notes
cite September 1979.

### Vatican medical miracles / Congregation for the Causes of Saints — **DOCUMENTED**

**The story:** Rome maintains a **medical board** (Consulta Medica) of independent doctors. For
beatification/canonization, a healing must be **rapid, complete, and lasting** — instant or abrupt,
total, **never to return**, scientifically inexplicable after full records review. Theological
consultors separate from doctors. Since 2016 reforms: **two-thirds** medical supermajority required.

**Google bait:** "Consulta Medica miracle", "Congregation for the Causes of Saints medical board",
specific decrees (e.g. John Paul II beatification medical report).

**In-fiction:** **Lena** or a civil contact has a cousin on a diocesan tribunal — not to preach
Catholicism, to show **another institution** that already treats mind/matter anomalies as **case law**.
Mara: "You trust a miracle board more than a session room?" / "They publish when they're *wrong*."

**Parallels to STAR GATE:** blind protocol, expert dismissal, record that outlives opinion.

### Project STAR GATE (1978–1995) — **DOCUMENTED**

**The story:** US gov remote-viewing program; Fort Meade; thousands of sessions; 1995 CIA report
says no actionable intel (program closed). Declassified files on CIA.gov.

**Google bait:** "CIA STAR GATE reading room", "Edwin May remote viewing", session transcript PDFs.

**In-fiction:** tape cold open, Mac's office, Mara's archive pull.
