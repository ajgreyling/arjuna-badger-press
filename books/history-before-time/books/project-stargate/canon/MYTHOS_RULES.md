# MYTHOS_RULES — *The Men Who Opened the Door*

> The binding "physics." Here the mythos is **woo-forward** — but still **earned in-scene** so it reads
> as thriller fact, not authorial hand-wave. Inverts HBT Rule 4: conspiracy and consciousness are
> features.

---

## Rule 1 — The fringe is true in-world (and contested in the footnotes)

STAR GATE produced usable remote viewing. Serpo happened *enough* that someone still guards the
after-action. Antarctica has warm-sector infrastructure not on public maps. The book does not wink.
The **Source Notes** wink for the reader afterward.

## Rule 2 — Observation collapses possibility (binding)

The **observer effect** is plot physics:

- Untrained observation = noise, anxiety, false positives (why the program burned so many viewers).
- Trained **coordinate viewing** = structured collapse: the viewer doesn't travel; the *target* becomes
  knowable to the session room.
- **Resonant observation** (chakra-aligned, whole-nervous-system) = **edit**: local matter reorganises
  to match the collapsed state — ice becomes air, stone becomes aperture, memory becomes recoverable.

**No teleportation.** No Marvel powers. Edits are **local**, **costly**, and **brief** — the body pays.

## Rule 3 — Chakra resonance is engineering, not wellness

The seven centres are treated as **tunable oscillators** in a conscious field (Dispenza register):

- Alignment is not "good vibes"; it is **phase-locking** with a structure built to answer a human
  frequency.
- The door under the ice is a **receiver**; the viewer is the **carrier wave**.
- Failed resonance = migraine, nosebleed, psych break — the Goats comedy curdles into horror.

**Dramatize with sensation:** heat in the sacrum, pressure at the crown, the moment before a note
breaks glass — not chakra lecture.

## Rule 4 — Podcast gospel is an epistemology

In-world, long-form interview shows function as **folk peer review**:

- "He said it on the show" ends family arguments.
- Clips are evidence in underground forums.
- Mara despises this — until her ledgers won't open the door and a clip of a dying colonel does.

This is **cultural fact** in the book, not author mockery. Mock the surrender; respect the hunger.

## Rule 5 — Bureaucracy weaponises the impossible

The antagonist force is **institutional**, not reptilian:

- Programs renamed, not ended.
- Viewers retired into contractors.
- Goats trained as assets; goats discarded as liabilities.

Absurd memos and lethal seriousness coexist (*Goats* rule).

## Rule 6 — Real programs, honest texture

STAR GATE, Grill Flame, Sun Streak, SCANATE, Ingo Swann's Jupiter session, McMoneagle's Mars
structure — use **real history as scaffolding**. Fictionalise only where the plot requires active
present-day actors.

## Rule 7 — Antarctica obeys travel cost

Getting to the warm sector is a **logistics thriller**: ice runway politics, fuel, clearance windows,
solar-quiet countdown. No teleport-to-pole. The Piri Reis / Highjump lore is **texture**, not
treasure map.

## Rule 8 — Designated mysteries stay mysterious

*Who built the door* and *what Serpo traded* are **named, not solved**. The trilogy pays off **Mara's
witness**, **one resonance edit on stone**, and **the sketch that finished itself** — not full
cosmology. The threshold shows **geometry of elsewhere** (wrong-scale architecture, attention as
place); never faces, ships, or on-page Ebens.

## Rule 8b — The sketch rhyme (trilogy proof object, LOCKED)

- Book I: margin detail Mara did not draw.
- Book III: the **same page** completes after the glimpse — ink fresh, unwatched, on Mac's wall.
- The proof is **not portable** until it is; the institution cannot confiscate what appears on a
  strip-mall office wall. Henry Sugar small proof at scale.

## Rule 9 — Google priming (reader mission, binding)

The series **on-ramps** readers new to mind-over-matter:

- Plant **specific, checkable** real cases (dates, institutions, sketches) — see REAL_CASE_FILES.md.
- **Wonder first**, citation second — no mid-scene Wikipedia voice.
- Back matter **Source Notes** tag DOCUMENTED / CONTESTED / INVENTED; include skeptic's best case.
- Fiction may invent Mara, Mac, the door; it may **not** falsely claim STAR GATE was hoax or Vatican
  tribunals are theatre when we're borrowing their **documented** existence.

---

## Rule 10 — Henry Sugar wonder (tone is a rule, not a vibe)

The impossible is **earned** and **landed without mockery**:

- **Practice before proof** — no instant psi; sessions fail until they don't (Dahl's months at the card).
- **Small first miracle** — the blind sketch's one impossible detail before the door moves (the reverse
  of the card, not the casino jackpot).
- **Nested tales told straight** — when elders describe how viewing was learned, narration shifts to
  documentary hush; return to Mara's dry voice only after.
- **No wink at the landing** — goats, podcasts, and Handler may surround the beat; they may not
  commentate during it.
- **Enlarged close** — reader finishes feeling the world got **wider**, not merely that they survived a
  conspiracy (see STYLE_GUIDE.md).
