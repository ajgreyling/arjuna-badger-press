## The one-line

**Dr. Mara Kincaid** — a former Defense Logistics records officer who lost her clearance after she
couldn't stop pulling threads on compartmented Antarctic transit manifests — is found by the last
living **STAR GATE** remote viewers and dragged through declassified goats, **Serpo** transcripts,
and a podcast culture that treats three-hour interviews as peer review, until she has to decide
whether to open a door under the ice that only opens when a human nervous system **resonates** with
it.

## Title (working) & provisional specifics

- **Title (working):** ***The Men Who Opened the Door***
- **Protagonist (working):** **Dr. Mara Kincaid** — mid-30s, PhD in information science, DoD contractor
  background; the woman who "worked for the government and found evidence."
- **Elder viewer (working):** **Col. Ray "Mac" McAllister** — McMoneagle/Swann composite; aged,
  broken, **survives trilogy**; sketch-wall witness.
- **Podcast oracle (working):** **Danny Kale** — not Rogan; an in-world *long-form* host whose audience
  treats his guests' claims as settled fact. Book V Egypt's Ben Van Kerk exists in a *different* register;
  Kale is the darker mirror — gospel without rigour.

## The hook (act-one shape)

Mara is scraping by as a compliance consultant — her name on a quiet industry blacklist — when a
session tape arrives with her employee number spoken in a voice that died in 1998. The viewers say
the ice door is opening on a schedule tied to solar quiet. Someone is still running STAR GATE, under
a new name. She has forty days of clearance left in an old badge cache. She goes.

## The promise to the reader

A propulsive thriller with **Henry Sugar wonder at the core** — the Dahl feeling when the card turns
over and the world gets larger. Goats absurdity and podcast gospel are the **frame**; the engine is
a skeptic's slow training, one small impossible proof, and a final set-piece where resonance rewrites
stone **without a wink**. The reader finishes enlarged, not merely survived. Source Notes separate
documented program history from invention.
