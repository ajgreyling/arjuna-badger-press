# READER MISSION — *The Men Who Opened the Door* (binding)

> **The Google priming contract.** Inverts *History Before Time*: that series serves readers who have
> **never** heard of David Icke and must not be pushed into paranoia. **This** series serves readers who
> have **never** heard of STAR GATE, Vatican miracle boards, or remote viewing — and should close the
> book **wanting to look it up**, discover the documented record, and return to the fiction **primed**
> in its glory.

---

## The mission in one breath

**Mind-over-matter is not invented here.** Governments ran programs. The Church runs tribunals. Labs
published data. Viewers drew submarines engineers laughed at — until the satellites came back. The novel
is the **on-ramp**; the internet is the **second act**; wonder is the **payoff when both meet**.

---

## Target reader (LOCKED)

- **New to the world** — no STAR GATE lore, no Serpo, no remote-viewing vocabulary required.
- **Curious, not captured** — will Google at 2 a.m., not join a cult.
- **Henry Sugar temperament** — wants to feel the card turn, then learn the yogi was **documented**.
- **Not** the podcast-deep reader (they get Easter eggs); **not** the debunker (honesty is not sneering).

Mara is their proxy: credentialled skeptic who only trusted paper — then learns paper was always the
**smaller** court.

---

## The priming loop (how each book works)

```
0. PREFACE (non-fiction) — remote viewing successes, Vatican protocol, stated AS FACT
1. EPIGRAPH → CHAPTER 1 — fiction; Mara may doubt what reader already knows
2. FICTION plants specific hooks (name, date, institution, sketch detail)
3. READER feels wonder in-scene (Henry Sugar — no footnote in the moment)
4. SOURCE NOTES at end expand citations + sceptics' best case + invented list
5. READER Googles → CIA PDFs, Vatican decrees, transcripts
6. READER returns enlarged — second read primed in glory
```

See [`PREFACE.md`](PREFACE.md) and [`../build/PREFACE.md`](../build/PREFACE.md).

**Rule:** the fiction may invent Mara, Mac, the ice door. It may **not** invent that STAR GATE was
fake, that miracle tribunals are theatre, or that McMoneagle's Typhoon sessions are authorial woo —
when we cite real cases, we cite them **straight** in the back matter.

---

## Craft obligations (binding)

| Obligation | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Plant before preach** | Mac tells the Typhoon story in nested-tale hush **before** Source Notes name it |
| **Specific hooks** | Dates, acronyms, institutions — "September 1979", "Consulta Medica", "Typhoon-class" |
| **Honesty tiers** | Every case file tagged: **documented** / **contested** / **invented** |
| **No clip-bait lies** | Don't claim CIA confirmed Mars cities if we're dramatising — flag contest in notes |
| **Wonder first** | Google bait is **after** the beat lands, never as mid-scene Wikipedia voice |
| **Mara stays skeptical on-page** | Reader skepticism honoured; the **world** and the **notes** carry the real record |

---

## What success looks like

A reader messages: *"I thought you made up the submarine thing. I spent an hour on the CIA reading
room."* Or: *"I didn't know the Vatican had doctors whose job is to disprove miracles — and couldn't."*

That reader reads Book II **primed** — podcast gospel hits harder because they now know real viewers
drew real hardware.
