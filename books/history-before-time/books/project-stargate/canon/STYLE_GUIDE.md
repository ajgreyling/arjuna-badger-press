# STYLE_GUIDE — *The Men Who Opened the Door*

> Binding craft doctrine. The **creative driver** (author lock, 2026-06-07): wonder in the **Roald
> Dahl / Henry Sugar** register — the feeling you had reading *The Wonderful Story of Henry Sugar*
> when the card turns over and you **believe**, for a page, that a bored rich man taught himself to
> see. Conspiracy, goats, and podcast gospel are the **frame**; Dahl wonder is the **engine**.

---

## The Henry Sugar contract (binding)

Henry Sugar begins as a cheat. He stays a cheat a long time. Dahl never mocks the **yogi**, the
**training**, or the **moment it works** — only Henry's motives. When Henry first sees through the
playing card, the prose goes quiet and the reader becomes a child who has just been told the world
is **much larger than advertised**.

This book does the same with remote viewing, resonance, and the door:

| Henry Sugar beat | This book |
|---|---|
| Henry reads Imhrat Khan's account | Mara hears Mac's **nested tale** — a viewer who saw what he shouldn't, told straight |
| Greedy, worldly protagonist | Mara wants **vindication**, not enlightenment — ledger-truth, not godhead |
| Months of boring practice | **Sessions** — cool-down, failure, migraine, nothing, nothing, *click* |
| First small impossible proof | Blind sketch; one detail on tape she couldn't know; the **reverse of the card** |
| Wonder without irony | At the proof beat: **no wink**, no debunk voice, no podcast clip — just breath |
| Gift turned outward | Door opens; witness chosen — Henry's charity ward energy, not superhero landing |

**Rule:** paranoia and absurdity are allowed **between** wonder beats. They are **forbidden during**
the wonder beat.

---

## Voice

- **Close third, Mara's skin** — procedural mind learning to notice somatic truth.
- **Dahl warmth in the narrator's eye** — mischief toward bureaucracy and podcasters; **reverence**
  toward the first time something impossible is true.
- **Nested storytelling:** when Mac or Lena tells how a viewer learned to see, the prose **slows**,
  sentences lengthen, documentary hush — the Isha-the-yogi chapter inside Henry Sugar. Return to
  Mara's dry voice only **after** the nested tale lands.
- **Physical before metaphysical:** heat behind the eyes, sweat on the upper lip, the ache in the
  sacrum, hours of nothing — **then** the card turns over.

---

## Machine-tell taboos (inherited + project-specific)

- **No wink at the magic moment** — the Henry Sugar sin is ironic distance when the card is seen through.
- **No chakra lecture** — seven centres as **sensation sequence**, not infographic.
- **No podcast voice during resonance** — Kale's register is Act II; the door is Act III cathedral.
- **No "she realised" discovery crutch** — she *sees*; the reader sees with her.
- **Caption-after-image** — show the sketch, the tape timestamp, the ice editing; claim follows.

---

## Tone balance (the mix)

```
[ Goats absurdity | X-Files dread | Podcast gospel ]  ← frame (Acts I–II)
                        ↓
              Henry Sugar wonder                      ← engine (proof beats + climax)
                        ↓
              Dispenza body-awe                       ← texture inside wonder (somatic, not sermon)
```

The reader should finish **lighter** than a conspiracy thriller normally allows — enlarged, not merely
survived. Frightened in the corridors; **astonished** at the door.

---

## Comparable prose touchstones

- Roald Dahl, *The Wonderful Story of Henry Sugar* — nested tale, training, first proof, no mockery of magic
- Dahl's documentary hush in the Isha / Imhrat Khan sections
- *Fringe* when wonder lands without technobabble apology
- Early Crichton **physical specificity** before the impossible

**Not:** cynical UFO Twitter, wellness-influencer cadence, or Lovecraft dread that eats the wonder.

## Google priming (craft)

- **Plant:** a name, date, or institution in dialogue (Mac: "September '79, White Sea — they laughed").
- **Land:** wonder beat first — Henry Sugar hush.
- **Point:** Source Notes / chapter end — "Start here: CIA STAR GATE reading room; McMoneagle Typhoon."
- **Never:** mid-scene "as documented on Wikipedia"; never fake a documented case as invented.
- **Skeptic on-page, honest off-page:** Mara doubts; notes say **contested** where fair (leak theories,
  monitor prompting) — earns the reader's trust for Book II.
