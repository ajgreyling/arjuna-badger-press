# CHARACTERS — *The Men Who Opened the Door*

> Provisional names. Composites for living plot roles; real historical figures as texture only.

---

## Mara Kincaid (protagonist)

- **Age:** 36
- **Background:** PhD information science; DLA records & compliance; clearance **revoked**, not quite
  prosecuted
- **Gift:** pattern recognition in **systems** — manifests, cost codes, metadata — not psi
- **Wound:** reported truth upward; institution reported *her*. Fears she is the crank they named her
- **Arc:** ledger-truth → somatic truth → **witness** who chooses broadcast over burial
- **Voice:** dry, procedural, allergic to podcast epistemology until it saves her

---

## Col. Ray "Mac" McAllister (elder viewer — McMoneagle/Swann composite)

- **Age:** 78; body failing, mind sharp
- **Background:** STAR GATE operational viewer; thousands of sessions; one Mars-structure-class hit
  nobody can explain away
- **Function:** mentor; session room; sketch wall; moral weight of "we were weapons"
- **Arc (LOCKED):** **survives trilogy** — broken witness at Book III close; passes resonance chair
  at the door but lives to see her return with the proof object
- **Voice:** clipped military courtesy; poetry only when describing targets

---

## Danny Kale (podcast host — Rogan-sphere, not Rogan)

- **Age:** 52
- **Background:** former local TV; built empire on long-form; genuinely curious, commercially hungry
- **Function:** epistemology made flesh; Act II pressure; clip that becomes evidence
- **Arc:** none heroic — **mirror** for the culture; may help, may exploit
- **Voice:** "I'm not saying it's true, I'm saying it's *weird*"

---

## Dr. Lena Okoye (viewer monitor / scientist)

- **Age:** 44
- **Background:** neuroscientist who ran feedback loops for the program; left; pulled back; family tie to
  a **diocesan miracle tribunal** (cousin on medical consult) — knows Rome's Consulta Medica process
- **Function:** observer effect without lecture; **institutional parallel** — Vatican miracle board;
  **Emoto water crystals** as popular parallel ("millions believe intent writes ice — you think that's
  sillier than a submarine sketch until the sketch matches?")
- **Arc:** from "this is trauma response" to "trauma response and **signal**"
- **Voice:** careful, angry, funny

---

## The Handler (antagonist — name TBD)

- **Background:** civilian cover; program never ended; owns the **warm-sector window**
- **Function:** institutional predator; not cartoon evil — **budget line** evil
- **Methods:** clearance leverage, medical coercion, "retirement" offers
- **Voice:** HR memo diction; lethal politeness
