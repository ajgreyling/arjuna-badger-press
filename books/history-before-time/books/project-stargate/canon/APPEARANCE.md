## Coherence flags
- **FLAG 1 — The Handler's age drifts mid-fifties → mid-forties.** ch-05: "Mid-fifties. A coat too good for the strip-mall ZIP code." ch-17: "Up close he was younger than the voice... Mid-forties, good coat." The ch-17 line ("younger than the voice") reads as if seeing him in person corrects an assumption — but in ch-05 Mara *already met him in person at the coffee shop* and read him as mid-fifties. Two in-person reads, ten story-days apart, give different decades. Recommend reconciling (e.g., make ch-05 "forties" or have ch-17 not frame it as a first close look).
- **FLAG 2 — Stray cross-book name intrusion: "Arin."** ch-18: "Mac couldn't manage the roll-up door. He stood back and let Arin—no, she corrected herself, there was no Arin here, only Dale and his bad shoulder—let Dale haul it up." "Arin" (Arin Ndlela) is a character from the *African Gold / RELIC* cast, not this book. This appears to be a generation bleed-through from the sibling project. It is framed as a self-correction in-text, but there is no in-world reason Mara would think the name "Arin" — she has never met an Arin. Recommend deleting the "Arin—no... there was no Arin here" clause entirely.
- **FLAG 3 — Lead has zero physical self-description.** Across 18 chapters Mara Kincaid (sole POV) is never given hair length, hair colour, eye colour, build, height, or skin/complexion. Only fixed anchors: age 36 and "a stretched cardigan." This is internally consistent (no contradiction) but is a coherence risk for cover art, continuity in later books, and any scene needing her described by another character. Flagging the absence, not a contradiction.
- **FLAG 4 — Mac's "Virginia accent" attribution is potentially confusable.** ch-01 introduces the monitor's voice as having "a Virginia in it"; ch-03 confirms Mac says "I was the Virginia accent." The dead viewer Bo Lindqvist's voice is separately "thin... copied." These are consistent on close read, but the ch-01 prose initially lets a reader assume the Virginia voice might be the viewer's. Not a contradiction — noting as a clarity (not coherence) risk only.

No physical-description contradictions found for build/hair/eyes/carried items among Mac, Lena, Voss, or Dale — their few specified traits (Mac's tremor/cane/gauntness/78; Lena's blunt-cut hair/precise voice/44; the sealed-cup Handler) are consistent wherever repeated.
