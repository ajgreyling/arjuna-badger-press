## BOOK-LEVEL EPIGRAPH (LOCKED)

> *Thou art God.*
>
> — Jubal Harshaw, in Robert A. Heinlein, *Stranger in a Strange Land* (1961)

**Why:** the book's physics — consciousness as participant in reality, not spectator — in four words.
Heinlein's grokking is the ancestor of Dispenza's "you are the observer collapsing the field." Mara
would hate the quote on first read. She earns it on the last page.
