## English

The Prophet Muhammad — peace be upon him — is never depicted, described, or caricatured anywhere in
this book. Not in word, not in image.

This novel was written in respect. Its single aim is to open a door: to offer readers outside Islam a
glimpse of the Qur'an as it is to those who carry it — a beautiful and holy text, recited and beloved,
that places mercy before all else.

The author is not a Muslim and claims no authority over the faith. He came as a guest, listened at the
edge of it, and is certain to have got things wrong. Every error here is his alone, and is meant in
honour, never in offence.
