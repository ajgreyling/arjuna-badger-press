# *The Recitation* — the Jakobus desert-years novel

***History Before Time: The Recitation*** (working title) — the second human, grounded backstory volume
of the Jakobus arc, and the **sibling to *The Silver Thread***: where that book is the Kalahari bush and
the gift of **peace**, this is the Sahara and the Sahel and the gift of **patience** — the seasons
Jakobus spent among Arabic-speaking desert people, learned the language, sat at the edge of the Quran,
and carried out of it the setting-down of fear, **without ever converting.**

## Locked decisions (author's call)

- **Frame: immersion, NOT captivity.** *Six Years a Hostage* (McGown) is the **tonal** touchstone only
  ("the stories are the same"; the recitation as ballast); Jakobus is a **guest, never a hostage.**
- **He never converts.** The book is the Quran entering his syncretic creed as **practical wisdom**, held
  with full respect — the honest outsider who learns and does not claim. SSOT-binding.
- **Setting: Sahara / Sahel** — Bidhan / Ḥassāniyya-Arabic core (Mauritania / western Sahara), the
  mahadra (desert Quranic-school) world; Tuareg/Songhai/Fulani kept distinct.
- **Register: NOT woo.** No ancient aliens, no impossible object, no miracle. The wonder is people, a
  language, and a recited Book.

## Chosen to draft (author said "you choose" — revisit freely)

- **Title:** *The Recitation* (literal meaning of *al-Qur'an*; alternatives *The Litany*, *Where There
  Is Water* held in the seed).
- **Form:** standalone companion volume, seq 0, the **sibling/diptych partner** of *The Silver Thread*.
- **POV:** close-third on Jakobus throughout (matches *The Silver Thread*).
- **Community:** the **Bidhan** of a desert well — Sidi's household, the matriarch Mariem, the boy Brahim;
  the marabout **Cheikh Lemine** and his *maḥẓara*. (ALL names fictional placeholders — sensitivity gate.)
- **Timeline:** the **wandering years after the bush peace** (distinct from the Afghanistan-opium beat
  and the Egypt/Nile leg). Confirm against the SSOT chronology.

## ⛔ Sensitivity-read gate (BINDING before this is anything but a draft)

- **Nothing Quranic, Arabic, or ritual prints without a qualified Muslim sensitivity read.** Every verse
  (sūrah:āyah + translation), every Arabic word, the Litany-of-Fear tattoo calligraphy, every ritual
  detail, and every community/ethnonym is an **unverified placeholder** until confirmed. See the respect
  protocol in [`../../canon/ISLAM_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT.md`](../../canon/ISLAM_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT.md).
- **Never conflate Islam with the militants** who operate in this geography — the faithful are the centre;
  the jihadists are the aberration that preys on these communities.
