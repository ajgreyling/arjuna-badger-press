# *The Straight Darkness* — the Longyou Jakobus novel

***History Before Time: The Straight Darkness*** — a **Jakobus-led wonder-of-place** volume: the Longyou
Grottoes of Zhejiang — twenty-four massive man-made caves with improbably straight walls, no quarry
debris, and **no historical record of who carved them or why**. Binding seed:
[`canon/SEED_STORY.md`](canon/SEED_STORY.md); site floor:
[`canon/PLACE_RESEARCH.md`](canon/PLACE_RESEARCH.md). Jakobus SSOT:
[`../../canon/characters/JAKOBUS_SWART.md`](../../canon/characters/JAKOBUS_SWART.md).

## Locked decisions (author seed — revisit freely)

- **Register: NOT woo.** No ancient aliens, no lost super-civilisation. The marvel is **checkable
  engineering** — the straightness, the volume removed, the absence of slag and spoil — held honestly
  against what archaeology *doesn't* know. The "hole" stays open; the wonder survives the not-knowing.
- **POV:** close third on Jakobus throughout. This is the book where his **made-thing read** (*what is
  it for?*) is load-bearing — and may fail to close (rhyme with Why Files *Yonaguni* / *Voynich* limit,
  but Jakobus-led, not crew-led).
- **Spine:** commission or detour into Quzhou prefecture → first chamber → the straight-wall tell → the
  missing history → local custodian/archaeologist who out-reads him → the function hypothesis (storage?
  quarry? ritual? — **never confirmed in narration**) → leave with the darkness in his ears.
- **Scale:** target ~25–35k (Jakobus-Book register).
