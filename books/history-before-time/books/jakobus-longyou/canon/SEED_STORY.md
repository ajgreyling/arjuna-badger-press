## The one-line

Twenty-four chambers carved **straight** out of solid rock — millions of cubic metres removed, **no
quarry debris**, no slag heap, no paper trail — and a fixer whose whole gift is reading *what a made
thing is for* finds a wonder that will not give up even its maker.

## Premise

Jakobus is in China on other business (a contact, a border, a favour — iceberg; not unpacked). A detour
into Quzhou prefecture: local officials have opened a **tourist path** through one of the Longyou
caves; the rest are gated. The official story is cautious — **discovered in 1992** when a farmer drained
a fish pond; caves numbered 1–24+; estimated **more than 1,000,000 m³** of rock removed; walls show
parallel tool marks; **no historical document** names them; hypotheses include quarry, storage, shelter,
ritual — **none proven**.

The book runs the **honest anomaly** move (Why Files-adjacent, but Jakobus-led and without the crew):

1. **The official story, straight** — what archaeology admits it knows.
2. **The potato** — how hard it is to remove that much stone that cleanly, even today.
3. **The hole** — the missing history (not aliens — the *absence* is the marvel).
4. **The wink** — the function stays open; the reader sits in the maybe.
5. **What is it for** — Jakobus's question; a **grounded function hypothesis** from the local expert,
   offered and not confirmed by narration.

## Jakobus here (lead — the made-thing read at its limit)

His gift is load-bearing and may **fail to close** — like Yonaguni in the Why Files line, but here he is
the POV, so the limit is **interior**: a man who always finds the use finds a chamber that keeps its
secret. He accepts not-knowing without turning it into woo.

The local archaeologist or custodian **out-reads him** on the cultural frame — what it means to the
region that the caves exist without a story; what it means that tourism arrived before certainty.

## The checkable wonder (witnessable; guardrail)

- Discovery story (1992, fish-pond drainage) · cave dimensions (varies by chamber; Cave 1 ~2,800 m² floor,
  ~30 m height — **verify in PLACE_RESEARCH**) · parallel wall marks · absent spoil heap · siltstone
  geology · current tourism/custody status. *(Full sourcing: PLACE_RESEARCH.md.)*

## Guardrails

1. **No woo** — no lost Han dynasty super-tech, no aliens. The marvel is **engineering + missing record**.
2. **Anti-poison pillar** — if ancient Chinese makers are invoked, their genius is real; living custodians
   out-read the outsider.
3. **The maybe stays open in narration** — hypotheses dramatised through characters, never authorial verdict.
4. **Sensitivity read** — local archaeologists/community; tourism ethics.
