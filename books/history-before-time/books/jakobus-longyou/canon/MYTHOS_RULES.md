# MYTHOS_RULES — *The Straight Darkness*

## Rule 1 — Engineering is real; record is absent

The marvel is **volume + precision + missing history** — not aliens or Han super-tech.

## Rule 2 — The maybe stays open

Hypotheses dramatised through characters; **no authorial verdict** on function.

## Rule 3 — Anti-poison pillar

If ancient Chinese makers invoked, genius is real; Dr. Lin out-reads outsider.

## Rule 4 — No fringe-as-narrator

Do not let ancient-aliens logic drive plot.

## Rule 5 — Artifact leads

Tool marks, absent spoil, dimensions — before wonder.

## Rule 6 — ch-99 honesty

Real / debated / invented table mandatory.
