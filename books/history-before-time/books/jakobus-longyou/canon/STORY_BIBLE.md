# STORY_BIBLE — *The Straight Darkness*

> Jakobus-led wonder-of-place. The anomaly is **engineering + missing record**, not super-tech.

## Premise

Jakobus is in China on other business (iceberg). A detour to Quzhou: officials have opened a tourist
path through one Longyou cave; the rest are gated. Twenty-four chambers carved **straight** from
solid rock — millions of cubic metres gone, **no spoil heap, no paper trail**. His gift for reading
made things hits a wall: the function stays open.

## Theme

Some wonders **withhold their maker** — the honest response is witness and maybe, not closure.

## Tone

Literary travel-adventure; Crichton-grade engineering curiosity; Why-Files-adjacent honesty without
the crew. Propulsive but the ending holds the gap.

## POV

Close third Jakobus only. ~16 chapters + ch-99. The maybe stays open.

## Promise

Stand in the straight darkness; leave with the right question (*what is it for?*) unanswered but
not cheapened.
