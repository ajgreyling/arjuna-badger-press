## Notes for the drafting threads

- **Living-author rule is absolute** (STYLE_GUIDE §"The hard rule"): paraphrase + attribute; quote only
  brief aphorisms (Procrustes is the safe well), never passages. *The line* beat = one short attributed
  aphorism OR a marked `plainly:` restatement + "go read it in <book>."
- **State his case at full strength before any pushback.** The guest is generous first.
- **`Where the smoke goes`** carries the honest critique (the abrasiveness, the empirical disputes,
  the over-reach) — mark it as contested, do not flatten the work into either hagiography or takedown.
- **`Plainly`** is where the book lives: land each idea on a working reader's ground (building under
  uncertainty, a venture, a press, a life) — once, cleanly, never as a lecture.
- The drafted three (proem, 01, 05) are the **voice exemplars**. Match their register before drafting 02–11.
- Wired into `site/build.py` as **SERIAL** (read-online, no downloads) until the full eleven are drafted,
  cover ≥500 KB is rendered, and the author has proofread. Then promote to `PUBLISHED`.
