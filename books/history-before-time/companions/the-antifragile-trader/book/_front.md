# About this book {.unnumbered}

*The Antifragile Trader* is the second visit to a fire I have sat at before. Its sibling, *The
Antifragile Reader*, retold Nassim Nicholas Taleb's *Incerto* plainly, so a reader who loved one
volume could hold all five. This book returns to the same fire carrying instruments. Every essay in
it pairs with a live, runnable exhibit on the lucid.rodeo Incerto wing
(https://lucid.rodeo/incerto/), and every essay ends the same way: **Run it** — the exhibit's
address, the one number you can check with your own hands, and an honest sentence about what the
instrument cannot say. The book sends you to run the number; the wing sends you back to read the
why. It is written for the reader who trades, or builds anything exposed to market risk, and wants
the mathematics in their hands and not just in their ears.

**A living author, in copyright — and a rule stricter than before.** This is a companion to a
writer who is alive, working, and entirely capable of reading it. In the *Reader* I allowed myself
a brief attributed aphorism where fair use permitted. Here I allow myself none. **His ideas are
re-derived and attributed; his prose is not reproduced, and no words of his appear inside quotation
marks anywhere in this book.** The thinking is his, named to the book it lives in; the phrasing is
mine, and I say so. Where you read **`plainly:`**, that is my reach, owned as mine. This book exists
to send you *to* him, never to stand in front of him.

**Structure, never advice — the second rule, equal in rank to the first.** I am not a licensed
financial advisor. I am not a licensed anything, in any jurisdiction, and this book gives no
personalised advice of any kind. It describes payoff shapes, estimators, and arithmetic. It never
recommends a position, an allocation, a product, or an action with your money, and no imperative in
it points at your wallet. This is also the author's own standing practice — he does not hand out
personalised investment advice, and a companion that did would betray the work it honours. If a
sentence here ever seems to tell you what to do with money, read it again: it is a description of a
shape, because that is all it is licensed to be.

**Three voices, always separate.** **(a)** What Taleb argues — stated at its strongest, attributed
to the book it lives in. **(b)** Where thoughtful people push back — marked as contested, never
smuggled in as settled. **(c)** My own plain gloss, always marked **`plainly:`**, so you never
mistake my paraphrase for his claim.

**Every number has a home.** The figures in these pages come from the paired wing pages themselves,
read and re-checked before each essay shipped, or from arithmetic simple enough to rebuild with a
pencil — and each essay tells you which. A number I merely remembered does not appear. And one
honesty carried through the whole book: a measured tail is the past, not a forecast.
