# STYLE_GUIDE — *The Antifragile Trader*

*A guest-at-the-fire companion to Nassim Nicholas Taleb's* Incerto*, for the reader who trades or
builds under market risk.*
*History Before Time · Companions. Sibling to* The Antifragile Reader.

---

## What this book is

The second visit to the same fire. *The Antifragile Reader* (complete, eleven essays, published)
carried the whole *Incerto* in one warm read for the reader who loved one volume and half-remembered
the rest. This book is its sibling, with one new premise: **the guest returns carrying instruments.**

Every essay pairs with a live, verified, runnable exhibit on the lucid.rodeo Incerto wing
(https://lucid.rodeo/incerto/). The Reader answers *what does he argue?* The Trader answers *what
does the arithmetic do when I run it myself?* It is written for the reader who trades, or builds
anything exposed to market risk, and wants Taleb's mathematics in their hands and not just in their
ears. Same guest, same manners, same fire — now with a bench of instruments by the woodpile.

## Inherited from the Reader — binding by reference

The base document is
[`../../the-antifragile-reader/canon/STYLE_GUIDE.md`](../../the-antifragile-reader/canon/STYLE_GUIDE.md).
Everything in it binds here unless a delta below overrides it. In particular, these carry over whole:

- **The guest-at-the-fire model.** A reverent, independent companion by an outsider who claims no
  authority over the work and exists to send the reader to the source.
- **The living-author hard rule.** Taleb is alive and the *Incerto* is in copyright. His ideas are
  paraphrased and attributed by name and book; his prose is never reproduced. (This book tightens
  the quotation clause further — see delta 2.)
- **The three voices, visibly separate.** (a) What Taleb argues, at full strength, attributed to the
  book it lives in. (b) Where thoughtful people push back, marked contested. (c) Our own gloss,
  always marked **`plainly:`** and owned as ours.
- **Voice and register.** Warm, plain, unhurried, first person, humble, specific. No professor, no
  hype. South African / builder's ground where it earns the gloss.
- **De-LLM taboos** per `docs/craft/LLM_TELLS.md`: no reflex tricolons, no "it's important to note,"
  no delve/tapestry/testament, no significance-narration. Thin the seams.

## The deltas — what is new or stricter here

### 1. The fifth beat — "Run it"

The Reader's four beats become five:

```
# <Essay N> — <The idea, named warmly>

### The fire
   What Taleb actually argues — voice (a). Where it lives in the Incerto. The strongest case.

### Where the smoke goes
   Voice (b). The honest pushback, the limits. Marked contested, never flattened.

### Plainly
   Voice (c). Our gloss, marked plainly:, landing the idea on the working reader's ground.
   In this book that ground is often a position, a drawdown, a payoff — described, never prescribed.

### The line
   The marked plainly: restatement of the essay's one idea + a pointer to the source book.
   Never a quotation. (See delta 2.)

### Run it
   One short paragraph. Name the paired exhibit by its full URL
   (https://lucid.rodeo/incerto/<slug>/). Give the one number the reader can check with their own
   hands. Close with one honest sentence on what the instrument cannot say — a measured tail is
   the past, not a forecast.
```

"Run it" is where this book differs from every summary of Taleb ever written: the reader leaves each
essay with a number they can go verify, not a feeling they can go repeat.

### 2. No quotes at all — stricter than the Reader

The Reader permitted brief attributed aphorisms under fair use. **The Trader quotes nothing.** Zero
quotation marks around any living author's words, anywhere in the book — not in The line, not in an
epigraph, not in passing. The line beat is always the marked `plainly:` restatement plus a pointer
to the source book.

Why stricter here: this book stands on his professional ground — markets, risk, payoffs — where a
borrowed sentence would read as borrowed authority. And the Reader already proved the discipline
costs nothing: the `plainly:` restatement carries the beat. His prose stays his, entirely.

### 3. STRUCTURE, NEVER ADVICE — the second hard rule

Equal in rank to the living-author rule. The book describes **payoff shapes, estimators, and
arithmetic**. It never recommends a position, an allocation, a product, or an action with the
reader's money. No imperative in this book ever points at the reader's wallet. "Run the number" is
always allowed; "buy the option" is never written, in any phrasing, at any distance.

Two reasons, both load-bearing:

- **The author's own standing line.** Taleb's public practice is to give no personalised investment
  advice. A companion that handed out what the source refuses would betray the work it honours.
- **The harm gate.** Before any sentence ships, ask: *can the reader lose money by doing what this
  sentence says?* If yes, refuse the sentence. Rewrite it as structure — what shape the payoff has —
  not action — what the reader should do. A sentence that fails the gate twice gets cut, not
  softened.

The front matter says it plainly: the author is not a licensed anything, and the book gives no
personalised advice of any kind.

### 4. The numbers law

Every number in the prose comes from exactly one of two places:

1. **The paired wing page** — read at drafting time, its exact figures used as the page renders
   them, re-checked before the essay ships. Never from memory of what the page probably says.
2. **Classroom-derivable arithmetic** — sums a reader can rebuild with a pencil (a coin-flip
   expectation, a compounding sequence), shown or pointed to so they can.

Every number is sourced in the sentence that carries it. A number with no home is a defect, not a
flourish. This is the wing's discipline imported into prose: verified, or absent.

### 5. The braid law

The book and the wing are one instrument braided from two strands. **The book sends readers to run
the number; the wing sends runners back to read the why.** Every essay names its exhibit; every
exhibit earns its essay. An essay does not ship until its paired exhibit is live and verified —
if the instrument is down or unbuilt, the essay waits. The Run it beat is the knot that ties each
pair.

## The mission fit

Same shelf, same job, one turn tighter: *send the reader to the source, warmer and braver than they
came* — and now with hands that have done the arithmetic. The proof is qualitative twice over: a
reader finishes an essay, runs the number, watches it move, and goes back to the book that named it.
