# OUTLINE — *The Subtracted Mountain* (12 chapters)

> Non-fiction. Every chapter: official story straight → the checkable numbers → the honest crack
> → the wonder lands last. All facts trace to `SITE_DOSSIER.md`. Target ~50–60k words,
> ~4–5k per chapter.

---

## Part I — The Thing Itself

### Ch 1 — The Mountain That Isn't There
Arrival, written from above — because that is how the temple was made and how you first see it.
You stand on the cliff lip where the first chisel landed and look *down* into a courtyard that is
an absence: everything you see was left behind; everything you don't see was carried away. The
one-line seed, planted: *most buildings are addition; this one is subtraction — 200,000 tonnes of
it — and subtraction forgives nothing.* The book's contract: no aliens needed; the true story is
stranger.

### Ch 2 — Trap Rock
The Deccan Traps: what basalt is, why it is here (the ~66 Ma flood eruptions), what it does to an
iron chisel. The Charanandri hills, the Elaganga stream, the monsoon. Geology as the first
protagonist — the stone chose the method as much as the makers did.

### Ch 3 — The Three-Faith Cliff
Ellora before and around Cave 16: thirty-four caves, two kilometres, four hundred years. Buddhist
1–12, Hindu 13–29, Jain 30–34 — three religions cutting into one mountain without erasing each
other. The trade-route economics of patronage. The chapter's quiet thesis: Kailasa is the loudest
sentence in a long, plural conversation, not a monologue.

## Part II — The Making

### Ch 4 — Krishnaraja
The Rashtrakutas: Dantidurga's coup over the Chalukyas, Krishna I's consolidation. The Baroda
copper plate and its astonished gods. The crack, staged honestly: **no dedicatory inscription at
the temple** — one reign or four? The book does not settle what scholarship has not.

### Ch 5 — Cut Down, Not Up
The method chapter. Cut-in vs cut-out; the U-trench; why top-down means **no scaffolding for the
main mass, no repairs, no second draft**. Plumb lines, strings, setting-out; drainage channels cut
*concurrently* — the builders engineered the monsoon before they finished the god's house. The
Gita-shaped idea the fiction sibling runs on, stated here as craft history: the form is already in
the rock; the work is removing what is not it.

### Ch 6 — The Arithmetic
Do the sums on the page. ~3 million cu ft; ~4 cu ft per worker-day; 250–400 workers; Dhavalikar's
~5.5 years for the core, 18–20 for the whole. Set against the legend (Kokasa; the one-week vow) —
with affection, not a sneer. The Banning move: the honest arithmetic is the astonishment; nothing
impossible is required, and that is the point.

### Ch 7 — Masterpieces Have Parents
The lineage chapter. Virupaksha at Pattadakal; Pallava Kailasanatha at Kanchipuram; how the
Rashtrakutas hired, borrowed, and scaled. Kills the "no precedent, therefore mystery" trope by
showing the precedent — then shows the order-of-magnitude leap that remains genuinely hard to
explain.

### Ch 8 — The Mountain of the Story
Iconography. Ravananugraha as the temple's thesis statement; the epic friezes; the lingam and the
axis; the vanished white plaster and painted ceilings — the temple once *dressed as* the snowy
Kailasa. The building is a myth you can walk through, and the myth is about force losing to
stillness.

## Part III — The Long Custody

### Ch 9 — Wounds and Weather
Aurangzeb's tradition-recorded attempt and the basalt's verdict. Then the slower enemies: seepage,
weathering, footfall. 1904 Act; grouting and grills; the 1992–2002 Japanese-funded drainage
project; what it means that the 8th-century drains still do most of the work.

### Ch 10 — The Scans
The news chapter. The 2024 ASI laser-scanning programme; the 2025 UNESCO/Ministry of Tourism
LiDAR campaign on Cave 16; GSI geophysics. What a millimetre-scale digital twin actually changes:
measured symmetry instead of asserted symmetry, tool-mark forensics, excavation-sequence
reconstruction, a weathering baseline. **Then the viral tape: the "Russian team" scans.** Trace
the claim, play it straight, set it beside the verified record, show the gap — and show the honest
residue that survives (real unentered voids, the under-explored drainage galleries). The fish
wink; nobody preaches. (SITE_DOSSIER §5 is binding here.)

### Ch 11 — What the Rock Still Holds
The open questions, kept open: the chronology; **where the 200,000 tonnes went**; the unentered
voids; the planning problem — what kind of mind cuts a finished building downward. The scans as
the next chapter of a 1,200-year custodianship rather than the end of the mystery.

### Ch 12 — Standing in the Courtyard
Close on the ground, at dawn, inside the U. The living shrine; the priests; Verul village; the
practicalities (the tourism mission — how to go, when to go, Ajanta as the pairing). Last image
returns to Ch 1 inverted: you leave looking *up* at the scarp, at the drill marks of people who
finished the mountain's sentence. The wonder, larger than when you arrived.
