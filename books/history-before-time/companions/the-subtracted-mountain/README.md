# The Subtracted Mountain

Non-fiction companion on the **Kailasa temple (Cave 16, Ellora)** — the world's largest monolithic
structure, carved top-down from a single basalt cliff in the 8th century CE.

## What this is

- **Series home:** *History Before Time* companions (sits beside *The Antifragile Reader*,
  *The Walls of Uruk*, etc.)
- **Fiction sibling:** [`../../books/book3-india-deccan/`](../../books/book3-india-deccan/) —
  *The Temple in the Rock* (HBT Book III) is the novel set at Ellora; this book is the sourced,
  non-fiction ground it stands on. Neither requires the other.
- **Register:** the estate's site-book voice — official story played dead straight, checkable
  numbers shown, real-vs-claimed kept binding, the wonder allowed to land *after* the facts.
