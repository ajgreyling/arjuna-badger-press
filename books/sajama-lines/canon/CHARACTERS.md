## 1. THE ND LEAD — BASHIR (the TONGUE) — *the spine of this book*

**The layer he reads: the WORD ITSELF.** *Sawubona*-with-the-mouth, deep — he reaches a person in their *own* language and the room half-disarms.

- **Profile / wiring (HOW HE SEES, not a wound):** a **hyperpolyglot**, stateless — he survived childhood and a string of borders by **switching tongues**, becoming whoever the next checkpoint needed to hear. Possibly on the spectrum; the two read together in him as a single wiring: an ear that *cannot not* parse a language's shape — grammar, register, the seam where a dialect changes — and a deep, sideways warmth that arrives through *words* before it arrives through eye contact. He is at home in every language and native to none; the polyglot's specific loneliness (belongs nowhere fully) is real and shown, never milked.
- **His gift, here (the book's hinge):** he reaches the **Aymara walkers in their own language** — not tourist-phrasebook Aymara but the real thing, the courtesy that **half-opens a closed door** (a door that *opens*, never a code that *cracks*). And what he *hears*, that no map could show, is the book's hinge: **the lines are sentences you walk, not maps you read.** The straightness isn't an engineering feat held against the terrain — it is **what the language lays down on the ground when it's walked.** The naming, the order, the saying-while-going holds the bearing.
- **The cost (binding, never sanitised):** he cannot stop translating — the menu, the overheard argument, the sign, the silence — and the loneliness of always *carrying* a conversation and never just *being* in one. His relief at a language's clean grammar after the noise of a crowded room is real and specific; render it once, from the inside.
- **HIS ARC (the book's hardest beat for him — and the firewall, lived in one person):** the polyglot who reaches everyone has to learn **when *not* to reach all the way in.** Some of what he hears walking with the *yatiri* is **not his to render into English**, not his to carry out — a sentence that *only lives in the walking* and would be *stolen* by translation. The man whose whole gift and whole worth is *reaching all the way in* must learn the one place where reaching all the way in is a **theft.** *(This rhymes Mara's "ask before you shoot": his version is "some sentences you don't translate.")* His want — to finally *belong* to a conversation rather than ferry it — is answered not by being let all the way in, but by being trusted enough to be told **where to stop.**
- ⚠ **Lived-experience veto** (autism / refugee experience) **and Aymara veto** on every word of the language he speaks on the page.

## 2. NAILA (the SYSTEM) — the potato-prover

**The layer she reads: PATTERN, STRUCTURE, the measure.** (Established crew; Priya-lineage, distinct from Priya.)

- **Profile / wiring:** autistic, pattern-cognition. Direct to the point of seeming cold; the warmth is real and shown sideways (the good clean quiet in her chest when the numbers line up — the thing others call *cold* when they see it on her face). Drowns in crowds with no exits — rendered with dignity, the crew covering without making her a problem to manage.
- **Her gift, here (the potato engine):** she **measures the impossible straightness** — the theodolite-and-cold-eye function — and establishes, honestly and on the page, **how hard it is** to hold a line arrow-straight over broken ground for ~20 km with **no instrument and no vantage.** Her decisive gift is **refusing the false binary:** it is neither "trivial footpaths" nor "impossible / therefore woo" — the straightness is *real, measurable, and humanly achieved,* and **that is stranger than either.** She runs the arithmetic that kills the over-reach (the "no one could do this" number) *and* the arithmetic that kills the sneer (the daylight potato). **She proves the residue; she never reaches past it.**
- **The mirror to remember:** she is the crew's Edie Vane (Book 1's local). Where this book lets her shine is the *one place her instrument runs out*: she can measure that the bearing is held, and how impossibly well — but **measurement cannot tell her *why*, and the why is not in the numbers.** Her honesty about that limit is part of the firewall.
- ⚠ Lived-experience veto (autism). Cost and warmth both shown.

## 3. MARA (the LENS) — the camera the line keeps asking to lower

**The layer she reads: WHAT GETS SEEN, and at whose cost.** *(Recurring franchise member — every book. NOT the ND constant; keep PTSD distinct from neurodiversity.)*

- **Profile / wound (her engine):** ex-conflict photographer, a name made on images of horror, PTSD rendered from the inside, never as thriller-spice. Her wound is **beauty as absolution** — photographing *beautiful* people after a career photographing the worst feels like redemption; the trap is that the people she frames are nobody's absolution and don't exist to heal her. Competent and steady in a crisis *because* of what she's seen; undone, quietly, by ordinary beauty.
- **Her load, here (Sajama punishes the overhead view):** the seductive frame at this site is **the aerial shot** — the god's-eye image that made Nazca a tourist mystery and would do the same to Sajama, flattening a **walked pilgrimage** into a **drone spectacle.** Her craft is *which frame turns a real thing into a headline*, and here the most beautiful, most sellable, most thieving image is the one from above. **Her hardest discipline is putting the drone down** — refusing the single image that would make her name and steal the most. The consent-to-photograph beat with the Aymara community is **live and recurring**, not a one-off.
- **Her arc, here:** *witness without consuming; lower the camera; ask, and mean it, before you shoot* — applied to a **living practice.** The frame she *doesn't* take rhymes the sentence Bashir *doesn't* translate: **the image you don't take, the words you don't carry out.** The one frame she *does* make is honest — the line itself, or a held bearing, with **nothing in it that shames a single walker** and nothing that lifts the meaning out of the feet.
- ⚠ Two consultants: PTSD lived-experience reader (never spectacle); the in-culture authority on how this community consents to being photographed.

## 4. THE WIDER CREW DRAW (light; cover-each-other's-blind-spots warmth)

The found-family is **established** (assembled in the Göbekli book); lean on the warmth. Field a tight draw — the spine is Bashir / Naila / Mara; the others appear as the ensemble needs, never crowding the firewall:

- **Frik (the LIE — Tourette's).** Involuntary, exquisitely-timed blurted truth — the anti-paintover made flesh. **His Sajama job is to name the adversary's lie** — the smooth "it's just footpaths" or its mirror "it belongs to everyone" — at the exact moment it can't be taken back, in front of everyone. He **never** punctures the *yatiri* or the practice; his blurt is aimed at the *telling-thieves*, gently and devastatingly. His tics spike with the plot's tension; the crew never so much as glances when one rides through. ⚠ Lived-experience veto; **no "Tourette's = swearing for laughs."**
- **Niamh (the UNSPOKEN — Deaf face-reader), with her interpreter.** Reads the truth on the body when the mouth lies — *the unheard recognising the unheard.* Light presence here, but load-bearing in **one beat**: she reads the *withholding* — sees, off the body, the difference between *the yatiri does not know* and *the yatiri will not say*, and is the one who teaches the crew (without a word out loud) that the second is not a wall to push but a door that is **hers to keep shut.** Niamh **honours the withholding** rather than decoding it — the firewall in the one crew member who has spent her life being talked over and refuses to do it to anyone.
- **The MONEY / fixer (optional, light).** If fielded, reads the adversary's grift from the inside — *who profits from owning the telling, and how the concession money really moves* — and gets the crew funded, fed, and unblocked. In recovery from having done it for the wrong side; restitution he'll never call restitution.

> **The ensemble thesis (the warmth that makes it a show, not a thesis):** Frik blurts the lie / Naila proves it in the measure / Niamh saw the withholding on the body / Bashir heard the truth in the walkers' own words / the fixer knows who's paying / Mara knows which frame would steal it and lowers the camera / Jakobus reads the made thing and gets them to where it can't be buried. **No one of them could do it alone** — and *that* is why the local completing the read is no insult to the crew: the read is **always a relay, and the last, decisive hand is the local's.** The comedy is real and warm (a polyglot who can't stop translating the menu, a literalist counting the cones) — never at the neurology's expense.

## 5. THE LOCAL — THE *YATIRI* (the firewall, the book's spine) — *she out-reads them all; the answer is hers*

**An Aymara line-walker / *yatiri* (ritual specialist) who walks the lines as the living pilgrimage they are.** Working name pending Aymara consultants — **do not lock a name without the sensitivity read.**

- **What she reads (out-reads the whole crew):** the crew read *layers* — the measure (Naila), the tongue (Bashir), the made thing (Jakobus), the image (Mara). **She reads the *practice*** — and the practice is **not a code the crew can crack from the outside.** She out-reads them on **the one thing that matters most: what the lines are *for*** — and that answer is **hers to give or withhold, on her own terms, in her own time, or not at all.**
- **Who she is NOT:** she is **not a guide hired to explain her sacred ground to tourists**, and **not** a riddle-keeper waiting to be solved. She decides what the crew are ready to be told and what they are not. She owes the outside nothing. Her relationship to the lines is complete without the crew, the survey, or the reader.
- **Her stake (the chase is hers):** what the adversary threatens is **her practice and her people's right to author what the lines are.** She is not a victim to be rescued by clever outsiders; she is the one holding the telling, and the crew's job is to help her **hold it on her own terms** and then get out of the frame. **The win is hers.**
- **Her turn (the structural hinge):** at the moment that matters she **hands the crew the reading — or as much of it as she chooses** — that the line is *a sentence walked, not a picture seen*; that the straightness is the fossil of the saying; that it climbs the hill because **wavering would break the sentence.** She extends the *defensible archaeology* (the ceque-like radial sightlines scaled across a district) **into living meaning** — a frame, never a verdict. And she withholds the rest, and **that withholding is honoured, not breached.** The narrator **never declares it proven.**
- ⚠ **Aymara sensitivity consultants have a REAL VETO** on her portrayal, the walking, the *yatiri* role, the ritual, and the language — the FLOOR (Bible §9). She is rendered with full dignity and full sovereignty; nothing ceremonial is spectacle.

## 6. JAKOBUS — THE ROAD (no POV; defers; reads the made thing into the local's hand)

⭐ **SSOT:** `../../../../history-before-time/canon/characters/JAKOBUS_SWART.md`. Do not re-derive. **The Beast:** `../../../../history-before-time/canon/characters/THE_BEAST.md`.

- **The road, never the lead.** No POV; seen only from the outside; **off home ground the entire book** (the humility engine — far from his African mastery on the Altiplano, and that is the point). Brother/uncle register with the crew. **He gathered this crew** and travels with or ahead of them — the proof of the keel.
- **He reads the made thing — and finds its proper limit here.** His gift: look at a *constructed* thing and read *not who, not is-it-real, but **what is it for.*** Here he is the one who first *sees* that the line climbing the ridge rather than skirting it is **intent, not accident** — a bearing held *on purpose*, a thing made to be **walked, not viewed.** But on this ground his gift meets its **proper humility:** he can read that the line *means* something and *how* it is meant to be used — but **what it means is not his to say.** He reads the made thing **into the *yatiri*'s waiting hand, and defers.** His *Sawubona* (the courtesy handful of Aymara) is genuine and **earns him nothing he hasn't earned** — it opens the *door*, never the *answer.*
- **His tells, carried:** the shades / uniform-rule; the no-gun rule; *Sawubona*; the closed border he'll open; the field kit (the cayenne tin, the always-clean hanky, the Beast's **weeping water bag — weeping at altitude**, the recurring Altiplano beat); music-as-metronome; the giving doctrine; coins-for-knives; **Stoffel the honey-badger as carried legend.** **Honour the tattoo-acquisition continuity — no ink unearned.**
- **The structural reason he can't be the lead:** the keel is *the marginalised are the experts.* A white ex-operator at the centre breaks it. The crew and the local hold the camera; Jakobus defers, carries, stands guard — and reads the made thing into the local's hand.

## 7. THE ADVERSARY — clean-handed; a mortal want; never a moustache

**The recurring *kind* (not necessarily one outfit):** a **heritage-laundering foundation / "geoglyph tourism" developer / survey concern / collector's cut-out** moving to **map, fence, monetise, or "protect"** the largest drawing on Earth **before the Aymara communities and the record can hold it on their own terms.** Working entity name in the line's convention (place/word + Heritage/Trust): e.g. **"the [Altiplano] Heritage Trust"** or a development concern — pick per draft; keep it bland and plausible.

- **The mortal want (the engine — masterpiece-campaign doctrine):** the adversary does **not** want to *destroy* the lines. They want to **own the telling of them**, because the *telling* — the brand, the dossier, the concession, the managed view — is what's worth money and prestige. They are trying to do to the lines exactly what Mara's worst aerial photograph would do: **lift the meaning out of the feet that make it.** **The adversary is the institutional version of the overhead view.**
- **They weaponise the official story** (the rhyme with the whole line): the cover is **"it's just footpaths"** *or* its mirror **"it's a world wonder that belongs to everyone"** — either framing strips the **living, owned practice** out of the line and turns it into an **asset** (a UNESCO-style dossier someone else authors; a drone-tour concession; a viewing platform that ends the walking).
- **The face of it — a fallible human, never a cabal.** Give the lead adversary clean hands and a real, sympathetic want — likely a person who genuinely believes they are *saving* the lines (the Göbekli-Reyes register: "his face wasn't a villain's face... he genuinely thought the careful hands were his"). He believes the telling is his to author *for everyone's good.* He is wrong in the exact way the book is about — and he is **never** sneered at.
- **The optional series whisper rides on him or near him:** the **already-filed overhead survey** — *someone got here first, mapped from above what was never meant to be read from the air.* Plant it through the adversary's materials; **never confirm it's one hand.** The fish wink, at season scale.
