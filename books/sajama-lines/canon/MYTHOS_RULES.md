# *The Long Straight* — MYTHOS RULES

> The binding rules that keep this book inside the line. Not a magic system — *the opposite.* The "mythos" of THE WHY FILES is a discipline: **the residue is real; the interpretation stays open; the maybe is never resolved in narration.** This book carries the line's **sharpest §3 firewall.** Fact-floor: `SITE_DOSSIER.md` (binding). Frame: `../../../canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md`.

---

## 1. THE CHECKABLE-THING-IS-REAL RULE (binding)

Every "hole" the book leans on is a **cited, real-world, genuinely-unresolved or strange fact** — checkable in the open record. The residue is real; the *interpretation* stays open. For this book the checkable things are:

- **The straightness.** Lines held **dead-straight over hills and ravines, up to ~20 km each, ~1–3 m wide, with no central authority, no surveying instruments, and no elevated vantage.** The published anchor: *neither the purpose nor how the makers achieved such precision is completely understood.* **This is THE hole.** Measure it cold (Naila). Never reach past it.
- **The scale.** **~16,000 km of line across ~22,525 km² — ~15× Nazca — very likely the largest single archaeological artwork on Earth**, and one of the least studied. Do the arithmetic on-page (~3× the width of the continental US, drawn by foot).
- **The continuity.** **Built and re-walked for 3,000+ years — ancient when the Inca arrived, still walked in Aymara ritual today.** Maintained *by the walking*; stop and it greens over.
- **The documentation hooks.** **Tony Morrison (1970s)** first brought them to outside attention; UPenn's **Tierra Sajama** project surveyed them. Any figure/quote beyond these is **NEEDS SOURCE** and may not be load-bearing until corroborated (≥2 sources).

**The rule of the cold eye:** the residue is the **gap between an instrument-less, vantage-less, uncommanded method and the arrow-straight result** — *not* "therefore something impossible." A character may over-reach on it (a flaw to dramatise); **the narrator never does.**

---

## 2. THE POTATO ENGINE (the per-chapter beat)

Most chapters carry a **potato** — the moment the tidy "easy" story visibly strains under the weight of the real thing. For this book the potato is specific and physical: the brightest people, with the best modern kit, a **vantage**, a plan, and **daylight**, try to lay a survey-grade straight line over a ridge — and make a potato. The makers had **no instrument, no vantage**, built **piecemeal across millennia** with **no one in command**, and held it arrow-straight for twenty kilometres for three thousand years. **The gap between the planned daylight attempt and the flawless hand-and-foot result is the whole engine.**

- **Naila owns the potato.** She proves *how hard it is* — and proves it is **neither trivial footpaths nor impossible/woo**, but real, measurable, humanly achieved, and stranger than either.
- **The potato never mocks the makers.** It mocks the *easy explanation* and the *over-confident outsider* — never the Aymara, never the practice.

---

## 3. WONDER-NOT-CONSPIRACY (the maybe, kept open)

- **No alien/von Däniken tradition specific to Sajama** — its obscurity spared it. **Keep the hole cleanly archaeological**, never "builders from elsewhere."
- **Fringe stays labelled as fringe.** Any **ley-line / Earth-energy grid** framing, or any "surveyed from space" reading, is **a character's over-reach, clearly labelled, never the narrator's verdict.** If a fringe author is ever named on-page: **NEEDS SOURCE.**
- **The adversary is greed/ideology in a fallible human** — clean hands, a mortal want — **never an omnipotent cabal.** No moustache.
- **The narrator never confirms or denies the speculation.** It confirms **only** the real residue and that the maybe is open. The reader is left **allowed to say "maybe"** — and here the maybe lives in *how a hand-walked sentence holds a bearing for three thousand years*, never in *aliens surveyed it*.

---

## 4. THE §3 FIREWALL, KEPT IN SPIRIT — AT ITS SHARPEST (the book's architecture)

Everywhere else in the line, the local out-reads the crew on the thing that matters. **Here that rule is the whole architecture**, because the "thing" is a **living practice**, not an artefact:

- **The crew own the telling; they never own the place.** They read *layers* (measure, tongue, made thing, image). The ***yatiri* reads the practice**, and the practice is **not a code the crew can crack from the outside.**
- **The "what is it for" is a *practice*, not a solution — and it is the local's to give or withhold**, on her own terms, in her own time, or not at all. **You don't solve a pilgrimage. You're invited on it, or you aren't.**
- **The crew never hold more of the camera than the local.** Mara makes this literal (the camera the line keeps asking to lower; the drone she puts down). Bashir makes it literal (the sentence he doesn't translate). Niamh makes it literal (the withholding she honours instead of decoding).
- **Jakobus defers.** He reads the made thing **into the local's waiting hand** and goes quiet. His gift is real and is **not a master key.**
- **THE FAILURE TEST (binding):** *If the book ever reads as "clever outsiders crack the natives' mystery," it has failed the line and must be rebuilt.* The read is **always a relay; the last, decisive hand is the local's.** The win is shared or the local's — **here, hers.**

## 5. DESIGNATED MYSTERIES — STAY OPEN (never resolved in narration)

The narrator **never** delivers any of these as settled:

1. **How, exactly, the straightness was achieved** over broken ground with no instrument and no vantage. The book establishes it is **humanly achieved** (kills both woo and sneer) and gives the *grounded, defensible frame* — but **never declares a proven method.** The honest verdict is: *real, measurable, and we do not fully know how.*
2. **The full "what is it for."** The *yatiri* hands the crew **a** reading (the line is a sentence walked, the straightness the fossil of the saying) — **or as much as she chooses.** The rest is **withheld, and the withholding is honoured.** The narrator never "completes" it for the reader's satisfaction.
3. **The optional series whisper** — *someone surveyed the lines from above before anyone should have.* **Plant; never pay off; never confirm it's one hand.**

**The grounded frame is a frame, not a verdict:** the **ceque-like radial-sightline parallel** (Cusco's radial shrine-lines, scaled across a whole mountain district; straightness as ritual intent over topographic convenience) is the *defensible archaeology the local can extend into living meaning* — present it as the reading the *yatiri* extends, **not** a solve the crew impose.

---

## 6. REAL PLACES, HONESTLY (the honesty rules)

- **The geography is true** (Nevado Sajama, the Altiplano, the *wak'as* / *chullpas* / chapels, the queñua woodland, the altitude and cold). Use it honestly; land each image once; never caption its meaning.
- **The people are alive and own the practice** — never scenery for a Westerner's epiphany. The wonder serves the place and its people (and points curiosity/attention toward the communities on their terms).
- **Back matter separates real / debated / invented** (see PLOT §back matter). The reader can *go and check*: the Morrison record, the Tierra Sajama survey, the scale — **the record is open.** But the **meaning stays with the people who walk it.**
- **Sensitivity reads are the FLOOR** (Bible §9), in two registers: the **ND renderings** (lived-experience veto — autism, Tourette's, Deaf culture, PTSD) **and** the **Aymara portrayal** (the people, the practice, the *yatiri*, the ritual, the language). **Pair this book with the strongest Aymara read in the line.**

---

## 7. THIS BOOK'S SPECIFICS (the locks unique to *The Long Straight*)

- **The overhead view is the wrong instrument** — structurally, not just thematically. Movement I gives the crew the god's-eye tool that "solved" Nazca and shows it explains **nothing**, because the makers never had it. The map looks like progress and is a dead end. **This is the inversion of Nazca's "the science caught up."**
- **The straightness is a side-effect of the sentence, not the point.** The book's quiet last turn: the crew came to answer *how is it so straight* and leave understanding that **the straightness was never the question** — only the thing an outsider could see. The real thing was always the **walking**, and the walking was never theirs.
- **The thing being fought over is *alive because it is used.*** The adversary's threat is to the **practice and the telling**, not just the ground — a managed view would *end the walking that keeps the lines bright.* The chase stakes are a living practice, not a buried object.
- **The TONGUE's discipline is the firewall in one body:** *some sentences you don't translate.* **Mara's is its rhyme:** *some images you don't take.* **Niamh's is its third face:** *some withholdings you honour, not decode.* Three crew members, one discipline — and it is the line's conscience at full strain.

*The residue is real. The interpretation stays open. The pilgrimage is never solved. The last hand is the local's. Roll the tape.*
