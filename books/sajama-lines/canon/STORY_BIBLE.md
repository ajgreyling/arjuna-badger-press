## 1. THE PREMISE (one breath, then the shape)

Three thousand years of separate walkers — no king to command them, no instrument to sight by, no hill to read the whole thing from — scored roughly sixteen thousand kilometres of dead-straight line across a mountain plain and held every one of them arrow-straight over the ridges. It is the largest single drawing on Earth, about fifteen times the extent of Nazca, and it is one of the least studied places its size on the planet. The official story is settled and **true**: it is Aymara pilgrimage, theirs, alive, still walked. A small, odd, found-family crew — neurodiverse, marginal, the kind of people the world stopped hiring — comes to the Altiplano around Nevado Sajama to do the one thing they were built to do with a made thing: ask **what is it for**. And on this ground, for the first time in the line, they learn the answer is **not theirs to find**. You don't solve a pilgrimage. You're invited onto it, or you aren't.

The crew arrive holding the most powerful tool an outsider owns — the overhead view, the god's-eye satellite tile that "solved" Nazca. They discover it is exactly the wrong instrument. The straightness that looks like an engineering puzzle from the air turns out to be **what is left on the ground when a sentence is walked the same way for three thousand years**. The meaning was never visible from above. It is legible only to the feet that make it. That is a *larger* wonder than any aerial mystery — and it belongs to the people who walk it.

**The chase** (the line's propulsion): a clean-handed adversary races the crew not to *destroy* the lines but to **own the telling of them** — to lift the meaning out of the feet that make it, fence it, brand it, file a heritage dossier somebody else authored, and sell a managed view from a platform that would end the walking. The crew race to *read* the line before the outfit gets there to *take the telling*. The thriller turns the pages; the wonder does its slow work underneath; the win, when it comes, is **the local's**.

## 2. THEME (lives in action — NEVER spoken aloud)

The load-bearing theme, dramatised and never stated by any character or the narrator:

> **Some things are read by being done, not by being looked at — and the people the world overlooks are the ones who keep the world's largest thing alive.**

The corollary, which is the book's spine and its conscience in one:

> **There is a kind of reaching-all-the-way-in that is a theft. The deepest respect a reader can pay a living thing is to stop short of taking it.**

These are never said. They are **built**: the overhead view that looks like progress and explains nothing; the drone that would sell the most and steal the most; the sentence the TONGUE could render into English and *chooses not to*; the photograph Mara doesn't take; Jakobus reading the made thing *into the local's waiting hand* and going quiet; the crew arriving to answer *how is it so straight* and leaving understanding that the straightness was never the question — only the thing an outsider could see. The theme is the book's *effect*. If a character announces it, cut the line.

**Secondary themes, also in action only:** the obligation that outlasts the reason (a line stays bright only because feet keep scraping it; stop and it greens over — a thing alive *because it is used*); found-family as the first room where none of them is the problem; and the line's permanent gift — that you are *allowed to say maybe*, and here the maybe lives in **how a hand-walked sentence holds a bearing for three thousand years**, never in *aliens surveyed it*.

## 3. THE WHY FILES REGISTER, APPLIED TO THIS BOOK (the move, LOCKED)

The per-book and per-chapter beat contract (Bible §2), tuned to Sajama:

1. **THE OFFICIAL STORY, STRAIGHT.** Open on the settled, *true* explanation, with total seriousness and real affection: **Aymara ritual pilgrimage footpaths**, first brought to outside attention by **Tony Morrison in the 1970s**, surveyed by UPenn's **Tierra Sajama** project — lines that link *wak'as*, *chullpas*, villages and chapels around Nevado Sajama, scraped to the pale subsoil and kept bright by the walking for 3,000+ years. The book *reveres* this. It is real, living, astonishing on its own terms. Earn the reader's trust as the sober one. *(Then: "...and yet.")*
2. **THE POTATO.** Show, concretely and in the body, how *hard* the "easy" version is. The brightest people, with the best modern kit, a vantage, a plan, and daylight, try to lay a survey-grade straight line over a ridge — and make a potato. The gap between the planned daylight attempt and the flawless hand-and-foot result, held for three thousand years by people with **no instrument and no vantage**, is the whole engine. **Naila proves it cold** (§5). Most chapters carry a potato — the tidy story visibly straining.
3. **THE HOLE.** The one genuinely-unresolved, *checkable* fact: the lines are **held dead-straight over broken ground — hills, ravines, obstacles — for up to ~20 km each, with no central authority, no surveying instruments, and no elevated vantage.** The published anchor states it plainly: *neither the purpose nor how the makers achieved such precision is completely understood.* Stated calmly, measured with a cold eye. **Never reached past toward aliens** (Sajama's obscurity spared it the woo — keep the hole cleanly *archaeological*). **Never sneered away** ("footpaths, move along" is the other failure state).
4. **THE WINK.** Name the tantalising question and decline to answer it: *No king commissioned this and no one ever saw it whole — so what told three thousand years of separate walkers to keep every line arrow-straight over the hills?* Let the reader sit in the maybe.
5. **WHAT IS IT FOR.** Jakobus's master question — and the book's hardest discipline. The answer is a **function**, grounded and physical, that *re-opens* the wonder: the line is **not a picture to be seen but a sentence to be walked** — a route through the sacred landscape said and re-said with the feet, whose straightness is the *fossil of the saying* (wavering would break the sentence the way a mis-said word breaks a prayer — *that* is why it climbs the hill rather than skirt it). **And the answer is the local's to give or withhold, on her own ground, in her own time, or not at all.** The narrator **never declares it proven**; the crew **never "solve" the pilgrimage.**

> **THE TONE LAW (LOCKED, inherited).** Play it **STRAIGHT**, like the show. Official story first, full respect and real love for the walkers. Then the hole. Then the wink. **Never** let a character announce the answer; **never** let the narrator confirm or deny the maybe. Pro-skeptic, pro-Aymara, pro-wonder, all at once. The fish heckles the *claims*; **nobody sneers at the people**, and **nobody — narrator least of all — pretends the pilgrimage is theirs to solve.** Have fun on the road and at altitude. This is still the cranked line. **Roll the tape.**

**The five forbidden registers (failure states, Bible §8) — each has a specific Sajama face:**
- *Credulous-believer drone* — "no vantage, therefore something impossible" / "someone surveyed it from space." **Killed by Naila measuring it as humanly achieved.**
- *Smug-debunker sneer* — "it's just footpaths, mystery solved." **Killed by the potato and the cold scale arithmetic.**
- *The woo* — anything that asks the reader to *believe* rather than to *sit in the maybe.*
- *The inspiration-poster* — the ND crew as a lesson for the neurotypical reader. **Inviolable: their wiring is HOW THEY SEE.**
- *The exotic-gaze* — the place as scenery for the band's epiphany. **This book's gravest risk, and the firewall's whole job.**

## 4. POV (crew own the telling; close third; the local owns the answer)

- **The crew own the POV** (Bible §3, the line's model). **Close third, rotating among the crew** — the spine voice is the **TONGUE (Bashir)**, this book's ND lead, with rotations into **Naila** (the measure), **Mara** (the LENS), and the occasional crew beat.
- **NEVER the *yatiri*'s POV.** The Aymara line-walker / *yatiri* is **seen from the crew's POV** and **out-reads them from there** (§5; CHARACTERS). The camera is the crew's; the *answer* is hers.
- **NEVER Jakobus's POV.** He is the road, seen only from the outside, off home ground the whole book (Bible §5).
- **The keel rule, on the page:** the crew never hold more of the camera than the local. When a draft centres the travellers' feelings over the Aymara stake in their own ground, it is wrong — pull it back. **Mara makes this literal** (the camera the line keeps asking to lower).

## 5. INTENDED READER

The reader of the best late-night rabbit-hole, with the credulity and the sneer both stripped out and the rigour and the wonder turned all the way up — who finishes wanting to *go and check* (the Tony Morrison record is real, the Tierra Sajama survey is real, the scale is real; the record is open). A newcomer reads a **complete standalone**: no prior book required. A binge-reader feels the line's conscience at full strain and catches the light, optional series whisper (§7). The reader leaves a person willing to say *maybe* — and **that they're allowed to is the whole gift.** Crucially: the reader leaves with curiosity and (the HBT-tourism echo) attention pointed **toward the Aymara communities and the practice, on their terms** — never toward a puzzle the book pretends to have cracked.

## 6. CONTENT BOUNDS (the binding floor for this book)

- **THE DIGNITY LINE (the FLOOR — Bible §9).** Aymara sensitivity consultants have a **real veto** on: the portrayal of the lines and the walking; the *yatiri* role; the language (Aymara on the page); the depiction of ritual, *wak'as*, *chullpas*, and what is sacred. **Pair this book with the strongest such read in the line.** Nothing ceremonial is rendered as spectacle; nothing the *yatiri* withholds is "recovered" by the crew or the narrator for the reader's satisfaction. The walking is real practice, never a set-piece.
- **THE ND FLOOR (the LOAD-BEARING LAW — Bible §4, inviolable).** The neurology of every crew member is rendered **from the inside, with real texture, real cost, real dignity — humanised, never caricatured.** No quirk for the reader's comfort, no meltdown as thrill, no savant magic, no inspiration close-up, never comic relief *at the neurology's expense*. **The TONGUE reaches people, not miracles** (and learns *when not to translate*); **Naila does pattern and arithmetic, not magic** (proves the residue, never reaches past it). Lived-experience consultants (autism, Tourette's, Deaf culture, PTSD) have a **real veto** per book.
- **THE FACT-FLOOR (binding — `SITE_DOSSIER.md`).** Every "hole" the book leans on is a cited real fact. The residue is real; the interpretation stays open. The **ceque-like radial-sightline parallel** is a *defensible frame the local can extend into living meaning, not a verdict the crew impose* — the narrator never declares it proven. Any ley-line / Earth-energy / alien-survey framing is **a character's over-reach, clearly labelled, never the narrator's verdict.** Facts added beyond the dossier are flagged **NEEDS SOURCE** and may not be load-bearing until corroborated (≥2 sources).
- **No gore, no thriller-spice trauma.** Mara's PTSD is rendered from the inside, with dignity, never as spice. The adversary is a fallible human with a mortal want and clean hands — **never a moustache, never an omnipotent cabal.**
- **The maybe is permanent.** The narrator confirms the real residue and that the maybe is open; **the narrator never confirms or denies the speculation**, and **never lets the crew solve the pilgrimage.**

## 7. THE CREATIVE DRIVER (why this book exists, and the one series whisper)

The driver: **this is the line's sharpest test of its own conscience.** Everywhere else, the local out-reads the crew on the thing that matters; here the *place is a living practice*, so the discipline is total — and the book's whole architecture is the §3 firewall made structural. The thrill of the rabbit-hole (a real, checkable, unclosed hole the size of a small country) is married to the hardest discipline in the line (a thing you may witness and respect, never crack). The crew's gift is real and is **not a master key** — and the *last, decisive hand is the local's*. The found-family warmth lives in the **genuine, warm contact the TONGUE opens** — a door that *opens*, not a code that *cracks*.

**The one light, optional series whisper (reverse-payoff; never required; never confirmed — Bible §6):** the crew find that **someone has already surveyed the lines from above** — filed the grid, mapped what was never meant to be read from the air — the same shared-hand whisper as Marree Man's American-English clues, Newark's shared unit, *The Quiet Men*'s shared silence. It is doubly resonant here: the throughline is itself an *overhead reader*, in the one book about why the overhead view misses the point. **Plant it; never pay it off; never confirm it's one hand.** The fish wink, at season scale. A newcomer never needs it; a binge-reader catches it.

*This bible is binding. The seed fills it in; the prose obeys it. Honour §3 and the humanity law above all. Roll the tape.*
