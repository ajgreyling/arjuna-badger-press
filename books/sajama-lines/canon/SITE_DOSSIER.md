## THE SAJAMA LINES — Bolivia (the best *open* hole in the Andean set)

### Official story (straight, with respect)
**Thousands of dead-straight lines** scored across the Altiplano around **Nevado Sajama** (Bolivia's highest
peak), made and re-walked by **Aymara peoples continuously for 3,000+ years** — ritual pilgrimage footpaths
linking ***wak'as*** (sacred places/shrines), ***chullpas*** (funerary towers), villages, and (after
contact) chapels. Not a finished monument but a **living sacred landscape**, still in use. First documented
to the wider world by the British explorer/filmmaker **Tony Morrison** in the **1970s**; later surveyed by
the University of Pennsylvania's **Tierra Sajama** project.

### How they were made
**Subtractive, *as trails*:** walkers scrape away the darker oxidised surface to expose the lighter subsoil
beneath, and **keep walking** — the line is maintained by the walking. Lines run **~1–3 m wide** and **up to
~20 km** each.

### THE REAL HOLE (genuinely open — the best of the Andean five for a novelist)
**How were the lines kept dead-straight over broken ground** — across hills, ravines, and obstacles, for
many kilometres — **with no central authority, no surveying instruments, and no elevated vantage point** from
which to sight or correct a line? Wikipedia (drawing on the published survey work) states it plainly: *neither
the purpose nor how the makers achieved such precision is completely understood.* A **decentralised** society,
building piecemeal **over millennia**, produced near-survey-grade straightness **by hand and by foot.**

**The second hole — scale.** The network covers **~22,525 km²** — roughly **15× the extent of the Nazca
lines** — with an estimated **~16,000 km** of line in total. This makes the Sajama Lines **very likely the
largest single archaeological artwork on Earth**, and — the quiet scandal of it — **barely studied** relative
to far smaller, far more famous sites.

### Fringe (labelled — useful as texture, never load-bearing)
The central dossier records **no developed ancient-astronaut / pseudo-archaeology tradition** specific to
Sajama (unlike Nazca's "spacecraft runway"); the site's relative obscurity has spared it the woo. The honest
open question is internal to archaeology, not a claim about builders-from-elsewhere — which is exactly what
makes it strong. *(Any "ley-line / Earth-energy grid" framing a character reaches for is **fringe**, clearly
labelled as the character's over-reach, never the narrator's verdict.)* **⚠ NEEDS SOURCE** if any specific
fringe author is named on-page.

### The grounded angle (the credible-strange engine)
**The archaeology of *movement*.** The lines radiate from sacred nodes — consistent with **ceque-like
sightline systems**: the *ceques* of Cusco were radial lines of shrines organised out from a centre, and the
Sajama network reads as that logic **scaled across an entire mountain district.** The straightness encodes
**ritual intent over topographic convenience** — the makers **refused to curve around a hill.** A line that
will not bend for a ridge is not taking the easy path; it is holding a *bearing* for a reason. The "what is it
for," grounded and physical, points at **a thing you walk, not a thing you look at** — the legibility is in
the *act of pilgrimage*, not in an aerial view no maker ever had.

### Scene-ready facts (the beats the prose hangs on)
- **~16,000 km of line, drawn by foot** — on the order of **~3× the width of the continental United States**,
  laid down without a wheel, an instrument, or a plan anyone has ever found.
- A single line **~20 km long and ~1 m wide that *will not bend* for a ridge** — it climbs the hill rather
  than skirt it.
- **~22,525 km²** of patterned ground — **~15× Nazca** — the **largest archaeological artwork on Earth**, and
  one of the **least studied.**
- **Built and re-walked continuously for 3,000+ years** — the network was **ancient when the Inca arrived**,
  and is **still walked in Aymara ritual today.** (Contrast Nazca, which fell silent; Sajama never did.)
- The maintenance mechanism *is the use*: the line stays bright because feet keep scraping it. **Stop walking
  and it greens over** — so its 3,000-year survival is itself a record of unbroken practice.
- Documentation hooks: **Tony Morrison (1970s)** first brought them to outside attention; UPenn's **Tierra
  Sajama** project surveyed them. *(For any specific figure or quote beyond these, **⚠ NEEDS SOURCE**.)*

### The wink (name the question; decline to answer)
*No king commissioned this and no one ever saw it whole — so what told three thousand years of separate
walkers to keep every line arrow-straight over the hills?*

## Notes for the book (binding constraints carried from the line canon)

- **The firewall, at its sharpest (Bible §3).** This is the line's **most "living people, not a riddle"**
  site. The lines are a **living pilgrimage the Aymara own**; the **"what is it for" is not a puzzle for the
  crew to solve but a practice to respect**, and the answer is the local's **to give or withhold**. If the
  book ever reads as "clever outsiders crack the natives' mystery," it has failed and must be rebuilt. The
  crew own the *telling*; they never own the *place.*
- **The straightness is the *checkable* residue — measure it with a cold eye.** Naila's function here is the
  potato-prover's: establish, on the page and honestly, how *hard* the straightness is to achieve by hand
  over broken ground with no vantage — without ever reaching past the measurement toward woo. The hole is the
  *gap between the decentralised, instrument-less, vantage-less method and the near-survey-grade result* —
  not "therefore something impossible."
- **Honesty about resolution.** Sajama is **genuinely open**, but the openness is *archaeological*, not
  alien. The ceque-system parallel is a **real, defensible frame**, not a solve; present it as the grounded
  reading the local can extend, not a verdict the crew impose.
- **Altiplano rhyme.** Per the slate, this book and the Puma Punku book share the **Bolivian high plain** and
  Jakobus's *Sawubona*/altitude beats — a deliberate echo, not a repeat.
- **Sensitivity read is the FLOOR** (Bible §9): **Aymara consultants** on the portrayal of the lines, the
  walking, the *yatiri*, and the language. Pair this book with the strongest such read in the line.

*Fringe items, where they exist, are texture only — never load-bearing fact. The narrator's verdict is always
the real residue + the maybe.*
