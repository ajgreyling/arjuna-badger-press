# Mythos Rules — AFRIKA 2100 (the grounded "physics", revised for the open-contact era)

> **Binding.** This file inherits RELIC's MYTHOS_RULES and states exactly which rules carry,
> which invert, and which are new. The trilogy's prime directive survives unchanged; the book
> lives or dies on holding it *with visitors on the page*.

---

## RULE 0 — carried unchanged: wonder from mechanism, never assertion

Every miracle is a machine someone built; every institution is a system someone maintains;
awe is allowed, the unexplained-because-magical is not. A scene that treats the visitors, the
gold, the Fold, or the instrument as mystical **as narrated fact** fails the gate. Characters'
reverence remains allowed and labelled (the Stewards revere; the Fold prays; the prose never
endorses reverence as mechanism).

## RULE 1 — **inverted, by sanction**: the heirs are here

The trilogy's Rule 1 ("they are gone; no ships land") was true *then*. The 2064 waking-note
changed it. This book's form of the rule:

- **The Builders themselves remain gone.** The visitors are their **heirs** — continuous with
  the tradition, not the ancients returned. The distinction is canon and characters know it.
- **They appear on the page only as counterparty:** protocol, manifests, liaisons, berthing,
  consequences. Physical description spare and factual. No POV, no interiority, no
  telepathy-with-the-reader.
- The mythic naming layer (Anunnaki/Enki, the Golem, the Nephilim — RELIC Rule 1) remains
  **tradition characters cite**, now with the eerie weight that the tradition turned out to
  point at something real. The prose still never adopts the mythic names as narration.

## RULE 2 — carried unchanged: gold is a resonance medium

Now *public* science (the one big epistemic shift since RELIC): purity + worked form =
resonant function. Gold still does nothing supernatural — no glow, no visions, no healing. The
consignments are components, not treasure. The bullion economy is history's punchline (the
decoy that ran the world for five thousand years).

## RULE 3 — carried: resonance is the ancient layer's only force

The instrument, the waveguides, the coupling galleries — all acoustic/vibrational/structural,
exactly as the trilogy built them. **New-era boundary:** the *visitors'* technique may exceed
human science, but on the page it must be (a) mostly withheld, (b) delivered as knowledge
rather than artifacts (Accord clause), and (c) never shown doing anything the book cannot
gesture at mechanically. If a scene needs visitor tech to behave as magic, cut the scene.

## RULE 4 — carried, re-keyed: the gift is pattern-reading, not prophecy

Naledi's gift is Priya's gift in a new domain: she reads **standing waves and system intent**
from cues — a signature, a tolerance, a repeated ratio. She never "just knows." Same gate as
the trilogy: every leap traceable to a cue on the page.

## RULE 5 — carried unchanged: the Court is analysis, never oracle

The Librarian's every utterance defensible as inference from data it names. It may be wrong;
it has memory (2064; and deeper — trilogy events) and a stake. **Rule 7-T binds forever:** the
instrument couples to present conscious minds; the Court cannot cross that threshold and the
book may not find it a loophole.

## RULE 6 — carried: real places used honestly

Witwatersrand, Vredefort, Bushveld Complex, Lancaster County, Aksum — real geography, real
geology, real distances. The 2100 layer built on them (eGoli Spaceport, the Compact zones)
must sit on the real bones plausibly. Geomagnetic physics of the Flare is real physics
(high-latitude GIC vulnerability); the one in-world addition (the instrument's role in 2064,
§M1) is designated mystery, not fake science.

## RULE 7 — new: the counterparty stays honest

The visitors may not be revealed as (a) secretly evil invaders, (b) secretly benevolent
guardians, or (c) a hoax. They are **other**: their frame of value and time is not ours, the
overlap is the Accord, and the book's resolution must work *without* ever fully opening them.
The three protocol-slip moments (CHARACTERS — Sela) are the total allowance of "something
looks through."

## RULE 8 — new: the Fold is rendered, never adjudicated

The prose takes no position on whether the Fold is religion's death or its distillation
(THEMES §5). Sunday scenes are written with the same respect as the trilogy wrote the
Brotherhood. The terminal ethic appears in full (*Don't be a cunt. Be kind.*) sparingly — it
must land as scripture lands, not as a gag. Liturgical short form *Be kind* for repeated use.

## RULE 9 — new: the Firstborn doctrine is human, and stays human

The Abokuqala / Firstborn movement (WORLD.md) — the belief that the visitors hold pure
African bloodlines sacred and read pale skin as vitamin-D deformation — is **a human
misreading, never the visitors' position and never the room's physics.**

- The visitors, asked, **decline to rank human genetics** — politely, totally, at protocol
  level only. They never endorse, never elaborate, never open (Rule 7 holds against this
  strand hardest of all, because this is the strand readers will most want opened).
- The room's coupling follows **residence** (ground, habit, spent presence), never genome.
  Canon refutations stand and may not be softened: Priya Ellis at the 2064 waking; the
  room's refusal of the visitors themselves; the academies' assessments tracking lived
  ground.
- **Gate taboos:** narration never states the doctrine as fact, never mocks its adherents
  as a class, and never lets a sympathetic character endorse the essentialism unrebutted
  by *event* (not by lecture). The movement's grief gets its best argument (both-sides
  discipline); its physics loses to physics.

## Designated mysteries (the withheld — gate-protected from over-explanation)

- **M1 — What the instrument did in 2064.** The custodians sang it awake in the Flare's worst
  hour; the logs are sealed; the Elder Steward *shows* rather than tells (Act II). The book
  gives evidence and testimony, never a full mechanism. (It may NOT be revealed as "it saved
  the world" in narration — belief, testimony, and sealed data only.)
- **M2 — The visitors' unanswered three:** where the failing components are; what happens if
  repair lapses; why the 2064 note mattered enough to cross for. Act III may answer **at most
  one**, as the C-07 payload requires; the others stay open forever.
- **M3 — The two halted consignments.** The Stewards' reasons are stated in Act III *to the
  conference*, but the page gives the reader the shape, not the schematic.
- **M4 — The vitrified bore's maker-process.** Carried from the trilogy, still never explained.
  Seventy more years of study; still a designated mystery. (The port catwalks through it —
  familiarity without explanation is the point.)
- **M5 — The rotor anomaly's mechanism (electrogravitics).** The one sanctioned speculative
  *human* physics (LEGACY_TECH §4): a spinning charged crystalline rotor weighs less than it
  should, the effect is real, engineering rules bound it, and **no accepted theory exists on
  the page.** The visitors' hulls visibly rhyme with it and the visitors decline to correct
  the human theory. The book may not explain it, may not let a character intuit the mechanism,
  and may not let the visitors confirm or deny. (This is the trilogy's vitrified-bore
  discipline applied to the future's own frontier — the world still contains things it uses
  and does not understand, which is the mythos's deepest continuity.)

## Diction taboos (gate-watched)

- Never "aliens" in narration (characters may). Never "gods," "angels," "demons" for the
  visitors except inside quoted belief.
- Never "magic," "miracle," "supernatural" as narrated fact — carried from the trilogy.
- The Flare is "the Flare" (capitalised); the dark years are "the Blackout Decade" (formal) or
  "the hunger years" (American vernacular). Never "the apocalypse" — the world did not end;
  that's the book's whole point.
- G is "G" in speech, "Gerhard van Niekerk" on first formal reference, never "the prophet"
  except inside labelled belief.
