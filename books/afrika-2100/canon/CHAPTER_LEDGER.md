## Beat sheets

### Ch 1 — The Tolerances (N · eGoli Spaceport · six weeks to Renewal)
Open mid-shift: a berthing, rendered as air-traffic-control-meets-organ-tuning. The 2100 world
arrives through work (platcaps, piezo pavers, the mesh, the catwalked vitrified bore on her
walk-in — all texture, zero lecture). During coupling calibration of an outbound consignment,
Naledi reads a resonance signature ~3σ off the repair manifest's declared profile. She logs it
correctly. **Plants:** guild-manual epigraph (uncredited engineer — Priya back-seed); the bore
(`crumb:vitrified-tunnel`); Sela at protocol distance; the sealed archive mentioned in passing;
her grandmother's survey plaque in the guild hall. **Ends:** the log entry timestamped — the
book's first domino.

### Ch 2 — The Blessing of the Load (R · Lancaster Compact zone · six weeks)
Fold Sunday in Lancaster: the folded liturgy in a barn-church, Deitsch hymn to a folded
reading; then the blessing of the load — the American gold consignment on Army gun-carriages
drawn up between draft teams. Ruth receives her orders (deliver; observe; answer the question
nobody will write down: *payment, offering, or surrender?*). **Plants:** German Bible + folded
coin; Amos Beiler; the brother who stayed (one glance across the pews, no scene); Assist
frames at harvest (`crumb:guardian-lineage`). **Ends:** wheels south.

### Ch 3 — Closed as Filed (N · eGoli · five and a half weeks)
Her flag comes back closed, review-level, signature illegible. The guild-master (Baba Sipho
Dlamini, proposed) kindly, correctly, tells her the Renewal season is the wrong time. Naledi
pulls the archive indexes at night: every consignment signature she can access drifts the same
direction, growing; the 2064 waking-note logs are custodially sealed — the one dataset that
would norm the curve. **Plants:** Tefo glimpsed (a Steward too young for his robe, watching
her terminal, not her); the two halted consignments (guild rumour); grandmother's silence.
**Ends:** she requests the sealed logs formally — on the ledger, irreversible.

### Ch 4 — The Burned Interior (R · CONUS rail south · five weeks)
The consignment train through the rebuilt-and-scarred East: transformer graveyards kept as
memorials, mesh-steeple towns, piezo-sprung wagons on corrugated roads charging their own
lights. Ruth's history braided in (the Flare at six — candlelight normal, the Army's arrival
salvation; why she enlisted). At the embarkation port: an e-grav gantry walks a forty-ton
platcap bank onto the freighter like a deacon carrying a font — her first sight of the
century's new physics. **Plants:** the hunger-years wrong order (named, not told); SSP
graveyard glimpsed from the train (`Okonkwo` seed). **Ends:** the gangway up.

### Ch 5 — The Custodial Brake (N · eGoli · five weeks)
Her formal request draws two responses in one day: Commissioner Marais (charming, exact,
terrifyingly reasonable — "curiosity is a luxury the Renewal can pay for in twenty-seven
years") and Tefo, who finds her in the refinery row and says the order knows her
grandmother's name. First exposition of the custodial brake and the two halts — from inside
guild rumour, uncorrected. **Plants:** Marais's genuine goodness (he fixes a junior tuner's
housing problem in passing — the antagonist-position must be loved); the Librarian named as
Renewal auditor. **Ends:** Tefo's question: *"What did she leave off the map, do you think?"*

### Ch 6 — The Crossing (R · South Atlantic · four weeks)
The sail-hybrid freighter: wave-flex piezo skins humming, watch-quiet nights. Amos and Ruth
and the long unpacking of the wrong order — 2081, the flooded bottomland harvest, the Army
plan that overrode the Plain elders' weather-knowledge, the eleven dead, Lieutenant Stoltzfus
carrying the order she knew was wrong. Why she stayed in: *someone who knows what wrong
orders cost should be the one carrying them.* **Plants:** the Fold's ethic as she actually
uses it (de-escalating a crew dispute: "be kind about it, sergeant"); the folded coin's
obverse quoted in full — first of the book's two full utterances. **Ends:** Table Mountain on
the horizon; the southern lights that never burned.

### Ch 7 — Liaison (N · eGoli · three and a half weeks)
Naledi assigned tuning liaison to the American consignment — Marais's move: visibility as a
leash ("my most exact engineer, where everyone can see her"). First working session with Sela:
protocol English, weights and tolerances; Naledi asks one off-manifest question (why this
purity?) and receives the book's first taste of polite-and-total refusal. **Plants:** Sela's
courtesy as texture; the rotor-anomaly testbed visible from the liaison office (hulls rhyme —
unstated); guild split beginning (two tuners quietly copy her closed flag). **Ends:** the
American freighter called in at Berth Nine.

### Ch 8 — Berth Nine (N · eGoli · three weeks) — END ACT I
Convergence. Ruth walks the consignment down; Naledi runs first calibration with Ruth
attending as consignment officer. The American gold — Compact-refined, hand-carried,
provenance-perfect — shows the discrepancy **clean**: strongest signature yet, no noise to
hide in. Both women see the same number. Ruth, plain: *"Captain's question: is that wrong?"*
Naledi: *"It's not wrong. It's undeclared."* **Payoffs:** ch-1 log entry becomes a curve; ch-3
drift becomes fact. **Ends (act turn):** private itch → shared evidence; Ruth signs the
calibration sheet as witness — the Compact is now in it.

### Ch 9 — Reasonable Men (N · eGoli · three weeks)
The partnership works the evidence inside the port; Marais closes doors *ahead* of them —
archive permissions narrowing, the guild-master formally warned, Naledi's night access
"rationalised." All of it procedurally correct. Baba Sipho torn open on the page: he trained
her to read exactly this, and he is ordering her to stop. **Plants:** Renewal-at-any-price as
world consensus (a delegates' reception scene — Give bloc voices, exhausted and human);
Okonkwo Diri seen at the port margin, arguing with a gate clerk. **Ends:** Tefo's invitation:
the Stewards will see her.

### Ch 10 — Sunday at the Port (R · eGoli · two and three-quarter weeks)
The Fold service in the port concourse — the folded liturgy in eleven languages, the ledger
of kindnesses read aloud; Ruth beside Naledi (who attends the way engineers attend: kindly,
unconvinced). **Slip one:** Sela, observing from the gallery, stays through the whole
service — and leaves before the blessing, one sensory detail (the pause at the threshold, a
weight shifted), no interpretation. Ruth and Naledi's friendship sets (land-and-provision
meets wave-and-tolerance; the two registers on one bench). **Plants:** the Fold rendered with
full respect (D9 test passes here or the book fails); Ruth's tribute-or-trade question said
aloud for the first time. **Ends:** word of Gogo Dineo's estate box arriving from the Free
State.

### Ch 11 — The Survey Books (N · Johannesburg · two and a half weeks)
The estate box: theodolite notebooks, 2071–2074 — the Accord survey. In the vellum margins,
in her grandmother's mine-shorthand, Naledi finds it: a gallery surveyed and then absent from
every published sheet, deep under the old workings, on the axis the waking-note logs would
norm to. Objects outlive explanations. **Payoffs:** ch-3/ch-5 grandmother plants. **Plants:**
the unmapped gallery's bearing (Act II end destination); the survey plaque re-read — the
names of three elders redacted from the 2064 descent roster (CROSSBOOK back-seed, never
resolved). **Ends:** she takes the books to the Stewards — trust, spent forward.

### Ch 12 — The Frame at Gate Eleven (N · the deep galleries · two and a quarter weeks)
The Stewards open. Ma-Rethabile walks Naledi down the catwalked bore into custody's own
country and **shows** (never tells): the 2064 story — the Flare hour, the descent, the
waking; why the order halted two consignments (both times, the drift curve did what Naledi's
curve is doing now, and both times the visitors *paused the schedule themselves* — the
Stewards braked, the counterparty did not object; nobody has said this publicly). En route,
the guild legend told at the place it happened: the Frame at Gate Eleven, eleven hours, four
hundred people — unnamed. **Payoffs:** the halts; the sealed logs seen at last (raw, cold,
partial). **Plants:** the threshold chamber door (closed; Act II end); Ma-Rethabile's line
for the conference ("a gift you don't understand is not yet a gift"). **Ends:** the Elder
Steward's condition: *take what you now know to the one who stood here first.*

### Ch 13 — Orders Home (R · eGoli · two weeks)
Marais moves on the messengers: Ruth's recall orders (consignment delivered, officer
surplus), the calibration sheet she witnessed now "administratively sequestered." Ruth
delays — Army-lawfully, using every hour of discretion a quartermaster owns. Okonkwo Diri
finds *her* (the American — he trusts uniforms that lost): the SSP archive, the 2066
detection tapes, the vindication wound. The tapes' one real fact: **departure before the
Flare.** The archive's seductive wrong answer: that the SSP understood the visitors (it
doesn't; Rule 7 holds). **Plants:** the tapes (Act II end component); *right too early is
the same as wrong, except for how it feels.* **Ends:** Ruth chooses — she misses her ship.

### Ch 14 — The Garden at Umdloti (N · KZN south coast · two weeks) — MIDPOINT
The pilgrimage. Priya Ellis, 109, in her garden above the sea: shortest sentences in the
book, longest silences. She verifies Naledi's curve by hand on paper, the old way, and then
gives her the question, correctly weighted — *"Everyone will ask whether we can trust them.
Wrong question. Ask what you'd need to know to give something away well — then notice nobody
at that conference can answer it either."* One name spoken for the trilogy reader (she looks
at the guild legend's dates and says, quietly, *Arin*). **Payoffs:** the uncredited manual
hand; the hinge. **Ends (midpoint turn):** Naledi comes back changed — from *stop the
Renewal* to *make the Renewal honest*.

### Ch 15 — The Librarian (N · eGoli · one and three-quarter weeks)
The Court facet audits her mathematics at her own request — publicly, on the ledger,
irreversible (the move Marais cannot sequester). The Librarian's cadence: analytical,
slightly inhuman, more honest than anyone in the room because it has no face to save. It
confirms the curve; it names what it cannot do (the threshold; Rule 7-T stated in its own
voice — *"this one is not for me"* lineage); it declines to speculate on intent — **and it
remembers 2064 aloud**, one paragraph, the only living witness with perfect recall.
**Payoffs:** ch-5 auditor plant; `crumb:court-lineage`. **Ends:** the curve is public. The
world's three positions form ranks overnight.

### Ch 16 — The Formal Answer (R · eGoli · one and a half weeks)
The Accord requires a response to a ledgered anomaly; Sela delivers it in full protocol —
true, partial, worse than silence: *the consignments perform maintenance within manifest
scope; the network requires retuning; scope definitions are a matter for the Renewal.*
**Slip two** (one sensory detail): when Ruth — not Naledi — asks the Fold's question ("Are
you being kind to us, or careful with us?"), the protocol pauses one beat too long.
**Plants:** "scope definitions" as the loaded phrase Act III will detonate; the Give bloc
celebrates the answer, the Keep bloc arms with it — both partially right (D4 held).
**Ends:** Naledi reads the answer's tolerances like a manifest and finds the shape of a
handover in the margins.

### Ch 17 — The Reference Pitch (N · the unmapped gallery · one week) — END ACT II
The descent: Naledi, Tefo, Ruth (witness), Ma-Rethabile keying the doors — down the
grandmother's bearing to the unmapped gallery: a coupling chamber the Accord survey was made
to forget, live telemetry running since 2064. **C-07 lands in evidence the reader has
watched all book:** (1) the node is failing — the waking was rough, the idle unstable, the
drift is *ours*; the consignments have been maintaining our machine. (2) The node slept
true: Earth's stone kept the Builders' original calibration; part of every consignment
carries our reference pitch outward to a drifted network — the 2066 tapes close (they left
before the Flare because the network was already failing; the note told them the reference
had survived). (3) The volume amendment is a handover's scaffolding. The three positions are
each right and none sufficient. **Ends (act turn):** the question can no longer be deferred:
*will Earth take its own machine back?* Six days to Renewal.

### Ch 18 — The Renewal Floor (R · eGoli conference · five days)
The conference opens on a changed board: the curve public, the gallery ledgered, the
delegations re-briefing overnight. Ruth — AWOL by any reading that matters — stands as the
Compact's witness at the American desk's sufferance, and gives the floor testimony in the
plain register: what she saw at Berth Nine, what she signed, what the folded ethic requires
of a quartermaster (*the first sentence is about this*). The Give and Keep cases at their
strongest, both honest. **Plants:** Marais managing the unmanageable, visibly exhausted;
the amendment's vote scheduled. **Ends:** the Stewards request the floor.

### Ch 19 — Stated Reasons (N · eGoli conference · four days)
The order breaks two centuries of silence: Ma-Rethabile states the custody's reasons —
the halts, the visitor pauses, the 2064 descent — plainly, on the record ("a gift you don't
understand is not yet a gift; we did not understand; we are beginning to"). Tefo's
generation wins its argument not by rebellion but by being the ones the elders chose to
send up the steps. Leila's letter arrives with the Aksum delegation — one paragraph, one
untranslated Ge'ez sentence, counsel from the book's absent scholar: *collate the variants
before trusting the conclusion — theirs and ours.* Marais cracks in chambers: the honest
core under Renewal-at-any-price is fear, and he says so, to Naledi, alone. **Ends:** the
amendment cannot pass as written; the visitors are asked to clarify scope.

### Ch 20 — The Question (N · eGoli conference · two days)
The first question Earth has formally asked the visitors in twenty-nine years that is not
freight: *Is the maintenance of Earth's node being transferred, and to whom?* Sela, before
the full floor, in protocol English: the answer that is a yes shaped like a schedule — the
handover confirmed, on their side already begun; custody, in their frame, *belongs to the
resident mind and was only ever borrowed.* **Slip three** (the last, largest, one detail):
delivering it, Sela uses — once — the guild's own word for the reference pitch instead of
the treaty term, and looks at Naledi as it lands. **Ends:** the amendment redrafted
overnight into a handover schedule; one requirement unfilled — a present conscious mind
must stand the node's watch, and the Court, asked formally, states its boundary and steps
aside (Rule 7-T, kept in public).

### Ch 21 — The Watch (N · the threshold · Renewal day) — CLIMAX
The consent act. The threshold chamber, the assembled witnesses (Ma-Rethabile, Tefo, Baba
Sipho, Ruth, the Librarian at its stated distance, Sela at the counterparty's). Not a
switch thrown — a **watch accepted**: the guild and the Stewards merged by signature into
what the Brotherhood always was, and a first watchstander named. Naledi takes it knowing
the price (a vocation, not a posting; the unencumbered life given up on-page, in her own
voice, with her own reasons — the want from CHARACTERS.md matured). The instrument
acknowledged the way the trilogy's discipline allows: telemetry, not theophany — the idle
steadies. **Payoffs:** `crumb:the-choice-at-the-switch` rhymed at species scale; the
Builders' A6 choice rhymed forward (they left it asleep; we take it waking). **Ends:** the
Renewal signed amended, Marais's pen.

### Ch 22 — Signatures (R · eGoli · two days after)
Aftermath, Ruth's register: the paperwork of the extraordinary (the Army resolving her
status with the face-saving genius of old institutions — the Compact claims her testimony
as duty performed). The price visible in daylight: the guild changed, Naledi's rostered
life, Marais retiring into the amendment's implementation (the institutionalist made
custodian of the thing he feared). Ruth's answer to carry home, formulated at last:
*neither payment, nor offering, nor surrender — provision. What you send ahead when your
own people are going to live somewhere.* **Ends:** the northbound freighter; the folded
coin left with Naledi (the second and last full utterance of the ethic, in Deitsch and
English, hand to hand).

### Ch 23 — The Note Carries (— · epilogue · months later)
Two beats, trilogy tradition. (1) The handing-on: watchstander Mokoena receives a young
Kenyan engineer's first impossible telemetry query, flags it *open — investigate*, and
signs her name legibly. (2) The garden at Umdloti: Priya, the survey books returned to a
shelf beside an old folded coin, and — for the trilogy reader only, unexplained for the
newcomer — the note in the deep rock, still carrying, exactly as an old book said it
shouldn't. **Closes:** `crumb:the-note-that-carries` across all four books. *The note
carries.*
