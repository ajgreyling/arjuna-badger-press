*The spiritual fourth of the* African Gold *trilogy — rewritten from the soil of* The Prophet
and his Brother *(2035). Not a cold logistics novel with saints on coins. A human century
asking the question the loud decade deferred:* who may give it away — and what does custody
cost when the weather never stops.

## One breath

The year is 2100. Sixty-five years after a degree-less physicist and a river-desk publisher
chose, in the same week and without sharing a room, what they would keep, unbind, and allow
said — the planet still argues about them. The Kind’s wall sentence became America’s Sunday
(**the Fold**); the Four Quarters never died — they fossilised into Give / Keep /
Understand-first, into saint-hunger and receipt-faith, into soft nos that learned new letterheads.
South Africa is the indispensable nation: platinum, gold, G’s stack, the spaceport. The
Builders’ heirs land at eGoli with manifests. They want worked gold. A tuning engineer who
loves her guild cannot un-see a discrepancy: the gold is not patching what the manifests claim.
An Amish-raised Army captain escorts America’s consignment south, carrying a Fold coin that
still has the cunt in it when honest, and a brother-wound that never became a tidy lesson. The
dramatic question is not invasion. It is **custody** — species-scale rhyme of 2035’s desk
consents: what Earth will sign, leave unsigned, and stand behind when the weather keeps blowing.

## High concept

> *First contact as logistics; faith as a sentence that survived crowns; two women stuck to the
> same public object (the gold, the Renewal, the ledger) choosing what they will keep and refuse
> — while the visitors wait like a freight company and the century’s weathers try to sand both
> of them into usable lies.*

It reads like grounded future-history with Prophet’s blood in it: competence as suspense,
belief honoured as meaning, climax as consent acts not battle, **no victory lap**. The
engineering mystery is real (the node, the retune, the handover). The soul is human: refuse
the easy wrongs, ink the costly ones, leave the weather undefeated.

## Why this rewrite exists

The first Afrika 2100 pass was flat: discrepancy → evidence chase → conference. Institutions
without itch. Saints without Bouwer. Fold without Kind paint. Consent without a desk that
hurts. *The Prophet and his Brother* is the human decade — loud vindication, four weathers,
partial authorisation, knives across distance, node still lit. **Rebuild 2100 from what that
book made true.** Do not protect old 2100 locks that sand the humans into architecture.

## The world in five facts (Prophet-harvested)

1. **The Flare (2064).** Carrington-class. North dark. South carried. Instrument sung awake;
   someone far heard the note.
2. **The Plain Compact.** US Army + Amish/Old Order fed a continent. Ruth’s bloodlines.
3. **The Fold ← the Kind.** The wall sentence (***Don’t be a cunt — be kind.***) that CongoSky
   ran as ops and the street painted in 2035 was folded, by Court-lineage salvage, into Sunday
   practice. Liturgical form often softens to *Be kind*; the full line survives on honest coins
   and in Ruth’s pocket. Antichristos residue still tries to put a face above the words —
   usually G’s. The Kind’s old refusal (messiah-less) is the Fold’s best argument against
   saint-tourism.
4. **G — man, then myth, never only poster.** Gerhard van Niekerk (b. **1980** Stellenbosch —
   Prophet soil): 420 Code, Constant, sculpture desk, brother Bouwer assassinated 2025, hospital
   stigma, unread father dedication, dab and talk shit left ugly in the life AJ shipped under
   partial rails. Died **2058**, Eastern Cape, before the Flare proved the stack at civilisation
   scale. 2100 reveres receipts; soft churches and hard academies still fight over the man.
   **He refused crowns in 2035.** The century that canonises him keeps trying to give them back.
5. **eGoli Spaceport.** SA = Wakanda-as-logistics. Visitors for the gold. Great Renewal due.

## The engine — weathers, not cartoon factions

Prophet taught: camps are **weather systems**, each with its best argument. 2100 inherits that
discipline.

| Weather | Stance | What it wants of Naledi / Ruth / the Renewal |
|---|---|---|
| **Give** | Bind the relationship; don’t risk the counterparty | Sign volume; bury the squiggle; treat curiosity as next term’s luxury |
| **Keep** | Inheritance / custodial brake; sleep had reasons | Halt, silence, Stewards’ unexplained no — ceremony or conscience |
| **Understand first** | Priya’s creed matured; guild conscience | Know the machine before you gift a component — or accept custody *knowing* |
| **Saint-hunger** (Quarter I fossil) | G as face above the Fold | Pilgrimage cosplay, ledger-coin relics, “what would G have signed” as evasion |
| **Receipt-faith** (Quarter IV fossil + Kind spine) | Measurement and practice | Keys without crown; paint without messiah; custody stated on the ledger |

The visitors are not a sixth weather. They are the **freight counterparty** — patient,
procedural, incomplete in their honesty the way institutions are incomplete.

## Protagonists (arcs in CHARACTERS.md)

**Naledi Mokoena** — primary. Tuning engineer; guild child; cannot un-see. Arc is Prophet-shaped:
ordinary competence → concrete asks that cost standing she valued → refuse easy wrongs
(stop-the-Renewal theatre / sign-blind) → consent: take the watch as vocation, not posting.

**Capt. Ruth Stoltzfus** — secondary. Compact blood; Fold coin; brother who stayed; wrong order
in the hunger years. Arc: deliver and know which it was → ordered home → refuse on Fold grounds
(*the ethic is two sentences; the first one is about this*) → North’s witness without becoming
priest or recanter.

**Braid rule (from Prophet C-07):** they share a week, a theme, a public object. They need not
become a buddy movie. Kinship can be ledger, consignment, and a coin handed across a table —
not a forced shared-soul speech.

## The elder

**Priya Ellis**, 109. Midpoint hinge. Not oracular. She does not solve. She weights the
question the way G and AJ weighted theirs: *what would you need to know to give something away
well — then notice nobody at that conference can answer it either.* Both women refuse the easy
wrong in the same week. No relief.

## The payload (engineering — kept; soul reframed)

C-07 still holds as mechanism: failing component is Earth’s node; sleep kept the calibration;
consignments maintain and retune; volume amendment scaffolds handover; climax is Earth accepting
custody of its own machine. **Reframe:** the reveal is not a TED twist that makes the conference
clever. It is the moment the easy wrongs become visible — sign blind, halt as theatre, bury the
messengers — and the costly consent remains: **partial, amended, inked, weather still blowing.**

## Climax shape (Prophet rhyme at species scale)

Not a battle. Not a presser. **Consent week:**

- Naledi at the threshold (Rule 7-T: present conscious mind; Court steps aside again).
- Ruth refusing recall on Fold grounds — witness, not silhouette for saint-hunger.
- Marais forced to put the question on the ledger (institutionalist who buried it must record it).
- Visitors asked, for once, a question they must answer or refuse in front of everyone.
- Renewal signed **amended** — volume + handover schedule + custody stated. Price on-page:
  guild innocence ends; Naledi’s unencumbered life ends; Ruth carries a true sentence home
  that will not quiet America’s weathers.

**Close:** no victory lap. Node (planetary) under human watch. Fold paint still scraped by
people who want a softer cunt. Quarters’ fossils still argue. Epilogue: a young engineer’s
impossible telemetry; a garden; a coin; a note in deep rock — still carrying.
