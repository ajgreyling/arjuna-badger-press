# Timeline — AFRIKA 2100 (two-layer chronology)

> Binding chronology. Like RELIC, this book runs two clocks: **Layer H** — the seventy-year
> history that built this world (delivered in fragments, never as lecture); and **Layer P** —
> the present-day story clock of 2100. Layer H fragments must each *land* on a present beat
> (no orphan history); the trilogy's events are fixed points that may not move.

---

## LAYER H — the history (2026 → 2100)

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2026 | **G publishes the 420 Code**, copyleft, at the420code.org. Ignored by the mainstream; adopted at the edges (mesh comms, deterministic compute, provenance ledgers). |
| ~2027–2029 | **The trilogy's events** (RESONANCE → Sacred Shadows/REVELATION → RELIC). Priya Ellis stands at the switch and refuses both the adversary's control and the easy waking: the instrument is left at the Brotherhood-tended threshold, *understood first*. The platinum alloy, the Guardian platform, and the Court enter the world. Public record: thin, disputed, half-classified. |
| 2030s–2050s | **The quiet dissemination.** AugmenTech's Assist/Guardian patents expire or are opened; the platinum-alloy work matures toward energy storage; G's stack spreads through the Global South (cheap, offline-first, sovereign). The Court lineage grows under the consent doctrine written after the rogue-preservation scare. |
| 2058 | **G dies** in the Eastern Cape, respected in his field, unknown to the general world. (Date proposed — C-03.) |
| **2064** | **THE FLARE.** Carrington-class geomagnetic storm. High-latitude grids collapse in hours; transformer fleets burn; the North goes dark. The southern belt holds. **In the worst hour, the custodians sing the instrument awake** — the one act that stabilises [designated mystery — see MYTHOS_RULES §M1] — and the note goes out. It is not known, then, that anything is listening. |
| 2064–2069 | **The hunger years / the Blackout Decade begins.** In the US: grid triage fails; the Army stands up the **Plain Compact** with the Amish and Old Order communities (2065). G's mesh — the only comms architecture with nothing long enough to burn — becomes the North's nervous system. His stack is adopted at civilisation scale in five years. Canonisation by adoption begins. |
| 2066–2070 | **Disclosure.** Orbital instruments the flare didn't kill detect approaching craft (2066). Governments, unable to coordinate a secret with the grid down, disclose — and the **Secret Space Programs** come out with it, fleets and budgets and all. Public shock is blunted by exhaustion: the world is hungry, and the sky can wait its turn. |
| 2071 | **The visitors land** — openly, at the Witwatersrand, beside the deep gold and the waking instrument's strongest coupling. They identify themselves, in effect, as the Builders' heirs. First protocol exchanges: they are patient, procedural, and interested in exactly one commodity. |
| 2073 | **The eGoli Accord.** The landing ground is formalised as **eGoli Spaceport**; the gold-for-technique trade begins under custodial oversight (the Brotherhood's successors hold a seat). The platinum supercapacitor enters mass production the same year — SA's century begins. |
| 2070s–2080s | **The rebuilding.** The North comes back meshed and modest. **The Fold** spreads (the folded liturgy first assembled 2068–2072 by Court-lineage minds as cultural salvage, adopted as practice across the Compact zones and beyond). Guardian-descended robotics do the heavy rebuild; LPGM consent law becomes the template for all machine-autonomy statute. |
| 2090s | The first post-Flare generation reaches adulthood knowing no other world. Priya Ellis publishes nothing, gives no interviews, and tends a garden in KwaZulu-Natal. G's face is on the ledger-coin. |
| **2100** | **The Great Renewal of the eGoli Accord falls due.** The story begins. |

> **Gate rules (Layer H):** the trilogy's internal chronology is frozen canon and may not be
> contradicted (Priya's age tracks RELIC: ~35 in ~2027 → ~109 in 2100). No Layer H fragment may
> run longer than a page in prose; history arrives on the shoulder of a present scene.
