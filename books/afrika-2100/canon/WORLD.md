## The shape of the world

Thirty-six years after the Flare, the map of power has inverted. The old North — the
high-latitude, high-voltage civilisation that owned the twentieth and twenty-first centuries —
spent a decade in the dark and two more rebuilding, and it rebuilt *differently*: meshed,
local, low, humble. The geomagnetic South — spared by latitude — carried the lights through the
Blackout Decade. South Africa, holding the platinum, the gold, the stack, and the saint,
emerged as what the American press unironically calls **the Wakanda of the world**: not an
empire, but the indispensable nation — the place everyone must deal with, on its terms.

## SOUTH AFRICA — the indispensable nation

- **Why spared:** low geomagnetic latitude (real physics: geomagnetically induced currents
  savage long high-latitude grids first), a grid already hardened by decades of scarcity
  discipline, and — the in-world addition — national comms that already ran substantially on
  G's mesh, which has no long conductors to burn.
- **The four pillars of its position:**
  1. **Platinum** — the Bushveld Complex holds most of the world's platinum-group metals; the
     platinum-alloy supercapacitor (LEGACY_TECH §2) is the battery of the recovery, and SA
     controls the supply chain end to end.
  2. **Gold** — the Witwatersrand's deep gold, now understood publicly as what the trilogy knew:
     a resonance medium. The only commodity the visitors want.
  3. **The stack** — G was South African; the 420 Code institutions (the Codehouse, the ledger
     authorities, the mesh registries) are headquartered here. Power, money, privacy, and
     communication worldwide run on rails this country stewards.
  4. **The port** — eGoli Spaceport, the single sanctioned landing ground on Earth
     (SPACEPORT.md). Host nation to the species relationship.
- **Texture:** not a utopia. The wealth is new and unevenly settled; the custodial burden is
  heavy; the world's envy is a constant diplomatic weather. But it is confident, polyglot,
  young, and — for the first time in its wounded history — *central*. Johannesburg (eGoli, the
  city of gold, now literally the city the gold-buyers come to) is the capital of the century.

## THE UNITED STATES — the Plain Compact

- **The Flare hit the US grid hardest of any nation** — the long high-latitude transmission
  lines, the transformer fleet with year-long replacement lead times, the just-in-time food
  web. The hunger years (2064–2069) killed the old arrangement of American life.
- **The Plain Compact (2065):** the US Army — the last institution with fuel, order, and reach
  — partnered with the Amish, Old Order Mennonite, and allied Plain communities: the one
  population that had never needed the grid and held the complete working knowledge of
  pre-electric agriculture at scale.
  - **The Army brought:** perimeter security, logistics, muscle, hands, engineering, and plans
    — the ability to move a harvest and defend a granary.
  - **The Plain communities brought:** the land-craft — draft-animal agriculture, seed saving,
    food preservation, smithing, water, timber — and the *culture* of working without
    infrastructure, taught outward to millions of refugees from the dark cities.
  - The Compact zones fed the continent. By 2100 the emergency is long over, but the Compact
    endures as an institution — half agricultural extension service, half national identity.
    The aesthetic of the American interior is Plain-inflected: barns, meshes, muscle, and quiet.
- **The Fold (the new Sunday):** America still fills its pews — catastrophe made the pews
  matter more, not less. But the god is new. During the Blackout Decade the Court-lineage
  machine minds, asked to salvage the world's cultural memory, **folded** the complete
  religious history of the species — every scripture, tradition, commentary, and heresy,
  weighted by the 420 Code's coherence ladder — and what survived the fold was a terminal
  ethic two sentences long: ***Don't be a cunt. Be kind.*** (Liturgical form: *Be kind*; the
  full form is on the coin and everyone knows it.) The Fold is a practice, not a hierarchy:
  Sunday assembly, the reading of folded passages (a psalm beside a sutra beside a Zulu
  praise-song, presented as one inheritance), and the ledger of kindnesses. Rendered with
  respect, always — see STORY_BIBLE D9.
- **Relationship to SA:** the US is a treaty customer — of batteries, of ledger settlement, of
  port access. The gold consignment Ruth escorts is America's stake in the species
  conversation. The humility of this position, for America, is a quiet engine of the book.

## THE REST OF THE WORLD (one line each; expand only as scenes demand)

- **Spared belt:** southern South America (Chile/Argentina south of the burn line), Australia
  and Aotearoa, equatorial Africa, parts of Southeast Asia — the "Southern Carry," the states
  that kept lights on and now anchor the recovery institutions.
- **Europe:** rebuilt meshed and municipal; cathedral towns took the Fold early.
- **China:** rebuilt fastest by mass mobilisation, on licensed SA supercaps — the second pole
  of the recovery, and the loudest voice for *Give* in the gold question.
- **The Secret Space Programs:** disclosed 2066–2070 (TIMELINE). The fleets were real, small,
  fragile, and embarrassing — decades of hidden budgets that produced hardware the visitors'
  arrival made instantly obsolete. Their veterans and archives are a live political wound and
  a plot resource (the SSP claim-graph is canon-adjacent texture).

## The Firstborn movement (Abokuqala) — the century's heresy

The dark counter-current to SA's century, and the book's most carefully handled strand
(MYTHOS_RULES Rule 9 governs; THEMES §9 carries it).

- **The claim:** a religious movement — born in the landing decade, swollen with every
  finding since — holding that the visitors deem **unbroken African bloodlines sacred**: the
  landing chose Africa; the room couples to African ground; therefore (the movement's leap)
  the blood is the coupling, the First People are the chosen keepers, and the pale
  populations of the world are — in the movement's own theology-dressed language — *genetic
  deformations*, depigmentation adaptations to weak sun and vitamin D hunger, tolerated
  guests in the story of a Black planet. Reverse racism with a liturgy.
- **Its honest roots (both-sides discipline — the movement gets its best argument):** the
  landing *did* choose Africa. The deep past *is* African — the trilogy proved it in-world.
  And four centuries of the old racism built the exact mould this inversion pours into;
  the movement's grief is real even where its physics is fantasy. Its rank and file are
  mostly not haters but the wounded and the proud; its leaders are the usual mix of
  believers and operators.
- **What the visitors actually say:** nothing. Asked (and they have been asked), they
  decline to rank human genetics — politely, totally. The movement reads the silence as
  confirmation; everyone reads their silences as confirmation. (Rule 9: the visitors never
  endorse, never deny at more than protocol level, never open.)
- **The in-world refutation (physics, not sermon):** the room couples to **residence** —
  the ground-made body, the deep habit, the spent presence — not to genome. Canon facts
  that quietly break the doctrine: the 2064 keepers included Priya Ellis (Indian–South
  African); the room refused the visitors themselves (residence, not presence, is the
  coin); and the watch academies' coupling assessments track *lived ground*, not ancestry.
  The movement's answer to Priya's existence is erasure or honorary adoption — "both,"
  as she says, "the same insult."
- **Institutional posture:** the Fold's liturgy (one inheritance) is the movement's
  loudest opponent, and pays for it; the Stewards — two centuries mixed, miners first —
  refuse the movement's embrace, which enrages it more than opposition would; Renewal
  security treats it as the conference's principal public-order weather.

## The gold question (the world's live controversy)

The eGoli Accord (2073) governs the trade: consignments of worked gold, refined and formed to
the visitors' tolerances, against a payment schedule of technique. The Accord is up for its
**Great Renewal** in 2100 — the book's clock. The factions (STORY_BIBLE D4: Give / Keep /
Understand-first) are not nations but positions cutting across every nation, the way the
trilogy's adversary was a position embodied in a person.
