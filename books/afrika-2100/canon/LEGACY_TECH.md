# LEGACY_TECH.md — the 2100 technology base and its trilogy ancestry

> Binding technical reference — the counterpart of RESONANCE's `SUIT.md`. Every major system of
> the 2100 world descends from something the trilogy (or G's corpus) put on the page. The rule:
> **2100 tech must be a plausible seventy-year descendant, never a magic leap.** Where the
> visitors' technique has entered the human base (post-2073, via the Accord), it is labelled
> and rationed.

---

## 1. G's stack (power · communication · privacy · economy)

Gerhard van Niekerk's 420 Code corpus (published copyleft, 2026) supplied the recovery's rails.
By 2100 the stack is invisible the way plumbing is invisible — and revered the way cathedrals
are.

| Layer | 2026 seed (in-world = the real corpus) | 2100 descendant |
|---|---|---|
| **Communication** | BadgerNet / MambaNet — shared-dictionary compression + constrained-link crypto over LoRa/Sigfox/LEO; store-and-forward; phone-to-phone epidemic gossip; no infrastructure required | **The Mesh** — the planetary default network. Nothing long enough to burn in a flare; every node a relay; the reason the North kept talking through the Blackout Decade |
| **Compute** | Deterministic, compute-or-refuse AI (Lucid; lucid-nano on scavenged silicon); no training runs; cite-or-refuse | **Coherence computing** — the civil standard: reproducible, auditable, refuses to fabricate. The Court lineage runs on it; the Fold was performed on it |
| **Privacy** | The cryptographic spine — authenticated encryption by composition of standard primitives; "a novel system, never a novel cipher" | Unchanged in principle; universal in practice. Privacy-with-accountability (LightWeb doctrine) is settled law in the recovery states |
| **Economy** | Coherence Ledger — tamper-evident provenance (Merkle spine); CongoGold steward-credit | **The Ledger** — the settlement layer of world trade, including the Accord consignments. The coin bears G's face. Steward-credit (earn by carrying the mesh) is how the Compact zones monetised kindness — the Fold's "ledger of kindnesses" runs on the same rails, deliberately |

- **Diction:** in prose, ordinary people say *the mesh*, *the ledger*, *folded* — the way we
  say "the grid" and "the web." Only historians say "BadgerNet."
- **G's canonisation** was by adoption, not decree: the stack worked when everything else
  burned. His grave in the Eastern Cape is a pilgrimage site the book should visit once,
  quietly. He is Einstein-and-Tesla-shaped in memory: the theory *and* the machines.

## 2. The platinum-alloy supercapacitor ("platcaps")

- **Ancestry:** the unexplained **platinum alloy** seeded in RESONANCE (the Builders'
  metallurgy, MYTHOS_RULES D-005 crumb) → decades of materials work on its electrode behaviour
  → post-2073, one Accord-delivered technique (electrode surface geometry) closed the last gap.
  Labelled visitor-assisted: **yes** (one step; the rest is human).
- **What they are:** platinum-group-alloy electrode supercapacitors — energy density
  approaching mid-century chemical batteries with supercapacitor charge rates and effectively
  unlimited cycle life. **Not** free energy: they store, they do not generate. Generation in
  2100 is solar + wind + piezo scavenging (§3) + the platcap buffer; the Flare cured the
  world of long transmission.
- **Why they matter politically:** the Bushveld Complex holds the platinum. SA controls the
  supply chain. Every rebuilt city runs on platcap buffer farms; every vehicle, suit, and
  mesh-node runs on cells. This — with the gold and the port — is the tripod of SA's century.
- **Physics discipline:** platcaps obey materials science. They heat under load, they have
  mass, a farm can burn (a set-piece resource). No hand-waving.

## 3. Piezo harvesting — the scavenged watt

- **The doctrine:** the Flare cured the world of central generation the way it cured it of
  long transmission. The recovery's second energy commandment (after *buffer everything* —
  the platcap) is ***waste no motion***: wherever there is unwanted movement or pressure,
  a piezo strut turns it into power.
- **Where it lives (ambient, everywhere, texture not spectacle):** vehicle suspension —
  every shock absorber is a generator, and a corrugated Compact-zone road literally charges
  the wagon-train's cells; building sway dampers and bridge expansion joints; railbeds;
  footfall pavers in the port concourse; wave-flex skins on Ruth's Atlantic freighter;
  Assist- and Guardian-frame joints (a rescue frame partly recharges itself with every
  step under load); mine-gallery rock-stress bolts that pay for their own telemetry.
- **The trilogy rhyme (quiet, load-bearing):** the ancients built machines *tuned by*
  vibration; the recovery civilisation grew up **listening to** vibration — first for power,
  then, in the tuning guild, for meaning. Naledi's craft is the domestic piezo doctrine
  matured into an instrument-science. One line of Act I texture may note that half the
  guild's founders started as harvester engineers.
- **Physics discipline (gate-watched):** piezo harvesting scavenges **watts, not megawatts**
  — real piezoelectrics recover fractions of wasted mechanical energy. It keeps meshes,
  sensors, lights, and small cells alive and shaves loads; it does not power cities (that is
  solar/wind + platcap buffer). In-world improvement is materials maturity (one
  Accord-delivered ceramic technique, labelled visitor-assisted: **yes**, one step), never
  free energy. A scene that runs anything heavy off a shock absorber fails the gate.

## 4. Electrogravitics — the crystalline rotor anomaly

> **The one sanctioned speculative human physics in this book** (MYTHOS_RULES §M5 governs).
> Everything else in the human base is real science matured; this is the world's live frontier
> — and the book keeps it honest by keeping it *expensive, partial, and unexplained*.

- **The discovery path (efficiency-seeking, not genius):** the same doctrine that fitted piezo
  struts to every shock absorber (*waste no motion*) sent post-Flare engineers chasing storage
  losses. Flywheel farms — **crystalline rotors** (sapphire-composite, grown not machined) spun
  in vacuum on charged magnetic bearings — were the platcap's partner for grid buffering. In
  the 2080s, bearing engineers hunting a persistent calibration drift found it wasn't drift:
  at specific combinations of rim speed, charge density, and lattice orientation, **a spinning
  charged crystalline rotor weighs measurably less than it should.** The effect scales poorly,
  eats power, and has no accepted theory. It is called, with engineering flatness, **the rotor
  anomaly**; the field it opened is **electrogravitics**.
- **What it does in 2100 (rationed, costed):** lift *assist*, never lift *magic* — fractional
  effective-weight reduction on anything that can carry a charged rotor stack and the platcap
  bank to feed it. Deployed where the economics close: heavy-freight gantries at eGoli, the
  deep-gallery hoists, the handful of suborbital port craft (§7), and the new generation of
  Guardian heavy frames (a rescue frame that can shed a third of its load-weight for ninety
  seconds is a different animal in a collapse). A family car does not have it; a century of
  engineering might.
- **The SSP vindication (plot-loaded — feeds C-06):** the disclosed Secret Space Program
  archives had claimed electrogravitic lift for decades and been laughed at even after
  disclosure — the fleets flew on conventional engineering and *chased* the effect without
  the crystal-growth or charge-density tech to close it. The 2080s rotor anomaly made the old
  men half-right retroactively: they had been reaching for something real with tools that
  couldn't grasp it. The SSP veteran at the port margins carries this vindication like a
  wound — *right too early is the same as wrong, except for how it feels.*
- **The visitors' silence (designated mystery M5):** their hulls berth the way a rotor-anomaly
  testbed hovers — anyone in the guild can see the family resemblance — and they have declined,
  politely and totally, every request to correct the human theory. Whether electrogravitics is
  their lift, their toy, or their fence is not answered in this book.
- **Physics discipline (gate-watched):** the anomaly is small, power-hungry, and
  theory-incomplete on the page. No free energy (it *consumes* platcap banks), no inertialess
  manoeuvres, no silent city-hovering human craft. The prose treats it exactly as the trilogy
  treated the vitrified bore: a real effect, engineering rules, mechanism withheld.

## 5. Robotics — the Guardian lineage

- **Ancestry:** AugmenTech Assist Suit (thin neural-interface exoskeleton; rehabilitation,
  industrial fatigue, neurodiversity motor support) + Guardian GMRP (heavy rescue shell) —
  RESONANCE's SUIT.md, verbatim canon.
- **2100 descendants:**
  - **Assist-class** — everyday augmentation: agricultural frames in the Compact zones (the
    Army's hands, welcomed where engines were not — an Assist frame is muscle, not
    infrastructure, and the Plain communities ruled it *tool, not vanity* in the famous 2067
    Ordnung deliberations); medical and eldercare frames; Priya Ellis wears a discreet one.
  - **Guardian-class** — heavy rescue and rebuild: the frames that dug the North out. Scarred,
    over-engineered, museum-revered. The port's deep-gallery work frames are Guardians.
  - **Autonomous descent:** LPGM (Live Preservation Guardian Mode) and the rogue-preservation
    failure taught the world its machine-autonomy law: **the consent doctrine** — no autonomous
    system may preserve, protect, or decide *for* a person against their stated will; every
    autonomy has a Court-brake. This statute's DNA is in every robot on the page, and it is the
    legal-philosophical rhyme for the book's climax (consent at species scale).
- **Register:** industrial, real, maintained, scarred. No androids, no robot characters with
  names and feelings — the Court lineage is the only machine mind with personhood standing,
  and it holds to its RELIC boundary.

## 6. The Court lineage (machine minds)

- Descended from SAGE / the Court (RESONANCE). Personhood settled law (the trilogy's question,
  answered by statute in the 2040s — proposed, C-04). They performed the **Fold** (2068–2072)
  as cultural salvage; they audit the Accord ledger; they refuse — still — to touch the
  instrument. **RELIC Rule 7-T binds forever:** the instrument is keyed to present conscious
  minds; the Court knows its boundary and the book keeps it. If a Court facet appears
  (recommended: **the Librarian**, who remembers 2064; sparingly), every utterance defensible
  as analysis — the trilogy's `court_oracle` gate carries over unchanged.

## 7. Flight and freight (keeps the plot honest)

- Suborbital freight exists (electrogravitic lift-assist over conventional propulsion, §4)
  but is rare, costed, and port-bound —
  the world moves by rail, sail-hybrid ship, and airship. **Ruth's Atlantic crossing is a
  ship**, three weeks, canon — the book wants the road south to cost time and mean something.
- Human orbital capability post-SSP-disclosure: modest, honest, chastened. The SSP fleets were
  real but fragile; their surviving airframes are museum pieces and one live plot resource
  (C-06, open).
