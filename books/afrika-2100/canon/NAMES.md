## Deprecated / forbidden

- No RESONANCE deprecated-cast revivals (carried from REL's NAMES.md).
- No "Wakanda" in narration — it's the American press's word inside the book (WORLD.md), a
  quoted cliché characters may roll their eyes at; the prose never adopts it.
- No cute visitor nicknames ("greys," "the neighbours," etc.) except once, in Amos Beiler's
  mouth, where it lands as characterisation and is gently corrected.
