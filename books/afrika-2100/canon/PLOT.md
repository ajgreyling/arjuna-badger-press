## ACT I — Ordinary loud (T-6 → T-3 wks)

Prophet open: competence, sensory life, manuscript/weather already pressing — not a cold
mystery box. Same here.

### Naledi thread
Open **inside work and itch**: eGoli choir line, berthing, tolerances — the world’s strangest
workplace as a workplace. The discrepancy logs on a routine calibration (repair manifest vs
resonance signature). She files; file comes back closed; guild-master, kindly: *drop it.* She
can’t.

**Human soil (not optional texture):** Gogo Dineo’s silence in the family — survey books,
unmapped margins, the grandmother who left something off the Accord’s map and died with the
silence intact (C-08). Saint-hunger weather at the port edges (ledger-coin pilgrims, “what
would G have signed”). Receipt-faith in the guild manuals (epigraphs, kill-switch discipline
half-remembered as piety). First watcher: not the visitors — the Stewards / the Accord’s
quiet.

### Ruth thread
Consignment leaves Lancaster: blessing of the load (Fold liturgy on a gun-carriage gold wagon —
American century in one image). **Honest Fold:** the coin in her pocket still carries the full
terminal ethic when she is alone; Sunday softens it; she notices the difference the way AJ
noticed soft scrubbing of Kind paint.

Road south through rebuilt-and-scarred interior; embarkation; Atlantic on a sail-hybrid —
watch-quiet nights for history: Flare at six, hunger years, **the brother who stayed**, **the
wrong order** (Army plan vs community knowledge; people died; she carried the lieutenant’s
share). No tidy lesson. Flat loaded line — Jamie-Uys restraint from Prophet’s Bouwer discipline.

### Convergence / end Act I
American consignment berths; Naledi assigned tuning liaison; first calibration of *this* gold
shows the discrepancy again — stronger, cleaner. They see it together. Neither can claim they
didn’t.

**(Act turn:** private itch becomes shared evidence — and the first shared recognition that
*drop it* / *just deliver* are the easy wrongs.**)**

## ACT II — The asks (T-3 → T-1 wks)

Prophet Act II name and job: concrete demands that cost. Not only “chase the telemetry.”

### The asks (arrive as mail, orders, protocol — not narrator charts)

| Ask | From | Toward | Easy wrong | Costly posture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drop the squiggle / delay curiosity | Marais / guild patronage | Naledi | Bury discrepancy until after signature | Keep reading the board |
| Halt without stating reasons | Elder Steward / Keep weather | Naledi (via Tefo) | Ceremony as courage | Demand reasons on the ledger |
| Go home; don’t make a mess at the host port | Compact / Army recall | Ruth | Obedience as virtue | Stay; witness; Fold grounds |
| Accept partial truth as full | Sela / protocol | Both | Relief at millimetre slip | Hold the incomplete honest |
| Stop asking / sign blind | Give weather (popular) | Both | Renewal-at-any-price | Understand-first without halt-as-theatre |
| Feed saint-hunger a silhouette | Port pilgrims / soft churches | Whoever will bless | “What would G do” as evasion | Refuse face; keep sentence |

### Inside the port
Odd-couple partnership without buddy fusion: wave-and-tolerance meets land-and-provision.
Reconstruct what the consignment gold is *doing* from guild instruments while Marais — genuinely
good, genuinely afraid — closes doors kindly. Renewal-at-any-price hardens: not villainy;
policy. Stewards split: Tefo wants stated reasons; Elder shows (does not lecture) sealed 2064
logs and what Gogo left off the map.

### Midpoint — the pilgrimage (Priya)
Naledi to KZN. **Hinge = Prophet midpoint grammar:** both refuse the easy wrong; **no relief.**

Priya does not hand the answer. She weights the question: *Everyone will ask whether we can
trust them. Wrong question. Ask what you’d need to know to give something away well — then
notice nobody at that conference can answer it either.*

Naledi’s turn inside the hinge: from *stop the Renewal* to *make the Renewal honest* — refuse
halt-as-theatre **and** refuse sign-blind. Same week, Ruth refuses the easy silhouette (bless
the consignment as pure tribute or pure surrender for home consumption) and holds the question
open.

### Escalation
Sela confronted; protocol slips a millimetre (first of three). Formal answer: true, partial,
worse than silence. Librarian audits Naledi’s math — public, irreversible. Marais moves from
burying the discrepancy to burying the **messengers** (revoked clearances; Ruth ordered home).
Saint-hunger and Abokuqala weather thicken outside the fence — grief and essentialism; neither
is the plot’s engine; both are climate.

### End Act II — payload in the deep workings
Vitrified bore / threshold chamber. **C-07 lands** (evidenced in telemetry the reader has
watched):

1. Failing component = Earth’s own node (rough wake, unstable idle).
2. Sleep kept the only true calibration; consignments maintain *and* carry reference pitch outward.
3. Volume amendment scaffolds maintenance handover — *will Earth take its own machine back?*

**(Act turn:** the question can no longer be deferred to institutions; the easy wrongs are now
named with the answer on the table.**)**

## ACT III — Consent week (T-1 wk → T0)

Prophet climax: desk decisions, not pressers. Species-scale rhyme.

### Conference floor (necessary theatre — undercut by desk truth)
Amendment (volume) on the table; ledger-published telemetry in every delegate’s hands; Marais’s
position collapses into honest core (fear dressed as prudence); Stewards state, at last and
publicly, why custody kept; Ruth — ordered home and refusing — stands as North’s witness on
Fold grounds: *the ethic is two sentences; the first one is about this.* Saint-hunger tries to
annex the moment; receipt-faith and Kind residue refuse the face.

### The consent act (climax)
Structured as RELIC’s climax rhymed at species scale **and** Prophet’s consent week rhymed at
treaty scale:

- **Who signs, knowing what** — not whether to have a relationship.
- Naledi at the threshold; present conscious mind; Court states boundary and steps aside (Rule 7-T).
- Visitors *asked*, first time in twenty-nine years, a question they must answer or refuse in front of everyone.
- **Price on-page:** guild innocence ends (tuning becomes custody; guild becomes what Brotherhood was); Naledi’s unencumbered life ends (watch = vocation); Ruth carries a sentence that will not quiet America’s weathers; Marais must put on the ledger the question he buried.

**Locked outcome shape (C-07 kept, soul reframed):** Renewal signed **amended** — volume granted,
handover schedule written in, custody stated in the open. Not triumph. Posture with a timestamp.
Partial the way G’s autobiography authorisation was partial: life-and-work (maintenance) allowed;
crowns refused; erasure refused.

### Resolution
Compact goes home with an answer Ruth can tell truthfully. Guild changed. Stewards’ silence
transformed into stated reasons. Visitors remain counterparty, not friends. **Weather still
blowing** — Give/Keep/Understand/saint-hunger/receipt-faith all still true for someone.
