## ACT I — the discrepancy and the road (T-6 → T-3 wks)

**Naledi thread.** Open inside competence: a tuning shift at eGoli, a consignment berthing —
the world's strangest workplace rendered as a workplace (the reader learns the 2100 world the
way RESONANCE taught the mine: through work). During a routine coupling calibration Naledi
logs a resonance signature that doesn't match the repair manifest. She files it; the file
comes back closed; the guild-master, kindly: *drop it.* She can't. First quiet inquiry into
the telemetry archive; the discovery that 2064's waking-note logs are custodially sealed;
first sign she's being watched — not by the visitors. By the Stewards.

**Ruth thread.** The consignment leaves Lancaster: the blessing of the load (Fold liturgy on a
gun-carriage gold wagon — the whole American century in one image). The road south through the
rebuilt-and-still-scarred interior; the port of embarkation; three weeks of Atlantic on a
sail-hybrid freighter — time for Ruth's history (the Flare at six, the hunger years, the
brother who stayed, the wrong order) to surface on watch-quiet nights.

**Convergence / end Act I:** the American consignment berths at eGoli; Naledi is assigned its
tuning liaison; and the first calibration of *this* gold shows the discrepancy again —
stronger, cleaner, undeniable. The two women see it together. Neither can now claim they
didn't. **(Act turn: private itch becomes shared evidence.)**

## ACT II — the evidence chase and the elder (T-3 → T-1 wks)

- **Inside the port:** Naledi and Ruth, oddest-couple partnership (wave-and-tolerance meets
  land-and-provision), reconstruct what the consignment gold is *doing* from the guild's own
  instruments — while Commissioner Marais, reasonably and with real institutional kindness,
  closes doors ahead of them. The Renewal-at-any-price position hardens: not villainy;
  policy.
- **The Stewards split open:** Tefo brings Naledi in; the Elder Steward shows her — not tells,
  *shows*, in the deep galleries — why the order halted two consignments: the sealed 2064
  logs, and what her own grandmother left off the map. (Gogo Dineo beat lands here — C-08.)
- **Midpoint — the pilgrimage:** Naledi travels to KZN to find the engineer whose uncredited
  hand is in the guild's founding manuals. The Priya scene: the hinge. Not answers — the
  *question*, correctly weighted: "Everyone will ask you whether we can trust them. Wrong
  question. Ask what you'd need to know to give something away *well* — then notice nobody at
  that conference can answer it either." Naledi comes back changed: from *stop the Renewal* to
  *make the Renewal honest*.
- **Escalation:** the liaison Sela is confronted with the telemetry; the protocol slips a
  millimetre (the first of the three moments); a formal answer arrives that is true, partial,
  and worse than silence. The Librarian audits Naledi's math and confirms it on the ledger —
  public, irreversible. Marais moves from burying the discrepancy to burying the *messengers*
  (administratively: revoked clearances, a recalled consignment officer — Ruth ordered home).
- **End Act II:** in the deep workings — the vitrified bore, the threshold chamber where RELIC
  ended — the honest answer lands (**C-07 payload**): what the network is being tuned *for*.
  The reveal must (a) be evidenced in the telemetry the reader has watched all book, (b)
  recast the visitors' request as neither lie nor simple truth, and (c) make all three world
  positions (Give / Keep / Understand-first) *each* partially right. **(Act turn: the question
  can no longer be deferred to the institutions.)**

## ACT III — the Renewal (T-1 wk → T0)

- The conference floor: the amendment (volume) on the table; the ledger-published telemetry in
  every delegate's hands; Marais's position collapsing into its honest core (fear dressed as
  prudence); the Stewards stating, at last and publicly, the reasons custody kept; Ruth —
  ordered home and refusing, in the plain-spoken register, on Fold grounds (*the ethic is two
  sentences; the first one is about this*) — standing as the North's witness.
- **The consent act (climax):** structured as RELIC's climax rhymed at species scale — not
  whether to sign, but **who signs, knowing what**. Concrete shape gated on C-07; the locked
  requirements: Naledi at the threshold; a present conscious mind where the Court cannot go
  (Rule 7-T kept); the visitors *asked*, for the first time in twenty-nine years, a question
  they must either answer or refuse in front of everyone; and the price paid on-page (the
  trilogy never gives the choice away free).
- **Resolution:** the Renewal signed *amended* — or refused *well* (C-07 decides which); the
  Compact goes home carrying an answer Ruth can tell truthfully; the guild changed; the
  Stewards' custody transformed from silence to stated reasons.

## EPILOGUE — the reverse-payoff (trilogy tradition)

Two beats: (1) Naledi, now the one who has stood at the threshold, receives a young engineer's
first impossible telemetry query — the creed handed on again. (2) One image for the trilogy
reader only, never explained for the newcomer: a garden in KwaZulu-Natal, a folded coin on a
table, and the note in the deep rock — still carrying, exactly as an old book said it
shouldn't.
