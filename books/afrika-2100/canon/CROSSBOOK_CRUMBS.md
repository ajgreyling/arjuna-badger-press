# Cross-book weave — the fourth strand

> How AFRIKA 2100 hooks into the trilogy braid **and** book 3.5. Invariants from RELIC:
> **STANDALONE-INTACT** (newcomer needs nothing) and **WEAVE-INTACT** (declared landings anchor
> existing seeds). This book is almost pure **landing** — harvest of seventy years + Prophet
> soil — plus back-seeds for readers who go back.
>
> Books: **R** = RESONANCE · **REV** = REVELATION · **REL** = RELIC · **P** = *The Prophet and
> his Brother* · **A21** = AFRIKA 2100.

## Landings (trilogy seeds this book pays off)

### `crumb:resonance-gold` — the keystone, made global
- R/REV/REL: gold as resonance medium (the trilogy's hinge).
- **A21 landing:** public science, treaty commodity, the entire plot. The bullion age is
  history's decoy, taught to schoolchildren.

### `crumb:platinum-alloy` — the battery in everything
- R seed: the unexplained platinum alloy (Builders' metallurgy, kept implicit in book 1).
- **A21 landing:** the platcap — the platinum-alloy supercapacitor that powered the recovery
  and made SA indispensable (LEGACY_TECH §2). One line in Act I names the alloy's ancestry for
  the trilogy reader; standalone-invisible.

### `crumb:guardian-lineage` — the suit that became the century's robots
- R seed: Assist/Guardian platform, LPGM, the rogue-preservation scare.
- **A21 landing:** Guardian-class rebuild frames, Assist frames in the Compact fields and on
  Priya's shoulders, and the **consent doctrine** in machine-autonomy law — the legal rhyme
  for the climax.

### `crumb:vitrified-tunnel` — the first kilometre of the spaceport
- R seed: Arin's tunnel; REL landing: the waveguide.
- **A21 landing:** the catwalked bore in eGoli's deep galleries. Naledi walks daily through
  the thing Arin nearly died discovering; only the trilogy reader feels the vertigo.

### `crumb:court-lineage` — the made mind at its boundary
- R seed: SAGE/the Court; REL landing: Rule 7-T (the threshold it cannot cross).
- **A21 landing:** the Librarian as ledger-auditor; the Fold as the lineage's greatest work;
  and the boundary held one more time at the climax — the fourth book keeps the third book's
  hardest promise.

### `crumb:anunnaki-tradition` — the belief that pointed at something
- REV seed: Leila's ancient-astronaut scholarship (tradition, never fact); REL landing: the
  Builders' works.
- **A21 landing:** the visitors' repair manifest echoes the Enki tradition (*mined to mend a
  dying world*) — and characters who know the texts *notice*. The prose still never adopts
  the mythic names as narration (MYTHOS Rule 1).

### `crumb:the-choice-at-the-switch` — Priya's refusal
- REL: the consent climax.
- **A21 landing:** the founding myth of the tuning guild (uncredited in their manuals); the
  midpoint pilgrimage; the climax rhyme — the same act, seventy years and one species-scale
  step later.

### `crumb:the-note-that-carries` — the throwaway line
- R back-seed (sanctioned in REL's weave): a tone in the deep rock that "shouldn't carry."
- **A21 landing:** the 2064 waking-note — the sound that crossed to somewhere and brought the
  visitors. The epilogue's final image closes this crumb across all four books.

## Back-seeds (planted here, reloading the trilogy on re-read)

- **The sealed 2064 logs** mention a calibration protocol older than the guild — a trilogy
  reader recognises Priya's hand; a newcomer reads "legacy protocol."
- **Guild manual epigraph** (Act I texture): an unattributed engineer's line about reading
  the agent behind the design — verbatim-adjacent to Priya's REL register.
- **A Steward's account of the Flare hour** names *three elders* who went down to the
  threshold in 2064 — never named. (Priya ~73, Arin ~64, Leila ~74 at the Flare. Never
  confirmed on the page; the weave rewards, never requires.)
- **Leila's letter** (Act III, if C-05 lands her alive in Aksum): one sentence of Ge'ez the
  book leaves untranslated; REV readers can read it.

## G — the corpus weave (special case)

G is not a trilogy character; he is the *world's* fourth strand — and after Prophet, a man with
a loud decade (2035), refused crowns, partial autobiography rails, and scars the coin cannot
polish. Discipline: **keep trilogy contact faint** — G and the trilogy cast never met on any
page; no retcon meeting. Prophet readers get landings (saint-hunger vs receipt-faith; Kind→Fold;
crowns offered back). Newcomers read “the stack works” and “pilgrims argue about a face.”
(Sanctioned wink, epilogue only: folded coin and garden in the same frame.)

- **A21 landing:** Give / Keep / Understand + saint-hunger / receipt-faith as climates with
