# Reader Glossary — AFRIKA 2100

> Back-matter glossary for the published book. Plain-language, no spoilers past what the
> jacket promises. (Working draft — trim at copy-edit; the book must never *need* it.)

---

**the Accord (the eGoli Accord, 2073)** — The treaty governing the gold trade between Earth
and the visitors: worked gold against technique, settled on the ledger, with a custodial
brake. Its 27-year term expires in 2100 — the Great Renewal.

**Assist frame** — Thin powered exoskeleton descended from the AugmenTech Assist Suit of the
2020s; everyday augmentation, from harvest work to eldercare.

**the Blackout Decade** — The years after the Flare (2064–~2074) when the high-latitude
North rebuilt without its grid. In America, "the hunger years."

**the Compact (the Plain Compact, 2065)** — The partnership between the US Army and the
Amish and Old Order communities that fed North America after the Flare: the Army brought
security, logistics, muscle, hands, and plans; the Plain people brought the knowledge of a
life that never needed the grid.

**eGoli** — isiZulu: *place of gold*; Johannesburg's own name for itself, and the name of the
spaceport on the old Witwatersrand mine belt — the only sanctioned landing ground on Earth.

**the Firstborn (Abokuqala)** — The bloodline-primacy movement holding that the visitors
deem unbroken African ancestry sacred and pale skin a vitamin-starved deformation. The
visitors, asked, declined to rank human genetics — once, unprompted, for the record. The
room reads residence, not blood. The movement marches anyway.

**the Flare (2064)** — The Carrington-class solar storm that collapsed the world's
high-latitude power grids. The southern belt — South Africa foremost — was spared by
geomagnetic latitude.

**electrogravitics / the rotor anomaly** — The discovery, in the 2080s, that a spinning
charged crystalline rotor weighs measurably less than it should. Power-hungry, poorly
scaling, and without an accepted theory, it nonetheless gives the century its heavy-lift
assist — gantries, hoists, suborbital craft. The visitors' hulls appear to do something
related. They decline to discuss it.

**the Fold** — The practice that gathered the world's religious inheritance after the Flare,
folded by the Court-lineage minds through the 420 Code's coherence ladder into a terminal
ethic two sentences long. Congregations still fill the pews on Sunday; the liturgy reads all
scriptures as one inheritance.

**G (Gerhard van Niekerk)** — South African engineer-philosopher (d. 2058), author of the
copyleft 420 Code. His mesh networking, deterministic compute, provenance ledger, and privacy
spine were the rails the recovery ran on. Revered as the Einstein and Tesla of his age.

**Guardian frame** — Heavy rescue and rebuild exoskeleton descended from the mining-rescue
Guardians of the 2020s; the machines that dug the North out.

**the guild (the tuning guild)** — The engineers who maintain the resonance interfaces at
eGoli Spaceport.

**the instrument** — The planetary-scale resonant machine left in deep antiquity beneath
Africa, sung awake in the worst hour of the Flare. Its note is why the visitors came.

**the Ledger** — The tamper-evident provenance and settlement layer of world trade, descended
from G's Coherence Ledger. The coin bears his face.

**the Mesh** — The planetary communications network descended from G's BadgerNet: nothing
long enough to burn, every node a relay. The only comms that survived the Flare.

**piezo strut** — Piezoelectric harvester fitted wherever motion or pressure would otherwise
be wasted — shock absorbers, sway dampers, railbeds, frame joints, footfall pavers. The
recovery's second energy commandment: waste no motion.

**platcap** — Platinum-alloy supercapacitor: the battery of the century, built on the
Bushveld Complex's platinum and one Accord-delivered technique.

**SSP (Secret Space Programs)** — The hidden orbital fleets and budgets disclosed 2066–2070,
when the approaching visitors made the secret impossible to keep.

**the Stewards** — The custodial order, successors of an older brotherhood, who kept the
instrument's gold dispersed and asleep for centuries and hold the Accord's custodial brake.

**the visitors** — The Builders' heirs, landed openly at the Witwatersrand in 2071. They
negotiate like a freight company, pay in knowledge, and buy exactly one commodity: worked
gold.
