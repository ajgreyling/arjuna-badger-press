## LOCKED — drafting locks (closed for the full-send draft)

- **C-03 · G's dates — LOCKED.** b. 1977; 420 Code published copyleft 2026 (corpus-fixed);
  d. **2058**, Eastern Cape — before the Flare. Vindication wholly posthumous: he never saw
  the world adopt his rails. (The stronger myth; the pilgrimage-site scene plays it quiet.)
- **C-04 · Machine personhood statute — LOCKED.** The Personhood Recognition Act, **2043**
  (SA first; treaty-standard by the 2050s). Librarian texture only.
- **C-05 · Arin & Leila — LOCKED.** **Arin Ndlela died 2077**, in the Johannesburg reservoir
  collapse, holding a gallery open in a Guardian frame for eleven hours while four hundred
  people walked out beneath his arms — the guild legend every tuner knows as "the Frame at
  Gate Eleven," **unnamed** in guild telling; only the trilogy reader (and Priya, once, in
  the hinge scene) puts the name to it. **Leila Aziz alive, 110, Aksum**; present in Act III
  only by letter, one untranslated Ge'ez sentence (CROSSBOOK back-seed).
- **C-06 · The SSP veteran — LOCKED: false key.** *Okonkwo Diri* (proposed name final),
  76, former SSP flight-test engineer. His archive appears to explain the visitors (Act II
  wrong-turn) and doesn't; his truth is the rotor-anomaly vindication — right too early.
  He hands Naledi one real thing: the 2066 detection tapes, which date the visitors'
  *departure* — they left for Earth **before** the Flare. The waking-note answered a journey
  already begun. (Deepens M2; explained by C-07's payload, below.)
- **C-07 · THE BIG ONE — LOCKED: reading 2 carrying reading 3.** The payload, in three
  steps as Act II lands them:
  1. **The failing component is Earth's own node.** The instrument beneath Africa — asleep
     for tens of millennia, woken 2064 — is failing: the waking was rough, the idle is
     unstable, and the drift is in the telemetry for anyone honest (Naledi's discrepancy).
     The consignment gold has been *maintaining our machine* — repair real, manifest true,
     and radically incomplete.
  2. **The carrying thread (reading 3):** Earth's node, precisely because it slept, is the
     only node that kept the Builders' original calibration. The network drifted; ours
     stayed true. Part of every consignment carries Earth's reference pitch outward — the
     retune of the whole network against *our* stone. The gift was always mutual; neither
     side said so. (This is why they crossed before the Flare: the network's drift was
     already critical; the 2064 note told them the reference had *survived*.)
  3. **The volume amendment is scaffolding for a handover.** They are not buying more gold;
     they are accelerating a maintenance transfer they doubt we're ready for — the node is
     ours, its upkeep must become ours (their frame of value: custody belongs to the
     resident mind; the Enki tradition read correctly at last). The Renewal's real question,
     hidden in a freight schedule: *will Earth take its own machine back?*
  **Climax shape (locked):** the consent act is **acceptance of the maintenance burden** —
  the guild and the Stewards jointly, a present conscious mind at the threshold (Rule 7-T
  kept: the Court states its boundary and steps aside, again). Naledi stands the first
  watch. The price paid on-page: the guild's innocence (tuning becomes custody; the guild
  becomes what the Brotherhood was) and Naledi's own unencumbered life — she takes the
  watch knowing it is not a posting but a vocation. Renewal signed **amended**: volume
  granted, handover schedule written in, custody stated on the ledger in the open. Marais
  signs it — his arc: the institutionalist who buries the question is the one who must
  formally put it on the record.
- **C-08 · Gogo Dineo — LOCKED: the survey books.** She died in 2098 with the silence
  intact; Naledi inherits the survey books in Act II and finds the unmapped gallery in the
  vellum margins. Objects outlive explanations.
- **C-12 · Spared-belt roster — LOCKED (texture level).** Named on the page: Chile,
  Australia, Kenya, southern Brazil. All others stay unnamed one-liners.
- **C-13 · The Firstborn movement (Abokuqala) — LOCKED.** The bloodline-primacy religious
  movement (visitors allegedly deem pure African blood sacred; whites as vitamin-D
  "deformations") is canon **as a human misreading only** — Rule 9 governs; the visitors
  never endorse or deny beyond protocol; the room's residence-physics and Priya Ellis's
  2064 presence are the standing refutations. Woven into the draft at three points:
  the Renewal-floor exterior (ch-18), Priya's garden (ch-14), and Sela's "to whom" answer
  (ch-20, "not to a bloodline"). Never the antagonist of the plot — the antagonist remains
  Renewal-at-any-price; the movement is the century's weather, not its engine.
