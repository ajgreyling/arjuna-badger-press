# Dedication — AFRIKA 2100 (draft)

> One page. House register: plain, load-bearing, no flourish. Candidates below — choose one
> at line-edit.

---

## Candidate 1

For the ones who keep things working
when nobody is watching and nothing is certain —
the linesmen, the surveyors, the quartermasters, the grandmothers.

The lights came back because of you.

## Candidate 2

For my daughters —

may you inherit a world that asks better questions,
and may you be the ones it asks.

## Candidate 3

For everyone who ever fixed something quietly and signed nothing.

The note carries.
