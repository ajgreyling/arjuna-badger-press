# Style Guide — AFRIKA 2100

> Binding register. The trilogy's house voice, one era on: dry, exact, wonder-from-mechanism —
> warmed by a world that, for once, is *working*. This is not a dystopia and must never read
> like one; it is a recovery, with the scar tissue showing.

---

## The register in one line

**Future-history written like reportage from a working port** — *The Martian*'s procedural
warmth, the trilogy's engineering exactness, the quiet of a Sunday in the Compact zones, and
**Prophet's desk-soul**: consent as posture, weather not cartoons, flat loaded wounds, no
victory lap.

## Sentence craft

- Declaratives carry; subordination earns its keep. Trilogy baseline.
- **Two POV musics** (gate-checked for distinctness):
  - *Naledi chapters:* quicker, warmer, technical nouns from acoustics and tolerance;
    Sesotho/isiZulu loanwords as natural seasoning (never glossed twice); humour at her own
    expense.
  - *Ruth chapters:* plainer, slower, load-and-provision nouns; Deitsch cadence under Army
    brevity; scripture and folded liturgy surfacing unforced. Shorter paragraphs.
- Exposition arrives on the shoulder of work (the trilogy's mine-scene method): calibrations,
  manifests, watch rotations, harvest logistics. **No lectures.** Layer H history fragments
  max one page, always anchored to a present object or scene (TIMELINE gate).

## World-diction

- NAMES.md governs terminology; MYTHOS_RULES governs taboos. The mesh, the ledger, platcaps,
  the Fold, the visitors — lowercase lived-in tech, capitalised institutions.
- The 2100 world's English has drifted *slightly*: ledger-idioms ("put it on the record" →
  "put it on the ledger"), Fold-idioms ("be kind about it" as the ordinary de-escalation), SA
  English as the port's prestige register. Season, don't soak — one drifted idiom per page is
  plenty.

## Rendering the strands

- **The visitors:** flattest prose in the book, on purpose — protocol English, weights and
  tolerances; the reader's unease comes from the flatness. Never adjectives of menace or
  majesty. The three protocol-slips get *one sensory detail each*, no interpretation.
- **The Fold / the Compact / Kind residue:** Brotherhood-discipline — interiority for
  believers, endorsement never in narration; no irony at faith's expense. Show coin/paint
  before explaining. Soft-scrub vs exact cunt is character (Ruth), not narrator sermon.
  Terminal ethic in full at most twice; load-bearing when the coin is honest (Prophet C-10).
- **Weathers (Give/Keep/Understand/saint-hunger/receipt-faith):** arrive as mail, orders,
  hymns, fence noise, ledger footnotes — never as a chart in a POV mouth. Best argument each.
- **Wounds (Gogo silence, brother/wrong order, G’s scars as memory):** Jamie-Uys / Bouwer
  restraint — flat line is the loaded one; no twist gotcha; no diagnosis tourism of the past.
- **Priya's scene:** RELIC's register, aged — shortest sentences in the book, longest
  silences. Do not imitate her old dialogue rhythms exactly; 74 years pass through a voice.
- **Action:** the trilogy's physical realism — loads, heat, failure modes; a platcap farm fire
  or a gallery decompression is written like SUIT.md action: mechanism, consequence, cost.

## Chapter conventions

- Chapter headers carry **place + clock** (e.g. *eGoli Spaceport · five weeks to Renewal*) —
  the two-thread structure needs the reader oriented in half a line.
- Thread alternation is irregular by design (Naledi-weighted ~65/35); never mechanical
  A-B-A-B.
- Layer H fragments set as unheaded italic passages, one page max, landing on the scene they
  shoulder (TIMELINE gate).

## The don'ts (gate-watched)

- No dystopian grime-signalling; no utopian gloss either. Things work *and* cost.
- No Wakanda-pageantry (THEMES §8): African centrality lands through exact work, not anthem.
- No visitor-POV, no visitor-interiority, no cute visitors (MYTHOS Rules 1, 7).
- No trilogy-recap paragraphs — crumbs are texture, never homework (CROSSBOOK invariant 1).
- No sermons: if a theme is stated aloud by a character, someone in the scene should be
  allowed to disagree (the trilogy's both-sides discipline).
