# Epigraphs — AFRIKA 2100

> Candidate epigraphs for the book and its parts. House rule (trilogy): real quotations
> verified before print; in-world quotations labelled as such. Final selection at line-edit.

## Book epigraph (candidates)

1. *"Don't be a cunt. Be kind."* — The 420 Code, terminal ethic (in-world: the whole of the
   law; the folded coin's obverse). **Risk:** leading with the vulgarity sets register hard —
   consider for Part II instead, with the liturgical form up front.
2. *"They came for the gold. It was never about the gold."* — attributed to a tuning-guild
   manual, author unrecorded (in-world; the Priya back-seed).
3. Real-world candidate: Carrington's own 1859 account of the white-light flare ("...two
   patches of intensely bright and white light broke out...") — verify exact text against the
   *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* before use.

## Part epigraphs (three-part structure assumed)

### Part I — THE MANIFEST
- *"The Army brought the plan. We brought the knowing. Neither fed anyone alone."* — oral
  history of the Plain Compact, collected 2081 (in-world).
- Real candidate: an authentic Amish proverb on work and neighbours — source and verify
  (do not invent one; if none verifies, use the in-world oral history alone).

### Part II — THE PILGRIMAGE
- *"Everyone will ask whether we can trust them. Wrong question."* — P.E., private
  correspondence (in-world; the hinge scene's echo).
- *"Be kind."* — liturgical short form, the Fold.

### Part III — THE RENEWAL
- *"A gift you don't understand is not yet a gift. It is a delivery."* — Ma-Rethabile Khumalo,
  to the Renewal conference (in-world).
- Real candidate: Feynman on knowing versus naming ("I learned very early the difference
  between knowing the name of something and knowing something") — verify exact wording and
  source before print; thematic warrant: G's Feynman-lineage in the world's memory.

## Epilogue

- *"The note carries."* — unattributed (in-world; closes `crumb:the-note-that-carries`).
