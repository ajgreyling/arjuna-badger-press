## 1. The place

**eGoli Spaceport**, Witwatersrand, Gauteng — built across the old deep-mine belt southwest of
Johannesburg. The name is the point: *eGoli*, the city of gold, now hosting the buyers.

- **Why here (in-world, evidenced):** the visitors chose the site — the strongest surface
  coupling to the instrument (the Witwatersrand's Archean gold and the Vredefort-adjacent deep
  structures; trilogy canon), plus the mine infrastructure: headgear, shafts, refineries, rail.
  The port is, functionally, **a mine that ships up instead of out.**
- **The bones:** repurposed headgear frames the landing aprons; the refinery row runs
  consignment gold to the visitors' tolerances; the old shafts give service access to the deep
  coupling galleries — including a vitrified bore the trilogy reader will recognise
  (CROSSBOOK_CRUMBS `crumb:vitrified-tunnel`), now catwalked and lit: **the first kilometre of
  the spaceport was carved tens of thousands of years before the spaceport.**
- **The institution:** run by the Port Authority under the eGoli Accord — SA sovereign ground,
  international oversight board, and a **custodial seat** held by the Brotherhood's successor
  order (the Stewards), whose assent is required for every consignment. The **tuning guild**
  (Naledi's) maintains the resonance interfaces; guild culture is the book's home texture —
  part air-traffic control, part organ-builders, part sangoma-respectful silence around the
  deep galleries.

## 2. The Accord (2073; Renewal 2100)

- **The trade:** consignments of **worked gold** — refined past commercial purity, formed to
  the visitors' geometries (the same physical logic as RELIC's key: purity + form = resonant
  function). In return: **technique** — materials science, energy methods, medical process —
  delivered on a schedule, verified on the ledger, never as artifacts (they hand over
  *knowledge*, not devices; a treaty clause the SSP veterans fought for and won).
- **The custodial brake:** the Stewards may halt any consignment without stating cause. Used
  twice since 2073. Nobody outside the order knows why.
- **The Great Renewal (2100):** the Accord's 27-year term expires; the renewal conference at
  eGoli is the book's clock and Act III stage. The visitors have asked for one amendment:
  **volume.** They want more, faster. The amendment is why the world's three positions
  (Give / Keep / Understand-first) are suddenly load-bearing.

## 3. The visitors

> Diction note (binding): in prose they are **the visitors** or by treaty name; never "aliens"
> in narration (characters may say it). Never comic, never horrific, never chummy.

- **Who they say they are:** heirs of the ones who worked this ground in deep antiquity — the
  Builders (trilogy canon). Their account, delivered in protocol language and never expanded:
  the old network of which Earth's instrument is one node has **components failing**; Earth's
  worked gold is required **for repair** (*the Enki tradition — "mined to mend a dying world" —
  walking out of Leila's texts and onto the tarmac*; the rhyme is on purpose and characters
  notice it).
- **How they read on the page (the grounded discipline):** through instruments, protocols,
  liaisons, and consequences. Physical descriptions are spare and factual. They are
  **procedural, patient, and exact**; their patience is the scariest thing about them and the
  book never says so out loud.
- **What they will not answer:** where the failing components are; what happens if repair
  lapses; why the instrument's 2064 waking mattered enough to cross for. Every refusal is
  polite and total. (These are this book's **designated mysteries** — MYTHOS_RULES §M2 —
  except the one Act III must answer: see D5/C-07.)
- **What they have never done:** threatened, demanded, landed anywhere else, or missed a
  payment. In twenty-nine years the relationship's entire weight rests on *commerce and
  courtesy* — which is exactly why the discrepancy Naledi finds is so quietly terrifying.

## 4. The discrepancy (plot-side; keep synced with STORY_BIBLE D5)

The instrument's telemetry — read by the tuning guild since 2064 — shows the consignment gold's
resonance signatures doing something the repair manifest does not describe: **the network is
being tuned, not patched.** What is being brought into tune, and whether that is salvation,
stewardship, or appropriation, is the mystery-box; its grounded resolution is **LOCKED at
CANON_CHOICES C-07** (Earth's node is the failing component *and* the network's surviving
reference pitch; the amendment scaffolds a maintenance handover).

## 5. The port as set-piece inventory

1. The apron at shift-change — headgear silhouettes, a descending hull, the guild at consoles.
2. The refinery row — gold poured to tolerances no jeweller ever needed.
3. The deep coupling galleries — the catwalked vitrified bore; the places phones are left at
   the gate; the silence the miners taught the guild.
4. The Stewards' lodge — the custodial seat; oral canon; the two unexplained halts.
5. The Renewal conference floor — the world's three positions in one room with the counterparty.
6. The threshold itself — where RELIC ended and where this book must also end. One place, two
   books, seventy years apart. (SET_PIECE discipline: each gets one wonder, one cost.)
