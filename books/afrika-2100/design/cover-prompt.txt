AFRIKA 2100 — cover brief (draft)

Series: An African Gold novel · The spiritual fourth
Author: Andries J. Greyling

Concept — "the headgear and the hull":
A Witwatersrand mine headgear (the iconic steel A-frame winding tower) silhouetted at dusk,
gold light breaking behind it — and above/behind it, understated and half-lost in the haze, the
suggestion of an immense berthed hull. NOT a flying-saucer cliché: read as architecture, a
patient shape at anchor. In the foreground, small human figures at a console line (the tuning
guild at shift-change) to set the scale.

Palette: continuity with the trilogy covers — deep golds, iron blacks, high-veld dusk blues.
The green resonance-note star (the trilogy's "true gold" mark) may appear once, small, as the
brand crumb.

Mood: grounded, monumental, working — a port, not a battlefield. Awe from scale and stillness.

Typography: series-consistent with RESONANCE / REVELATION / RELIC. Title "AFRIKA 2100" in the
trilogy's display face; "2100" may carry the gold foil treatment.

Avoid: saucers, beams of light, green aliens, ruined-city dystopia signalling, Wakanda
pastiche. The world on this cover WORKS.

cover.json metadata — per sibling books' design/ folders.
