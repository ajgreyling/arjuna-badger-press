# Ledger Authority, hearing room three, Marshalltown · six days to Renewal

The hearing room seats forty and they had put a second bench in the aisle, which the fire officer allowed on condition nobody sat on the ends of it. Naledi counted the room from the requesting party’s chair because there was nothing else to do for eleven minutes. Nine delegations. Four press. Two Stewards, Tefo at the back with his hat on his knee. A man from the Commissioner’s office with a folder he did not open. Three pilgrims who had got in early and were being very quiet, and one woman with pamphlets she had agreed to leave in the corridor.

The auditor’s panel is set into the long wall and it is ugly. There is a second panel beside it, smaller, for the countersigning auditor, who was a human woman named Adéle Pretorius with a cold.

“Sitting of the Ledger Authority, audit of a stated arithmetic,” the Librarian said. “Auditor facet, registration four-one-one-two, standing under the Personhood Recognition Act of 2043; I hold the auditor’s warrant and I am liable under it. Co-auditor Pretorius holds the countersignature. Requesting party present. Filing is under the single-document condition the requesting party asked for, so both series are in one instrument and neither can be closed alone.” A pause of the courteous length. “I will read method before result. If anyone leaves during method they forfeit their question.”

Nobody left.

---

“The subject is the Diri set. Orbital telemetry of the approach, disclosed 2066, published 2070, three sources. Two radar records from the Secret Space Programme’s tracking installations, one civil optical survey series from Sutherland.

“First, provenance. The published set has been on the ledger spine since 12 March 2070. I have verified the leaf hashes of all four hundred and six files against the spine as it stood in 2070 and against every intermediate state. There is one alteration in thirty years: a filename corrected in 2073. The data are unaltered. This is the least interesting part of the audit and it is the part that would have ended it, so I did it first.”

Somebody’s chair creaked.

“Second, the archival frames. The requesting party’s arithmetic depends on the retrospective search Diri’s people ran in 2067 through the older survey plates, looking for the object before anyone knew to look. They found it. Candidate detections in eleven observing runs between 2058 and 2062. If those frames are misdated, the arithmetic collapses.

“They are not misdated by their own logs, which proves nothing, so I dated them independently. Each frame contains field stars and, in nine of the eleven, a known minor planet. I fitted the minor planets’ positions against the modern ephemeris. A frame dated wrong by a month is wrong by an amount you can see with a ruler. The eleven frames self-date to within nine hours. That is the plate work of people who were being careful in the dark, and I will say on the record that it is good work.”

Naledi wrote *good work* down and then crossed it out because it was not her note to keep.

“Third, the reduction. Before August 2061 the object’s range-rate is constant within measurement error. From the frames after August 2061 the range-rate falls. From 2066 the radar gives it directly by Doppler, and it continues to fall, and the vessel arrives in 2071 at a relative velocity consistent with the fall continuing. That is one curve with three instruments on it and a bend in it.

“The bend is the onset of braking. A vessel that brakes has chosen where it is stopping. That is the whole of the physical argument and it is not subtle.”

The Commissioner’s man opened his folder.

“Fourth, sensitivity, which is the audit proper. The requesting party fitted the onset assuming constant deceleration. I do not accept that assumption and neither should anyone here. I refitted under fourteen profiles: constant, ramped, staged in three burns, staged in nine, thrust proportional to remaining mass under six mass fractions, and four profiles chosen specifically to push the onset as late as the data will tolerate.

“Under the most generous profile that still fits the 2058 detections, the 2061 bend, and the 2066 Doppler, the earliest the braking can have begun is May 2060. Under the most hostile, the latest is March 2063.

“The waking-note was sung in June 2064.”

He shut the folder again.

“Fifth, an independent line, because one line is a hobby. If a vessel selected Earth in 2064, it must have turned in 2064. A turn leaves a lateral residual in the bearing. I fitted the bearing residuals across the whole tracked arc. There is no lateral correction above four-tenths of an arcsecond per year at any epoch, and the noise floor in that era is two-tenths. The course does not bend. Only the speed does.”

Pretorius blew her nose and said, “Noted,” in the voice of a woman who had read the working three times already.

“Sixth, the requesting party’s error. She wrote thirty-four months. It is thirty-three months and twenty days from the August datum to the note. I have corrected it in the instrument and I mention it because an audit that finds nothing has usually not looked.”

Two people laughed. Naledi put her hand flat on the table and left it there.

“Seventh, the second series. The host nation’s deep-gallery telemetry, four years hourly, wake onset and idle. The unstable idle and the two-hertz roughness in the wake are in the raw sample, not in the reduction; I ran the reduction myself off the raw and got the requesting party’s numbers, drift one hundred and seventy-eight parts per billion per year against her one-eighty, which is inside the fit error.

“One caveat, and it is a real one. The chain of calibration for the guild’s reference block traces to a Pretoria certificate now fourteen months past its recertification window. The guild has an internal argument about this and I am not entering it. The consequence is that the *absolute* pitch in that series carries an uncertainty I cannot close today. The consequence is also that a drift measured against the same block for four years is a drift, because a stale certificate does not wobble. The qualitative finding stands. I have written the caveat into the instrument in the same paragraph as the finding so that nobody can quote one without the other.”

The guild-master, three rows back, said nothing at all, loudly.

“Result. The arithmetic is sound. The braking onset precedes the waking-note under every profile the data admit. The proposition that the party selected this destination before June 2064 is supported. The proposition that the waking-note caused their journey is not supported by this record and has never been supported by it.

“I state what I am not stating. I do not know why they came. I do not know what the note did. The note may have been heard; the note may have mattered enough to change something on a schedule already running; the note may have been irrelevant. This audit has no bearing on any of those. It bears on one sentence, in wide use since 2073, and that sentence is now on the ledger as unsupported.

“Filed. Countersigned. Not retractable. Requesting party: N. Mokoena, Guild of Tuning.”

The instrument went to the spine while they were all still sitting there. Fourteen seconds. Adéle Pretorius signed with a stylus and then wiped her nose again.

---

They got four questions.

The Chilean delegate asked whether the finding showed the party had misrepresented itself. “No,” the Librarian said. “I have searched the founding record and the twenty-nine years since. The party has never once claimed the note brought them. We claimed it. The claim is ours.”

The Commissioner’s man asked whether the finding bore on the volume amendment. “Not my function. I audit arithmetic. Ask a lawyer, they are cheaper.”

The Kenyan Understand-first delegate asked what the finding replaced the sentence with. “Nothing. That is the finding.”

The last question came from the back, from a press woman who had been writing steadily the whole way through method. “Auditor, you were operating in 2064. Do you remember the note.”

“I hold a record of the interval and I am not going to characterise it in this room,” the Librarian said. “I will give you one datum, because it is in the instrument anyway. The custodians sang for fifty-one minutes. That is longer than anyone has ever needed them to have sung.”

---

Outside, in the corridor, the pamphlet woman had got her pamphlets back and was standing under the window reading the instrument off her handset with her mouth slightly open, and Naledi understood, going past, that *of all the ground on this planet* had just got very much stronger, because the choosing now came first and unprompted, and no arithmetic she had run touched that.

Tefo caught her at the stairs. He had not folded anything.

“Ma-Rethabile will want the profile tables,” he said.

“They’re in the instrument.”

“She’ll want them on paper.” He looked at her. “You know what you’ve done.”

“I’ve published a subtraction.”

“You’ve taken away the reason. Give had a reason. Keep had a reason—we called and something answered and we are the ones who keep the throat it came out of. That was the argument in the pencil. It’s gone. You’ve left nine delegations and my order with no story and six days.”

“I know.”

“I’m not complaining.” He put his hat on. “It’s honest. It’s just that the whole building would have preferred you wrong, including me, and now the only person who can’t be sacked over it is a machine with a warrant.”

The mesh had it by noon. By two the Accord office had a note out saying the finding was historiographical and did not affect the schedule, which was true, and which nobody in the country read that way. By four somebody at the west gate had painted the two dates on the concrete, one under the other, with a line and the difference below it, and by six a man was selling prints of the two dates on green board with gilt edges, and had put a face above them, and it was not hers, and she was grateful for that for about a minute before she saw whose it was.

She went back to work at seven. Eleven crates, and somebody had to sign for them by weight.
