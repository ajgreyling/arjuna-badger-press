THE CREW:
- **Frik** (lead): ND (Tourette’s, verbal tics, compulsive truth-telling); the fact-puncturer, blurts out inconvenient realities, immune to social smoothing. Reads the data, not the room; his wiring is his engine, never a wound. Arc: learns when to *withhold* (for the local’s sake), not just to blurt.
- **LENS**: ex-war photographer (PTSD), sees what *shouldn’t* be in frame; her camera is the reader’s, always hovering at the edge of the action, asking to be lowered. Arc: learns to let the local set the frame.
- **Other crew**: (rotating, but at least one other ND member; e.g., autistic systems analyst, anxious pattern-spotter; never caricature, always specificity and dignity).
- **Jakobus**: the weathered fixer; reads made things for function, not myth; always the road, never the lead, no POV; hands the moment to the crew/local, fades into the background as soon as they can stand.

THE LOCAL:
- **Dr. Mara Tallchief** (local/custodian): Native archaeologist, cultural authority, descendant of Nations with ties to the earthworks but careful not to claim what cannot be claimed. Out-reads the crew on the land’s meaning; owns the “what is it for.” Arc: weighs what to share, what to withhold; never a gatekeeper, always the true owner of the answer.

THE ADVERSARY:
- **Heritage Foundation Acquisition Team**: led by Eric Sandoval, project manager; clean-handed, earnest, wants to “settle the story” for the world heritage application and the visitor experience; not a villain, but invested in a single, marketable answer. Arc: learns the cost of certainty, but never “defeated”—they are changed, not destroyed.

VOICES: Crew is close-third, each interior distinct and specific, never omniscient. The local owns the answer; the crew never out-frame her. Wiring is how they see, not a wound. Dignity and agency for all, especially the local.
