The travelling ND crew (load-bearing ND lead: the MONEY/fixer — reads grift, extraction, and modern stakes with the clarity of the outsider always watching the room; wiring is how they see, never a wound or caricature), the LENS (ex-war photographer, PTSD, the camera the series keeps asking to lower), and two supporting crew: the young engineer (hyperfocus, pattern-spotting, literal-minded), and the archivist (quiet, encyclopedic, always reading the footnotes). Jakobus: the weathered fixer, the only thread across books, reads a made thing by function, never takes the lead, defers to crew and local, never POV. THE LOCAL/custodian: a Louisiana state-site archaeologist, rooted in the descendant community, whose knowledge and authority over the site’s story is absolute — out-reads the crew, can withhold or hand off the “what is it for.” The clean-handed adversary: the “Heritage Development Consortium,” represented by a plausible, charming project lead who genuinely believes in “unlocking” the site’s value — profit, not conspiracy, no villain. Voices are distinct, arcs driven by what they *see* and *miss* because of their wiring, never a healing journey. Close-third POV: always inside the crew, never omniscient, the place and the local never reduced to props.
