Act I: ARRIVE — The crew lands at Poverty Point, summoned by news that a heritage consortium is negotiating for “development” rights. The ND MONEY/fixer reads the grift in the gleaming proposal; the LENS is drawn to the landscape’s stubborn endurance. OBSTACLE — The consortium’s lead has state officials’ ear, promising jobs and preservation, but the local archaeologist (the custodian) is wary. The site’s official story is staged: the foragers with no kings, the baskets of earth, the marvel of engineering. The crew attempts to reconstruct (POTATO): even with plans and daylight, their earth-move is slow, muddy, and the math goes sideways.

Act II: READ — The crew digs into the soil science (Kidder’s microstratigraphy), the basket-load calculations, the post-ring magnetometry, the continental trade routes. They interview the local, who deflects on the “why” but points them to the engineering marvel: the deliberate mixing of soils, the resistance to erosion, the absence of weathering between layers. COST — The consortium offers funding for the site, but strings are attached: “interpretive centers,” curated narratives, and limited local access. The ND fixer sees the extraction coming; the LENS documents the tension, the pride, the risk of loss.

Act III: KEY — The crew pieces together the evidence: a gathering of thousands, a fevered build, a mountain raised in a season, no palaces, no leadership, no heir. The local, pressed, names the residue: “We know how they built it, but not what called them here — or why they stopped.” The “what is it for” is offered, not solved: a gathering place, a ceremony, a map, a memory, a function lost to time but present in the ground. HANDOFF — The local decides what to share and what to hold back; the crew respects the boundary. The consortium’s offer is rebuffed: the community votes to preserve, not commodify. The chase resolves: the site remains, the story open, the wonder intact.

Back matter: All measurements, dates, and claims are cited; rapid-build evidence sourced to Kidder and others; trade network debate footnoted; “impossible” labelled as extraordinary but not magic; function left open. The reader is left with the residue, the maybe, and the right to wonder.
