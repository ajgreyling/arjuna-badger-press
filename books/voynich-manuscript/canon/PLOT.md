**Act I — The Object**  
ARRIVE: Crew enters the Beinecke, seeking the physical MS.  
OBSTACLE: Security, protocols, the object’s fragility; adversary’s agent lobbying for exclusive access.  
READ: C-14 dating, pigment analysis, codicology — the MS is physically real, 1404–1438, not a modern fake.  
COST: The easy “forgery” answer is dead; the crew must confront a real, used, unreadable book.  
KEY: The custodian (codicologist) demonstrates the limits of what can be known from the object.  
HANDOFF: Crew is allowed a supervised session; the adversary is rebuffed, for now.

**Act II — The Cipher**  
ARRIVE: Crew tries to crack the code, enlists codebreakers, studies Friedmans’ work.  
OBSTACLE: Statistical anomalies, too-fluent hand, verbose cipher theory fails under scrutiny.  
READ: The best cryptanalysts failed; modern AI cracks only toy ciphers, not the MS.  
COST: The “easy” code angle closes — no plausible cipher fits the evidence.  
KEY: The custodian/living expert shows the limits of cryptanalysis; the real “crack” is a mirage.  
HANDOFF: Crew forced to let go of the code-solve; adversary pivots to claiming a “breakthrough.”

**Act III — The Language**  
ARRIVE: Crew pursues the language angle — Hebrew, Turkic, proto-Romance, etc.  
OBSTACLE: Every claimed language needs the glyphs bent to fit; circular reasoning exposed.  
READ: Linguistic statistics (Zipf’s law, topic clusters) — language-like, but no known tongue.  
COST: The “find the language” door closes; the honest “we don’t know” looms.  
KEY: The linguist local/custodian demonstrates the difference between reading and projecting.  
HANDOFF: Crew must admit the MS defeats language-matching; adversary claims a new “solution” for publicity.

**Act IV — The Nonsense**  
ARRIVE: Crew tests hoax theories — Rugg’s grille, self-citation, AI pseudo-text.  
OBSTACLE: Demonstrations fail to reproduce the MS’s deep structure, low entropy, multiple scribes, heavy use.  
READ: The MS resists “hoax” — too complex, too used, too consistent.  
COST: Even “elegant nonsense” fails — the book is lawful, but not empty.  
KEY: The custodian’s refusal: the MS’s silence is its own answer.  
HANDOFF: The adversary attempts to “own” the narrative with a media stunt; crew steps back.

**Act V — The Hand**  
ARRIVE: Jakobus’s master question: “what was it for?”  
OBSTACLE: The MS’s function is use and keeping, not content; nobody ever read it — not even Jakobus.  
READ: The book was a working manual, used but unread; the act of keeping is its function.  
COST: Naila faces the cost — the maybe will never be a yes, and that is allowed.  
KEY: The custodian’s final handoff — “some things are made to be unread.”  
HANDOFF: The crew leaves the MS unclaimed, the question open, the adversary’s narrative hollow.  
Back matter: dossier of real facts (dating, ink, statistics), labelled fringe theories (clearly marked), and invented chase details (clearly separated, for story only).
