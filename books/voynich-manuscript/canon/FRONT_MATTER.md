> **A note before you begin.**
>
> Everything physical in this book is real. Beinecke MS 408 — the Voynich Manuscript — is a genuine early-fifteenth-century book, its calfskin radiocarbon-dated to between 1404 and 1438, written in a fluent hand by roughly five scribes, in cheap honest period inks, thumbed soft from daily use. It obeys the statistics of language and matches no language on Earth, and it is not random. The finest codebreakers of two world wars failed it. Every claimed solution since has failed verification. The whole manuscript is online, free, in perfect colour, at the Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library — naked in public, and still shut.
>
> The crew who sit with it here are invented. The failed solvers named as record — Newbold, the Friedmans, Tiltman, Currier, Bax, Gibbs, Cheshire, Rugg — are real, and are held with respect: the ache of the reach, never the laugh at the reacher. No living person is the villain. The one who manufactures a solution for profit is a fiction.
>
> This book does not solve the manuscript. No one has. That is not its failure; it is its subject.
