The ND crew (travelling, at least one ND member load-bearing): 
- The LENS (crew lead for this book): world-famous aerial photographer, ex-war photographer (PTSD, not ND), sees the world in patterns from above, both a gift and a limit—can see the lines but not walk them. 
- Hydrology-reader (ND, pattern-finder): obsessed with water systems, traces the lines’ subtle correlation with groundwater and faults, never quite closes the loop.
- Other crew: support roles, all marginal in some way, wiring is how they see, never wounds, never caricature.
- Jakobus: the weathered fixer, reads a made thing for “what is it for,” never leads, no POV, hands the moment to the crew/local, always the road.
- The Local: Cahuachi-born Peruvian archaeologist, field-trained, keeper of the lines, out-reads both crews, owns the “what is it for,” never outflanked or appropriated, their answer is theirs to give or withhold.
- The adversary: the international tech consortium, well-funded, clean-handed, wants data and patents, not a villain but a plausible, fallible human force.
All arcs dramatized through action, never declared; crew POV is close-third, the local owns the answer and is never out-read.
