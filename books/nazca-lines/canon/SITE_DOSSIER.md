Nazca Lines — Peru (Nazca/Palpa pampa geoglyphs)

**REAL:**
- Geoglyphs located on the Nazca and Palpa pampas, southern Peru.
- Created by removing the reddish iron-oxide-patinated surface pebbles (~10–15 cm deep) to reveal pale subsoil beneath; lines are the contrast.
- Dated to c. 400 BC – AD 500 (Paracas and Nazca cultures); cult centre at Cahuachi.
- Construction method: stakes and cord; no advanced or unknown technology used. A wooden anchor stake at a line terminus was radiocarbon-dated.
- Maria Reiche surveyed, mapped, and protected the lines from 1940 onward.
- 2024 PNAS study (Sakai et al., Yamagata University/IBM/DLR): AI analysis mapped 303 new figurative glyphs.
    - "Relief-type" glyphs: ~9 m, ~43 m from footpaths, 81.6% human themes.
    - "Line-type" glyphs: ~90 m, 64% wild animals.
    - Conclusion: roadside ritual stations (relief-type) + communal line-figures (line-type); not a single unified mystery.
- The Nazca plain is one of Earth's driest, most windless surfaces (~20 min of rain per year). Morning mist hardens a crust that armours the lines.
- The puquios (ancient, still-functioning corkscrew aqueducts) are real hydro-engineering features.
- Some lines correlate with faults channelling groundwater to the puquios (David Johnson, minority view).
- Mainstream view (Donald Proulx, UMass): lines are ritual/processional, tied to water/fertility, not literal water maps.
- Hawkins (1973) and Aveni (1982) tested star/calendar alignments: only ~20% align, no better than chance—statistically refutes the "calendar" theory.
- The lines are only ~10–15 cm deep; the ground is soft enough for a boot to mark it.

**CLAIMED/FRINGE:**
- Erich von Däniken (1968, *Chariots of the Gods?*): "spacecraft runways"—universally dismissed by mainstream archaeologists; ground is too soft for heavy vehicles or craft.
- Tourist lore persists regarding astronomical/calendar functions, but these are not supported by statistical or archaeological evidence.
- Water/fault overlay: correlation between lines and faults/puquios is real, but the strong version ("lines = water map") is a minority position and interpretation is contested.

**SCENE-READY:**
- AI survey continues to find more glyphs, expanding the known catalogue.
- The preservation of the lines is a product of hyper-arid, windless climate and the crust formed by morning mist.
- The lines' true scale and many figures are only visible from the air.
