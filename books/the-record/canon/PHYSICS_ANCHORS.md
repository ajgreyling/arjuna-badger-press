# Physics Anchors — *THE RECORD*

> Real physics the fiction stands on, and the exact line where it stops being real.
> Extends `SERIES_BIBLE.md` §1 (canon and boundary map). Same rule applies: **the novel's
> credibility depends on preserving the honest boundary.**

---

## 1 · Landauer's Principle — SOLID GROUND

**Rolf Landauer**, IBM, **1961**. (Frequently misspelled *Ralph*. A character getting this wrong,
and being corrected flatly by someone who cares, is good texture and free characterisation.)

Landauer did **not** calculate the weight or mass of a bit. He established something better for our
purposes:

> Erasing one bit of information requires a minimum thermodynamic energy of
> **E = k**<sub>**B**</sub>**T ln 2**, dissipated as heat.

At room temperature (300 K) that is **2.87 × 10⁻²¹ J** — 2.87 zeptojoules — per bit erased.

Two consequences, both load-bearing for this series:

1. **Information is physical.** Data operations are not abstract; they are tied to matter and
   thermodynamics. This is not a metaphor and not fringe.
2. **Erasure is not free, and it is not silent.** Forgetting has a minimum price, and the price is
   paid in heat that goes somewhere.

**Status:** mainstream, and **experimentally confirmed** — Bérut *et al.*, *Nature*, 2012, measured
the bound in a single-particle system; later work confirmed it in other substrates. This is the one
piece of the machine's philosophical foundation that needs no fictional extrapolation at all.

### What it buys the story

- **It answers Dries's crude question with a real ancestor.** In Beat 5 he asks what physical
  operation releases a record when ordinary traces disappear. Landauer is the century-old hint that
  the question is not stupid: the universe already charges for deletion.
- **It grounds Ghana's ingestion rule.** Ama's system never deletes — it demotes. Landauer says
  deletion is a thermodynamic act with a signature; demotion is cheaper, reversible and honest.
- **It gives concealment its physics.** Destroying evidence is *erasure at scale*. The cover-up is
  thermodynamically expensive and therefore loud. This is why the machine reads the tidying better
  than the killing (Beat 30) — not merely because tidying takes longer, but because erasing costs.
- **It is the series' quiet thesis in one line.** *You cannot forget for free.* Book I: the past
  will not stay erased. Book II: a civilisation that stops paying for behavioural diversity finds
  out what that cost was for.

**Line to preserve:** “Landauer worked out the price of forgetting in 1961. Nobody thought to ask
for the receipt.”

## 2 · Mass–energy–information equivalence — EXPLICITLY FRINGE

**Melvin Vopson** (2019) extended Landauer with **E = mc²** to propose that a stored bit carries a
small mass:

> **m = k**<sub>**B**</sub>**T ln 2 / c²** ≈ **3.19 × 10⁻³⁸ kg** per bit at 300 K.
> A completely full 1 TB device would, on this proposal, weigh about **2.6 × 10⁻²⁵ kg** more than an
> empty one. *(8 × 10¹² bits × 3.19 × 10⁻³⁸ kg. Figures verified: 3.194 × 10⁻³⁸ and 2.556 × 10⁻²⁵.)*

**Status: speculative and not accepted.** It is a proposal, not a result. Landauer's principle
concerns the *energy cost of an operation*; the mass claim converts a dissipation bound into stored
rest mass, which is a large step that mainstream physics has not taken. No experiment has confirmed
it.

### BINDING — how this is used

The consortium **does not stand on it, does not need it, and refuses to claim it.** The machine
reads dispersed physical consequence, not stored mass. Nothing in record inversion requires
information to weigh anything.

Its role in the novels is as **the fringe limb** — the second live falsifiable claim beside the
already-fired neutron–proton limb:

- **The press and the believers love it**, because "history has weight" is a sentence anyone can
  repeat and nobody has to understand.
- **The promoters of the smooth fake reach for it**, because a claim that the past has mass makes
  the reconstruction sound like retrieval of a substance rather than solution of a constraint.
- **G refuses it publicly and irritably**, exactly as he refused "confirmed" and "proved" in Beat 3.
  He will not accept a free win from a claim he cannot kill.
- **The consortium's refusal to claim it reads as weakness** to people who wanted a bolder story,
  and that is the point: the discipline that makes them credible is the same discipline that makes
  them look unconvincing on television.

**Never write** the consortium's instruments as weighing information, detecting the mass of a
record, or confirming Vopson. Any character who says the machine "weighs" the past is either
selling something, mistaken, or being quoted.

## 3 · Where the fiction actually starts

| Claim | Status | Used how |
|---|---|---|
| Information is physical | **Established** (Landauer 1961) | Foundation; stated plainly, early |
| Erasing a bit costs k<sub>B</sub>T ln 2 | **Established, measured** (Bérut 2012) | Concealment physics; Ghana's no-delete rule |
| A stored bit has mass ~3.19 × 10⁻³⁸ kg | **Speculative, unconfirmed** (Vopson 2019) | Fringe limb; press, believers, forgers. Consortium refuses |
| Actualised events leave *recoverable* dispersed correlations | **Fiction** (2030 in-world) | The primary speculative break |
| Those correlations can be inverted to a unique past | **Fiction** (2031 in-world) | Book I's invention |
| Verified history + live telemetry yields decaying future cones | **Fiction** (2033 in-world) | Book II's invention |

The honest ladder matters more than any single rung. A reader who checks Landauer finds it true;
that earns the two rungs above it. A reader who checks Vopson finds it contested — and finds that
the book said so first.

## 4 · Words to police (extends `CRAFT.md`)

| Avoid as unqualified shorthand | Prefer |
|---|---|
| "Information has mass." | A proposed equivalence puts a bound-derived mass on a stored bit; it is unconfirmed and we do not rely on it. |
| "Landauer proved data has weight." | Landauer set the minimum energy to *erase* a bit. The mass claim is a later, separate, contested extension. |
| "We weighed the record." | We measured dispersed consequences and solved backward under constraint. |
| "Erasure is impossible." | Erasure is possible, priced, and dissipative — it leaves heat, and heat goes somewhere. |
| "Ralph Landauer" | **Rolf** Landauer. |
