## Notes on two of them

**The names are not a formality.** The bible marks every ensemble name provisional and the beat
ledger uses them throughout. That is fine — they are working handles. But a name that survives to
draft by inertia has been chosen by nobody, and this book cannot afford that in Accra or Nairobi.

**The composite case review runs the other way from usual.** It does not check a case for
permission; it checks that no permission is needed — that nothing has drifted close enough to a real
disappearance to require one. Drift is the failure mode, and it accumulates quietly during drafting.
