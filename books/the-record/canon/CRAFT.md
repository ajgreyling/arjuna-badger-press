# Craft — Exposition & Drafting Toolkit — *THE RECORD*

> §10 and §13 of the bible. How to make readers enjoy the mechanisms without lectures, and how to
> turn architecture into chapters.

---

## The five-step explanation loop

1. Give the team a concrete **observable problem**.
2. Let two specialists **disagree** using different mental models.
3. Build or alter an **experiment that distinguishes** the models.
4. Show the **result before explaining** its full meaning.
5. Make the result create an immediate **operational or moral consequence**.

## Recurring demonstration scale

| Concept | Small demonstration | Later payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Record persistence | Break a marked object in a sealed room and reconstruct the break | Recover the historical death |
| Distributed trace | Remove individual sensor families; no single one contains the image | Prove the Anchor is not magical storage |
| Constraint | Wrong coordinates produce nothing useful | Material anchors and jurisdiction matter |
| Uncertainty | A hand crosses behind an obstruction and becomes a probability volume | Leaked synthetic face is exposed |
| Prediction decay | Forecast a pendulum, then a human choice, then a market response | Forward cone remains probabilistic |
| Observer effect through information | Tell one test subject the forecast and withhold it from another | Public forecasts modify catastrophe probability |
| Behavioural diversity | Two crude local controllers outperform one optimised controller after an unexpected fault | Distributed local foresight prevents Convergence |

## Words to police

| Avoid as unqualified shorthand | Prefer |
|---|---|
| "The aether recorded a video." | Residual correlations across the substrate constrain a reconstruction. |
| "Quantum memory remembers everything." | Actualised interactions leave distributed physical consequences. |
| "Lucid sees the future." | Lucid derives a decaying probability cone from verified present constraints. |
| "The blockchain proves it is true." | The ledger proves sequence and integrity; independent evidence supports truth. |
| "The AI knows." | The engine computes, cites, conflicts or refuses. |
| "CODATA proved the 420 Code." | A frozen expression remains compatible with a measured value; future precision can strengthen or kill it. |

## Scene card fields

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| POV need | Why is this the only useful consciousness through which to experience the scene? |
| Concrete objective | What must the character obtain, test, prevent or decide before the scene ends? |
| Constraint | What resource, rule, relationship or clock prevents the obvious solution? |
| Method | What competent action does the character attempt? |
| Result | What measurable thing changes? |
| Reversal | How does success reveal a worse problem, or failure reveal a new path? |
| Ethical residue | Who bears the cost even if the technical problem is solved? |
| Plant/payoff tag | Which earlier element is used, or which later element is quietly planted? |

## Chapter rhythm

- Alternate instrument-scale problem solving with human-scale consequences; avoid three consecutive conference-room chapters.
- End most technical chapters on a **result**, not on an explanation.
- Allow occasional **quiet awe chapters** after major breakthroughs so the reader can feel the achievement before exploitation begins.
- Use documents sparingly: query receipts, confidence maps, incident logs and quorum notices should sharpen stakes, not replace scenes.
- When the machine reconstructs history, focus on **what remains missing** as much as what appears.
- Action arises from access, custody, time pressure, environmental danger and infrastructure — **not generic gunmen** whenever avoidable.
- No villain explains the whole plan. Institutions reveal themselves through procedures, offers, injunctions and resource control.

## Competence-porn checklist

- The problem is legible enough that the reader can form a hypothesis.
- The solution uses a tool or rule already established.
- Specialists make different, defensible assumptions.
- Failure produces **diagnostic information** rather than mere delay.
- Improvisation carries governance or safety cost.
- The final answer remains falsifiable and displays uncertainty.
- The smartest person in the scene is not automatically the highest-status person.

## Fair-play mystery rules

- Every major solution must use a principle demonstrated earlier in a smaller experiment.
- Do not conceal a rule of the machine solely to manufacture a twist.
- The leaked reconstruction must contain **visible confidence smoothing** before characters identify it.
- The valid-signature flaw must be stated early: the ledger proves an event occurred, not that its content is correct.
- The forward cone must show multiple branches from its first appearance; avoid a single cinematic vision.
- Mercer's saved lives must be numerically and emotionally present before the harms of convergence are revealed.
- The final distributed solution must cost speed, elegance, convenience and some preventable lives; otherwise decentralisation becomes a slogan.
