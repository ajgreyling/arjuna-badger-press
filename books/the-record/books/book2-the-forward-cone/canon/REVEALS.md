# Reveal Ladder — *THE FORWARD CONE* (Book II)

| Reveal | Reader initially believes | Later reinterpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 96.8% catastrophe | One hidden event must be found | Many independent routes converge |
| Successful interventions barely help | Lucid may be rigged or fatalistic | Local successes are real; **system substitution** preserves global risk |
| Antarctic blank | The future leaves a negative record | Predictive coherence fails where **human feedback dominates** |
| Mercer's platform works | The antagonist has a trap | The benefits are genuine and create **voluntary** adoption |
| Seed leak | Someone seeks market domination | Mass access creates **computational monoculture** |
| Lucid recommends devolution | The machine has solved the crisis | It labels its own answer contaminated and **refuses command** |
| Global plan fails | Humanity is doomed | **Global legibility itself** is the failure mode |
| Partition succeeds | Ignorance saves the world | **Plural, bounded knowledge** preserves both warning and adaptive diversity |
