# SEED_STORY — *THE FORWARD CONE* (Book II)

> §6 of the series bible. **2033–34.** Systems countdown → prediction feedback → restoration of
> uncertainty.

---

## Controlling ideas

| Element | Design |
|---|---|
| **External goal** | Identify and prevent a global discontinuity **before the team's interventions become part of its cause** |
| **Internal goal** | Move the ensemble from responsible **custody of truth** to responsible **surrender of control** |
| **MacGuffin** | **THE SEED** — a portable, deterministic Lucid core plus compressed causal graph that runs on ordinary CPUs |
| **Central mystery** | Why do thousands of unrelated causal paths converge on the same narrow catastrophe window? |
| **Apparent catastrophe** | Market crash, grid failure, logistics seizure, military escalation, Antarctic signal loss |
| **True catastrophe** | **THE CONVERGENCE** — predictive systems cause institutions to behave alike, eliminating counter-behaviour and making the world catastrophically brittle |
| **Antagonistic force** | Mercer's optimisation coalition, which **demonstrably saves lives** and therefore gains voluntary control |
| **Climactic solution** | Partition global foresight into local, incomplete warning systems that cannot be recombined by any single authority |
| **Ending change** | Humanity retains bounded foresight but gives up omniscience; **the future becomes plural again** |

## Future-prediction rules (binding)

| Rule | Effect |
|---|---|
| **No future record exists** | Lucid calculates admissible continuations; it never reads tomorrow |
| **Confidence decays with horizon** | Seconds can be near-certain in bounded systems; months yield broad event families |
| **Forecast publication changes the graph** | A disclosed result creates new behaviour and must be recalculated |
| **Already-made decisions are "nearly present"** | Contracts, shipments and concealed orders can make announcements highly predictable |
| **Human models are conditional** | People may choose differently, but choices are constrained by incentives, information and institutions |
| **No fluent salvation** | When evidence ends, Lucid **refuses**. It will not invent a plan because the audience wants one |

## Ending state

- The Convergence is prevented, but **several regional disasters still occur**; the ending is earned, not painless.
- Lucid survives as **local sovereign instruments** that compute, cite, conflict and refuse.
- No actor retains the universal ingestion keys needed to recreate Lucid World quickly.
- Markets undergo deep reform around causal-data asymmetry, but prediction does not disappear.
- The History Machine remains possible **only** through the multi-node African federation.
- **Mercer survives and is partly vindicated:** central optimisation did save lives. His error was treating the right scale as *every* scale.
- The final emotional payoff is not ignorance; it is **trust among people who cannot fully see one another's part**.
