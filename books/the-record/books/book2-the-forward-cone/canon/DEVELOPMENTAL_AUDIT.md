# Developmental Audit — *THE FORWARD CONE*

> **Revision map · 2026-08-20.** This audit treats the 27-sequence `PLOT.md` and series canon as
> binding. It does not use word count as a target. The first draft already proves the mechanisms;
> the finishing pass adds only scenes that complete character movement, embody cost or turn a
> technical conclusion into a human choice.

## What already works

- The 27-sequence causal ladder is complete, legible and fair-play.
- Mercer saves lives before the Convergence diagnosis and remains partly vindicated at the end.
- Prediction never becomes prophecy: every cone is conditional, publication changes the graph and
  Lucid refuses unsupported authority.
- The solution is planted at instrument scale before it appears at civilisation scale.
- Partition visibly costs speed, money, convenience and lives; decentralisation is not magic.
- One Record and Lucid World remain correctly distinct.

## Explicit non-actions

- No new villain, conspiracy, hidden initiator or cinematic catastrophe.
- No third book door.
- No global answer at the end and no later proof that secretly certifies victory.
- No removal of Mercer's successful interventions to simplify the argument.
- No publication claim: market-structure, cryptography/provenance and SANAP human reviews remain
  required before release.
