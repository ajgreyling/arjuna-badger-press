# Chapter 10

The first result looked like weather on a broken television.

Ama removed the colour.

The Technopark display had mapped confidence to a scale running from midnight blue through green to
yellow, with unsupported regions rendered as a dark violet haze. It was beautiful in the specific
way that made people assume somebody understood it.

In greyscale, the reconstruction volume became what it was: unstable statistical noise occupying
most of the sealed room and agreeing with itself nowhere.

“Put the colour back,” Dries said.

“Why?”

“I can see the gradients.”

“You can see the palette.”

The actual run had taken eleven minutes. Processing had taken thirty-one hours.

Inside the room, an operator selected three sealed instruction capsules. One directed her to move a
marked block from the table to the floor. One told her to rotate a chair. One required a human choice
between breaking a ceramic strip and bending a copper tab. She chose the copper. Outside the room, a
witness observed through the opened door only after the action window had closed, registered the
final state and signed the action key. The conventional record was sealed from everyone working on
the reconstruction.

Karoo delivered the weak-signal streams. Nairobi delivered the evidence root. Antarctica did
nothing, as agreed. Ghana ingested every registered sensor family and preserved the isolated clock
alongside them.

Technopark's solver returned weather.

Ama's team worked in shifts because the Rain did not become smaller while people slept. The run
occupied a microscopic interval inside days of environmental, instrument and infrastructure data.
Every stream arrived with its own sampling behaviour, dropout pattern and uncertainty. The task was
not to find the sealed-room answer. Ghana did not possess it. The task was to prevent unrelated
events from being mistaken for one another merely because the solver wanted a result.

At hour thirty-six, Dries requested a smoothed product.

Ama declined.

At hour forty, he requested a visual diagnostic view with “non-destructive interpolation.”

She declined with a note asking him to define destructive.

At hour forty-three, he called.

“I am not asking you to alter the evidence,” he said.

“You are asking me to create values where the instruments supplied none.”

“For visual inspection.”

“Inspection of what?”

“Spatial tendency.”

“There is no spatial tendency.”

“You cannot know that until we look.”

“We looked. The confidence field does not stabilise.”

“Because high-frequency gaps dominate the render.”

“The gaps are part of the result.”

On the display behind him, the room pulsed in patches as the solver admitted incompatible past
states and discarded them again. Occasionally a plane seemed to form where the table should be. It
survived for three frames, then became a diagonal volume passing through the floor.

“If we smooth temporally,” Dries said, “we can tell whether that plane persists.”

“If you smooth temporally, persistence is the property you add.”

He took off his glasses and rubbed the bridge of his nose. “We need diagnostic information.”

“Then diagnose the failure. Stop trying to make it resemble success.”

Ama terminated the colour render at source. Technopark could apply its own palette to the certified
product, but the Ghana view would remain black, white and uncertainty.

For three more days, nothing improved.

That was not the same as nothing happening.

They removed one sensor family at a time. The output changed, but never in proportion to the removed
signal. They shifted the time bound. The haze translated in some streams and expanded in others.
They reran the inversion against wrong coordinates in the adjacent office and obtained a result
almost as convincing as the correct room.

That last failure ended the argument about visual inspection.

If a room where nothing had occurred could produce an equally persuasive cloud, the cloud contained
no recoverable claim.

Ama compared the transformation rules rather than the amplitudes. Karoo's electromagnetic stream
decayed according to one window. The geological instruments integrated across another. Building
vibration arrived with a periodicity imposed by ventilation. Vacuum-noise products had been grouped
under a stationarity assumption valid for their original experiment and false for this one.

Technopark had aligned them by timestamp and treated time as if it meant the same operation in every
instrument.

It did not.

One stream forgot sharply. Another smeared. A third retained transitions but lost scale. The solver
was searching for one common decay shape across sensor families that did not share one.

Ama wrote the finding in the incident channel.

`EMPTY RESULT IS GENUINE UNDER CURRENT MODEL.`

Dries responded immediately.

`Genuine empty or model empty?`

`Both. The model cannot distinguish them.`

`That is diagnostic information.`

She considered reminding him whose sentence it had been.

Instead she attached the comparative decay plots.

The witness opened the room on the seventh day. The marked block sat on the floor. The chair had
been rotated. The copper tab was bent. None appeared in the reconstruction at better confidence than
objects that had never been present.

Kiki certified that the run had occurred under the registered procedure.

Lwazi certified nothing about the physics.

Ama archived the grey haze without smoothing it.

The first honest product of the History Machine was a picture of what it did not know.
