# Chapter 49

The engineering-yard path fixed him beside the vehicle door, where the certified uncertainty had

remembering to open the door.

The next supported action appeared after impact.

The revered path moved between the security figure and Path D, absorbed force directed towards the

interposition, opposing force, repeated contact with the fallen person.

“You mean he tried to help.”

“The record does not contain his purpose.”

He had spent his life applying stricter verbs to other people's heroes than to his own.

“He intervened physically,” he said. “We cannot say what he intended to achieve.”

The finding kept both sentences.

The security figure's causal action arrived through the chair.

Before the fall, his path drove it sharply across the concrete while Path D's weight remained partly
supported against its frame. The chair rotated. A rear leg trapped or displaced D's foot; the upper
load continued while the base did not. Descent followed within the measured interval.

The security figure had initiated the chair movement.

Whether he intended injury, intimidation, restraint or only to move it could not be recovered. The
room showed no prepared restraint equipment, no waiting transport for a body and no cleaning
material on site. The vehicle left, returned with added supplies and opened its door a second time.
The concealment was organised after the fall.

Lwazi wrote: `SECURITY PATH INITIATED THE PHYSICAL SEQUENCE`  
`CAUSING THE FALL.`

He did not write murder.

Then the revered figure's path changed.

For seven minutes after impact, it remained near Path D and repeatedly opposed the security figure's
movement. After the first vehicle departure, all surviving paths separated. When the vehicle
returned, the revered path helped transfer the fallen mass across the threshold. It held the vehicle
door against its obstruction. Later, it crossed the room during two of the cleaning passes.

Intervention and concealment belonged to the same load path.

The daughter leaned towards the display. “If he was stopping them, why did he help them afterwards?”

No field in the room changed.

Lwazi could name fear, coercion, loyalty, calculation, shock or a decision made after the vehicle
left. Each would fit inside the seven unmeasured minutes before it returned. The same person could
intervene without courage enough to continue, or conceal without having intended the death. A clean
answer would require the interior layer the family had forbidden and the machine had destroyed.

“We cannot recover why,” he said.

“You keep saying that as though why is decoration.”

“No.” He looked at the two parts of the path. “It is the part we cannot measure.”

She left her hand beside the stop control.

Lwazi reran the identity constraint with the public name removed. The actions remained. He restored
the identity. Nothing shifted except the meaning people would assign them.

The daughter did not press stop.

Before the final checkpoint, the medical-council witness challenged the identity field. The family's
third question asked whether another person was accountable. The physical sequence could answer that
at role level: security path, revered path, two further paths. Naming the revered figure would expose
a dead person who had never consented and living institutions built around him.

The church archivist supported the challenge for a different reason. Sacred and political archives
both contained names whose release could summon consequences no custodian could reverse.

The trade-union witness opposed removing it. “Role level is how powerful people become weather,” she
said. “Things happened around them. Nobody did them.”

Kiki put the choice to the daughter. The identity had crossed the threshold under the same rule as
every candidate. The family could keep it from publication under the consent instrument. They could
not make it physically unsupported by withholding it.

“Does the name change what he did?” the daughter asked.

“No,” Lwazi said.

“Does hiding it change what people will say he did?”

Nobody offered a technical answer.

“Keep it in the finding,” she said. “Bring it to us before you give it to them.”

The medical council recorded its concern. The church archive recorded its objection. Neither
withdrew its key.

At the final checkpoint, four quorum institutions signed the physical sequence. The church archive
attached an objection to publishing the revered identity before the family received the finding.
The medical council endorsed the three-question boundary. The critical university recorded that
causal action did not establish criminal intention.

No objection invalidated the others.

The plain-language finding filled one page.

The missing man was present and did not leave alive. A security figure initiated the physical
sequence that caused his fatal fall. A revered liberation figure entered during the fall, physically
intervened after it and later participated in moving the body and cleaning the site. The record did
not establish whether the unresolved contact was a push or a catch. It did not establish a planned
killing.

Lwazi read the page once.

He read it again with the name covered by his hand. The sentence still described intervention and
concealment. When he removed his hand, the mechanics acquired the weight of school assemblies,
party stages and the portrait in his mother's passage. The machine had not added that weight. The
country would.

The version of history that protected the old security order failed at the chair. The version that
kept the liberation figure morally untouched failed at the vehicle door.

Before publication, both political camps obtained the summary through their lawyers. Each accused
the reconstruction of serving the other.

For the first time, they agreed on what should not become the record.
