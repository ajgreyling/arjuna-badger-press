# Chapter 27

Lwazi watched a man fall without seeing a man.

Path D lost its ordinary relation to the floor. For seven-tenths of a second, weight left the
sequence and returned as an impact spread across concrete.

The display held no outline. It showed a narrow field descending, a broad field at contact and the
limits of both. Where a shoulder or head might have been, the old slab retained too little state to
distinguish either.

Lucid certified three statements.

`HUMAN-COMPATIBLE MASS DESCENDED.`

`FLOOR CONTACT OCCURRED AT SPEED.`

`SECOND LOAD PATH EXCHANGED MOMENTUM`  
`DURING DESCENT.`

The third statement divided the room.

Dries wanted its geometry. Ama wanted the run stopped until the wording had survived Nairobi. Sanna
wanted the time interval widened because the contact entered at the edge of Antarctic resolution.
Lwazi wanted the missing fraction of a second.

He submitted a higher-resolution branch before the review began.

Kiki saw it in the queue.

“Under which question?” she asked.

“Whether anyone else was accountable.”

“That is a legal conclusion.”

“Whether another person caused the fall.”

“That is not the registered wording either.”

“Then register it.”

Ama opened the family's instrument on the shared display. The authorised question read: `IS THERE
SUPPORTED EVIDENCE OF ACCOUNTABILITY BY ANOTHER PERSON?`

It did not say hand, force or intention. Lwazi had argued for that breadth when they drafted it.
Now the breadth felt like a locked door.

The higher-resolution estimate arrived while they spoke. It offered two admissible histories.

In the first, the second path applied force before D's descent accelerated.

In the second, D's descent began first and the second path opposed it.

Push or catch.

The confidence values shifted when they changed priors, but neither history disappeared. The
missing interval lay in the material record itself: demolition had broken the relevant propagation
path, and no amount of compute could restore a state that had not survived.

Lwazi reran it with the Anchor weighted more heavily.

The first history led by six points.

He changed the surface model.

The second led by three.

He removed the watch layer and both returned to overlap.

“One of them happened,” he said.

“Yes,” Ama replied.

“We can narrow it.”

“You have narrowed it.”

He pointed at the queue estimate. “Another block buys us two hundred milliseconds.”

“Around the contact. Not inside the missing path.”

“You don't know that until we run it.”

Ama moved the cost panel beside the family's questions. The remaining authorised allocation could
be spent on the contact or on the interval after the impact: enough, perhaps, to determine whether
Path D left the room and whether the vehicle's second opening carried additional load.

“They asked if he left alive,” she said.

“They also asked if someone was accountable.”

“Force is not accountability.”

“It is closer than a door.”

“Only if you already know what the hand was doing.”

Lwazi looked again at the two histories. In one, the second figure entered the fall as its cause. In
the other, the same figure entered too late to stop it. Every surviving measurement after contact
was identical.

He had grown up on photographs of men whose actions came with captions. Courage. Betrayal.
Resistance. Collaboration. The photographs had not lied, exactly. They had removed the milliseconds
in which one word could become another.

He cancelled the branch.

The released allocation went to the post-impact interval.

Lucid preserved the conflict receipt with both histories, all priors and the broken propagation
path. It did not average them. It did not choose the version with the higher temporary score.

The certified finding became smaller and harder.

A human-compatible mass had fallen. Another person had made contact. The record could not establish
whether that contact caused the fall or tried to arrest it.

On the display, Path D reached the floor and did not resume its former gait.

Beyond the room, the vehicle waited for its door to open a second time.
