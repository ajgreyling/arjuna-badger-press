# Chapter 38

The project ended by letter at 09:12 on a Tuesday.

The order came through the university registrar, copied to the consortium, the family's attorney and
the evidence custodian holding the Anchor. It suspended historical compute under an emergency review
of unauthorised access and directed the custodian to transfer the object into court-supervised
storage.

No police entered Technopark.

At 09:17, Dries's cloud login failed.

He called the service desk. A polite administrator confirmed that the account remained in good
standing and had been disabled by legal instruction. She could not restore it. She apologised twice
and gave him a reference number.

At 09:24, the Anchor's custody camera showed two court officers, the family attorney and a university
witness sealing the evidence tray inside a new container. The daughter had objected. The court had
heard the objection and issued the order anyway.

The transfer was lawful, witnessed and careful.

By ten, Technopark's reconstruction room contained only furniture and revoked credentials.

Dries tried the Karoo diagnostic account. Suspended. Ghana's ingestion queue would accept ordinary
work but rejected every historical root. Antarctica could publish measurements and no longer attach
them to the case. Nairobi's ledger remained readable and could sign no new execution.

The shutdown had been designed by people who understood the architecture.

The five nodes met on an ordinary call because the secure room now belonged to the review authority.
Ama reported that the Rain was draining into cold storage. Kiki read the order clause by clause.
Lwazi asked how long a review could last.

Naledi did not answer for the court. “Longer than the residue will remain politically containable.”

“Then the leak becomes the record,” Lwazi said.

Nobody contradicted him.

Dries opened the cabinet beneath his workbench.

Inside were four commodity servers purchased under the seed grant, the same machines used when GPU
procurement slipped and the sealed-room diagnostic had to run somewhere. Their cases did not match.
One fan clicked at low speed. None appeared in the cloud suspension because none belonged to the
cloud.

“There is another path,” he said.

Kiki looked towards him. “Another diagnostic path.”

“A reduced Lucid path.”

“Reduced how?” Ama asked.

“No generated rendering. Limited spatial resolution. Local corpus shard. CPU execution.”

Lwazi understood first. “The whole solver?”

“Slowly.”

“How slowly?”

“The historical run would take weeks without the Anchor. Days with it.”

Kiki went back through the procurement record. The approved purpose was method validation and field
resilience. It authorised components, diagnostics and reduced test cases. It did not authorise an
operational reconstruction capability outside the consortium queue.

“When did you make it complete?” she asked.

“It isn't complete.”

“When did it become capable of answering a historical query?”

“After the sealed-room repeat.”

“You did not register that.”

“I registered every code change.”

“As diagnostics.”

“They were diagnostics.”

“Together they are a machine.”

Dries rested his hand on the warm server case. He had built the path one practical compromise at a
time: a CPU kernel when GPUs were late, a local index when the network failed, a deterministic
renderer for field tests, a checkpoint format that survived power loss. No single change had felt
like a secret capability. He had known what the changes added up to and chosen not to give that sum
a name.

Ama asked whether the offline path could consume the existing certified products.

“Yes.”

“Without the Anchor?”

“At lower identity confidence.”

“Without the five-node quorum?”

Dries looked at Kiki.

“It can execute,” he said. “It cannot become certified.”

The difference had once felt absolute. With the smooth video replacing their result in public, it
felt like a luxury.

Naledi warned that any run would breach the suspension and support the claim that the consortium had
maintained a covert system.

“We did maintain one,” Kiki said.

“I maintained a fallback,” Dries replied.

“You maintained the ability to proceed after authority said stop.”

The server fan clicked between them.

Dries had built the path because procurement was slow and instruments failed far from reliable
networks. It had become resistance without changing a line of code.

He switched the cabinet off.

For now.
