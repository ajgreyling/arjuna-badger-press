# Chapter 15

The second experiment began with nineteen days in which nothing was allowed to be interesting.

They removed the chair.

They replaced the folding table with a smaller one whose legs could be registered against four
floor marks without recording its final load state. They reduced the object set to a wooden block,
a ceramic strip and one steel cylinder. The operator would perform one mechanically selected action
and one human choice. The mechanical selector was sealed before the room and designed so the
operator could actuate it without learning the unused alternatives.

The action commitment went public forty-eight hours before sealing.

So did the clock root, room inventory, uncertainty policy, solver version, wrong-coordinate controls
and the conditions under which Kiki would refuse certification.

The university complained that they had published a manual for sabotaging the experiment.

Kiki replied that this was another name for a protocol.

Karoo lost its first instrument window to wind noise, the second to a scheduled observation that
outranked them, and half the third to a cooling fault. Ghana opened two physically separated
ingestion paths, one through Accra and one through Kumasi, then prohibited the teams from comparing
intermediate products. Technopark rebuilt the inversion in a clean environment whose dependencies
had been frozen before the action key existed.

Lwazi spent the nineteen days expecting the result to disappear.

That was what good controls did. They removed the weak reasons to believe until either the claim
failed or the remaining reason became expensive to dismiss.

On the morning of the run, the sealed room looked less like a laboratory than before. No smart
switch. No access point. No chairs. The isolated oscillator sat outside the boundary in a locked
cabinet, its signal witnessed by Nairobi and mirrored beyond consortium control.

The operator entered at 10:00.

Nobody in the reconstruction team knew who she was.

At 10:03 she actuated the selector. At 10:05 she performed the human-choice action. At 10:08 she
left. The witness entered only after the action window, registered the final room state, sealed the
conventional record and sent the destruction commitment to Nairobi.

Then the consortium waited.

Karoo's streams closed at 10:30. Accra and Kumasi ingested independently. Their transformation roots
were published without comparing products. Technopark received two query packages identified only
as Path A and Path B.

The first inversion required fourteen hours and produced no stable surface.

The second required eleven and produced a plane too broad to distinguish the table from the floor.

At hour thirty, the clean environment exhausted its allocated GPU time. The queue moved them behind
a climate run and a commercial materials model with contractual priority.

Dries switched the diagnostic solver to the commodity CPUs bought under the seed grant.

“That path is for validation,” Lwazi said.

“We are validating whether we can finish.”

“How long?”

“Thirty-eight hours if nobody uses the coffee machine on the same circuit.”

It took forty-one.

The Path A result arrived first. A horizontal edge stabilised at table height. A second bounded
volume occupied the registered position of the steel cylinder after the action window. Between them,
motion appeared not as a body but as a sequence of admissible spatial volumes—narrow in open space,
expanding where the table obstructed the sensors' surviving consequences.

Path B arrived three hours later.

The edge remained. The cylinder remained. The motion volumes overlapped within the fixed uncertainty
policy.

Lwazi did not open the action key.

He ran the wrong-coordinate controls. Empty.

Shifted the time bound. The edge weakened and vanished.

Removed the electromagnetic family. Resolution worsened; geometry remained.

Removed geological products. The table plane widened.

Removed the two ingestion products that had disagreed most strongly. The solver became more certain.

That last result frightened him. Certainty increasing when disagreement disappeared could mean the
disagreement was contamination. It could also mean the disagreement was honest information the
solver found inconvenient.

He restored both products.

The field became less beautiful and more reproducible.

Only then did Nairobi release the action key.

The mechanical selector had instructed the operator to move the steel cylinder from a marked tray
to the table. Her human choice was to pass her hand behind the table edge or above it. She had chosen
behind.

The registered final-state record confirmed the cylinder's position. It did not record the path of
her hand.

Lwazi placed the action key beside the reconstruction.

The steel cylinder occupied the supported volume. The motion path crossed from tray to table. Where
the hand passed through unobstructed space, the admissible volume narrowed. Behind the table, it
expanded into an incomplete shape with no fingers, skin or definite orientation.

Technopark's renderer offered a surface-fit option.

Dries disabled it.

The reconstructed interval stabilised for 2.7 seconds.

No sound. No face. No continuous body. Surfaces arrived at different confidence. Motion tore through
the field. Unsupported space remained black.

The operator's action was unmistakable.

For several seconds nobody on the consortium call spoke.

Then somebody in Karoo swore.

Accra laughed. Technopark laughed because Accra had. A second voice accused the first of contaminating
the recording, and Kiki reminded them that the call was not part of the evidence package.

Lwazi did not laugh.

He watched the unresolved hand move behind the supported table edge and become less visible exactly
where sight would also have failed.

The machine had not filled the gap.

That was the first thing about it he trusted.
