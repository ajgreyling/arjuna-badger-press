# Style Guide — *THE RECORD* (Book I)

> Craft law for drafting. The series-level rules live in `../../../canon/CRAFT.md` (five-step
> explanation loop, chapter rhythm, competence checklist, words to police) and
> `../../../canon/SERIES_BIBLE.md` (§14 anti-tropes). This file is what those imply at sentence level.

---

## POV

**Dries with rotating close-third ensemble chapters** (bible default). One consciousness per section;
section break when the skull changes. Each POV owns one indispensable layer and is **wrong outside
it** — write the wrongness, don't protect anyone from it.

Node tags for the chapter ledger: **T** Technopark · **K** Karoo · **A** Accra–Kumasi · **N** Nairobi ·
**I** Antarctic station.

## Writing the machine

- **Never cinema.** A reconstruction is a sparse solved configuration with an uncertainty field over
  it. Unsupported regions are blank, noisy or multiply resolved — on the page, not in a footnote.
  The leaked fake is persuasive precisely because it looks like the movie everyone wanted.
- **Focus on what remains missing** as much as on what appears.
- **Show cost every time.** Compute, cryogen, grid, queue position, quorum time, a borrowed anchor,
  a night nobody slept. A free query is a continuity error.
- Vocabulary is fixed (`../../../canon/TERMINOLOGY.md`): residue, inversion, anchor, uncertainty
  field, the Rain, Witness Protocol, temporal reference. Journalists and politicians may say
  "footage" and "playback" — and somebody in the room should wince.
- **No infodumps.** Physics is earned inside experiments. If a principle must be named, name it the
  way working people name their tools: fast, casually, mid-task.

## Register per node

- **Technopark:** office park, not campus. Renamed buildings, hand-written access labels, boardrooms
  with the ghosts of failed startups, R44 traffic, mountain wind, generator starts, fibre cuts,
  procurement delay, coffee-shop intelligence. Blunt, funny, unimpressed by ceremony.
- **Karoo:** engineered silence, dust, no-transmission discipline, distance, physical danger, the
  long argument with anyone who arrives with a running engine.
- **Accra–Kumasi:** heat and racks, power discipline, the Rain arriving, the fastest arguments in the
  book, professional rivalry with Nairobi that is affectionate and real.
- **Nairobi:** paper, seals, quorum notices, court corridors, the specific politeness of people who
  will not be hurried.
- **Antarctic station:** dry cold, dark, seasonal isolation, the arithmetic of a person and an
  instrument and no witnesses. Sanna talks to instruments more easily than to people — write that as
  competence first and grief second.

## Dialogue

- Everyone is good at their job and speaks like it: short, unfinished sentences among peers; careful
  complete ones for the record.
- Institutions reveal themselves through **procedures, offers, injunctions and resource control** —
  no villain explains the plan. Mercer is the most reasonable person in any room he enters.
- The family are not a grief chorus. They have lawyers, opinions about physics, and a veto they
  intend to use.
- **Lucid** computes, cites, conflicts or refuses. Never a voice of doom, never cute, never a POV.
  It must be able to **refuse even G** — plant that in the middle of the book.

## What not to write

- A reconstruction rendered as video, hologram or ghost-walk.
- A lone-white-genius beat: Dries is a viewpoint, not the author of any breakthrough he did not earn.
- Traditional knowledge as a mystical reveal. It shapes ethics and interpretation; the instrument
  works through testable fictional physics.
- "The blockchain proves it." The ledger proves sequence and integrity. Say so early (Seq. 3) so the
  valid-signature betrayal is fair play.
- Clean historical catharsis. Actions clarify; motive, memory and national mythology get harder.
- Generic gunmen. Jeopardy comes from access, custody, clocks, environment and infrastructure.
- Time-travel language of any kind, including as a joke that goes unchallenged.
- CODATA as endorsement — the honest 0.57σ framing survives into the prose or the book loses its
  spine.
