# SEED_STORY — *THE RECORD* (Book I)

> §5 of the series bible. **2031.** Scientific mystery → historical cold case → evidence siege.

---

## Controlling ideas

| Element | Design |
|---|---|
| **External goal** | Prove that a recovered historical event is physically derived and independently reproducible before the consortium is captured or discredited |
| **Internal goal** | Move the ensemble from **possession of truth** to **responsible custody of truth** |
| **MacGuffin** | **THE ANCHOR** — a small material object present at the disputed event, with a documented but incomplete chain of custody |
| **Central mystery** | Why does a blind laboratory reconstruction contain a voice from decades before the laboratory existed? |
| **Human case** | An apartheid-era disappearance that implicates both a former security figure and a revered liberation figure in a morally complicated event |
| **Antagonistic force** | A coalition seeking to delay, relocate, classify or selectively edit the evidence — **each for defensible reasons** |
| **Climactic proof** | A fully independent reconstruction using a different material path and a reduced quorum, published with uncertainty and kill switches |
| **Ending change** | History becomes readable, but not simple; an unexplained future-dated Lucid job appears |

## Ending state

- The disappearance is historically resolved but **morally complicated**.
- Recovered records are recognised as a **new evidence category**, not automatically as courtroom truth.
- The African Record Consortium survives as a **tense federation**, not a unified institution.
- The public knows physical history can be recovered — but **not** that experiential records may be possible.
- The leaked synthetic reconstruction permanently demonstrates that **plausible imagery is not evidence**.
- The first market anomalies have already begun in the background.
- **Dries possesses a hidden future-correlated result and has compromised his own ethic.**
