### Density signal for the chapter target

53 beats against the bible's 45–55 chapter target is close to **one beat per chapter**, with the
short movements merging and the six discoveries (Beats 27–30, 33, 49) likely to split. Treat 53 as a
floor, not a map.

## What still gates the chapter ledger

From the bible's work order (`../../../canon/OPEN_DECISIONS.md`):

1. **Lock the composite historical case** and the family's present-day stakes.
2. **Culturally ground the ensemble** with Ghanaian, Kenyan and South African readers — every name is provisional.

Beat work does not have to wait for these; a chapter ledger does, because it fixes ordering and
names that both steps can still move.

## Note on the two plant/payoff tables

`../../../canon/PLANT_PAYOFF.md` is the bible's ledger — settled, cross-book. The scratch table at the
foot of `../BEAT_LEDGER.md` is live and beat-level. They agree today. **Do not merge them**: when a
scratch plant proves out, promote it up; the scratch table is allowed to be wrong.
