THE LITTLE KEY — ILLUSTRATION SPECIFICATION
(Prompts for ChatGPT web image generator / OpenRouter image model. One book, one style.)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOUSE STYLE — paste this STYLE BLOCK at the top of EVERY prompt so all 14
spreads + the cover read as one book:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STYLE: Warm, painterly children's-picture-book illustration in the tradition of
hand-painted gouache and watercolour storybooks — soft visible brushwork, gentle
edges, a little chaotic and a little funny, deeply tender. Lamplight palette:
honey gold, warm ochre, soft cream, deep night-blue shadows. Cosy, intimate,
storybook warmth. NOT digital-slick, NOT 3D-render, NOT photoreal. Picture-book
art that a tired parent and a small child read together by one lamp at bedtime.

CHARACTERS (keep consistent across every image):
- THEMBI: a seven-year-old Black South African girl, short natural hair in little
  twists or puffs, expressive warm face, wearing soft cotton pyjamas (pale yellow
  with small stars). Curious, kind. She is the only big person in most scenes.
- NKWE: a tiny hand-carved WOODEN honey badger, the size of a teaspoon — clearly
  carved from warm old wood (visible grain, rounded tool-marks), but ALIVE: black
  and white badger markings, a stubborn proud little face, bright dark eyes. Small
  but never cute-helpless — stubborn and brave.
- GOGO: Thembi's grandmother, older Black South African woman, grey hair, kind
  lined face, soft morning gown. Appears near the end.
- THE LITTLE ONES: a carved wooden bird (in flight), a carved wooden tortoise, and
  a carved wooden girl no bigger than a thumb — each clearly carved wood, each alive.

THE OBJECTS: an old wooden bathroom-style cupboard with a small mirror door, paint
worn at the handle; a small brass key on a red thread.

FORMAT: landscape 3:2 spread, full-bleed, no text in the image (text sits beneath
on the page). Leave the composition readable with the sound off — the FEELING must
land before any words.

PER-SPREAD PROMPTS (append each to the STYLE block above):

COVER → file: design/cover.png  (also reused as a hero marketing image)
An old wooden cupboard with a little mirror door standing in the warm corner of a
sunlit spare room, dust turning slowly in a golden sunbeam. On the cupboard handle
hangs a small brass key on a red thread, catching the light. Tiny and easy to miss,
sitting on the middle shelf inside, is a little carved wooden honey badger. Cosy,
inviting, full of quiet promise. Warm honey-gold light. Room to feel the whole book
is waiting inside this cupboard.

SPREAD 01 → spread-01-cupboard.png
The old wooden cupboard with its little mirror door, alone in the corner of Gogo's
sunlit spare room, dust drifting down through a sunbeam slow as falling snow. Quiet,
still, waiting. Nobody in frame yet.

SPREAD 02 → spread-02-key.png
Close, warm: Thembi's small brown hand reaching for the small brass key on its red
thread hanging from the cupboard handle. Her face lit with curiosity. Golden light.

SPREAD 03 → spread-03-carving.png
Inside the open cupboard, on the middle shelf, the little carved wooden honey badger
the size of a teaspoon, with a stubborn wooden face. Thembi's wondering face leaning
in close beside it. The carving still looks like a toy here — not yet awake.

SPREAD 04 → spread-04-turn.png
Night. Thembi in her star pyjamas, lit by a bedside lamp, turning the small brass key
in the cupboard's mirror door. The carved badger sits on the shelf inside. Hushed,
secret, the rest of the room in deep night-blue shadow. NO creature awake yet.

SPREAD 05 → spread-05-awake-HERO.png   ★ HERO SPREAD — generate this FIRST, main scene
HERO IMAGE. Morning light floods in. The tiny wooden honey badger Nkwe is now ALIVE —
standing on the shelf, breathing, blinking UP at an ENORMOUS wondering girl whose huge
gentle face fills the other half of the spread. Extreme scale contrast: teaspoon-sized
creature, sky-sized girl. He is small but not afraid. Wonder, tenderness, the moment
the magic is real. This is the emotional centre of the whole book.

SPREAD 06 → spread-06-toy.png
Thembi, too happy to be careful, playing with tiny Nkwe like a toy soldier — pushing
him along to "march", rolling a crumb for him to "fetch". His small wooden face has
gone hard and unhappy: he is NOT a toy. A gentle wrongness in the air.

SPREAD 07 → spread-07-runs.png
The world seen at Nkwe's tiny scale: he runs across a vast tabletop that looks like a
cliff-edge, the bedroom floor a deep canyon below. In the dark beneath the bed, two
glowing green cat-eyes have opened. Dramatic scale, the cosy room turned huge and
perilous, but still painterly and warm — danger of SCALE, not horror.

SPREAD 08 → spread-08-composite-COMPOSITE.png   ★ COMPOSITE — main scene + the mini ones
COMPOSITE SPREAD. The same bedroom seen small, and now the OTHER carved creatures are
also awake and superimposed into the scene, each living its own life among the giant
furniture: a little wooden BIRD in flight near the window, a little wooden TORTOISE
crossing the rug, and a wooden GIRL no bigger than a thumb who looks not afraid but
BUSY — she has her own whole life to live. Nkwe among them. Thembi's giant form in
soft focus at the edge, realising they were never hers. Many small lives at once.

SPREAD 09 → spread-09-longnight.png
The long cold night. Tiny Nkwe, clever and stubborn, hiding behind a giant shoe,
holding very still — a knot of wood — as the cat's enormous green eyes search past
him in the dark. He survives by wit, not strength. Deep night-blue, one sliver of
moonlight.

SPREAD 10 → spread-10-cornered.png
Morning. The cat has tiny Nkwe cornered against the skirting board. From above,
Thembi's huge hand is coming down to snatch him to safety — to keep him, to own him
again. Tension, the wrong rescue about to happen.

SPREAD 11 → spread-11-asks.png
Thembi lying flat on the floor, making her big self small, cheek almost to the carpet,
the window open and the cupboard open behind her, her open empty palm resting on the
carpet — NOT grabbing, OFFERING. Tender, humble, brave. The bravest thing a big person
can do.

SPREAD 12 → spread-12-stays.png
Tiny Nkwe walking calmly across the warm carpet and climbing onto Thembi's open palm,
choosing to stay. The cat has slunk away in the background. Warm forgiving morning
light. He stays because he was asked.

SPREAD 13 → spread-13-gogo.png
Gogo standing in the bedroom doorway in her morning gown, smiling, completely
unsurprised. On the moonlit-into-morning windowsill, the thumb-sized carved girl waves
to Gogo, and Gogo waves back — two old friends. Warm, knowing, generational.

SPREAD 14 → spread-14-closing.png
CLOSING IMAGE. Night again. Thembi asleep in bed, peaceful, the small brass key on its
red thread now tied around her neck. On the moonlit windowsill, four tiny carved
creatures keep watch. On the pillow beside her face, wide awake, sits the little wooden
badger Nkwe — free to go anywhere at all, and staying. Tender, safe, complete. Deep
night-blue and silver moonlight with one warm note.
