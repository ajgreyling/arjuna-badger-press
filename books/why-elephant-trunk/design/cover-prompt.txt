STYLE
Warm painterly children's-storybook illustration in soft gouache and colour-pencil texture,
in the tradition of classic read-aloud picture books. Golden African bushveld light — honey
dawns and amber sunsets, dusty sage-greens, river blues, warm red earth. Visible brushwork,
gentle rounded shapes, generous negative space for a calm read-aloud feel. RECURRING CHARACTERS
(keep identical across every spread): NKULU, a soft dove-grey young elephant, plump and round
with big honest dark eyes and small floppy ears — he STARTS with a tiny short stubby nose and
his nose grows visibly longer spread by spread, ending as a full long trunk. {{CHILD}}, a
brown-skinned village child with short hair, a bright patterned wrap-cloth (terracotta and
ochre), bare feet, lively and brave. NGWENYA, a long olive-green crocodile, characterful and a
little comic — never monstrous, with a knobbly back and a sly yellow eye. SETTING is the real
bushveld: yellow-barked fever-trees, tall reeds, hanging weaver-bird nests, a wide slow river,
red dust. Landscape 3:2 aspect, full-bleed composition, no text or lettering anywhere in the
image.

Long-ago golden dawn over the wide bushveld. Little NKULU the grey elephant calf with a TINY
short stubby nose stands among yellow-barked fever-trees, big curious eyes, ears soft. The dozing
herd is hazy in the warm background. Dewy, peaceful, full of morning light.

spread-02-trunk.png
Hot humming midday. The elephant herd dozes in fever-tree shade. In the foreground {{CHILD}}, the
brown-skinned village child in a bright terracotta wrap-cloth, walks barefoot beside little NKULU
(still tiny stubby nose), the two of them heading toward a far glint of blue river. Heat shimmer,
golden grass.

spread-03-trunk.png
Reedy riverbank, cool blue water, hanging weaver nests, red mud. Little NKULU leans down and dips
his SHORT stubby nose to take a drink. {{CHILD}} crouches on the bank watching, dappled light
through the reeds. Calm, inviting.

spread-04-trunk.png
NGWENYA the long olive-green crocodile lies still as a sleeping log in the shallows, just one
yellow eye sliding open. Unaware, little NKULU drinks nearby; {{CHILD}} on the bank. Tension held
gently — suspense, not horror — warm light, the river glassy.

spread-05-trunk.png
The dramatic SNAP: NGWENYA lunges and clamps onto NKULU's little stubby nose, pulling him toward
the deep water. {{CHILD}} on the bank gasps, eyes wide, hands flying up. Splash and ripples,
dynamic motion, but playful and storybook-safe.

spread-06-trunk.png
Tug-of-war begins. NKULU digs his heels into the red mud and leans back hard; his nose is a
little longer now from the pull. Brave {{CHILD}} has grabbed the elephant's tail and pulls with
all their might, feet braced, wrap-cloth flying. Dust and determination.

spread-07-trunk.png
The line of pullers grows: a bright yellow weaver bird tugs at {{CHILD}}'s wrap with its beak, a
vervet monkey grips NKULU's back leg. NKULU's nose is longer still. Loud, lively, comic energy
along the riverbank.

spread-08-trunk.png
A bristly warthog backs into the line snorting, and a slow determined tortoise grips on at the
very end. Everyone heaves together in a long chain, dust rising, NKULU's nose stretching longer.
Joyful group effort.

spread-09-trunk.png
The whole elephant herd thunders down the bank in a cloud of red dust and adds its weight to the
pulling chain — big grey bodies, raised stubby noses, deep effort. NKULU at the front, nose long
and straining. Epic, warm, full of movement.

spread-10-trunk.png
The big comic stretch spread: NKULU's nose stretching like soft warm clay, looooong across the
whole page between NGWENYA the crocodile on one side and the laughing chain of pullers on the
other. {{CHILD}}, monkey, bird, warthog, tortoise, herd all leaning back. Silly, elastic, full of
glee.

spread-11-trunk.png
NGWENYA finally lets GO and slips back into the river with a splosh and a sly grin. All the
pullers tumble backward into a giggling, dusty heap. NKULU sits up surprised, now sporting a brand
new LONG trunk. Relief and laughter, golden light.

spread-12-trunk.png
Joyful montage spread in one scene: NKULU using his wonderful new long trunk to drink from the
cool river, spray-wash his own dusty grey back, pluck the highest sweet marula fruit from a tree,
and — centre and tenderest — curl gently around {{CHILD}} in a soft hug. Warm, loving, golden.

spread-13-trunk.png
Sunset over the bushveld. A long line of elephants, now ALL with long trunks, raise them in
greeting against an amber sky. {{CHILD}} waves from the riverbank beside NKULU. Fever-trees
glowing, peaceful close, deep warm light.
