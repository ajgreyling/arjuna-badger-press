# Reader Glossary — *The History Machine*

> Back-matter draft. Plain language, no equations. Anything a reader needs, they should have
> already met inside a scene; this is confirmation, not instruction.

---

**420 Code** — the unified frame published copyleft in the mid-2020s, reducing physics,
consciousness, ethics and philosophy to four first principles. By 2038 it is simply how physics is
taught.

**Symmetry · Break · Record · Constraint** — the four principles. An undifferentiated state; a
distinction that occurs within it; the difference that distinction leaves in the world; and what that
difference forbids thereafter. Time runs forward because **Record** does.

**Residue** — what an event leaves behind: scattered electromagnetic radiation, gravitational
perturbation, quantum correlation, displaced dark-sector structure, vacuum fluctuation. Individually
noise. Jointly, under the Code's grammar, a redundant encoding of one break.

**Record inversion** — measuring the surviving residue, identifying the original break, and reversing
the dispersion mathematically to solve for the smallest past configuration capable of producing every
consequence still measurable. Not a simulation of the probable: a solution for the forced.

**Anchor** — the matter, coordinates or authenticated document that connects the machine to an event.
No anchor, no read. Anchors are owned, inherited, stolen, repatriated and fought over.

**Separation** — disentangling one break from the billions of overlapping breaks at the same
location. Accra's problem. Described in the project as unmixing a choir back into people.

**Gap** — a region the residue does not force. The engine marks it and leaves it. Filling a gap is
forbidden.

**The Reader** — the sealed inversion engine. Integer, no GPU, cite-or-refuse; under seal it may not
infer, interpolate or complete. Chosen for one property: it will not invent.

**Physical / experiential / interpretive record** — the three recoverable layers: what happened; what
a conscious observer perceived; what that observer believed it meant. Two honest witnesses produce
different experiential records of one event, and the machine can show that neither lied.

**ARIADNE** — Antarctic Record Interferometer and Actualisation-Date Navigation Engine. Under the
ice, operated from Cape Town. Supplies the temporal coordinate. Known to its build crew as **Bara**,
after the proposal name nobody let them forget.

**Ice memory** — the faint ordered correlation in the quantum state of the deep ice lattice. The ice
stored no images; it stayed coupled to the atmosphere, radiation and gravity through which history
propagated, and its dated layers let the residue be timed. *The Karoo says that something happened.
Antarctica says when.*

**Read-residue** — the trace that the act of reading leaves. Because observation is itself an event,
every read writes a new record into the archive. The rule is the seventh and least discussed of the
consortium's founding constraints, and it is the one the book is about.

**Pre-echo** — informal crew word for a temporal signature that dates a record earlier than the event
that produced it. Not an accepted term. Nobody wanted to accept a term.
