## LAYER P — story clock

One Antarctic year is the master clock: the ship goes down, the ice closes, the ice opens. Yusuf
cannot be reached in person from the closing to the opening, and the anomaly lands inside that
window. That is the book's pressure vessel.

| Phase | Above the ice | Under the ice |
|---|---|---|
| **P0 — Proof** | The first small cases land; the machine does unambiguous good | Bara nominal; last ship out; the season closes |

| **P2 — Anomaly** | Two timestamps; contamination assumed; suspicion lands on Yusuf | Alone with an impossible number and a data budget |
| **P3 — Elimination** | Accra proves column-wide; Nairobi proves custody; Technopark re-inverts from raw | Cold, dark, and correct |
| **P4 — Consequence** | The chair's veto is bypassed; experiential reading demonstrated; the second instrument | The read-residue signature is identified for what it is |
| **P5 — Constraint** | Not a shutdown: a binding. What the consortium promises now | The ice opens. The ship comes. Someone gets off it. |
