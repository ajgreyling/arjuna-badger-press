# Canon Choices — *The History Machine*

> **LOCKED** = binding. **OPEN** = may not be written as fact until closed.
> Upstream: *Afrika 2035* (2035) is soil. Downstream: *AFRIKA 2100* is far future — no winks.

---

## LOCKED

- **C-01 · Title & year.** Working title **The History Machine**; story year **2038**. Four parts
  named for the four principles: **SYMMETRY · BREAK · RECORD · CONSTRAINT**. Alternate titles kept
  live for the author only (*Record Inversion*, *Ice Memory*, *The Quiet Places*) — see O-02.
- **C-02 · POV.** **Ensemble close third**, one consciousness per section, section break when the
  skull changes. Five threads tagged by centre: **K** Karoo · **T** Technopark · **A** Accra ·
  **N** Nairobi · **I** Ice (Antarctica). No omniscient consortium overview; the project is only
  ever seen from inside one of its five rooms.
- **C-03 · Primary and secondary spine.** **Refilwe Mangope** (K) carries the book. **Yusuf Salie**
  (I) carries the isolated second spine and, from the anomaly on, the dread. The other three threads
  are full principals, not support: Accra proves, Nairobi authenticates, Technopark builds.
- **C-04 · Continuity state.** The **420 Code** is the accepted unified frame by 2035 (*Afrika 2035*
  C-03) and is copyleft. This book stands on that and does not re-litigate it. The Code's four
  principles as read here — Symmetry, Break, Record, Constraint — are **the consortium's reading**,
  formally published as such, and the Code's author has never endorsed it (C-06).
- **C-05 · Record inversion is real in-world and works.** The machine is not a fraud, not a
  metaphor, and not secretly a simulation. Everything it returns is forced by residue or marked as
  gap. Any character who claims otherwise is arguing, not narrating.
- **C-06 · G and AJ stay offstage.** **G** is alive in 2038 (b. 1980; d. 2058 per continuity), aged
  58. He appears **only** as a letter, a refusal, a citation, or third-party report — never on the
  page as a scene principal, never as a mentor cameo, never at a ribbon-cutting. His position:
  *the Code describes structure, not permission.* **AJ** appears only as press/CongoNet texture.
  This is not coyness; it is the book's argument — the instrument does not need its author's
  blessing and cannot be stopped by withholding it.
- **C-07 · The five centres.** Karoo (detection) · Technopark, Stellenbosch (sensors +
  reconstruction engine) · Accra (ingestion + separation) · Nairobi (authentication) · ARIADNE,
  Antarctica (time reference), operated from Cape Town through **SANAP**. Antarctica is inside the
  African project, not a foreign appendage. The Cape Town–ship–ice corridor is physical, seasonal
  and load-bearing.
- **C-08 · Consortium composition.** Credible and named as fiction-world institutions: **CERN**
  (fundamental measurement), the **SKA** in South Africa (weak-signal detection), quantum labs in
  **China, India, Europe and the United States**, **African universities** holding the philosophical
  and ethical architecture, **global archival institutions** supplying historical anchors, and the
  sealed inversion AI (MACHINE §9). No invented crimes attributed to real institutions or living
  people.
- **C-09 · The ethical architecture is African and is not decorative.** The chair that governs how
  recovered history may be used sits in African universities by design, not by diversity gesture,
  and it has real veto power in the first act — which is precisely what the second act attacks.
- **C-10 · The seven hard rules bind** (MACHINE §5). Read-only · anchor required · resolution costs ·
  records overlap · gaps stay gaps · conscious experience protected · **every read writes**. No
  scene may quietly suspend one for convenience.
- **C-11 · The Reader will not invent.** The inversion engine is a sealed Lucid lineage: integer,
  no GPU, cite-or-refuse, no inference under seal. Never a POV, never a voice, never cute
  (continuity: *Afrika 2035* C-14/C-15/C-16).
- **C-12 · Reconstructions are never cinema.** On the page they are sparse, measured, gap-marked,
  slow and physically ugly. The emotional weight comes from what people do in the room, not from
  what the render looks like. No holograms, no ghost-figures walking, no soundtrack.
- **C-13 · The first cases are small and land.** An unsolved murder; a missing child; who fired
  first in a disputed massacre; whether a convicted man was innocent. Each is answered, each is
  verified, and each answer teaches somebody powerful what the instrument is for. The book earns its
  ethics by first showing the machine doing unambiguous good.
- **C-14 · The asks arrive as pressure, not villainy.** Governments demand access. Religions request
  and dread verification. Families want their dead. Courts try to admit reconstructed experience.
  Intelligence services begin with rooms and end with skulls. Indigenous communities argue the past
  and the dead are not public property. Corporations file for ownership of recovered performances,
  inventions and lost works. Every one of these gets its best argument on the page.
- **C-15 · Indigenous custody is a position, not a plot obstacle.** The claim — that the dead did not
  consent, that some knowledge is held by right and not by capability, that a repatriated object is
  an anchor and therefore repatriation is now also a physics question — is argued by people with
  standing and is never resolved by a scientist explaining reality to them.
- **C-16 · The anomaly is real.** Two temporal signatures for one event; second one earlier by an
  unabsorbable margin. Elimination sequence binding and in order (MACHINE §8): contamination →
  instrument → column-wide presence → provenance → re-inversion from raw. No shortcut, no lucky
  hunch, no single genius seeing it first.
- **C-17 · The anomaly threatens the Code, not just the machine.** Because **Record** is what makes
  time directional, a record predating its event means the machine is wrong, or the Code is
  incomplete, or the universe has already been observed from outside its own history. All three stay
  honest on the page until O-05 closes.
- **C-18 · Prior access.** Somebody — or something — has already been in the archive. Binding shape:
  what the team finds is **read-residue**, the same signature their own instrument writes under Rule
  7. Identity, intent and origin of the prior reader: **OPEN (O-05)**. Whatever closes, it is never
  a species with a spaceship, never a government with a secret earlier machine as the *final*
  answer, and never a benevolent guide.
- **C-19 · No paradox plot.** Nothing is changed. Nobody is warned. There is no message in the ice
  addressed to anyone. The horror is epistemic and it is enough.
- **C-20 · The thesis.** Civilisation ran on the decay of the past. Forgetting was load-bearing in
  justice, faith, marriage, diplomacy and grief. Removing it does not end conflict — it removes the
  protections that made conflict survivable. The book argues this by consequence, never by a
  character delivering it as a speech.
- **C-21 · Cost discipline.** Every read costs energy, computation, custody and somebody's peace, and
  the reader of the novel should always know roughly which budget is being spent. A read that costs
  nothing is a continuity error.
- **C-22 · The Bara joke stays.** The Stellenbosch build crew's name for ARIADNE, from the submitted
  proposal name **B**oreal **A**ctualisation **R**eference **A**rray. Used once as a joke, then
  forever as an ordinary crew word — including in the scenes where it stops being funny.
- **C-23 · Living-person discipline.** As *Afrika 2035* C-09: no invented crimes, no diagnosis
  tourism, private pain private unless already on the record. Real institutions are rendered as
  fiction-world institutions under pressure, never as conspiracies with membership lists.
- **C-24 · Ending shape.** Not a victory, not an apocalypse, not a shutdown that solves it. The
  machine works, the anomaly stands, and the last act is a **constraint** — what the consortium
  binds itself to now that it knows every read writes and something else is reading. Whether the
  binding holds is not answered.

## OPEN

- **O-01 · Shelf placement.** Companion in *The African Gold Trilogy* line (2038 sits neatly between
  *Afrika 2035* and *AFRIKA 2100*) **vs** first book of its own line. *Recommendation: companion —
  the continuity is doing real work and the shelf already carries a 2035 and a 2100.* Nothing in
  `site/build.py` until this closes.
- **O-02 · Final title.** *The History Machine* is clear and sells the premise. *Record Inversion* is
  colder and more precise. *Ice Memory* is the most beautiful and the least honest about the book's
  scope. *Recommendation: keep* The History Machine *as title, use the other two as part- or
  section-titles.*
- **O-03 · The first deliberate historical reconstruction.** Which event is the one that returns two
  timestamps? Constraints: it must be old enough that the second signature is unabsorbable, and it
  must matter to somebody in the room. Candidates: a deep-prehistory southern African site; the
  disputed massacre from the early cases; the missing-child case's anchor location read far deeper
  than intended.
- **O-04 · The missing child case.** Locked as one of the first four (C-13); the specific family,
  town and outcome are open. Care rail: the answer must not be a twist.
- **O-05 · What the pre-echo is.** Recommended: read-residue of a prior reader (MACHINE §8). Open:
  whether the book names the reader at all, and whether the consortium ever answers it.
- **O-06 · Does experiential reading get used on a living person?** The rule says protected. The
  question is whether somebody does it anyway, to whom, and whether the book shows it from inside.
- **O-07 · Whether Bara survives the winter.** Physical jeopardy in Antarctica is available but must
  not become an adventure plot. If Yusuf is in danger it is from cold, isolation, and being the only
  person who can be blamed for an impossible number.
- **O-08 · Consortium fracture.** Does the northern half relocate the centre, fork the Code's
  reading, or build a second machine without the ethical chair? *Recommendation: the third — a
  second instrument with no seal on its Reader is the most frightening thing available and costs no
  new physics.*
