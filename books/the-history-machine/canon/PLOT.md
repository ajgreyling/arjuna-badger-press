# Plot — *The History Machine*

> Skeleton. Four parts named for the Code's four principles (C-01). Ensemble braid across five
> centres; threads share a week and a problem, not a room.

---

## Part I — SYMMETRY (ch 01–06)
*The undifferentiated state: a project that has not yet broken anything.*

- **K:** the Karoo hears its first clean residue. Refilwe runs the detection chain the way she has
  run every instrument — quietly, defensively, and with the district's silence as the real asset.
- **T:** Nadia's sensors survive contact with the world. The last ship of the season loads at Cape
  Town; Yusuf goes south with it.
- **N:** Wanjiru's custody standard is written before it is needed, which is the only reason it works.
- **A:** Ama's separation runs on a busy coordinate for the first time and unmixes the choir.
- **The first cases land** (C-13): a murder, a wrongful conviction, a disputed massacre, a missing
  child. The machine does unambiguous good and everybody sees it.
- **Turn into II:** the good is undeniable — which means the queue now matters more than the physics.

## Part II — BREAK (ch 07–12)
*A distinction occurs.*

- The asks arrive (C-14). States, courts, services, corporations, communities, families. Each with
  its best argument.
- **N:** the first attempt to admit a physical reconstruction as evidence. Then the first request for
  an **experiential** read, and the discovery that "protected" is a word and not a mechanism.
- **Chair:** Nomvula's veto is used, holds, and is immediately routed around by people who never
  intended to break it.
- **I:** the first deliberate historical reconstruction (O-03). ARIADNE returns **two** temporal
  signatures. Contamination assumed. Yusuf files it, flags it, and cannot be visited.
- **Midpoint:** the ice closes. The instrument that dates the past is now unreachable for eight
  months, and the number it produced is impossible.

## Part III — RECORD (ch 13–19)
*What the break leaves behind — and what leaves a record without a break.*

- Elimination in the binding order (C-16, MACHINE §8):
  1. **Accra** — the second signature is present throughout the entire ice column. Contamination dies.
  2. **Nairobi** — custody chain intact end to end. Fraud dies.
  3. **Technopark** — re-inversion from raw residue. Pipeline error dies.
  4. What survives: **a record that existed before the event that generated it.**
- The suspicion that lands on Yusuf before it lands on physics, and what eight months of that does to
  a man who cannot be visited.
- **The Code is what breaks**, not just the machine (C-17): Record is supposed to be the arrow.
- Above the ice, the consequences compound while the physics burns: the queue is captured, the
  experiential read is demonstrated (O-06), the north proposes a second instrument with an unsealed
  Reader (O-08).
- **Identification:** the pre-echo's signature is the same class of trace the consortium's own reads
  write under Rule 7. It is **read-residue**. Somebody was here first.

## Part IV — CONSTRAINT (ch 20–24)
*What the record forbids thereafter.*

- Not a shutdown, not a victory, not an apocalypse (C-24). The instrument works and will keep
  working; the anomaly stands and will not resolve; the second machine is being built regardless.
- The last act is a **binding**: what the consortium constrains itself to, now that it knows every
  read writes and something else has been reading. Written as a document, argued line by line, and
  signed by people who each lose something in it.
- Nomvula's argument arrives at its full weight: Constraint is a principle, not a footnote. A
  civilisation that can recover everything has removed the mercy that made memory survivable.
- **Close:** the ice opens. The ship comes in. Someone gets off it. The machine hums in the Karoo,
  and the archive now contains, permanently, the record of the day humanity introduced itself.
