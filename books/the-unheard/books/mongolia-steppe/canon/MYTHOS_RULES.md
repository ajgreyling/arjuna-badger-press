# MYTHOS_RULES — *The Felt and the Sky* (Mongolia / the steppe herders)

> The book's binding "physics" and HARD guardrails — the seven series guardrails (`../../canon/
> SERIES_BIBLE.md` §4) specialised to Mongolia, plus the grounding contract. **Gate-enforceable.** These
> are not constraints on the wonder; they are **the condition of being allowed to tell it.** Where a rule
> and a plot beat collide, **the rule wins.**

## Rule 0 — There is NO fantastical layer (the grounding contract)

The "wonder" is the **true** one (WORLD): a living nomadic pastoral civilisation reading the open steppe
with a precision no instrument matches. **No magic, no mysticism, no aliens, no noble-savage sixth sense.**
The astonishing is always **competence**, and every astonishing thing is **earned by a real, checkable
object first** (the ger raised in three hours; the migration timed to grass; the sky read right and beating
the data; the dzud's arithmetic; the spring that is the only water for a district). **We earn the wonder by
earning the object** (series §4.6). If a beat asks the reader to believe something the herders *couldn't
demonstrate*, cut it.

## Rule 1 — Never depict the secret/sacred (withholding is dignity)

The book honours the **existence** of the sacred and **never scripts its content.**
- **The ovoo / Tengri sky-worship / shamanic practice:** show the **threshold** — a companion taught to
  **circle the ovoo three times clockwise, add a stone, leave an offering, tie the blue khadag** — and
  **look away** at the sacred itself. **Never script a shaman's invocation, a real rite, or a real sacred
  song.** Characters **withhold**: *"that's not mine to tell," "you're not placed to hear that."*
  Withholding is **dignity, not a tease.**
- **The long-song (urtiin duu) the book uses must be FICTIONAL** (or used only with a tradition-holder's
  explicit permission via the sensitivity read). Honour the *form* and the *meaning*; never put a real
  sacred verse on the page.
- Khöömei, the morin khuur, the deel, süütei tsai, airag, bökh — these are **public cultural practice** and
  may be shown with care and accuracy; the **sacred** (ovoo, Tengri, shamanic rite, any closed knowledge)
  is the line.

## Rule 2 — Never conflate or homogenise peoples (the critical one)

**The Khalkha Mongol steppe-herders this book centres are NOT the Kazakh eagle-hunters.**
- If the **eagle-hunters** appear at all, they are a **named, distinct people**: ethnic **Kazakhs**,
  **Turkic-language, Sunni Muslim**, of **Bayan-Ölgii** in the far-western Altai — *not* "the Mongols and
  their eagles." Prefer a clear contrast or a separate thread; **never blur them into the steppe herders.**
  (PLACE_RESEARCH §0.)
- **Khöömei is western-Altai in origin** — attribute correctly; don't assume every famous "Mongolian"
  element belongs to the central steppe family the book follows.
- Within the steppe world, name the **specific** (fictional) community/family; honour internal distinctions;
  **prefer a clearly-fictional district** over putting words in a real, nameable, living sum's mouth.

## Rule 3 — No deficit framing, no white/outsider saviour, no trauma-spectacle

- The **herders lead, decide, and own the win.** The crew and Jakobus **witness and amplify; they never
  rescue the culture.** Tuyaa (the local lead) carries the heart; the visitors help her *see* and then get
  out of the way.
- **Hardship is a thing done *to* people by a system** (mine + law + climate) — made visible (the broken
  neighbour family, the dry spring, the dzud's toll, the smoke-trap ger district) **without gore-for-thrill
  or misery-spectacle**, never as the people's defining trait.
- The **wrestling champion**, **Batu**, and the herders are **competent, complex, self-determining, and
  frequently *more* capable than the visitors in their domain.** They never exist to teach the outsider a
  lesson or be saved by him.

## Rule 4 — Honour the protocols of the sacred, the dead, and the named

Defer to Mongolian cultural protocol in-world and in production: the ovoo, the sky, the recently dead, the
closed place. When in doubt, **a character withholds** and the camera looks away.

## Rule 5 — No "empty land," no "timeless Genghis-land," no exoticisation (the anti-poison pillar)

- The steppe is **alive, modern, adapting, expert, and under threat** — **not** empty, timeless, backward,
  or a dead conqueror's leftover. The "empty frontier" is the **antagonist's lie** the reader arrives
  believing and the book dismantles.
- **Genghis is neither demonised nor lionised** — the **present** people are the subject; the cliché is the
  paintover the book lifts (PLACE_RESEARCH §7, [CONTESTED] — handle as theme, not assertion).
- The achievement, knowledge, and beauty are **the herders'**. The crew witnesses; it never claims. **No
  noble-savage mysticism, no "vanishing people" dirge** (§7a) — honour the **grip, not the grave.**

## Rule 6 — The wonder must be witnessable and real (the Crichton spine)

Every astonishing claim — the land-use system, the sky-read, the migration, the felt home, the dzud, the
water — is earned by a **real, checkable thing** grounded in [`PLACE_RESEARCH.md`]. The science/data keeps
arriving **late** to what the herders already know (the survey is *blind* where Batu *sees*). Skeptics
respected: Tuyaa is a trained scientist whose re-conversion earns the reader's. **No silly, no woo.**

## Rule 7 — The sensitivity read is the FLOOR, not the finish (hard gate)

In-culture Mongolian consultants — **herders, long-song / wrestling tradition-holders, scholars** — engaged
**before publication, with a real veto.** **Every people-, place-, community-, and personal-name in this
canon is PROVISIONAL** until set on that advice. Lived-experience consultants (Tourette's, autism, Deaf)
have a real veto on the crew's representation (series §5b). **The book is not publication-final without
this**, regardless of how complete the manuscript is.

## The contested-figure discipline (specific to this book)

PLACE_RESEARCH flags items as **[CONTESTED]** (notably the 2009–10 dzud "3% national" snapshot — corrected
to ~20% / ~9.7–10.3M; and the partial "resolution" of the Khanbogd case). **Write [CONTESTED] material
carefully** — as the *texture of a real, complicated situation*, never as a flat asserted fact, and never
inflated for drama. When the book needs a number, use the **corrected** one; when it needs a victory, prefer
the **honest, partial** one over a clean rescue.

> **These rules are the condition of telling this story. The wonder is real; the people are the experts; the
> camera stays with the unheard.** (Series §4; THEMES; STORY_BIBLE.)
