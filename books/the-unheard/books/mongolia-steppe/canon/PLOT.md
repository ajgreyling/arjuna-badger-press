## The series spine, in this book's key

> *The crew (+ Tuyaa) arrives → a local who is **done** being spoken for → a flattening force
> (mine + "empty frontier" + the law) → the visitors' instinct to help/tell/fix is **wrong** (the humility
> beat) → the locals take the telling back → the visitors' real job turns out to be **witnessing and
> standing guard** → the thread (the road, the next unheard place, the lesson of Sawubona) pulls onward.*

## The dramatic engine

**Two ways of reading the same land collide:** the consultancy's survey ("underused frontier" — Tuyaa's
city fluency, the mine's frame) vs. Batu's thousand-year grammar of grass, water, sky — describing the
**same ground**, with the herders there first and **the law on the mine's side** (use-right, not title).
Tuyaa is the hinge: which language she chooses decides the book.

## ACT I — THE EMPTY STORY (arrival; the lie everyone's come to tell)

- **Cold open (the keystone image — SEED).** A herding family **strikes the ger at dawn to follow the
  grass** — the whole felt home folded onto a few animals in an hour, a thousand-year choreography — while
  on the ridge a **mining-lease fence** cuts the route the grandfather rode. *A home that moves, meeting a
  line that doesn't.* (Establish the wonder and the threat in one picture.)
- **Tuyaa returns** as the survey's acceptable face; the **crew arrives** for the "timeless Genghis-land"
  travelogue. Both commissions are the same erasure (one hard, one soft). Establish Tuyaa's two worlds (the
  city smoke vs. the steppe) and her shame/longing under the cynicism.
- **The ger welcome — and the tea that beats Jakobus (PAYOFF #2).** The hospitality cup, **süütei tsai**:
  Jakobus, who eats and drinks everything, is **undone by the salt-where-his-body-wants-sweet and the
  butter turning on his tongue.** He owns it — *his* limit, never the host's fault — and the failing earns
  him the fire (SSOT). *(Also: the airag by the door he must gently decline — no alcohol — handled with
  his usual grace.)* Place early; it's the first crack in the visiting master.
- **First sight of the wonder:** Batu reads the sky / the migration / the land in a way the survey's data
  can't — and the crew's instruments and the consultancy's "empty" map start to look *blind*. Naila (crew
  eyes) clocks the **lease documents** don't add up.
- **The flattening force shows its face:** the **mine's fixer** — reasonable, Mongolian, sincere about
  "development" — frames the steppe as frontier and the herders as the past. **Frik's blurt** names the
  polite lie out loud (the anti-paintover beat).

## ACT II — THE GROUND HE CAN'T READ (the humility engine; the wonder deepens; the wound)

- **The wrestling humbling (PAYOFF #1 — the strand's showcase).** At a **local Naadam / informal bökh
  contest**, the district quietly checks the visitors out — watches them eat, take a joke, carry
  themselves — and clocks Jakobus as *a man who can handle himself* (true). Offered a friendly bout, **a
  half-step too sure, off his own ground,** he accepts *like he's the dangerous one* — and the **regional
  champion**, half his fuss and twice his craft, **lands him on his back in the dust in front of
  everyone.** He **laughs, bows, makes no excuses** — *"On my ground I'd have given him a game. This is his
  ground. He gave me a lesson."* — and the district **trusts him more.** (Render the champion with full
  dignity; the eagle-dance ritual; the open contest on bare grass.)
- **The horse he won't trust (PAYOFF #3).** On the **most horse-defined ground on earth**, the wheel-man's
  flat distrust of *a thing with no brakes that bites* — used once or twice, light, the herders finding it
  quietly funny and **liking him more for owning it.** The generous flip side lands: he won't ride for
  himself but is **first to catch and saddle a horse for whoever's joy it is** (the children; a grace-note
  of *Sawubona* + the giving doctrine). *(Keep distinct from the wrestling — not a defeat, just a true
  seam.)*
- **The wonder, deepened (the relay nodes):** the crew is *let in* — the ger life; the migration timed to
  grass and water; the **ovoo** (a companion taught to **circle it three times, add a stone** — the
  threshold shown, the sacred content **withheld**, MYTHOS Rule 1); the **sky read** beating the data; the
  **water** — and the discovery that the **sacred year-round spring is going dry where the drilling
  started** (the real Bor Ovoo / Undai pattern). The "empty frontier" is the most sophisticated land-use
  the crew has ever seen — **and legally undefendable.**
- **The humility beat (the crew's, and Tuyaa's).** The visitors' instinct — *help, tell it for them, fix
  it, file the report that exposes the mine* — proves **wrong**: it would put words in the herders' mouths,
  trip the "outsiders stirring up the natives" frame, and **cost the locals control.** They learn the
  hardest lesson: **get out of the way.**
- **The wound / the loss (the fear-of-loss heart).** Show it: a neighbour family already broken by the last
  dzud, half-gone to the city; the grandchildren leaving; Batu watching his world close without saying so.
  **Rhyme with Jakobus** — present, unhurried, not fixing it, sitting *with* the grief (and giving
  something away). **Tuyaa's wound opens:** she didn't escape the loss by leaving; she carried it
  differently — and her city-fluency is the weapon at her father's pasture.
- **The fire (PAYOFF — the dance grace-note; §7b).** A **long-song** night / celebration: Jakobus **joins**
  the grandmother's circle — *with* them, on their ground, one quiet earned grace-note (never re-staging
  the Egypt aria). Joy as resistance: the dance that empties the fear out so people can rest. Retro-proves
  the man; makes the loss bearable for a night.
- **Midpoint turn.** The mine moves: a **"strategic site" designation** / a fence extension / the spring
  diversion accelerates — the **law** (use-right, not title; herders can't refuse) becomes the real
  antagonist. The travelogue/survey Tuyaa was hired for is about to grease it. **She has to choose.**

## ACT III — HANDING BACK THE TELLING (the locals lead; the win is theirs)

- **Tuyaa chooses her father's country.** She turns her **city-fluency on the mine** — but *for* the
  herders and *at their direction*, translating their undefendable use-right knowledge into the language the
  law and the press understand. **She does not save them; she gives them the megaphone** and steps back.
  (Her vindication: belonging again, not the city's approval.)
- **The locals take the telling back (the relay's point).** Batu and the district lead the defence — the
  **ovoo / the spring / the migration route** as the ground they will not yield; the **champion** the young
  spine who stays and fights with his body and standing; the **grandmother** the song handed on (a
  grandchild learning the long-song). The crew's real job: **witnessing and standing guard** — Jakobus
  between the herders and the mine's muscle (the road, not the lead); Naila's document-read and the
  crew's amplification *serving* the herders' own move.
- **The set-piece climax (grounded, not gunfire-first).** The collision over the **spring / the route /
  the determination** — the herders' knowledge and standing vs. the mine's law and capital. Keep it
  **propulsive and physical** (a dzud closing in; the fenced route; the dry spring; a night drive Jakobus
  *can* do — the wheels he trusts — to get the herders' testimony / the evidence where it can't be buried),
  but **the win is the herders' call and the herders' voice**, corroborated, not led, by the outsiders.
- **The win — culture handed on, not saved (§7a discipline).** Where there is a win, it is **specific and
  modest and theirs**: a **migration route kept open / the spring defended / the determination held / the
  herd carried through one more winter / the long-song sung one more night.** Hope, not certainty — the
  dzud and the city and the law don't vanish; the people are still fighting, still here, still expert. *(A
  bittersweet partial win, true to the real record — the Khanbogd "resolution" left the most important
  things unmet — is more honest than a clean rescue. Decide the exact ledger in drafting; keep it
  hope-not-triumph.)*
- **The road pulls onward.** Jakobus and the crew leave — the **Sawubona** lesson carried, the next unheard
  place ahead. **Tuyaa stays** (or leaves *changed*, the daughter who chose) — the camera last on the
  **herders and the steppe**, not the visitors. A **completed Sawubona/Ngikhona pair** seeded here if earned
  (series §3 SSOT) — a companion who's absorbed the ethos answering him — without ever explaining it.
