# Acknowledgements

This book exists because of every historian of ancient science who has spent a career reading clay
that most of the world walked past without a second glance. My thanks to the historians of
Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Hellenistic, Islamic, and medieval science whose patient, unglamorous
archival work — deciphering cuneiform, cataloguing papyri, translating Arabic astronomical treatises
— made a book like this one possible to write honestly at all. My thanks, too, to Bill Bryson and
Yuval Noah Harari, whose own books proved that a hard, technical history could be told with warmth
and without condescension to either the reader or the people in the story.

And to everyone who ever looked up at a pattern they couldn't yet explain and decided to keep
watching anyway: this book is for you too.
