# CRAFT DOCTRINE (binding)

- **Embody, don't state** (§16): cut the sentence that explains the beat you just dramatised.
- **Body before thought** under pressure (§8); syntax **breaks** under peak stress (§18), not refines.
- **The funny is sacred** in Act I house material: comedy must actually be funny; grief is collateral on it.
- **One quotable closer per scene, one mouth** (§7).
- **Negative space** (§28): gestures unread; seam silent.
- **No LLM tells**: ration "not X, but Y"; kill em-dash addiction; specific nouns over smooth abstractions.
- **End on motion or turn**, not a thematic summary.

# CHARACTER VOICES (§7 — differentiate by failure mode, not vocabulary)

- **Vance (LIVED):** dry, kind, mechanical competence; quiets as he fades.
- **Dora:** clipped, deflecting; one true thing per scene, immediately undercut.
- **Renko:** torrential, technical, syntactically *clean* — finished prose as menace.
- **Hutch:** plain, short, generous; when syntax breaks later, the floor goes.
- **Stark:** flat, procedural; bureaucrat's menace; one sentence said slightly wrong.
