# STYLE_GUIDE — *The Scramble*

> The voice laws. The book is built on **two registers that are one man**, and the whole craft problem
> is keeping them distinct, keeping the seam silent, and letting them bleed only on purpose.

## The split-feed (the spine)

### LIVED — close third on Vance in the house
- Warm, present, sensory. Comedy lives here. Sentences breathe and vary.
- Funny *under the breath* — the humor is observational, not joke-joke. Paranoid digressions that are
  also, annoyingly, half-right.
- This is where the reader bonds. It must be a real place with real affection in it, or the curdle
  fails.
- **Degrades over the book:** as Tilt splits Vance, the LIVED prose loses warmth a notch at a time —
  the sensory detail thins, the jokes land flatter, the present tense starts skipping.

### WATCHED — the surveillance record on Subject M
- Flat, clausal, timestamped. The body-cam-and-report register. Sees everything, understands nothing.
- Renders the *same events* the LIVED sections render, but stripped of interiority — so the reader does
  the cruel work of recognizing the man.
- **No "the subject appeared sad."** The report cannot read feeling; it logs behavior. That gap is the
  horror. (ANTI_PATTERNS §16: trust the image; never caption.)
- **Degrades inversely:** as the book goes on, the WATCHED record starts catching small things only the
  LIVED man could know — a detail no camera could log — and the reader feels the membrane tear.

### The seam law (binding)
The prose **never states** that LIVED and WATCHED are the same man. Not once. The reader is given both
columns and trusted (PROJECT split-feed; ANTI_PATTERNS §28 negative space). The single allowed
collapse is the **coda**, and even there it is *image, not explanation* (§20).

## House voice non-negotiables (inherited from Craft Doctrine)

1. **Embody, don't state.** Cut the sentence that explains the sentence before it (§16, the master
   pattern). Especially: never let a character *say the theme* (dissociation, surveillance, the self).
2. **Body before thought** under pressure (§8); under peak stress, syntax **breaks**, not refines (§18,
   §22). Vance does not think in maxims while coming apart.
3. **Modulation.** Vary voltage; plain pages between sharp ones (§3, §12). The WATCHED register is the
   built-in flatness; do not let the LIVED register run at continuous peak.
4. **One quotable closer per scene, in one mouth** (§7). Not every paragraph ends on a mic-drop.
5. **No LLM tells:** ration the "not X, but Y" reframe (LLM_TELLS); kill em-dash addiction; cut half
   the "the way…" similes; restore the specific noun over the smooth abstraction.
6. **The funny is sacred.** Velocity in the house scenes; the comedy must actually be funny, because the
   grief is collateral on it.

## Character voice (noun-swap test binding — §7)
- **Vance (LIVED):** dry, kind, mechanical competence; quiets as he fades.
- **Dora:** clipped, deflecting; one true thing per scene, immediately undercut.
- **Renko:** torrential, technical, syntactically *clean* — finished prose as menace (his trait, not a
  tell).
- **Hutch:** plain, short, generous — the room's baseline; when it breaks, the floor goes.
- **Stark:** flat, procedural; bureaucrat's-menace; one sentence said slightly wrong.

## The ending guard (§20, §26, L-08)
- The coda is **smaller than the idea**. Documentary flatness. No paragraph explaining what it means.
- Hope, not certainty. The dedication carries the last weight — and it is **original**, never a copy of
  PKD's.
