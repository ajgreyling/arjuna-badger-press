## One sentence

An undercover narc so deep he is coded **Subject M** gets ordered to watch his own house through the shroud rig, while **Tilt** eats the part of him that could have noticed: and the only net left is **Northway**, which has uses for minds already scrubbed quiet.

## Why this book exists

PKD's novel is the best structural account of surveillance dissociation and the drug war's human invoice. We keep the **machine** (split-feed, self-surveillance, comedy-to-grief, institutional coda) and invent every scene, name, and sentence: same standing as [`../../no-fear-cycle/`](../../no-fear-cycle/) and [`../the-dreaming/`](../the-dreaming/).

**North star:** the Archive.org text above. If a beat is not in PKD, it is ours; if it is in PKD, it must rhyme.
