## What not to plant (avoid unfired guns)

- PKD character names, Substance D, scramble suit, New-Path as labels.
- Double conspiracy behind Northway (§6 cap).
- Clinical lecture on split brain mid-scene (§16).
- Caption explaining LIVED = WATCHED (seam law).
- Film-only beats not in SSOT PDF (e.g. Hank = Donna reveal).
