# WORLD — *The Scramble*

> Near-now, realist, low-spectacle. The dystopia is *now, harder* — not the future. No neural lace, no
> chrome. A burner phone, a stim epidemic, an undercover unit underfunded into using the people it
> watches.

## The city

Unnamed mid-size American city in decline — call it the kind of place with one good hospital, three
shuttered malls repurposed as warehouses, and a downtown that empties at six. Heat. Strip-mall
churches and strip-mall clinics, often the same architecture. The book never tours it; it leaks in
through what people can afford and what they cannot.

## Tilt (the drug)

A pharmaceutical-grade stimulant, originally a wakefulness/focus compound, now cut and pressed in
volume. Sold as a way to **keep up** — two jobs, the algorithmtic shifts, the kids, the rent. That is
the obscene part: it is not sold as escape. It is sold as *competence*. (MYTHOS holds the mechanism and
the long cost.) Street names rotate; the house calls it Tilt. Users are not depicted as exotic; they
are the gig driver, the night nurse, the cop.

## The department / the unit

A federally-blended narcotics task force, chronically broke, structured so that no officer knows the
identity of the undercover working a given house — anonymized end-to-end, ostensibly to protect them,
actually because the org chart is a liability shield. Reports come in under subject-codes. The unit's
own surveillance of a house can therefore be assigned to the very officer embedded in it, and the
system will not flag it, because the system was built not to know.

## The shroud rig (the anonymity apparatus)

Department-issue: a wearable that scrambles the wearer's identity on every camera, mic, and report it
touches — face, voice, gait, all smeared into a statistical blur the case files call **a subject**. In
briefings, officers wearing it are literally unrecognizable to each other. The rig is the modern,
grounded analogue of the conceit: it is *how* a man can be ordered to watch himself and no one in the
room knows. It is bureaucratically mundane, glitchy, and over-budget. No magic. (MYTHOS rule: the rig
only ever does what a real over-engineered anonymization system would plausibly do.)

## Northway (the institution)

The region's only rehab that takes burned-out, insurance-less, court-referred users — a venture-backed
"recovery and reintegration" campus with a working farm and a clean intake video. It is genuinely the
last net. It is also the one place a mind that has been scrubbed quiet by Tilt is *useful*, and the
book earns — late, grounded, no woo — what Northway does with the men nobody is looking for. Northway
is not a cartoon villain; it is **competent custody** (Craft Doctrine L-07), and that is worse.

## Texture rules

- Power as paperwork, timing, and procedure — never as conspiracy theater.
- Surveillance is *ambient and boring* until it is intimate and total.
- The funny is real and load-bearing (the curdle only works if the comedy worked).
- One good hospital, one good rehab, one underfunded unit: scarcity is the engine of the tragedy.
