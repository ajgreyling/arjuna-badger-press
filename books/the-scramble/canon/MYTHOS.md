# MYTHOS — *The Scramble* (binding rules)

> The house rule is **engineering, never woo** (Craft Doctrine non-negotiable #1). This is a realist
> book; the only "mythos" is mechanism and magnitude, and it must stay grounded enough that the human
> tragedy lands as *fact*, not metaphor. Nothing here is supernatural. Everything here is plausible.

## Tilt — mechanism and long cost

- **What it does, short-term:** a stimulant that sharpens focus and suppresses fatigue. Real, banal,
  marketed (illicitly) as *competence* — keep up, don't fall behind. Users function; that's the trap.
- **What it does, long-term (the engine):** chronic heavy use degrades the integration between the
  brain's monitoring/self-narrating systems and its acting/experiencing systems. Grounded analogue:
  think of dissociative damage, split-attention burnout, the way exhausted people stop recognizing
  their own choices. The body keeps acting; the watcher in the skull stops being able to *match* the
  record to the life. **This is rendered behaviorally and physically — never explained as the theme.**
- **The hard line:** the split is never magic and never literally two people. It is one damaged nervous
  system that can no longer reconcile its surveillance of itself with its experience of itself. The
  book's two-register structure *dramatizes* this; the prose must not *diagnose* it (ANTI_PATTERNS §16,
  §18, §22). At most, one grounded clinical sentence, late, in a doctor's mouth — contested, partial.
- **No redemption chemistry.** Tilt does not get cured. Damage is damage. Hope at the end is human, not
  pharmacological (Craft Doctrine L-08).

## The shroud rig — what it can and cannot do

- **Can:** smear identity (face/voice/gait/metadata) on capture so a wearer is logged only as a subject
  code; persist that anonymity through the report chain; fail in ordinary over-engineered ways (lag,
  partial decode, a tech who hates it).
- **Cannot:** read minds, predict, or do anything a real, expensive, glitchy anonymization system
  couldn't. Its menace is **bureaucratic**, not magical (WORLD; ANTI_PATTERNS §5 — give the apparatus
  seams and human limits).
- **Why the assignment can happen:** the unit is *designed not to know* its own undercovers. A man can
  be tasked to surveil his own cover because the system that would catch the overlap was deliberately
  blinded for liability reasons. The horror is a **policy**, not a conspiracy.

## Northway — grounded, not cartoon

- What Northway ultimately does with scrubbed-quiet men is **plausible, mundane, and legal-adjacent** —
  a labor/extraction arrangement that only a mind past self-advocacy could be enrolled in. It is
  revealed late, by *consequence*, not by a villain's speech (ANTI_PATTERNS §4, §16). It rhymes with —
  does not copy — the source's institutional twist. The reveal is **one final level** (§6, capped); no
  conspiracy-behind-the-conspiracy.

## The one sanctioned intensity

The realist register holds the whole book so that **one** moment can break the baseline: the coda,
where the two registers collapse into a single plain documentary paragraph and the dedication lands.
That is the book's only "thunderclap," and it is earned by everything staying grounded (house Rule 0).
