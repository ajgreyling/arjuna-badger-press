## I. Architecture (named levels)

| Craft term | PKD (original) | *The Scramble* |
|---|---|---|
| **Dual register** | House comedy vs scramble-suit debrief vs holo-playback flatness | **LIVED** vs **WATCHED** (STYLE_GUIDE) |
| **Split protagonist** | Bob Arctor / Fred / Bruce: one nervous system, three files | Vance Roan / Subject M / late intake name |
| **Reader contract** | Reader knows Fred = Arctor long before Fred does | Reader holds both columns; seam silent (§28) |
| **Surveillance spine** | Six holo-scanners; safe-apartment playback | Rig-anonymized reports; Vance replays own house |
| **Institutional coda** | New-Path grows D; Author's Note in memoriam | Northway harvest; original dedication |

No matryoshka frame (unlike Henry Sugar). PKD's **Author's Note** is extradiegetic close, not open frame.
