## Disciplines wired in (from ANTI_PATTERNS)
- **Friction (§10):** one true dead end: a lead in Renko's case that costs effort and pays nothing.
- **Distributed competence (§1):** Vance is not the smartest in every room; Dora reads danger faster,
  Renko reads systems faster (and wrong), a tech reads the rig faster.
- **Asymmetry (§15):** Stark and Northway both "triage" but are not mirror images: one mortal-frightened,
  one warmly procedural. Rhyme, don't diagram.
- **Negative space (§28):** beats that end one early, gestures left unread, the seam left silent.
