## I. One-line engines

| Craft term | PKD (*Scanner*) | *The Scramble* |
|---|---|---|
| **Premise / what if** | What if an undercover cop had to watch himself on tape and the drug split him so he never knew? | What if the anonymity policy let a unit task a man to surveil **Subject M**: himself: until Tilt ate the watcher? |
| **Story spine** | House comedy → self-surveillance → Barris knife → brain split → Donna delivers to New-Path → flower | House warmth → WATCHED self → Renko case → seam tear → leash → Northway → documentary coda |
| **Controlling idea** | *We are punished too much for wanting to feel alive; the system that watches you also farms you.* | Same engine; original scenes (ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE) |
| **Dramatic Q (Act I)** | Who is the dealer / who is Subject M? | Who is Subject M? (reader knows) |
| **Dramatic Q (Act II)** | Can Fred stay Fred while watching Bob? | Can Vance analyze the house without the registers touching? |
| **Dramatic Q (Act III)** | What is Northway? | What does Northway do with scrubbed-quiet men? |
| **Blueprint** | PKD split-brain surveillance tragedy + Author's Note coda | Split-feed + Craft Doctrine editorial pass |

## II. Want vs. need

| | PKD (Arctor/Fred) | *The Scramble* (Vance) |
|---|---|---|
| **Want** | Score D; keep cover; have Donna | Keep the seam shut; keep the house; keep Dora's look |
| **Lie** | *I can carry both lives* · *Donna is safe affection* | *Discipline feels like competence* · *She likes me* (ch-01) |

### Press distinction (binding)

```
MACGUFFIN (hollow chase)     →  Tilt source · Subject M identity · Renko conspiracy
```
