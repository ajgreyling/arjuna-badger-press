# *The Scramble* *(working title)*

An **unauthorised homage** to Philip K. Dick's *A Scanner Darkly* — a craft exercise under the Arjuna
Badger Press house tooling. **Not licensed. Not for commercial release.** The book borrows the *engine*
of Scanner (a man assigned to surveil his own undercover identity; a drug that severs the watching self
from the living self; comedy that curdles into grief; a documentary coda that turns into an indictment)
and builds an **entirely original** story over it — original cast, city, drug, tech, and scenes.

Provenance is disclosed, never hidden — same standing as the press's *Warhammer 40,000* fan novel
([`../no-fear-cycle/`](../no-fear-cycle/)). See [`canon/ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md`](canon/ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md)
for the line we hold: **take the engine, never the text.**

## Substrate
Near-now realist. A stimulant epidemic (**Tilt**), an underfunded narcotics unit whose anonymity
policy lets it task a man to watch himself (**the shroud rig**), and the region's only rehab
(**Northway**). No neural lace, no chrome — the dystopia is *now, harder*.

## The structural engine — the split-feed
Two registers that are one man and don't know it:
- **LIVED** — close third in the house: warm, funny, sensory.
- **WATCHED** — the surveillance record on *Subject M*: flat, timestamped, sees everything, understands
  nothing.

The reader holds both halves the protagonist has lost. The prose **never** captions the seam; the coda
collapses the two into one plain paragraph and lets the dedication land.
