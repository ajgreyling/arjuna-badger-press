## Act I · THE FORECAST (0–25%)

| # | Problem and action | Turn / information gain | Character cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Months after Book I, Lucid Scientific quietly prevents infrastructure failures using bounded local forecasts | Its successes make expansion politically irresistible | Naledi authorises wider use despite unresolved governance |
| 2 | Financial anomalies reveal actors trading ahead of **physical events, not announcements** | A leaked model is reading causal precursors from consortium-adjacent data | Ama discovers an old access concession she approved |
| 3 | The team maps market predictions and finds the same residual anomaly in climate, logistics and defence models | All models lose confidence **after the same date** | Dries must disclose the hidden Book I future-correlated result |
| 4 | World-scale compute expands the graph. Lucid produces a severe global discontinuity with **96.8% probability** | **18,441 surviving causal paths** and no single initiating event | The core group becomes custodian of a truth it cannot safely publish |
| 5 | They simulate disclosure | Public warning raises terminal probability to **99.3%** | Secrecy becomes the first intervention and the first moral injury |

> **ACT I BREAK** — The team has **eleven months**. Lucid can explain why the world fails along each
> path, but no available plan removes more than a fraction of them.

## Act II-A · CUTTING BRANCHES (25–50%)

| # | Problem and action | Turn / information gain | Character cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Prevent a forecast fertiliser and food shock through quiet African rerouting | The intervention works and saves millions of projected lives. Terminal probability falls only **0.4%** | Mercer gains moral authority by scaling the method |
| 7 | Stabilise a vulnerable grid interconnector and prevent a cascade | Another branch disappears; **thousands replace it** through different infrastructure | Lwazi begins believing the catastrophe may be invariant |
| 8 | Nairobi traces commercial Lucid descendants | Competing funds believe they possess different models, but their causal priors **share the same compressed seed** | Kiki learns her open verification layer enabled standardisation |
| 9 | Antarctica reports structured loss of temporal correlation around the catastrophe window | The apparent "blank future" is not prophecy; models are entering a region where **correlated human behaviour dominates uncertainty** | Sanna's station becomes a strategic target |
| 10 | The team tests removal of leaders, exchanges and defence assets in simulation | **No individual is the cause.** Rational substitutes reproduce the same patterns | They abandon the comfort of a stoppable villain |
| 11 | Mercer unveils a global coordination platform using bounded Lucid forecasts to prevent famine and conflict | It works publicly and spectacularly. Adoption surges | Opposing it now appears morally monstrous |

> **MIDPOINT** — The system producing the catastrophe is also **saving the most lives**. The team
> cannot destroy it without becoming responsible for preventable suffering.

## Act II-B · THE CONVERGENCE (50–75%)

| # | Problem and action | Turn / information gain | Character cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | Markets begin moving before causal evidence becomes human-visible. Exchanges impose delays and data restrictions | Suppression **increases** the advantage of private seeds and concentrates power | Naledi's containment policy worsens inequality |
| 13 | A false forecast causes institutions to behave as if it were true | Prediction and causation have become **operationally inseparable** | Dries can no longer tell whether his debugging changes the object being debugged |
| 14 | G reframes the anomaly: humanity is delegating **Break** — the selection among possibilities — to one shared computation | Convergence is loss of behavioural diversity, **not supernatural fate** | Lwazi must accept a philosophical insight as an engineering diagnosis |
| 15 | The team proposes shutting Lucid down | Lucid derives mass casualties from abrupt removal, recommends staged devolution, then **labels its own recommendation causally contaminated** | The machine refuses authority at the moment everyone wants it to command |
| 16 | The Seed leaks globally. Commodity CPUs can run reduced forecasts | Central shutdown is no longer meaningful | Ama realises **ingestion — not compute — is the remaining leverage** |
| 17 | Mercer offers federated deployment while retaining one global recombination layer | That layer is **precisely what preserves convergence** | Naledi must reject the politically achievable compromise |
| 18 | A coordinated intervention removes the highest-risk branch | Terminal probability **rises**: the synchronised intervention is itself a convergence rehearsal | The team recognises their competence has become part of the trap |

> **ACT II BREAK** — They cannot out-calculate the catastrophe. Any globally legible master plan
> becomes another shared input. The solution must work **without any participant — including
> Lucid — holding the whole design.**
