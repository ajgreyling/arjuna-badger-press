## Relationship web

- **Dries ↔ Ama** — mutual respect expressed as arguments about whether discarded data is ever truly discarded. Emotional spine: **trust without total access.**
- **Dries ↔ Kiki** — builder versus verifier. He experiences her controls as obstruction; she sees his improvisation as an unaudited threat.
- **Lwazi ↔ G** — credentialled experimentalist versus outsider theorist. Each needs the other and resents that fact.
- **Sanna ↔ Ama** — Antarctica supplies the temporal signal; Ghana decides whether it is contamination. Their professional dispute becomes **the hinge of both books**.
- **Naledi ↔ Mercer** — sovereignty versus universal stewardship. Both can articulate real harms caused by the other's preferred model.
- **G ↔ Lucid** — creator of the corpus versus a machine that operationalises it. **Lucid must be able to refuse even G.**

## Character payoff tests

| Arc | Plant | Crisis test | Payoff action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dries:** observability → restraint | He insists "if it happened, log it" | Total logging exposes private experiential records | He deletes the global recombination path while preserving local evidence |
| **Ama:** neutrality → stewardship | She calls ingestion "plumbing" | A technically neutral query targets vulnerable communities | She invokes Ghana's right to refuse ingestion |
| **Kiki:** integrity → contestability | A valid signature ends arguments | Sabotage bears a valid signature | She proves that authority without independent challenge is only authenticated power |
| **Lwazi:** recognition → independence | He rehearses a prestige lecture | Recognition is offered for silence and relocation | He publishes the killable raw derivation from Technopark |
| **Sanna:** isolation → relation | She speaks more easily to instruments than people | The Antarctic anomaly cannot be interpreted alone | She trusts a distributed human chain she cannot personally audit |
| **Naledi:** secrecy → federated legitimacy | She locks the first result down | Secrecy raises catastrophe probability | She disperses authority across African jurisdictions |

## Note on G (continuity)

**G is a principal**, working from Technopark — not offstage, not posthumous, not a consultant on a
video call. He refuses soft claims, asks what measurement would kill a thing, states the reflexivity
trap, and finds the blind spot in Act III. The alternative on the table
(posthumous notebooks) buys mystery at the cost of intellectual conflict and consent, and the bible
recommends against it. See `OPEN_DECISIONS.md`.

Continuity with the press shelf: G is the 420 Code originator of *Afrika 2035* — economics degree,
no physics degree, copyleft corpus, kill-switch discipline, zero appetite for worship. Book I's G is
that man three or four years later, being asked to let the work leave his hands.
