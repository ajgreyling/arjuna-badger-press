DIE VUUR IN DIE DONKER (Winter sonder Einde · Boek I) — Cover image prompt
(image plate ONLY; title/author overlaid by make_cover.py)

DESIGN INTENT: A LITERARY cover, not a genre/romance paperback. Think Penguin Modern Classics,
Folio Society, or a serious literary-press jacket — a single strong, controlled GRAPHIC image with
texture and restraint. NOT a smooth digital fantasy painting, NOT a romance clinch, NOT three
figures posing, NOT a glowing sunset behind anyone's head. Deliberately UNLIKE a commercial
Viking-romance cover.

STYLE: A textured RELIEF-PRINT / LINOCUT / woodcut-and-gouache hybrid — bold carved lines, visible
grain and tool-marks, flat planes of colour with hand-printed texture, slight mis-registration
between colour layers, the matte ink-on-paper look of a fine-press print. Limited, confident
palette. Strong negative space. Graphic and composed, the way a literary jacket is designed rather
than illustrated.

THE IMAGE (one controlled idea — fire held in the dark, the heart of the book):
A single Norse longhouse hearth — the langeldr, a low line of fire — burning in a vast dark
interior, OR a single woman's silhouette standing alone before that low fire, seen from behind,
small against the dark, her braided hair and the line of her shoulders just caught in the firelight.
Most of the frame is deep, textured darkness (the endless winter, the long hall at night); the only
warmth is the carved, glowing line of the hearth-fire and its amber light pooling on the figure and
the floor. The cold blue-black of the dark vs the single ember-amber of the fire. Restrained,
haunting, adult, literary — the fire that never went out, alone in the dark.

PALETTE: deep blue-black and charcoal for the dark; a single band of ember-amber, ochre and red for
the fire and its glow; bone-white for the few carved highlights. Three or four inks at most, like a
limited-run print.

COMPOSITION: vertical portrait 2:3. Keep the UPPER THIRD calm/dark (for the title overlay) and a
quieter band at the FOOT (for the author line). The fire and figure sit in the lower-middle.

MOOD: solitude, banked heat, the long dark, dignity. A woman and a fire, and a great deal of
winter. Sensual only by implication (warmth in cold), never explicit, no nudity, no romance pose.

NEGATIVE: no smooth digital painting; no glowing sunset; no three figures; no romance clinch; no
faces turned to camera; no horned helmets; no text/lettering in the image; not busy; no modern
objects; no children.

ASPECT: portrait, 2:3 (1024x1536).
