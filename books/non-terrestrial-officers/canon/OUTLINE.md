## The bridge (McKinnon → Serpo)

Movement I ends when Gary copies a registry row he should not be able to read — a **fleet-to-fleet**
transfer whose destination designator is not terrestrial and whose duration field reads **4,680 days**.
He does not know the word **Serpo**. He knows the number won't leave him alone.

Movement II opens on **1965** with that same designator on a paper form — believing prose, no hedge.
The 2005 forum leaks are Movement II's **Archive coda**, reframed as the archive he almost downloaded
before the connection died.
