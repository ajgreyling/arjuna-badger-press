## 0. The pitch in one breath

A **standalone novel** that zooms in **heavy** on **Gary McKinnon** — the Glasgow-born systems
administrator who, from a bedroom in North London (2001–2002), walked into US military and **NASA**
networks and came out insisting he had seen evidence of **non-terrestrial officers**, **fleet-to-fleet
transfers**, and craft that did not belong on any public ledger.

This is **The Altas Resonance Engine** franchise's **boundary-pusher**: where the main saga keeps the maybe open,
this book **believes on the page** — Crichton-immersed, Weir-checkable, Dan Brown-propulsive — and
hands the reader back to reality through **the most exhaustive back matter in the press**: every file
name McKinnon named, every spreadsheet column, every legal filing, every NASA denial, every sceptic
rebuttal, every Serpo post timestamp.

**Part II (or integrated coda):** a **Serpo retelling** in **house style** — the 2005 forum leaks
rendered as lived narrative: twelve Americans, the Ebens, two suns, thirteen years away, legend with
paperwork made **story** the way *The Men Who Opened the Door* made STAR GATE story, but here the
commitment goes **further**. The reader finishes having *been there* — then turns to the dossier and
sorts the bins themselves.

## 2. The believing register (LOCKED — this book's exception)

**On the page:** McKinnon's discoveries are **real in-world**. The spreadsheets exist. The craft in
the image buffer exist. The non-terrestrial officer roster is **there**. The prose does not wink. The
prose does not hedge every paragraph. The reader is **inside** the terminal session — heart rate, cursor
blink, the moment a directory name stops being funny.

**In the back matter:** **Full honesty.** Real / Debated / Invented / Legend — every claim McKinnon ever
made in interview, every UK/US extradition document, every NASA FOIA response, every sceptic column,
every Serpo post attributed to "Anonymous" and Victor Martinez's list. The dedication to Gary McKinnon
is **respect for the man**; the dossier is **respect for the reader**.

**Rule:** No composite "Gary" who did things the real man never claimed. Invention is for **scene
texture** (the flat, the dial-up lag, the Asperger's exhaustion), not for smuggling new UFO claims
past the reader as McKinnon's.

## 2b. What drives the draft (LOCKED)

**Joy and wonder drive this book — not word counts, not chapter metrics, not expansion targets.**

The manuscript ends when the story is told: when the terminal glow has done its work, when Serpo has
earned its thirteen years on the page, when Gary's decade lands, when ch-99 can audit without thinning.
If a scene needs one paragraph, it gets one paragraph. If a year on Serpo needs room to breathe, it gets
room. Length follows the story; the story does not follow length.

**Wonder register (this book's variant):** immersive belief on the page — the Why Files *maybe* replaced
here by **commitment** in fiction, then **honesty** in back matter. The reader should finish Movement I
feeling the size of what Gary saw. Movement II should feel like *Arrival* seriousness under two suns.
The extradition arc should feel like a life, not a summary. The integer **4680** should haunt because
the story makes it haunt, not because a scaffold said repeat it.

Build tooling (`expansions/`, `stitch.sh`) is for **assembly**, not for padding. Never add words to hit
a number. Add words when the scene demands them.

## 3. Structure (two movements)

### Movement I — *Solo* (McKinnon)

Chronological + terminal-session braid:

1. **Before** — Glasgow, systems brain, the UFO curiosity, the suppressed-tech motivation (his own words:
   looking for evidence of free energy / antigravity held back).
2. **The walk-in** — `SOLARIS` / military networks; weak passwords; the moral panic of realising you're
   *in*; leaving the message ("your security is crap").
3. **The finds** — sequenced for slow burn, each beat a chapter cliff:
   - **`Non-Terrestrial Officers`** — the phrase that names the find.
   - **Fleet-to-fleet transfers** — logistics language in a human military system.
   - **The spreadsheet** — ship names, off-books craft, registry that shouldn't compile.
   - **The image** — structured craft above the atmosphere; the download that takes too long; the copy
     that may or may not still exist.
   - **Building 8 / Johnson Space Center** (as he described) — photographic department; UFO imagery
     scrubbed from public feeds (his claim; NASA denial in back matter).
4. **The knock** — discovery, the decade-long extradition fight, **Theresa May's 2012 block** on human-rights
   grounds, the man who never went to America to stand trial.
5. **After** — what it cost; what he still says; the files that were never produced in court.

**POV:** Close third on a **fictional composite witness** who can carry scene (legal, domestic, terminal)
**or** disciplined close-third on **McKinnon himself** — author's call at outline; either way **no
mockery**, no "crank" framing in the prose. The world treats his reads as **true**.

### Movement II — *Serpo* (house-style retelling)

Embedded as **what the spreadsheet was pointing at** — or as a **found document** McKinnon glimpsed a
pointer to — or as a **second part** the reader enters after the extradition chapter. Author locks at
outline; seed allows all three.

**Serpo on the page (believing):**

- April 1964 — **Roswell** survivor (**EBE-1** / "Ebe") lives long enough to broker contact.
- **1965** — exchange team selected; **twelve** Americans; two Ebens as guides.
- **Planet Serpo** — Zeta Reticuli; binary suns; harsh light; Ebens' society rendered with anthropological
  seriousness, not cartoon aliens.
- **1965–1978** — thirteen years; one death; children born; the culture shock **both ways**.
- **Return** — debrief; compartmentalisation; the forum posts in 2005 as **delayed leak** of the archive
  McKinnon almost found.

**House style:** the Arjuna Badger contract — propulsive chapters, short scenes, technical texture you
can touch, emotional cost for the twelve, **no wink in the Serpo chapters**. Cross-reference the Serpo
legend map in Project Stargate WORLD.md but **this book confirms on-page** what that line only rhymes.

## 4. Back matter (maximal — binding)

The back matter is **not an appendix**. It is **half the product**. Target: the most detailed McKinnon +
Serpo reference in any novel's closing pages.

### Required sections

1. **Dedication** — to **Gary McKinnon** (full name; the extradition fight; the human-rights outcome).
2. ***What Gary McKinnon Said He Found*** — itemised catalogue:
   - every directory / system name he cited in interviews (e.g. **Johnson Space Center Building 8**,
     **U.S. Space Command**, **Fort Meade** adjacency claims — each sourced to interview or documentary)
   - **Non-Terrestrial Officers** — exact phrasing variants across media
   - **Fleet-to-fleet transfer** logs — what he described vs what sceptics say was misread mundane metadata
   - **Spreadsheet / ship registry** — column headers as he recalled them
   - **Photographic evidence** — the cigar-shaped craft above Earth; resolution / metadata debates
   - **Free-energy motivation** — his stated reason for looking
3. ***The Legal Record*** — UK Crown Prosecution Service; US extradition request; **Office of the Director
   of Public Prosecutions**; **Theresa May** Oct 2012 decision; **Asperger's** / **Autism** in the medical
   reports cited in press; timeline table.
4. ***NASA & DoD Responses*** — public denials; FOIA outcomes; what was **not** released.
5. ***Sceptic & Journalist Rebuttals*** — best-faith mundane readings (misidentified naval logistics,
   admin titles, image artefacts); fairly quoted.
6. ***Project Serpo — Complete Legend Dossier***:
   - Victor Martinez / **UFO Updates** list; 2005 serial posts; "Anonymous" / **Request Anonymous**
   - **Listman**; **Three_Dimension** and poster genealogy where documented
   - The **12** team; **Ebens**; **Serpo** orbital mechanics as claimed; **Ebe**; **Roswell** linkage
   - Payload figures, duration, designators — the numbers that **rhyme** with manifest anomalies (cf.
     Project Stargate ch-06)
   - **Debunked / hoax** arguments — Richard Doty disinformation theories; **no authentication**
7. ***Real / Debated / Invented / Legend*** — four-bin sort for **every load-bearing beat** in both movements.
8. ***Further Reading & Primary Sources*** — interviews (e.g. **RichPlanet**, **Project Camelot**, BBC,
   Guardian legal coverage); **Disclosure Project** appearance; Serpo PDF mirrors; CIA reading room adjacency.

Per-book binding dossier: [`MCKINNON_SERPO_DOSSIERS.md`](MCKINNON_SERPO_DOSSIERS.md) (to be populated at research lock).

## 5. Tone & content bounds

- **Believing prose; honest back matter** — the inversion of *The Why Files* (maybe in story, bins in back).
- **McKinnon is not a punchline.** Autism / Asperger's is **context**, never mockery — the extradition
  medical evidence is documented in the legal dossier section.
- **No US jingoism; no UK bathos.** A man in a flat with a dial-up connection and the largest military
  network on Earth accidentally open.
- **Serpo retelling:** treat the Ebens and the twelve with **full human dignity** — this is *Arrival*
  seriousness, not *Mars Attacks*.
- **Altas Resonance Engine visual DNA optional** on cover — terminal green, spreadsheet grid, two suns reflected
  in a CRT. Not required to match main-saga fractal cover rule.

## 7. Creative test (this book's variant)

> Does this honour Gary McKinnon's **courage to look** — and leave the reader with **more wonder and more
> facts** than they came in with, not less trust?

> Does the page **feel** the size of the world Gary opened — terminal glow, two suns, the knock, the
> integer — without counting words to get there?

If the fiction believes but the back matter lies or thins, the book fails.
If the fiction pads to a metric but the wonder dies, the book fails too.
