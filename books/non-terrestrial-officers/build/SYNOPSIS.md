# SYNOPSIS — *The Non-Terrestrial Officers*

**Slate:** `are-mckinnon` · **Status:** full-send · wonder + line pass · 2026-06-20

From a Crouch End flat on dial-up, Gary McKinnon walks into US military and NASA networks through empty password fields and finds what no court would later let him show: a roster branch coded **NTO — Non-Terrestrial Officers**, fleet-to-fleet transfer logs, Building 8 imagery, and a spreadsheet row that reads **4680 days** — thirteen years — pointing at **Zeta Reticuli**.

This is a **joy-and-wonder book** first: the awe of the empty password field, the slow horror of a roster that compiles, the progress bar at sixty-two percent, two suns on Serpo, the forum speaking an integer aloud in public. Length follows the story; the story does not follow a word target.

Movement I is the hack, the copy, the knock, the extradition decade, and Theresa May's 2012 block.

Movement II believes Serpo: Ebe, twelve Americans, two suns, thirteen years, return, leak.

Gary reads the 2005 forum serial in 2006 and whispers: *They leaked it anyway.*

Ch 99 audits everything.

**Dedicated to Gary McKinnon.**
