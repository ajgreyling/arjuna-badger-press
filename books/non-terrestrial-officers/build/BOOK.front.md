# NULL HORIZON

**Andries J. Greyling**

***

*For Gary McKinnon —*
*who looked when looking was expensive,*
*who told the truth as he saw it,*
*who paid in years and headlines and extradition papers,*
*and who was never required to recant*
*what he copied,*
*because the files were never shown in court.*

*Sawubona.* *I see you.*

***

*This novel believes on the page what Gary McKinnon said he found: Non-Terrestrial Officers, fleet-to-fleet transfers, imagery that should not have been reachable, spreadsheets that compiled like payroll. Chapter 99 believes nothing. Chapter 99 is the audit.*

*Read the fiction. Do the homework. Sort the bins.*
