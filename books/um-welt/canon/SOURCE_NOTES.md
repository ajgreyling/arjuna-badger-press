# SOURCE_NOTES: *Um Welt* (working title)

> **Subject:** Heinz Stücke (11 January 1940 – 22 July 2026), German long-distance touring
> cyclist, Hövelhof, North Rhine-Westphalia. Rode out of his hometown in November 1962 on a
> three-speed steel-framed bicycle and stayed on the road for more than fifty years, covering
> over 600,000 km through 195 countries and 78 territories — the most widely travelled bicycle
> journey on record. Died five days before this book was commissioned (2026-07-27), at home in
> Hövelhof, surrounded by friends, neighbours, and a care team.
>
> **This is a real person's real journey.** Every date, place, injury, arrest, theft, and named
> incident below must be traceable to a source in this file. Interior life, dialogue, weather,
> and sensory texture are the novelist's invention, in service of the record — never against it.
> Author's Note (final chapter, mandatory) states plainly what is documented and what is imagined.

**Note on the title:** No source supports a Stücke book titled literally "Um Welt." His own 1995
self-published memoir is *Mit dem Fahrrad um die Welt* ("By Bicycle Around the World"). The 2015
biography by Eric van den Berg is *Home Is Elsewhere: 50 Years Around the World by Bike*. AJ's
"Um Welt" is being used as **our working title** for this new English-language retelling — a
compression of "um die Welt" (around the world) — not a claim that it reproduces an existing book.
Confirm final title with AJ before publish; do not present "Um Welt" as Stücke's own title on cover
copy.

**Totals (binding numbers, do not round differently across chapters):** 600,000+ km / 378,000+
miles; 195 countries; 78 territories; over 100,000 photographs; five bicycle thefts across the
journey (Siberia 1997 and Portsmouth 2006 are the two we can date and place — treat the other three
as "elsewhere in the fifty years," not invented locations).

## What is NOT documented (do not invent as fact — may invent as scene colour only if flagged in Author's Note)

  by friends, neighbours, and a dedicated care team"

## Funding model (binding, use as recurring texture not one-off exposition)
Licensing fees on his 100,000-photo catalogue; postcard and booklet sales made directly from the
bike at roadside; donations from people he met; no sponsor, no employer, no fixed income.

## Craft note — why this book, why now
Stücke died five days before this commission. The book is being written into a still-open wound,
not a settled legacy. That should be let into the Author's Note honestly — not into the narrative
voice, which stays in Stücke's decades on the road, not in mourning-mode. See STYLE_GUIDE.md.
