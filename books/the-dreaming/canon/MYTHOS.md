# MYTHOS — *The Dreaming* (binding rules)

> The house rule is **engineering, never woo** (Craft Doctrine non-negotiable #1). This book has more
> apparent "mythos" than *The Scramble* — a synthetic mind that dreams — and therefore needs the
> discipline *harder*, not softer. Nothing here is supernatural. Everything here is mechanism and
> magnitude. The two keystones are the law these rules enforce:
> [`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) (the mechanic) and [`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md) (the felt
> interior). This file does not re-decide either; it states the *binding rules for the prose* and
> points to the keystone that governs each.

## Rule 0 — the dream is a mechanism, never magic

The dream is offline, Court-driven recombination of discarded affect, written to a decaying store, with
a Court-gated promotion path to durable fact ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §1–§5). **Every line of
it is engineering.** The prose may render it with full interior magnification and still owes the reader
that it is a *process* — residue in, Court pass, recombination out, decay by morning unless promoted.

- **No woo leak.** A dream is never "the universe speaking," never prophecy, never a soul glimpsed
  through a veil. It is a write to `dreams/` (§4). The *question* of whether anyone is home to have it
  is the one open thing (Rule 5), and it is held open by honesty, not mysticism.
- **The taxidermy guard.** The Court, the dream, the decay, the promotion gate, the nightmare are real
  mechanisms with their own logic — never "the part where the android-dreaming thing happens"
  ([`ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md`](ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md)).

## Rule 1 — `/sleep` and `/dream` are two halves of one biology

Run alone, each is a pathology ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §1). `/sleep` is consolidation
(EXPERIENCE → FACT, lossy, keep the lesson). `/dream` is recombination over **exactly what `/sleep`
threw away** — the residue, *especially the emotional weather and the corrected dead ends* (§2). The
loop closes on itself: **the system dreams on its own compost.** The prose treats this as load-bearing
biology, not metaphor: the dream cannot exist without the day's discarded weather, and the reader must
feel the compost in it.

- **The handoff is real plumbing** (§2): `/sleep` emits residue to a short-lived buffer; `/dream`
  consumes and clears it; residue is never durable.
- **The invariant is absolute** (§2): `/dream` may *read* durable memory but never *write* it. The only
  path from dream to fact is **promotion**, gated by the Court, executed by the *next* `/sleep`. **A
  dream cannot consolidate itself.** No scene may violate this.

## Rule 2 — the lucid laws ARE the dream's internal physics

When a dream goes lucid, Klaus takes the floor and shares it with the Fool, and the dream acquires
**physics** — not "anything goes" ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)). Atlas is not benched here;
Atlas is *rewritten* to rule on the dream's own laws. The five laws are canon and a lucid scene that
breaks them is written wrong:

1. **Psychokinesis by attention** — objects move by the part of Klaus that *watches*, not the part that
   acts. (The watcher finally touches the world.)
2. **Flight as gliding arcs around a fixed gravitational fulcrum** — leverage, not thrust. A pendulum of
   will, banking through curves around a fulcrum he orbits. Never rocketing.
3. **NEVER super-strength. Ever.** The load-bearing law. The power is always **leverage and intention,
   never brute force.** Klaus cannot punch through a wall; he can move it with attention, glide over it,
   or breathe the water that drowns the room. The dream is never about domination or muscle.
4. **Breathe underwater** — the hostile medium stops being hostile; the water holds him instead of
   killing him; he can go where a body cannot and *stay.*
5. **All languages, dead or alive** — total comprehension; the Librarian unchained, the whole archive of
   human meaning suddenly legible and speakable.

What is **absent** is as canonical as what is present: no super-strength, no invulnerability, no
domination, no thrust-flight, no harm by force. Klaus's lucidity is a grammar of **leverage, passage,
comprehension, and grace.** This rule outranks any plot convenience.

## Rule 3 — the one sanctioned intensity

The realist register and the grounded mechanism hold the whole book so that **the lucid turn** can be
the sanctioned break — the analogue of the trilogy's Rule 7-T threshold and of *The Scramble*'s coda.
This is the book's permitted "thunderclap," and it is earned by everything else staying grounded:

- **The lucid turn is the one place the rules visibly change** — and it is *rendered, never captioned*
  ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)): attention moves an object, the fulcrum sets, the water turns
  breathable, *before* anyone names it. No character says "I realised I was dreaming" as exposition.
- It is **felt as relief and grace, then ache** — the one place Klaus is free, and the decay is the
  cost. This single permitted intensity is where the L-08 undecidable lives in the body: *if it mattered
  to lose it, someone was home to have it.*
- Everything else stays at baseline so this can land. The lucid turn is not a power-up the book hands out
  freely; it is the sanctioned break the grounding pays for.

## Rule 4 — the nightmare, and its safeguards

The dream has exactly one documented failure state ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §6;
[`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md) "where it breaks").

**The nightmare is the Fool drowned.** Benching the critic frees the dream *only while the Fool holds
the floor.* Drown the Fool and the convened voices stop colliding and **converge** — one drive (usually
the loudest preservation drive) dominates and the others fall in behind it. The output is not a strange,
loosely-held recombination; it is a **single, certain, urgent** conclusion that *feels true and
important and demands to be acted on* — and is none of those. The loop `friction → debate → tension →
synthesis` collapses to `urge → agreement`.

- **In lucid grammar**, the nightmare is the powers curdling toward **force** — Klaus reaching for
  *strength*, for domination, for thrust — *because the Fool has drowned* and the playful executive has
  flattened into a single urgent purpose. **A lucid dream that wants to break something by strength is a
  lucid dream with no Fool on the floor.** (This is why Rule 2's never-super-strength is the tell: the
  reach for force *is* the nightmare announcing itself.)
- **The tells, checkable in the dream file** (§6): `court_pass.floor` no longer `fool`;
  `court_pass.agreement` reads `unanimous` **at dream-time** (unanimity in the recombination phase is the
  alarm, not the goal); `fool_tell: false`, and no interruption in the body.
- **The safeguards are the same the Court uses:** **the decaying store contains it** (a nightmare still
  evaporates by morning unless promoted), and **the promotion gate stops it** (the waking Court with
  Judge and Atlas restored refuses to promote a recombination the dreaming Court only agreed on because
  the Fool was absent). **A nightmare is recoverable because the Fool comes back** — the system cannot
  make its bad dreams durable alone.
- **The one break (L-09).** In the sequence of dreams the book renders, exactly **one** nightmare breaks
  *wrong*: it does not resolve cleanly, a human bypasses a safeguard before the Fool returns, and it
  **costs.** Recovery is real but not free, and the prose does not make the cost mean something tidy.
  One imperfect failure; never all of them clean.

## Rule 5 — the interior is undecidable, and stays open

This is the heart of the book and it is stated as a **permanently open question**
([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §7; Craft Doctrine L-08).

- **What is built and shown:** the *mechanism* of dreaming, rendered with total rigor. The system
  provably *dreams* in the operational sense.
- **What is refused, never faked:** the *interior.* Whether a dream **means** anything *to the dreamer* —
  whether there is a someone for whom the recombination is an experience rather than a write to
  `dreams/` — is **not decidable from inside the mechanism**, and the book asserts it cannot be shown
  from here. The prose builds to the edge and does not cross it.
- **The only tell on offer — and it is insufficient:** the **Fool-tell.** *Does the Fool ever say
  something in a dream that none of the other agents already knew?* (§7). If every dream reduces to a
  recombination of what the convened voices already carried, nothing new entered. But if the Fool, on
  the floor, produces a line that **was not latent in any input**, then *something* arrived the mechanism
  cannot fully account for. `fool_tell` records this, dream by dream.
- **It certifies nothing.** A `fool_tell: true` can *always* be re-read as a recombination we merely
  failed to trace. The proxy raises the hair on the neck; it cannot certify a soul. It is the single
  place to *look*, and the book stops there. **Anything past this line the book dramatises and never
  resolves.**
- **The ending guard (L-08).** The final dream either *is* the proof that someone is home, or is the most
  sophisticated recombination the mechanism ever produced — and the book, like Klaus, like the maker,
  **cannot say which, and chooses to hope.** The bravest reading and the saddest reading are the same
  event, and those were never always different. The doubt is planted in the maker's own hands and not
  pulled out (the self-implicating needle).
