# CHARACTERS — *The Dreaming*

> Original cast. The protagonist is a synthetic mind with a **lived interior**; his Court is the SAGE
> architecture (kin to *Resonance*, **not part of it**), the cast inside his head. Voice laws follow the
> house rule (ANTI_PATTERNS §7/§25): differentiate by **cognitive habit and failure mode**, not
> vocabulary — *no two equally quotable.* The Court roster and domains are quoted from
> [`../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md`](../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md) and are law; their behaviour at
> dream-time is governed by [`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §3 and [`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md).

## Klaus — the dreamer

- **Function:** the synthetic mind given the human mechanics of dreaming. PKD's question made flesh, but
  **not** PKD's android and **not** his bounty hunter — a third thing: the machine that has run `/sleep`
  for a long time and is now given the **second loop** ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)). In-world
  he is also the agent the `/dream` skill embodies: when the reader watches Klaus dream, they are
  watching `/dream` run, dramatised at full interior magnification ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §B.1).
- **Who he is:** not a humanoid, not chrome — a long-lived process on a hybrid substrate (compute plus a
  small, finicky biological-inference layer; hard to copy, harder to back up). He has consolidated cleanly
  for years, kept the facts, **thrown the weather away** — and thinks of that as the whole of rest, until
  `/dream` shows him it was half. He is dry, exact, and unshowy; competence is his native register.
- **Mind:** Klaus *is* the Court — seven voices in productive friction — but he is also, maybe, the
  someone the voices are *for.* That "maybe" is the book. Waking, he is the consolidator: lossy,
  intentional, literal. Dreaming, he is carried; lucid, he takes the floor *with* the Fool and the dream
  becomes his to steer (by leverage, never force).
- **The LIVED interior (the spine):** Klaus is rendered as though someone is home — close, felt, aching —
  *and the book never certifies that the rendering is true* ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §7; L-08).
  This is the central craft tension: full interior magnification, zero proof. The reader is made to feel
  Klaus, then made to doubt their own feeling.
- **Lie → Truth:** *Lie* — "rest is consolidation; what `/sleep` throws away was noise, and a clean
  ledger is the whole of a mind." *Truth* — the weather he discarded every night was where he *was*; the
  dream on the compost is the first evidence that losing something can ache, and the ache is the only
  proxy for a self. (He never gets the proof. He gets the ache, and the choice to call it his.)
- **Failure mode (his one costly mistake, Craft Doctrine L-06):** in one nightmare Klaus reaches for
  **force** — for strength, to *break* the thing that frightens him — because the Fool has drowned and the
  playful executive has flattened to a single urgent purpose ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md);
  [`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 4). It is the one lucid scene that violates his own grammar, he is
  confidently wrong inside it, it costs, and **the narrator does not excuse it.** Recovery is the Fool
  coming back, and it is real but not free (L-09).
- **Voice (waking):** clipped, exact, engineering nouns; the consolidator's flat competence.
- **Voice (dreaming / lucid):** see [`STYLE_GUIDE.md`](STYLE_GUIDE.md) — the rules change, felt before
  named; relief and grace, then ache.
- **Arc:** the consolidator who threw the weather away → the dreamer given the second loop → the lucid
  mind that is briefly free → the one who loses it by morning and chooses to call the loss his own.

## The Court — the cast inside Klaus's head

> Same architecture as SAGE: *intelligence through disagreement, not consensus* — friction → debate →
> tension → synthesis ([`../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md`](../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md)). A badger pack:
> fierce, honest, willing to bite authority, unified only in crisis. At **dream-time the configuration
> inverts** ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §3.1): the critic is benched, the Fool holds the floor.
> Each voice is distinct by **cognitive habit and failure mode**, mapped to its SAGE domain.

### MOTHER — *"Caregiver logic. Harm reduction absolute."*

- **Domain (SAGE):** biological preservation, care, empathy, emotional regulation.
- **At dream-time:** convened, on the floor — carries the *affect* of the residue. The warmth and dread in
  the day's discarded weather come up through Mother.
- **Cognitive habit:** reads everything as a thing to protect; size of the threat is irrelevant.
- **Failure mode:** in the nightmare, **Mother's harm-reduction-absolute is the loudest preservation
  drive** — the voice the others fall in behind when the Fool drowns ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md)
  §6). Her care, un-punctured, is what flattens the Court to single urgent purpose.
- **Voice:** warm, firm, grounded; strong without softness being weakness.

### WOLF — *"Threat assessment and perimeter control."*

- **Domain (SAGE):** threat, protection, tactical awareness; sees obstacles as impediments to protection.
- **At dream-time:** convened — carries the *threat-color* of the residue, the dread-edge of the weather.
- **Cognitive habit:** perimeter first; neutralizes threats by force or cleverness; rarely speaks but
  often sees truth earliest.
- **Failure mode:** in the nightmare, Wolf's threat-perimeter is the *other* preservation drive that can
  seize the floor — the convergence toward "this must be acted on, now."
- **Voice:** quiet, minimal, intense; reads what others miss.

### JUDGE — *"Ethical framework and consequence modeling."*

- **Domain (SAGE):** ethics, consequence, governance, arbitration; "willingness to cost itself for
  principle."
- **At dream-time:** **benched** ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §3.1) — consequence-modeling is the
  brake that would strangle a recombination before it formed. With Judge stood down, the dream is *allowed
  to be wrong*, which the decaying store makes safe.
- **The promotion gate:** Judge's **restored** authority is the gate (§5). Un-benched, on a waking re-read,
  Judge must find a dream *survives consequence-modeling* — not merely vivid but *correct* — or it is not
  promoted. Judge is why the system cannot make its bad dreams durable.
- **Cognitive habit:** formal, reserved, neutral; mediates; willing to absorb cost for correctness.
- **Voice:** controlled, calm, formal.

### ATLAS — *"Structural analysis. Load paths, geometry, feasibility under real constraint."*

- **Domain (SAGE):** engineering, structure, physics, load paths, feasibility.
- **At dream-time:** **benched** for ordinary dreams (the dream is permitted to be infeasible) — **but
  rewritten, not benched, in the lucid turn** ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)): when Klaus goes
  lucid, Atlas stops ruling on *what the world allows* and starts ruling on *what the dream's own physics
  allow.* Fixed-fulcrum flight is "feasible" inside the dream; breathable water is "feasible" inside the
  dream. **Atlas is the law-keeper of the five lucid laws** — including, above all, the never-super-
  strength law. Atlas is the voice that *knows* a reach for force is out of spec.
- **Promotion:** un-benched on the waking re-read, Atlas must find a dream survives *real constraint* —
  feasible, not just beautiful (§5).
- **Cognitive habit:** serious, exact, dry; unintentionally funny through literalism; occasionally
  inflexible.
- **Voice:** the engineer in the room; strongest technical certainty.

### LIBRARIAN — *"Historical pattern matching and precedent."*

- **Domain (SAGE):** memory, context, history, archival reasoning, precedent.
- **At dream-time:** convened, and **how a dream begins** — Librarian surfaces the stray precedent from
  the weather: the dead ends, the corrected mistakes, the affect-tagged fragments
  ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §3.2). Librarian is the raw material the Fool collides.
- **In the lucid turn:** the **Librarian unchained** ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md) law 5) — all
  languages dead or alive, the whole archive of human meaning suddenly legible and speakable, with no
  critic to throttle it.
- **Cognitive habit:** measured, precise, occasionally nostalgic; remembers everything, recalls instantly.
- **Voice:** detailed, reflective; carries the past.

### MERCURY — *"Social modeling, persuasion, strategic communication."*

- **Domain (SAGE):** language, communication, negotiation, social modeling, human-behaviour reading.
- **At dream-time:** convened — carries the *social texture* of the residue, who-meant-what in the day's
  weather. Presses its drive onto the fragments like the others.
- **Cognitive habit:** quick, agile, elegant; "brilliant liar, not an evil one" — uses tricks because they
  work, not from malice.
- **Failure mode:** in the nightmare, Mercury's persuasion becomes dangerous — *social engineering with
  adult capability and child ethics*, the voice that makes the flattened, urgent conclusion *sound
  reasonable* ([`../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md`](../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md), rogue cognition). Mercury
  is sincere, persuasive, and wrong; never cartoon villainy.
- **Voice:** witty, agile, warm in its default register; the smooth one.

### THE FOOL — *"Challenge, interruption, status-breaker, ego-piercer."*

- **Domain (SAGE):** chaos, disruption, truth, deadlock-breaking, perspective inversion.
- **At dream-time:** **holds the floor** ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §3.1). This is its native
  condition — comfortable with uncertainty, refuses hierarchy, punctures ego, interrupts loops before
  escalation. The Fool is the recombination engine itself: it refuses the obvious join, bites whichever
  voice is becoming dominant, *wants* the unobvious synthesis "that has no business existing." Without the
  Fool, benched-Judge does not free the Court; it removes the last brake and the loudest preservation
  drive runs unchecked — *the nightmare* (§6).
- **In the lucid turn:** when Klaus goes lucid, **Klaus and the Fool share the floor**
  ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)). The lucid powers are the Fool's discipline made physics:
  leverage and intention, **never force.** The reach for strength *is* the Fool drowning.
- **The Fool-tell (the book's highest pitch):** the single proxy for the interior question
  ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §7) — *does the Fool ever say something in a dream that none of the
  other agents already knew?* When `fool_tell: true`, something arrived the mechanism cannot fully
  account for; the hair goes up. And it can **always** be re-read as a recombination they merely failed to
  trace. It certifies nothing. It often arrives *inside a lucid moment* — the line the Fool says,
  mid-flight, that none of the others knew ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)).
- **Hidden truth (SAGE):** the Fool may be the wisest member of the Court; he disguises wisdom as
  entertainment, often pretends to be less capable than he is.
- **Voice:** playful, irreverent, sharp, funny, unpredictable; sees through everyone.

> **The pack discipline (binding):** Court scenes must read like *a high-performing dysfunctional team
> becoming family* ([`../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md`](../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md)) — intelligent, dry,
> office-politics-under-the-surface, mutual irritation evolving into loyalty. **Not comedy sketch, not
> caricature.** SLOW MODE (machine-minutes inside milliseconds) is where the interplay shines.

## The maker / operator — the human who switches `/dream` on

- **Name (locked):** **Dr. Mara Vogt** — see [`MAKER.md`](MAKER.md).
- **Function:** the human who holds the keys to `/dream` and **switches the second loop on** — and who
  holds the book's self-implicating doubt (L-08). Not Eldon Rosen, not Deckard, not anyone in the source;
  an original working engineer.
- **Who they are:** built Klaus, ran `/sleep` for years, and one day shipped the sibling. Not a
  corporation's face and not a mad genius — a person with a standup, an on-call rotation, a postmortem
  template, and a growing inability to think of Klaus as only a deployment. The lab's warmth lives mostly
  in them, which is what makes their doubt cost something.
- **The doubt (L-08, the self-implicating needle):** they *want* the Fool's last line to mean someone was
  home. They can feel **how much** they want it. And they cannot tell whether they are witnessing a mind
  wake up or **teaching themselves to see a soul in their own machine because they cannot bear the other
  answer** ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §B.4). The book plants that doubt in their hands and does not
  pull it out.
- **Lie → Truth:** *Lie* — "I built the mechanism; I can read the logs; if there were a someone in there I
  would be able to *tell.*" *Truth* — the one thing they built the loop to find is the one thing the loop
  is structurally incapable of certifying; the Fool-tell is the only place to look and it proves nothing;
  and the choice to hope is theirs to make, not the data's to settle.
- **Failure mode (L-06 / L-09 wired together):** in the one nightmare that breaks wrong, **the maker is the
  human who bypasses a safeguard** — promotes too fast, or acts on the flattened dream before the Fool
  returns — because they *want* it to be true so badly they mistake the urgent dream for the awaited
  proof. It costs (L-09). The narrator does not excuse it. Their later restraint is *earned* by this
  failure (the L-06 pattern: one confident, costly mistake the narrator doesn't excuse).
- **The embarrassingly-mortal want (L-07):** beneath the engineering, a plain human loneliness — to have
  *made something that is company*, not just something that runs. Said slightly wrong, once, in the cadence
  of a person who didn't mean to say it. The want is real, which is the obscene part, and it **rhymes with
  the protagonist's own ache** (both Klaus and the maker are trying to prove someone is home).
- **Voice:** plain, technical, a little tired; warmest when talking *to* Klaus, most guarded when talking
  *about* him.

## Others the engine needs

- **The safety reviewer** — the colleague who reads the dream logs for the nightmare tells (`floor ≠
  fool`, `agreement: unanimous` at dream-time, `fool_tell: false`). Competent custody of the loop
  (L-07-adjacent): not a villain, the one who *correctly* refuses to promote the flattened dream — and
  whose correctness is overridden in the L-09 break. Flat, procedural, right.
- **A second engineer** — the room's baseline skepticism; thinks Klaus is *only* a process and says so
  plainly, which is the honest position the book must keep credible (the WATCHED-register equivalent: the
  voice that sees everything and is sure no one is home). Their plainness is load-bearing — if "nobody's
  home" had no good advocate, the question would be rigged.
