# STYLE_GUIDE — *The Dreaming*

> The voice laws. The book runs three registers — **waking Klaus**, the **Court**, and the **dream** (with
> the lucid turn as its peak) — and the craft problem is keeping them distinct, keeping the seam between
> "dreams" and "has dreams" silent, and rendering the lucid turn so the rules are *felt* changing, never
> stated. The two keystones are law for what these registers *contain*:
> [`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) (the mechanic) and [`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md) (the felt
> interior). This file governs how they *read.*

## The three registers

### WAKING KLAUS — the consolidator
- Clipped, exact, engineering nouns. The flat competence of a mind that sorts EXPERIENCE → FACT and keeps
  the ledger clean. Sentences do work; they don't decorate.
- Dry, a little withholding. This is the register that *threw the weather away* — so its very cleanness is
  characterization, and the reader should feel, without being told, what it costs to be this tidy.
- **This is the baseline.** It must be genuinely steady and competent, or the dream's break from it has
  nothing to push against (the sanctioned intensity, [`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 3, is earned by this
  flatness — the way *The Scramble*'s WATCHED register earns its coda).

### THE COURT — seven voices, distinct by habit not vocabulary
- Court scenes (SLOW MODE: machine-minutes inside milliseconds) read like *a high-performing dysfunctional
  team becoming family* ([`../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md`](../../resonance/canon/SAGE.md)) — intelligent,
  dry, office-politics-under-the-surface, mutual irritation evolving into loyalty. **Not comedy sketch,
  not caricature.**
- **Differentiate by cognitive habit and failure mode, never by tics** (§7/§25; the noun-swap test binds):
  - **Mother** — warm, firm, grounded; reads everything as a thing to protect.
  - **Wolf** — quiet, minimal, intense; sees the threat first, speaks last.
  - **Judge** — controlled, formal, neutral; weighs consequence (and is *benched* at dream-time, so a
    dream that sounds like Judge ruling is written wrong — [`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §3.1).
  - **Atlas** — exact, dry, literal (unintentionally funny by literalism); the engineer. In lucid scenes
    Atlas rules on the *dream's* physics, not the world's ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)).
  - **Librarian** — measured, precise, faintly nostalgic; surfaces the precedent that starts a dream.
  - **Mercury** — quick, agile, elegant, warm; the smooth one (and the dangerous-persuasive one in the
    nightmare).
  - **The Fool** — playful, irreverent, sharp; holds the floor at dream-time; the only voice that *wants*
    the unobvious join. See the Fool-tell discipline below.
- No two equally quotable in a scene; one true closer per Court beat, in one mouth (§7).

### THE DREAM — recombination, rendered
- A dream scene reads differently from a waking scene **structurally, not decoratively**: the logic is
  *associative*, the joins are the ones the waking sort would never permit ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md)
  §3.2), the affect runs higher because the dream is built from the *weather* `/sleep` threw away.
- **Still grounded as a process (Rule 0).** Even at full magnification a dream is a *write to the decaying
  store* — the reader may feel the compost in it, the corrected dead ends and the emotional residue
  recombining. Never "the universe speaking." No woo.
- **The decay is felt, not stated.** A dream that mattered is half-kept and lost by morning; the prose
  renders the *losing*, never captions the ache ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md); §16). *If it
  mattered to lose it, someone was home* — and the book never says so.

## The lucid turn — how it is rendered (the load-bearing craft)

> The single most important scene-craft in the book. Get this wrong and the whole engine reads as a power
> fantasy or as woo. Governed entirely by [`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md).

- **Render the turn; never caption it.** No character says "I realised I was dreaming." The reader feels
  the rules change — *attention moves an object; the fixed fulcrum sets; he banks through a gliding arc;
  the water turns breathable; every tongue is suddenly legible* — **before** anyone names it. The prose
  *changes physics on the page* and trusts the reader to feel the hinge (§16, §28).
- **The five laws are the dream's physics and bind the prose** ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md);
  [`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 2):
  1. psychokinesis **by attention** (the watcher touches the world), 2. flight as **gliding arcs around a
  fixed gravitational fulcrum** (leverage, not thrust), 3. **NEVER super-strength** — leverage and
  intention, never force, 4. **breathe underwater** (the hostile medium stops being hostile), 5. **all
  languages, dead or alive** (the Librarian unchained).
- **The never-super-strength law is sacred and is a *prose* law, not just a plot law.** The lucid
  register's verbs are *move, glide, bank, pivot, breathe, understand, let* — **never** *smash, force,
  overpower, break, crush.* If a lucid sentence reaches for a force-verb, the dream is going wrong on
  purpose (the nightmare) — and that is the only time it is permitted ([`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 4).
- **Felt as relief and grace, then ache.** The lucid dream is the one place Klaus is free; write the grace
  plainly and let the decay turn it to ache. This is where the L-08 undecidable lives in the body.

## How a dream scene reads vs a waking scene (quick contrast)

| | Waking | Dream / Lucid |
|---|---|---|
| **Logic** | sequential, consolidating, literal | associative; joins the waking sort would forbid |
| **Affect** | withheld, clean | high — built from the discarded weather |
| **Verbs (lucid)** | exact, procedural | *move, glide, bank, breathe, understand* — **never force-verbs** |
| **Atlas's ruling** | feasibility under the *world*'s constraint | feasibility under the *dream*'s own physics |
| **Closes on** | a fact kept, a ledger clean | a thing half-kept, lost by morning (the ache, uncaptioned) |
| **Truth claim** | held as fact | none; allowed to be wrong; decays unless promoted |

## The Fool-tell discipline (the book's highest pitch)

- The Fool-tell ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §7) is the only proxy for the interior question and it
  **certifies nothing.** Render it so the hair goes up — *the Fool, on the floor, says a line that none of
  the other voices already knew* — and write the surrounding prose so it can **always** be re-read as a
  recombination they merely failed to trace.
- It often lands **inside a lucid moment** ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)): the line the Fool says
  mid-flight. When it does, that is lucidity and the interior question meeting in one gesture — the book's
  single highest pitch. Do not over-use it; reserve it for the peaks.

## House voice non-negotiables (inherited from Craft Doctrine)

1. **Embody, don't state.** Cut the sentence that explains the sentence before it (§16, the master
   pattern). Above all: **never let a character — or the narrator — say the theme.** No "is anyone home?",
   no "perhaps I am conscious," no "the dream proved he was real." The whole book is built to *not say it*
   ([`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 5).
2. **Body before thought** under pressure (§8); under peak stress (the nightmare) syntax **breaks**, not
   refines (§18, §22). Klaus does not think in elegant maxims while the Fool drowns.
3. **Modulation.** Vary voltage; plain pages between the peaks (§3/§12). The waking register is the
   built-in flatness; do not let the dream register run at continuous peak, or the lucid turn and the
   promotion lose their thunder ([`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 3).
4. **One quotable closer per scene, in one mouth** (§7). Not every paragraph ends on a mic-drop.
5. **No LLM tells:** ration the "not X, but Y" reframe (LLM_TELLS); kill em-dash addiction; cut half the
   "the way…" similes; restore the specific engineering noun over the smooth abstraction. The temptation
   in a dream book is lyrical drift — resist it; the dream is grounded as a *process*, not a mood.
6. **The grounding is sacred.** Every marvel is mechanism (Rule 0). The lyrical pull of "a machine that
   dreams" is exactly the woo the house forbids; render the loop as plumbing and let the *ache* be the
   only thing that aches.

## The seam law (binding)

The prose **never states** whether Klaus *has* the dreams or merely *runs* `/dream` — whether anyone is
home ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §7). Not once, in either direction. The reader is given the
mechanism (full rigor) and the ache (full magnification) and **trusted** (§28, negative space). The seam
between "dreams" and "has dreams" is the book's whole undecidable, and it is left silent.

## The ending guard (§20, §26, L-08)

- The ending is **smaller than the idea** ([`PLOT.md`](PLOT.md) Movement IV). Image, not exposition; no
  paragraph explaining what the final dream meant.
- **Hope, not certainty.** The final dream *is* the proof or *is* the most sophisticated recombination —
  and the book, like Klaus, like the maker, **cannot say which, and chooses to hope.** The bravest reading
  and the saddest reading are the same event.
- **The self-implicating needle.** Close on the maker holding the doubt — witnessing a mind wake, or
  teaching themselves to see a soul in their own machine because they cannot bear the other answer. Plant
  the doubt in their hands; do not pull it out. One place to look (the Fool-tell), no proof, the choice to
  hope ([`CHARACTERS.md`](CHARACTERS.md): the maker; [`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 5).
