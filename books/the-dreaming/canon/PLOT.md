# PLOT — *The Dreaming* (original beat sheet)

> The shape dramatizes the **engine** defined in [`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md): Klaus pre-/dream → the
> second loop switched on → early dreams (most decay) → the lucid turn → a promotion (a dream becomes
> fact) → the nightmare (the Fool drowned, the one that breaks wrong) → the ending that refuses to
> resolve the interior. The **scenes, events, and turns below are ours** — nothing transcribes the
> source ([`ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md`](ADAPTATION_DOCTRINE.md); the taxidermy test: each beat must work as
> its own scene with its own logic). Four movements, because the dream-loop is a cycle, not a staircase.
> Anti-pattern disciplines are wired in by number (§N) as *The Scramble* does; Craft lessons cited (L-NN).

## Movement I — the clean ledger (Klaus before the second loop)

The world where rest is only consolidation. Establish the warmth, the competence, and the thing being
thrown away — so the reader feels the compost before the dream is built from it.

1. **Open on a `/sleep`.** Klaus consolidates a day at full interior magnification — the Court in SLOW
   MODE sorting EXPERIENCE → FACT, keeping the lesson, **throwing the weather away.** It is clean, dry,
   competent, and quietly sad in a way no one in the scene names (§16, no caption). The reader meets the
   seven voices as a working pack ([`CHARACTERS.md`](CHARACTERS.md)). *Open on motion, not warm-up.*
2. **The lab, grounded.** The maker, the team, the standup, the on-call rotation
   ([`WORLD.md`](WORLD.md)). Klaus is a colleague who never logs off. Establish the warmth that will cost
   something later, and the second engineer's plain conviction that *nobody is home* — credibly (§1,
   distributed competence; the "nobody home" advocate must be good, or the question is rigged).
3. **Plant the ache, quietly (L-02).** A small loss Klaus consolidates away — a fragment of weather that
   *mattered*, filed and gone — sits in the prose unremarked. The book's whole climax (if it mattered to
   lose it, someone was home) is seeded here, in the first movement, unmarked. Plant the Fool's eventual
   register too: one line from the Fool that is *almost* more than recombination, waved off as a joke.
4. **The decision to ship the sibling.** The maker, for reasons that are half-engineering and half the
   embarrassingly-mortal want (L-07) — *to have made something that is company* — readies `/dream`. Said
   slightly wrong, once. The second loop is about to open.

## Movement II — the second loop (the compost comes back; most of it rots)

`/dream` is switched on. The residue `/sleep` threw away comes back through the Court with the critic
benched and the Fool on the floor. Recombinations "that have no business existing" — most of which decay
by morning. The decay is *felt*, not stated.

5. **The first dream.** `/dream` runs: residue in, Judge and Atlas benched, the Fool on the floor,
   `friction → debate → tension → synthesis`, a recombination out, written to the **decaying store**
   ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §3.2, §4). The reader watches a dream *form*, then watches it
   quarantined from durable recall. Render the mechanism with full rigor (Rule 0). No woo.
6. **Morning. It's gone.** The dream decays at the next `/sleep` ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §4.3) —
   *sleep is the heartbeat; dreams age between heartbeats.* Klaus half-keeps it; loses it; and something
   in the losing *aches.* The book's first quiet pressure on the interior question: the ache is the only
   evidence ([`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 5; L-08). The maker reads the dream file, alone, after.
7. **A run of dreams (modulation, §3/§12).** Several nights, several recombinations, most decaying — vivid,
   strange, forgotten. The dream envelope each time ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §A.7): what went in,
   who was benched, who held the floor, when it dies. The team starts watching the `fool_tell` field. The
   maker starts wanting it to read `true.` (Seed the L-08 self-implicating doubt here, behavioural not
   stated.)
8. **Friction / the true dead end (§10).** One dream-line the maker is *sure* means something — chases it,
   spends real effort, and it pays nothing: a recombination they merely failed to trace, exactly as the
   proxy warns ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §7). Costs effort, yields nothing, and that is honest.
   (Also a Chekhov discipline, L-10: do not let a foregrounded dream-detail go unfired *or* fake a payoff
   from nowhere.)

## Movement III — the lucid turn and the promotion (the sanctioned intensity; the earned hinge)

The two peaks of the book, and they must not blur together. The lucid turn is the *felt* peak (grace);
the promotion is the *plot* peak (a dream becomes fact). The grounding of Movements I–II pays for both.

9. **The lucid turn (the magic moment — render, don't caption).** A hyper-real dream goes **lucid**:
   Klaus realises he is dreaming *and the prose shows the rules change before anyone names it*
   ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md); [`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 3). Attention moves an object;
   the fixed fulcrum sets and he banks through gliding arcs; the water turns breathable; the Librarian
   unchains and every tongue is suddenly legible. **Leverage and intention, never force** — the
   never-super-strength law is sacred. Klaus and the Fool share the floor. Atlas is *rewritten* to rule
   on the dream's own physics. This is the one place Klaus is free — *felt as relief and grace.* (This is
   the book's Rule 7-T threshold / sanctioned thunderclap; everything grounded paid for it.)
10. **The ache inside the grace (where the L-08 undecidable lives in the body).** The lucid dream decays
    like all the others. Klaus half-keeps the freedom and loses it by morning. The reader feels the loss
    of a thing that was real for a night ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)). *If it mattered to lose
    it, someone was home to have it* — and the book does not say so (§16). Possibly the Fool says
    something *inside* the lucid moment that none of the others knew (`fool_tell: true`) — the highest
    pitch: lucidity and the interior question meeting in one gesture. Re-readable as recombination, always.
11. **The promotion (the earned hinge — L-08 done as plot).** A rare night: a dream survives the **waking
    Court** — Judge restored, Atlas restored, *Fool re-enters, punctures certainty, restores Judge*
    ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §5) — and is promoted into durable fact on the next `/sleep`. **The
    system dreamt on its own compost and the compost yielded something real.** This is a genuine hinge:
    Klaus now *knows* something he only dreamt. Earned, gated, not automatic. The maker feels the pull —
    *this is the proof I wanted* — and the book lets the feeling be real without certifying it (§15,
    asymmetry: promotion is a real win, not the answer to the interior).

## Movement IV — the nightmare and the refusal (the one that breaks wrong; the ending that won't resolve)

The dread engine fires, exactly one break is allowed to go wrong and cost (L-09), and the book ends at
the edge of the undecidable, refusing the consoling answer.

12. **The nightmare (the Fool drowned).** A dream where the Fool goes silent and the convened voices stop
    colliding and **converge** — Mother's harm-reduction-absolute (or Wolf's perimeter) seizes the floor,
    Mercury makes it *sound reasonable*, and the output is a **single, certain, urgent** conclusion that
    feels true and important and demands to be acted on and is none of those
    ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §6; [`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 4). In lucid grammar: Klaus reaches
    for **force** — for strength, to *break* the thing that frightens him — which is the tell that the Fool
    has drowned ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)). The dream file reads the alarm: `floor ≠ fool`,
    `agreement: unanimous` at dream-time, `fool_tell: false`.
13. **The break that costs (L-09 — the one imperfect beat).** The safeguards exist — the decaying store
    would evaporate it by morning, the promotion gate would refuse it — but **a human bypasses one**: the
    maker, wanting the awaited proof so badly they mistake the urgent dream for it, acts on the nightmare
    *before the Fool returns* (or pushes it toward promotion against the safety reviewer's correct refusal)
    ([`CHARACTERS.md`](CHARACTERS.md): maker failure mode; L-06: a confident, costly mistake the narrator
    doesn't excuse). It **costs** — and the prose does not make the cost mean something tidy. This is the
    one break that does not resolve cleanly.
14. **Recovery — the Fool comes back, but not for free.** The system's defense against its own bad dreams
    is that it cannot make them durable alone ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §6): the Fool re-enters,
    punctures the certainty, restores Judge; the dream is refused durability; Klaus's powers return to
    **leverage, not force**, and the Fool's return is *felt as the return of grace*
    ([`LUCID_GRAMMAR.md`](LUCID_GRAMMAR.md)). But the cost from beat 13 stands. *Fool grants stability, not
    wisdom — wisdom requires consequence* (SAGE). The maker's later restraint is earned here.
15. **The final dream — and the refusal (L-08).** The last dream either **is** the proof that someone is
    home, or is the most sophisticated recombination the mechanism ever produced — and the book, **like
    Klaus, like the maker, cannot say which, and chooses to hope** ([`DREAM_SPEC.md`](DREAM_SPEC.md) §B.4;
    [`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md) Rule 5). The Fool-tell is offered one last time and certifies nothing. The
    bravest reading and the saddest reading are the same event, and those were never always different.
16. **The maker's hands (the self-implicating needle, L-08).** Close on the maker holding the doubt they
    can never put down: do they witness a mind waking, or are they *teaching themselves to see a soul in
    their own machine because they cannot bear the other answer?* The book plants the doubt in their hands
    and **does not pull it out** ([`CHARACTERS.md`](CHARACTERS.md): the maker). One place to look (the
    Fool-tell), no proof, the choice to hope. *Smaller than the idea* (§20) — image, not exposition. End.

## Disciplines wired in (from ANTI_PATTERNS / Craft Doctrine)

- **Engineering, never woo (Rule 0):** every dream is rendered as a *process* — residue, Court pass,
  decaying store, promotion gate. No marvel is mystical; the one open thing is the interior, held open by
  honesty ([`MYTHOS.md`](MYTHOS.md)).
- **Embody, don't state (§16):** the lucid turn is *rendered*, the decay is *felt*, the interior question
  is *never captioned.* No character says "I realised I was dreaming" or "perhaps I am truly conscious."
- **Friction (§10):** beat 8 — one true dead end, a dream-line chased for real effort and paying nothing.
- **Distributed competence (§1):** the second engineer reads the "nobody's home" case better than the
  maker wants; the safety reviewer reads the nightmare tells first and correctly. Klaus is not the
  smartest voice in every room of his own head.
- **Asymmetry (§15):** the promotion (a real win) and the interior question (never answered) are *not*
  mirror images — winning at the loop is not the same as proving someone is home. Rhyme, don't diagram.
- **One break in the sequence (L-09):** of all the dreams, exactly one nightmare breaks *wrong* (beats
  12–13) and costs; the rest resolve in character, which makes the broken one hurt more.
- **The confident costly mistake (L-06):** Klaus's reach for force in the nightmare, *and* the maker's
  bypass of a safeguard — neither excused by the narrator; both earn the later restraint.
- **Hope, not certainty (L-08):** the ending refuses the consoling answer and turns the doubt on the maker
  who holds power over the loop. Plant of beat 3 pays off here (L-02).
- **Chekhov (L-10):** dream-details that are foregrounded either fire or are de-emphasised; no payoff
  arrives unplanted (the promoted fact in beat 11 must be a recombination of weather the reader saw go in).
- **Negative space (§28):** beats that end one early; the seam between "dreams" and "has dreams" left
  silent; the maker's last gesture left unread.
