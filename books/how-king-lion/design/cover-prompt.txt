================================================================================
ART SPEC — King Lion and the Birds Who Stole the Sky  (slug: kinglion)
Children's Library · ages 4–8 · warm painterly storybook tradition
Landscape 3:2, full-bleed, NO TEXT in image. Keep characters consistent across spreads.
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE (apply to EVERY image — the house look + recurring characters)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Soft painterly gouache-and-watercolour storybook illustration in the warm classic
read-aloud tradition: visible brush texture, glowing golden-hour African light, big
generous skies, gentle edges, rich but never garish colour. Setting is the southern
African veld and small farmland — open grassland, tall ripe maize, marula and acacia
trees, low rocky outcrops, round thatched homes and a kraal fence in the middle
distance. Dignified and specific; never generic "jungle", never caricature.

RECURRING CHARACTERS (keep identical across all spreads):
• THE BIRDS — medium birds, brilliant and proud: a flame-orange crested crown, a
  deep cobalt-blue throat and breast, slender dark beak, long elegant neck. They must
  be drawn so their crest + throat visibly become the orange-and-blue form of a
  bird-of-paradise / strelitzia flower at the reveal.
• KING LION — an old, heavy, dignified lion with a warm tawny coat and a deep amber
  mane going silver; slow kind eyes, grave not fierce; a tired just king.
• {{CHILD}} (the watching child-hero) — a small brown-skinned African child, about
  6, barefoot, simple warm-coloured clothes, soft curious face. Small in frame.
• THE LAND — duiker, scrub hare, tortoise (the innocent small animals); tired farmer
  villagers of the veld, working clothes, dignified.

MOOD ARC: bright/joyful → busy/greedy → fearful/dim → grave → tender-sad reveal →
quiet warm close. Sorrow rendered gently — sad, never frightening; no gore.

PER-SPREAD PROMPTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

spread-01-kinglion.png
Wide veld at soft dawn. Rows of tall golden maize in the foreground; round thatched
homes and a kraal fence in the far distance. The small barefoot child {{CHILD}}-figure
stands among the stalks, face tilted up, watching a scatter of bright orange-crested
blue-throated birds wheeling joyfully across an enormous pink-and-gold morning sky.
Happy, spacious, full of air.

spread-02-kinglion.png
Closer painterly portrait: a small flock of the bright birds perched proud and loud
along an acacia branch against a big open sky. Flame-orange crests raised, cobalt
throats puffed, beaks open mid-song, posture haughty and self-satisfied. Dazzling,
beautiful, a touch arrogant.

spread-03-kinglion.png
The birds swooping down in a noisy greedy crowd into the maize field, tearing golden
cobs and kernels loose, husks and seed scattering through warm sunlight. Motion,
mess, brilliance — beautiful birds doing a thoughtless, wasteful thing.

spread-04-kinglion.png
An emptied, harvested-bare field at dusk: broken maize stalks, stripped husks on the
ground. A small group of tired farmer villagers standing with empty baskets and pots,
worried faces, a worried child among them. Dimmer, cooler, anxious light.

spread-05-kinglion.png
Villagers at twilight, fearful and turning angry, peering out into the dark bush with
lamps and spears, suspicion on their faces, looking at the whole wild veld as if it
were the thief. Tense but not violent — fear, not bloodshed. Long shadows.

spread-06-kinglion.png
Soft sorrowful scene: a duiker, a scrub hare and a tortoise hiding low among long
grass and rocks, wide frightened eyes, lamplight passing as distant shadows. The
innocent animals afraid. No violence shown — only fear and hiding. Tender, hushed.

spread-07-kinglion.png
A great old spreading marula tree on the open veld in late-gold light. King Lion —
old, heavy, silver-maned, kind-eyed — seated in judgement beneath it. Every bright
bird summoned, perched throughout the branches above. The small {{CHILD}}-figure
peeks from the long grass at the edge, watching. Grand and grave but warm.

spread-08-kinglion.png
King Lion lifting his great head to address the birds in the branches — mouth open in
speech, not a roar; expression grave and pleading. The bright birds look down from the
leaves. Serious, gentle, gold evening light. A warning given, not yet a punishment.

spread-09-kinglion.png
Next morning: the bright birds laughing and diving defiantly back into the maize,
brilliant against a fresh dawn, ignoring the warning, mid-mischief. Energetic,
beautiful, heedless.

spread-10-kinglion.png
King Lion standing tall and sorrowful beneath the marula at deep evening, head bowed,
passing his judgement. The bright birds gone suddenly still and silent in the branches
above. Deep, sad, glowing gold light. The weight of a fair, unhappy decision.

spread-11-kinglion.png
The gentle transformation: the birds' small clawed feet sinking and taking root into
dark earth, wings stiffening into green leaves, bodies lengthening into upright stems.
Soft painterly magic — sad and tender, NOT frightening or grotesque. The colours of
bird and growing flower beginning to merge.

spread-12-kinglion.png
REVEAL spread: a whole field of strelitzia / bird-of-paradise flowers — each an
orange-crested, blue-throated bird-head lifting on a tall green stem toward a vast open
evening sky. At each flower's beak, a single shining bead of nectar like a tear. The
{{CHILD}}-figure stands among them, gazing up with the flowers. Beautiful, aching,
luminous — the heart of the book.

spread-13-kinglion.png
Evening close: the small {{CHILD}}-figure walking home across the cool veld toward the
warm-lit round houses, glancing back once over a shoulder at the field of sky-gazing
flowers beneath the first faint stars. Peaceful, warm, gently wistful.

COVER (kinglion-cover.png)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Landscape 3:2, full-bleed, NO TEXT. Hero composition: King Lion — old, dignified,
silver-amber mane, kind grave eyes — seated beneath the great marula tree in glowing
golden-hour veld light, with the brilliant orange-crested, blue-throated birds perched
in the branches above him. In the foreground, just beginning to bloom, one or two
strelitzia / bird-of-paradise flowers lift their bright bird-heads to the sky,
quietly foreshadowing the turn. The small barefoot {{CHILD}}-figure stands to one side
in the grass, looking up. Warm, painterly, inviting; a wide open African sky filling
the top third. The whole story's promise in one image: pride, judgement, and the
flower that remembers the sky.
