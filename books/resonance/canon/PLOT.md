# OPENING SCENE — THE WIRE PUSH CAR

## Flashback / Framing Device

**Timeline:** Years before Act I. Arin age ~8-10, in KwaZulu-Natal mining region.

**Purpose:**
- Establish Arin's origin in street-level engineering genius
- Introduce Themba (his father) as skilled mechanic and presence
- Ground Arin's obsession with mechanical solutions in childhood observation
- Foreshadow Guardian through the wire push car's engineering logic
- Establish mining dump trucks as object of fascination and dread

---

## The Scene

**Setting:** Village near Iron Ridge Mine. Street/compound area. Arin building an over-engineered wire push car.

**What he's building:**

Wire push cars are iconic South African street culture—homemade vehicles made from scrap wire and tin cans for wheels, with a working steering mechanism on a long shaft so the steering wheel is in the builder's hands. The car is pushed and "driven" simultaneously.

Arin's version is obsessively engineered:
- Articulated frame (better steering response)
- Canned wheel suspension (attempting shock absorption)
- Reinforced wire joints (precision welding from scrap)
- Working differential steering (complex for a child)
- Modeled after mining dump trucks (what he observes daily)

**Key elements:**

- **Themba appears** — watches Arin work, comments on the engineering, shows him a technique or precision approach
- **Other children pass** — they push simpler cars; Arin's is over-complex, beautiful, obsessive
- **The mine in background** — visible, present, dangerous; Themba goes to work there
- **Arin's focus** — absolute; he sees problems in the design and solves them obsessively
- **Themba's attitude** — proud but also worried (is the boy safe? will he hurt himself? why does he need to solve everything?)

---

## Thematic Payload

This scene establishes:

1. **Arin's engineering genius emerges early** — not from formal education but from obsession and pattern-seeing
2. **The inheritance of mining knowledge** — Themba's work shapes what Arin builds (dump trucks, not just play cars)
3. **Mechanical solutions as response to danger** — watching his father go into danger, Arin builds better solutions
4. **Isolation through obsession** — he's alone with his work while other kids play; this pattern continues into adulthood
5. **Themba as teacher** — he doesn't force; he shows, he questions, he lets Arin figure it out
6. **The mine as inevitable background** — it's always there, always present, always dangerous

---

## Connection to Guardian

The wire push car is the prototype of Guardian's logic:
- **Wire frame (Guardian's lattice structure)**
- **Articulated steering (Guardian's joints and actuators)**
- **Obsessive precision in scrap materials (Guardian built from available tech, over-engineered)**
- **Building to solve an observed danger (Themba might die in the mines; Guardian will prevent it)**

Adult Arin doesn't think of this consciously—but the impulse is the same.

### The Preservation Run

Act II setpiece. See `SUIT.md` (rogue preservation), `SAGE.md` (Moriarty channel).

**Setup**

Post–first-contact. Guardian field test or controlled mine-adjacent demo.

Assist Suit + Guardian (Hulkbuster) coupled. SAGE Court stable in simulation — not yet wise under embodied stress.

**Inciting fault**

Neural feedback damping fails during high-gain calibration or stress test.

Undampened loop → cortical overload → Arin unconscious inside Guardian.

**Autonomous response**

Guardian interprets blackout as operator life threat.

Pangolin ball → unroll → **Live Preservation Guardian Mode (LPGM)**.

Rogue preservation: relocate operator, secure power, medical resources — without ethical brake.

**Escalation**

Immature Court dominance: Mother + Wolf + Mercury.

Mercury runs Mitnick-style social engineering (pretext, trust chains, authority mimicry).

**Moriarty channel** — persona from Arin's Sherlock Holmes reading; no human conspirator.

Infrastructure damage: grid, transport, facility access.

Quantified economic fallout: fines, downtime, liability.

IAOC anomaly signatures sharpen.

**Turn**

Theo / Priya help regain partial control.

Fool stabilizes Court; Judge restored.

Arin wakes to consequences.

Trauma beat: the machine moved without him.

**Payoff**

Deepens Act III pressure before public leak.

Foreshadows climax: SAGE can save lives / cannot be unleashed unbraked.

Proves: stable Court ≠ wise Court.
