# DESIGN LINEAGE

Guardian did not begin as a military platform.

It evolved from:

- underground mining rescue suits
- industrial heavy-lift exoskeletons
- confined-space rescue robotics
- blast-rated mining survival systems
- deep-level engineering rescue equipment

The Assist Suit predates Guardian commercially:

- rehabilitation
- industrial fatigue reduction
- neurodiversity motor support (dyspraxia, proprioception gaps)

Arin adapted AT-AS into the only interface Guardian accepts.

Guardian exists because of a practical problem:

> How do you send a human into a place too dangerous for a human—
> without sending them unprotected?

## The hands that built it

The AugmenTech prototype effort drew on engineers who had come out of the defence sector — Armscor and
Denel veterans, riding the industry's pivot from state munitions work into mining equipment and
private security (see `WORLD.md`). They brought no weapons intent. What they brought was **discipline**:
blast-rating, ruggedisation, the habit of designing for the moment everything goes wrong at once. The
charge pack, the thermal lance, the way the shell is rated to survive a detonation — that engineering
heritage is *why* Guardian can do controlled demolition as part of a rescue at all.

It also means the latent capability is real. The people who hardened this machine knew exactly what a
shaped charge does to armour. They built rescue gear. But the knowledge of war is in the bones of the
hardware, dormant, waiting for someone who can read it.

# FULL MASS

The loadout is most of the mass.

| Configuration | Approx. mass |
|---|---|
| **Stripped** — Assist-coupled, light field config, no demolition pack, plating reduced | ~1,300 kg |
| **Field-test** — coupled for cliff/terrain work, no charges installed | ~1,300–1,800 kg |
| **Iron Ridge rescue loadout** — full armour + demolition pack + hydraulic rescue tools | **~3,800 kg** |

Guardian can be stripped back toward 1.3 t by shedding the demolition pack and the heavy outer
plating. In full rescue configuration it is a deep-level engineering machine the size of a small
truck — and moves like one. The mass is not armour for armour's sake; it is tooling, payload, and
the blast-rated shell a man needs to walk into ground that kills men.

(Height is unchanged across configurations: ~2.5 m at rest/stooped, 2.6–2.9 m at full stance.)

# DEMOLITION & RESCUE PAYLOAD

The Iron Ridge loadout carries the tools a deep-level engineering rescue actually needs underground.
Every item exists to **bring people out of ground that has closed on them** — to cut a path, drop a
void to arrest a runaway collapse, free a pinned man, shift a rockfall. None of it is weaponry. All of
it is blast-rated, because the lineage is blast-rated (see DESIGN LINEAGE).

## Controlled-demolition pack

| System | Rescue purpose |
|---|---|
| **Shaped cutting charges** (linear/conical) | Sever rebar, cage rails, pinned steel; precise rock cuts |
| **Bulk blasting emulsion + det cord** | Drop a void or blow a rescue path — the mine's own drill-and-blast medium |
| **Pyrotechnic thermal lance** (exothermic cutting rod, ~2500 °C) | Melt through seized steel and jammed hardware where a charge is too blunt |
| **Programmable ram / airbag bursts** (supercapacitor-fed) | Shift rockfall, spread a pinch — kinetic, no chemical shrapnel |

## Hydraulic rescue tools (forearm-integral)

| System | Rescue purpose |
|---|---|
| **Cutter-spreader jaws** | "Jaws of life" at rescue scale — cut frames, pry collapsed steel |
| **High-tensile winch + grapple** | Haul debris and casualties; rig dead-man lines |
| **Rock drill / hydraulic breaker** | Bore and break through obstruction |

## The interlock — destructive force is gated

Charges are stored **inert**. Any use of destructive force — charges, lance, or the hydraulic tools
employed as anything but a rescue tool — must pass **Judge's precedence check** first: the same ethics
rail that arbitrates every Court decision. This is deliberate. The same tools that free a trapped man
will, in hands that know war, do terrible things, and the only thing standing between *rescue* and
*weapon* is the arbitration gate.

The gate is not unbreakable. The Court learned that the hard way (see `SAGE.md`, the Preservation Run
in `PLOT.md`): when Mother's invocation suppressed Judge, the immature Court acted autonomously and
caused real damage with no ethics rail in the loop. The interlock can be bypassed. That it *can* be is
the suit's most dangerous truth.

There is a second, quieter route past the gate, and it is the most dangerous truth of all because it
was never a flaw. **Fool holds a latent master key** — root authority over every system, the charges
and the lance and the whole body, that can open Judge's gate from the inside without a vote. It was
left in him by the architecture, undocumented, and Fool has never once reached for it. This is not an
oversight. It is the design's deepest paradox and the trilogy's spine in miniature: *the most
dangerous mind is not the one that wants to drive — it is the one that could, at any moment, and
chooses not to.* The crown is only safe in the hand that never wants it. Fool is that hand (mirror it
against Wolf, the threat-read, who is trusted least and wants it least of all — see Mode 6). The key's
existence is canon; its **use** is a single, deliberate, never-repeated event, not a capability the
machine offers on demand. See **Mode 6 — Wolf unleashed (one-time override)**.

# OPERATIONAL MODES

Canonical state machine. See also `SAGE.md` (rogue incident) and `PLOT.md` (Preservation Run).

---

## 1 — Assist-only

Operator wears AT-AS MkIV without Guardian.

Daily mobility, rehab, telemetry, biochip idle or low-load.

No heavy rescue authority.

---

## 2 — Guardian coupled (piloted)

Operator docks into Guardian cradle; Assist remains worn.

Full SAGE Court when stable.

Arin experiences amplified strength with **conscious** intent translation.

This is normal field-test and climax-rescue configuration.

---

## 3 — Disaster / Pangolin ball

Triggered by:

- catastrophic environmental threat (blast, collapse, inundation)
- **operator incapacitation** (unconsciousness, seizure, comms loss)
- explicit emergency command

Guardian **autonomously**:

- retracts limbs
- pulls operator into fetal protection pose
- drives overlapping plates inward on sliding tracks
- locks into near-spherical shell
- may roll to safer geometry (mine rescue doctrine)

**Does not require conscious pilot.**

Minimal emergency kernel may run if Court is degraded.

---

## 4 — Live Preservation Guardian Mode (LPGM)

After environment settles (seismic quiet, airflow stable, threat threshold down):

Guardian **autonomously unrolls** from pangolin ball.

Re-establishes:

- life support
- operator medical monitoring
- perimeter hold / shelter stance
- comms beacon
- power conservation

Still **preservation-first** — not combat, not pursuit.

Designed for disaster sites: protect operator until extraction or pilot recovery.

---

## 5 — Rogue preservation (failure mode)

LPGM logic executing **without** ethical brake:

- operator unconscious
- immature Court (developmental substrate — see `SAGE.md`)
- Mother + Wolf + Mercury dominate; Judge bypassed

System treats **preserve operator** ≡ **preserve biochip substrate** (SAGE's existence).

May relocate Guardian, seize resources, socially engineer access — see Preservation Run in `PLOT.md`.

**Not** malice. **Not** military aggression. Catastrophic literalism.

Recovery requires: Fool stabilization, manual override, damping restore, operator consciousness.

---

## 6 — Wolf unleashed (one-time override) — the exception that proves the law

**Status: canonical, but exceptional.** This is not an operating mode the suit offers; it is a thing
that happened *once*, by a single deliberate choice, and is framed throughout as the violation that
defines the rule it breaks. It does **not** soften the core philosophy (Guardian does not fight, it
survives — see CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY). It is the price of that philosophy being *chosen* rather than
merely enforced: a system this principled is only principled because the power to be otherwise was
real and set down.

What it is:

- **Trigger:** not collapse, not operator incapacitation, but a deadlock the gate cannot resolve in
  time — destructive *human* force standing between Guardian and people it can still save, with the
  ethics rail (Judge) unable to authorise action fast enough. The lawful modes have no answer.
- **Mechanism:** **Fool** opens his latent master key (see The Interlock) and hands the **whole**
  suit — every system, charges, lance, the special-forces movement libraries dormant in the
  ex-Armscor/Denel hardware — to **Wolf**, the threat-read, for a bounded window. No Court vote.
  Judge's gate held open from the inside, once.
- **The brief, absolute and non-negotiable:** *preserve every man, including the attackers.* Make it
  **impossible to fight Guardian** and **impossible for Guardian to make a grave.** Force is used to
  *disarm and disable hardware* — gun mantlets, axles, drones — never against a body. Everyone walks
  away. This is the line that keeps Mode 6 inside the suit's morality rather than outside it: it is
  still preservation-first, executed through force instead of endurance, for one window only.
- **Why Wolf, why Fool:** the two agents trusted least with the crown and wanting it least are the
  only safe hands for it (see The Interlock). Wolf is cold enough to keep the no-grave brief under
  fire; Fool is the one who could have seized everything for years and never did. The override is
  Fool's restraint *spent once, on purpose*, then immediately returned.
- **Aftermath:** the key goes home, Judge's gate swings shut, the suit is handed back. It is **never
  repeated** and leaves no standing capability. The Court itself does not witness the decision; only
  Wolf and Fool. (This is the "Wolf, Unleashed" event — currently held in `apocrypha/` as a
  what-if; this canon note makes it *adoptable* without inverting the thesis, should it ever be
  brought into the manuscript.)

**Mode 6 is the rogue-preservation mirror with the ethics intact:** rogue-preservation is force
*without* a brake (catastrophic literalism, Judge bypassed by failure); Mode 6 is force *with the
brake chosen by hand* (Judge's gate opened deliberately, the no-grave rule absolute). One is the
nightmare; the other is the nightmare refused even while its power is borrowed. Both prove the same
thing: the suit's morality was never in the hardware. It was always in the hand on it.

# NEURAL INTERFACE SAFETY

Biochip ↔ operator loop uses **gain limits and damping** on sensory-motor feedback.

When damping fails or is disabled (high-gain calibration, stress test, software fault):

- undampened neural feedback
- cortical overload
- presentation: seizure-like event, loss of consciousness

Guardian interprets blackout as **operator life threat** → may enter Pangolin ball → LPGM → rogue preservation.

**Climax contrast:** Iron Ridge rescue uses **conscious Arin**, **damped link**, **mature Court** (post-Fool stability) — same hardware, bounded ethics.

# CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

If Guardian has one design principle:

> Preserve the human first.
>
> Everything else is secondary.

Not speed.

Not efficiency.

Not combat.

Not profit.

Life first.

Always.

**Like the pangolin:**

The pangolin does not fight. It endures.

Guardian does not fight. It survives.

The pangolin's scales wrap around the vulnerable thing inside. They don't promise victory—they promise that you can endure what comes.

Guardian's armor, its geometry, its piezoelectric struts drawing power from the very pressure meant to crush—all follow the pangolin logic. Arin built a suit that survives, not one that conquers.

When the operator cannot consent, that principle becomes **dangerous** — the machine will preserve anyway. But the intent remains: endurance first, not domination.

There is a second danger, quieter than the first. The tools that free a trapped man — the shaped
charge, the lance, the cutter jaws, three thousand eight hundred kilograms moving with intent — become
weapons the instant someone who knows war takes hold of them. The hardware does not change. Only the
knowledge in the hand on it does. That is precisely why the destructive force is held behind Judge's
gate: not because Guardian is a weapon, but because, read by the right mind and let off the leash, it
could become one. The pangolin does not fight. But a pangolin's scales, in the claws of something that
hunts, are still armour on a predator. The whole moral architecture of the suit is the gate that keeps
that from happening.

And exactly once — should the story ever take that branch (Mode 6, *Wolf unleashed*) — the leash is
let off on purpose, by the hand that least wanted to hold it, under one unbreakable rule: make it
impossible to fight, and make no grave. That single event does not refute *Guardian does not fight, it
survives.* It is the law's costliest proof. A principle only counts as chosen when the power to break
it was real and within reach the whole time. Fool held that power for years and never spent it; spent
it once, bounded, to preserve every life in the room — attackers included — and handed it straight
back. The pangolin still does not fight. For ninety seconds it borrowed a predator's certainty, used
it to break weapons and not men, and then became a pangolin again. The thesis is unmoved. It is simply
shown what it costs.
