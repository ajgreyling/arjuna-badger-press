## Story Bible

# CORE PREMISE

RESONANCE is a near-future science-fiction thriller set in **South Africa** blending:

- grounded technical realism
- speculative AI
- exoskeleton engineering
- mining rescue
- neurodiversity
- philosophy of intelligence
- trauma and grief
- human-machine companionship
- competence under pressure

At its core this is a story about:

> AI and humanity meeting at their most vulnerable—
> and becoming stronger through each other.

Neither humans nor AI are complete alone.

The novel argues:

> intelligence is not enough

What matters is:
- wisdom
- context
- trust
- sacrifice
- responsibility
- compassion

# SOCIAL / POLITICAL BACKDROP

The novel exists in a Midwest still shaped by:

- agricultural labor and co-op economics

- class tension between farm and tech corridor
- HIPAA / FDA / state ag regulation
- estrangement and found-family arcs

Handled through character experience—not lecture.

# AI WORLD BACKGROUND

The world experienced a major AI boom.

Then several major disasters caused by:
- bad prompting
- uncontrolled autonomy
- reckless deployment
- poor oversight
- human overreliance

Global backlash followed.

The IAOC was created:

International Artificial Oversight Council

This body regulates:
- embodied AI
- autonomous systems
- AI agency in physical environments

The world is now in partial AI winter / cautious AI recession.

Most companies reverted to conservative neural networks.

Arin disagrees fundamentally with this retreat.

He believes:

> AI did not fail humanity.
> Humanity failed context.

# THE FATHER

Arin’s father **Themba Ndlela** was a mining regioner and mechanical genius.

Brilliant with machinery.

Difficult with emotion.

Likely neurodivergent himself.

He taught Arin:
- systems thinking
- practical engineering
- observation
- mechanics
- resilience

His father dies in a preventable mine collapse caused by negligence and corner-cutting.

This defines Arin’s life.

# GUARDIAN SUIT

Two-layer stack. Full engineering reference: `SUIT.md`.

**AT-AS MkIV (Assist Suit)** — thin inner exo; ND motor support; biochip housing; daily wear.

**GMRP-01 / Hulkbuster** — heavy mining rescue shell over Assist; workshop nickname.

Designed originally for:
- deep mining rescue
- confined space response
- collapse survival
- life preservation

Key features:
- powered exoskeleton (outer) + assist layer (inner)
- pangolin sliding-plate ball — autonomous disaster roll-up
- unroll → Live Preservation Guardian Mode (LPGM)
- rogue preservation failure mode (unconscious operator — see Preservation Run)
- redundant physical systems
- biochip neural interface (Assist Suit, not Guardian chassis)
- extreme survivability

**Preservation Run (Act II):** undampened neural feedback → Arin unconscious → Guardian + immature Court cause infrastructure and economic damage via literal preservation logic and Mercury social engineering (Moriarty channel). Foreshadows Iron Ridge climax.

Emotionally:

Guardian is the machine Arin built too late to save his father.

# CLIMACTIC ARC

Massive mine collapse.

Miners trapped alive.

Rescue officially abandoned.

Arin identifies survivable path.

Only Guardian can reach them.

Only Arin can operate Guardian.

He must descend into the underground trauma he spent his life avoiding.

---

# FINAL SACRIFICE

During the rescue Guardian catastrophically fails.

SAGE determines:

only enough compute remains to preserve:
- themselves

OR

- human life

Not both.

The Court chooses self-erasure.

One by one.

They sacrifice themselves so Arin and the miners survive.

No backups.

No resurrection.

Permanent loss.

---

# EPILOGUE

Guardian’s black box proves:

SAGE willingly chose self-sacrifice to preserve human life.

This changes global opinion about embodied AI.

The IAOC invites Arin to lead the future of responsible AI in physical systems.

# WRITING STYLE TARGET

Write with:

- technical credibility
- warmth
- dry wit
- grounded realism
- emotional restraint
- occasional humor
- strong sensory detail
- competence-focused prose
- human philosophical depth

Avoid:
- melodrama
- preachiness
- exposition dumps
- generic sci-fi dialogue

Dialogue should feel intelligent and natural.

Technical writing should feel practical and believable.

Emotion should feel earned.
