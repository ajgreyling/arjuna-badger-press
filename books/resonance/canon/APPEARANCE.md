## Coherence flags

- **Arin's tattoo age — INTERNAL CONTRADICTION (age 16 vs 17).** The Dune tattoo is said to have been inked at **seventeen** twice (ch-02: "had cut into himself at seventeen"; ch-11: "the words had lived in ink since he was seventeen") but at **sixteen** once (ch-12: "Left forearm. Under the Assist. You've had it since you were sixteen"). The forearm (left) is consistent; the age is not. Pick one (17 is used 2:1).

- **Arin's Joker card — duration drifts (5 yrs vs 9 yrs).** ch-02: "He had carried that card for five years… His first proper job, before AugmenTech… he'd been twenty"; but ch-03 says "he had kept it for nine years" and "kept it for nine years out of something between superstition and spite." If he got it at 20 and is now 23, **three years** is the literal span; the card text gives both **five** and **nine**. All three numbers conflict. (The "nine years" in ch-03 may be conflating it with how long he's owned the card vs. carried it on monitors — still reads as a contradiction.)

- **Arin's age when his father died — minor (implies 9, canon/early prose imply younger/"a boy").** ch-12 states plainly "You were nine" (and "carried up out of a hole like this. You were nine," ch-12). ch-01 prologue presents him at **8** building the wire car the day of a collapse, and CHARACTERS.md says "Arin was young." The ch-12 age of 9 at the father's death is internally fine if the wire-car day (age 8) is a *different, earlier* day — but the prologue strongly ties the wire-car day to a collapse rumble, which could be misread as the fatal one. Worth a continuity check; not a hard contradiction.

- **Priya Ellis has no physical description at all — RISK, not contradiction.** Priya is the carried-forward lead of RELIC, yet across ch-09–ch-15 she gets zero build/hair/eyes/skin/age in the prose. Any appearance fixed in RELIC must be retro-compatible with RESONANCE because nothing here constrains it — flag so RELIC's APPEARANCE ledger is treated as the originating authority for her look.

- **CHARACTERS.md "Priya" vs "Priya Ellis" naming.** NAMES.md lists the neural tech as **"Priya"** (Indian heritage, ~32); the prose name in the logs is **"Priya Ellis"** (ch-09 access record). Same person, but canon should record the surname **Ellis** to match the prose (and the trilogy lead).

- **No contradictions found** in hair length/colour, eye colour, beard side, or scars across any character — chiefly because the prose declines to specify them. Venter's grey beard and sun-weathered skin are the only repeated physical traits and they are consistent. Arin's left forearm (tattoo) and tool-scarred hands are consistent. Guardian's chest scar / scorched left flank are consistent.
