# Style Guide — *The Sky Still Runs the House*

> Binding with `STORY_BIBLE.md`. The book is a history of a structure, not a prosecution and not a
> conversion tract.

## Register

- Plain, narrative nonfiction in the house’s Bryson-adjacent register: concrete first, technical
  term second, joke only after the object is secure.
- Prefer “a court scholar in Nineveh copying eclipse omens” to “premodern man sought meaning.”
- Translate the job, not only the noun: an *ummânu* is a learned court scholar doing records,
  calculation, interpretation, and advice.
- Short endings. Let the last physical image carry the claim.
- South African English. En dashes with spaces. Avoid American corporate language unless the
  modern analogy needs it.

## Honesty markers

- State when a date or transmission route is debated.
- “Helped produce” is not “single-handedly invented.”
- “Functioned like” is not “became.”
- “Culturally effective” is not “physically causal.”
- “Not supported in controlled tests” is preferable to theatre about foolish believers.

## Machine-tell taboos

No “in conclusion.” No chapter-end recap in list form. No “throughout human history.” No anonymous
“ancients.” No stacked rhetorical questions. No claim that a complicated history is “really” one
simple thing. No sequence of three near-synonymous abstractions where one object would do.

## Recurring objects

Clay tablet; reed stylus; watch roof; twelve equal rooms; peg calendar; physician’s zodiac figure;
court table; translation desk; almanac page; psychology classroom; phone screen. Each recurrence
must advance the argument rather than merely decorate it.
