Checkable-Thing-Is-Real:
- Every “hole” is a cited, real-world, unresolved fact: the lunar standstill cycle’s invisibility to a single observer; the sub-degree alignment; the shared measurement standard 53 miles away. No invented mysteries.

The Potato Engine:
- The “potato” is every attempt to reduce the wonder: “It’s a fort” (but the ditch is inside); “It’s a coincidence” (but the cycle is invisible to one lifetime); “It’s a local fluke” (but the same ruler appears at High Bank). Each debunk is played straight, respected, and then shown to fail on the facts.

Wonder-Not-Conspiracy:
- No aliens, no lost master race, no omnipotent cabal. The residue is what the evidence itself says: ordinary people, extraordinary patience, a shared standard across time and space. The wonder is real and human.

§3 In Spirit:
- The local/keeper out-reads the crew on what matters. The “what is it for” is theirs to give or withhold. The crew never hold more of the camera than the local.

Designated Mysteries Stay Open:
- The official story is true; the only mystery is the one the evidence requires: who kept the record, and who taught the standard? The book never answers this fully. The “shared measure” thread is planted for the series, never confirmed or denied.

Real-Places-Honestly:
- The earthworks, the town, the golf course, the tribal governments, and the legal battles are all rendered accurately and with respect. No composite sites or invented cultures.

This Book’s Specifics:
- All measurements, dates, and claims are sourced from the binding fact-floor. The “Moon apparent-size ratio” claim is labelled soft and may be gripped by a character, but never by the narrator. The adversary is fictional but plausible, never a stand-in for a real-world entity.
