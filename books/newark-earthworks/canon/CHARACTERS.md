The Travelling ND Crew:
- **Naila (Lead, ND):** Autistic, pattern cognition, mathematical and geometric intuition. Reads the squared-circle nest and shared measurement the way others read body language. Holds both awe and skepticism without contradiction. Her arc: learns to trust that seeing clearly is enough, even when the answer isn’t hers to give.
- **Frik:** ADHD, relentless questioner, comics-nerd energy, punctures overreach (especially his own). Quick to spot the “reach” when someone pushes a soft claim too far. Brings the fish-heckle and the wink.
- **The LENS:** Ex-war photographer, PTSD, not ND but recurring. The series’ camera — sees what others miss, but carries the cost. Reminds the team (and reader) to lower the camera and let the moment belong to those it should.
- **Other crew (optional):** Additional ND or marginal team members as needed for warmth, coverage, and perspective.

Jakobus:  
The weathered fixer, never POV, always road. Reads made things for their function. Arrives with the question “what is it for?” and hands the answer to the local. Defers, never leads.

THE LOCAL/Custodian:
- **Dr. Tessa Redwing:** Hopewell-descendant-affiliated archaeologist/THPO (Tribal Historic Preservation Officer). Both scientist and culture-bearer. Out-reads the crew on the ground, the record, and the “what is it for.” Her arc: weighs whether to hand the answer to outsiders or keep it for her people. The dignity floor is hers to set.

Clean-Handed Adversary:
- **The Foundation:** A plausible-deniability research/cultural foundation with legal access to the site (via remaining lease or research grant). Their rep is well-meaning but motivated by control, narrative, and monetization. Never a villain, always a rival to read and publish first.

Voices/Wiring-not-Wounds/Arcs:
- Every ND character’s wiring is how they see, never a wound or caricature.
- The crew covers each other’s blind spots; the story’s warmth is in their difference.
- The local’s arc is about stewardship, not “mystery solved.”
- The adversary is a mirror: what does it mean to “own” a record, and who gets to interpret it?

Close-Third Rule:
- Close-third POV rotates among the ND crew, never inside the local or Jakobus. The local is always seen from outside; her authority is never usurped.
