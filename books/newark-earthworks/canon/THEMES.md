Theme(s) in Action:
- **Patience and Record:** The hardest, truest wonders are built by ordinary people handing a record forward, knowing they will not live to see the pattern close.
- **Precision is Human:** The most astonishing math is done with baskets, cord, and patience, not miracles. The genius is in the keeping, not the knowing.
- **Ownership and Stewardship:** Who gets to interpret a living record? The answer belongs to the descendants, not the outsiders who read it fastest.
- **Allowed to Say Maybe:** The central gift: the reader (and crew) are permitted to stand in the gap and say “I don’t know.” The maybe is the wonder, not the failure.
- **The Humanity Law:** The crew’s difference is their strength; their wiring is how they see, not a wound. The world’s tendency to overlook the odd or marginal is what lets the record be missed.
- **Dignity Contracts:** The local’s authority is final. The book never lets outsiders “solve” what isn’t theirs; the answer is given or withheld by the keeper, and the reader is left in the draught.

All themes are dramatized, never declared.
