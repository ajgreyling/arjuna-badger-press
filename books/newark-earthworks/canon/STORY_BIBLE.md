Premise:  
A neurodiverse found-family crew travels to Newark, Ohio, to document and understand the Newark Earthworks — the most precise ancient lunar calendar in the Americas, built by the Hopewell two thousand years ago. The official story is true and astonishing: the Octagon tracks the Moon’s 18.6-year standstill cycle to within half a degree, using a shared measurement standard also found 53 miles away. The only mystery left is the one the evidence itself demands: who kept a record longer than a human lifetime, and who taught them to use the same ruler?

Theme (never stated aloud):  
The world’s true wonders are often buried under the weight of easy explanations or ignored because they need no debunking. The awe is in the arithmetic, the patience, and the living line of people who notice and remember. The greatest marvel is not aliens or lost races, but ordinary humans handing a record forward across generations.

Why Files Register:  
Rabbit-hole curiosity; play the official story dead straight and with affection; find the one hole; wink; let the reader decide. Pro-skeptic, pro-hoaxer, pro-wonder, all at once. No preaching; the fish heckles. The middle path is the point: never confirm or deny the maybe, never reach for the easy answer.

Intended Reader:  
Curious, skeptical, wonder-seeking adults and teens who love real mysteries, rabbit holes, and the feeling of standing in the draught between what’s known and what’s possible. Readers who want to be allowed to say “I don’t know.”

POV:  
Close-third, rotating among the ND crew (never the local or Jakobus); the crew own the telling, but the local out-reads them on what matters. The LENS (ex-war photographer, PTSD) as recurring camera; at least one ND crew member is central and load-bearing.

Content Bounds:  
No invented “ancient mysteries”; all checkable facts must be real and cited. Fringe/soft claims are labelled and may be gripped by a character, never by the narrator. No white-saviour or “outsiders crack the native code” moves; the local’s authority is final and the answer is theirs to give or withhold.

Creative Driver:  
The irresistible draw of a real, checkable “hole” in the official story — not a gap in evidence, but a truth the evidence itself demands. The joy of hard science, patient curiosity, and the dignity of the people who built and keep the record.
