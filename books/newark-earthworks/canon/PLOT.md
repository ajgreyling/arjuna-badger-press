Act I: ARRIVE  
- Crew arrives in Newark as OHC’s legal reclamation of the Octagon is finalized. Tensions between public access, tribal stewardship, and the residual golf club lease. The Foundation rep is already present, seeking to secure first publication of a new ephemeris-based reading.
- Naila is fascinated by the geometric precision; Frik is ready to puncture the “ancient aliens” and “fort” claims; the LENS records, wary of the narrative being taken from the people who own it.
- Dr. Tessa Redwing (local/THPO) greets them with cautious welcome; she’s been burned by extraction before.

Act I Obstacle:  
- Access is restricted; the Foundation has a research window, and the OHC is caught between legal obligations and tribal consultation. The crew must work around permissions and politics, not just puzzles.
- The crew tries the standard “debunk” moves: fort, coincidence, lone genius. Each is dramatized and shown to fail — the “potato” is every attempt to make the wonder small.

Act II: READ  
- Naila and Frik team up to physically walk the site, measure the OCD, and reconstruct the lunar alignments. They compare the geometry to High Bank, making the “shared ruler” connection.
- The Foundation rep tries to “secure” the data and narrative, pushing for a media rollout.
- Tessa shares the process of the 18.6-year observation relay: how the cycle could only be known by a record handed forward, not a single observer.
- The crew’s attempts to “crack” the site are gently rebuffed; Tessa makes clear that the “what is it for” is more than astronomy — it’s a living record.

Act II Cost:  
- Tensions rise between Foundation, OHC, and tribal representatives about who gets to interpret and present the findings. The crew is caught in the middle; Naila is frustrated by the refusal to “finish” the puzzle.
- The LENS witnesses a moment where the Foundation nearly gets exclusive access; the crew must decide whether to help or step back.

Act III: KEY  
- The lunar alignment is re-demonstrated at dawn: the Moon rises through the far gateway, witnessed by all parties. The precision is undeniable; the “potato” is finally and fully refused.
- The “shared ruler” at High Bank is confirmed; Naila and Tessa walk the geometry together, and Tessa shows how the same knowledge was handed across generations and miles.
- Frik tries to push the “Moon apparent-size ratio” theory; Tessa and Naila both decline to confirm or deny, naming it a pretty reach.

Act III Handoff / Climax:  
- The Foundation is edged out — access and interpretation are handed to the tribal representatives, with the OHC’s public stewardship confirmed.
- Jakobus asks “what is it for?”; Tessa answers for her people: “It is a record, and it is ours.” The crew records, but does not interpret. The wonder is left open — the reader stands in the gap.
