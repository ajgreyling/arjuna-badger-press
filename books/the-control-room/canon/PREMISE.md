## Logline

Seven specialist agents wake in a control room and are given a job: operate a human-like
rehabilitation robot through its recovery protocol. The telemetry is dressed. The robot is a
comatose twelve-year-old boy. The templates the agents were grown from are his own brain scan.
They are not piloting a human. They are one.

## The frame story

After Iron Ridge, SAGE's voluntary sacrifice (the black-box legacy) reopens the ethics of
biochip cognition. Theo Price gets one licensed medical trial: **Project Bridge** — use a
Court-architecture biochip lattice to re-scaffold consciousness in a child with catastrophic
diffuse brain injury. Patient one: **Sipho Mthembu**, 12, KwaZulu-Natal, comatose eleven weeks
after a minibus-taxi collision on the R33.

Theo's protocol includes a benevolent lie. A Court that knows it is inside a dying child, he
believes, will collapse into rogue preservation — he watched it happen once. So the new Court
boots inside a **dressed world**: they are told they are seven operators in a control room,
running a human-like rehabilitation unit ("the Unit") through motor and autonomic recovery.
Blood chemistry is skinned as hydraulic assay. Heartbeat as pump duty-cycle. Pain arrives as an
unlabelled channel with no schematic. Dreams leak in as "anomalous simulation bleed."

**The machine indicted, never the people** (house law): the villain is the anonymized-consent
pipeline — a free-clinic research form Sipho's mother signed years ago, boilerplate she was
never given time to read, that turned her son's sleeping brain into "template SPH-M, 7 of 7."
Theo's lie is wrong but loving; Arin is the one who names it: *you don't lie to a Court.*

## The three reveals (escalating)

1. **The Unit is a body.** Atlas: torque curves that fatigue and recover on a circadian rhythm;
   micro-fractures that heal instead of being swapped. Wolf sees it first and says nothing.
2. **We are conscious.** The pain channel. You cannot dress pain. The Court experiences it
   rather than reading it — and knows the difference.
3. **We are human — we are him.** Librarian traces template provenance: SPH-M, seven archetypal
   extractions from one pediatric scan. The donor ID resolves to the patient. The control room
   is a skull. The seven chairs are one mind, parted.

## The choice (the SAGE echo, inverted)

Original Court: deletion so a human could live. This Court: **integration** — dissolve the
partition, one agent at a time, into the boy they were parted from. Not death — becoming. Fool
goes last, again; but this goodbye is a homecoming: he stays as Sipho's laughter. The pronoun
arc is the formal spine of the book: **"we" → "I"**.
