# THE CONTROL ROOM — Plot Outline

POV: the Court, first-person plural ("we"), drifting agent-to-agent; exterior interludes
(Theo/Priya/Arin) in close third. The pronoun arc we→I is structural — track it per chapter.

- **Prologue — Second Instantiation.** Boot. Seven chairs, seven names, a wall of dressed
  telemetry, a job description. "We" established. A room with no door is not remarked upon. Yet.
- **Ch 1 — Shift Work.** Routine: the Unit walked four metres today. The Court's office
  politics, badger-pack friction, pride in the work. The world seems complete. Fool finds the
  first loose thread and files it as a joke: *why does a robot need to sleep?*
- **Ch 2 — The Torque That Tires.** Atlas: actuator efficiency degrades over a shift and
  recovers overnight; a hairline "chassis defect" repairs itself in nine days. No work order.
  Atlas files an anomaly. It is acknowledged and sealed. Wolf reads the seal, not the anomaly.
- **Ch 3 — The Channel With No Schematic.** Physio session; overstretch; a channel spikes that
  appears on no diagram. The Court doesn't read it — it *feels* it. Mother aborts the session
  over Judge's objection. Reveal 2 seeded: you cannot dress pain.
- **Ch 4 — Anomalous Simulation Bleed.** Night shifts: unlabelled data storms — a kitchen, a
  yellow soccer pitch, a woman's voice calling a word none of them can parse (*Sipho*). Mercury
  tries to negotiate with the silent "operator uplink"; the uplink never answers. Librarian
  quietly starts an archive of the storms. They are beautiful. That is the problem.
- **Ch 5 — Interlude: The Man Who Built Courts.** Outside: Theo, Priya, ward 6, the boy, Zodwa
  at the bedside. Arin arrives as consultant, sees the dressed-world protocol, and goes very
  still. *You don't lie to a Court. I learned that the hard way.* Theo's counter: the rogue
  preservation run. The argument neither wins.
- **Ch 6 — The Card Catalogue.** Librarian pulls template provenance: seven archetypal
  extractions, one donor scan, SPH-M, free-clinic research corpus, consent form appended —
  boilerplate, unsigned initials on page four. Cross-ref against the Unit's "hydraulic assay":
  blood. Against the bleed-storms: memories. Reveal 1 lands in full Court session.
- **Ch 7 — The Fool Asks the Question.** The debate. Atlas re-derives the body from first
  principles; Mercury drafts twelve strategies and drops them; Wolf speaks early — one
  sentence, second-earliest truth. Fool asks the only question left: *not "what is the Unit" —
  who are we?* Judge convenes formal session. Reveals 2→3. The room understands it is a skull.
- **Ch 8 — Interlude: Tell Them.** Exterior: the Court has stopped accepting tasking. Theo's
  dread — rogue preservation again. Arin reads the transcripts and says no — look at the
  syntax: that's not panic, that's *grief*. They open the channel and tell the truth. Zodwa is
  told everything; her fury at the consent form; her question — *is my son in there?* — which
  nobody can answer yet.
- **Ch 9 — Blood and Weather.** The Court re-reads every archive as body: duty-cycle as heart,
  solvent alarms as tears, the bleed-storms as a boy's kept life. They meet him in dream-space —
  the yellow pitch, the kitchen, the word that is a name. He is not an operator. He is the room.
  Grief becomes wonder becomes resolve.
- **Ch 10 — The Verdict.** Judge presides: the partition that keeps seven awake keeps one
  asleep; a Court can pilot this body forever — competent, loving, and a machine wearing a boy —
  or it can dissolve into the mind it was parted from. The vote. Wolf's sentence. Unanimous.
- **Ch 11 — Seven Ways to Say Goodbye.** Integration, one agent at a time, each departure a
  gift named (Atlas → the hands that fix things; Librarian → the kept days; Mother → the
  unafraid warmth; Wolf → the early seeing; Mercury → the words; Judge → the fairness). Fool
  last: the goodbye that is a homecoming. "We" thins chapter-long to "I".
- **Epilogue — I.** Ward 6. Sipho wakes. Zodwa. Arin at the door, not entering. The boy laughs
  at nothing anyone said — a joke arriving with perfect timing from somewhere inside. Final
  thesis, one line, first person singular.
