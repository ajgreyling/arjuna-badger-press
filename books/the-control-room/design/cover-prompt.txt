Cinematic matte-painting book cover art, vertical portrait 2:3, absolutely no text or logos. Interior of a vast white circular control room with no door: seven empty operator chairs in a gentle arc, each chair haloed in its own hue of warm light, facing an enormous curved wall of soft glowing telemetry. The streams of telemetry — heartbeat lines, golden data-weather, drifting motes — converge and resolve into the huge faint profile of a sleeping African boy's face, eyes closed, serene, twelve years old, emerging from the data like a constellation. One small bright thread of gold runs from the smallest, tipped-back chair to the boy's smile. Clinical white and deep teal shadows, warm amber-gold data light, tender and awe-struck, not sterile. Photoreal cinematic lighting, volumetric haze, 8k detail. Leave a quieter darker band across the top fifth and bottom sixth for typography.
