THE FLOWER THAT WATCHES THE SKY — ILLUSTRATION SPECIFICATION
(Prompts for ChatGPT web image generator / OpenRouter image model. One book, one style.)
Companion to "King Lion and the Birds Who Stole the Sky" — keep the bird + flower designs
consistent between the two books.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOUSE STYLE — paste this STYLE BLOCK at the top of EVERY prompt so all 13
spreads + the cover read as one book:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STYLE: Warm, painterly children's-picture-book illustration in the tradition of
hand-painted gouache and watercolour storybooks — soft visible brushwork, gentle
edges, deeply tender, a little wistful. Golden-hour African veld palette: honey
gold, warm ochre, soft cream, red earth, wide sky-blue — with the flower's own
ORANGE-AND-BLUE burning bright at the centre of every key scene. Storybook warmth,
big skies, lots of air. NOT digital-slick, NOT 3D-render, NOT photoreal. Picture-
book art a tired parent and a small child read together by one lamp at bedtime.
Sorrow handled gently — wistful, never frightening; no violence, no gore.

CHARACTERS & SUBJECTS (keep consistent across every image):
- {{CHILD}}: a young Black South African child, about six, short natural hair,
  barefoot, in a simple soft cotton dress the colour of the veld. Curious, kind,
  unhurried. (Where {{CHILD}} appears, the child-hero is shown; the literal token
  is the personalised name in the text, not in the image — never letter the name.)
- GOGO: the grandmother — older Black South African woman, grey hair often wrapped
  in a soft headscarf, warm lined kind face, an apron or simple dress, a watering
  can. Patient, sure of the world.
- THE CRANE-FLOWER (Strelitzia reginae, true to life): a fan of bright ORANGE sepals
  like a feathered crest, a vivid BLUE petal-tongue like a throat, rising from a long
  grey-green beak-shaped sheath (spathe), beaded with small shining drops of nectar.
  It genuinely looks like a small bright bird tipping its head back to look UP.
- THE BIRD THAT WAS (memory spreads only): the SAME orange-crested, blue-throated
  bird as in the companion "King Lion" book — its crest and throat are exactly the
  orange-and-blue of the bloom. Slender, bright, glad of its wings.
- THE LIVING VELD: a tiny iridescent sunbird, drifting bees, morning wind in the
  grass, sometimes a duiker at the far fence.

THE SETTING: Gogo's garden at the edge of open grassland — red earth, aloes, a low
stone wall, marula and acacia beyond, round homes and a kraal fence in the far
distance. Specific and dignified. NEVER generic "jungle", NEVER caricature.

FORMAT: landscape 3:2 spread, full-bleed, no text in the image (text sits beneath
on the page). Compose so the FEELING lands with the sound off — especially the small
lifted face against the big sky.
