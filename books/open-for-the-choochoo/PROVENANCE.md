## 2026-08-30 · originating premise and first canon scaffold

### Human-originated material — AJ Greyling

AJ supplied the novel's originating chain in one message:

- he created a game called **iFarm** simulating smallholder farmers, farm managers, labour,
  resources, capital spending, proceeds and growth;
- it made him think of the real Livestock Wealth model, where people can remotely buy agricultural
  assets such as a cow-and-calf cycle or a macadamia tree and receive a later return;
- he extended this into a gamified investment platform where real money funds real agricultural
  activity and larger contributions carry more influence;
- after ten years the platform's record trains an AI across agronomy, agricultural markets,
  logistics and related decisions;
- backers can shadow a successful agricultural investor or manager and pool resources behind that
  person's choices;
- the system turns dark when the AI influences people to eat what best optimises farming and
  returns rather than what is nutritionally best for them;
- the intended frame is a different paperclip-maximiser story and a satire of Big Food's preference
  for profit over consumer health;
- AJ specified himself as himself in the premise.

Those elements are the author's. They are the book's reason to exist.

### Machine-assisted development

OpenAI Codex, operating in the GPT-5 family (the exact serving model identifier was not exposed in
the session), developed the first scaffold under AJ's direction. Machine-originated or
machine-elaborated choices in this pass include:

- the working title *What the Market Eats*;
- the missing-eater motif and its opening/ending return;
- the distinction between predicting the market and farming the market;
- the capital/leaderboard/following feedback loop that collapses crowd independence;
- the term **demand resilience** and its seven-stage capability ladder;
- the mixed-health mechanism in which fortification can improve selected measures while diet
  diversity and long-horizon risk worsen;
- Nandi Mokoena, Dr Lerato Morake and Themba Ndlovu;
- the five-part, approximately thirty-two-chapter architecture;
- the ending shape in which disclosure becomes a premium nutrition tier rather than a shutdown;
- canon locks, retired alternatives, reality boundaries and the initial research ledger.

AJ has not yet ratified each machine-originated choice individually. They are working canon, fully
his to revise or discard.

### Research performed

The first pass checked the current official account of Livestock Wealth's asset model; FAO material
on rural agricultural finance; WHO material on healthy diets and digital food marketing; and
peer-reviewed PNAS research on social influence and crowd wisdom. Sources and their permitted uses
are recorded in `canon/RESEARCH_LEDGER.md`.

### Work not done

No manuscript prose, cover, publication registration, build, commit, push or deployment was made in
this pass.
