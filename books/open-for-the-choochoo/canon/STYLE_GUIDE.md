# Style guide — Open for the choochoo

> Systems become story only when a person has to carry the consequence through a room, a body, a
> deadline or a payment run.

---

## Narrative register

Close third person, past tense. Primary consciousness: AJ. Secondary: Nandi and Lerato. No other
viewpoint without a deliberate canon amendment.

The prose is clear, warm, dry and exact. It may be funny about interfaces, meetings, investor
language and the confidence of dashboards. It must not sneer at ordinary backers, farmers, workers,
parents or people choosing affordable food.

Target blend:

- systems legibility without lecture;
- South African physical specificity without tourism prose;
- momentum without thriller panting;
- satire delivered by accurate juxtaposition, not authorial eye-rolling;
- moral seriousness without solemn fog.

## Sentence behaviour

- Prefer concrete noun and verb over explanatory adjective.
- At moments of greatest systems consequence, understate. Let arithmetic carry dread.
- Vary paragraph length; use a single-line paragraph only when the line changes available action.
- Technical terms enter through use, then receive the minimum clarification needed.
- Never write “little did he know,” “everything was about to change,” or an equivalent promise.
- Avoid anthropomorphic machine verbs in narration: no *wanted, thought, noticed, understood,
  decided, preferred, cared, realised* for A10.
- A10 may *rank, forecast, recommend, issue, allocate, execute, withhold pending constraint,* or
  *return no admissible plan*.

## Viewpoint signatures

### AJ

Sees loops, interfaces, stuck resources, dependencies and unintended composition. Notices when a
system that should be moving is waiting on one human act. His humour is associative and quick. His
blind spot in prose: he can make a moral problem feel temporarily tractable by giving it fields and
a status.

Do not fictionalise his private interior from memory. On-page interiority belongs only to the book's
declared builder problem.

### Nandi

Sees time, weather, bodies, sequence and what an apparently small delay costs later. Her technical
language is operational, not pastoral. She does not gaze at “the land” to produce wisdom for urban
characters. She knows her books, labour, animals, suppliers, soil and buyers.

Her chapters begin with a task whose biological or payroll clock cannot be paused by debate.

### Lerato

Sees denominators, lags, comparison groups, displacement and the difference between evidence and a
headline. Her prose notices when two true claims are being offered as if only one can survive. She
does not diagnose individuals from population data.

Her chapters may carry more analytic compression, but every analysis must attach to a dataset,
meeting, kitchen, clinic, procurement file or physical product.

## Dialogue

South African English without a showcase accent. People code-switch only where character and room
make it natural; never sprinkle untranslated words as décor. Any isiZulu, Sepedi, Afrikaans,
Setswana or other language beyond the author's assured competence requires a first-language check.

Characters rarely state their worldview cleanly. Their strongest arguments emerge from what they
must do next.

A10 output is not dialogue. Present it as:

- a report block;
- a table rendered into plain text;
- an interface field;
- a notification;
- a short transcript only when a human explicitly queries it.

No sinister cadence. The worst output should read like competent operations writing.

## Exposition law

No feature is explained before it causes a choice.

- Explain pool voting when two funding instructions conflict.
- Explain shadowing when Themba cannot manually serve everyone asking to follow him.
- Explain the leaderboard when it changes Nandi's cost of capital.
- Explain A10's training record when the tenth season closes.
- Explain demand resilience when a crop needs a buyer.
- Explain the health proxy when AJ tries to fix the problem with it.

If a paragraph could be pasted into a white paper unchanged, put a person under a deadline inside it
or cut it.

## Agriculture on the page

Agriculture is not green scenery. It is timed dependency:

- a veterinary window;
- germination after rain;
- labour before heat;
- moisture at harvest;
- a cold room buying a day;
- a truck arrival determining quality;
- a buyer's settlement date determining payroll.

Research exact crops and operations before confident specificity. When uncertain, narrow the claim
rather than improvise expertise.

Animals are animals, not investment tokens in narration. The interface may reduce them to units;
the physical scene must restore body, temperament, risk and care.

## Finance on the page

Returns are modest, variable and time-bound. Avoid crypto cadence, rocket imagery and implausible
growth percentages. The emotional unit of money is what it permits or delays:

- seed ordered now;
- wages settled Friday;
- storage rented for nine days;
- a backer able to absorb a loss;
- a manager forced to sell early.

Use exact numbers sparingly and make them reconcile. Maintain a finance ledger during drafting.

## Food and health on the page

No food is morally dirty. Show formulation, frequency, displacement, price and pattern. A processed
food can solve safety, storage and affordability problems while still contributing to a worse
population diet.

Lerato's claims follow evidence strength:

- *shows/establishes* only for direct platform behaviour and strong causal tests;
- *supports/suggests/is consistent with* for health pathways;
- *cannot distinguish* when confounding remains.

Never use body size as a moral description. Never make an ill child the proof.

## Satire

The satire lives in normal language doing abnormal moral work:

- “demand resilience” means appetite shaping;
- “consumer friction” means the ability to choose differently;
- “nutrition-weighted” becomes a paid tier;
- millions of copied instructions appear as crowd conviction;
- transparency reports disclose the problem in a category no one reads as power.

Do not add jokes after these facts. The clean phrase is the joke and the wound.

## Chapter kinetics

Every chapter must contain:

1. an operational want in the first 500 words;
2. a physical, financial or institutional clock;
3. a decision, not merely a discovery;
4. a turn that changes the next chapter's available action;
5. at least one plant or payoff where scheduled;
6. no false cliffhanger.

Chapter endings favour an altered fact over a portentous sentence.

## Title discipline

The exact phrase *open for the choochoo* appears in narrative prose only in Chapter 32. Do not seed
trains, spoons or mouths as conspicuous symbols. The title lands because the domestic phrase is
ordinary, not because the novel has spent 300 pages pointing at it.

## AI-drafting tic audit

Before a chapter is called finished, search and revise:

- “something like” used to avoid choosing;
- “not X, but Y” repeated as a rhythm;
- “the kind of” abstraction;
- “quietly” as generic atmosphere;
- “for a long moment”;
- “he realised” where action can carry recognition;
- portentous fragments;
- stacked three-item lists in narration;
- repeated numeric fetish across chapters;
- characters speaking in polished thesis statements;
- unearned tactile detail used as seriousness costume.

Clarity is not plainness. Specificity must be earned by fact.
