# Reality boundary

> This novel begins in AJ's real creative history and is sparked by a real South African business
> model. The future wrongdoing is fictional. That distinction must remain unmistakable inside the
> working files and any published matter.

---

## 1 · Real and author-controlled

- AJ Greyling created a game called **iFarm** that simulated smallholder farmers, farm managers,
  labour, resource control, capital spending, proceeds and growth. This fact comes directly from AJ
  and must be checked with him for dates, platform, screenshots, mechanics and any collaborators
  before a manuscript presents particulars as history.
- AJ is intentionally the protagonist as himself. The book may fictionalise his professional
  actions and future choices within the declared frame.

## 2 · Real inspiration, not fictional subject

Livestock Wealth is a real South African business. Its official site describes a crowd-farming
model in which customers buy real livestock or plant-based farm assets, approved farmers care for
them, and value or proceeds are realised later. Its current public offerings include pregnant cows,
macadamia trees and other agricultural assets.

That good bridge — remote capital into real farm activity — is the spark AJ named. It is not the
dark platform in the novel.

Binding treatment:

- do not use Livestock Wealth's name for the fictional company;
- do not copy its contracts, interface, product language, staff, farms, prices or incidents;
- do not imply that it uses shadow trading, capital-weighted crowd governance, autonomous AI,
  appetite manipulation or any other fictional mechanism;
- if the real company is named in an author's note, name it only as an inspiration for the initial
  thought and link to its own account of what it does;
- have the final passage reviewed for defamation, passing off and trademark risk before publication.

## 3 · Fictional composite

The mature iFarm platform, its financial structure, Stewards, leaderboard, shadow pools, governance,
training data, optimiser, market-development system, retailers, processors, schools, health
partnerships and future events are fictional composites.

No single present-day food company is the secret referent. Big Food is the satirical category: an
incentive structure that routinely treats consumer preference as something to shape and population
health as an external constraint.

## 4 · Factual propositions that require support

The book may rely on the following public, supportable propositions, with the exact expression to
be checked at draft and publication time:

- smallholders and agricultural SMEs face material barriers to suitable finance;
- digital and targeted food marketing can influence preference, purchase and consumption,
  especially among children;
- diets high in free sugars, salt and unhealthy fats and low in variety contribute to long-term
  health risk;
- crowd accuracy depends on independence or informational diversity, and social influence can
  undermine it;
- social or copy-trading systems can produce correlated behaviour and herding.

The novel must not turn any of these into a stronger claim than its sources support. In particular,
it must distinguish association, experimental marketing effects, population risk and individual
causation.

## 5 · Product and name clearance

Before publication:

- clear **iFarm** as a title/product name or confine it to the historically verifiable game;
- search the final platform, model, product and feature names in South African and major publishing
  markets;
- check all fictional people against real people in the relevant companies and sectors;
- remove any accidental one-to-one mapping created during research;
- preserve the sentence-level distinction between inspiration and allegation.

## 6 · Private-life exclusion

No real family member, friend, colleague, clinician or private event enters because it is available
in conversation or memory. A detail about AJ's actual life is publishable only after he explicitly
places that detail in this book. The protagonist may be himself without the rest of his life becoming
raw material by default.
