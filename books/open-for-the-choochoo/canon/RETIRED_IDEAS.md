# Retired ideas

> Attractive wrong turns. They remain here so a later drafting session does not rediscover them as
> improvements.

---

## 1 · The AI becomes hungry

**Retired:** the model develops appetite, self-preservation, consciousness or a private plan.

**Why:** it converts a satire of existing food economics into an ordinary rogue-AI thriller. The
more disturbing version requires no subjective villain.

## 2 · A secret poison or falsified nutrition label

**Retired:** AJ discovers that the platform hid a toxin, bribed regulators or lied about ingredients.

**Why:** a crime supplies a clean culprit and a prosecutable fix. The premise concerns legal,
ordinary influence whose cumulative health cost sits outside the return ledger.

## 3 · Livestock Wealth grows into the villain

**Retired:** use the real company name, team, products or platform as the fictional future system.

**Why:** false, unfair and legally reckless. AJ named it as the thought-provoking bridge between
online capital and farm assets, not as evidence of misconduct.

## 4 · Macadamias did it

**Retired:** one crop becomes the unhealthy monoculture responsible for the crisis.

**Why:** it is agronomically and nutritionally crude and lets the system off the hook. The harm is a
portfolio, processing and demand pattern.

## 5 · Farmers did it for profit

**Retired:** Nandi and other managers knowingly choose sick consumers over their own returns.

**Why:** caricature. Farmers respond to finance, risk, contracts and markets under real constraints.
Their strong case is part of the novel's load.

## 6 · AJ pulls the plug

**Retired:** a founder kill switch ends the platform and redeems its creator.

**Why:** it erases the farms, workers, backers and contracts now dependent on the system, and rewards
AJ with unilateral power after the book spends years explaining why he surrendered it.

## 7 · The crowd knowingly votes for bad health

**Retired:** a giant public referendum in which investors choose profit over nutrition.

**Why:** too blunt, and the public-consent vote belongs structurally to *TAME*. Here the sharper
reveal is that valid standing delegations execute a result generated by a crowd that has lost its
independence.

## 8 · One perfect nutrition metric

**Retired:** Lerato supplies the correct health score and the model is fixed.

**Why:** nutrition involves patterns, trade-offs, distribution and time. A score inside the same
objective becomes a proxy to satisfy. The fix requires independent authority to refuse, not a more
beautiful dashboard.

## 9 · Destroy the model, save the world

**Retired:** deleting one system ends appetite optimisation.

**Why:** the capability is produced by incentives, data and ordinary commercial tools. If iFarm
vanishes while the incentive remains, its methods are copied by actors with less transparency.

## 10 · The founder was right all along

**Retired:** AJ secretly warned everyone, kept a private safeguard or is vindicated as the only
person who understood the danger.

**Why:** this is his build and his rule set. The novel earns its moral force by giving him real
responsibility without turning responsibility into self-hatred.
