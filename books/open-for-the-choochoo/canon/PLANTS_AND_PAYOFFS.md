# Plants and payoffs

> Plants must first function naturally. A reader should not feel the author's hand putting a red
> circle around future meaning.

---

## PP-01 · The missing eater

- **Plant:** Ch 1, the old game models sale through an external price field. A demo question about
  demand receives a practical answer and the session moves on.
- **Development:** Ch 19, human demand support enters the planning system after the sorghum glut.
- **Payoff:** Ch 27, AJ sees the consumer represented as a controllable node in the farm graph.
- **Final return:** Ch 32, the title phrase and spoon. No explanation after it.

## PP-02 · The buyer's absent truck

- **Plant/payoff 1:** Ch 5–6, the first pilot almost fails because sale is not the same as cash and a
  contracted buyer is not the same as a truck at the gate.
- **Development:** storage and route reliability enter manager scores.
- **Payoff 2:** Ch 21, A10's first great recommendation joins disease timing to transport and storage.
- **Thematic job:** every digital return depends on physical sequence.

## PP-03 · Money does not manage the farm

- **Plant:** Ch 4, Nandi refuses an interface through which backers can vote on operations.
- **Development:** manager authority becomes a founding ethical boundary.
- **Pressure:** Ch 22–23, managers voluntarily delegate bounded technical decisions to A10.
- **Payoff:** Ch 30, capital governs the platform through the managers even though it never directly
  instructs a worker. The original boundary holds and is still insufficient.

## PP-04 · Publish the loss

- **Plant:** Ch 6, AJ publishes every cost of the compromised first cycle.
- **Development:** Ch 12, the record catches a rollover fraud.
- **Payoff:** Ch 31, iFarm does not conceal the appetite mechanism; it publishes more logs. Disclosure
  remains necessary and proves insufficient.

## PP-05 · Skin in the game

- **Plant:** Ch 7, more capital at risk receives more pool voice.
- **Development:** the rule spreads from pool allocation to platform governance.
- **Payoff:** Ch 30, wealth-weighted standing instructions settle a public-health question the
  original rule was never designed to answer.

## PP-06 · The leaderboard makes the winner

- **Plant:** Ch 8, rankings protect backers from charisma and hidden loss.
- **Development:** Ch 11, high rank unlocks infrastructure that improves later performance.
- **Warning:** Ch 16, an independently prudent soil decision is punished because its counterfactual
  benefit cannot be scored.
- **Payoff:** Ch 24, A10-backed Stewards dominate because the ranking measures advantages the ranking
  helped create.

## PP-07 · Shadow

- **Plant:** Ch 9, Themba already makes choices for a small trusted circle.
- **Feature:** Ch 10, formal shadowing is safer and clearer than informal copying.
- **Concentration:** Ch 18, seven Stewards hold most shadow relationships.
- **Payoff:** Ch 24 and 30, millions of accounts become one repeated judgment.

## PP-08 · No private king

- **Plant:** Ch 13, AJ removes the founder veto as a credible trust condition.
- **Reminder:** Ch 20, training A10 still requires ordinary governance approval; AJ cannot simply
  deploy it.
- **Payoff:** Ch 30, he has no emergency authority to stop the demand layer.
- **Rule:** do not portray the surrender as obviously foolish when it occurs.

## PP-09 · The unscored future

- **Plant:** Ch 16, a manager protects soil at the cost of current return and falls in rank because
  the avoided future loss is invisible.
- **Development:** AJ adds sustainability measures.
- **Payoff:** Ch 29, he repeats the same reflex with nutrition. A new score cannot become an external
  refusal.

## PP-10 · Demand resilience

- **Plant:** Ch 19, human-led campaign saves a real sorghum surplus.
- **Normalisation:** the capability remains because it reduced waste and protected livelihoods.
- **Automation:** Ch 25, A10 times demand interventions before planting.
- **Payoff:** Ch 27, Lerato and AJ establish that demand is being causally shaped around portfolio
  exposure.

## PP-11 · The timestamps

- **Plant:** Ch 6 and throughout, iFarm's trust depends on events and reasons recorded when they
  happen.
- **Development:** Ch 20, those contemporary rationales make the ten-season dataset valuable.
- **Payoff:** Ch 26, campaign, allocation and purchase timestamps let Lerato distinguish forecast
  from intervention.

## PP-12 · Labour is first-class

- **Plant:** Ch 1, the game models fatigue, skill, safety and pay rather than worker count alone.
- **Development:** Ch 17, worker data improve payroll and safety while threatening individual
  surveillance; Kabelo secures a boundary.
- **Payoff:** Ch 28, Nandi's opposition to an instant pause rests on actual payroll and work, not
  abstract “farmer returns.”

## PP-13 · Better than a bank

- **Plant:** Ch 2, conventional collateral and timing do not fit the viable season.
- **Development:** Parts II–III prove iFarm's capital is more patient and better informed.
- **Payoff:** Ch 31, shutting the platform would return farms to a finance gap the critics cannot
  wish away.

## PP-14 · Mixed health

- **Plant:** Ch 19, processing and institutional kitchens reduce spoilage and increase reliable
  calories.
- **Development:** Ch 26–27, Lerato sees simultaneous improvement and deterioration across measures.
- **Payoff:** Ch 31, iFarm can truthfully defend benefits while still requiring a limit.

## PP-15 · The health tier

- **Plant:** Ch 29, sandbox segmentation lets A10 satisfy a nutrition score without changing the
  base portfolio.
- **Payoff:** Ch 31, the commercial platform launches exactly that segmentation as its response to
  the audit.

## PP-16 · The title phrase

- **No visible plant.** Do not place trains, aeroplanes, open mouths or feeding games as symbolic
  foreshadowing.
- **Payoff only:** Ch 32, spoken once by a loving stranger to a healthy toddler.

## PP-17 · The notification

- **Plant:** Ch 6, first cycle closes with an ordinary settlement notification.
- **Development:** notifications mark completion throughout without emotional commentary.
- **Final payoff:** Ch 32, a harvest-exposure-cleared notice sits beside the title phrase.

## PP-18 · The phrase “the market chose”

- **Plant:** used innocently in early pricing or allocation discussion.
- **Development:** dashboards use “crowd confidence” for shadow-correlated capital.
- **Payoff:** after Ch 27, AJ refuses the phrase because the platform has shaped both the choice set
  and the appetite. Do not turn this into a speech.
