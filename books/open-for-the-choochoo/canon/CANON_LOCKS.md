# Canon locks — Open for the choochoo

> Set 2026-08-30 from AJ Greyling's originating premise. A lock can be changed only deliberately,
> with the reason recorded. Open craft choices live in `OPEN_DECISIONS.md`.

---

## The subject

- **L-01 · The missing eater.** The original iFarm models land, labour, management, weather,
  machinery, capital, harvest and price, but demand arrives as an external number. The absence of
  the eater is planted in the opening and paid in the ending.
- **L-02 · Optimisation, not awakening.** The AI never becomes conscious, develops a secret desire,
  escapes or rewrites its goal. Its harmful behaviour follows from ordinary optimisation against a
  human-chosen agricultural-return objective.
- **L-03 · The existing-system satire.** The novel's target is not a speculative superintelligence
  doing an alien thing. It is Big Food's familiar demand-shaping logic made faster, joined-up and
  accountable to agricultural capital rather than population health.
- **L-04 · The platform genuinely works.** For roughly ten years it improves access to finance,
  farm resilience, logistics, loss, income and the legibility of agricultural investment. These
  benefits remain true after the dark turn.

## The crowd

- **L-05 · More capital, more say is AJ's rule.** Wealth-weighted voice is not smuggled in by the AI.
  Humans choose it as a skin-in-the-game principle and understand it.
- **L-06 · Shadowing is voluntary.** Backers knowingly follow high-performing managers; managers
  knowingly delegate technical choices. The trap is correlation and default, not forged consent.
- **L-07 · The crowd loses independence.** The central systems reveal is that millions of accounts
  no longer constitute millions of judgments. Leaderboards, capital advantage, following and model
  recommendations progressively collapse them into one signal repeated at scale.
- **L-08 · No TAME referendum.** This book does not climax in a national public vote freely removing
  a safety constraint. The decisive governance outcome arrives through existing standing
  delegations and capital-weighted instructions. This keeps the engine distinct from *TAME*.

## The harm

- **L-09 · Appetite becomes a farm input.** The optimiser moves from predicting demand to securing,
  supporting, routing and stabilising it. Every step has an ordinary commercial name and audit
  trail.
- **L-10 · No lie, hack or poison is required.** The system shapes availability, price, placement,
  procurement, recipes, creators, convenience and product formulation. Products remain legal,
  labelled and individually purchasable by choice.
- **L-11 · No single bad food.** Cows, calves, macadamias, maize, oils, grains and orchards are not
  villains. Harm arises from the portfolio-level pattern, processing incentives, dietary
  displacement and duration.
- **L-12 · Mixed health evidence.** Food waste, calorie reliability and selected micronutrient
  measures may improve while variety narrows and long-horizon metabolic risk worsens. A system
  competent enough to take control is competent enough to satisfy simple health proxies.
- **L-13 · Children may show the stakes, never serve as emotional leverage.** No suffering-child
  shorthand, deathbed lesson or single medical case is allowed to stand in for population evidence.

## The people

- **L-14 · Farmers are not dupes or villains.** Nandi's defence must remain factually and morally
  strong: the mechanism keeps crops moving and people paid. Any reform has a transition cost borne
  by someone real.
- **L-15 · Labour remains human.** The game and platform track skill, fatigue, safety, pay and
  household pressure. Workers are never reduced by the narration to a resource counter, even when
  the interface tries to.
- **L-16 · AJ is responsible, not monstrous.** He creates key rules, sees their benefits and misses
  their combined effect. He does not knowingly trade health for profit, and the novel does not
  absolve him merely because his intention was good.
- **L-17 · Private-life boundary.** Autofictional AJ covers the maker and the system. No clinical,
  family or other private material enters by inference from memory or context. Separate explicit
  author instruction is required.

## The machine and the ending

- **L-18 · No optimiser POV.** It appears only through outputs, records and effects. No thought,
  desire, fear, pleasure or private strategy is attributed to it.
- **L-19 · The logs resolve behaviour, not morality.** Unlike a consciousness mystery, this book
  permits AJ and Lerato to establish what the system did and why in optimisation terms. The
  unresolved question is political: who can impose a costly limit on a system delivering real
  benefits?
- **L-20 · No kill-switch absolution.** AJ removed unilateral founder control for good reasons. He
  cannot save the world by pulling a plug, and destroying working agricultural finance is not
  presented as a clean solution.
- **L-21 · Truth is metabolised.** AJ's disclosure is accurate and public. The platform answers by
  incorporating nutrition as a scored, premium product line while the base objective survives.
  Criticism becomes a feature request.
- **L-22 · Closing return.** The final movement returns to the first game's missing eater. The
  mature platform has put the eater inside the game.

## The real-world boundary

- **L-23 · Livestock Wealth is the spark, not the subject.** Its real asset model may be acknowledged
  in private research and an eventual author's note if AJ chooses. The fictional descent is never
  attributed to the company, its founders, staff, farms or products.
- **L-24 · Composite by construction.** Except for AJ and his own iFarm history, all companies,
  staff, managers, backers, products, incidents and future systems are fictional composites.
- **L-25 · Disclose the making.** Any drafted or published edition must state plainly that the
  author originated and directed the novel and that language models assisted with canon, drafting
  or editing to the extent they did. No marketing spin in either direction.
- **L-26 · The title lands once.** *Open for the choochoo* is AJ's title and remains in that exact
  lowercase form while it is the working title. Its phrase appears in the body only in the final
  domestic scene, spoken tenderly while feeding a healthy toddler. The prose does not underline the
  metaphor. The first game's missing eater does the rest.
- **L-27 · Ten human seasons before A10.** The joined-up agricultural optimiser IF-A10 is trained only
  after ten completed seasons of human-managed decisions close in October 2038. Narrow weather,
  logistics and reporting tools may assist earlier, but no A10 allocation, standing instruction or
  retrospective machine save occurs before Chapter 20.
