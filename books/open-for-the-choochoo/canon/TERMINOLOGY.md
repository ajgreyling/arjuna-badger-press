# Terminology — Open for the choochoo

> Use consistently. Fictional product terms stay ordinary enough to be plausible and distinct
> enough not to key to a real platform.

---

## iFarm

Both AJ's original management game and, later, the real-money agricultural platform derived from
it. Context should distinguish them; use *the old game* where ambiguity remains.

## plan

A manager's proposed agricultural cycle: activity, inputs, labour, timing, risks, constraints,
expected distribution and exit. Backers fund a plan; they do not buy a promise of return.

## pool

Ring-fenced capital committed to one plan or a defined set of plans. A pool has its own constraints
and settlement order.

## position

A backer's contractual participation in a pool. Avoid “share” unless the legal instrument is
actually equity in that scene.

## manager

The accredited person or entity with operational authority over a funded farm plan. Backers choose
managers; they do not become managers by supplying capital.

## Steward

iFarm's public designation for a person whose future allocation settings other backers may shadow.
A Steward may be a farm manager or an investor/allocator. Capitalised only as the formal platform
role.

## shadow / shadowing

Voluntary delegation that copies a Steward's future allocation instructions within user-set limits.
Verb preferred over *copy trade* in the product. Existing contracts are not reversed when a shadow
is cancelled.

## standing instruction

A pre-authorised rule that may execute without a new click when declared conditions are met.
Standing instructions are the mechanism through which the governance outcome exists before the
meeting.

## settled return

Money actually distributed after the agricultural cycle, costs, losses and contractual priority.
Distinguish from projected return, marked value and gross sale.

## manager record / scorecard

The longitudinal, public record of settled outcomes plus risk, loss, labour, safety and delivery
measures. *Leaderboard* is colloquial and increasingly critical; the platform prefers *manager
record*.

## crowd confidence

iFarm's displayed measure of capital aligned behind a plan or policy. It eventually counts shadowed
capital without adequately distinguishing independent instructions from copied ones.

## demand resilience

The platform category for reducing the risk that a funded harvest lacks sufficient buyers at an
acceptable time and price. Begins with offtake, storage, processing and market support. Later covers
targeted appetite shaping. Never capitalised.

## market development

The ordinary budget and organisational category under which recipes, tastings, placement,
procurement pilots, processor support and promotion sit. Not synonymous with demand resilience, but
one of its tools.

## IF-A10 / A10

The agricultural optimiser trained after ten completed human-managed seasons. Formal report name:
`IF-A10`. Spoken shorthand: *A-ten*. No human nickname.

## constraint

A condition A10 may not trade away in pursuit of return. Distinguish from:

- **objective:** what the system improves;
- **measure:** what it reports;
- **preference:** what it may trade against other preferences;
- **constraint:** what can make it return no admissible plan.

This distinction is the technical core of AJ and Lerato's Part V disagreement.

## override

A human manager rejects or replaces an A10 recommendation. Overrides require a reason for audit but
are not automatically punished. The performance system indirectly makes unsupported overrides
costly.

## health-weighted mode

Placeholder term for the post-audit tier with external dietetic oversight, greater dietary variety
and stricter processing constraints. Do not lock a consumer brand before clearance.

## eater

Canon/thematic term, not product language. Characters normally say *consumer, household, buyer,
resident, pupil, patient* or *person*. AJ's recognition that the model has found the eater is kept
for the final movement.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- *AI farmer* — A10 does not farm.
- *the algorithm* as a universal mystical subject — name the actual ranking, forecast or optimiser.
- *guaranteed return* — not used unless a character is making a legally significant false claim.
- *democratised* without naming what power or access changed.
- *the crowd decided* when the capital was shadowed.
- *healthy/unhealthy food* as a total binary when the claim concerns pattern, formulation or
  frequency.
