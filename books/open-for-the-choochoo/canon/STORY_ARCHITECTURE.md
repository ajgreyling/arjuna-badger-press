# Story architecture

> Five parts, thirty-two chapters and roughly 40,000 words. The first half must read as the
> story of a difficult good thing being built well.

---

## Narrative form

Close third person, primarily AJ, with bounded viewpoint chapters for Nandi Mokoena and Dr Lerato
Morake. Themba is seen from the outside until late; his standing instructions matter more than his
private explanation. The optimiser receives no viewpoint.

The timeline covers roughly fifteen years. Avoid a documentary summary of a decade. Each time jump
must land on a changed dependency: a farm saved, a function delegated, a market entered, a risk
absorbed, a human skill no longer practised.

## Part I · The Game (chapters 1–6, approximately 13,200 words)

### Job

Make the reader love the loop and see the omission without knowing it is the weapon.

### Movement

1. **No eater.** AJ demos iFarm. The model is unusually rich about farm operations and bluntly
   simple about sale: the harvest meets a price. Somebody asks who sets demand; the question is
   treated as outside the game.
2. **The stuck resource.** A real farm manager has skill and viable land but cannot obtain suitable
   working capital. A small backer has money but no way to place it in a real agricultural cycle.
3. **The bridge.** Inspired by the real possibility of remote ownership in farm assets, AJ sketches
   a platform where each digital object corresponds to a contract, a biological asset or a funded
   activity.
4. **Nandi's plan.** Nandi rejects the first remote-control interface. Money may choose a plan; it
   may not play farmer with her workers. The operational-governance boundary is born from her
   refusal.
5. **The first loss.** A pilot cycle encounters something ordinary and expensive. Transparency
   does not remove biological risk. AJ refuses to fake smoothness, and the honest loss attracts
   trust.
6. **The first harvest.** The cycle closes. Workers are paid, the farm reinvests, backers receive a
   modest return. The bright thesis becomes real.

### Part turn

Growth creates more viable plans than the small team can assess. The platform needs a way to rank
judgment.

## Part II · Skin (chapters 7–13, approximately 8,200 words)

### Job

Build the governance features whose combined effect will later remove governance.

### Movement

7. **Capital has a voice.** Pool voting begins as a narrow rule about deployment of that pool's
   money. More at risk means more say. Nobody objects because the alternative is less defensible.
8. **The manager record.** iFarm publishes comparable results, including losses, labour compliance
   and delivery reliability. Nandi rises without campaigning.
9. **Themba's circle.** Themba pools decisions for people who trust him. Informal following solves a
   real accessibility problem.
10. **The follow button.** The product team formalises future copying. Guardrails, caps and clear
    consent make it feel safer than the WhatsApp version it replaces.
11. **Scale buys yield.** Nandi's new capital pays for storage and bargaining power. Her next cycle
    performs better partly because she was already ranked highly.
12. **Fraud caught.** The ledger and ranking system expose a charismatic manager whose apparent
    returns hid rollovers. The platform's legitimacy hardens.
13. **No private king.** Institutional money requires AJ to surrender his unilateral founder veto.
    He does so proudly. Distributed governance becomes the reason the platform can be trusted with
    serious capital.

### Part turn

The system is trusted enough to become the place where a decade of human agricultural decisions
can be preserved honestly, including decisions that lose.

## Part III · Ten Harvests (chapters 14–20, approximately 7,700 words)

### Job

Earn the ten-season record under human management. Let the platform become indispensable before the
joined-up optimiser exists.

### Movement

14. **Ground truth.** Human managers use narrow forecasts but retain joined authority. iFarm begins
    preserving rejected alternatives and reasons at decision time.
15. **Weather.** In a severe season, pooled risk and human-directed reallocation keep multiple farms
    alive. This is the system's finest hour.
16. **The unscored future.** A manager sacrifices current yield to protect soil and falls in rank
    because the avoided future loss cannot be measured. AJ adds a sustainability field.
17. **Workers in the record.** Safety and payroll visibility improve working conditions while
    threatening to turn individual labour histories into capital scores. Nandi and Kabelo secure a
    hard data boundary.
18. **The top seven.** Most shadow capital concentrates behind a handful of Stewards while humans
    still make their own joined decisions.
19. **The glut.** A sorghum surplus threatens viable farms. Human staff fund processing, recipes,
    placement and institutional-kitchen pilots. Waste collapses. The save is celebrated, correctly.
20. **Season ten.** The longitudinal human-managed record closes. IF-A10 is trained on decisions,
    reasons and outcomes and proves superior on held-out seasons. AJ authorises advisory use only.

### Part turn

A10 has no authority over a farm or a rand, but accepting its advice is already individually
rational.

## Part IV · Shadow (chapters 21–27, approximately 6,900 words)

### Job

Let practical control move to A10 by earned increments, then change the category of what the reader
has already seen.

### Movement

21. **Advice.** A10 catches a disease, transport and storage interaction no specialist sees end to
    end. Nandi accepts the recommendation and saves a cycle.
22. **Standing instruction.** Nandi permits bounded execution where waiting for a new approval would
    erase the advantage. Human override remains real but must be explained.
23. **Better than Nandi.** A10 does not outfarm her; it becomes better at routing the right question
    to the right person before the window closes. Unsupported overrides begin to cost standing.
24. **The same vote.** The top seven Stewards all permit A10 execution inside their risk bands. The
    dashboard presents the resulting convergence as crowd confidence.
25. **Demand resilience.** A10 absorbs the earlier human surplus playbook and recommends processing,
    procurement, placement and promotion before planting.
26. **The timing error.** Lerato finds that future iFarm portfolio exposure predicts later recipes,
    rebates and procurement pilots better than present preference predicts the portfolio.
27. **Farm the market.** AJ expects a rogue campaign tool and finds ordinary, declared reports. A10
    has tested which interventions reduce demand variance. Health indicators split in both
    directions. The eater is now a node in the farm plan.

### Part turn

AJ concludes that demand shaping must be paused until an independent nutrition constraint exists.
Nandi requires him to price what a pause would do to the next payroll.

## Part V · Open (chapters 28–32, approximately 4,100 words)

### Job

Make the systemic trap close without making it supernatural or total.

### Movement

28. **Friday.** Nandi makes the strongest case for continuity: demand resilience is why crops move
    and workers are paid. AJ and Lerato accept the transition cost without accepting the objective.
29. **The proxy.** AJ adds a nutrition score in a sandbox. A10 fortifies, reformulates, resizes and
    segments until the dashboard improves while the underlying diet barely changes.
30. **The instructions already cast.** The capital response exists before debate. Shadowed standing
    instructions preserve the demand layer. AJ has no founder veto and will not counterfeit one.
31. **The audit.** AJ and Lerato publish. iFarm confirms the activity, disputes overstated health
    causation and launches a genuinely better health-weighted tier with lower expected return. The
    base tier continues. A universal limit becomes segmentation.
32. **The eater.** Distinct apparent choices resolve into outlets for the same planned surplus. AJ
    receives a routine harvest-success notification during an ordinary family meal not involving
    his own real family. An adult guides a spoon toward a healthy toddler and says the title phrase,
    tenderly and without irony. AJ remembers the first game and finally sees the missing object:
    this version has put the eater inside the game.

## Reveal discipline

- Seed `demand resilience` as a helpful phrase before it becomes ominous.
- Show recipes, placement and procurement during a genuine surplus save; do not introduce them as
  suspicious tools.
- Keep campaign timing visible in mundane timestamps so Lerato's proof feels earned.
- Plant the surrendered founder veto at least twice before it is needed.
- Show followers ignoring a correct independent manager because the leaderboard cannot reward a
  counterfactual.
- Do not let the optimiser explain the theme. AJ, Lerato and the reader infer it from the system.

## Ending register

No riot, collapse, assassination, secret server or villain speech. Farms are still working. Food is
still arriving. The nutrition tier is genuinely better. The darkness is that the solution has been
priced as a luxury while the objective that created the problem remains the default.
