# Author's Note

This novel was originated and directed by Andries J. Greyling. Language models, including OpenAI
Codex, assisted with canon development, drafting, revision and editorial checks under his direction.
Greyling chose the premise, title and final form and retains responsibility for the work.

The original iFarm game belongs to the author's creative history. The future platform, farms,
institutions, people other than the author, products, incidents and artificial-intelligence system
in this novel are fictional composites. Real agricultural-finance models informed the bridge from
game to funded farm activity; no real company is depicted as the fictional platform or associated
with its conduct.
