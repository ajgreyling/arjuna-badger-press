# MYTHOS_RULES — *The Spaces Between* (Nan Madol / Pohnpei)

> The binding craft-and-truth law for this book. Parents: SERIES_BIBLE §0.1, §2, §3, §8; the fact-floor `…/books/nan-madol/canon/SITE_DOSSIER.md` (+ series §D.11, governs where they touch). **This is not *History Before Time*: no Order, no gold thread, no mythos that is "true in-world." The central claim is the *opposite* — the maybe stays open forever; the narrator never confirms or denies it.**

---

## 1. The checkable-thing-is-real rule (the fact-floor binds)

Every "hole" the book leans on is a **genuine, cited, real-world unresolved or strange fact.** Every astonishing beat in the prose traces to a `[FACT]` row in the dossier. The load-bearing reals for this book:

- **~750,000 tonnes** of basalt; **~1,850 t/yr** over **~4 centuries**; a single **~50-tonne cornerstone**; columns **5–25 t**; **Nandauwas** walls **5.5–7.5 m**; ~92 islets; core ~1.5 × 0.5 km. *(Arithmetic straight — the cold-eyed measure, never false precision; an order-of-magnitude truth.)*
- **Naturally prismatic columnar basalt** (columnar jointing — nature pre-cut the blocks), stacked **dry, header-and-stretcher, log-cabin style** on **coral fill**.
- **Megalithic phase from ~AD 1180** (radiocarbon) — **medieval**; Saudeleur capital; overthrown by **Isokelekel** around the early 17th century.
- Basalt quarried **~25 miles** away (Sokehs side); **no documented transport method**; **replication has failed** ("not yet reproduced," *not* "physically impossible"); columns on the seabed (**U. Oregon, 1988–89**) → **lost cargo**.
- **No fresh water, no food** on-site — uninhabitable without constant canoe resupply.

**`[DO-NOT-STATE]` (the narrator must never assert):** the seabed columns as a drowned structure; the dragon as literal; any specific transport/lift method as proven; any "lost civilisation older than the dynasty"; any single exact year for the Saudeleur fall (write **"around the early 17th century" / "~1628"** as a range). **Fringe stays clearly labelled fringe; a *character* may over-reach (a flaw to dramatise), the narrator's verdict never does.**

## 2. The potato engine (show how hard the "easy" story is)

The series engine: the brightest people, in daylight, with the best kit and a plan, make **a potato**; the real thing stands flawless and silent. Here the potato is **literal and cited** — *experimental archaeology has tried to reproduce the quarry-to-wall feat and fallen short.* Most chapters carry a potato: the moment the tidy story visibly strains.

- **The two holes that feed the engine:** (1) **HOW** — no proven transport-and-lift; rafting up to ~50 t over 25 miles of open water, then lifting columns into 7.5 m mortarless walls on a reef with no crane, no ramp, nothing left behind. (2) **WHY-HERE** — a stone metropolis on a foodless, waterless reef. *It is not only how did they lift it; it is why drag three-quarters of a million tonnes across the sea to a place that could keep no one alive.*
- **The honest framing:** the residue is **mainstream-acknowledged and genuinely open** — a logistics-and-provisioning gap the experts themselves leave open, *not* a claim against the textbook. State it calmly, cold-eyed; never reach past it toward aliens; never sneer it away.

## 3. Wonder, not conspiracy (the macro-structure)

The propulsion is **a chase with a clean-handed adversary** — a thing the crew races to **READ** before a well-funded, plausible-deniability outfit gets there to **TAKE** it. The adversary is **greed/ideology — a fallible human with clean hands, NEVER an omnipotent cabal.** Here the thing being raced for is **not a buried object but a withheld answer**, and the adversary weaponises the official story: *"even the islanders say they don't know"* is the best cover a land grab ever had. **The chase keeps the rabbit-hole propulsive; the wonder does its slow work underneath.**

Forbidden: the omnipotent cabal; the cover-up that explains everything; any structure that asks the reader to *believe* rather than to *sit in the maybe.*

## 4. The §3 firewall, kept in spirit (the crew own the TELLING, never the PLACE) — SHARPEST IN THE LINE

This is **the book that most tests** "crew owns the telling, never the place," and the firewall is kept by four binding rules:

1. **The local out-reads the crew on the thing that matters.** The custodian out-reads them not on the arithmetic (the crew can do tonnages) but on **what the city is, what the silence is keeping, and who has the right to open it.** The crew read the *pattern* and the *logistics*; the custodian reads the *place.*
2. **The crew never holds more of the camera than the local.** The win is the custodian's or shared. Edith (the LENS) makes this literal — she is the camera the line keeps asking to lower, and here the consent-to-photograph protocol is live and recurring.
3. **The place is not a puzzle-box for the band's growth.** Nan Madol is a real place with living people near it — **and here it is a grave.** The crew's job is to **witness and protect**, never to solve. The wonder serves **Pohnpei and its people.**
4. **Jakobus still defers.** He reads the made thing into the custodian's waiting hand and lets the custodian decide what the wall is *for.*

> **The fail test (binding):** if a draft reads as "clever outsiders crack the natives' mystery," it has **failed the line and must be rebuilt** (Bible §3).

**The dragon is the cleanest application:** the Olisihpa/Olosohpa-and-the-dragon tradition is the **Pohnpeians' own true account** that the doing looked impossible — **not woo to be debunked, not aliens to be reached for, and not the crew's to decode.** It is the custodian's to tell. The ancient-aliens "spaceship" is **an outsider's theft of it** — a theft the book *notices* (Frik names it), never commits.

## 5. The designated mysteries (stay open — the maybe never resolves in narration)

The narrator **never confirms or denies** the following; the prose holds them open to the last page:

- **HOW** the ~50-tonne cornerstone (and the rest) was rafted and lifted. The precise rig stays, honestly, **a gap.** The grounded *lens* (Pacific wayfinding / seamanship — *the sea was the road*) is offered as a **lens, never an asserted method** (`[ADDED — NEEDS SOURCE]` for any specific claim).
- **WHAT IT IS FOR** in full. The crew reach a **function** (a city built *apart*, for the dead and the god-kings; the foodless siting as the point, the resupply as devotion) — **but the meaning is the custodian's to give, and the narrator never declares it proven.** What the custodian withholds stays withheld, by right.
- **Whether the dragon "flew."** Never adjudicated. It is the islanders' memory; it is theirs.
- **The season-spine whisper** (light, reverse-payoff, never required): an earlier survey quietly bought up, a record gone missing, the resort's options dated suspiciously early — the *someone-got-here-first* tell that rhymes Newark's shared unit and Marree Man's American-English clues. **Plant it; never confirm it's one hand.** The fish wink at season scale.

**The unique twist for this book:** the *maybe* the reader is left holding is **not "did a dragon fly"** — it is **"are some things nobody's to open?"** The gift the book protects is the reader's right to say *maybe* to *that.*

## 6. Real places, honestly (the dignity floor + the open record)

- **Nan Madol is living, restricted, sacred, permission-bound ground** with living custodians. The depicted *Nahnmwarki* protocols, consent-to-enter / consent-to-photograph customs, the local lead's identity, and any rendering of the Olisihpa/Olosohpa tradition are **PROVISIONAL pending a Pohnpeian / FSM in-culture read — FLOOR, not nicety.**
- **The ND rendering (Mira)** is bound by the **lived-experience / Deaf-culture veto — FLOOR.** Humanised, never caricatured; reads *that* a thing is held, never its content; no savant magic, no inspiration-poster.
- **Back matter separates real / debated / invented** (see PLOT §back-matter) and points the reader at the **open record** — the real radiocarbon, the real Oregon survey, the real failed replications — and at the **living custodians' right** to the rest. *Go and check; the record is open.*
- **The five forbidden registers (failure states):** credulous drone, smug sneer, woo, inspiration-poster, exotic-gaze. On living sacred ground the last two are the live dangers.

## 7. This book's specifics (the rules that bind the prose, in one place)

- **The two-silences rule:** the book turns on **a silence that protects vs a silence that erases.** They must look identical on the page and mean opposite things; **Mira is the only one who tells them apart**, and she does it by reading *that* a thing is held, never by extracting it.
- **The hands-off discipline is dramatised twice:** Mira leaves the silence shut; Edith lowers the camera. Neither act is captioned; both are the theme in action.
- **The arithmetic is the cold eye, not the awe** — Naila/Yusuf-style measure (Mira's book, but the potato-prover function still runs): tonnages stated flat, so the wonder lands in the *gap*, not in adjectives.
- **The dragon belongs to the custodian; the spaceship is the theft.** Frik names the theft; the narrator never confirms the dragon and never sneers at it.
- **The answer is handed, never seized.** Jakobus reads *how it stands* and hands the *what-it-is-for* to the custodian; the custodian decides what to give. The climax is the custodian's beat.
