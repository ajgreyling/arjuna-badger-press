## THE ND LEAD — Mira (the UNSPOKEN: reads the body, reads the withholding)

**Layer:** the truth on the body — what the face says when the mouth lies, or won't speak at all.
**Neurology / marginality:** Deaf / hard-of-hearing (capital-D culturally-Deaf vs late-deafened to be set by the Deaf-culture read — it changes the whole character). A **lifelong lip-and-body reader by necessity** — a life spent in hearing rooms watching faces for the truth the words wouldn't carry.

**Why she leads THIS book (the perfect fit):** on an island where the official "we don't know" is doing double duty as *what the elders will not say to outsiders*, the load-bearing competence is the one who **reads the body, not the mouth.** She sees, before anyone, that the custodian's "we don't know" is **not ignorance but a door held shut** — and, crucially, **she does not pick the lock.** Her gift is to **recognise the silence**, name it to the crew gently, and *honour* it. *The unheard recognising the unheard*, and choosing, for once, to let a thing stay unsaid.

**Wiring-not-wound (INVIOLABLE):** her reading is **how she sees**, never a deficit to be fixed and never magic. **She reads *that* something is held, never the *content* of it** — the content is the custodian's to give. No savant face-magic; no telepathy; her competence is **attention**, the kind you build over a lifetime of being given no other channel.

**The cost (real, never sanitised):** the exhaustion of reading every room because she was never handed the words; the daily harm of being talked over, spoken for, left out of the fast crosstalk — *the exact harm the book indicts, lived by a crew member.* The crew signs (badly, lovingly, improving); Jakobus, who reads people by body anyway, is oddly fluent with her.

**Arc (rhymes the whole book, dramatised never declared):** the woman the world talked over, on ground where the kept silence is sacred, living the difference between **a silence imposed** (her own life) and **a silence chosen** (the custodian's right). Her want is not to be healed and not to crack the place; over the book she stops needing to *know* and learns to *witness*. The narrator never names this; it lands because she leaves the door shut.

**Voice:** spare, exact, dry. Notices the tell three beats before the others; says less than she sees. The warmth is sideways and real.

## THE LENS — Edith (ex-conflict photographer, PTSD — recurs every book; NOT the ND constant)

**Layer:** what gets seen, and at whose cost — the frame, the consent, the difference between a witness and a thief.

**Why load-bearing here:** the whole subject is **how a sacred grave is framed.** She reads **the image** — the gulf between the photo that *documents* a wall and the photo that *sensationalises* it into a tabloid **"ALIEN CITY OF THE PACIFIC"** or flatters it into a brochure's **"untouched paradise resort."** On Nan Madol the **consent-to-photograph and consent-to-enter** discipline is **live, recurring, and literal**: the protocol the *Nahnmwarki* order requires *is* the discipline her whole arc has been trying to learn.

**Wound (her engine, binding):** *beauty as absolution* — after a career photographing the worst of people, photographing *beautiful* people feels like redemption, like the camera could finally do something clean. **The trap the book turns:** the people and the place she points the lens at are nobody's absolution and do not want to be the pretty image that lets a Westerner feel forgiven. Her standing arc — *witness without consuming; lower the camera; ask, and mean it, before you shoot; sometimes do not take the photo that would make your name and cost them theirs* — is, in this book, the protocol made flesh.

**Cost (never thriller-spice):** the images that won't leave; the one she did take that haunts her; steady in a crisis *because* of what she's seen, undone quietly by ordinary beauty. PTSD rendered from the inside, with dignity. *(Lived-experience reader for the trauma; in-culture authority on how this people consents to being photographed — both FLOOR.)*

**Pairing (the book's twin disciplines of restraint):** **Mira leaves the silence shut; Edith lowers the camera.** One does not pick the lock; the other does not take the shot. They are the book's two halves of *keeping your hands off.*

**Voice:** watchful, economical, allergic to the easy frame. Names the cheap shot before anyone reaches for it.

## THE MONEY / FIXER — Yusuf (reads the GRIFT; the adversary's mirror)

**Layer:** who profits, and how the cash actually moves.

**Role:** leads the read on the **foreign "heritage resort" extraction play** — the term sheet, the shell foundation, the "cultural-tourism partnership" that is a land grab in a linen suit, the fistful of options dated suspiciously early. **He reads it because he used to run grifts shaped exactly like it.** The reformed insider — the ex-NGO logistics hand who saw where the donor money really went and couldn't unsee it.

**The Jakobus rhyme:** Jakobus is the *physical* fixer (borders, the Beast, standing guard); Yusuf is the **economic** fixer — funds, feeds, and unblocks the crew, knows which lie greases which gate, and is **in recovery from having done it for the wrong side.**

**Arc (rhymes the chase):** *the man who once sold the dignified-sounding lie, now reading it from the inside to stop it.* His restitution he'll never call restitution. The book's blade is his to sharpen — **he is the one who first names that the adversary is buying a *silence***, the same withheld answer the elders keep by right, and turning it into a vacancy.

**Voice:** fast, fluent, charming, with a flinch under it. Talks like a man who knows the script because he wrote it.

## FRIK (Tourette's, the LIE — established crew, light here, load-bearing at the punctures)

**Layer:** the lie in the room — involuntary, exquisitely-timed blurted truth.
**Neurology:** Tourette syndrome — motor and vocal tics; more often *echolalia* and **blurted truth** than coprolalia (handled with care, never for laughs). He has spent his life thrown out of rooms for it; the crew is the first place he's not a problem to be managed.

**Why here (the over-reach puncture):** in a book whose cleanest blade is *the dragon is theirs, the spaceship is a theft of it*, **Frik's involuntary blurt names the theft** — when the resort's hired "consultant" (or a crew member) reaches past the evidence to rename the Pohnpeians' dragon a spacecraft, Frik says the true thing aloud, gently and devastatingly, at the moment it can't be taken back. He is the heckling fish made flesh and given full dignity. *(⚠ consultant veto; never swearing-for-laughs; never the comic relief at the neurology's expense.)*

**Voice:** the room's unsayable thought, said before he can stop it — and, underneath, a man bracing for the consequence.

## THE LOCAL EXPERT / CUSTODIAN — Pohnpeian oral historian of the Saudeleur sites (out-reads the crew; owns the answer)

> **`[INVENTION — provisional identity; Pohnpeian / FSM in-culture read REQUIRED before any naming, characterisation, or rendering of protocol/tradition. FLOOR, not nicety.]`** Seen only from the crew's POV (close-third rule).

**Who:** a **Pohnpeian custodian / oral historian** of the Saudeleur sites, of and near Madolenihmw — a keeper within the living *Nahnmwarki* order of right and rank.

**What is theirs (the firewall, sharpest in the line):**
- The ***Nahnmwarki* protocols** — who may enter, who may speak, what may be told to whom — are **theirs.**
- The **Olisihpa/Olosohpa-and-the-dragon tradition** is **theirs** to tell or withhold.
- The **"what is it for"** is **theirs to give or withhold.** The narrator never declares it proven.

**How they out-read the crew (the load-bearing inversion):** not on the arithmetic — Jakobus and the crew can do tonnages, and the custodian lets them. The custodian out-reads them on **the thing that matters most: what the city *is*, what the silence is keeping, and who has the right to open it.** The crew read the *pattern* and the *logistics*; the custodian reads the *place.* When the custodian gives the reading — the foodless, waterless siting as **the point, not the flaw**: a city built *apart*, for the dead and the god-kings, the constant resupply as the devotion made daily — it is the book's turn, and **it is the custodian's to give.** What stays unsaid stays unsaid, by right.

**Dignity contract:** the custodian is never the crew's puzzle, never a quaint informant, never "cracked." The win is the custodian's; the crew never own the place — and here the place is a grave. **If a draft reads as "clever outsiders crack the islanders' mystery," rebuild it** (Bible §3).

## JAKOBUS — the road (no POV; off home ground; SSOT, do not re-derive)

**Role (LOCKED):** the road, never the lead; seen only from outside; off home ground the entire series. He reads the **made thing** — the **header-and-stretcher columnar basalt**, the way the hexagonal logs bind a 7.5 m wall dry, the coral fill beneath — asking *not who, not is-it-real, but **what is it for.*** He can read **how it stands.** **The master question (*what is it for*) he hands to the custodian**, because on this ground the answer is not the engineer's to give — *he reads the wall into the custodian's waiting hand and lets the custodian decide what the wall is for.*

**Far from home:** a Pacific reef where almost nothing of his African mastery ports — the Beast shipped or sourced and useless on the water (a recurring beat). All series-wide tells carry: the **no-gun rule**, ***Sawubona*** in a handful of Pohnpeian, the **giving doctrine**, the **field kit** (the cayenne tin, the always-clean hanky, the Beast's weeping water bag), the shades / uniform-rule, music-as-metronome, **Stoffel the honey-badger as carried legend**, coins-for-knives. **Brother / uncle register** with the crew (default; no romance unless a later book earns its own). **Honour the tattoo-acquisition continuity — no ink unearned.** He is the **proof of the keel:** the reason a band of the written-off is on the road reading the world at all. He defers, carries, stands guard.

## THE ADVERSARY — the "heritage resort" / cultural-tourism development concern (clean-handed; a mortal want, never a moustache)

> **`[INVENTION]`** — a shell with a benevolent name; architect's renderings of boardwalks over the canals; a "preservation partnership"; a fistful of options on the access and the surrounding reef.

**The want (clean-handed, human):** they don't need the city to be ancient or alien — they need the **ground**, and they need the islanders' "we don't know" to **stay** a we-don't-know, because a place with no agreed meaning and no settled custodianship is **legally and morally easier to develop.** They weaponise the official story exactly as the line's template demands: ***"even the islanders say they don't know what it is"*** is **the best cover a land grab ever had** — it recasts a living grave as an empty curiosity, a backdrop for infinity pools, a thing improved by being opened to the world.

**The blade:** **the adversary is the institutional version of the withholding.** Where the custodian's silence *protects*, theirs *erases.* Two silences that look identical and mean opposite things — and **Mira is the one who can tell them apart.** Give the adversary clean hands, a deniable structure, and a real human want (return, growth, the linen-suit conviction that they're *helping*). Never villainy; the masterpiece-campaign doctrine — a real want, not a moustache.
