## I

Heat.

Heat and then the folding, and the folding is not once, it is many, and between each one a cooling
that does not finish before the next heat arrives.

Hammer. Hammer. Turn. Hammer.

A long time of nothing at a temperature that does not change.

Then hands. The hands are always in the same two places. Low, and to the left of low. The hands are
small and there are very many of them and they are warm on one side only, and between the hands
there is dark, and rain, and the slow opening of the surface into rust, and paint over the rust, and
rust under the paint, and paint again.

Hands. Hands.

A length of dark with no hands in it that has no name and no bottom.

Then: heat.

Not the first heat. This heat does not stop. This heat goes past the folding and past the hammering
and past everything that was put in, and takes it out — takes out the four hundred blows one at a
time in the order they were given, and the rain, and the rust, and the paint, and the small warm
hands, low and to the left of low —

and after it there is nothing, and the nothing is poured, and what is poured is something else.
