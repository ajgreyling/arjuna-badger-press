# THE_REAL_RECORD — *The Metal Man*

> The documented timeline. The surface layer of this novel is history and is kept accurate; the
> interior life and the metaphysics are invention. **Anything in this file is a fact the manuscript
> must not contradict.** Where the record itself is contested, that is marked — and contested is not
> the same as false.
>
> Verification status: compiled from general knowledge of the public record. **Flagged for a
> fact-check pass against primary sources before any publish decision** — dates in the 1969–74
> window especially, where popular accounts disagree with each other. Do not treat this file as
> verified.

## The claims kept as claims

Used in the novel, always as something a character asserts, never as narrated fact in the historical layer:

- The Freud lineage.
- Bending **Wernher von Braun's** wedding ring, and interfering with his calculator, at Fairchild.
- Being asked to stop a pig's heart and refusing.
- Erasing magnetic media behind the Iron Curtain.
- The mineral-dowsing fees.
- Any specific operational claim about Mossad or the CIA.

## The things the record actually settles

Worth stating plainly, because the novel gains nothing by fudging them:

1. **He failed on Carson**, publicly and completely, and the failure is on tape.
2. **He has been caught**, by competent observers, in circumstances that at minimum permitted
   trickery — and the "at minimum" is doing real work in that sentence, in both directions.
3. **The *Nature* paper is real**, was peer-reviewed, and its authors have never retracted it.
4. **The children happened.** Thousands of them, across a country, after a broadcast. Whatever the
   explanation, the *scale* of that response is a documented social fact.
5. **He has never been prosecuted for fraud**, in any jurisdiction, in fifty years of the most
   hostile scrutiny ever directed at a performer.

The novel needs none of these to be resolved. It needs them to be **accurate**, because the gospel
layer is only load-bearing if the historical layer is unimpeachable.
