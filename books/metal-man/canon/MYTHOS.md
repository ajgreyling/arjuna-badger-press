# MYTHOS — *The Metal Man* (BINDING)

> The rules that break the book if broken. Eleven of them.

1. **It is all true.** The novel never adjudicates, never hedges, never winks.
   [`THE_GOSPEL_PREMISE.md`](THE_GOSPEL_PREMISE.md)

2. **Metal only.** Every apparent exception has a metal object in the room doing the work, and it is
   findable in every single scene. No exceptions, ever.
   [`THE_MEMORY_OF_METAL.md`](THE_MEMORY_OF_METAL.md#4-the-hard-rules-of-the-power-binding)

3. **The household object is the only delivery mechanism.** If it will not fit in a kitchen drawer,
   it does not enter a scene.

4. **The intelligence never speaks in language.** No dialogue, no name for itself, no message. Only
   sequence, pressure, temperature, duration.
   [`THE_INTELLIGENCE.md`](THE_INTELLIGENCE.md#8-the-binding-restraint)

5. **The metal register never tells the reader anything Uri could not know** — except by implication.
   Seven appearances in the whole novel.

6. **The power fails, often, and is never excused.** Including twice when it matters enormously. He
   cheats twice and is caught twice.

7. **No cabal.** The containment is a convergence of separate reasonable decisions by people who
   never meet. Everyone is competent. The documents are dull.
   [`THE_CONTAINMENT.md`](THE_CONTAINMENT.md#5-the-rules-that-keep-this-from-being-conspiracy-slop)

8. **Randi is never a fool, never a villain, never mocked, and never confirmed.**

9. **No invented public statement is attributed to a real person.**
   [`LIVING_PERSONS.md`](LIVING_PERSONS.md#2-the-five-hard-rules)

10. **The narration is eloquent about metal and never about his interior.** He has no vocabulary for
    what he is, and the prose does not rescue him with one.

11. **Both readings survive the last page** — the gift and the infestation — and every fact in Act V
    supports both.
