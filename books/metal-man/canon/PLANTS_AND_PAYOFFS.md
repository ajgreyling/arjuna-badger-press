## Deliberate misfires (✗)

Guns that are hung and **never fired**, on purpose. Each one is a place the novel refuses to tidy:

- **PP-002, the backwards watch.** Never explained. Resist every temptation.
- **The Freud lineage.** Raised twice by other people, never confirmed, never denied, never relevant.
- **A. Meir's employer.** Never named, in fifty-seven years and one decent act.
- **Whether Randi knew.** See PP-018.
- **The Isle of Lamb treasure.** Mentioned once, in passing, as an absurdity. Nothing is buried there.
  Nothing is ever found. It is simply a thing a rich old man did.
