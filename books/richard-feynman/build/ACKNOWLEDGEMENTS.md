# Acknowledgements

This book exists because of everyone who has ever taken the trouble to write down, honestly and in
detail, what a real scientist's life actually cost and actually gave. My thanks to the biographers,
archivists, and oral historians whose careful documentation of Richard Feynman's life made an
honest retelling possible — and, most of all, to Ralph Leighton, whose patience with a tape
recorder preserved a voice this book has tried, in its own words, to do justice to rather than to
copy.

To everyone who was ever handed an encyclopedia and told to go find out what a thing actually
does, rather than merely what it is called: this is for you too.
