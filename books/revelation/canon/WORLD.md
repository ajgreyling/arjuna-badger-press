# WORLD.md

## Place
The primary setting for *REVELATION* is Ethiopia, a country steeped in rich religious history and cultural significance. The novel traverses various locales, including the ancient rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, the bustling markets of Addis Ababa, and the remote highlands where the Brotherhood of Abraham holds clandestine meetings. These settings serve as a backdrop for the story’s exploration of faith, power, and the ongoing battle between light and darkness. The Brotherhood operates from a hidden library deep within the mountains, containing original texts of the Bible, Torah, and Quran, safeguarded for centuries against corruption. The contrast between Ethiopia’s serene landscapes and the undercurrents of tension provides a vivid canvas for Dr. Leila Aziz's journey.

## Time period
*REVELATION* is set in the present day, reflecting contemporary issues surrounding religion, technology, and the intersection of ancient wisdom with modern society. This time frame allows for the inclusion of current events, such as geopolitical tensions, the rise of AI in translation efforts, and ongoing debates over religious interpretations. The urgency of the present day amplifies the stakes of Dr. Leila's quest, as she races against time to uncover truths that powerful forces would rather keep hidden.

## Rules of this world
In the world of *REVELATION*, the following rules govern the narrative:
- **Religious Text Integrity**: It is widely accepted that original religious texts hold the purest forms of wisdom and guidance. However, the manipulation of these texts by historical figures has led to widespread misconceptions and sectarian divides.
- **Existence of Secret Societies**: The Brotherhood of Abraham is a clandestine organization believed to be one of many secret societies that operate in the shadows, influencing political and religious landscapes. Their existence, though denied by mainstream institutions, is a reality that characters must navigate.
- **Technology and Tradition**: The use of AI-assisted re-translation is seen as a revolutionary tool, yet it also raises ethical questions about the preservation of sacred texts. This tension between technology and tradition is a recurring theme in the narrative.
- **Paranoia and Surveillance**: Characters experience a heightened sense of being watched, reflecting the real-world implications of modern surveillance and the fear of oppressive systems. This atmosphere of paranoia drives the conflict and character motivations.

## Sensory texture
The sensory experiences in *REVELATION* are vivid and immersive:
- **Sights**: The golden hues of Ethiopian sunsets, the intricate carvings of ancient churches, the vibrant colors of traditional clothing, and the stark beauty of the Ethiopian highlands contrast sharply with the dark, shadowy corners of the Brotherhood's secretive meetings.
- **Sounds**: The rhythmic chants of religious ceremonies, the bustling noise of city life in Addis Ababa, the whispering winds in the mountains, and the ominous silence during moments of tension create an auditory landscape that enhances the narrative's emotional weight.
- **Smells**: The mingling scents of incense from sacred rituals, the rich aroma of Ethiopian coffee brewing in local cafes, and the earthy smell of the highlands after rain contribute to the immersive experience, grounding the reader in the setting.

## Constraints the plot must respect
The plot of *REVELATION* must adhere to the following constraints:
- **Faith and Doubt**: The exploration of faith must remain nuanced, presenting the complexities of belief without promoting a singular viewpoint. Dr. Leila’s journey should reflect her internal conflict and growth as she grapples with her faith.
- **Historical Accuracy**: While the narrative is fictional, it should respect the historical and cultural significance of the religions and locations depicted. Any portrayal of real events or figures must be well-researched and thoughtfully integrated.
- **Character Integrity**: Dr. Leila Aziz’s motivations and emotional journey must remain consistent throughout the novel. Her guilt over her father's death and her paranoia should drive her actions and decisions without diminishing her intelligence and agency.
- **Tone and Theme**: The novel should evoke feelings of hope amidst darkness, allowing for moments of levity and human connection even in the face of overwhelming odds. The balance between tension and resolution is critical to maintaining the desired reader feeling.
