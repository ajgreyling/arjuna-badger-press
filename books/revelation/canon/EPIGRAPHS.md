# EPIGRAPHS.md — Per-Chapter Multilingual Epigraphs

_Author-approved 2026-05-30. Every chapter of_ REVELATION _opens with a short quote
from "the desert people / the old original people / the ancient ones," in an original
language with an English translation. This file is the canon the chapter rebuild reads —
the same way it reads `EVIDENCE_DECK.md`._

---

## Intent

Deepen the **desert mystique** and the **ancient, rich history of Africa / Ethiopia / the
Horn / the Arabian sand** that already underlie the setting (Lalibela, Aksum, the highlands,
the interfaith braid of Islam / Judaism / Christianity). The voice to evoke: the Fremen's
sand-wisdom of *Dune*; the lived devotion of desert-savvy believers (cf. *Six Years a
Hostage*); the unforced spirituality of Watts / Daoism / the good-natured Quran. Each
epigraph should **rhyme with its chapter's beat** — a discovery, a betrayal, a grief, a
choice — never be mere decoration.

## Locked decisions

1. **All 18 chapters** carry one. Retrofit Ch 1–9 (already rebuilt); add Ch 10–18 during rebuild.
2. **King Musa (Mansa Musa)** is included as a revered "ancient one" / desert legend — his
   trans-Saharan hajj of gold fits the mystique — even though he is West African (Mali), off
   the Ethiopian axis. The **Ethiopian / Aksumite tradition is the spine** (it's the setting);
   Musa sits among the ancients beside it.
3. **Free real/invented mix, lightly marked.** Real quotes where they land; invented
   "elders / the ancient ones" sayings elsewhere, attributed to fictional voices WITHOUT
   telling the reader which is which. Never let an invented line pose as a checkable historical
   quote (i.e. don't fabricate a specific verse-and-number or a named real author's words).

## Format (slots between a chapter's `# Title` and its first prose line)

```
> <original-script line(s)>
>
> *<English translation, italic>*
>
> — <attribution>
```

- Keep it **short**: 1–3 lines of source text.
- **Attribution** options: a real source named plainly (e.g. "Rumi"; "Kebra Nagast"); a fictional
  oral voice ("an elder of the sand-roads"; "saying of the keepers"); or a tradition
  ("Amharic proverb"). For the Brotherhood's own archive, attribute to the archive.
- **Script accuracy is mandatory.** Arabic / Amharic (Fidäl) / Ge'ez / Swahili must be correct,
  with a transliteration line when it helps. If a script can't be verified, mark it
  `⚠ unverified` in the table below and prefer a language we can get right, OR render the saying
  in transliteration only. Do NOT ship garbled script.
- Honors `STYLE_GUIDE.md` DON'T #4 and the retired-"Luciferian" override: no cosmological /
  Satanic framing; the desert wisdom is humane, not occult.

## Source palette

- **Languages:** Arabic (فصحى), Amharic (አማርኛ), Ge'ez/Liturgical (ግዕዝ), Swahili (Kiswahili);
  occasionally Tigrinya, Hebrew, Aramaic, Beja/Tuareg (Tamasheq) for texture.
- **Ethiopian / Horn spine:** Kebra Nagast (Queen of Sheba / Solomonic line), the Lalibela
  churches, Aksumite kingship, Ethiopian Orthodox liturgy, Amharic/Oromo proverbs.
- **Desert & Sufi:** Quran (humane verses) & hadith; Rumi; Rabia al-Adawiyya; Bedouin/Tuareg
  sand-lore; the trans-Saharan caravan world of **Mansa Musa**.
- **Cross-faith:** Torah/Tanakh & Talmudic sayings; desert-father Christian sayings — to mirror
  the book's "shared origins" thesis.

### Verified source notes (scripts confirmed via web before placing)
- **Ch 17 — sensitivity replacement (2026-06):** the original Qur'an 2:256 epigraph was removed per a
  sensitivity read (scripture used as a literary device above the chapter's fanaticism material was the
  trilogy's single highest-point reception risk, Islam-facing). Replaced with the Amharic proverb
  *የፈሰሰ ውሃ አይታፈስም* — "Spilled water cannot be gathered back up" — which matches the chapter's actual
  theme (Leila's released truth becoming uncontrollable; "a vacuum is filled by the loudest") and sits
  naturally in the book's Amharic-proverb register (ch-16 and ch-18 are also Amharic). No Qur'an quote
  now appears anywhere in the trilogy. Verified at easyamharic.com + allaboutethio.com. NOTE: a spelling
  variant exists across sources (ውሃ / ውሀ); confirm the preferred Fidäl in the native-speaker proofing pass.
- **Amharic proverbs** (Ch 1,3,9,10,11,13,14,16,17,18): exact Fidäl + English confirmed at easyamharic.com (70-proverb list) and allaboutethio.com (155-proverb list).
- **Swahili** (Ch 6): "Haraka haraka haina baraka" confirmed at Wiktionary / swahiliproverbs (U. Illinois).
- **Tuareg/Tamasheq** (Ch 4,12,15): "Aman iman" (water is life), "Gold is for the gods, salt is for men", and "…the desert, so that he can find his soul" confirmed via Tuareg-proverb sources; rendered in transliteration (Latin) — Tifinagh script intentionally NOT used (unverifiable here), per the "transliteration only" allowance.
- **Invented elder-sayings** (Ch 2,5,7,8): attributed to fictional oral voices / the Brotherhood's archive — rendered in **transliteration + English only** (no original script claimed), so none can be mistaken for a checkable historical quote. Arabic transliteration used for the desert/Sufi register; Hebrew transliteration for the Covenant fragment.
